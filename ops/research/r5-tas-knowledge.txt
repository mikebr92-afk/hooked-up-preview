BIG RULE CHANGE - SAND FLATHEAD (NRE Tas, in effect from 1 March 2026)  *** add to "Rules to verify and cite" ***
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- South-eastern zone: sand flathead bag limit 0 and on-water possession 0 (catch and release only). The zone is all water
  between a north-south line at Whale Head (146 52'15"E) and one at Cape Pillar (148 00'41"E), including the old
  D'Entrecasteaux Channel, Derwent River, Frederick Henry and Norfolk Bays zone; northern boundary is the southern end of
  the Dunalley Canal. So Margate/Blackmans Bay/Opossum Bay (Diver's) and Dodges Ferry (Mr Flathead bay charter) are inside;
  Pirates Bay appears to be inside too (confirm on the NRE map).
- Eastern zone: sand flathead bag 5, legal size 35-40 cm slot (10 on land). Northern/Western zone: bag 10, 35-40 cm slot.
  King and Flinders islands: bag 10, min 35 cm. Tiger flathead: bag 20, min 32 cm. Combined sand + tiger: bag 20,
  possession 30.
- Statewide: all flathead must be landed whole or as fillets with intact frames (head and tail attached).
- Sources: https://fishing.tas.gov.au/community/flathead-for-the-future/new-rules-and-research-to-rebuild-tasmanias-sand-flathead-fishery
  and https://fishing.tas.gov.au/recreational-fishing/rules/size-and-bag-limits
  (old /Pages/... URLs redirect to these).

OTHER OFFICIAL RULES (re-confirmed 2026-11-08)
---------------------------------------------
- IFS 2026-27 season dates unchanged from round 1 (1 Aug 2026 open; rainbow waters 3 Oct 2026; Canal Bay 28 Nov 2026 -
  28 Mar 2027; most close 2 May 2027; rainbow + lower Brumbys/Macquarie/Meander/South Esk + lakes Mackintosh and
  Rosebery close 30 May 2027). New detail: Gone Fishing Day 18 Oct 2026; Penstock Lagoon No. 1 and 2 canals closed above
  the white posts; some waters restricted to 1 hour before sunrise - 3 hours after sunset. Indigenous fish waters (bream)
  need no licence; trout caught there unlicensed must be released.
  https://www.ifs.tas.gov.au/the-rules/season-dates-and-times
- IFS licence 2026-27 (one rod / two rods): adult season $96 / $115.50; 28 days $76 / $85.50; 7 days $49 / $58.50;
  48 hours $29 / $38.50; five seasons adult $480; juniors 14-17 free; young adults 18-21 $52.50; 80+ $29. Licence year
  1 Aug - 31 Jul. https://www.ifs.tas.gov.au/the-rules/angling-licence
- IFS still says all guides operating in Tasmania must meet certified, audited accreditation including Tourism Council
  Tasmania endorsement; private fisheries need no licence (Currawong Lakes listed).
  https://www.ifs.tas.gov.au/get-expert-advice  (the /trout-fish-tasmania/registered-private-fisheries URL 404s)
- NRE Tas sea licence 2026-27: licensing year 1 Nov 2026 - 31 Oct 2027; standard first licence $68.60, each extra type
  $9.80; concession $39.20; no short-term licences; new online system at https://reclicence.fishing.tas.gov.au/.
- Charter rules unchanged: no charter licence under LMRMA; clients need any recreational licence the activity requires;
  crew cannot set gear for clients; operator must enforce bag/boat/possession limits; future registration/reporting
  possible. https://fishing.tas.gov.au/commercial-fishing/commercial-fishing-licences/charter-and-seafood-tourism
- Tuna (NRE size and bag limits): southern bluefin + yellowfin + bigeye combined bag 2, possession 2, boat limit 4 (only
  2 over 1.5 m); albacore bag 5; skipjack bag 10. Striped trumpeter eastern bag 2, min 55 cm.
- SBT timing (official): NRE Tas species page - SBT arrive as water cools, "generally from March to June", targeted off
  the east and south coasts in autumn and into winter. Use this instead of the South East Charters "March-August" claim.
  https://fishing.tas.gov.au/species/southern-bluefin-tuna
- Seasons page (2026-11-08): south-east squid closure live until 14 Nov 2026; garfish Southern Waters closed
  15 Nov - 14 Dec 2026 (new); rock lobster ECSRZ opens 5 Dec 2026; scallops open 20 Mar - 31 Jul 2027 (D'Entrecasteaux
  Channel stays closed). Wild shellfish alert: Pipe Clay Lagoon, White Beach, Nubeena.
- NRE Tas species URLs for striped trumpeter (/species/striped-trumpeter) and flathead (/species/flathead) 404; sand
  flathead is at /species/flathead-sand.
