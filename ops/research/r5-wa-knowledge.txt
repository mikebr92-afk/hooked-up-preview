3. RULES CONFIRMED THIS ROUND (official pages read first-hand unless noted)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- NEW, confirmed: from 1 June 2026, outside the West Coast bioregion (North Coast, Gascoyne, South Coast): mixed
  demersal bag limit 4 per person per day (was 5); dhufish 1; coral trout, coronation trout, western blue groper 1;
  pink snapper, red emperor and tuskfish (incl. baldchin, bluebone) 2; pink snapper and red emperor min size 45cm.
  Sources: DPIRD rules page and bag-and-size-limits page (mixed 4, snapper 2, red emperor 2 worked example), and
  Recfishwest's summary. Guides now state these numbers instead of 'check the DPIRD rules'.
  https://www.dpird.wa.gov.au/individuals/recreational-fishing/recreational-fishing-rules/
  https://www.dpird.wa.gov.au/individuals/recreational-fishing/recreational-fishing-rules/recreational-bag-and-size-limits/
  https://recfishwest.org.au/news/new-demersal-rules-and-50-tackle-rebate-announced/
- Possession limits (DPIRD bag-and-size page): general 10kg fillets + 10kg skin-on large pelagic fillets (or other
  combos); 20kg of fillets allowed after an extended charter tour with overnight accommodation if labelled with
  name, boat name and tour end date (explains Apache's 'up to 20kg'); Abrolhos: 5kg fillets + 5kg large pelagic
  fillets, or one day's bag of whole fish, and the West Coast demersal boat limit applies; barramundi away from
  home: 2 whole or 4 fillets.
- Bioregion boundaries (DPIRD): North Coast = Ashburton River (south of Onslow) to the NT border; Gascoyne =
  Zuytdorp Cliffs (north of Kalbarri) to the Ashburton River. Shark Bay pink snapper closures: Freycinet Estuary
  15 Aug-30 Sep; Northern Bernier Island 1 Jun-31 Aug (useful for a future shark-bay page).
- Abrolhos (DPIRD): anyone travelling by boat must notify DPIRD online before entering the fish habitat protection
  area; jointly managed by DPIRD and DBCA; strong southerlies (>32km/h) 44% of the time; register trips with
  Marine Rescue Geraldton.
- Unchanged: charter page (clients exempt from RFBL; strategic charter review consultation closed 11 May 2026,
  outcome still not published); West Coast demersal closed until spring 2027; metro charter demersal closed from
  16 Dec 2025, 25t tag limit elsewhere.
- Parks and Wildlife (DBCA): exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au and dbca.wa.gov.au return HTTP 406 to the pipeline, so these
  facts come from search-result text of the official pages (not fetched first-hand): sanctuary zones are 'look but
  don't take' (marine-park-conservation page); Tantabiddi boat ramp sits beside a recreation zone next to the
  Tantabiddi Sanctuary Zone; Rowley Shoals: no fishing in sanctuary zones at Clerke and Imperieuse reefs, cod,
  groper and wrasse fully protected in all zones (2023 visitor guide); Abrolhos NP: responsible fishing permitted,
  no camping (park page); Be CROCWISE: saltwater crocodiles throughout the Kimberley, active year round, also in
  freshwater far from the coast (factsheet). Owner may want to spot-check these in a browser.
- Tourism WA source now cited: https://www.westernaustralia.com/en/things-to-do/beaches-and-lakes/water-sports-and-activities
  (features Evolution Fishing Charters, Birchy's and Blue Lightning Fishing Charters).
