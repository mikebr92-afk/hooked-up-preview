1. OFFICIAL RULES - NOW CONFIRMED FROM OFFICIAL PAGES (use these on the site)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Licence prices 2026-27 (Service Victoria, fetched: https://www.service.vic.gov.au/fishing)
  3-day $10.00; 28-day $24.70; 1-year $43.30 ($2.50 cheaper online = $40.80); 3-year $117.40 ($6.20 cheaper online = $111.20).
  Licence needed if 18 to 69; under-18 and 70+ get a FREE junior/senior fisher card. The licence is valid in Victorian waters only.
  (Official schedule PDF agrees on $10 / $40.80 online / $111.20 online: djsir.vic.gov.au 2026-27 Fees and Penalties VFA.)
  Charter clients: no charter exemption in Victoria (VFA licence page via search; operators repeat it: Gone Fishing, I'm Hooked, Sharkmen FAQ).
Lake Hume (fetched: https://www.vic.gov.au/fisheries-management-lake-hume-notice-2026, published 15 Apr 2026 by VFA)
  Anyone who has paid the NSW fishing fee is exempt from needing a Victorian licence on Lake Hume, if carrying the NSW receipt.
  VFA manages Lake Hume (so a Vic licence is valid there); NSW DPI manages Lake Mulwala.
  -> Replace older wording "Lake Hume is the exception, where a Victorian licence is valid" with "either licence is accepted".
Murray River / Lake Mulwala: NSW licence needed even when fishing from the Victorian bank; a Victorian licence is NOT valid on the
  Murray except Lake Hume (VFA "Murray River (NSW)" article, via search). Lake Mulwala = NSW water (yarrawongamulwala.com.au, fetched).
Murray cod (VFA murraycod page, via search extract; vfa.vic.gov.au still 403 to pipeline and fetcher)
  Closed 1 Sep - 30 Nov INCLUSIVE; not closed at Lake Eildon, Eildon Pondage, Arcadia Pondage (+ other listed lakes).
  Slot 55-75 cm; bag 1 in rivers, 2 in specified lakes/impoundments (+ urban lakes). The guides say "specified lakes the VFA lists"
  rather than naming Eildon as one. OWNER: confirm Lake Eildon is on the specified-lakes list before quoting "2 a day at Eildon".
  NSW reopened inland waters for cod on 1 Dec 2025 (DPI media release). Lake Mulwala Sportfishing's site says "until 1 December";
  official end is 30 Nov - its FAQ was rewritten to 30 November.
Trout (VFA trout and salmon regulations, via search)
  River/stream closed season 2026: Tue 9 June to Fri 4 Sept inclusive (first open day Sat 5 Sept 2026). Rule of thumb: from the
  Monday of the King's Birthday weekend to the first Friday in September. Lakes are not closed. VRFish (1 Jun 2026): total ban on all
  hook-and-line fishing in sections of the Goulburn, Mitta Mitta, Tanjil and Kiewa during the closure.
  -> Replaces round-1 "trout river seasons NOT confirmed".
Marine national parks (Parks Victoria, fetched)
  Port Phillip Heads MNP: "Fishing from the shore or at sea is prohibited"; you may carry fish caught outside and a rod while
  travelling straight through. Yaringa MNP page points anglers to the VFA app boundary locator. French Island / Churchill Island MNPs
  are no-fishing per Visit Bass Coast (search) - VFA restricted-areas page cited.
