RULES AND OFFICIAL SOURCES (new or changed)
- Coral reef fin fish closures 2027: 26-30 October and 25-29 November 2027 (Fisheries amendment SL 2024 No. 189, per web search, plus
  the Fishos Tackle World Hervey Bay rules page, which mirrors the official list; it also gives 2028 as 15-19 Oct and 13-17 Nov).
  All guides rolled forward from the 2026 dates. The legislation PDF did not parse in the pipeline and the DPI page returns 403, so the
  owner may want to confirm once on dpi.qld.gov.au.
- Southern Spanish mackerel closures: 1-21 February and 1-21 March EVERY YEAR, east coast waters south of latitude 22 S (just north of
  Stanage Bay), so they apply to every QLD-south destination. Added to the state page, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Bundaberg, Yeppoon and
  the Southern GBR hub. Northern (north of 22 S) closures are new-moon based and vary by year.
- Fisheries pages: DAF has become DPI. Use https://www.dpi.qld.gov.au/services/fisheries/closures/tidal-seasons (403 to the pipeline).
  Limits page fetched OK: https://www.qld.gov.au/recreation/activities/boating-fishing/rec-fishing/rules/limits-tidal (combined coral
  reef fin fish limit 20; no blue-spotted coral trout fillets on boats; two five-day Oct/Nov closures).
  The old URL .../rec-fishing/rules/closed-seasons now 404s.
- Designated coastal bars (MSQ, https://www.msq.qld.gov.au/Safety/Crossing-a-bar, per search; page blocks the pipeline): include Gold
  Coast Seaway, Jumpinpin, Currumbin, Bribie Island, Caloundra, Mooloolah, Maroochy, Noosa, Round Hill Creek and Cawarral Creek. South
  Passage was NOT in the list returned. New lifejacket rules for bar crossings started 1 Dec 2024 and are enforced from 1 Jul 2025;
  sources differ on scope (open boats of any length vs open boats under 4.8 m), so the guides don't state the rule.
- Round Hill Creek (1770): MSQ Discovery Coast beacon guide says cross only in daylight without local knowledge and contact VMR Round
  Hill first; VMR Round Hill base 07 4974 9383 (VHF 16, 81, 82; 0630-1800 daily). Buoys moved in 2023 and Oct 2024 (MSQ notices).
- Marine parks: https://www.qld.gov.au/environment/coasts-waterways/marine-parks/zoning (fetched: green = no take; three state marine
  parks). Great Sandy Marine Park new zoning plan commenced 21 May 2024 (https://www.qld.gov.au/greatsandymarinepark, per search; green
  zones reportedly up from 3.9% to 12.8% - not used). Moreton Bay FAQ (parks.qld.gov.au, per search): no fishing in green zones; transit
  with fish caught elsewhere is allowed.
- Agnes Water tourism (Discovery Coast Tourism and Commerce): visitagnes1770.com.au says the closest reef is "approx. 30 miles offshore"
  (the Bunker Group), while Hooked On 1770 fishes deep-water reef patches within 15 nm; the guide now explains both. Also lists Baffle
  Creek, Jenny Lind Creek, Wreck Rock and Agnes Point; Miriam Vale is the nearest train station (40 min).
