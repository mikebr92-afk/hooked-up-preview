SAND FLATHEAD CLOSURE (priority 1)
----------------------------------
NRE Tas: from 1 March 2026 sand flathead have a bag limit of 0 and an on-water possession limit of 0 in the south-eastern zone. The zone is all water between north-south lines at Whale Head (146 52'15"E) and Cape Pillar (148 00'41"E). It includes the D'Entrecasteaux Channel, Derwent, and Frederick Henry and Norfolk bays, with its northern boundary at the southern end of the Dunalley Canal.
