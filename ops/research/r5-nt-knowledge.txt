OFFICIAL RULE SOURCES (new; nt.gov.au pages read via search results, as the pipeline is still blocked)
- No recreational fishing licence or recreational boat licence in the NT:
  https://nt.gov.au/marine/recreational-fishing/rules/about-recreational-fishing
- Mary River Fish Management Zone: 3 barramundi per person, minimum 55 cm, vessel limit of 1 barra over 90 cm; no cast
  or drag nets; within 100 m of the Shady Camp barrage wall only a lure or fly with a single-point hook.
  https://nt.gov.au/marine/recreational-fishing/rules-for-fishing-in-specific-areas/mary-river-fish-management-zone
  Year-round opening below the barrage since 2014: https://mediareleases.nt.gov.au/article?id=14713
  (An older PFES release says 2 barra; the current NT page says 3.)
- Daly River seasonal closure, 1 Oct–31 Jan: no fishing, no possessing barramundi, no baited or hooked line in the
  closure area (map on the page). Boundary per a 2015 release: downstream of the old outlet channel of Moon Billabong to
  the mouth. https://nt.gov.au/marine/recreational-fishing/rules-for-fishing-in-specific-areas/daly-river-fish-management-zone
  The 2016 'Fishing the Daly River' guide also gives 3 barra/55 cm/1 over 90 cm per vessel for the Daly zone (not
  published, as it's old).
- Kakadu (Parks Australia now serves from kakadu.gov.au; parksaustralia.gov.au/kakadu/... redirects):
  - passes valid 7 days; adult $40 dry season (15 May–31 Oct), $25 tropical summer; NT residents free; passes for
    1 Jul–31 Oct 2026 are charged at wet-season rates. https://kakadu.gov.au/plan/passes/
  - Kakadu has the same bag limits as the Mary and Daly rivers; fish only in visitor areas; stand at least 5 m back
    from the bank. https://kakadu.gov.au/things-do/activities/fishing-boating/
- Northern Land Council permits (https://www.nlc.org.au/permits): written permit needed to enter Aboriginal land,
  including intertidal waters; recreational fishing permits cover specific zones. CHANGE: the page now says
  "Recreation applications are temporarily unavailable online" (download the application pack instead). Guides no
  longer say "free online permit since 2023". Fines: 50 penalty units ($18,200) Commonwealth or 8 units ($1,512) NT.
