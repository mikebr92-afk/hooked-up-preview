1. OFFICIAL RULES (with sources)
--------------------------------
Do charter clients need a Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence (RFL)?  YES, unless personally exempt.
  - Official VFA answer: the VFA licence page says an RFL "covers all forms of recreational fishing in all of
    Victoria's marine, estuarine and inland waters" and "unless you are exempt, it is your responsibility to
    hold a current RFL when fishing in Victoria" (penalties apply). The exemption list is personal only:
    under 18, 70 and over, Victorian Seniors Card (or interstate equivalent), Veterans Card Victoria,
    DVA pensioner / TPI-coded cards, Commonwealth Pensioner Concession Card coded DSP, DSP Blind, AGE,
    AGE Blind or CAR, and anyone who identifies as Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander. There is NO
    charter-vessel exemption (unlike NSW's charter fee-exemption certificate).
    Source: https://vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/fishing-licence
  - Operators repeat the government wording: "all anglers, including clients on fishing charter vessels, are
    required to obtain a Recreational Fishing Licence prior to their charter"
    (https://www.gonefishing.com.au/charter-locations/queenscliff-5-hour-fishing-charter/ ;
     https://imhookedfishingcharters.com.au/fishing-charter-faq). A Productivity Commission submission also
    notes that in Victoria each charter customer must hold a licence
    (https://assets.pc.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/208285/subdr068-fisheries-aquaculture.pdf).
  - CAVEAT: vfa.vic.gov.au blocks both the session's fetcher and the GitHub pipeline (Cloudflare 403), so the
    VFA text above comes from search-result extracts of the VFA page, not a direct read. Owner: open the VFA
    page once and confirm the wording before quoting it on the site.
  - Possible one-day exception: Geelong Fishing Charters says a licence was "NOT required" on its "Gone Fishing
    Day" special (Sunday 18 October). Not verified with VFA - check before mentioning it anywhere.

Murray cod closed season - CONFIRMED.
  - VFA: closed 1 September to 30 November inclusive, in most rivers and lakes north of the Great Dividing
    Range plus the Yarra River. Not closed in Lake Eildon, Eildon Pondage, Arcadia Pondage, Lake Lascelles,
    Willow Lake and Ouyen Lake; also removed for Eppalock, Cairn Curran and Nillahcootie (from 2024);
    Wimmera/Millicent basins open except the Wimmera River. Source: https://vfa.vic.gov.au/murraycod
    (also https://vfa.vic.gov.au/about/media-releases/murray-cod-closed-season-removed-at-lake-eildon).
    A 2026 Government Gazette notice (GG2026S182) also removes the closed season in Arcadia Pondage.
  - Statewide slot limit 55-75 cm; bag 1 per day in rivers, 2 in lakes (VFA, via search) - verify before
    quoting numbers.
  - Murray River / Lake Mulwala are NSW waters: NSW closed season 1 Sep-30 Nov; the Murray from Yarrawonga
    Weir to Tocumwal Road Bridge is closed to ALL fishing 1 Sep-30 Nov
    (https://www.yarrawongachronicle.com.au/news/fishing-downstream-of-the-weir-off-limits).
    Some operator sites (Lake Mulwala Sportfishing) say "until 1 December" - the official end is 30 Nov.
  - Lake Mulwala needs a NSW recreational fishing licence (https://www.yarrawongamulwala.com.au/things-to-do/fishing).
    Lake Hume: VFA says NSW licence holders don't need a Victorian licence there (reciprocal), via search.

Southern bluefin tuna: VFA checks recreational and charter-boat clients against the SBT daily bag limit
  (https://vfa.vic.gov.au/about/news/tuna-fishing-a-long-weekend-drawcard-for-anglers). Number not quoted.
Trout river seasons: NOT confirmed this round (VFA blocked). Guides say "check current VFA rules".
Life jackets: everyone must wear one crossing Port Phillip Heads (operator statement, I'm Hooked).

-----------------------------
  - Port Phillip/Western Port snapper: Oct-Dec peak (most operators); Pro Red Oct-Apr; Cassar Sep-Jan;
    Geelong Sep-Mar/Apr; Hastings fires early, run moves to Carrum later.
  - Portland SBT: local operators May-Aug (Game On); Unreel schoolies Apr-Jul, barrels Mar-Oct; Sharkmen Apr-Aug;

  - Kingfish: mid-Jan to early Apr (Queenscliff, Western Port, Portland/Port Fairy).
  - Lakes Entrance: snapper all year (pinkies from May; biggest Oct-Apr per Visit East Gippsland).
