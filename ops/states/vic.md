## Where to start (discovery)
- Visit Victoria (visitvictoria.com) and Visit Melbourne fishing pages; regional tourism for Great Ocean Road, Phillip
  Island and Gippsland.
- Victorian Fisheries Authority (vfa.vic.gov.au).
- VRFish.

## Rules to verify and cite
- Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence: **check whether clients of a licensed charter are covered** (commonly yes)
  and cite the VFA page.
- Murray cod closed season (about 1 Sep – 30 Nov) and slot limits: confirm. Trout seasons in rivers: confirm.
- Snapper: Port Phillip peak season is spring and early summer (operator sources).
- Southern bluefin tuna: Portland season about Mar–Jul (operator sources).
