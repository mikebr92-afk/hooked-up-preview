## Rules notes
- No recreational fishing licence in the NT.
- nt.gov.au blocks the pipeline, so give exact possession limits only if sourced; otherwise "check the current NT
  Fisheries rules".
- The Mary River Fish Management Zone has lower barra possession limits and gear rules near the Shady Camp barrage.
  Below the barrage it is open year-round (closure removed 2014).
- Permits are needed for Aboriginal land and waters (Arnhem Land, Tiwi, parts of Kakadu). Kakadu and Mary River
  National Park need park passes.
- Saltwater crocodiles everywhere.
