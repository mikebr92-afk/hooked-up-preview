  (TPTGA).

## Rules to verify and cite
- Marine rod-and-line fishing: no general licence. Licences are needed for rock lobster, abalone, scallops and nets.
  **Confirm with fishing.tas.gov.au (NRE Tas).**
- Inland waters need an angling licence from IFS. **Confirm whether clients of a guide still need their own licence**
  (they usually do).
- Brown trout season dates (roughly Aug–May) and some all-year waters: give dates only if confirmed.
- Southern bluefin tuna season is about Mar–Jul off the Tasman Peninsula and the east coast (confirm with operators).

=====================================================================================

OFFICIAL RULES (confirmed from the official pages, Nov 2026)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Marine (sea) fishing - charter clients
   - "You don't need a licence for rod and line fishing in marine waters in Tasmania."
     Licences ARE needed for: rock lobster (pot, ring or dive), abalone, scallop, graball net,
     mullet net, beach seine net, set line. Licensing year 1 Nov 2026 - 31 Oct 2027; no short-term
     licences; standard first licence $68.60, extra types $9.80 each.
     Source: https://fishing.tas.gov.au/recreational-fishing/recreational-sea-fishing-licences
   - Charter clients: NRE Tas says sea charter boat fishing "has no specific licence requirements
     under the LMRMA, but charter participants must ensure they comply with all recreational
     fishing rules", and "All clients engaging in a recreational fishing activity require the
     appropriate recreational fishing licence(s), with bag, boat, possession and gear limits
     applying." Crew cannot set gear on behalf of a client. So: rod-and-line charter clients need
     NO licence; clients diving for crays/abalone or potting need their own licence.
     Source: https://fishing.tas.gov.au/commercial-fishing/commercial-fishing-licences/charter-and-seafood-tourism
     (old URL https://fishing.tas.gov.au/Pages/Charter-and-Seafood-Tourism.aspx redirects there)
   - NRE Tas notes it may introduce charter registration/licences/catch reporting in future - watch.

2. Inland (trout) - guided anglers
   - IFS: "Any person who is at least 14 years of age must have a current angling licence to fish
     with a rod, reel or line at any inland water in Tasmania. This includes farm dams on private
     property." No exemption for guided clients. Exemptions only: registered private fisheries
     (e.g. Currawong Lakes; fees apply), native fish in specified indigenous-fish waters, under-14s
     with a single rod. Licences: 48 hours, 7 days, 28 days, one season, five seasons; 1 or 2 rods.
     2026-27: adult season 1 rod $96.00; 28 days $76.00; 7 days $49.00; juniors 14-17 free;
     licence year 1 Aug - 31 Jul. Some guides (Trout Territory, Red Tag) are IFS licence agents.
     Source: https://www.ifs.tas.gov.au/the-rules/angling-licence
     and https://ifs.tas.gov.au/get-expert-advice (private fisheries need no licence)
   - IFS also states all guides operating in Tasmania must meet certified accreditation standards
     incl. Tourism Council of Tasmania endorsement; TGALT is the industry body.

3. Trout season dates 2026-27 (CONFIRMED, IFS)
   - Sat 1 Aug 2026: opening of most waters (brown trout waters: first Sat in Aug to Sunday nearest 30 Apr)
   - Sat 3 Oct 2026: designated rainbow trout waters open
   - Sat 28 Nov 2026 - Sun 28 Mar 2027: Canal Bay, yingina / Great Lake open
   - Sun 2 May 2027: closure of most waters
   - Sun 30 May 2027: closure of rainbow waters, lakes Mackintosh and Rosebery, and lower Brumbys
     Creek, Macquarie, Meander and South Esk rivers
   - Open all year: Great Lake (except Canal Bay), Bradys, Brushy Lagoon, Craigbourne, Huntsman,
     Huon below Huonville Bridge, Barrington, Binney, Burbury, King William, Pedder, Meadowbank,
     Pioneer, Derwent below New Norfolk Bridge, Leven below Whisky Creek, Tamar below Charles St /
     West Tamar Rd bridges, Tungatinah Lagoon.
   - Closed: waters flowing into Great Lake, Lake Leake, Arthurs Lake and Dee Lagoon (+50 m below).
   - Trout Weekend, Liawenee 10-11 Oct 2026.
   Source: https://www.ifs.tas.gov.au/the-rules/season-dates-and-times

4. Marine seasons (NRE Tas): striped trumpeter CLOSED 1 Sep - 31 Oct every year; squid/calamari
   South-East (Lemon Rock to Whale Head incl. Coles Bay/Great Oyster Bay) closed 15 Oct - 14 Nov
   in 2025 and 2026, North Coast closed 1 Sep - 31 Oct; banded morwong closed 1 Mar - 30 Apr;
   rock lobster east reopened 31 Oct 2026 (ECSRZ 5 Dec 2026).
   Source: https://fishing.tas.gov.au/recreational-fishing/rules/recreational-fishing-seasons
   Shark refuge areas: Georges Bay (St Helens); Frederick Henry, Norfolk and Blackman bays.
   Sources: .../hot-fishing-spots/fishing-around-st-helens and .../fishing-around-the-tasman-peninsula-region

5. Southern bluefin season: South East Charters advertises game fishing March-August from
   Eaglehawk Neck/Port Arthur (operator source). Not confirmed by government.
