## Where to start (discovery)
- Discover Tasmania (discovertasmania.com.au) and its fishing pages.
- Trout guides: Inland Fisheries Service (ifs.tas.gov.au) and the Tasmanian Professional Trout Guides Association
  (TPTGA).
- East Coast Tasmania and Hobart/Tasman visitor information.

## Rules to verify and cite
- Marine rod-and-line fishing: no general licence. Licences are needed for rock lobster, abalone, scallops and nets.
  **Confirm with fishing.tas.gov.au (NRE Tas).**
- Inland waters need an angling licence from IFS. **Confirm whether clients of a guide still need their own licence**
  (they usually do).
- Brown trout season dates (roughly Aug–May) and some all-year waters: give dates only if confirmed.
- Southern bluefin tuna season is about Mar–Jul off the Tasman Peninsula and the east coast (confirm with operators).
