## Where to start (discovery)
- Tourism Western Australia (westernaustralia.com), Australia's North West tourism, Australia's Coral Coast,
  Experience Perth, and the Broome / Kununurra / Exmouth visitor centres. Search each region's "fishing charters"
  page once, then fetch it with the pipeline.
- Recfishwest has charter information. Fisheries is DPIRD (fish.wa.gov.au / dpird.wa.gov.au), which runs fishing tour
  operator licensing.

## Rules to verify and cite (these are hints, not facts)
- Fishing from a powered boat normally needs a Recreational Fishing from Boat Licence (RFBL). **Check whether clients
  on a licensed fishing tour operator's boat are exempt**, and use the official wording.
- West Coast Bioregion demersal scalefish (dhufish, snapper, baldchin) seasonal closures have changed in recent years.
  **Find the current 2026 rules on the official page.** If you can't confirm them, say "check the current DPIRD
  rules".
- Ningaloo and Shark Bay marine park sanctuary zones; Rottnest Island zones.
- Kimberley and Pilbara: saltwater crocodiles, big tides.
