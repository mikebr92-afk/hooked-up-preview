Workman’s Beach parkrun
October 31 @ 6:50 am - 9:00 am 
Workman’s Beach parkrun 
Workman’s Beach parkrun is a free, friendly, timed 5 km run or walk through coastal bushland in Agnes Water. Whether you’re a local or just visiting, this event is open to all ages and fitness levels — runners, joggers, walkers, kids, and dogs on short leads all welcome. The course starts at the northern gate of the Workman’s Beach day-use area and consists of two gravel loops, returning through grassy and sandy track sections, including part of the Discovery Trail. Along the way you’ll enjoy lush coastal scenery, fresh ocean air, and a relaxed community atmosphere. Facilities at the day-use area include toilets, water, picnic spaces, and a cold shower […]
