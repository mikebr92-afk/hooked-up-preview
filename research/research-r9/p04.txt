### Our full-day reef fishing trips run for 7 hours, heading either south or north depending on where the fish are and what species are in season. Popular catches include kingfish, bonito, salmon, striped tuna, snapper, and flathead, along with many others. We often target deep reefs between 40m and 80m using paternoster rigs and the freshest bait, but we also like to mix things up with shallow water drifting, float lining on anchor in a burly trail, or trolling the coast with deep-diving hardbodies—choosing whatever technique works best at the time to get fish on board. Our Odd Bod Days (individual trips) cater to all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced anglers. You can have the crew do everything for you or as little as you like. We’re happy to share tips, a few secrets, and teach you about the species you catch, including how to prepare them for eating or release. Our crew will even clean and bag your fish so it’s ready to take home and cook.
