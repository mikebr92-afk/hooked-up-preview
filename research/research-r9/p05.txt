Our full day reef fishing trips are 7 hours in duration, and we travel either south or north depending on where the fish are or what fish are available at that time of year that can include kingfish, bonito, salmon, striped tuna, snapper and flathead and many more too many to list, but they are our most popular species we target.
Pick up location is Cronulla public ferry wharf Tonkin st Cronulla.
We mostly fish the bottom with fresh baits and also sport fish for Pelagic species like kings and bonito tuna and salmon and many others.
We also jig and live bait on the popular reefs like 12 mile and the peak and everything in-between this is offshore so it’s a good idea to have seasick meds if you're not active on the water often.
The most common technique used is to fish the deep reefs that are 70m to 100m deep and to use paternoster rigs with the freshest bait possible, we also do this on occasion but we like to mix it up with some shallow water drifting or float lining on anchor in a burly trail and trolling the coast with deep diving hardbodies, whatever we choose, it is because it's the most affective technique at that time to get fish in the boat.
Our charters are for all levels of fishing skill or none at all we do everything for you or nothing at all its up to you how you would like the crew to help we will give advice and a few secret's on how to get the fish in the boat teaching you what species it is and how to eat it or release it our experienced crew will help out in all aspects we will even clean your fish and bag it ready to take home and consume.
