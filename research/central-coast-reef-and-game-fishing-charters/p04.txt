Pricing 
All prices stated include GST
REEF FISHING CHARTER
6:00am - 1:30pm (7.5 hours). All bait and tackle supplied. You may bring your own if you wish. *Please note: for mixed groups, we will only put a maximum of 10 people on the boat for comfort and to keep tangles to a minimum.
• MIXED GROUPS $250 per person (min 5, max 10)
• LARGER GROUPS $250 per person (max 10)
• EXCLUSIVE BOAT HIRE $2,500 (no min, max 10)
GAME FISHING CHARTER
6:00am - 4:00pm (10 hours). All bait and tackle supplied. You may bring your own if you wish. *Please note: for mixed groups, we will only put a maximum of 6 people on the boat for comfort and to keep tangles to a minimum.
• GROUP BOOKINGS ONLY up to $3,000 (max 6 people)
WHALE WATCHING
2:30pm - 4:30pm (2 hours). This is seasonal and while there is no guarantee of seeing whales, we will only do the charter when we've observed whales migrating.
• GROUP BOOKING ONLY $1,100 (max 14 people)
ASH SCATTERING
Depends on availability. (up to 2 hours)
• GROUP BOOKING ONLY $660 (max 15 people)
Payment Options
The booking is not confirmed until payment is received. We do not 'pencil in' bookings, they must be paid for at the time of booking.
• Visa or Mastercard
• Cash on the day BUT only with a credit card guarantee to hold your booking.
• Cheque or money order must be received insufficient time to clear prior to the charter.
REFUNDS: Payment will be refunded due to bad weather prior to departure or held if a mutual rescheduling is possible. If the trip has to be cancelled due to unforseen circumstances a refund will also be made. Cancellations must be made 14 days before charter. If you cancel within 14 days we will try and replace your booking and if we do we will refund your payment. Sea Sickness during the charter is not a reason for a refund, you must accept the risk. We cannot return to port because you are sick. It must be remembered that there are other full paying customers on board who do not want their day ruined. We will return to port only if there is a mutual agreement made with the other patrons on board or you agree to refund their payment in full. We recommend you visit your medical practitioner/pharmacist for advice on available medications. Many passengers have indicated ginger tablets are not effective for sea sickness.
Recreational Fishing Licences: The Government has introduced a Recreational Fishing Licence. The good news is Central Coast Reef & Game Fishing has purchased a block recreational fishing licence which will covers our clients when fishing on our vessel.
Directions 
GOOGLE MAP 
Terrigal is about an hour north of Sydney by car.
Head north from Sydney on the F3 freeway and take the Gosford exit. Follow the signs to Terrigal. 
Drive through the main street of Terrigal and up the hill at the south end past the Crowne Plazza hotel
You will see the boats moored in The Haven on your left at the south end of the beach
Take the unamed road on the left as you go up the hill. This is where The Esplanade becomes the Scenic Highway if you are using a GPS.
The road into The Haven has no name so we cannot provide a street address.
Follow the road along past the football field, swing to the left and you will come to the boat ramp.
There is plenty of parking but be sure not to park in the boat trailer spaces.
If you are at the fish cleaning table at the top of the ramp about 10 minutes before the charter you should see Paul come in from the Sea Runner to pick you up in the dingy. He will be waiting at the bottom right hand side of the ramp as you look down it. Please come down when you see him.
