Reef Fishing Charters 
Bait used: Australian pilchards, squid, prawns & fresh caught bait fish.
Reef Fishing Equipment: We use overhead fishing reels, Shimano TLD 15's and Samaki rods to suit. You are welcome to bring your own gear if you wish.
To book please call during business hours on 0427 66 55 44 
No unlisted/private numbers please.

Reef Fishing Charters
• Fishing all year round
• You catch them, we clean them, you keep them
• Equipped with Samaki rods & Shimano reels
• Bait & tackle supplied
• Complimentary morning tea
• Rod holders & seating for each angler
• Qualified/Certified Captain on board
• We cut the bait, clean & ice your fish
• We assist with fishing technique & rig lines
• Recreational fishing licence is not required

Game Fishing Charters
• Game Fishing Seasonal
• Maximum of 6 people per boat (you can have less if you wish)
• Rods & reels supplied
• Bait & tackle supplied
• Complimentary morning tea
• Tag & Release of marlin preferred
• Stand up tackle used and supplied
• Lures of assorted size, colours and brands supplied
• Qualified/Certified Captain on board
Game Fishing Equipment
• Reels - Shimano TLD50 2 speed reels
• Line - Stren 24kg
• Rods - Samaki 24kg fully rollered rods
• We use and recommend ZACATAK lures 
Game Fishing Grounds
• Browns Mountain (south) which rises 600ft from the ocean floor to within 1200ft of the surface
• Norah Head Canyons (north) is a series of rugged deep trenches in the ocean floor
• Vessel and crew are qualified to 30 N miles
• The Continental Shelf is 23 N miles offshore
Game Fishing Tournaments
NSWGFA Interclub at Port Stephens in February & March annually. Ask about charters for other competitions & locations.
