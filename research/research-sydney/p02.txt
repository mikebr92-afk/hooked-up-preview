About
Welcome to Mystery Bay Charters, the leading fishing charter in Rose Bay, Australia. With a dedicated team of experienced fishing enthusiasts, Mystery Bay Charters offers an unforgettable deep-sea fishing experience like no other.
Conveniently located at Rose Bay Wharf, Mystery Bay Charters provides easy access to the pristine waters of Sydney Harbour and beyond. Whether you are a seasoned angler or a first-time fisherman, our professional guides are committed to ensuring a memorable and successful fishing adventure.
Equipped with state-of-the-art fishing equipment and a modern fleet of boats, Mystery Bay Charters ensures that you have everything you need for a day of thrilling fishing. We pride ourselves on providing a comfortable and safe environment, allowing you to relax and focus on reeling in the catch of a lifetime.
Our experienced guides possess an extensive knowledge of the local waters and fishing techniques, guaranteeing that you will be in the best possible hands. They will share their expertise and tips, ensuring that even novice fishermen have the opportunity to land a trophy-sized fish.
At Mystery Bay Charters, we offer a range of fishing charter options to suit your preferences and needs. Take a half-day or full-day charter and explore the stunning coastline while targeting a variety of fish species, including snapper, kingfish, flathead, and more. We also offer specialised charters, such as game fishing, deep-sea fishing, and bottom fishing, for those seeking a specific fishing experience.
In addition to our fishing charters, Mystery Bay Charters also provides memorable harbour cruises. Cruise along the iconic Sydney Harbour, indulge in breathtaking views, and enjoy a relaxing day on the water. Whether you are celebrating a special occasion or simply want to unwind with friends and family, our harbour cruises offer an unparalleled experience.
We understand that fishing is not only a hobby but also a passion for many people. That is why Mystery Bay Charters offers the option of purchasing gift vouchers, allowing you to share the joy of fishing with your loved ones. Treat them to a unique and exciting adventure that they will remember for years to come.
We are committed to providing exceptional customer service and ensuring that your fishing experience with Mystery Bay Charters is nothing short of extraordinary. For more information and to book your fishing adventure, please contact us today. Let us take you on a journey to the bountiful waters of Sydney, where thrilling fishing opportunities await.
