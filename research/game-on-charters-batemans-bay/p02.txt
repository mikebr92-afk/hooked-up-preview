Game on Charters Batemans Bay
About Us Located in the iconic town of Batemans Bay, New South Wales, Game on Charters provides an unforgettable experience. At the heart of the operation is our 36 Riviera Platinum Game cruiser that offers a comfortable, air-conditioned cabin, leather lounges and our 42ft Westcoaster centre cab, with a large cabin, spacious full walk around deck for fishing, cruises and whale watching Batemans bay. 
Why Choose Us
With over 20 years of experience we are committed to safety, excellence, reliability, professionalism, and sustainability. We are well known for our expertise, local knowledge and great customer service.
Our Services
Reef Fishing
Experience the thrill of catching Snapper, King Fish, Trevally, Leather Jacket, Pigfish, and Morwong around the Batemans Bay Area, we have excellent reef structures and know the best spots!
Game Fishing
The south coast offers some of the best game fishing on the east coast. Depending on the time of year, our target species differ depending on water temperature, current and bait congregations.
Whale Watching Batemans Bay
Embark on an exciting whale watching adventure aboard our spacious vessels. Capture stunning photos of the magnificent Humpback whales dolphins and other marine life as they make their migratory journeys up and down the east coast.
Sunset Cruise
Experience the serenity of a sunset cruise on the Clyde River or the tollgates harbour taking in breathtaking views, and soaking up the tranquil surrounds.
Dolphin, sight seeing Cruise
Join us for an enchanting Tollgate Island Dolphin Cruise, where you can spot playful dolphins in their natural habitat and enjoy the breathtaking beauty of the tollgate islands and surrounds.
Clyde River Cruise
Embark on a relaxing Clyde River Cruise. Discover the tranquil beauty of the Clyde River, meandering through unspoiled nature and picturesque landscapes.
Snorkeling Adventure
Dive into an aquatic wonderland with our Snorkeling Adventure. Explore the vibrant marine life beneath the crystal-clear waters of Batemans Bay around the Tollgate islands and remote beaches.
Tollgate Islands and Surrounds
Discover the untouched paradise of the Tollgate Islands and surrounds with Game On Charters Batemans Bay, revealing the secrets of the South Coast.
Contact Us
To book your next adventure with us, call 0499499600 or email us at info@gameoncharters.net.au and we’ll be happy to arrange your trip. You can also find us at Pier B19, 27 Beach Road, Batemans Bay NSW.
