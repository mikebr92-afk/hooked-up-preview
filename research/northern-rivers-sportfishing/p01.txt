Northern Rivers Sportfishing
Located near the Gold Coast Australia, the Northern Rivers NSW area offers some exciting sportfishing. Join us as we target Australian native fish species including the Australian bass. Whether you fish lure or fly, beginner or advanced, allow us to customize a fishing experience for you. Our fleet of vessels includes boats, kayaks or canoes, or simply fish from the bank of one of the many rivers and lakes within easy reach. 
Fishing the Northern Rivers 
• Less than 1 hr from the Gold Coast Airport
• Season: (all year)
• Species: Australian Bass
• Boat or Kayak Fishing
• Fly or Lure Fishing
• Fish the Tweed River or Clarrie Hall Dam
