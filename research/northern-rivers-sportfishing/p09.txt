Fishing the Northern Rivers
Located in mountainous terrain, the Gold Coast & Tweed Valley are arguably some of the most picturesque of all fishing spots in Australia. The sheer beauty of these wetlands ensures that time spent fishing here is time spent regenerating the soul. Kayaking, canoeing and electric-powered boats are the best way to access the fishery. 
“Join me on a Northern Rivers Fishing Charter. I can take you to the most pristine local waterways where the bass are fat and healthy. I will accompany you throughout the day, sharing all the local secrets on catching Australian bass.” Simon Fitzpatrick – Bass Fishing Guide 
• Less than 1 hr from the Gold Coast Airport
• Season: (all year)
• Species: Australian Bass, Saratoga
• Boat or Kayak Fishing 
• Fly or Lure Fishing
• Fish the Tweed River , Clarrie Hall Dam or Hinze Dam 
• Suits Beginners to Advanced
HALF DAY 5 HOURS 
This trip is suited for 1-4 anglers. 
Pick up from your Northern Rivers accommodation (see Pick Up Points) . Morning tea is provided. 
One angler: $550 Two anglers: $325ea Three anglers: $275ea Four anglers: $225ea 
(+10% GST)​​ 
FULL DAY 8-10 HOURS 
This trip is suited for 1-4 anglers. 
Pick up from your Northern Rivers accommodation (see Pick Up Points) . Lunch & morning tea is provided. .
One angler: $720 Two anglers: $440 each Three anglers: $330 each Four anglers: $275ea 
(+10% GST)​​ 
All tackle boat/kayaks included (all damages/losses are at your expense). Catch & release only . 
Or contact Simon for more info on 0409 760 380 or email northernriverssportfishing@gmail.com

I know Simon best through his great habitat work with OzFish Unlimited , but he’s also a top notch fishing guide with wonderful knowledge of a range of waterways.” – Steve Starling, Fishing Writer & TV Presenter.
