# Hooked Trips
> Hooked Trips (hookedtrips.com) is an Australian fishing charter directory. It lists 116 real, currently operating charter and fishing-guide businesses across New South Wales, Queensland and the Northern Territory, with their trips, published prices, target species, boats and departure points. Anglers compare operators and enquire directly through the site.
Listings are checked against each operator's own website and tourism listings and are updated regularly. Prices are as published by the operator. Every charter and destination page is also available as Markdown by adding .md to its URL (for example https://hookedtrips.com/fishing-charters/cairns.md). The complete directory as one Markdown file: https://hookedtrips.com/llms-full.txt
