Town of 1770, Agnes Water & 1770 · From $320 per person
About Hooked On 1770
Hooked On 1770 is a small offshore fishing charter at the Town of 1770, ferrying anglers by tender from the 1770 Camping Grounds beach for half-day and full-day reef trips after red emperor, coral trout, nannygai and sweetlip, for up to seven anglers.
Hooked On 1770 is a small offshore fishing charter working out of the Town of 1770 on the Discovery Coast. Guests are picked up by tender from the beach at the 1770 Camping Grounds and ferried to Hooked on 2, a 9.7-metre Clayton Gallant monohull with a covered fishing deck, cabin seating, live bait tank and an enclosed toilet. Trips are capped at seven anglers. Most days are spent bottom fishing the deep-water reef country within about 15 nautical miles of 1770 for red emperor, coral trout, nannygai, sweetlip and mackerel, with trolling for pelagics available on request. Custom charters can also be arranged to the rocks off Bustard Head, the trawler wrecks off the coast or Pancake Creek. Heavy reef rods, reels, bait and tackle are supplied, so it suits visitors without their own gear as well as regular reef anglers. Bring your own food and drinks.

Cancellation policy
All seats booked are paid in full on the day. Cancel at least 24 hours before departure or the full booking is payable.
Weather policy
The boat does not go offshore if forecast winds are too strong; trips are rescheduled where possible. The booking calendar also flags days when the 6am departure or 4pm return tide is below the safe creek-entrance height.

Frequently asked questions about Hooked On 1770
Where does Hooked On 1770 depart from?
Hooked On 1770 departs from Town of 1770. Meeting point: Beach at 1770 Camping Grounds, Town of 1770 QLD 4677. Trips fish Bustard Head and Pancake Creek. See all fishing charters at Agnes Water and 1770 on Hooked Trips to compare nearby operators.
How much does a fishing charter with Hooked On 1770 cost?
Half Day Charter (6 hours) is $320 per person and Full Day Charter (10 hours) is $450 per person. Prices are as published by the operator — confirm the current price when you enquire.
What fish can you catch with Hooked On 1770?
Hooked On 1770 targets Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Nannygai, Sweetlip, Mackerel, Cobia (year-round), Tuna (year-round) and Trevally (year-round). What's biting depends on the season and conditions on the day.
How many people can go on a Hooked On 1770 trip?
Trips take up to 7 guests. Private charters book the whole boat for your group. Shared trips let individuals and small groups join other anglers.
Is Hooked On 1770 suitable for beginners?
Yes. Hooked On 1770 welcomes beginners, and also suits experienced anglers. Mention who's coming when you enquire so the crew can plan the right trip.
What's included on a Hooked On 1770 trip?
Hooked On 1770 includes rods and reels (6ft 15-24kg rods, Shimano TLD20 or Penn 750 reels, 50lb line), bait and tackle. Bring food and drinks, hat, sunglasses and own fishing gear if preferred.
How do I get on board with Hooked On 1770?
Guests are collected by tender from the beach at the 1770 Camping Grounds at 6am and taken out to the charter boat, Hooked on 2. Full days come back to the same beach at about 4pm and half days at about midday.
Can Hooked On 1770 run a custom or private trip?
Yes. Private charters for up to seven can be built around a particular area or style of fishing, such as Pancake Creek, Bustard Head, sport fishing or a scenic cruise along the 1770 and Agnes Water coast. Departure and return times are set to suit, weather and tide permitting.
How are Hooked On 1770 charter dates released?
While the business restructures, charter dates are being opened at short notice. The online calendar lets visitors register the dates they are in the area and be notified if a trip opens; this is a notification only, not a booking or a reserved seat.
Do I need a fishing licence in Queensland?
No. Queensland doesn't require a general recreational fishing licence for saltwater fishing, so you won't need one for a reef, offshore or estuary charter. A Stocked Impoundment Permit is only needed for certain freshwater dams. Size and bag limits still apply.
