Fishing at Agnes Water and 1770
The Town of 1770 and neighbouring Agnes Water sit where the Great Barrier Reef begins, about halfway between Bundaberg and Gladstone. Charter boats leave Round Hill Creek for deep-water reefs within about 15 nautical miles of the coast, the rocks off Bustard Head, trawler wrecks, and on longer days the southern reefs of the Capricorn and Bunker Group such as Lady Musgrave Island and Fitzroy Reef.
Reef trips target coral trout, red emperor, red throat emperor, nannygai and sweetlip, with cobia, mackerel and tuna around the wrecks. Closer in, Round Hill, Eurimbula, Middle and Pancake creeks hold mangrove jack, barramundi, queenfish, grunter and whiting. Departures are timed around the tide because the Round Hill Creek bar is shallow and always shifting.
Where the 1770 charters fish
Most day trips work reef country within about 15 nautical miles of the coast, so there's plenty of fishing time without a huge run. On calmer days and longer trips, boats head for the southern reefs of the Capricorn and Bunker Group, which lies roughly 45–75 km from the coastal departure ports.
• Inshore deep-water reefs — Within about 15 nm of 1770: nannygai, sweetlip, cod, hussar and parrotfish, with red emperor, coral trout, cobia and mackerel
• Bustard Head rocks — Rocks rising out of about 30 m of water north of 1770: mackerel, tuna, trevally, snapper in season and bottom fish
• Trawler wrecks — Cobia, tuna, trevally and cod year-round
• Lady Musgrave Island — The closest Bunker Group reef: coral trout and red throat emperor on the eastern side, red emperor in deeper water
• Fitzroy and Lamont reefs — Pinnacles east of Fitzroy in 60–100 m hold schooling red emperor, mainly May–October
• Boult and Hoskyn reefs — Deeper country to the east with red throat emperor, coral trout, cod and big pearl perch
• Round Hill, Eurimbula and Pancake creeks — Mangrove jack, barramundi, queenfish, grunter, flathead and whiting in the estuaries
When to go
You can fish 1770 all year, but local anglers rate the cooler months highest for the reef: bites improve as the water cools and the currents ease, and sharks are less of a problem around the outer reefs.
• Red emperor — Best from about May to October, when schools gather on the pinnacles off Fitzroy Reef
• Coral trout and red throat emperor — Year-round outside the coral reef fin fish closures
• Cobia, tuna and trevally — Year-round around the wrecks
• Snapper — Off Bustard Head in the cooler months; closed 15 July–15 August
• Barramundi — In the creeks; closed on the east coast 1 November–31 January
Departure points and the Round Hill Creek bar
Charters leave from the Town of 1770 on Captain Cook Drive — from the marina, the public boat ramp and pontoon on Round Hill Creek, or by tender from the beach at the 1770 camping grounds. The creek mouth is very shallow and the bar moves constantly, so departure and return times are set around the tide. Operators check the tide height before confirming a trip, and VMR Round Hill can advise on current bar conditions.
Good to know
Most of the offshore grounds are inside the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park, and the Capricorn and Bunker Group has many green (no-take) zones, especially on the western side. Charter skippers fish the open zones; if you're on your own boat, check the official GBRMPA zoning map rather than relying on the map at the ramp. Eurimbula Creek is a mud crab sanctuary.
Getting there: Agnes Water is about 5.5 hours' drive from Brisbane, around 1 hour 25 minutes from Bundaberg and 1 hour 30 minutes from Gladstone. The nearest airports are Bundaberg and Gladstone, and Greyhound coaches stop in Agnes Water daily. If you'd like more choice of boats, Bundaberg and Gladstone charters also fish the southern reefs.

Frequently asked questions
How much does a fishing charter at Agnes Water and 1770 cost?
Across the 1 charter listed at Agnes Water and 1770 on Hooked Trips, trips start from $320 per person. Prices depend on trip length, boat size and season, so check each operator's trips for exact pricing.
What fish can you catch at Agnes Water and 1770?
Charters at Agnes Water and 1770 most often target Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Nannygai and Sweetlip. Each listing shows the species that operator targets and when they're in season.
When is the best time to go fishing at Agnes Water and 1770?
1770 fishes year-round, but locals rate the cooler months (about May to October) best for reef fish, with red emperor schooling on the Fitzroy Reef pinnacles. Cobia, tuna and trevally are on the wrecks all year. Note the closures: coral reef fin fish 8–12 October and 6–10 November (2026), snapper and pearl perch 15 July–15 August, and barramundi 1 November–31 January.
Do I need a fishing licence in Queensland?
No. Queensland doesn't require a general recreational fishing licence for saltwater fishing, so you won't need one for a reef, offshore or estuary charter. A Stocked Impoundment Permit is only needed for certain freshwater dams. Size and bag limits still apply.
How far out is the reef from 1770?
Some of the reef fishing is close: charters work deep-water reefs within about 15 nautical miles of 1770. The Capricorn and Bunker Group, including Lady Musgrave Island and Fitzroy Reef, lies roughly 45–75 km from the coastal ports, so those trips are longer days.
Why do fishing charter times at 1770 depend on the tide?
Boats leave through Round Hill Creek, where the bar is shallow and keeps shifting. Operators time departures and returns for enough water over the bar and may reschedule if the tide is too low at the planned time.
Can you fish around Lady Musgrave Island?
In parts. Much of the Capricorn and Bunker Group is green zone, where fishing isn't allowed, but there are open zones around the reefs. Charter skippers know where you can fish; private boaties should check the GBRMPA zoning map first.
Is 1770 a good place to catch red emperor?
Yes — the area is known for it. Schools of red emperor gather on deep pinnacles east of Fitzroy Reef, mainly from about May to October, and they're also caught on the closer deep-water reefs.
Do I need to bring my own fishing gear?
No. Day charters from 1770 supply rods, reels, bait and tackle. Bring food and drinks if they're not included, sun protection and an esky for your catch.
