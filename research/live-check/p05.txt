Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters
Evans Head, Ballina & Byron Bay · From $200 per person
About Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters
Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters has fished from the Evans Head marina since 2005, specialising in pearl perch and snapper about 30 km offshore in 80 m of water, with six-hour individual and group trips, wide trips and midweek accommodation packages.
Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters has operated since 2005 from the marina at Evans Head, between Ballina and Yamba. Owner Mick McGillivray has worked his own commercial boats off Evans Head for more than 25 years and runs a 38 ft Randall customised for charter fishing, with skipper Simon named in recent reviews. Trips specialise in pearl perch and snapper in around 80 m of water about 30 km offshore, with jewfish, samsonfish, kingfish and teraglin also common. Individuals can book a six-hour trip, groups of up to ten can take the boat for six hours, and an eight-hour wide trip runs when the weather allows. Midweek packages combine a week of accommodation with three or five days of fishing, including optional afternoon jewfish sessions. Bait, tackle and the NSW fishing licence are supplied; bring food, drinks and an esky.

Frequently asked questions about Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters
Where does Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters depart from?
Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters departs from Evans Head, Ballina & Byron Bay. Meeting point: Evans Head marina, 12-32 Ocean Drive, Evans Head NSW 2473. See all fishing charters around Byron Bay and Ballina on Hooked Trips to compare nearby operators.
How much does a fishing charter with Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters cost?
Individual fishing charter (6 hours) is $200 per person, Midweek package - 7 nights, 3 days fishing (7 days) is $670 per person, Group fishing charter (6 hr) (6 hours) is $1,900 per group and Group fishing charter out wide (8 hr) (8 hours) is $2,300 per group. Prices are as published by the operator — confirm the current price when you enquire.
What fish can you catch with Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters?
Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters targets Pearl Perch, Snapper, Jewfish, Samsonfish, Kingfish and Teraglin. What's biting depends on the season and conditions on the day.
How many people can go on an Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters trip?
Trips take up to 10 guests. Private charters book the whole boat for your group. Shared trips let individuals and small groups join other anglers.
Is Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters suitable for beginners, families and kids?
Yes. Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters welcomes beginners, families and kids. Mention who's coming when you enquire so the crew can plan the right trip.
What's included on an Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters trip?
Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters includes bait and tackle and NSW fishing licence. Bring hat, sunglasses and sunscreen, hand towel, sensible shoes and spray jacket, food and drinks for 6 hours and empty esky (knives if filleting).
Does Evans Head Deep Sea Fishing Charters offer accommodation packages?
Yes. Midweek packages pair seven nights' accommodation with three days' fishing ($670 per person) or five days ($1,030), priced on a group of 10 though smaller groups are welcome. Each fishing day is six hours, with optional afternoon jewfish sessions or wider trips when weather allows.
What should I bring on an Evans Head fishing charter?
Bring a hand towel, sensible shoes, a spray jacket, food and drinks for six hours, and an empty esky for your fish. If you want to fillet your catch at the marina cleaning tables, bring knives and packaging too.
What happens if my Evans Head charter is cancelled for weather?
Deposits are refunded when a charter is cancelled because of the weather, or can be moved to another suitable date. Departure times are confirmed the afternoon before your trip.
Do I need a fishing licence on a charter in NSW?
No. Anglers fishing from a boat that holds a NSW charter fishing boat licence are covered by the operator's licence, so you don't need to pay the NSW recreational fishing fee for the trip. You'll need your own licence if you fish from the shore or a private boat.
