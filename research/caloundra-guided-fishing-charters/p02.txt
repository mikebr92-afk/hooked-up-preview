Charter Options 
Fishing Charter - 3.5 Hours 
This option is tailored specifically for individuals, small groups and families with children looking for a calm water fishing charter experience with a fun and interactive morning on the water within the sheltered estuary waters of the North Pumicestone Passage.
This is a small group charter with a maximum of 3 adults or 2 adults with 2 children (under 16years) to ensure a personalised experience and the price is total for the boat (not per person). 
Fishing charter itinerary: 
Departure: The Boat Ramp at 1 Lamerough Parade, Golden Beach 4551 (next to the Powerboat Club) 
The boat will be waiting docked at the end of the floating pontoon at the boat ramp. 
After a safety briefing, we will leave the jetty and enter the waters of the North Pumicestone passage enroute to our first fishing spot. 
Over the next few hours we will move between multiple spots throughout the Passage using a variety fishing techniques to target a variety of fish species, we will continue to move spots and use different techniques to ensure we do our best to keep the fish biting. 
Our focus is creating an enjoyable, memorable and sustainable fishing experience with our preferred fishing method being catch, photograph and release of the fish caught during the charter.
​We also create a bit of friendly competition with a trophy awarded for the 'Fish Of The Day' and a $20 gift voucher from our friends at Caloundra Fishing World for the most fish caught during the charter as we keep score on our running tally leaderboard. 
Despite our absolute best efforts fish are wild animals and can be unpredictable in their locations and feeding patterns due to factors out of our control, but we will always dedicate 110% to get fish in the boat and will guarantee everyone will have a great time while we're fishing. 
Maximum number: 3 adults OR 2 adults/2 children (under 16 years) 
Price: $450 total for the boat and guided charter 
Island Tour - 3.5 hours 
This option is for those who want to have an easy fishing experience combined with sight-seeing on the waters of the North Pumicestone Passage. 
This a small group charter with a maximum number of 4 adults or 2 adults with 2 children (under 16 years) to ensure a personalised experience and the price is total for the boat (not per person). 
Island tour itinerary: 
Departure: The Boat Ramp at 1 Lamerough Parade, Golden Beach 4551 (next to the Powerboat Club) 
After a safety briefing, we will leave the jetty and enter the waters of the North Pumicestone
Passage and travel 10km's South down the Pumicestone Passage where the Pumicestone Passage changes from the shallow northern sandflats to a vast mangrove lined estuarine systems. 
We then stop at a few fishing spots over the next hour and a half while we work our way back up the Passage. The fishing is fun and interactive and is focused on creating an enjoyable and memorable experience. 
After fishing we will make our way to the new Bribie bar which now separates Bribie Island by a 500m wide channel. The new channel is the remnants left by Ex-Tropical Cyclone Seth which has significantly changed this area of the island.
We will go ashore on the sandbank at the southern side of the bar and walk across to the ocean beach while keeping an eye out for bird life, marine life, take photos and leave footprints to be covered by the next tide. 
Leaving the Bribie bar area we will then make our way to the Pelican Waters Canals past the waterfront properties and up to the new Pelican Waters Marina precinct before returning back to the departure point with new memories from new experiences. 
Maximum number: 4 adults OR 2 adults/2 children (under 16 years)
​ Price: $450 total for the boat and guided tour
