FAQs 
Frequently Asked Questions 
What are we likely to catch? 
We target a variety of estuary based species using both bait and lures with the most common 
species being Whiting, Bream and Flathead 
Where will be fishing? 
We will be fishing in the Northern Pumicestone Passage between Caloundra/Golden Beach and down towards the Roys Road area and exact spots depend on the tides and conditions 
What if the forecast is windy/rainy for my booked date? 
As we will be fishing in calm waters the majority of the Northern Pumicestone Passage is protected from the wind, however if the wind forecast is over 20 knots (37km/hour) or the forecast is for heavy rain (20mm or above) a full refund will be give or the option to reschedule to the next available date 
Where do we leave from? 
The boat ramp at 1 Lamerough Parade Golden Beach next to the Power Boat Club 
Can we bring our own fishing gear? 
Yes you can bring your own fishing gear, just let us know prior to your charter you want to use your own gear and we can rig any rod you bring to suit the fishing we will do though we do provide all fishing rods, reels, terminal tackle and lures specifically chosen and set up for the Pumicestone Passage 
Can I bring my own food and drinks? 
Yes you can bring any food and drinks you like, however to keep the deck clear for fishing a carry bag or backpack is preferred with all ice boxes/eskies having to remain ashore 
Is there a toilet onboard? 
No we do not have a toilet onboard however there are a number of toilet facilities located along the Pumicestone Passage including at the boat ramp departure point we can stop in at if required 
Terms and Conditions 
​Bookings 
• For bookings just send a message on the bookings page and include preferred dates and group details and we will get back in contact with options based on the tides 
• Once a date and time are confirmed we will send a bookings confirm via email with the charter itinerary and invoice for the charter 
Payments 
• The charter invoice payment is by bank transfer (BSB/Acc No.) 
• Once the payment has been received you will receive a payment confirmation by email 
• ​U nfortunately we cannot accept payments on the morning of the charter 
Charters and Tours 
• Departure location is the boat ramp at 1 Lamerough Parade Golden Beach next to the 
Power Boat Club • We will confirm with you the day prior to your charter/tour 
• For the fishing charter all fishing equipment is supplied 
• Health conscious drink and snack options are provided however for special dietary requirements you are welcome to bring your own food and drinks in a carry bag 
• To maximize deck area for fishing unfortunately ice boxes/eskies have to remain ashore 
• Please bring Sunscreen, Hat, Sunglasses, Camera and a Weather jacket if required 
• Life jackets are available to wear while we are making way traveling to, from and between fishing spots or tour locations if required 
• Fishing methods can be catch, photograph and release (all fish caught are correctly and gently handled for a photo prior to release) however if you would like to keep legal size fish we will have ice and an esky bag on the boat and will transfer the fish and ice to your esky at the boat ramp at the end of the charter 
Weather 
• Postponement of a charter/tour due to bad weather is rare as Caloundra Guided Fishing Charters & Island tours operates in sheltered smooth waters 
• However we will postpone a charter if the forecast winds are over 20 knots or if heavy rain (20mm or above) is forecast 
• If the weather forecast is not looking suitable leading up to the charter/tour day we will contact you to discuss the forecast and available options from a safety perspective 
• In the rare circumstance the charter/tour is postponed due bad weather a full refund of the payment will be provided or we can look at options for another available date 
Cancellations 
• If for any other reason we cannot do your charter (not weather related) we will contact you to discuss and a full refund will be provided 
• We definitely understand unforeseen circumstances happen so for any reason you become unavailable for your charter just get in contact and we'll discuss option ​s
• If you arrive late for your charter the charter will still conclude at the pre-confirmed finishing time 
• If you do not show up for your charter/tour on your booked date unfortunately no refund or option to rebook another charter is available, though we will always attempt to contact you via phone call incase you are running late 
Safety and Medical 
• Safety is always the most important consideration during the charter 
• Please advise at the time of booking of any medical conditions that may put yourself or others at risk while fishing or boating 
Qualifications 
• Guide – Kyle holds a Coxswain Grade 1 Near Coastal Certificate II commercial vessel license to carry passengers for commercial purposes 
• Vessel – Our vessel Tiger Shark has Certificate of Operation Scheme NS for commercial use in all Australian waters for up to 4 passengers in 2D and 2E survey operations issued by the Australian Maritime Safety Authority (AMSA) ​​
