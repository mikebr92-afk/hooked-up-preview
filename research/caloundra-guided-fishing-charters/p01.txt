Welcome to Caloundra Guided Fishing Charters & Island tours 
We offer a personalised experience tailored towards first time fishers, families with children and individual groups interested in experiencing fishing and exploring in a relaxed, friendly and safe environment within the unique and picturesque waters of the Northern Pumicestone Passage

Fishing and Tour Guide 
Kyle Roberts 
Kyle is a third generation fisherman having grown up on the Capricornia Coast of Central Queensland. 
Now having lived and fished in Caloundra for the past 15years of which 10years were dedicated to offshore kayak fishing and include surviving a shark attack with a 4metre Tiger Shark biting and sinking his kayak while fishing a mile off the coast of Caloundra
(link to the 9WIN News video report below). 
The Caloundra Guided Fishing Charters boat 'Tiger Shark' was named after that day. 
Kyle is an easy going fishing and tour guide with a genuine passion for sharing time with people on the water.
