About Top Gear Fishing Charters
Skipper Drewe Jensen
Gold Coast Offshore
Top Gear Fishing Charters isn’t a tourist operation. It’s a serious offshore fishing business run by a skipper who eats, sleeps, and breathes fishing. Drewe Jensen has spent a decade on Gold Coast waters learning every reef, current, and feeding pattern that matters. He’s a father of three, a descendant of people who have always lived by this ocean, and someone who decided to build a business around the one thing he truly loves. When you step on board, you’re getting all of that.

MEET THE SKIPPER
Meet Your Captain
Drewe Jensen
I’m Drewe, skipper of Top Gear Fishing Charters. Fishing has always been a huge part of my life — and over the years it’s grown into a real addiction. I’ve spent countless hours on the water learning how fish behave, where they move, and what it takes to consistently put them on deck. Time on the ocean builds experience, and experience is what puts fish on the deck. Through my ancestors being native to this land and ocean, I’ve always felt a natural connection to the water. Running charters lets me live that every day — chasing fish and sharing the experience with people who want to be part of it. Whether you’re an experienced angler or picking up a rod for the first time, my goal is simple: give you a great day on the water and the best shot at landing something you won’t forget.
Built for Real Offshore Fishing 
The Berjun
Berjun is an award-winning, Australian-built Randell fishing vessel designed for serious offshore fishing on the Gold Coast. Built for strength, safety, and stability, she provides a solid and comfortable platform whether we’re fishing close reefs or heading wide offshore. To make sure everyone gets the best experience, we cap every trip at 12 guests maximum — so there’s room to move, room to fish, and room to actually enjoy yourself. No sardine tins. No standing three deep on a rod. Just a proper offshore vessel doing what it was built to do.
• Foam-filled hull for safety, buoyancy, and stability in offshore conditions
• Caterpillar V8 3208 diesel — serious power and long-range reliability
• Large, stable fishing platform with massive deck space
• Plenty of undercover seating for comfort between drops
• Private lockable onboard toilet
• Running water, hand soap, and hand sanitiser
• Bluetooth stereo — good tunes make the day
• Roll-down weather curtains to keep things dry when it matters
• Carpeted decks for comfort and grip
• Side-opening boarding door — wheelchair accessible
OUR MISSION
The Most Memorable Fishing Charter on the Gold Coast
The goal is clear: to be the most memorable fishing charter experience on the Gold Coast — not just for the fishing, but for the energy, the atmosphere, and the way the day is run. Drewe can’t promise the fish will always cooperate, but he can promise every trip is run properly, with premium gear, plenty of space, and hands-on attention so that everyone on board is genuinely part of the experience. Whether you’re brand new or a seasoned angler, the aim is simple — you walk off the boat feeling like it was worth it. Something you’ll tell people about.
• Passion — fishing isn’t a job here, it’s everything
• Authenticity — real offshore fishing, no theatrics, no shortcuts
• Inclusivity — everyone belongs on the water, regardless of ability or experience
• Excellence — premium gear, smart planning, and a skipper who genuinely cares
• Connection — to the ocean, the fish, and the people on board
Fishing Is for Everyone
Including You
With the skipper close by, guests are also welcome to step into the wheelhouse, see how the boat operates and even grab a photo at the helm.
If you have specific mobility requirements or need assistance, reach out beforehand and Drewe will do everything possible to make sure you can safely enjoy a day offshore.
Top Gear Fishing Charters takes accessibility seriously. We believe fishing should be something everyone can enjoy. Where most charter operators hesitate to take guests with mobility challenges, Drewe takes the opposite approach and does his best to accommodate people of all abilities.
The Berjun’s side-opening boarding door makes getting on and off the boat easier and allows wheelchair access — something most Gold Coast charters simply can’t offer. Over the years, guests of all abilities have come out fishing with Top Gear and had an incredible time.

What We Offer
From half-day reef sessions to full-day offshore game fishing — Top Gear has a charter for everyone.
Fishing Charter 
Mixed group and private offshore fishing charters departing from the Gold Coast — all skill levels welcome.
5 Hour Fishing Charter
A half-day offshore fishing charter giving you serious time on the water to target reef and pelagic species.
7 Hour Fishing Charter
Extended offshore charter for anglers who want more time, more ground covered, and more fish on deck.
Private Fishing Charter
Full boat hire for your group — corporate, family, mates, or any occasion. Your day, your way.
Professional Fishing Charters
Serious offshore fishing run by an experienced local skipper with premium Penn and Shimano gear supplied.
Reef Fishing Charter
Bottom fishing on productive Gold Coast reef systems targeting snapper, cobia, kingfish, and more.
Game Fishing Charter 
Targeting pelagic species including mahi mahi, mackerel, tuna, and marlin in offshore Gold Coast waters.
Deep Sea Fishing Charter
Head wide offshore into deep water targeting big pelagic species in some of the most productive fishing grounds on the Gold Coast.
