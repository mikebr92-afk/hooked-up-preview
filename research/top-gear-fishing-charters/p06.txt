Reef Fishing Charters Gold Coast — Snapper, Kingfish & More
The Gold Coast’s offshore reefs are some of the most productive fishing grounds in south-east Queensland — and Drewe Jensen knows them better than almost anyone. Top Gear reef fishing charters target snapper, kingfish, cobia, teraglin, tailor, flathead, and whatever else is running. Every trip is planned around tides and structure, so you’re not just fishing over open water and hoping. You’re on the fish. Real reef fishing, done properly.

WHAT WE TARGET
Gold Coast Reef Species — What's on the Cards
The Gold Coast reefs deliver a wide variety of quality eating fish throughout the year. Drewe targets different species and structures depending on conditions, seasons, and where the fish are holding — ensuring every reef charter has the best possible chance of a solid result.
Snapper
Gold Coast snapper are one of the most sought-after reef species on the east coast. Drewe targets them year-round, with the bigger fish running harder during the cooler months. Firm, white flesh with a delicate flavour — hard to beat on a barbie or in the pan. These are the fish guests talk about when they get home.
Kingfish & Cobia
Hard fighting, excellent eating, and a genuine test of your tackle. Yellowtail kingfish and cobia are regulars on Gold Coast reef charters and among the most rewarding catches on the deck. When one goes on, everyone knows about it.
Tailor, Flathead & Teraglin
Consistently reliable reef and inshore species that feature on most Gold Coast charters. Teraglin (jewfish) are a prized catch for their eating quality. Tailor are fast, aggressive, and fun. Flathead are one of the best eating fish in Australian waters — and the Gold Coast has them in numbers.
Mackerel & Pelagic Species
Spanish mackerel, spotty mackerel, and other pelagic species regularly appear over reef structure, especially during warmer months. Fast, aggressive, and tremendous on light gear. When the mackerel are on, it’s non-stop action.
Ikejime Fish Handling — Your Catch Deserves Better
Top Gear Fishing Charters dispatches fish using the ancient Japanese ikejime method — a technique Drewe learned during time spent fishing in Japan. The method instantly kills and bleeds the fish, reducing stress and lactic acid buildup in the flesh. The result is a fish that tastes noticeably better and keeps significantly longer than conventionally dispatched fish. Because we keep numbers small and handle each fish individually, your catch gets a level of care that simply isn’t possible on bulk operations.
• Ikejime dispatch where possible — instant, clean, and humane
• Fish bled immediately to preserve flesh quality
• Each fish handled individually — not thrown into a bulk pile
• All fish cleaned and bagged for you after the trip
• Straight on ice to maintain prime eating quality
Reef Fishing Charters
Frequently Asked Questions
What reef species can I expect to catch on a Gold Coast reef charter? 
The Gold Coast reefs produce snapper, kingfish, cobia, tailor, flathead, teraglin, mackerel, and more. What’s running depends on season, tides, and conditions — Drewe plans every trip to target the best-performing species on the day.
Do I need to bring my own fishing gear? 
No. All rods, reels, bait, and tackle are supplied on every charter. You get your own rod position with premium Penn or Shimano gear. Nothing to buy, hire, or lug to the boat.
What happens to the fish I catch? 
All your fish are cleaned, bagged, and iced on board before you leave. Top Gear uses ikejime dispatch and immediate bleeding to give you the best possible eating quality when you get home.
