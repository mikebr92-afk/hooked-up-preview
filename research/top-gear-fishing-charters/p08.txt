Our Trips
We don’t just take you for a boat ride; we put you on the fish. From the glassy waters of the sunrise shift to the deep-sea battles offshore, these are the moments that define a day with Top Gear. Explore our recent hauls of Snapper, Pearl Perch, and Marlin from the Gold Coast’s most productive reefs.
