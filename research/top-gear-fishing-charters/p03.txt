Top Gear Fishing Charters
Mixed Group Offshore Fishing Charters Gold Coast
Want to get out on the water without booking the whole boat? Top Gear’s mixed group charters run twice daily — 5:30am and 11:00am — and they’re the best-value way to experience real offshore fishing on the Gold Coast. Max 12 guests per departure means you’ve got space, gear, and a skipper who’s actually paying attention to your line. All rods, reels, bait, and tackle supplied. Fish cleaned and iced before you leave. Just show up and fish.
Prices & Bookings 
WHAT'S INCLUDED
Everything You Need Is Already on Board
Top Gear mixed group charters run fully equipped. There’s nothing to bring and nothing to hire. Every angler gets their own rod position, premium Penn or Shimano gear, and expert guidance from a skipper who fishes these grounds every single day.
Premium Penn & Shimano Gear — Supplied
Every rod position comes loaded with quality Penn and Shimano rods and reels — the same gear serious offshore anglers use. No hiring rods, no fighting for tackle. Your gear is ready when you step on board.
Your Own Rod Position
With a maximum of 12 guests on a large deck, every angler gets their own space and their own rod position. No bumping elbows, no tangled lines, no waiting for someone else to reel in before you can fish.
Fish Cleaned, Bagged & Iced
Every fish you catch gets cleaned, bagged, and placed straight on ice before the trip is over. You walk off with a full bag ready for the pan. The ikejime dispatch method means your fish is handled with care from the moment it comes over the rail — better eating quality and longer shelf life.
Experienced Local Skipper
Drewe Jensen knows the Gold Coast’s offshore grounds better than almost anyone. Every mixed group departure is planned around real conditions — tides, structure, and where the fish are holding that day. You’re not just going for a ride.
Great Fishing. Great Value. No Boat Full Required.
Mixed group charters are perfect for solo anglers, couples, or small groups who want a quality offshore experience without committing to a full private charter. You’ll fish alongside other like-minded people in a relaxed, energetic atmosphere with a skipper who keeps things moving and keeps things fun.
• Two departures daily — 5:30am and 11:00am — plan your day
• All gear, bait, and tackle supplied — nothing to hire or bring
• Max 12 guests — more room, more attention, better fishing
• Perfect for solo bookings, pairs, and small groups
• Suitable for complete beginners through to experienced anglers
• Fish cleaned and bagged — take your catch home ready to eat

Frequently Asked Questions
What time do mixed group charters depart? 
We run two scheduled mixed group departures daily — typically at 5:30am and 11:00am. Exact times may vary slightly based on conditions. We’ll confirm your departure time when you book.
Do I need any fishing experience? 
None at all. Drewe and the crew are there to help everyone on board, whether it’s your first time or your hundredth. We’ll show you what to do, when to do it, and how to land the fish when it’s on.
What should I bring on a mixed group charter? 
Just yourself, comfortable clothes, sunscreen, and a hat. Leave the large esky, glass bottles, and heavy liquor at home — we’ve got ice on board and the trip runs better without the hazards. Oh, and no bananas. Ever.
