Our Services
Gold Coast Fishing Charters
Reef, Game & Deep Sea
Top Gear Fishing Charters
Top Gear Fishing Charters runs a full range of offshore fishing experiences on the Gold Coast — from mixed group reef trips at dawn to full-day private charters offshore chasing pelagics. Every trip is run by skipper Drewe Jensen aboard the Berjun, an award-winning Randell vessel capped at 12 guests. Premium gear supplied. Fish cleaned and bagged. Real offshore fishing, the way it should be.
Book a Charter Prices & Bookings 
Every Type of Offshore Fishing — One Charter Company
Whether you want a half-day reef session before work, a full-day private run offshore, or a family trip where the kids experience their first fish — Top Gear has a charter built for it. Browse below and find the right trip.
Mixed Group Charters
Two scheduled departures daily. Share the boat with fellow anglers. Great for solo bookings or small groups. All gear and bait supplied.
Private Fishing Charters
Full boat hire for your group. Half day, three-quarter, or full day. Corporate, family, bucks parties, and more.
Reef Fishing Charters
Bottom fishing on productive Gold Coast reefs targeting snapper, kingfish, cobia, tailor, and more.
Game & Deep Sea Fishing Charters
Head wide offshore and target mahi mahi, mackerel, tuna, and marlin in serious blue water.
Family & Beginner-Friendly Trips
Hands-on guidance for first-timers, family trips with kids, and fully accessible charters including wheelchair boarding.
HOW WE FISH
Every Trip Is Planned. Nothing Is Left to Chance.
Top Gear Fishing Charters doesn’t just show up and hope for the best. Every charter is planned around tides, structure, current, and where the fish are actually moving. Drewe fishes these waters constantly — that real-time knowledge is what separates a proper charter from a lucky dip. You show up, cast a line, and trust the skipper. That’s the deal.

What We Offer
From half-day reef sessions to full-day offshore game fishing — Top Gear has a charter for everyone.
Fishing Charter 
Mixed group and private offshore fishing charters departing from the Gold Coast — all skill levels welcome.
5 Hour Fishing Charter
A half-day offshore fishing charter giving you serious time on the water to target reef and pelagic species.
7 Hour Fishing Charter
Extended offshore charter for anglers who want more time, more ground covered, and more fish on deck.
Private Fishing Charter
Full boat hire for your group — corporate, family, mates, or any occasion. Your day, your way.
Professional Fishing Charters
Serious offshore fishing run by an experienced local skipper with premium Penn and Shimano gear supplied.
Reef Fishing Charter
Bottom fishing on productive Gold Coast reef systems targeting snapper, cobia, kingfish, and more.
Game Fishing Charter

Deep Sea Fishing Charter
