Charter Tours Customised for Your Needs 
At Whitsunday Fishing Charters, our private charters are designed to give you an exclusive and tailored experience on the water. As one of the only charters in the Whitsundays with Yellow Zone certification, we can take you to exclusive fishing areas other operators can't access. 
Whether you're planning a fishing adventure, a sightseeing trip, or celebrating a special occasion, we'll customise the day to suit you. Our friendly and experienced 
crew ensures your time with us is seamless, enjoyable, and unforgettable. 
With access to our custom-built boat, the Reef Adventurer, your group can enjoy comfort and performance throughout your journey. Our private charters are perfect for families, friends, or corporate groups looking for a personalised experience. From reeling in prized fish to exploring the pristine waters of the Whitsundays, we'll help you create memories to treasure. 
Make your next event or outing truly special. Call us at 
0457 822 553 to book your private charter and start planning your perfect day on the water. We also offer

• Family Gatherings : Enjoy quality time together with a customised fishing or sightseeing adventure. 
• Birthdays & Special Occasions:

• Corporate & Work Events:
