About Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters
Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters runs exclusive game and reef fishing days from Exmouth on a 44ft O'Brien and a 25ft centre console, plus Ningaloo and Montebello live-aboards, chasing marlin, sailfish, red emperor and coral trout.
Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters is an Exmouth game and reef fishing business run by owner-skipper Brendon Hawker, a New Zealand-raised billfish specialist whose Exmouth tournament results include Gamex 2021. It runs two boats: Innkeeper, a 44ft O'Brien gamefisher surveyed for eight passengers that sleeps seven on live-aboards, and Keeperinn, a fast 25ft Sportsman centre console for day trips. Exclusive day charters chase blue, black and striped marlin, sailfish, tuna, mahi mahi and wahoo off the continental shelf, or bottom fish 15 to 120 metres for red emperor, coral trout and other reef species, with fillets bagged at the end of the day. A single-seat reef option fills a shared boat, an October to November Exmouth Gulf sailfish day sight-casts to feeding packs, and two- to six-day live-aboards fish Ningaloo or the Montebello Islands. First-timers are coached through stand-up and chair fights.

Frequently asked questions about Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters
Where does Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters depart from?
Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters departs from Exmouth. Trips fish Ningaloo Reef, Exmouth Gulf, Continental shelf off Exmouth and Montebello Islands. See all fishing charters in Exmouth and Ningaloo on Hooked Trips to compare nearby operators.
How much does a fishing charter with Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters cost?
Exmouth Gulf sailfish day (October-November) (8.5 hours) is $2,700 per group, Ningaloo live-aboard, 2 days / 1 night (1 day) is $8,800 per group and Montebello Islands live-aboard, 4 days / 3 nights (3 days) is $17,600 per group. Prices are as published by the operator — confirm the current price when you enquire.
What fish can you catch with Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters?
Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters targets Blue Marlin, Black Marlin, Striped Marlin, Sailfish (exmouth Gulf packs October to November), Yellowfin Tuna, Mahi Mahi, Wahoo and Red Emperor (year-round). What's biting depends on the season and conditions on the day.
How many people can go on an Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters trip?
Trips take up to 8 guests. Private charters book the whole boat for your group. Shared trips let individuals and small groups join other anglers.
Is Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters suitable for beginners and families?
Yes. Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters welcomes beginners and families, and also suits experienced anglers. Mention who's coming when you enquire so the crew can plan the right trip.
What's included on an Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters trip?
Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters includes western Australian fishing licence, all tackle, water and soft drinks and catch filleted (reef trips). Bring lunch (day trips) and alcohol if wanted (BYO).
When is the Exmouth Gulf sailfish trip run?
Only in October and November, when large numbers of sailfish move into Exmouth Gulf to feed on baitfish. It is a full-day exclusive charter on the fast centre console, listed at $2,700 per day.
How much is an Innkeeper live-aboard?
Ningaloo live-aboards are quoted for a group of four: $8,800 for 2 days/1 night up to $21,000 for 5 days/4 nights, plus $500 per extra person per day. Montebello trips run $17,600 to $26,400 for 4 to 6 days.
Do I need a fishing licence on a charter in Western Australia?
No. WA's fisheries department (DPIRD) says paying guests on licensed charter fishing tours don't need a Recreational Fishing from Boat Licence. Recreational bag and size limits still apply, and West Coast demersal scalefish (such as dhufish and snapper) have special rules and closures. Fishing from your own powered boat does need a licence.
