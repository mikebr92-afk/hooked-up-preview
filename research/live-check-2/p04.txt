Fishing in Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay
Port Phillip is Melbourne's home water: a big, mostly sheltered bay ringed by boat ramps from St Kilda and Black Rock round to Carrum, Mornington, Sorrento and Queenscliff, with Geelong's Corio Bay in the west. Charter boats fish the bay for snapper, King George whiting, flathead and calamari, and the bigger boats head out through the Heads into Bass Strait for gummy shark, kingfish and tuna when the weather allows.
Most Melbourne charters are shared trips of four to five hours, so you can book a single seat or the whole boat. Several operators trailer their boats and choose the ramp a few days out, depending on where the fish are, so confirm the departure point after you book.
Where the charters fish
Bay trips work the reefs, grass beds and sand flats around the edge of Port Phillip, moving with the season and the weather. Queenscliff boats sit right at the Heads, so they can fish the bay or, in good conditions, Bass Strait.
• Carrum and the south-east bayside — Main base for several Melbourne operators; snapper, whiting and calamari
• St Kilda and Black Rock — City-side departures for snapper and pinky snapper
• Mornington, Rye and Sorrento — Southern bay ramps for snapper, whiting, squid and gummy shark
• Queenscliff, Swan Bay and the Heads — Whiting, pinkies, calamari, salmon and reef fish; offshore when the weather allows
• Corio Bay, Geelong — Sheltered family trips for flathead, pinkie snapper and whiting
• Bass Strait outside the Heads — Gummy, mako and thresher sharks, snapper, kingfish and tuna
When to go
• Snapper — Peak October to December; some operators fish them from September to April
• King George whiting — Most of the year; Queenscliff bay charters run September to mid-May
• Calamari — Year-round
• Gummy shark — A dedicated Queenscliff gummy trip runs from late April to mid-July
• Kingfish and tuna — About mid-January to early April outside the Heads
Departure points
• Patterson River ramps, Carrum — Pro Red, Unreel and Sharkmen Fishing Charters
• St Kilda, Mornington and Black Rock — Alternative snapper ramps used by Melbourne operators
• Martha Cove (Safety Beach) and Blairgowrie Pier — Southern Mornington Peninsula departures used by Sharkmen
• Queenscliff Harbour — Gone Fishing Charters, with Portsea Pier pick-ups for groups
• Wangim Walk, Geelong waterfront — Geelong Fishing Charters, at the end of Yarra Street
Good to know
Victorian charter clients need their own Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence unless they are exempt; operators say it is your job to have it before the trip.
On boats crossing Port Phillip Heads, everyone must wear a life jacket through the Heads. Bag and size limits apply to snapper, whiting and calamari, so check the current VFA rules.

Frequently asked questions
How much does a fishing charter in Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay cost?
Across the 5 charters listed in Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay on Hooked Trips, trips start from $80 per person. Prices depend on trip length, boat size and season, so check each operator's trips for exact pricing.
What fish can you catch in Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay?
Charters in Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay most often target Snapper, King George Whiting, Gummy Shark, Squid, Southern Bluefin Tuna and Whiting. Each listing shows the species that operator targets and when they're in season.
When is the best time to go fishing in Melbourne and Port Phillip Bay?
Spring is the drawcard. Melbourne operators run their main snapper season from about October to December, with some extending it into January or April, and run three sessions a day at the peak. Whiting and calamari fish well for most of the year, gummy sharks are a cooler-season target from Queenscliff, and kingfish and tuna turn up outside the Heads from about January to early April.
Do I need a fishing licence on a charter in Victoria?
Usually, yes. Unlike NSW, Victoria has no charter-boat exemption: unless you're personally exempt (for example under 18, aged 70 or over, or holding certain concession cards), you need your own Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence, which you can buy online in minutes. Murray cod also have a closed season from 1 September to 30 November in most waters.
Can I book a single seat on a Melbourne snapper charter?
Yes. Most Port Phillip operators sell individual places on shared trips as well as whole-boat charters, and some will fill the boat with other anglers. Snapper trips can need a minimum number of people to go ahead, so book early in peak season.
Which ramp will my Port Phillip charter leave from?
It often isn't fixed until a few days before. Operators such as Pro Red, Unreel and Cassar move between Carrum, St Kilda, Mornington, Hastings and other ramps to follow the fish, and confirm the meeting point after booking.
Are Port Phillip charters suitable for children?
Many are. Geelong Fishing Charters runs family trips where under-10s fish free, and bay trips generally welcome children from about five or six, but offshore Bass Strait trips usually have a minimum age of 12.
