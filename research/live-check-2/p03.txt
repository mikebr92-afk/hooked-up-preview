Fishing in Exmouth and Ningaloo
Exmouth, on the tip of the North West Cape, is one of the few places in Australia where the continental shelf runs within a short run of the boat ramp. Charter boats leave Exmouth Marina on the Gulf side or Tantabiddi on the Ningaloo side and can be trolling for blue, black and striped marlin and sailfish soon after clearing the reef.
Inside Ningaloo Reef and around the reef edges, charters chase Spanish mackerel, giant trevally, coral trout and spangled emperor, while deeper drops hold red emperor, ruby snapper and goldband snapper. Exmouth Gulf adds sheltered water and, each spring, sailfish packs feeding on bait. Most operators run private day charters for up to six to eight anglers, with some live-aboards to remote islands including the Montebellos.
Where the charters fish
• Continental shelf off Tantabiddi — Blue, black and striped marlin, sailfish, yellowfin tuna, wahoo and mahi mahi, often within minutes of the reef
• Ningaloo Reef — Reef edges and bommies for coral trout, spangled emperor, Spanish mackerel and giant trevally
• Exmouth Gulf — Sheltered water for queenfish, trevally, longtail tuna and the October-November sailfish run
• Islands north of Exmouth — Red emperor, rankin cod and mackerel around the islands at the top of the Gulf; live-aboards run on to the Montebellos
Departure points
Gameboats work from Exmouth Marina (Madaffari Drive) or launch at Tantabiddi boat ramp on the Ningaloo side, which is closest to the shelf. Several operators collect guests from town accommodation.
• Exmouth Marina — Gulf side, close to town
• Tantabiddi boat ramp — Ningaloo side, closest to the shelf
Good to know
Check the Ningaloo Marine Park zoning before fishing; licensed charters work within it. Paying guests on a licensed fishing tour do not need their own Recreational Fishing from Boat Licence. Many billfish are tagged and released; reef fish are usually filleted for you. Check the current DPIRD Gascoyne bag and size limits, which changed for demersal species from 1 June 2026.

Frequently asked questions
How much does a fishing charter in Exmouth and Ningaloo cost?
Across the 3 charters listed in Exmouth and Ningaloo on Hooked Trips, shared trips start from $360 per person and private charters from $2,700 per group. Prices depend on trip length, boat size and season, so check each operator's trips for exact pricing.
What fish can you catch in Exmouth and Ningaloo?
Charters in Exmouth and Ningaloo most often target Sailfish, Blue Marlin, Black Marlin, Red Emperor, Mahi Mahi and Coral Trout. Each listing shows the species that operator targets and when they're in season.
When is the best time to go fishing in Exmouth and Ningaloo?
Fishing runs year round. Local operators say billfish can be caught in every month, with different species peaking at different times, and Exmouth Gulf sailfish sight-casting is sold only in October and November. Reef fishing for red emperor and coral trout is available all year, weather permitting.
Do I need a fishing licence on a charter in Western Australia?
No. WA's fisheries department (DPIRD) says paying guests on licensed charter fishing tours don't need a Recreational Fishing from Boat Licence. Recreational bag and size limits still apply, and West Coast demersal scalefish (such as dhufish and snapper) have special rules and closures. Fishing from your own powered boat does need a licence.
Can you catch marlin from a day charter in Exmouth?
Yes. Because the continental shelf is unusually close to the coast, blue, black and striped marlin and sailfish are targeted on full-day charters from Tantabiddi and Exmouth Marina. Private gameboat days typically run around eight to nine hours.
When is sailfish season in Exmouth Gulf?
Operators sell dedicated Exmouth Gulf sailfish days in October and November, when large numbers of sailfish follow bait schools into the gulf.
