About Us
25 Years Experience 
We have many years of experience in charter fishing in Westernport Bay in Melbourne and Bass Strait, Westernport Fishing Charters take you on a fishing experience like no other! You can enjoy a great days fishing onboard a well maintained 50ft (15 Meter) Vessel flying fish 2.
All Year Round 
There's no better way to explore Westernport Bay and Bass Strait and its surrounds than in the comfort and privacy of a Westernport Fishing Charter adventure 7 Days a week - All year round.
Vessel 
The Westernport Fishing Charters vessel is modern and is complete with state of the art equipment including GPS, colour fish finder, radar, fast cruising speed & excellent stability for group or individual bookings.
We Cater For 
Westernport Fishing Charters cater for private charters, individuals, small groups, large groups (max 20), Schools, social clubs & groups and corporate charters also bucks parties.
Prices starting from as low as $140 per person. Westernport & Bass Strait Fishing Charters can be arranged to meet the needs of groups or individuals. Bookings Essential.
Fishing Trips 
Depart from Westernport Marina for a 4,5,6 or 8 hour charter, fishing for Snapper, Gummy Shark & Whiting and many more!
