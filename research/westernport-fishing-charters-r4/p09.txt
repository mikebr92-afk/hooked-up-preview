Scattering Ashes Ceremony
We offer our services to enable you to scatter ashes in a dignified and simple way. Family and friends can gather to celebrate or remember the life of a loved one on their own private charter to accommodate a respectful ceremony. The scattering of ashes at sea gives you a comforting way to remember and feel connected to the cycle of life.
Scattering Ashes
Scattering ashes at sea can be a touching, symbolic way of saying farewell in a calm and tranquil environment. We can help you arrange a trip on the bay to that special place where you can scatter the ashes of a loved one, and celebrate their life in a beautiful setting. you are ensured of a memorable way to say goodbye to a loved one. we will work with what times suits you best. For this trip there are no minimum numbers required and we can cater to up to 16 family and friends.This type of trip is hired out on an hourly basis and costs are $440 per hour with a minimum of 2 hours hire.
If we can assist you with any inquiries regarding this special type of cruise, please don’t hesitate to contact us 03 97695544
