About US
Highly experienced guides, passionate about Tasmania’s wilderness, leading unforgettable trout fishing and walking adventures year‑round.
Our Fishing Experience
Experience fly fishing like never before with Miena Guiding Tasmania, where anglers from around the world come to the Highlands’ thriving wild trout fishery.
Spend your days exploring endless alpine tarns and dramatic wilderness, then unwind in style—by a roaring pub fire, in a cozy fishing cabin, or under the stars on a remote campsite. Whether it’s adventure, relaxation, or both, Miena Guiding Tasmania turns every fishing trip into an unforgettable Tasmanian experience.

Who we are
Spend your days exploring endless alpine tarns and dramatic wilderness, then unwind in style—by a roaring pub fire, in a cozy fishing cabin, or under the stars on a remote campsite. Whether it’s adventure, relaxation, or both, Miena Guiding Tasmania turns every fishing trip into an unforgettable Tasmanian experience.
Whether you’re seeking a day‑trip or multi‑day expedition — with private or shared options — we provide local expertise, tailored itineraries and deep knowledge of the region.
Proudly Tasmanian and community‑minded, we offer authentic experiences that show you the wild heart of the Highlands — and make a lasting connection to this special land.
JASON GARRETT “JUNIOR” – HEAD GUIDE
Jason has called Tasmania’s Central Highlands home since age ten. Born in Papua New Guinea, he first learned to fly fish in the 1970s before moving to Derwent Bridge, where he spent childhood holidays fishing, swimming and climbing Mt Rufus. By fourteen, he was guiding clients at London Lakes on his father’s renowned fly fishing estate. After years travelling and working around Australia, Jason returned full‑time to the Highlands in 2019, establishing Miena Village and Miena Guiding Tasmania. As the third generation of his family here, he shares his deep local knowledge and love of the wilderness with every guest.
OUR MISSION
We are a dedicated guiding tour company focused on delivering exceptional, personalised experiences across Tasmania. While our core offering is expert guiding, we can also arrange accommodation on request and frequently create customised packages tailored to each guest’s needs — we make planning your adventure seamless.
Our walking and fishing experiences span the entire state, ensuring that wherever you’re dreaming of exploring, we can match you with the right adventure. Just give us a shout and we’ll take care of the rest.
We are proudly Tasmanian. We are proudly local. And we are proudly Highlanders.
