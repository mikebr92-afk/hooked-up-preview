Western Lakes Fly Fishing Safari
What we love about the Western Lakes Fly Fishing Safari.
This is for the purists at heart, where we pit you against nature over an amazing three days.
The sight fishing is unbelievable, but be warned, these fish are wily. Nature will sooth the soul, it is an enchanting and enriching experience for the well travelled fly fisher.
“Jason finally convinced me to come out to the back country and catch one of his pets. It was a monster and I am so glad I was convinced to come on this incredible fly fishing odyssey. The experience will stay with me forever. All I had to arrange were the arrival and departure dates at the airport. Dry fly fishing mecca, the scenery and the elements. Just to hear Jason say …here it comes put your fly there…wait… STRIKE! I will live that moment for a long time. Thank you my friend.” 
Ian

About the Safari Tour
Welcome to the land of one thousand lakes, an immersive exclusive wilderness adventure designed exclusively for you and your party.
Over three days and up to four nights, you’ll traverse near and into the breathtaking Walls of Jerusalem National Park, guided by two experienced wilderness guides.
This once-in-a-lifetime journey will take you through remote Tasmanian bush that few ever experience. You’ll walk through ancient pine forests, note glorious mountains, and breathe in the world’s freshest air. Along the way, encounter Tasmania’s unique wildlife, swim in its crystal clear tarns and savour rustic camp side meals.
With everything taken care of—from carrying gear to setting up camp—your focus will be entirely on soaking in the beauty and tranquillity of this untouched wilderness and the possibility of catching a wild brown trout.
A moderate level of fitness is recommended for this unforgettable experience.

Itinerary
We recommend you arrive the night before and we can arrange transfers if required.
Day 1 – Starting at 8:30am with a safety briefing, then transfer to the trail head. Setting off from the picturesque Lake Ada, the aim is to arrive at our fishing base camp by the end of the day. It is hoped that we have caught a couple of wild browns on the walk out, had lunch by a tarn full of fishing rising to black spinners and find ourselves well away from the Nineteen lagoons. Hopefully by the time we reach camp, the cooking smells of a hearty meal await. Relax over a lazy red before retiring for the evening.
Day 2 – After an enjoyable breakfast, its time to head out for a day of sight fishing on one of the one thousand tarns that abounds the area. We will be away from camp for most of the day but we will not venture too far, and depending on how the fishing is, may even return to camp for lunch. After an enjoyable day’s fly fishing, we will return to camp for the last evening under the southern stars.
Day 3 – Starts with packing up camp, and venturing back to the trail head whilst fishing a few locations yet to be explored. The plan is to be off the Plateau and back at Miena by about 4:30pm and then hit the hot showers and be treated to a cold ale near a roaring log fire. It is recommended that guest spend the final evening in local accommodation before heading off the next day.

Grade
This three days, 2 nights fly fishing safari is moderate to demanding but not difficult.
For those that love the outdoors this is the premier remote fly fishing attraction you will experience in Tasmania.
The guides are your pack horses, allowing you to tread lightly where very few people have travelled.
This is a curated experience but if the weather is inclement we may need to be more mobile, and whilst we can cater for 8 to 88 year olds, on this particular experience it is more likely you are aged from 16 to 76.

Tour Details
WHAT TO BRING 
– Good walking boots and woollen socks with preference for wet wading.
– Campsite shoes are a must.
– Long and short pants and long and short sleeve shirt.
– Warm jacket (fleece or puffer), warm sweater, thermal pants and top.
– Water proof rain jacket, sunhat and sunglasses, warm hat and warm gloves.
– Ability to carry up to 12Kg including personal items and fishing gear.
WE WILL SUPPLY 
– Four Season tents, overnight walking packs sleeping bags, liners and mats.
– Water proof bags for carrying personal items.
– Water bottles, eating utensils, headlamp, gaiters.
– Toilet paper!
