Hook! Fight! Repeat!
For the best on‑water experience, our charters are designed for small groups of up to 4 guests — with all pricing covering the entire group. 
All charters depart from the Foreshore Rd Boatramp, Port Botany, Sydney.
Secure booking & payment through FishingBooker — the world's largest fishing charter platform, trusted for 1M+ trips worldwide. 
Full Day – Sport Fishing Charter
Open water. The real fight. This is the trip for kingfish that pull like a truck, mahi mahi lighting up the surface, and snapper putting up more of a scrap than you'd expect — with Vinnie calling the target on the day based on what's actually running, so you're chasing what's biting, not a fixed script. Departs 5:00am. You'll come back sunburnt, arm-sore, and already planning the next one.

Half Day (AM) – Reef Fishing
Dawn light, and the reef wide open. Starting at 6:00am — right as snapper, kingfish, and squid switch on for the morning — you're working structure for a proper mixed bag: snapper, kingfish, john dory, flathead, jewfish, and squid.
Short trip, serious action.

Half Day (AM) - Spearfishing
Skip the hassle of towing, launching, and designated boat drivers. We take up to 4 breath-hold divers out to Sydney’s offshore reefs, drop you on the fish, and stay dedicated to top-water safety cover for the whole session.
• 
Includes: Vessel, skipper/top-water safety, local reef knowledge.
• 
Requirements: Own spearfishing gear, breath-hold only (no SCUBA), current NSW fishing fee.

🌦 Weather Conditions 
• 
Rain will not cancel trips.
• 
If conditions are unsafe for fishing (e.g., high swell or strong winds), the captain will decide the day before whether to cancel.
• 
In case of cancellation, you may reschedule or receive a refund of your deposit if rescheduling is not possible.
