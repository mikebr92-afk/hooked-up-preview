Frequent Asked Questions
Everything you need to know before you set the alarm and head to the dock.

What types of Sydney fishing charters do you offer?
Reel Adventure Fishing offers small-group (max 4 people) Sydney fishing charters with both full-day and half-day options. We offer fishing experiences as well as Spearfishing boat hire. 
How do I make a booking enquiry?
You can send a Sydney fishing charter enquiry through the site and the team will help get you sorted. If you’re ready to book, the click the book now buttons and you will be directed to our booking platform on Fishbooker.com 
How much does a Sydney fishing charter cost with Reel Adventure Fishing?
Prices depend on trip length and target: Half Day (PM) Reef Fishing starts at $1,200, Half Day (AM) Reef Fishing is $1,400, and a Full Day Sport Fishing charter is $1,600. Seasonal Full Day Marlin Adventure charters (December–February) run $2,200. These prices are for the boat and skipper, not per person, up to the maximum group size. 
A deposit will be taken at the time of booking to secure your charter, with final payment made on or prior to the charter. 
Current trip information and pricing are listed on the Trips & Pricing page . 
Do I need a fishing licence to go on a charter in Sydney?
Yes, required for anyone fishing including spearfishing, not included in your charter price; $7 (3-day) to $35 (1-year) via Service NSW . 
Where does the charter depart from?
All charters depart from Foreshore Road Boat Ramp, Port Botany unless otherwise specified. 
What's your cancellation policy if I need to change my booking?
Cancel 3 or more days out and you'll get a full refund. Cancelling within 3 days of the charter and you will forfeit your deposit. If we're the ones cancelling — for weather or a mechanical issue — you're offered a reschedule or a full refund. 
What happens if the weather is bad?
Safety comes first — if conditions aren't safe to fish, Captain Vinnie makes that call, not the calendar. Charters we cancel due to weather or a mechanical issue are rescheduled or fully refunded, your choice.
