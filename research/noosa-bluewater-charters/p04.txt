ABout NoosaBluewater Charters
Based in Noosa Heads on Queensland’s Sunshine Coast, Noosa Bluewater Charters is the region’s original deep sea fishing operator. With access to prime offshore grounds and a reputation built on experience, we offer unforgettable reef and game fishing adventures for individuals, families, and groups of all skill levels.
