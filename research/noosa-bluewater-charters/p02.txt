OFFSHORE Fishing Charters
Explore Noosa’s many offshore reefs for a memorable day of offshore fishing 
Welcome aboard Noosa Bluewater Charters, operated by Noosa Watersports.
As Noosa’s first offshore fishing charter and one of the East Coast’s most esteemed offshore fishing ventures, our local skippers will ensure you are in the best location to hook up.
From novices to seasoned anglers, we cater to individual or group bookings seeking an exhilarating day of reef and game fishing.
Everything for your trip is organised – from rods and bait to food and snacks. Plus, our deckhands will gladly clean your catch, leaving you free to relish the thrill of the catch!

Plan Your Ultimate Offshore Fishing Adventure
With decades of local experience, our skippers know exactly where to go for the most productive offshore fishing. Backed by a passionate and helpful crew, we do everything we can to give you the best chance of hooking quality fish – and having fun while you do it. Our friendly deckies will bait your hooks, provide guidance, and even clean your legal catch so it’s ready to take home and cook.

Local Knowledge on Every Trip
Our vessels are powered by two huge 250hp Yamaha Outboards, ensuring maximum fishing time! We are the fastest fishing vessel available out of Noosa, meaning you’ll enjoy ample time with a rod in your hand while participating in our deep sea fishing adventures. Life doesn’t get much better!
Our crew are expert fishermen who are out on the water every day. They know where to look for fish at all the best fishing spots. We’ll target abundant reefs, wrecks, and pristine deep blue water on our offshore fishing charters. We’ll troll, jig, and bottom fish for a range of fish species, ensuring a fantastic experience on your fishing charters on the Sunshine Coast.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What types of fishing charters do you offer? 
We offer a variety of trips , including Half Day (5 hours) , 3/4 Day (7 hours) , and Full Day (9 hours) offshore fishing charters from Noosa. Each trip is tailored to the best conditions and seasonal catches. 
Do I need prior fishing experience? 
Not at all! Our friendly crew provides all the guidance you need, from baiting hooks to cleaning your legal catch. 
What’s included in the charter price? 
All fishing gear, bait, tackle, and safety equipment are provided. Onboard restrooms, refreshments, and snacks or meals (depending on trip length) are included, too. 
What should I bring on the trip? 
Bring sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, non-slip shoes , and any personal items. We’ll handle the rest, gear, bait, and fishing advice included. 
Are food and drinks provided? 
Yes! 
3/4 Day and Full Day trips include lunch and drinks. 
Half-day trips include snacks and refreshments. You’re also welcome to bring your own snacks or drinks (no glass bottles, please). 
What fish can I expect to catch? 
Depending on the season , you might reel in snapper, coral trout, red emperor, Spanish mackerel, tuna, mahi mahi , and more. 
What happens if the weather is bad? 
Your safety comes first. If weather conditions are unsuitable, we’ll reschedule your trip or offer a full refund. 
Do I need to take seasickness tablets? 
Many guests enjoy their trip without any issues, however seasickness can affect anyone, including experienced boaters.
If you are prone to motion sickness, have not been offshore before, or are concerned about seasickness, we recommend speaking with your pharmacist or doctor before your trip about suitable prevention options.
If you choose to take seasickness medication, please follow the manufacturer’s directions and take it at the recommended time before departure.
Please note that our staff are unable to provide medical advice or recommend specific medications.
What's the difference between charter lengths? 
Half-Day Charters (5 hrs): Local Reefs – includes Sunshine Reef and North Reef , which are approximately 30–45 minutes away .
3/4-Day Charters (7 hrs): Outer Reefs – includes Chardons Reef and the outer edges of North Reef , located around 45 minutes to 1.5 hours away .
Full-Day Charters (9 hrs): Further Distance Locations – includes Double Island , the Banks , and the Hards , which are generally 1.5 to 2 hours away . 
How can I book a charter? 
You can book online through our website or contact us directly via phone or email . Early bookings are recommended, especially during peak seasons. 
Where do charters depart from? 
All trips depart from Noosa Watersports Jetty , 
256 Gympie Terrace, Noosaville QLD 4566 
Is parking available nearby? 
Yes, there is ample parking available close to the jetty on Gympie Terrace. 
Can I bring my own fishing gear? 
Absolutely! While all gear is provided, you’re welcome to bring your own rod or tackle if you prefer. 
Do you cater to groups or special events? 
Yes, we host private charters for groups, birthdays, and corporate events – perfect for a day of fun and friendly competition. 
Are children welcome? 
Children aged 6 and above are welcome with adult supervision. Our crew ensures everyone – young and old – has a safe and enjoyable time on board. 
How early should I arrive before departure? 
Please arrive at least 15 minutes before departure for check-in and a safety briefing.
