Kiosk by Noosa Watersports
Opening Hours: Daily from 5.30am - 12.30pm
At KIOSK by Noosa Watersports, you can enjoy freshly brewed coffee, in-house baked pastries, healthy snacks, and refreshing smoothies on the sandy shores of the beautiful Noosa River.
Watch the sunrise at this local favourite while relaxing on our deck chairs and taking in the stunning view out to Noosa Heads.
Alternatively, stock up on takeaway essentials for a fun day of boating on the river.
