COMMON QUESTIONS
Private Charter FAQs
What's included in a private charter? 
Every private charter includes full use of the vessel, all fishing gear, bait, tackle and safety equipment, an experienced skipper and deckhand, and onboard restroom facilities. Drinks are included on all charters, with snacks included on the 5-hour and lunch included on the 7 and 9-hour charters.
Do we need fishing experience for a private charter? 
Not at all. Our crew looks after everyone from complete beginners to experienced anglers, handling bait, tackle and technique so your group can focus on having a good day out.
How many people can join a private charter? 
Each of our three vessels is set up for private groups of up to 12. Pricing above is based on 11 guests as standard; for larger groups, or to charter more than one boat for a bigger event, get in touch and we’ll put together a group quote.
Is a private charter suitable for corporate events, birthdays or bucks parties? 
Yes. Private charters are one of our most popular options for corporate days out and team events, milestone birthdays, bucks and hens celebrations, and other group occasions where you want the whole boat to yourselves.
Where do private charters depart from? 
All charters depart from Noosa Watersports Jetty, 256 Gympie Terrace, Noosaville QLD 4566, with ample parking nearby.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
Your safety comes first. If conditions are unsuitable, we’ll reschedule your charter or offer a full refund.
How do we book a private charter? 
You can book directly online, or send an enquiry below with your group size, preferred date and occasion, and we’ll confirm availability and put together a tailored quote.
What's your cancellation policy? 
You’ll receive a 100% refund if you cancel within 24 hours of booking. Trips are also subject to weather conditions and minimum numbers.
