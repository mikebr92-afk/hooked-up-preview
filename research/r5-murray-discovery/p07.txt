Fishing
Cast a line
and discover some of the Murray region’s best fishing spots.
Renowned as one of the region's top fishing destinations, Yarrawonga Mulwala offers year-round opportunities to cast a line.
With access to Lake Mulwala, the Murray River and surrounding waterways, anglers can enjoy everything from relaxed shoreline fishing to full-day trips on the water.
It's as much about the experience as it is the catch.
Fishing on Lake Mulwala
Lake Mulwala is classified as New South Wales waters, so anyone fishing on the lake is required to hold a valid NSW Recreational Fishing Licence. This applies whether you're fishing from a boat, kayak or the shoreline.
Before heading out, it's a good idea to familiarise yourself with current rules, bag limits and seasonal regulations. You can find everything you need, including how to purchase a licence, via the NSW Department of Primary Industries fisheries website.
For those planning a day on the water, a boating facilities map is also available to help you locate boat ramps, access points and key areas around the lake - making it easy to plan where to launch and fish.
Whether you're casting a line at sunrise or spending the day exploring the waterways, being prepared ensures a smooth and enjoyable experience on Lake Mulwala.
