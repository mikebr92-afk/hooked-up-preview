Welcome to Spring Tide Safaris 
Looking for Darwin Fishing Tours? Spring Tide Safaris is based in Darwin (Northern Territory) Top End. We operate Chartered tours in and around some of the most awe-inspiring remote locations in the Northern Territory. Owner/founder of Spring Tide Safaris Micky Qaiser has been fishing the Territory Rivers and has guided many anglers into the Barramundi Metre Club.
We are one of the most celebrated Barramundi fishing Darwin charters, known for providing the best Darwin Barramundi fishing experience to our clients coming from across the country. Our Darwin Fishing Tours target the mighty barramundi and a variety of other saltwater sport fish including Black Jewfish, Tuna, Golden Snapper, Trevally, Spanish & Spotted Mackerel, Coral Trout, GTs , Queen Fish, Threadfin Salmon and more.
Barramundi Fishing Darwin Charters
Want to go Barramundi Fishing in Darwin ? We have extensive knowledge of the Territory waterways and fish habitats is guaranteed.
Feel free to contact us for further information on charter availability. All safaris are fully guided and all-inclusive. Fishing tackle is supplied on a ‘replace if lost’ basis. Visitors participate at their own risk. No liability accepted.
Only one day to spare? 
Check out our Barramundi Fishing in Darwin one day tour. Our one day tour, Barramundi Fishing Darwin, departs from Darwin, travel time to your destination in approx 1.5 hours in air-conditioned 4WD. After a memorable day your guide will drop you back at your accommodation in Darwin.
Fishing Charters Darwin, Top End Fishing Tours
Our Extended Top End Fishing Tours are designed best for keen anglers who target our big barramundi in order to join the METER CLUB. Available all year round; the best time is after the monsoonal wet (run-off) from March to May.
We’ll keep an eye out for you!
