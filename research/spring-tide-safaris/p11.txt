FAQ 
Ready to book or want to make an enquiry? Just click the booking button and in the checkout you can choose to make full payment, pay a deposit, or just send an enquiry. No matter which option you choose, Micky will talk with you before your trip to work out the best itinerary and options to suit your needs. 
Perfect for the whole family including non-angling partners.
This half day tour includes pick up and return to your accommodation in Darwin. 
Fishing locations are all in and around Darwin Harbour so as soon as we're together we'll be at our fishing spots quick smart. Depending on tide and time of year, you'll have a chance to catch Barramundi and take part in the Million Dollar Fish competition and win exciting prizes.
This Darwin Barra fishing tour includes transfers, refreshments, and all fishing equipment.

Are there minimum numbers needed for each trip? 
Yes. We need 4 people booked per trip to cover the costs of crew and fuel.
If you don't have four people, Micky can arrange for a small extra charge per person to still make your trip possible.
An alternative is that Micky will allow other customer(s) to join your trip.
