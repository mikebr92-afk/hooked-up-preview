Online Ordering 
Online Ordering
Shipping
Order online and we’ll ship your fly fishing goodies anywhere in the world.
If your order is $500 or more, we’ll ship it for free.
If you would like to pick up your order form our storefront, simply select Local Pickup. No freight will be charged.
For orders less than $500, shipping to an Australian address costs $15 (Express) or anywhere else in the world costs $60. Pretty reasonable, don’t you think?
Custom Tied Fly Delivery
Fly orders are custom tied and may take up to 14 days delivery as listed
Returns
If you think something is wrong with your products when they arrive, please contact us on +61 (03) 6334 8386 and we’ll see what is needed.
Pricing 
It’s rare that our webshop contains a pricing error, but when it does and a product is purchased at the incorrect price, we will contact you immediately to organise a purchase at the correct pricing, or a full refund. (To date this has only occurred once – unfortunately a 100 pack of hooks does cost more than 0.89c)!
Thank you!
