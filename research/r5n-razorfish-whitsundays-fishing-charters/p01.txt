FISHING CHARTERS 
IN THE WHITSUNDAYS 
At Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters, we believe a great day fishing comes down to the right crew, quality gear and knowing where to go. With a maximum of six guests aboard our 7.5m Sailfish offshore catamaran, we offer personalised private and shared fishing charters backed by experienced local skippers, quality Shimano equipment and genuine knowledge of the Whitsundays and Great Barrier Reef.
From half-day trips to full-day reef charters and extended Outer Reef experiences, we’re all about making the most of your time on the water.
Whether you’re chasing your next personal best or planning a day fishing with mates or family, we’ve got a charter to suit.
Our standard departure location is Cape Gloucester Resort, with private charters also available from Airlie Beach and Bowen by arrangement. Shared charter departures may change to Airlie Beach depending on weather and fishing conditions.
Ready to get out on the water? Explore our charters, check availability or simply get in touch. We’ll take care of the rest!

PROUD SPONSORS OF THE STONKA FISHING CHALLENGE
Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters is proud to support the Stonka Fishing Challenge, bringing the local fishing community together for a great cause.
More than just a fishing competition, the event raises vital funds for the Leukaemia Foundation’s Leukaemia House in Townsville, helping support families travelling for blood cancer treatment.
We’re proud to be involved in an event that combines our passion for fishing with giving back to the community.
