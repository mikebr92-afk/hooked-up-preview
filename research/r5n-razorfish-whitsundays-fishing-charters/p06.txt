MEET THE RAZORFISH CREW
Meet the people who keep Razorfish running, from the crew on the water to the person behind the bookings.
MEET KRIS 
OWNER & SKIPPER
Decades of fishing experience. A passion for the ocean. Making every charter count.
Kris, owner and skipper of Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters, has spent decades fishing and boating along Australia’s east coast. With a lifelong passion for the ocean and a genuine love of sharing his knowledge, he’s built Razorfish around one simple goal: giving every guest an unforgettable day on the water.
Having owned and operated multiple vessels, Kris brings extensive experience in navigating changing conditions, understanding tides and fish behaviour and finding productive fishing grounds. His knowledge of the Whitsunday Islands, surrounding reefs and offshore waters helps him tailor each charter to the conditions and the people aboard.
For Kris, there’s nothing better than seeing someone land a quality fish, whether it’s their very first catch or a personal best they’ve been chasing for years. From families and first time fishos to experienced anglers and groups of mates, he enjoys sharing the excitement of fishing with people of all ages and abilities.
With species such as Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Nannygai, Tuskfish, Spanish Mackerel and Tuna found throughout the region, Kris loves the variety of reef and offshore fishing the Whitsundays and Great Barrier Reef have to offer.
Above all, Kris takes pride in providing a safe, comfortable and enjoyable experience, with a crew that’s always willing to share their knowledge, lend a hand and put in the effort to make every charter memorable.
For Kris, a great day on the water is about more than the fish you catch, it’s the experience, the people and the memories you take home.
MEET AMANDA (SKIP) 
RAZORFISH SKIPPER
A lifetime on the water. A genuine passion for sharing the thrill of fishing.
Amanda’s love of fishing started at just three years old, casting a line with her father in Redcliffe. After landing her first bream, she was hooked. Growing up fishing around Redcliffe and Grasstree Beach, she earned her boat licence at 16 and has spent most of her life on the water ever since.
That passion led to an impressive 22-year career in the Merchant Navy, working aboard commercial vessels, barges and offshore facilities throughout Australia and internationally. Her experience includes vessel operations, offshore crane operations and helicopter landing duties, giving her an extensive maritime background.
After returning to the Whitsundays, Amanda completed her Coxswain qualification and joined Razorfish, where she’s been skippering fishing charters for the past two years. Combining her professional maritime experience with a lifelong love of fishing, she’s found exactly where she belongs.
For Amanda, nothing beats helping someone land their first fish or the catch they’ve always dreamed of. Whether you’re an experienced fisho or picking up a rod for the first time, she brings a positive attitude, local fishing knowledge and a genuine commitment to making every day aboard enjoyable.
Her favourite species to chase include Coral Trout, Nannygai and Spanish Mackerel, and she loves the variety of reef and offshore fishing opportunities around the Whitsunday Islands and Great Barrier Reef.
Above all, Amanda wants every guest to leave with great memories, a smile on their face and hopefully a few quality fillets to take home.
MEET JESS 
BEHIND THE SCENES BOSS
Chief of Bait, bookings and keeping the Razorfish crew organised!
Not exactly the most accomplished fisho in the Razorfish crew, but someone has to keep things running behind the scenes!
Jess is the friendly voice answering your calls, responding to enquiries and organising charter bookings. From helping guests plan their Whitsundays fishing adventures to keeping the bait orders sorted, she takes care of the details so Kris and Amanda can concentrate on what they do best, putting you onto fish.
She might not be the first one to grab a fishing rod, but she’s always happy to help get you out on the water for a cracking day with Razorfish!
ABOUT THE VESSEL
Purpose-Built for Comfort, Speed & Serious Fishing
Meet Knot Guilty, the 7.5m Sailfish Commercial Offshore Catamaran operating our Razorfish Whitsundays fishing charters. Purpose-built for offshore fishing, she’s designed to deliver a comfortable, stable and enjoyable experience throughout the Whitsundays and surrounding Great Barrier Reef waters.
Powered by twin 200hp Mercury outboards, Knot Guilty combines speed, stability and spacious deck space to get you out to the fishing grounds and make the most of your time on the water.
Accommodating up to six fishing guests, plus a professional skipper and deckhand, she’s equipped with comfortable seating, an enclosed flushing toilet, quality fishing equipment and plenty of room to fish. Whether you’re chasing quality reef species or heading further offshore, Knot Guilty is ready for a cracking day on the water.

Fishing the Whitsundays
Reef Fishing, Offshore Adventures & Unforgettable Whitsundays Scenery
The Whitsundays is one of Queensland’s most spectacular fishing destinations, offering an incredible variety of reef, island and offshore fishing opportunities. From the waters surrounding the Whitsunday Islands to the deeper fishing grounds of the Great Barrier Reef, the region combines productive fishing with breathtaking tropical scenery.
At Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters, we operate fishing charters from Cape Gloucester, with departures from Airlie Beach and other locations available by arrangement. Our experienced skippers use their local knowledge of the reefs, tides, weather and fish activity to make the most of each day on the water.
Our Whitsundays fishing charters target a wide range of sought-after species, including Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Nannygai, Tuskfish, Spanish Mackerel and Tuna. Whether we’re fishing reef drop-offs, exploring island waters or heading further offshore, each charter is tailored to the conditions and the experience of the guests aboard.
From first-time fishos and families to experienced anglers chasing their next quality catch, Razorfish offers a personalised fishing experience aboard a comfortable, purpose-built vessel. With quality Shimano fishing gear, knowledgeable crew and some of the best scenery in the Whitsundays, it’s about much more than the fish you catch, it’s the entire experience.
OUR PROMISE
Good people. Great fishing. A genuine commitment to every charter.
At Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters, we believe a great day on the water is about more than just catching fish. It’s about feeling welcome, enjoying the experience and knowing you’re in capable hands from the moment you step aboard.
Our crew takes pride in providing safe, comfortable and personalised fishing charters, backed by local knowledge, quality equipment and a genuine willingness to go the extra mile. Whether it’s your first time holding a fishing rod or you’ve been chasing reef fish for years, we’ll put in the effort to make your day one to remember.
From your first enquiry to your final moments aboard, we’re committed to friendly service, honest communication and creating the kind of fishing experience you’ll want to come back for.
PROUD SPONSORS OF THE STONKA FISHING CHALLENGE
Proudly supporting local fishing, community and a great cause since 2023.
Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters has proudly sponsored the Stonka Fishing Challenge every year since 2023, supporting an event that brings anglers, families and the local fishing community together for a great cause.
More than just a fishing competition, Stonka raises valuable funds to support the Leukaemia Foundation’s Leukaemia House in Townsville, helping families who travel for blood cancer treatment access accommodation and essential support during difficult times.
We’re proud to stand behind an event that combines our passion for fishing with genuine community spirit. For the Razorfish crew, supporting Stonka is about giving back, getting behind a worthwhile cause and making a difference beyond the water.
