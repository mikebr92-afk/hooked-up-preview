All Inclusive Features
All rods and reels, tackle and the freshest bait is supplied for your day. The vessel has a live bait tank for the hardcore angler. Sunscreen and Bottled Water is provided onboard.
You bring:
• You will need to bring your own morning tea & lunch for the day.
• We no longer supply food If you are running short on time, the Bakery at the Shell Service Station in Rainbow Beach is open from 4am and offers a huge range of breakfast & lunch choices. Phone 0754 863 080 ***Best to call (day prior) for early morning pick up. 
• Rainbow Beach IGA is open 7 days 6am to 8pm Phone 0754 868 700
• Hat and sunglasses.
• Suitable footwear (sandshoes are a good choice) and warm clothing in the cooler months.
• Personal medication or diet requirements.
• We recommend sea sickness tablets to first timers, but be sure to consult your doctor or pharmacist first.
• Have an esky in your vehicle for transporting your catch home.
• And don't forget your camera!

About us
Catch an abundance of spectacular fish on a full day deep sea fishing charter departing beautiful Rainbow Beach. Our crew of professional fishermen have fished the waters off Rainbow Beach, Double Island Point and world heritage listed, Fraser Island exclusively for the past 30 years.
