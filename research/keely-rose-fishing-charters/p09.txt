Accommodation
Rainbow Beach offers a variety of accommodation options. We have listed a few below, but there are many more resorts and holiday rentals available. 
All charters are weather permitting. If you are considering booking accommodation, please keep this in mind. 
Rainbow beach Holiday Village
Near the beach front, right in the heart of Rainbow Beach. Cabins, tent and caravan sites available.
Phone (07) 5486 3222 
View Website 
big4 breeze Holiday Park
Situated at Carlo Point in Rainbow Beach. Hop, skip and a jump to our boat. Cabins, tent and caravan sites available.
Phone (07) 5486 3200 
View Website

About us
Catch an abundance of spectacular fish on a full day deep sea fishing charter departing beautiful Rainbow Beach. Our crew of professional fishermen have fished the waters off Rainbow Beach, Double Island Point and world heritage listed, Fraser Island exclusively for the past 30 years.
