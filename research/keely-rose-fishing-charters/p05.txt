Terms and Conditions
• A $100 deposit (per person) is required upon booking
• A $1000 deposit is required for a full boat booking (max. 10 persons)
• This deposit is non-refundable unless the charter is cancelled by the ship's master.
• Safety is our number one priority and if the ship's master deems conditions to be unsafe the charter will be cancelled.
• Any guest arriving intoxicated will not be permitted onboard the vessel and we will exercise the right to terminate a charter at any time for drunk and disorderly behaviour - refunds will not apply.
• Any fishing rods or reels or equipment lost overboard or damaged due to guest negligence must be paid for prior to disembarking the vessel.
• Please note: Prices are subject to change without notice. Any new pricing will be kept updated on our website and social media platforms.
