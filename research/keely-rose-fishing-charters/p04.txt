Departures
Passengers are required to make contact by phone the day before the charter. 
This is to confirm departure time and that weather and sea conditions are suitable.
We depart from the pontoons, which are located behind the restaurant at Carlo Point in Rainbow Beach.
Departure time is dependant on daylight hours.
We require passengers to be ready at the pontoon 15 minutes prior to departure time (which is confirmed the day prior to charter).
You will be greeted and guided through a short safety briefing. It's all fun from there on.
Depending on time of year and daylight hours, we return between approximately 2pm to 2:30pm.
Generally - Earlier departure through Summer and a later departure through Winter.
For guests that have to travel - Please call first to confirm charter is 100% definite before you leave home. We cannot stress this enough. 
Our departure times range from:
5:00am to 5.30am eastern standard time
Your departure time will be confirmed the day prior to charter
