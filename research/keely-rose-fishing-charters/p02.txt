Vessel & Crew
The Vessel
Getting there is part of the adventure on board Keely Rose, a 32" centre cabin ocean prowler ideally suited to deep sea fishing with its superior sea handling and stability.
It boasts a spacious walk around deck which allows the anglers to fish the entire boat comfortably and spaciously.
She is powered by the latest high performance, fume free Yanmar Diesel engine which cruises at 25 knots to reach your destination fast. She is fitted with highly sophisticated fish finding, satellite and radar navigation equipment.
The vessel has the best ship to shore communications via telephone, VHF and HF radios. The Keely Rose has fresh running water, a full shade canopy and toilet facility onboard. The vessel and all safety gear is always maintained and kept in first class condition.
Your Crew 
You're in safe hands on the Keely Rose. We run two professional skippers with thousands of sea hours of professional fishing and charter operations.
Owner, Ed Falconer, has pro fished and chartered fishing trips throughout the past 30+ years. He started on the very first charter boat in Rainbow Beach, the MV Mikado in 1990, and possesses an intimate knowledge of the surrounding reefs to give you the best chance of catching that big one.
You will often see the latest catches from the ' Keely Rose' featured on Channel 9 NEWS during Luke Bradnam's full weather round up, along with some epic footage in episodes of Creek 2 Coast with Scotty Hillier .
