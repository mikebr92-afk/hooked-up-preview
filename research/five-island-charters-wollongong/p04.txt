Game Fishing is for those people who want to catch the fish of a lifetime. We target different species at different times of the year.
December to April: black marlin, striped marlin, blue marlin, mahi-mahi (dolphin fish), Mako shark, yellowfin tuna, wahoo, kingfish
June to October: yellowfin tuna, bluefin tuna, albacore tuna, mako shark
