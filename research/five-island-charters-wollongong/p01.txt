WELCOME TO 
FIVE ISLAND FISHING CHARTERS 
We specialise in Game, sports & Reef Fishing 
Every day is a good day for fishing! 
The Fishing is amazing off the Wollongong coastline.
Our boat is fully equipped with everything you’ll need for a great day or week on the water. Our boat Seafox is a 40ft flybridge cruiser which can sleep up to 6 people + crew , with all the amenities of home with a double room , 2 bunks, large lounges, shower , toilet and fully equipped Kitchen.
The offshore trips will be targeting all reef species as well as Mako Shark, Mahi Mahi, Sailfish, Wahoo , Spanish mackerel Black, Blue and Striped Marlin, and Albacore, Yellowfin and Bluefin Tuna.
No fishing licence required in NSW on our charters. We offer the perfect trip catered to individual skills and interests.
