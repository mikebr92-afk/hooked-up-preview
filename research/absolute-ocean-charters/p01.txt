Broome Reef Fishing Charters
When you visit Broome, a reef fishing charter is an absolute must-do experience that really captures what Broome’s all about. Enjoy the spectacular scenery and an incredible array of fish on offer; from Coral Trout, Blue Lined Emperor, Red Emperor, & Golden Snapper to the incredible fight of a Spanish Mackerel or Giant Trevally.
Catching that fish of a lifetime is an everyday occurrence during a half day or full day Broome Fishing Charter on-board our 65ft vessel, Contessa C. Catch yourself a quality fish to take home for dinner, or earn bragging rights by landing some of Australia’s best sport fish right here in Broome!
Go Fish! 
Broome Creek & Estuary Fishing Charters
If you’d rather target Barramundi or catch a mud crab, then our half day creek & estuary fishing tour will have you exploring the local Broome estuarine systems. Whether you’re an amateur or expert, our experienced staff are always on hand to help you have an unforgettable experience reeling in that big one on a Broome Fishing Charter!
Go Fish! 
Broome Whale Watching &  Snubfin Dolphin Cruises
For a relaxing experience, join one of our Broome Whale Watching cruises off Cable Beach, guided by our Marine Biologist. Spend an afternoon watching majestic Humpback whales breaching and playing, including mothers and calves migrating along the Kimberley coast.
We also offer daily Snubfin Dolphin Cruises. Explore the coastline, visiting Gantheaume Point, Reddell Beach, Entrance Point, and Roebuck Bay, while learning about local biodiversity. Spot Snubfin dolphins, turtles, sea snakes, and shorebirds.
Relax in comfort on one of the daybeds while our Marine Biologist keeps you informed with all the fascinating facts! On the ride home, top off your Whale-Watching or Snubfin Dolphin Eco Tour with one of Broome’s magnificent sunsets.
Talk to us about organising an Exclusive Cruise or Fishing Charter for your next event. We tailor charters to your needs, and our trained staff provide first-class service.

Broome Bus Tour
A trip to Broome is never deemed complete without experiencing an enthralling adventure on the water – be it a boat ride, fishing excursion or jet ski tour. However, there’s more to Broome than just water activities. The town is also home to some of Australia’s most fascinating historical sites, showcasing its rich past.
If you’re interested in learning about Broome’s marine environment and its history, then join us for an all-inclusive town tour. Our expert guides will take you on a journey of discovery, sharing fascinating insights into the marine life surrounding Broome. 
So, whether you’re a first-time visitor to Broome or a seasoned traveler, our town tour promises to be a unique and enthralling experience that you will cherish forever.
