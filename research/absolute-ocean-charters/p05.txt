Exclusive Charters & Cruises

Private Boat Charters
Would you love to celebrate a day on the water and tailor the experience to suit you and your family, friends or workmates?
Or perhaps you’ve wanted to organise for your family or small group to embark on an overnight adventure on the magnificent waters surrounding Broome?
Whatever exclusive experience you may be dreaming of, let us help you make the adventure become reality! 
Exclusive Reef Fishing Charter
Not unlike our iconic full day or half day Reef Fishing Charters , this is where you can book the charter exclusively for you and your mates only. You could opt for AOC’s signature catering experience or tailor-make a menu to suit. We could also discuss the type of fishing you’d prefer to do and pre-arrange specific fishing experiences to suit your group.
Exclusive Creek & Estuary Fishing Charter
Experience the thrill of estuary fishing in complete privacy. Perfect for you and your group, this exclusive adventure aboard our custom creek fishing vessel lets you explore Broome’s picturesque estuarine systems at your own pace. Tailor your fishing experience to target specific species like Barramundi and Mangrove Jack, or even try your hand at mud crabbing. With our expert guides, top-notch fishing gear, and personalised service, you’re set for a memorable and unique fishing trip. Book your exclusive charter today and enjoy a private, customised fishing adventure in Broome’s beautiful turquoise waters.
Exclusive Whale Watching Cruise
Experience the awe-inspiring beauty of Humpback whales in complete privacy. This exclusive charter allows you and your guests to enjoy an intimate whale watching adventure aboard our spacious 65ft vessel. With three viewing platforms and a walk-around deck, you’ll have unobstructed views of these majestic creatures as they migrate along the Kimberley coast. Enjoy personalised service, informative insights from our onboard Marine Biologist, and the luxury of relaxing on daybeds while savouring complimentary appetisers and an onboard bar. Book your exclusive whale watching cruise today for an unforgettable and private encounter with nature’s giants.
Exclusive Snubfin Dolphin Cruise
Experience the beauty of Broome’s coastline in privacy, encountering Snubfin dolphins up close with personalised commentary from our onboard Marine Biologist. Cruise along Gantheaume Point, Reddell Beach, and Roebuck Bay, relaxing on our spacious 65ft vessel with multiple viewing platforms, daybeds, and complimentary appetisers and an onboard bar. End your day with a breathtaking sunset over the water.
Exclusive Afternoon / Sunset Cruise
Similar to our magnificent Roebuck Bay Eco Dolphin Sunset Cruise , you could book the entire vessel for up to 32 of your own special guests and enjoy a cruise through the spectacular waters of Roebuck Bay, Reddell Beach and Cable Beach. Again, catering can be selected from AOC’s signature sunset cruise menu, or we can discuss other tasty treats and icy beverages. And if you’d like our expert marine biologist guide to join the adventure, please let us know.
Exclusive Broome Town Bus Tour
Experience the charm and history of Broome with our exclusive Broome Bus Town Tour , where you can hire the entire bus for a private, personalised adventure. This engaging tour is perfect for groups looking to explore Broome together and offers a comprehensive look at the town’s iconic sites and hidden gems. From the bustling streets of Chinatown to the scenic views of Roebuck Bay, our knowledgeable guides will share captivating stories about Broome’s pearling past and vibrant present.
Looking for something different?
Head to our Tailored Experience page to create your ultimate day. Whether it’s a bespoke adventure on land or sea, we’ll help you design the perfect itinerary to suit your needs and interests.
Have we tempted you yet…
All of the above suggested exclusive private charter experiences have base whole boat charter prices with the option to add or remove various activities and catering. Prices based on minimum guest numbers with additional charges for extra people above 14pax on fishing charters.
‘Contessa C’ sleeps a maximum of 6 guests in comfort and style for overnight or extended charters. To find out more on the features of this remarkable vessel, click here .
Times and dates are available dependent on weather, time of year and existing booking schedules. If you have a special occasion to celebrate, we certainly encourage booking early to avoid disappointment.
Please don’t hesitate to contact us to discuss further options as the above suggested itineraries are just a guide. We do our utmost best to accommodate all interests.
We look forward to welcoming you aboard for your own unique AOC experience!
