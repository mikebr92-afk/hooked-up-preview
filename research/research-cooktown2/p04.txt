Tropical Boating Adventures
Tropical Boating Adventures specialises in blue water sport fishing across the top of the Cape York Peninsula. 
'Bat' is your host and guide aboard 'No S Caping' a custom built vessel, at home in the turquoise waters of the Torres Strait.
Whether you are a novice or experienced, young or not so young, you can be assured of a relaxed, and enjoyable day out. 'Bat' has fished the area for over 10 years and will take you to where the fish are biting, including Spanish Mackerel, thumping Giant Trevally, Bluefin Tuna, Queenfish and Cobia to name a few.
Taking a maximum of four passengers, you are guaranteed personalised service with patient, friendly advice.
Tropical Boating Adventures is located at Punsand Bay, 7km to the west of “the tip” of Australia, and 28km north of Bamaga.
