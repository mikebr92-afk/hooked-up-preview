Hammerhead Sport Game Fishing Charters 
Hammerhead Charters is your premiere Fishing Charter business in Port Stephens. We offer a variety of charter experiences that cater for the beginner angler, through to the experienced game angler such as: Reef Fishing, Game/Tournament Fishing Charter, Estuary Fishing, Whale/Dolphin Watching Cruises, Dinner Cruises and Private Charters.
MV Hammerhead is a 41-foot Game Fishing Steber. The vessel boasts a comfortable cockpit area, fly bridge, lounge cabin, kitchenette, bathroom and a forward queen sized cabin. So you will always be comfortable on all of your charter experiences.
MV Hammerhead is fully equipped for game and sport fishing with a game chair, game reels and rods. Reef fishing rods and reels and all the accessories that you need to enjoy your Port Stephens Fishing Adventure. At Hammerhead Charters we can tailor any charter experience to your needs. MV Hammerhead is skippered by a team of regular skippers including ex commercial fishermen, with detailed knowledge of the area, we give you the best chance to hook up on your next catch.
For the most professional and friendly charter in Port Stephens, drop us a line at Hammerhead Charters.
Phone us on 0498 479 420 or contact us via email
