Reef Fishing
Your Port Stephens fishing adventure starts at the d’Albora Marinas at Nelson Bay onboard Hammerhead II.
You will cruise from Nelson Bay past sparkling Shoal Bay and head out through Tomaree and Yaccaba Heads, taking in the most beautiful natural environment on the NSW coast. As you leave the port you will see Cabbage Tree Island and Broughton Island where you may choose to try your luck fishing for Snapper, Pearl Perch, Flathead and a variety of reef fish.
Our experienced Staff know the best spots to give you the greatest chance at a catch.
