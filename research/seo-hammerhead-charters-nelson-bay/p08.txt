Private Charters
Have you ever wanted to have your own boat for a day or a few hours, but without all the fuss of organising everything involved? You just want to turn up, get on, and be driven around to where ever you want to go for the day. At Hammerhead Charters we offer a variety of charters to suit any of your needs. Maybe fishing is not your passion, and that is not a problem. Here at Hammerhead you can charter our boat with almost any purpose in mind.
Here are a few ideas to get you started:
Island Cruising Port Stephens 
Should you choose to cruise off shore you can see the amazing volcanic formations, which are Tomaree, and Yacabba heads that once formed the rim of the volcano that is now the harbour of Port Stephens. Our skipper will take you into formations including “Mrs Murphies” and show you the Tomaree “Indian Head”.
Destination Cruising Port Stephens 
You can choose to cruise to a destination on Port Stephens, or we can plan a cruise for you. Many of our customers like to travel upriver and visit sleepy Karuah or take a cruise to Tea Gardens and enjoy the town’s hospitality. Buy fresh oysters direct from the farmers. The day is up to you; create your own experience with Hammerhead Charters.
Corporate Charter 
If your workplace is in need of a well-deserved break, why not organise a private charter aboard Hammerhead. There are a range of options to suit your staff; from cruises to fishing. Relax, unwind, refresh your staff and be a great boss with Hammerhead Charters.
All of our private charters are POA. 3hrs minimum . Depending on client numbers and extras you would like for your charter experience prices may vary.
For more information on how to book this charter experience fill out the online contact enquiry or call us on 0498 479 420 . We can’t wait to see you aboard Hammerhead Charters.
Please note for all charters booked online or by phone through Hammerhead Charters, we require bookings in advance to ensure your charter experience is professionally organised. Full payment is required at this time and 100% refundable only if the charter is cancelled at the captain’s discretion. 25% deposit is required to secure all bookings (no refund if cancelled 14 days prior to charter). Full payment prior to all charters. 100% refund if cancelled by unfavourable weather conditions.
