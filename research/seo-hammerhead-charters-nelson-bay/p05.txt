Contact Us
Get in touch.
Simply Complete the form below or call us on 0498 479 420 
The team at Hammerhead Charters are able to assist you with all your Port Stephens Game Fishing, Cruising and Charter experiences. Simply contact us and we will take care of all the details ensuring that your Game Fishing, and cruising the waters of Port Stephens create the memories of a lifetime.
Port Stephens is located just 2.5 hours north of Sydney by car and enjoys a unique position on the NSW Mid North Coast. The port boasts the southern most coral reefs in Australia and boasts some of the best Game Fishing in the Country.
Hammerhead II is berthed at D’Albora Marinas at the holiday township of Nelson Bay one of Australia’s favorite holiday destinations. There is a large paid ticket parking area at the rear of the marina.
