OUR VESSEL
Custom-Built for Maximum Fishability
Our vessel isn’t just another boat – it’s a purpose-built fishing machine, designed from the ground up by a commercial fisherman in partnership with a naval architect. Built because no production vessel could meet our standards, this rig is the ultimate offshore weapon – fast, stable, safe, and made to fish hard.
With 650 horsepower, she cruises effortlessly at 21–22 knots and can hit up to 44 knots when needed. Backed by a 1,000L fuel capacity, we can reach distant grounds quickly and confidently.
