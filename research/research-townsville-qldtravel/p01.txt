Fishing Charters

Townsville Fishing Charters specializes in Blue water sports fishing and tours in the Townsville creeks and waterways.
Full-day charters are scheduled.
Take in the beautiful surroundings whilst sports (lure) casting, trolling, and Jigging with a professional instructor with 10 years of guiding experience. All fishing equipment is provided.
Your tour starts when you meet at the Breakwater Marina on Mariners Drive - off the Strand.
They will be targeting sports fish such as Giant Trevally, Queenfish and Mackerel and Tuna as well as having the opportunity to see some of their local wildlife in their natural surroundings including Whales, Dugongs, turtles, and sea snakes
Good quality equipment, rods, reels, and braid fishing line are supplied. Quality fishing charter vessel with professional tuition and instruction.
Tours 
Full-day tours are available for a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 clients - for lure fishing. They will happily accommodate tours out of town for interested groups. Specializing in Sports (lure) casting, trolling, and Jigging, Michael has a vessel that comfortably suits his style of fishing
Bait fishing tours can be organized for larger groups.
What to Bring/Wear 
Hat, Video Camera/Camera, Sunscreen, lunch
Rates 
$300.00 pp min 2 / 3 $280.00 for a group of 4 Sole charter/book boats 1,2 or 3 for $1100.00

Conditions of Tour 
The operator reserves the right to change the fishing location due to wind or other conditions outside the operator's control. All tackle is provided on a replacement if-lost or damaged basis. No charge for lost lures Full payment before departure 100% refund if canceled within 1 month of tour. 50% within a fortnight and no refund within 7 days.
We strongly recommend travel insurance.
No bottles or glass in the vessel BYO lunch and soft drinks - water will be provided
