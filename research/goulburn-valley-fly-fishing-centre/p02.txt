Goulburn Valley Fly Fishing / On the river since 1994
A day afloat or four days downriver?
We started taking anglers down the Goulburn in rafts in 1994. One of the pleasures is still the same: coming round a bend and finding a trout rising along a bank you couldn’t reach on foot. Come for a single drift, or bring a few mates and follow the river through the valley.
01 / Single day
Half-day & full-day drifts
5 or 8 hours · Private boat for 1 or 2 anglers
A half day suits a morning on the water or an afternoon timed for an evening rise. A full day gives us time to fish a longer reach and still pull in for a trout you’d otherwise have to row past.
The price is per boat, not per person. For a bigger group, we can arrange extra boats and guides when available.
See day trips & prices 
Book a single-day drift online 
02 / Multi-day
The Goulburn River Journey
4 fishing days · Usually 4 anglers in 2 guided boats
Fish selected reaches from below Eildon Pondage towards Molesworth, as flows allow. The gravel runs around Thornton give way to long bends below Alexandra, where the river leaves the road behind.
We organise the boats and shuttles and quote for your group. We can include accommodation, or plan a three-day journey when you have less time.
Explore the four-day route 
Ask about group dates & pricing 
Not sure which trip to choose?
Tell me how many of you are coming and when you can get away. I’ll talk you through the options.
