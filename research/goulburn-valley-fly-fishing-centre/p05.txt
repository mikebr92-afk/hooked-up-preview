Goulburn Valley Fly Fishing / Gift vouchers
Give them time on the water.
It might be the first fly fishing lesson they’ve been talking about, or a day in a drift boat with someone they don’t see often enough.
Give a particular experience, or choose a dollar-value voucher from A$50 and let them decide. There’s no need to pick a fishing date when you buy.
Already have a voucher? 
A card to unwrap or a voucher to print
Tell us who it’s for, who it’s from and what you’d like it to say. We personalise both versions.
A printed card by post
The gift card features trout artwork by Derek DeYoung and comes with a hand-tied fly. There’s something to put in an envelope and a fly they can keep or take fishing.
A$19 extra includes the card, fly and Express Post.
A digital voucher by email
We email you a personalised, high-resolution PDF. Print it on A4 to slip into your own card, or forward it to someone you won’t see in person.
Need it for a particular time? Call Anthony on 0418 995 611 before ordering.
Which gift would suit them?
For someone starting out
Our two-day beginner workshop takes them from casting practice on Saturday to fishing the river on Sunday afternoon. A private lesson is the alternative for someone who’d prefer to learn at their own pace. They don’t need to own a fly rod.
For a day together
A private Goulburn drift is priced by the boat, not by the angler. Give them a half or full day for one or two people. You could be the one in the other seat.
For the angler who’d rather choose
Give a dollar amount. They might put it towards a streamcraft session to learn where trout sit in a current, flies or equipment, or a hosted trip to New Zealand, Montana or Patagonia. You choose the budget; they choose the fishing.
Choose a voucher 
Already have a gift voucher?
Call or email us with your certificate number and the redemption code, if your voucher has one. For a lesson or trip, tell us the dates you have in mind. We’ll check availability and arrange the booking with you.
New vouchers are valid for three years from the date of issue. The expiry date is printed on the voucher. Older vouchers issued without an expiry date are still honoured without expiry.
Call or text Anthony: 0418 995 611 · Email about your voucher
