Goulburn Valley Fly Fishing / Fly fishing school 
Two-day beginner workshop
Learn to fly fish in Alexandra
On Saturday we’ll turn over a stone at the river’s edge and look at the insects underneath. On Sunday afternoon, when you open a fly box and decide what to tie on, you’ll have something to go on.
You’ll start with putting a rod together, practise your first casts on grass, then fish a local trout stream with an instructor alongside you. You don’t need any experience, and there’s no need to buy a rod. Come and use ours.

The two days
How the weekend works
01 / Saturday
First casts and a look under the stones
Start with the rod, reel and line. We’ll show you where the leader fits, how to attach a fly and which knots you’ll actually use. You tie them yourself, with time to have another go.
On the grass, your instructor makes a short cast, then it’s your turn. You’ll learn to lift the line and lay it down again, to roll cast when there are trees behind you, and to carry line in the air. Putting the fly where you intend is more useful than seeing how far you can throw it.
Our first visit to the river is a short one. We’ll look at what lives among the stones and compare it with the patterns in the fly box.
02 / Sunday
Take those skills onto the river
We pick up the rods again in the morning. With the first day behind you, it’s easier to work on timing and accuracy, and on letting out a little more line without losing control of the cast.
The main fishing session is on Sunday afternoon. Your instructor will help you look for water worth fishing, approach without disturbing it and make your own cast. Once the fly lands, watch how it travels. Does it drift with the current, or does the line pull it away?
You’ll learn what a take can look like and how to play, land and release a trout. We can’t promise a fish, but you’ll have fished for yourself, not just watched a demonstration.
The river and session times depend on conditions. Sunday’s fishing is part of your A$490 workshop, not a separate guided booking.

Teaching on our home water since 1994
A little help at the right moment
When a cast lands in a heap, you don’t need a list of everything you could do differently. Often the most useful thing your instructor can say is, “Try giving the line a little longer to straighten behind you.” You can try the next cast straight away and see what changes.
We’re working guides on the Goulburn and its surrounding rivers. With no more than five people per instructor, we have time to watch you cast. Before you head home on Sunday, we’ll talk through what to practise next. You’ll take your course notes with you, and you’re welcome to ring us with questions once you’ve been out on your own.
“All the essentials were condensed and simplified, and by the end of two days I felt confident heading out on my own.”
Jess, workshop participant 
Before you come
We supply the fishing gear
What’s here for you
Rods, reels, lines, leaders, flies and waders for the river sessions. You’re welcome to bring a rod you already own. It’s a good opportunity to ask about it before deciding whether to buy anything else.
What to put in the car
A hat, sunglasses for eye protection, a belt to secure the supplied thigh waders, comfortable outdoor shoes, a warm layer and a rain jacket. Pack lunch, snacks and a refillable water bottle.
You’ll need a Victorian recreational fishing licence unless you’re exempt. We’ll send you the meeting point and daily times with your booking confirmation.
A few things people ask
Is this the right workshop for me?
Can I come on my own, or with someone else? 
Yes. You don’t have to find a partner. Come on your own, bring a friend or learn alongside someone in your family. The group includes people who’ve never held a fly rod, as well as those who’ve had a go or are coming back after a few years away.
Can a younger angler join? What about access to the river? 
Give Anthony a call before booking for a younger angler so we can discuss their age and experience. There’s outdoor casting practice, uneven ground on the banks and some wading may be involved. Tell us about any mobility or access concerns and we’ll talk through the weekend with you.
Can I give the workshop as a gift? 
Yes. When you’d rather let someone choose their own weekend, see our fly fishing gift vouchers .
What happens after Sunday? 
Take the notes home, practise and go fishing. You can get in touch for help with a cast or a question about gear. A private day of streamcraft and fishing on the following Monday is an optional, separate booking, subject to instructor availability.
What about a private group or a different date? 
For a club, family or group of five or more, contact Anthony about a private workshop. Private beginner lessons may also suit other dates or an angler looking for help with one particular skill. We’re happy to suggest places to stay in Alexandra.
