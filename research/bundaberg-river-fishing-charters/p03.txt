Charters 
All our charters are on calm protected waters and accommodate all ages and levels of experience. Whether you have fished all your life or yet to have wet a line, we can cater for all fishing needs. 
We target estuary species such as Bream, Cod, Flathead, Tailor, Trevally, Mangrove Jack and Whiting. We use and supply all bait, lures and soft plastics on all our charters. 
We use top quality Okuma rods and reels, Platypus line and Zman soft plastics. We don't just take you to the spots, we show you how, when, and where to fish these areas. 
Our charters are great for introducing children to fishing, family fun days, special occasions or even staff team building days.

Calm Waters 
Our charters are all inside the protected waters of the Bundaberg estuary river systems, so should the wind pick up, there is always a location for us to anchor up, out of the wind. Please note that every charter is different as the weather conditions are constantly changing. If you have any questions regarding our charters, please give us a call on 0419 767 396 or send us an email at troy@bundabergriverfishing.com.au
