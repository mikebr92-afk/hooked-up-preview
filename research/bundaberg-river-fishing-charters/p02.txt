About 
We provide private, calm water fishing charters for small groups up to 4 people on Bundaberg’s rivers. 
No experience is necessary as we cater for all levels of experience and provide fishing guidance on all our charters. 
We provide everything you need for fishing, including quality Okuma rods and reels, Zman soft plastics, bait and lures, as well as all tackle. We will even cast and bait your line if required. 
We are happy for you to bring a favourite rod or some of your own tackle, however we ask that you keep it to a minimum as we are limited for room. 
Our commitment to Sustainability and future fishing generations, we encourage a catch and release policy to protect our future fish stocks. However, we are more than happy for you to take your catch home should you wish. 
We strictly adhere to Queensland fishing size and bag limits. 
Our Boat - the Drifter 
The Drifter is a 4.9 metre Haines Hunter Side Console, powered by a Brand New 115hp 4 Stroke Mercury Outboard motor. With amazing stability, front and rear casting decks, the Drifter offers plenty of fishing room whilst ensuring comfort and safety. Fitted with a Lowrance Hook Reveal 7 Triple Shot Fish Finder/Sounder, the Drifter can take you to and put you on the best spots fast. Drawing very little water, the Drifter allows us the ability to fish all tides, estuaries, sand bars, mangroves, as well as rock walls, making the Drifter the perfect estuary river fishing boat.
