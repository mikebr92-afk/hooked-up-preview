The Vessel 
We invite you to jump aboard "DISCE PATI" a custom built 8 metre forward cabin commercial charter boat. Built Tassie tough by the team at Lyndcraft Boats, St Helens, Tasmania. 
"DISCE PATI" is powered by twin Honda 200hp 4 stroke outboards. This vessel offers a safe and stable fishing platform for your family and mates to enjoy. 
​ 
​ Vessel operations: 
Survey Class Level 2C / 2D / 3C . 
Operational limits are within 30 nautical miles from the shore. 
​ 
​ Vessel crew: 
1 or 2 crew (owner/operator) with Coxswain, Elements of Shipboard Safety and Level 2 First Aid Certificate. ​
