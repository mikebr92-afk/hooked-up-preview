G'day and Welcome to Mackay Fly & Sportfishing Charters 
​ 
MFSF is the only guided sportfishing operation of this style in the Mackay Region specialising in fly and lure fishing. 
​ 
Paul's mission, first and foremost, has always been one of getting his clients connected to good fish and anglers having an enjoyable day on the water. His ability to locate and catch fish is second to none, Paul and Mackay Fly & Sportfishing Sportfishing Charters are proud in being environmentally aware of the creatures and fish that live in the waters. He uses only artificial hardbody lures, plastics or flies and implements a catch and release policy on some species emulating famous sustainable sports fisheries around the world. Paul's respect for the environment in which Mackay Fly & Sportfishing operates is highlighted by his intimate knowledge of how it's intricate eco system functions. The nuances of time, tides, winds, currents and moons are integral factors for successful anglers in these waters and Paul is constantly considering all aspects of the environment. He is available to consult with you as you plan your Mackay Fishing charter in order to get the best results within your available vacation time. 
​ 
The Seaforth area has recently been gazetted by the Queensland Government as a net free zone and this will see this regions fishery boom in the coming years for species like Barramundi Threadfin Salmon and many other inshore species. 
​ 
Mackay Fly & Sportfishing offers personalised full day fishing adventures in both salt and freshwater for a variety great sportfish species for both fly fisherman and conventional lure anglers.
