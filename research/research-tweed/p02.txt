River Ter, Tweed Heads, NSW, 2485 Cushy Fishing Charters, located at Tweed Marina in Tweed Heads, New South Wales, specializes in offshore fishing adventures, offering excursions to the best reefs of the Tweed and southern Gold Coast. With over 20 years of experience, their knowledgeable crew caters to both seasoned anglers and novices, ensuring a safe and memorable day on the water.
In addition to deep-sea fishing targeting species like snapper and marlin, Cushy Fishing Charters offers whale watching experiences during the winter months, allowing guests to encounter humpback whales in their natural habitat. The charter service is owner-operated, providing personalized attention and insight to enhance each fishing trip.
