Good to know 
What can the voucher be used for? Any charter: snapper, mixed bag, whiting, squid, kingfish or offshore, on any date with space. Enter the code at the online checkout, or read it to Mal over the phone. 
How long is it valid? Three years from the day it is issued. Any balance left after a booking stays on the voucher for next time. 
Can I choose the amount? Yes. Pick one of the set amounts or enter your own, anywhere from $50 to $5,000. A spot on a bay charter is $200 and the whole boat is $1,550, so those make tidy presents. 
How do I pay? By card, online, at the time you order. The payment page is run by Stripe, so no card details touch our site, and Visa and MasterCard are accepted. The voucher is emailed as soon as the payment goes through. 
When does the voucher arrive? Straight after payment, by email, to the person you name, with a copy to you. If it is for a birthday and you would rather they did not see it early, put your own email as the recipient and forward it on the day. 
I bought a gift card on the old website. Is it still good? Yes. Call Mal on 0418 769 384 with the card details and he will honour it over the phone.
