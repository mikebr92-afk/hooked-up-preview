Quick Answers 
When do I pay? 
Full payment is taken online when you book, through Stripe's secure checkout. Your spot is only held once payment goes through, and there is nothing more to pay on the day.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
If Mal deems the conditions unsafe, your charter will be cancelled and your payment can be used to reschedule for another date. If no other date suits, you get a full refund.
Do I need my own gear? 
No, we supply all premium rods, reels, tackle, and bait. However, if you have a favorite rod you'd like to use, you're welcome to bring it along.
Departure Locations
We depart from various boat ramps across Port Phillip & Western Port Bay to ensure we're closest to where the fish are biting. 
Your exact departure point is confirmed when you book.
