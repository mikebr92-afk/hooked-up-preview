4 Best Local Spots 
Mal confirms the ramp with you before the day, depending on the charter, the season and the weather. Tap a spot for directions.
Launching Way, Carrum
Launching Way, Carrum VIC 3197
Home base. Snapper charters on Port Phillip Bay from October, and the closest ramp to the city.
Directions Port Phillip Bay Snapper charters 
Hastings Boat Ramp
Hastings Boat Ramp, Marine Parade, Hastings VIC 3915
Western Port Bay: early snapper, whiting on the banks, squid and gummy sharks.
Directions Western Port Bay Mixed bag charters 
Mornington Boat Ramp
Mornington Boat Ramp, Schnapper Point, Mornington VIC 3931
Quick access to the Peninsula end of Port Phillip Bay. Snapper, pinkies and squid from January to May.
Directions Port Phillip Bay Squid charters 
Rye Boat Ramp
Rye Boat Ramp, Point Nepean Road, Rye VIC 3941
The southern end of the bay and the way out through the heads. Whiting, kingfish, and offshore trips into Bass Strait.
