Vessel 
Seafox is a 40' flybridge cruiser powered by twin 380hp cummins diesel engines. With modern galley, comfortable lounges, two bedrooms and modern bathroom. We have plenty of deckspace, live bait tanks, Rupp double stringer outriggers, iceboxes, and the best gear Shimano has to offer.
Skipper 
As owner and skipper of Seafox, Trent has extensive local and offshore fishing knowledge. You can be sure to return home safe as Trent's qualifications are:
• 
Master 5 Certificate III in Maritime Operations
• 
Certificate II in Marine Engine Driving (MED2)
• 
First Aid Certificate
With Trent at the helm, there'll be plenty of laughs on board Seafox.
