EXCLUSIVE ROTTNEST ISLAND CRUISES FOR A PERFECT DAY (or night) ON THE WATER 
What’s the best thing about a Perth summer? With the unique Rottnest Island on your doorstep, spending quality time on the water has to be at the top of anyone’s list!
Imagine wowing your friends, family or workmates at your next event, cruising the beautiful pristine waters and sandy beaches of Rottnest Island in the elegance of our luxury charter, perfect for entertaining small intimate groups of up to 42 people.
Organising a Rottnest Island private cruise for your next event is so easy with Blue Horizon Charters. Corey and the team are here to help. Choose your own caterer, and bring your playlist for a fun party, or an elegant champagne sipping afternoon. Whatever you choose - it’s all up to you! You can even select your most convenient pick-up point, in either Fremantle or Perth City.
So when you’re looking to customise an unforgettable experience and create a splash for your next corporate event, or want an intimate venue with the best views in Perth for your bucks, hens or birthday party, choose Blue Horizon Charters for your Rottnest Island cruise that will be the talk of your business associates, friends or family for years to come!
