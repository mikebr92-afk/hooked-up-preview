Welcome Aboard the Blue Horizon 
Enjoy a day on the pristine water game fishing or bottom fishing on the Blue Horizon, a luxury vessel equipped with the latest electronic equipment, 240 V power and air-conditioned. The Blue Horizon is the largest charter fishing vessel in Exmouth with a spacious deck with plenty of room for our passengers.
