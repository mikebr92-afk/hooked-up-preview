Billfishing
Innkeeper Game Fishing Adventure
Experience Exmouth’s world class game fishing grounds targeting blue marlin, black marlin, striped marlin, sailfish, yellowfin tuna, mahi mahi (dolphinfish) and wahoo, fishing with lures, teasers and baits.
When that big hook-up comes, you can choose to take up the fight in a stand-up harness or a fighting chair if you prefer. You do not have to be experienced in this style of fishing as we coach many first timers through it. But be warned; this fishing can be highly addictive!
If you’re ready for a premium, high‑energy bluewater experience with a crew who lives and breathes game fishing, this is the charter you book.
Full Day Exclusive Charter – 44ft Game Fishing Vessel
Exclusive booking, meaning your group only onboard. Bring your group of up to 8 people.
Price: Please contact us
Includes: Western Australian fishing licence, all tackle, bottled water and soft drink (BYO lunch and alcohol.)
Times : Normally leaves at 8am and returns at 4pm.
Full Day Exclusive Charter – 25ft Centre Console Vessel
Exclusive booking, meaning your group only onboard. Bring your group of up to 4 people.
Price: Please contact us
Includes: Western Australian fishing licence, all tackle, bottled water (BYO lunch and alcohol.)
Times: Normally leaves at 8am returns at 4pm.
