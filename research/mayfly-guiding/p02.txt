Meet Dylan
Dylan is your dedicated guide, bringing 25 years of fly fishing experience, including 5 years of competitive fishing. He earned 3rd place in the Australian National Fly Fishing Championships in 2020 and won a Tasmanian state round competition in 2024. Dylan has fished alongside world champions, participated in advanced clinics, and played a key role in guiding the Polish team ahead of the World Fly Fishing Championships in 2019. He has extensive knowledge of the Central Highlands lakes and local rivers, where he spends his time off fishing, hiking, and exploring. Dylan owns a shack in Miena, providing him with a perfect base for his adventures in the Western Lakes. 
His respect for the natural environment fuels his passion for sharing Tasmania's pristine waters. Join Dylan for an unforgettable fishing adventure and benefit from his expert guidance.
