Deep Sea Fishing Gold Coast. Half Days, Full Days, Private Charters. 
Welcome to the Gold Coast fishing charters website. Home of our crew, a passionate team of fishermen that aim to provide the best Gold Coast fishing trips every time. Over the years we have built up a solid reputation as being a professional, reliable and accommodating crew that is truly passionate about what we do.
If you are searching for the opportunity to immerse yourself in a truly authentic deep sea fishing experience . Then you have most certainly come to the right place. Rest assured that with Our Fishing Charters you won’t be sat quietly next to a lake, catching the occasional Trout. In fact we are all about providing quality deep sea reef and game fishing charter s ! That means powering out into the deep blue ocean on one of our purpose built charter boats, and catching some quality fish!
Take An Offshore Fishing Adventure With GC Fishing Charters
To us deep sea fishing gold coast is much more than just a hobby. Moreover it’s a lifestyle! – and we are dedicated to sharing our experience with both seasoned fishermen and amateurs alike. We don’t mind if you have absolutely no experience with fishing at all. Additionally, nothing excites us more than having the opportunity to introduce new people to this truly magnificent sport.
When it comes to fishing trips the Gold Coast really is one of the best spots in the world – you’ll be looking at catching the likes of Snapper , Yellowfin Tuna , Spanish Mackerel , Cobia , Wahoo , Mahi mahi and many more other exciting species.
Trust Us For Your Gold Coast Fishing Trip
We’ve a number of different packages for you to choose from. These include full day and half day deep sea fishing Gold coast packages. and group events like birthdays and buck parties! As a Gold Coast Fishing Charters company we supply absolutely everything; all you need to do is turn up and get involved. We can organize customized catering for you upon request; however you are all more than welcome to bring your own snacks and beverages to see you through the day – don’t forget the sunscreen and a pair of sunnies too!
