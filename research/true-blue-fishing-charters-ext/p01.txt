About us 
True Blue Fishing Charters - Gold Coast Fishing Charters
Founded in 1997, True Blue Fishing Charters was the result of one mans passion for fishing and boating. Let us help you discover the beauty of the ocean, its inhabitants and its mystery. 
From beginners to experts we’re here to connect you with the sea and the big fish that call it home! For nearly 20 years we have been industry leaders when it comes to Deep Sea Fishing Charters Gold Coast. Our friendly professional Skippers and deckhands are experienced and fully qualified to safely operate our vessels in offshore conditions. 
We have successfully fished the Gold coasts local reefs for many years and our crews have many years experience in both the recreational and commercial fishing sectors. 
We are located just 10 minutes from Surfers Paradise , and one hour from Brisbane . 
The Spit Pontoon, 8 Muriel Henchman Drive, Main Beach Qld.
