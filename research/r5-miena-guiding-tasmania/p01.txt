Experience the beauty of Tasmania
Celebrating the Tasmanian Central
Highlands is what we are all about.
We aim to show our guests how we can tread lightly on
this planet and make a lasting contribution to our local
community, guided by the right ethos and endeavour,
while remembering our past and present.
Immerse yourself in nature and experience the
unique and protected environment of this Tasmanian
Wilderness World Heritage area. Fully guided and
supported day or multi-day walks in the Central
Highlands, Western Lakes, and Walls of Jerusalem
National Park. Private bookings are available.
