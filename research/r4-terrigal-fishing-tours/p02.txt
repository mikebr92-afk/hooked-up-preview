Fishing Charter Options
Private Charter
You can book the boat for your own group. A Private Charter is a great idea for corporate functions, birthdays, parties or anytime you want to get a group of friends together to enjoy fishing on the NSW Central Coast. A private Charter options give you exclusive use of vessel and allows for flexible departure times.
Book Private Fishing Charter 
Shared Charter
Our fishing charters can be booked on a shared basis. This means you don’t need to worry about getting a whole group together. We do have a minimum number of 5 people required for the tour to go ahead. If you book, we will do our best to fill the seats. If we are unable to fill the tour and we cancel you are entitled to a full refund. If travelling, don’t book your accommodation until a full boat is confirmed. Please note the minimum age for a shared charter is 8. If our online booking system is not showing availability for the days you require, please call on 0449999868 or email bookings@terrigaltours.com.au.
Book Shared Fishing Charter 
Young Guns Kids Charter
What a perfect birthday or holiday activity for kids. A kid friendly fishing charter.
Give your kids a real fishing charter experience in a safe and educational environment. Enjoy some fishing action with your friends. Learn about sustainable fishing and the marine environment.
We depart from the delightful seaside village of Hardys Bay. This gives us access to a range of habitats including estuaries, bays and the offshore reefs and wrecks of the Bouddi Peninsula and their bountiful fish. Depending on the confidence of your crew, we can head offshore, or we can stay within the bay for some calm water fishing around the headlands and estuaries. With plenty of options for a great day of fishing.
Duration 5 Hours.
Target species include; Snapper / Flathead / Bream / Jewfish / Kingfish and Tailor.
We supply quality rods, reels and bait.
You can bring your own equipment if you prefer.
All you need to bring is your own food & drink, hat,
camera & sunnies. Also bring along a cooler bag to take your cleaned fish home.
