Come fishing on board the Central Coast's premier charter boats with Terrigal Fishing Tours. Terrigal Fishing Tours is a locally, family-owned and operated fishing charter business operating at Terrigal and Hardys Bay on the beautiful Central Coast of New South Wales. Located just 60 minutes north of Sydney.
The professional and friendly crew aim to provide you with the best charter experience on our fully equipped, purpose-built fishing vessel, Skye 1. They cater for both experienced anglers and people new to the sport. If you are new to fishing, the crew will be happy to assist you and give you a range of skills to take home.
Terrigal Fishing Tours also provides for Corporate Groups, Whale Watching, Party Cruises and Family Fun Days.
