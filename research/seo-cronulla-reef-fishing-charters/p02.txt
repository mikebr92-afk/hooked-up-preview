Vessel Romanda
• Home 
• Vessel Romanda
Romanda was built specifically for charter fishing and has been operating and providing customers with that enjoyable fishing adventure for over 20 years.
Features
• Romanda is 7.5 metres in length & travels at 20 knots which is an ideal speed for getting to and from our fishing grounds.
• With plenty of room on the deck making it easy to move around the boat and fish in any condition.
• There is ample seating, a cover over the rear of the boat, and a toilet for your convenience.
• Romanda carries the latest in electronic equipment such as GPS, Radar, Auto Pilot and Depth Sounder.
• Romanda carries all the required emergency equipment including Life jackets, Life rafts and 406 EPIRB.
• Romanda is in N.S.W. Maritime survey 2C for 8 persons and 2d for 9 persons and is a licenced Charter Fishing Boat, is fully insured.
• Electrics, accessories, and tackle are expected to be found on Cronulla reef fishing charters.
• 6x estuary/ egi rods with Daiwa 4000 exist reels.
• 10x 6-10 kg taipan fishing rods with 6000 to 10000 Shimano Socorro reels.
• 4x 50w tiagra on 24kg ultra rods
• Live bait 50 baits capacity.
• Relax 4in tuna tubes.
• 4.5m outriggers.
• 4x strobe lights for bait collection.
• Scotty mega Downrigger.
• Pakula dredge with dredge arm.
• Romanda just had an upgrade to the 21st century with Simrad electronics package, Simrad NSS EVO 12-inch screen, C-MAPS, A halo 24in radar with bird mode and dual range, 1kw LHW chirp transducer. Lonestar marine anchor winch with 200m of 6mm Dyneema 7500kg rated anchor line.
• Gas bbq stainless marine grade available.
• Marine toilet (head) seawater deck wash and fresh water hand wash basin. VHF radio, Bluetooth head unit with deck speakers.
• Fridge available onboard for drinks
• Romanda is powered by twin honda 135hp outboards.
• That has just done their major engine service and is in amazing condition for her hours.
