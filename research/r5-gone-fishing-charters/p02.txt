Seasonal Queenscliff Fishing Charters
Gone Fishing Charters run Fishing Charters from Queenscliff Victoria, just outside Geelong. Our Queenscliff Fishing Charters head out to Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait where we focus on fishing a variety of seasonal fish, even Mako shark during the warmer months. 
We offer fishing charters in Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait and our charters depart from Queenscliff. Portsea Pier pickup is also availble for private bookings. Check out the fish species for the range of commonly caught fish in Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait.
Our Queenscliff fishing trips are available as shared or private charters and range for 5 to 8 hours. They are ideal for beginners to experienced fishers and make an idea gift!

Our Skipper & Boat
Gone Fishing’s Skipper Chris is a qualified charter operator with over 20 years experience in the industry and has fished extensively in Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait. His knowledge of these areas has been gained through hands-on experience by being out on the water almost every day of the year. Chris’s fresh approach and experience make for a fishing trip long to be remembered.
Our Queenscliff fishing charters operate on our 10.5m custom built vessel Poseidon, the vessel was built with maximum fish-ability in mind!

What’s been catching
Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait treat us to an array of fish species to target throughout the year, ranging from Gummy Shark, King George Whiting, Snapper, Yellowtail Kingfish, Assorted Reef Species and mako shark during the warmer weather. Visit What’s Biting to find out what’s available this time of year or follow our socials.
