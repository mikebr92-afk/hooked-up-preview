Soak in the Breathtaking Beauty of the Great Barrier Reef in Port Douglas!
Welcome aboard Atlas and Reef Therapy, custom built vessels designed for speed, comfort and stability at the forefront of boating technology – ready to get you to your desired destination FAST ensuring maximum fishing time.
Whether it’s the inshore reefs, a quick trip to the Low Isles or cruising the estuary systems – the Stabicraft 609 Frontier or our Ocean Blue centre console, will get you there and offer a sense of security along the way. Vessels boast top of the line rod and reels, electric anchor winches, the latest electronics, shade canopies and comfortable seating. Parents can relax while on-board as both vessels are designed with high sides to keep the little ones safe.
