FISHING: Program 
WRECK & FERN 
Get out on the water and explore some of the deeper reefs and wrecks of Keppel Bay. This trip is perfect for anglers looking to target Red Emperor, Goldband Snapper, Large Mouth Nannygai, Coral Trout, and a variety of other reef species.
​ 
• 
Mixed Group Wreck & Fern - 9+ hours – $500 per person
• 
Private Wreck & Fern Charter - 9+ hours, – $5000
​ 
Our experienced crew will help you maximise your catch while enjoying the stunning scenery and the thrill of deep
reef fishing.
Book Now 
REEF & SHOALS 
Head offshore to the region's prime reef and shoal systems for a full day fishing adventure. Perfect for anglers chasing Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Spanish Mackerel, and a variety of other prized reef species, this trip combines exciting fishing with the beauty of Keppel Bay's offshore waters.
​ 
• 
Mixed Group Reef & Shoals - 9+ hours - $600 per person
• 
Private Reef & Shoals Charter - 9+ hours - $6000
​ 
Our experienced crew will guide you to the best fishing spots, offering tips and support to help you land the catch of a lifetime, all while enjoying a comfortable, well-equipped vessel.
Book Now 
EXTENDED REEF & SHOALS 
For the ultimate fishing adventure, our Extended Reef & Shoals trip is our most extreme offering. Choose from 18 to 36 hours on the water and experience fishing in untouched grounds that few have explored.
​ 
Depending on conditions, we may take you from Heron Reef to the Swains, or up the coast to Island Head and beyond, chasing big reef species and the thrill of offshore fishing.
This trip is perfect for anglers seeking adventure, variety, and the chance to land a trophy catch.
​ 
For prices and availability, please contact us directly.
