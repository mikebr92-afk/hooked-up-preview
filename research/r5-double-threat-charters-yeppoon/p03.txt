DOUBLE THREAT CHARTERS YEPPOON 
Owned and operated by Tim and Jess Price since 2017, Double Threat Charters is built on a love for fishing, great company, and the beautiful waters of Keppel Bay.
​ 
Tim and Jess pride themselves on delivering quality service, unforgettable fishing adventures, and a modern take on home-style cooking — served as fresh, bento-style meals onboard. Whether you're chasing trophy fish or enjoying a relaxed day on the water, they'll make you feel right at home with great food, good company, and genuine local hospitality.

SKIPPER 
TIM PRICE 
Double Threat Charters is led by experienced skipper Tim Price.
​ 
Having grown up on charter boats running out of Gladstone, Tim brings a lifetime of local knowledge and the expertise needed to make every trip unforgettable.
​ 
Passionate about both fishing and the reef, Tim loves sharing his experience with others — from seasoned anglers to first-timers. He's not just about catching fish, but about teaching and helping guests land the catch of a lifetime.

FISHIN' IMPOSSIBLE 
Our vessel, Fishing Impossible, is a 2006 Leisurecat 3500 Deepwater — purpose-built for comfort, stability, and serious fishing adventures.
​ 
Designed tough for offshore conditions, she features a fully enclosed cabin with comfortable seating inside and out, ensuring a dry and comfortable ride no matter the weather.
​ 
Onboard you'll find all the essentials — a fridge, stereo, modern electronics to help us find the fish, and of course, the most important feature: a toilet.
​ 
Whether it's a full day chasing reef species or a relaxed cruise around Keppel Bay, Fishing Impossible is built to get you there safely and comfortably.
