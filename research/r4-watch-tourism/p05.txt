About
Welcome to Five Island Fishing Charters, located in Wollongong, Australia. Five Island Charters specialises in game, sports, and reef fishing. With the stunning Wollongong coastline as our playground, every day is a good day for fishing!
Our vessel, Seafox, is a 40ft flybridge cruiser that can sleep up to 6 people plus crew. It is fully equipped with all the amenities of home, including a double room, 2 bunks, large lounges, shower, toilet, and a fully equipped kitchen. We aim to provide you with a comfortable and enjoyable experience on the water.
On our offshore trips, we target a variety of reef species, as well as exciting game fish such as Mako Shark, Mahi Mahi, Sailfish, Wahoo, Spanish mackerel, Black, Blue and Striped Marlin, and Albacore, Yellowfin, and Bluefin Tuna. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a beginner, we have the perfect trip catered to your skills and interests.
One of the advantages of fishing with Five Island Charters is that no fishing licence is required in NSW on our charters. We provide all the rods, reels, tackle, and bait you'll need for a successful day of fishing. Additionally, our experienced and friendly crew are always there to assist you and ensure your safety and comfort.
We offer a range of charter packages to suit different preferences and budgets. Our Reef Charter, priced at $175 per person, includes all the necessary equipment, as well as a delicious BBQ lunch. Charters depart from Wollongong Harbour at 6am and return at midday.
For those looking for an afternoon fishing adventure during daylight savings, our Afternoon Reef Charter is the perfect choice. Priced at $140 per person, this charter includes all the equipment needed and departs from Wollongong Harbour at 2pm, returning at 7pm.
Game fishing enthusiasts can opt for our Game Fishing Charter, priced at $2000 for the entire charter, accommodating up to 6 people. We provide quality rods, reels, lures, and even breakfast and lunch to enhance your fishing experience.
If you prefer sports fishing, our Sports Fishing Charter is priced at $1400 and includes all the necessary equipment, as well as a delicious BBQ lunch. Charters depart from Wollongong Harbour at 5am for an 8-hour charter with a maximum of 6 people.
For those looking to try deep dropping, our Deep Dropping Charter is priced at $2500. This charter includes all the equipment, as well as a BBQ breakfast and lunch. Charters depart from Wollongong Harbour at 5am and have a minimum duration of 10 hours, accommodating a maximum of 6 people.
Don't just take our word for it, check out our testimonials from satisfied customers who have had a great time fishing with us. We pride ourselves on providing a memorable and enjoyable fishing experience for all.
Whether you're a fishing enthusiast or just looking for a unique experience on the water, Five Island Fishing Charters is the perfect choice in Wollongong. Book your adventure with us today and get ready for an unforgettable day or week of fishing!
