Frequently Asked Questions
Got questions about our fishing charter operations, we have the answers 
• 
Does a skipper go with you? 
Yes, Cooper Walker is your guide on every trip
• 
Do you provide bait ? 
Yes – all bait provided, Cooper uses all bait types from traditional bait to live bait and all types of lures, vibes, hardbodies and soft plastics
• 
What kind of fish is there to catch ? 
All estuary fish such as Jewfish, Kingfish, Flathead, Snapper, Bream, Whiting, Tailor, Australian Salmon, and occasionally unusual species such as Dolphin Fish and Cobia come into the lake
• 
Where can we take the boat on Lake Macquarie ? 
We travel all through Lake Macquarie from the Channel to Wangi, Pulbah Island to Gwandalan trying out all Coopers secret spots and following the bat and fish as they travel around in the different seasons
• 
Where do I get onto the boat ? 
Main pickup spot is Kilaben Bay public jetty, We are able to meet you at most other public jetties depending on your location.
• 
Can we book extra time? 
Depending on availably extra time can be added at $120ph payable at end of trip
• 
Payment terms? 
All charters must be paid before commencement of trip
• 
How many people fit on the boat ? 
Maximum 4 customers plus skipper
• 
Do we need to bring food? 
Cooper has water, soft drinks and snacks on board but feel free to bring any extra food and drink you want
• 
Can we bring alcohol? 
No, is not encouraged due to the extra risk of injury and gear breakage
• 
Are we guaranteed to catch a big fish ? 
Cooper always tries his best across over a thousand potential locations but the simple fact is that if the fish are not biting there is not much you can do. That is why it is called fishing rather than catching!
