Game & Tournament Charter
Game fishing is the ultimate experience for everyone who loves to fish. It is exciting, memorable and the most exhilarating.
Game Fishing from Port Stephens offers the very best fishing experience. Port Stephens enjoys a pristine environment and the clusters of offshore islands create ample opportunities for a great days fishing. Fishing the Continental Shelf from Port Stephens offers anglers the opportunity to fish for Marlin, Bill Fish, Hammerhead Sharks, Trag and other fighting fish.
When you board Hammerhead II for your Port Stephens Game Fishing adventure you are on a vessel fully equipped to meet your every need. Hammerhead has a full range of rods, reels and bait for all your game fishing needs.Stand up gear provided.
Our experienced crew can take you to all the top fishing spots maximising your chances of a hook up and catch.
Annually the NSW Game Fishing Tournament is held in Port Stephens and Hammerhead Boat Charters is available to host you and your party in this prestigious event. When you Charter Hammerhead II you will be guided by our experienced captains, have all the gear and safety equipment to have the best chance at hooking up on your dream fish.
Game/Tournament Fishing Charter 
9 Hours
Numbers: We are able to take a maximum of 8 people based on NSW law. 
Time: 9 hours (i.e. 7am-4pm) 
Cost: These are private charters- POA .(Additional time charged per hour) 
For more information on how to book this charter experience fill out the online contact enquiry or call us on 0498 479 420 . We can’t wait to see you aboard Hammerhead Charters.
Please note for all charters booked online or by phone through Hammerhead Charters, we require bookings in advance to ensure your charter experience is professionally organised. Full payment is required at this time and 100% refundable only if the charter is cancelled at the captain’s discretion. 25% deposit is required to secure all bookings (no refund if cancelled 14 days prior to charter). Full payment prior to all charters. 100% refund if cancelled by unfavourable weather conditions.
