Estuary Experience
If deep-sea fishing is not the experience for you, why not try our private estuary experiences. Hammerhead Charters can guide you and your friends on an estuary adventure to explore the best spots around Port Stephens.
Within Port Stephens you will have the chance to catch a variety of fish species without needing to venture into the open ocean. We have all the equipment you will need, from bait to lures, rods and reels. This charter experience is perfect for beginner anglers and those who only have a few hours in Nelson Bay and would like to fish.
If fishing is not your thing, relax and let us guide you around the beautiful waters of Port Stephens. Look out for the dolphins, or just simply sit back and enjoy a leisurely picnic and a drink BYO.
Take advantage of the fresh seafood on site and treat your special person/s to a relaxing private river cruise.
Curent Price is $780 for 3 hours, for up to 6 persons. Any additional people will cost $95 per person, additional hours are $200 per hour. There is a maximum of 12 people.
. Prices may vary due to fuel price instability. Contact us for current pricing.
For more information on how to book this charter experience fill out the online contact enquiry or call us on 0498 479 420 . We can’t wait to see you aboard Hammerhead Charters.
Please note for all charters booked online or by phone through Hammerhead Charters, we require bookings in advance to ensure your charter experience is professionally organised. Full payment is required at this time and 100% refundable only if the charter is cancelled at the captain’s discretion. 25% deposit is required to secure all bookings (no refund if cancelled 14 days prior to charter). Full payment prior to all charters. 100% refund if cancelled by unfavourable weather conditions.
