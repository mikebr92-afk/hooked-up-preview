Fly fishing locations in Tasmania
Learn more about Tasmania's fly fishing locations, including in depth information, video overviews, access and fishing tips - Great Lake, Penstock Lagoon, Little Pine and more.
Fly Fishing at Currawong Lakes in Tasmania
Currawong Lakes is a managed private fishery of three interconnected lakes on the eastern edge of Tasmania's highland country, offering clear water, wild brown trout and reliable seasonal hatches.
learn more 
Fly Fishing at Meander River in Tasmania
Meander River fly fishing in Tasmania offers wild brown trout, stunning scenery, and easy access near Deloraine. Learn when to fish, top flies, and gear tips.
learn more 
Fly Fishing at Tyenna River in Tasmania
Deep dive the Tyenna River, one of Tasmania’s best fly-fishing spots near Hobart. Learn when to fish, what to bring, and plan your next adventure with Trout Tales.
learn more 
Fly Fishing at Little Pine Lagoon in Tasmania
Learn more about fly fishing at Little Pine Lagoon in Tasmania's Central Highlands, including its stunning backdrop and excellent fishing conditions. 
learn more 
Fly Fishing at Great Lake in Tasmania
Learn more about fly fishing at Great Lake, Tasmania - a popular and well known spot amongst both visiting and local anglers.

Fly Fishing at Arthurs Lake in Tasmania
Get the full run down of fly fishing at Arthurs Lake, Tasmania, including angler insights and our popular video overview. 
learn more 
Fly Fishing at Bronte Lagoon in Tasmania‍
Bronte Lagoon is a great fly fishing in Tasmania spot, often sheltered from harsher conditions in the highlands - learn more about it in our location overview. 
learn more 
Fly Fishing At Penstock Lagoon in Tasmania
Needing no introduction, fly fishing at Penstock Lagoon is ever common among anglers in Tasmania - known for both its wet and dry fly options.
