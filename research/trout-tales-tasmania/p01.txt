About Trout Tales
"Hey, Matt here - welcome to Trout Tales."
With a wealth of experience as fishing guides, we provide tailored fly fishing tours all across Tasmania. 
Based in Hobart and Miena, our packages are for all anglers - whether it's your first time picking up a rod or you've spent years on the water. 
about trout tales 
What's included in your fly fishing tour 
Our all-inclusive Tasmania fly fishing tours include everything you need for that memorable day on the water, including:
• All fly fishing gear, including premium rods, reels, waders and boots, locally tied flies
• Transport to and from Hobart or Miena, in the Central Highlands
• Lunch and refreshments across the day
• Access to Tasmania's best fishing waters, including our exclusively accessed private waters
