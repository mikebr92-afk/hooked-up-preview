Fishing Charters 
Without doubt the best variety of species and most diverse fishery anywhere in the country. Come fishing in Weipa.
Over the last 34 years I have fished and lived in some of the remotest parts of Australia and the South pacific, and have found nowhere that is as consistently as good as Weipa. 
Prevailing South easterly winds blowing offshore for 9 months of the year and so many different species to target, there is always something to keep the keen angler busy and interested. With a myriad of different species available to both fly and lure our fishing trips strive to make your fishing holiday a memorable one. From chasing GT's and queenies on poppers to finessing a permit or a blue bastard to eat your crab fly in a foot of water, or casting lures in the river trying to tempt a barramundi, it is all available. Our custom fitted vessels experienced and knowledgeable guides make sure you will have an exciting and awesome holiday. Fly fishing to lure casting to live bait fishing we have all of the expertise and background required to ensure your fishing holiday will also be a valuable learning experience. 
We operate between April and Nov and during that time for the last 12yrs have lost just 3 days to weather which would be unheard of anywhere else.
The expansive estuary systems and blue water environment all within easy access of your luxurious lodge allow for both comfort and remoteness.
WEIPA FISH SPECIES 
Without doubt the best variety of species and most diverse fishery anywhere in the country
Barramundi
Lates Calcarifer 
A great fighting fish and a very highly prized target fish. Almost everyone that comes to North Queensland wants to catch a "barra".
Button 
Blue Bastard
Diagramma pictum 
A sought after target for fly fishermen as they sometimes tail in shallow water and grub along in the sand looking for small crustaceans.
Button 
Blue Salmon
Eleutheronema tetradactylum 
The Blue Salmons colour is silver bluish green in appearance along the back and flanks this progresses to a shiny silver white belly area. The Blue salmon differs from the King Salmon having shorter thread like filaments and only up to four which are located below the pectoral fins.
Button 
Cobia
Rachycentron canadus 
Many anglers rate cobia as one of the best fighters in the ocean, generally giving it their all right up until you get a firm hold on them. They can perform extraordinary runs at speed, but are best known and respected for their sheer doggedness. And they are first-class on the plate too.
Button 
Diamond Trevally
Alectis indica 
Button 
Giant Herring
Elops hawaiensis 
The Giant Herring can be recognised by its olive and silvery colour,slender body, relatively large mouth, single dorsal fin and strong forked tail. A spectacular fighting fish which can dazzle an angler with its speed and amazing jumps.
Button 
Golden Trevally
Gnathanodon speciosus 
The "goldy" as it sometimes affectionately called is prolific along the Weipa shoreline. And sometimes showing up in large schools providing a fun target for both fly fishing and the conventional spinfisherman. Most of the bigger specimens however are taken jigging soft plastics or with live baits on the offshore reefs.
Button 
Tarpon
Megalops cyprinoids 
Truthfully known as ox eye herring being of the same family of Megalops as the atlantic version "Megalops atlanticus" which grows to 300lb this little dynamite growing to about 5kg is no less worthy and will give an energetic airborne account of itself with many gill rattling jumps and shakes.
Button 
Giant Trevally
Caranx Ignobilis 
The GT is a ferocious and dogged fighter and can grow to 200lb.In Weipa most of the GT's caught are not quite that big however they are no less tenacious and will certainly pull your string.
Button 
King Salmon
Polydactylus macrochir 
Or sometimes called threadfin salmon found in all of Weipa's Albatross bay rivers are great fun on a rod and provide some exciting arial acrobatics and a nice feed back at the fishing lodge as they are possibly one of the top 5 table fish available around Weipa.
Button 
Permit
Trachinotus blochi 
Are quite prolific along the coastline around Weipa and are prized as a flyrod target. They can be quite difficult and sometimes frustrate the angler with their unwillingness to eat the fly. Conditions need to be right but they are a very catchable quarry for a good angler.
Button 
Mangrove Jack
Lutjanus argentimaculatus 
As with the other well known Lutjanid species the fingermark (Lutjanis johnii) we catch around Weipa and Cape York, these species prefer dense cover and will keep you on your toes as they have a tendency to grab the lure or fly as they are returning to that cover. Your reflexes had better be quick.
Button 
Queenfish
Scomberoides commersonnianus 
A very exciting sportfish and what tropical fishing is all about. The queenie grows to over 20kg provides a heap of fun for all that hook them. One of our most abudant species both along the shore and offshore they pull hard and jump and do all the things that a great sportfish are expected to do.
Button 
Longtail Tuna
Thunnus tonggol 
Previously called northern bluefin tuna, longtails are one of the most prolific species in the area during April – July and one of the most sought after sport fishing targets due to their speed and their willingness to take a lure or fly. The longtail is warm blooded like most tunas and has speed and energy to burn.
Button 
Spanish Mackerel
Scomberomorus commerson 
Fast and furious this high speed predator will peel off line in seconds with its blistering pace. Don't get in the way of those teeth either, they can cut through flesh like a razor blade. 
Spaniards are here all year round, however, they are more prevalent inshore in the winter months.
Button 
Milkfish
Chanos chanos 
Considered one of the most challenging species to catch on a fly rod the milky is not only very difficult to tempt with a fly as it has such a diverse diet, including plankton but is also an awesomely powerful sportfish.
Button 
OUR WEIPA FISHING GUIDES 
Weipa Fly and Sport Fishing use only highly experienced guides.
"Bargy" 
Mark Bargenquast 
Owner / Operator 
After operating his own guiding business in Hervey bay for almost ten years, Bargy sold up and came to Weipa in 2010 and hasn't looked back since. Having an intimate knowledge of the area its rare you go back to the ramp empty handed after spending the day with him. An equally passionate hunter, you won't find Bargy without a rod or rifle in his hands on a day off. 
"Skirt" 
Kurt Rowlands 
Guide 
Another former Hervey Bay guide, Kurt started our season is 2022 and moved up not long after and has been a full-time guide since. Growing up fishing the Sandy Straits he has experience fishing for anything and everything and is an equally passionate fly tier. If he isn't guiding you'll most certainly find him on the water chasing everything from Blue Bastards to Barra and everything in between. 
"Jacko" 
Jackson Bargenquast 
Guide 
Young gun guide 'Jacko' grew up fishing with Bargy in Hervey Bay chasing everything that swam from marlin to bass. He's also a passionate bowhunter and loves the outdoor lifestyle. For a young bloke you will be blown away from the information he has to share about the local wildlife and fishing in general, you will think you're fishing with someone double his age and experience level. 
"Richo" 
Jack Richards 
Guide 
Another young passionate fisherman Jack brings a laid back relaxed attitude but gets it done on the fishing front. Growing up also in the Hervey Bay area but moving to Gladstone and the Mackay area he has plenty of experience fishing a range of techniques and scenarios. Jack helps us out over the super busy periods for about 3 months over the year. 
"Platzy" 
Gavin Platz 
Guide 
A man that needs no introduction, a true legend of the Saltwater fly fishing scene in Australia. A man that has over 30 years guiding experience, he has recently 'retired' and what better way to retire than to guide part time In Weipa! An exceptional guide, fly casting tutor and all-round bloke, he has been an awesome part of the team helping for the busy periods for us. 
"Scooter" 
Justin Duggan 
Guide 
Scooter has been coming up doing extensive stints in Weipa since 2009 and has guided clients to many trophy captures since! Regarded as one of the best fly casters in Australia he is also a great tutor for your own fly casting as well. Flyfishing isn't where it stops but, he hasamazing knowledge of all things conventional fishing as well, you won't leave the boat not having learnt something or improved your fishing!
