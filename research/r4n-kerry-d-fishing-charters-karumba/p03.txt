Fishing Charters
Our charters are the perfect way to experience world-class fishing in a relaxed, comfortable and safe environment.
We operate 7 days a week, all year around (weather permitting) so you are sure to find the perfect time slot to suit you and your group.
Estuary Charters
Explore the smooth, sheltered waters of the Norman River, Bynoe, Broncos or Brannigans .
Perfect for those who enjoy calmer conditions while still chasing some of the Gulf’s most exciting sport fish.
Target prized estuary species such as:
• Barramundi
• Threadfin King & Blue Salmon
• Black Jew
• Grunter
Book your charter 
Offshore Charters
Head up to 40–50 km out into the Gulf of Carpentaria for a true blue water adventure. Offshore trips give you the chance to hook into some of the Gulf’s most sought-after species including:
• Spanish Mackerel
• Cobia
• Black Jew
• Grunter
• Fingermark
• Trevally
• Queenfish
• & much more 
Book your charter 
Half Day Charters 
Times: 7:00 am – 12:00 pm OR 12:00 pm – 5:00 pm (5-hour trips, two available daily)
Inclusions: All top-quality Penn fishing gear , bait, tackle, morning or afternoon tea, water, and soft drinks BYO Alcohol (Cans Only)
Full Day Charters
Times: 7:00 am – 4:00 pm
Inclusions: All top-quality Penn fishing gear , bait, tackle, morning, lunch, water, and soft drinks, BYO Alcohol (Cans Only)
Book your charter 
Barramundi Charters Full Day ONLY
For anglers chasing the ultimate Barra experience , we offer specialised Barramundi charters limited to a maximum of 4 people for a more focused and personalised trip.
• Techniques: Casting lures, bait fishing, and trolling
• Season: Available February to April and again from September through to October 7th 
Private Charters
For a personalised experience, book a private charter and have the boat to yourselves. Perfect for families, groups of friends, or special occasions. We’ll tailor the trip to suit your needs — estuary or offshore.
