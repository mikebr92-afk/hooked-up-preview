What to Bring
We want to make sure you have the trip of a lifetime, which means making sure that you come well prepared!
Here’s a short list of things to make sure you don’t forget to bring;
• Personal bits & bobs 
• Sunscreen
• Hat & sunnies
• Camera (if you require it)
• Weather-appropriate clothing
• Any specific medications/dietary options
• Footwear (optional)
If there’s anything else you would like to bring but not sure if you’re able to, please contact us!
