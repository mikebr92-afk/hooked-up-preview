Proline Fishing Charters (Sorrento)
Between the months of October & April, our boat Strait Shooter fishes the reef system around Port Phillip Heads, and ventures into Bass Strait when conditions permit.
Catches on these fishing trips include Snapper, Whiting, Sweep, King George Whiting, Trevally, Kingfish, Bass Strait Flathead, Calamari, Shark and other reef fish too numerous to list.
All 5hr fishing trips are fully inclusive of all rods, Bait, Tackle, tea & coffee and a sausage sizzle BBQ. 8 & 10hr exclusive fishing trips include a salad and BBQ lunch.
Located at Berth 1, Sorrento Pier.
