Ace Fishing Charters (Hastings)
Our aim is to take you to the best Fishing Spots in Westernport and Bass Strait and provide an enjoyable days fishing. With over thirty years of fishing behind us, many of them chartering in Port Phillip Bay, Westernport and Bass Strait. Ace Fishing Charters have the knowledge, equipment and expertise to provide an enjoyable fishing trip for both the novice and serious fisherperson. 
We supply: All Quality Rods, Reels, Bait & Tackle, Refreshments Supplied on all Trips, Barbeque and Toilet on board. 
Depart from Westernport Marina, Hastings.
