Impulse Charters (Queenscliff)
Impulse Charters offers fishing trips and bay cruises departing from Queenscliff. The business operates the Teri-Lyn, a 34-foot traditional timber fishing boat equipped for fishing on Port Phillip Bay. Half-day and full-day charters are available, and bay cruises are also offered. The trips are fun and family-friendly.
Fishing trips include quality bait and tackle, and the crew provides full tuition. The boat has a toilet on board and a BBQ, and fish are cleaned and bagged on return. A BBQ lunch can be supplied on the full-day option, and gift vouchers are available.
Opening Hours:
Trips operate by booking. Contact Impulse Charters for current trip times and availability.
Cost:
See the current Impulse Charters prices and trip details .
