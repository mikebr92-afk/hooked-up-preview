Wodonga - Indulgence Flyfishing
Our fully guided fly fishing tours of Victoria's North-East mountain streams, rivers and lakes are the perfect getaway. Mind you, we do more than just fishing... Choose between gourmet rolls or four star dining by the river. No matter what your taste, we can arrange a unique experience just for you:
• Outings are individually designed to suit your needs. 
• From beginner levels to highly experienced. 
• Fine dining to light snack. 
• From half a day to a week or longer . 
• Individuals, couples and corporate groups are welcome. Operating from Albury/Wodonga, Indulgence Flyfishing is perfectly located on the Hume Highway between Melbourne and Sydney. We are surrounded by some of Australia's premier trout rivers and streams - the Kiewa, Mitta Mitta and Buckland Rivers and Nariel Creek just to name a few. We are also on the doorstep of extremely productive fishing lakes such as Dartmouth Dam.
Not only is the fishing good... but we are also located next to the world famous wineries of the King Valley and Millawa regions. No matter what kind of location you are after, and regardless of your current proficency, Indulgence Flyfishing can make sure you have an unforgettable experience of great food, wine... oh, and did we mention flyfishing?
Included in our packages are all on stream meals, flies and expendables so that you can focus on the important things... catching that prize trout, or maybe just lazing beside a river thinking about how good life is. No matter what your needs, we are happy to arrange a package that suits you.
