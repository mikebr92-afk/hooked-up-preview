MV AMBITION…
Ambition is a purpose built 11.6 metre Game Fishing Boat built to target the Oceanic Game Fish; Marlin, Tuna and all the other great ocean wanderers.
The AMBITION is equipped with the latest Fishing Tackle and Electronics and owner/skipper Ivan, has fished extensively along the NSW coast between Bermagui and Port Stephens. For more years than he wants to admit Ivan has been chasing Marlin of all three species as well as Tuna, Wahoo and in fact all the Game Fish found along our East Coast. Much of this time was spent competitively fishing with the Sydney Game Fishing Club winning and placing in many tournaments.
Since 2004 Ivan has been Chartering Ambition introducing both novice and experienced anglers to the exciting sport of Game fishing.
So if you want to catch the fish of a lifetime – come and join me…
