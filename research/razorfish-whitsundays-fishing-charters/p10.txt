TERMS AND Conditions 
Cancellation Policies: 
• Private Charters Cancellation Policy: Cancellations made less than 7-days prior to departure will result in complete forfeiture of the charter. If your charter is cancelled by your skipper due to unsafe weather conditions and circumstances out of our control, you are entitled to a date change (depending on availability and weather conditions) or a refund.
• Shared Charters Cancellation Policy: Cancellations made less than 48-hours prior to departure will result in complete forfeiture of the full price. If your charter is cancelled by your skipper due to unsafe weather conditions and circumstances out of our control, you are entitled to a date change (depending on availability and weather conditions) or a refund.
Any loss or damage to tackle and the vessel will incur replacement or repair cost.
