Frequently Asked Questions
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW BEFORE YOUR FISHING CHARTER
Who is welcome aboard Razorfish? 
Our charters cater to everyone — from solo fishers and couples joining a shared trip to private groups like corporate teams, families, bucks/ hens parties, or mates on a getaway. Kids 12 and under must wear a lifejacket while onboard. 
Do I need previous fishing experience? 
Not at all! We welcome complete beginners and seasoned anglers. Our friendly crew is here to guide you every step of the way. 
What charter options do you offer? 
We run both Private and Shared Charters , available as: • Half Day ( 5 hours) • Full Day ( 8 hours) 
Shared charters require a minimum of 5 guests to proceed . All trips depart from Cape Gloucester Resort or Airlie Beach , and are tailored to suit weather and fishing conditions. 
What do you provide? 
Every charter includes: 
• Quality bait and tackle 
• All fishing rods, reels, and gear 
• Bottled water and light snacks 
• Iced esky 
• Catch cleaned, filleted, and packed on ice 
• Onboard toilet and amenities 
• Access to a hot plate on board (full day charters only)
What do I need to bring? 
• Hat, sunscreen, sunglasses
• Comfortable sun- safe clothing and footwear 
• Your own lunch and any extra snacks 
• BYO lunch and snacks (light snacks provided)
• BYO alcohol (cans only)
No need to bring an esky — we’ve got that covered. 
How many people can you take? 
Private charters : up to 6 guests , plus skipper and deckhand 
Shared charters : individual bookings up to capacity ( 6 total guests max) 
How do I book? 
You can check live availability and make an instant booking online via our website: razorfishcharters. com. au . 
Prefer to speak to someone? Call Kris on 0437 850 300 or email us at bookings@ razorfishcharters. com. au and we’ll help lock in your trip. 
Find us on Facebook and Instagram 
What happens with what I catch? 
All legal catches are cleaned, filleted, and packed on ice to take home. Bag limits apply under Queensland Fisheries regulations. 
What weather will you not operate in? 
We don’t operate in unsafe conditions such as high winds, rough seas, or storms. You’ll receive confirmation 24 hours before departure based on the latest marine forecast. 
Who operates the boat? 
All trips are operated by a licensed commercial skipper , assisted by an experienced deckhand— both with extensive local knowledge of Whitsundays fishing grounds. 
What food is provided? 
We provide light snacks and bottled water on all charters. 
Guests are welcome to BYO lunch and additional snacks , especially if you feel light snacks won’t be sufficient for your day. 
For full day charters , there’s access to a hot plate onboard — perfect if you’d like to bring sausages or something to cook during your trip. 
Where does the boat go? 
We fish the Whitsunday Islands , inner and outer reefs , and parts of the Great Barrier Reef , with locations chosen based on weather and fish activity. 
Razorfish is based in Airlie Beach , but we can travel to areas like Bowen or Mackay for an additional towing fee. Night charters to the reef or islands are also available on request. 
What days do you operate? 
We operate on demand , year- round , and weather permitting . Early bookings are recommended to secure your preferred date. 
Why choose Razorfish? 
Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters delivers a relaxed, personalised experience with expert local crew, premium gear, and a fast, stable offshore vessel. 
Our 7.5m Sailfish Catamaran reaches up to 40 knots with twin 200hp Mercury outboards , getting you to the fish quickly. It seats up to 8 comfortably , with an open, dry deck built for serious fishing. 
Whether you’re chasing reef species or just out for a great day, we tailor every trip to suit your group. 
What brands of equipment do you provide? 
We use reliable, well- maintained gear from trusted brands including Jarvis Walker — ideal for targeting both reef and pelagic species. 
What is your cancellation policy? 
Private Charters Cancellation Policy: Cancellations made less than 7-days prior to departure will result in complete forfeiture of the charter. If your charter is cancelled by your skipper due to unsafe weather conditions and circumstances out of our control, you are entitled to a date change (depending on availability and weather conditions) or a refund.
Shared Charters Cancellation Policy: Cancellations made less than 48-hours prior to departure will result in complete forfeiture of the full price. If your charter is cancelled by your skipper due to unsafe weather conditions and circumstances out of our control, you are entitled to a date change (depending on availability and weather conditions) or a refund.
Any loss or damage to tackle and the vessel will incur replacement or repair cost.
Gift Vouchers 
Voucher Validity 
Gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
Transferability 
Vouchers are fully transferable if the original recipient cannot attend.
How to Redeem 
Recipients can redeem their voucher by booking online, emailing bookings@razorfishcharters.com.au, or calling Kris directly on 0437 850 300.
Using the Voucher 
If the booking value exceeds the voucher amount, the difference can be paid at the time of booking.
If the booking is less, the remaining balance can be used toward another trip within the validity period.
Refund and Cash Policy 
Gift vouchers are non-refundable and not redeemable for cash.
Delivery Options 
Gift vouchers are sent by email only. No physical or printed vouchers are available.
Use Toward Merchandise 
Vouchers can also be used toward the purchase of Razorfish Fishing Shirts, subject to availability.
