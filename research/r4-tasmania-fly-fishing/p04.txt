Other Tours in and around Launceston
Other Things to Do in Launceston 
Relax, explore, and discover more while you’re here. 
Tasmania Fly Fishing tours are just the beginning. Whether you’re taking a rest day between fishing trips or travelling with friends and family, Launceston offers plenty of things to do nearby — from nature and adventure to food, wine, and local history.
Princes’ Square – Launceston 
Here are a few of our favourite local attractions — perfect for non-anglers, travel companions, or anyone keen to experience more of what Tasmania has to offer.
*****
Penny Royal Adventures – Family Fun at the Gorge 
Located at the entrance to Cataract Gorge, Penny Royal is a unique adventure park that mixes natural beauty with a bit of adrenaline. Try cliff walks, zip-lining, gold panning, ghost rides, or take a relaxing boat cruise.
A great option for kids, couples, or anyone looking for an entertaining afternoon.
Visit Penny Royal 
Cataract Gorge Reserve – A Tassie Classic 
No visit to Launceston is complete without a walk through Cataract Gorge . Just minutes from the city centre, this iconic reserve offers riverside trails, lush gardens, a scenic chairlift, swimming areas, and even wild peacocks.
Relax, stretch your legs, and soak up some of Tasmania’s natural beauty.
Explore Cataract Gorge 
Hollybank Treetops Adventure – Forest from Above 
Just 20 minutes from Launceston, Hollybank is a must-visit for thrill seekers and families alike. Zip-line through the trees, take on the high ropes course, or book a Segway tour through the forest.
Safe, well-run, and suitable for all ages.
Book Hollybank Adventure 
Tamar Valley Wine Tours – Tasmania in a Glass 
Take a day off the rod and explore the Tamar Valley Wine Route . These tours offer curated visits to local vineyards where you can taste premium cool-climate wines, meet the winemakers, and enjoy the rolling countryside.
A laid-back way to discover Tasmania’s wine scene.
Wine Tours Info 
Discover Tasmania Tours – See More Without the Hassle 
Want to visit Cradle Mountain , Freycinet , or Bay of Fires but don’t want to drive? These small-group and private tours are a great way to see more of Tasmania with minimal effort.
Ideal for couples or solo travellers looking for a deeper Tassie experience.
Explore Discover Tasmania Tours 
Callington Mill – A Step into Heritage 
Heading south? Make time for a stop at Callington Mill in Oatlands. This restored 1837 windmill is one of the oldest working mills in Australia, complete with heritage gardens, guided tours, and artisan flour on sale.
A great cultural stop if you’re travelling along the Midlands Highway.
Visit Callington Millentre .

Whether you’re looking for thrills, nature walks, wine tasting, or easy day trips — Launceston has plenty to offer.
