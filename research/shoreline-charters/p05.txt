Local Boat. Local Knowledge.
Whether you’re chasing a dream catch, bonding with mates, or just soaking up time on the water, our fishing trips offer a hands-on, personalised experience for all levels.
With over 40 years of local fishing knowledge , Rob knows these waters like few others. He reads the conditions, follows the fish, and tailors each trip to give you the best chance of success—no matter the weather or your experience level.
You’ll head out on Lila Ray , our quiet, stable 11-metre vessel designed for comfort and calm seas, with plenty of deck space and individual seating.
Whether it’s your first time or your fiftieth, you’ll be supported every step of the way—learning techniques, reading the ocean, and gaining insight from someone who’s spent a lifetime on this coastline. Or if you prefer to go at your own pace, we’ll give you the space to enjoy it in your own rhythm.
This isn’t a generic charter—it’s a true local experience, run by locals, with local knowledge, and a deep respect for the sea.
