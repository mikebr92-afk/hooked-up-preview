Join Us On The Water.
Experience Portland’s wild southern coastline with Shoreline Charters. Whether you're joining a wildlife tour, heading out fishing, or supporting work offshore, our locally crewed, expertly operated vessel is here to get you there—safely and comfortably.
Booking a tour or fishing trip? 
Please select the total number of guests in your group when booking.
To ensure the best experience, we may coordinate smaller groups together. Our trips fill quickly, so we recommend booking early. We’ll do our best to accommodate you and get you out on the water.
Looking to hire our vessel for marine industry work? 
We support a wide range of field operations—from research and documentary filming to crew transfers and offshore surveys. To enquire about private vessel hire or marine support, head to the Marine Industry Projects tab to complete the enquiry form and explore the full list of project types we assist with.
