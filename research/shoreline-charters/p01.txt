Services
Marine Wildlife Tours 
From $100
Portland’s wild coastline is alive with marine activity, where the nutrient-rich waters of the Bonney Upwelling create a thriving ocean ecosystem. From playful seals and soaring seabirds to pods of dolphins weaving through the waves, every tour offers an unforgettable encounter with nature. Shoreline Charters’ purpose-built vessel brings you up close, while our expert crew shares insights into the intricate balance of life that unfolds before your eyes.
Fishing Trips 
From $150
Portland’s nutrient-rich waters are a paradise for anglers, home to prized catches like snapper, bluefin tuna, and gummy shark. Whether you're after a quick 2-hour bay trip or a full-day deep-sea adventure, Shoreline Charters tailors each trip to suit your experience level and fishing goals. With 40 years of expertise in local and Australian waters, Robby knows where to find the best action, ensuring an unforgettable day on the water with top-quality gear and expert guidance.
Marine Industry Projects 
Contact us for pricing 
With decades of experience navigating Portland’s rugged coastline, Shoreline Charters provides expert support for a range of maritime projects. From ecological research and marine surveying to photography and film work, our purpose-built vessel and skilled crew ensure safe and efficient access to offshore environments. Whatever your project demands, we have the knowledge and capability to get you where you need to be. Fill out the form on the marine industry projects tab above and we will get back to you with a quote.
Discover 
our story
With over 40 years of experience in local and Australian waters, Robby Davis has dedicated his life to the sea. His passion extends far beyond fishing—he has a deep respect for the intricate web of life that thrives in Portland’s nutrient-rich waters. From playful seals and soaring seabirds to pods of dolphins and powerful ocean predators, Robby’s greatest joy is sharing this incredible marine environment with others. At Shoreline Charters, we’re committed to bringing guests up close to one of Australia’s most dynamic ecosystems , combining expert knowledge with unforgettable ocean adventures.
