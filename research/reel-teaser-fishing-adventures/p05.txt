Barramundi Fishing Charters
• Our main target species on all of our Kimberley Charters is the elusive Barramundi. However, the highly sought after Barramundi are not the only fish you are likely to come across. Mangrove Jack, Fingermark, Threadfin Salmon and GT’s are just a few of the other fish caught up in the Kimberley on our fishing adventures.
• We mostly target Barramundi by casting lures, but can also use baits and other trolling methods. Barramundi are targeted on the low tides as the creeks and estuaries empty out and the Barramundi have fewer places to hide. Ross has been fishing for Barramundi for over 25 years and has many tips and techniques that he uses to help his clients catch the big ones.
• For more information about our Barramundi Fishing Charters see our Kimberley Barramundi Safari.
