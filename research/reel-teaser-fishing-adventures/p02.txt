About Us
A fishing experience like no other…
Reel Teaser Fishing Adventures based in Broome Western Australia offers exclusive live aboard fishing charters to all the hottest fishing spots off the WA coast including The Kimberley, Rowley Shoals, Scott Reef, Broome and Exmouth. Our fishing adventure charters are very personal as we only cater for small groups of up to 6 anglers. Our fishing adventures are set as a 9 day itinerary. If you would like a longer adventure please enquire directly. We will customise your next fishing adventure to suit your needs, whether it be heavy or light tackle game fishing, marlin fishing, sports fishing, barramundi fishing or fly fishing. 
SUSTAINABILITY 
On Reel Teaser, sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. As a strictly catch-and-release operation other than the fish we eat on board, we are committed to preserving marine ecosystems and protecting fish populations for future generations. Our crew follows best practices to ensure fish are handled carefully and released safely, minimizing stress and injury. We also educate our guests on responsible angling techniques and the importance of conservation. By combining adventure with environmental stewardship, we offer an unforgettable fishing experience that respects the ocean and supports its long-term health. As a certified eco-tourism operator , we are dedicated to reducing our environmental footprint while providing unforgettable, conservation-minded experiences that safeguard ocean wildlife and foster a deep respect for marine ecosystems. 
The Kimberley Region of Western Australia is one of the most ecologically and culturally significant areas in the world, known for its rugged coastlines, ancient gorges, and diverse marine and terrestrial ecosystems. Sustainability in the Kimberley is closely tied to the protection of its natural landscapes—home to endangered species and pristine marine environments—and the rich cultural heritage of its Traditional Owners, whose deep connection to Country spans tens of thousands of years. Efforts to preserve the Kimberley’s biodiversity and respect Indigenous knowledge are central to our operation, ensuring that this unique region is protected for future generations. 
What Fish You’ll Hook
Blue water fishing target species include Blue Marlin, Black Marlin, Striped Marlin, Sailfish, Broadbill Swordfish, Yellowfin Tuna, Dogtooth Tuna, Wahoo, Spanish Mackerel, GT’s and all reef species. Estuary fishing target species include Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Mangrove Jack, Fingermark, Cods, Queenies, Trevally and many more. Some fishing is seasonal. We cater for all levels of fishing ability and supply top quality Shimano fishing gear from 10kg to 60kg. All fishing is done following Codes of Best Practice and Minimal Impact practices. We use a variety of fishing styles and techniques including trolling, lure casting, popper fishing and switch baiting.
We can Build a Fishing Adventure Charter to Suit You
Although Reel Teaser Fishing Adventures specialise in fishing charters and expeditions we also offer customised adventure safaris to some of the most remote and scenic areas of the North West Coast of Australia. Other activities on our charters can include, snorkelling, bushwalking, mudcrabbing, collecting oysters, Island hopping, sightseeing, swimming and exploring. A mix of these activities along with fishing is recommended at most of our destinations.
Adventure and Fish in Style
Our 65ft custom built fibreglass fly bridge cruiser “Reel Teaser”, has all the comforts you will need to enjoy the fishing trip or adventure cruise of a lifetime whilst being guided by our fully qualified crew. Your skipper Ross has been fishing and exploring the Kimberley Coast for the past 30 years. Whether you want to do some serious game fishing for marlin, light tackle sports fishing for sailfish, estuary fishing for barramundi or you’re more interested in taking a leisurely cruise of discovery – Reel Teaser is the ideal charter vessel.
Stability AND Comfort
Now fitted with a Veem Gyrostabiliser that removes up to 90% of vessel roll caused by swell, your fishing experience is going to be smoother and more comfortable than ever. No more of the dreaded sea sickness that plagues some guests*. The ability to stop and drift in choppy seas and troll when the winds would normally stop us.
