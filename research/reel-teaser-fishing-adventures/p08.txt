Marlin Fishing
You have two options for targeting Marlin onboard our vessel Reel Teaser.
Over the past few years we have discovered a number of large Blue Marlin which seem to congregate in the warmer waters off the Rowley Shoals from October to December each year. This seems to be a breeding ground of some sort as all species of Marlin found on the West Coast of Australia have been caught here during this time. 
Option 1
• We fish heavy tackle using both 37kg and 60kg line classes with now the option of either fishing stand up or with a chair. For the best chance of catching a 1000lb Blue Marlin in Australia give us a call or look up more information about fishing at the Rowley Shoals on our Fishing Rowley Shoals page.
Option 2
• We fish the waters off Exmouth. Our vessel Reel Teaser operates here every year between February and March. The Continental Shelf off Exmouth creates a series of canyons that hold good numbers of both Blue and Black Marlin along with Sailfish, Striped Marlin and Broadbill Swordfish. The grounds are close to the mooring area so it does not take long to get the lures in the water. Most of our Marlin Fishing is done using lures however we do often switchbait. Marlin Fishing Charters in Exmouth can range from just 1 day to live aboard trips for as many days as you like. For more information about marlin fishing charters in Exmouth see our Fishing Exmouth page.
