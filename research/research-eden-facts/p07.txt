Welcome To Merimbula Big Game & Lakes Angling Club
Come & Visit Us At Our Clubroom Every Friday Night From 6.00pm Every One Is Welcome 
2026 / 27 Membership 
Would you love to be part of an awesome family club? Become a member today. We would love to have you join us. NEW & renewing members welcome. 
Membership page 
The club is open to all members and guests, Friday nights from 6.00pm. Visitors are very welcome. Come one and all and enjoy, the fishing report, the ambience, friendship and lovely views with very competitive bar prices. 
For any information please, ring Shane on 0428424496.
