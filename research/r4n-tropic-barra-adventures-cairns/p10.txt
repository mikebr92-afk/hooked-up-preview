FULL DAY RIVER CHARTER 
TROPIC BARRA ADVENTURES 
A full day on the river delivers the ultimate sports fishing experience in Far North Queensland. Pursuing the Australian icon Barramundi across pristine river systems, mangrove-lined estuaries and world heritage listed rainforest waterways. 
Tropic Barra Adventures takes you deep into the tropical river systems that most operators simply can't access. With a master permit covering from the Daintree River to the Hinchinbrook Channel and every system in between, we have pure flexibility to operate where conditions are best. 
A full day river charter offers Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Queenfish and more. All gear, tackle, lunch and transport included.
