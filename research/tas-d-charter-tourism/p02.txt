Hot Fishing Spots
Page Content
​​​​​​​​Local knowledge is always your best source for finding out where the fish are biting in Tasmania. Download our free colour guides to 11 different fishing regions, ask local fishers and talk to staff in bait and tackle shops.​
• Bruny D'Entrecasteaux Region Which fish are biting and where in the Bruny Island and D'Entrecasteaux Channel Region.
• Derwent River Which fish are biting and where in the Derwent River including Hobart, Bridgewater, Kingston and South Arm.
• East Coast Region Find out which fish are biting and where around the East Coast including Bicheno, Swansea, Triabunna and Orford.
• Tasman Peninsula Region Where the fish are biting around turrakana / Tasman Peninsula Region including Frederick Henry and Norfolk Bays, Dunalley and Eaglehawk Neck.
• St Helens Region Find out which fish are biting and where in the St Helens Region including Georges Bay, Binalong Bay and Grants Lagoon.
• North East Coast and Flinders Island Which fish are biting and where around the North East Coast including Bridport, Musselroe Bay, Ansons Bay and Flinders Island.
• Tamar River Which fish are biting and where in the kanamaluka / Tamar Estuary Region including around Launceston, Beauty Point and George Town.
• Devonport and Port Sorell Region Find out which fish are biting and where around Devonport and Port Sorell.
• North West Coast Find out which fish are biting and where along the North West Coast.
• King Island Find out which fish are biting and where around King Island.
• Macquarie Harbour Find out which fish are biting and where around Macquarie Harbour including Strahan.
• Fishing Information Maps Fishing guides including maps for eleven different regions around Tasmania. Where to fish, gear advice and what you'll catch.
