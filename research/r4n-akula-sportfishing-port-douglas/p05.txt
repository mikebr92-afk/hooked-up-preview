Frequently Asked Questions
What Is Reef Fishing? 
Reef fishing is catching fish that live around coral reefs, often using bait or lures near the reef structure.
What Is the Most Common Reef Fish? 
Common reef fish include emperors, cod, nannygai, coral trout and much more! 
Why Can’t You Touch a Reef? 
Touching a reef can damage fragile coral and harm the marine ecosystem. 
Are Reef Fish Good to Eat? 
Yes, many reef fish like coral trout, nannygai, gold band snapper and red emperor are excellent eating. 
Is It Better to Do a Reef Tour From Cairns or Port Douglas? 
Both are great, but Port Douglas offers closer access to the outer reef with fewer crowds.
