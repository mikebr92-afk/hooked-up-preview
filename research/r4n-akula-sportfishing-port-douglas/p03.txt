Cairns or Port Douglas — Which is Better For Game Fishing?
It is among the most frequently asked questions of a visitor intending to go fishing: cairns or port douglas which is better?
The thing is, they both are good fishing destinations. However, in case you want to relax, access better reefs and offshore fishing experience more quickly, and still have an outstanding charter experience without the crowds, Port Douglas will be an excellent option.
With Akula Sportfishing, you’ll enjoy:
• A scenic, stress-free departure point
• Expert local guidance and tailored trips
• A personalised offshore experience designed around your goals
So if you’ve been wondering cairns or port douglas which is better, Port Douglas offers an unbeatable mix of reef beauty, offshore action, and laid-back luxury, especially when you’re on the right boat.

Frequently Asked Questions
What Is the Meaning of Game Fishing? 
Game fishing is catching large, challenging fish for sport rather than food. 
What Is Considered Game Fishing? 
Targeting species like marlin, tuna, trevally, and other big, strong fish counts as game fishing. 
Why Is It Called Game Fishing? 
It’s called “game” fishing because these fish are treated like a sport or challenge, similar to hunting game. 
What Is the Most Common Game Fish? 
Popular game fish include marlin, tuna, sailfish, dolphin fish, spanish mackerel and giant trevally. 
Is It Okay to Drink Alcohol While Sportfishing? 
It’s not recommended; alcohol can impair safety and judgment on the water.
