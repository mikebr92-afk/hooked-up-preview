About
Byron Bay Charters is a reputable fishing charter and boat rental service located in Brunswick Heads, just 15 minutes north of Byron Bay in the picturesque NSW North Coast of Australia. This family-owned business offers a range of services including fishing charters, whale watching tours, and private charters tailored to suit the needs and desires of their customers.
Whether you are an experienced angler seeking an exciting day out on the water, or simply looking to unwind and admire the stunning coastal scenery, Byron Bay Charters has something to offer for everyone. With their team of experienced and knowledgeable skippers, you can be assured of a safe and enjoyable adventure.
For avid anglers, Byron Bay Charters provides top-quality fishing charters, allowing you to reel in some of the region's most sought-after fish species. Whether you're targeting Snapper, Flathead, or Spanish Mackerel, you'll have access to state-of-the-art fishing equipment and expert guidance to maximise your chances of success.
Whale watching enthusiasts will be thrilled by the opportunity to witness the majestic marine giants up close. During the annual migration season, Byron Bay Charters offers unforgettable whale watching tours, providing you with an unforgettable experience as these incredible creatures breach and play in the ocean waves.
In addition to fishing and whale watching, Byron Bay Charters also caters to those who wish to explore the underwater world. Offering scuba diving and snorkelling experiences, you can discover the vibrant marine life that resides in the secluded locations near Brunswick Heads. Photographers will also be delighted with the opportunity to capture the awe-inspiring scenery and wildlife, including whales and dolphins, through their lenses.
Byron Bay Charters is committed to providing exceptional service and ensuring that your time on board is nothing short of extraordinary. With a dedication to customer satisfaction, their friendly and professional staff will go above and beyond to cater to your needs, ensuring a memorable and enjoyable experience.
To make a booking, customers can conveniently do so online through their user-friendly website. Simply provide your name, phone number, email address, and the number of guests joining the charter. Select your preferred date and the type of charter you are interested in, whether it be fishing, whale watching, snorkelling, or another private charter. If you have any specific requests or additional comments, Byron Bay Charters welcomes them and will do their best to accommodate your needs.
Discover the beauty of the NSW North Coast with Byron Bay Charters. Book your unforgettable adventure today and create memories that will last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions
What kinds of charters do you offer? 
We offer Fishing charters, Whale Watching charters, and other private charters, including Scuba Diving and Snorkelling.
Where are you based and how far are you from Byron Bay? 
We're located and run out of Brunswick Heads, 15 minutes north of Byron Bay.
Do you run charters every day? 
Yes, Byron Bay Charters is available for charters seven days a week.
