Deep Sea Fishing Gold Coast. 
Half day morning deep sea reef and game Fishing Charter departs at 6:00 am. (please arrive by 5:40am) We head out from the Spit and out through the Gold Coast Seaway to the Offshore fishing grounds for a day of deep sea reef and game Fishing. 
Our 5 hour morning fishing trips are perfect for both beginners and expert fisherman alike, or those that have limited time available and need the afternoon free. 
Our friendly, experienced professional Skipper and deckhand will show you the best techniques for catching a wide variety of bottom dwelling and pelagic fish species. Target species include Cobia, Mackerel, Marlin, Teraglin, Tuskfish, Snapper, Jewfish, Tuna, Wahoo, Tailor, Trevally and many more. Great fun for the whole family, individual and group bookings available. 
Inclusions: All bait and fishing tackle, ice cold bottled water, full tuition, legal fish gutted, gilled and bagged.
