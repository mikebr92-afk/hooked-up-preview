Half Day Charter FAQs 
Everything You Need to Know
Before you head out for your afternoon fishing adventure, here’s everything you need to know to make your trip easy, safe, and unforgettable. From what to bring to where to meet, our crew has taken care of every detail so all you need to do is relax, cast off, and enjoy the Gold Coast sunshine.
1. What happens if the weather turns bad? 
Your safety is always our top priority. If strong winds or large swells are forecast, we’ll reschedule your trip to another day. You’ll receive a text or call the evening before if conditions look unsafe. Charters still operate in light rain – just bring a jacket and enjoy the experience.
2. Can I change or cancel my booking? 
Bookings can’t be cancelled, but you can reschedule with notice:
• Shared charters: 7 days’ notice 
• Private charters: 14 days’ notice 
If you miss your trip or provide less notice than required, your booking (or voucher) will be forfeited as per our Terms and Conditions.
3. Do I need fishing experience? 
Not at all. Our afternoon charters are ideal for both beginners and experienced anglers. The crew provides expert guidance on baiting, casting, and reeling – helping you feel confident on the rod and ready to land your catch.
4. Can families and children join? 
Yes, kids aged 7 and up are welcome on all shared charters. Younger children can join via private booking. Please note, children under 14 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.
5. What should I bring for my afternoon trip? 
Bring a hat, sunscreen, and a light jacket if rain is expected. You’re also welcome to bring snacks and drinks. Beer (in cans) and wine are allowed in moderation but please, no spirits or glass bottles onboard.
6. What if I start feeling seasick? 
We don’t issue refunds for seasickness, so it’s a good idea to take preventative medication before boarding. Our vessels are large, stable, and shaded to help keep you comfortable during your trip.
7. What payment options do you accept? 
We accept both card and cash payments. A deposit secures your booking, and the balance is due before departure. If you book online, payment can be made easily through our secure booking system.
8. Do I get to keep my fish? 
Yes! All legal-sized fish are yours to take home. Our crew will gill, gut, and bag your catch so it’s ready for the fridge, freezer, or BBQ once you return to shore.
9. How safe are the boats? 
Every True Blue Fishing vessel is fully insured, Queensland Government–survey certified, and equipped with modern safety gear including radios, GPS, lifejackets, and first-aid kits. You’ll receive a full safety briefing before departure
10. Where do we meet for the afternoon charter? 
All afternoon trips depart from The Spit Pontoon, Muriel Henchman Drive, Main Beach, QLD (near Sea World). Follow the blue “Charter Boats” signs – there’s plenty of free parking available close to the jetty.
