Bucks Party FAQs 
Everything You Need to Know
Before you crack that first cold one or start casting, here’s everything you need to know to make your bucks day smooth sailing. From what to bring and when to show up, to how the day runs and what’s included, we’ve covered all the essentials – so you and the boys can focus on fishing, celebrating, and sending off the groom in style.
1. What’s included in a Bucks Party fishing charter? 
Everything you need for an epic day out – premium rods, reels, bait, tackle, expert tuition, and a top-notch skipper and deckhand who’ll keep the energy high. We also supply ice-cold bottled water, safety gear, and clean onboard facilities. BYO food and drinks are welcome, or add catering for a hassle-free feast.
What can we bring onboard? 
Bring your own food, drinks, and good vibes. Beer (in cans) and wine are welcome in moderation, but no spirits or glass bottles for safety reasons. Sunscreen, hats, and sunnies are a must and if it looks like rain, bring a jacket. 
3. Can we have topless waitresses or strippers onboard? 
Absolutely! Adult entertainment options are available upon request, including topless or lingerie waitresses and strippers (subject to availability). Just let us know during booking and we’ll sort it out discreetly and professionally.
4. How long does the trip go for? 
You’ve got options:
• 5-hour offshore fishing charter 
• 9-hour full-day deep sea adventure 
• 3–5 hour Broadwater cruise for a more relaxed, scenic day
We run 7 days a week, year-round , so you can pick the time that suits your crew.
5. How many people can come? 
Our vessels cater for groups of 6 to 28 blokes , depending on your booking and chosen boat:
• MV SeaQuest – up to 12 mates
• MV Fat Hooker – up to 16 mates
Got more than 20? We can arrange multiple vessels for large-group party packages up to 50.
6. What happens if the weather turns bad? 
Safety first, always. If high winds or rough seas are forecast, we’ll reschedule your charter for another suitable date. You’ll be notified the evening before if conditions aren’t safe. Light rain? No worries – just bring a jacket and keep the beers cold.
7. Can we play our own music? 
You bet! Bring a playlist and connect to our onboard sound system via Bluetooth. Whether it’s classic Aussie rock or dance floor anthems, we’ll keep the tunes pumping between catches.
8. Do we get to keep the fish we catch? 
Yep – all legal fish are yours to keep! Our crew will gill, gut, and bag your catch ready for the BBQ, esky, or freezer. You can even turn it into bragging rights back at the pub later.
9. Do we need fishing experience? 
Not at all! Our crew caters to every level from total rookies to seasoned anglers. We’ll show you the ropes, share local tips, and make sure everyone lands something worth talking about.
10. Where do we meet and when should we arrive? 
All bucks party charters depart from The Spit Pontoon, Muriel Henchman Drive, Main Beach, QLD (near Sea World) . Follow the blue “Charter Boats” signs. Please arrive 20 minutes before departure – there’s plenty of free parking near the jetty.
