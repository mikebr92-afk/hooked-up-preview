Half Day Charter FAQs 
Everything You Need to Know
Before you cast off, here’s everything you need to know to make your morning deep sea fishing trip smooth and enjoyable. From where to meet to what to bring, our crew has you covered so you can relax and focus on landing your next big catch.
1. What if the weather is bad? 
Safety always comes first. If strong winds or rough seas are forecast, your trip will be rescheduled to another day. We’ll notify you the night before via text or phone. Trips still go ahead in light rain – just bring a jacket and your sense of adventure!
2. What happens if I can’t make it on the day? 
Bookings can’t be cancelled, but you can reschedule with notice:
• Shared charters: 7 days’ notice
• Private charters: 14 days’ notice
If notice isn’t provided or you miss your trip, your booking (or voucher) will be forfeited per our Terms and Conditions.
3. Do I need experience to join the charter? 
Not at all! Our charters are perfect for beginners and experienced anglers alike. Our friendly crew provides hands-on tuition and will help you learn local techniques for baiting, reeling, and landing your catch.
4. Can children come on the charter? 
Yes! We welcome kids aged 7 and over on all shared charters (younger children by private booking only). Children under 14 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times.
5. Can I bring food or drinks? 
Absolutely! You’re welcome to bring your own snacks, lunch, and drinks. Beer in cans and wine are permitted in moderation. Please note – no spirits or glass bottles are allowed onboard.
6. What if I get seasick? 
We don’t issue refunds for seasickness, so if you’re prone to motion sickness, take preventative medication before boarding. Our boats are large and stable, with shaded seating to help keep you comfortable.
7. What payment methods do you accept? 
We accept cash and card payments. A deposit is required to confirm your booking, with the balance due before departure. If you book online, you can pay safely through our secure booking system.
8. What happens to my fish after I catch them? 
All legal-sized fish are yours to keep! Our crew will gill, gut, and bag your catch so it’s ready for cooking or freezing once you return to shore.
9. Are your boats safe and insured? 
Yes. All True Blue Fishing vessels are fully insured and Queensland Government-survey certified. Each boat features lifejackets, radios, GPS tracking, and modern safety gear. You’ll receive a full safety briefing before departure.
10. Where do we depart from? 
All charters depart from The Spit Pontoon, Muriel Henchman Drive, Main Beach, QLD (near Sea World).
Look for the blue “Charter Boats” sign — free parking is available nearby.
