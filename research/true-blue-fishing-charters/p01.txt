Everything You Need to Know Before You Fish 
Frequently Asked Questions
From booking your trip to what you’ll catch, we’ve answered the questions we hear most often. If you need any additional information, our friendly team is just a phone call or email away and happy to help.
What is included in your Gold Coast fishing charters? 
All of our Gold Coast fishing charters include quality fishing rods, reels, bait, tackle and cold bottled water. Our experienced crew is on hand to help throughout the trip, whether you’re a beginner or an experienced angler. Simply bring your food, drinks, hat, sunscreen and a camera to capture your catch.
What fish can I catch on a Gold Coast fishing trip? 
The species vary throughout the year, but our Gold Coast fishing trips regularly target snapper, pearl perch, venus tuskfish, teraglin, amberjack, cobia, Spanish and spotted mackerel , mahi mahi, wahoo, tuna, marlin and various other species. Our experienced skippers choose the best fishing grounds based on the conditions and time of year.
What’s the best time of year for deep sea fishing on the Gold Coast? 
The Gold Coast offers excellent offshore fishing year-round. Summer is ideal for pelagic species like mahi mahi, Spanish mackerel, wahoo and marlin, while winter is renowned for quality bottom reef fishing for species like venus tuskfish, snapper, cod, and cobia. Every season offers different opportunities, making any time a great time to book a fishing charter.
Are your Gold Coast fishing charters suitable for families and children? 
Yes. Our Gold Coast fishing charters are a great family activity, and we welcome children aged 7 years and over . Whether it’s your child’s first-time fishing or a fun family day out on the water, our experienced crew will provide guidance and support to help everyone enjoy a safe, exciting and memorable offshore fishing adventure.
What should I bring on my Gold Coast fishing trip? 
We recommend bringing comfortable clothing, enclosed shoes, a hat, sunscreen, polarised sunglasses, food, drinks and any personal medication. Everything you need for fishing—including rods, reels, bait and tackle—is supplied by True Blue Fishing Charters.
Do I need fishing experience to join a deep sea fishing trip? 
No. Our Gold Coast deep sea fishing trips are suitable for everyone over the age of seven, from complete beginners to experienced anglers. Our friendly crew will show you how to use the equipment, bait your hook and land your fish, making it a fun experience for the whole family.
Where do your Gold Coast fishing charters depart from? 
Our fishing charters depart from the pontoon located at Muriel Henchman Drive, Main Beach on the Gold Coast, providing quick access through the Southport Seaway to productive offshore reefs and deep-water fishing grounds. This means less travel time and more time fishing.
Can I keep the fish I catch? 
Yes. You are welcome to keep any legal-sized fish caught during your trip in accordance with Queensland fishing regulations. Depending on the day’s catch, our crew can also assist with gilling and gutting your fish so you can enjoy it fresh.
How far offshore do your fishing trips go? 
The distance we travel depends on the weather, sea conditions and the species we’re targeting. Our deep sea fishing Gold Coast charters visit various offshore reefs, and game fishing grounds, giving anglers the best chance of catching quality fish throughout the year.
