Byron Bay Charters
Is located and run out of Brunswick Heads, 15 minutes north of Byron Bay, and is available for Byron Bay Fishing, Whale Watching, Scuba Diving ,Snorkelling and other private Charters seven days a week.
Whether you’re after a quality Fishing trip on the beautiful NSW North Coast, want to go Snorkelling in a secluded location or take photos of our spectacular local scenery and wildlife, Byron Bay Charters is at your service.
We can take you on a personalised and private charter which caters to all of your needs.
Byron Bay Whale Watching
We are the oldest Byron Bay Whale Charter company in Brunswick Heads with the most Modern and comfortable vessel (Windarra) along with our experienced crew , enjoy your Whale Watching off Brunswick Heads and Byron Bay, visit the Cape Byron Marine Park.
Allow us to take you on an unbelievable experience, with one of natures giants, the Humpback Whales ,on their migration north and again on their return south , enjoy one of the worlds most magnificent creatures, the Hump back Whale, from the comfort of (Windarra)
Byron Bay Fishing Charters
Byron Bay Charters provides quality charter services on the far North Coast of NSW. Let us know what your passion is whether its chasing pelagic’s or fishing the bottom, we can tailor a charter for you.
See our latest catch and voyage news on our facebook page 
Catch (and keep or release) marlin, tuna, mackerel, wahoo, king fish, jewfish, snapper, pearl perch, samson fish and more—all in the comfort of our fully equipped fishing boat Windarra .
Charters operate for a full day from 6am to 2pm. Shorter Charters are also available
on request.
All boat trips depart from Brunswick Heads jetty and run seven days a week, weather permitting.
See if your up for a challenge and get your name in our record book.
There is a no smoking policy with Byron Bay Charters
