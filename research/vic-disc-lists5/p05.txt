Fishing
Fishing along the Murray River
Fishing is one of the Murray’s classic experiences, and the legendary native Murray cod, Australia’s largest exclusively freshwater fish, is what most anglers are after.
The Murray cod featured in Aboriginal mythology and made a big impression on early settlers, who were amazed by their size and abundance. These days the Murray cod is a prized catch but other river lures include golden perch (yellow belly), trout, catfish and freshwater cray.
Remember, the Murray is regarded as being in NSW, so you will need a NSW fishing license, available at local stores and service stations or online from New South Wales Department of Primary Industries .
Top Fishing Spots
• Yarrawonga and Lake Mulwala : Home to Australia’s largest natural population of trout cod, a rare and exciting catch for enthusiasts.
• Kings Billabong (near Mildura): Known as the “fish nursery” by locals for its thriving fish population.
• Mungabareena and Heywood’s Bridge (Albury): Renowned for housing some of the largest river-dwelling trout in the region.
Visitor Information Centres : Stop by to get insider tips and local fishing hotspots from those who know the river best.
Target Species
• Murray Cod : The legendary Murray Cod is the largest freshwater fish in Australia, growing over 1 metre in length and weighing up to 25kg. Its size and strength make it a prized catch and a thrilling challenge for anglers.
• Golden Perch (Yellowbelly): Another favourite, these fish often exceed 50cm and 5kg, offering a rewarding experience for anglers.
• Redfin (English Perch): Identified by their red pectoral fins and spined dorsal fin, these noxious pests can’t be returned to the water. While invasive, they provide excellent sport and are highly regarded as a table fish.
• Murray Crayfish : Found around the Rutherglen/Wahgunyah stretch, these are the second-largest freshwater crayfish in the world. A winter delicacy, but strict bag limits and regulations apply.
Other species include Silver Perch, Brown Trout, Rainbow Trout, and the rare Trout Cod. European Carp, unfortunately abundant, are considered a pest but can provide fun for children, though they are not suitable for eating.
Fishing Regulations and Conservation
When fishing in the Murray region, it’s essential to follow local fishing regulations, including bag and size limits. Protecting native species and maintaining the health of the ecosystem ensures this incredible fishing destination can be enjoyed for generations to come.
Whether you’re a seasoned angler chasing the mighty Murray Cod or a family looking to introduce kids to the joys of fishing, the Murray River and its surrounding waterways provide a truly unforgettable experience.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the land and waters that surround the Murray River. We acknowledge and respect their history, culture and continuous connection to country. We pay our respects to elders – past, present and emerging who have cared for this country for over 60,000 years.This landscape, rich in story and opportunity, plays a pivotal part in our visitor economy and we thank Victoria and New South Wales’ First Peoples for sharing this with us all.
