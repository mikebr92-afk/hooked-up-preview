Acknowledgment
Visit Echuca Moama acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the land in which we live and work and we pay our respects to Elders past, present and future. We are nestled proudly on the banks of Dhungala (Murray River) in Yorta Yorta Country and thank them as traditional custodians for their ongoing care and connection to the land & waters.
