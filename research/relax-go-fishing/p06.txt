Exploring the Charm of B&Bs in Boothbay Harbor, ME: A Tranquil Retreat by the Sea 
by relaxgofishing March 15, 2024 
written by relaxgofishing 
Nestled along Maine’s picturesque shoreline, Boothbay Harbor enchants visitors with its blend of maritime history and small-town warmth. As the salty sea breeze drifts through the harbor, it’s easy to understand why this destination remains a favorite escape. Amid its scenic beauty and nautical spirit, B&Bs in Boothbay Harbor, ME stand out as havens of comfort and authenticity.
Exploring Boothbay Harbor’s Unique Atmosphere 
Boothbay Harbor is known for its stunning ocean views, working waterfront, and vibrant local life. From watching lobster boats to browsing art galleries or exploring boutique shops, every moment feels like a step into coastal Maine’s heart. After a day of discovery, returning to a cozy bed and breakfast offers a peaceful retreat from the bustle of the harbor.
Why Choose a Bed and Breakfast in Boothbay Harbor 
Personalized Service and Genuine Warmth 
Unlike larger hotels, B&Bs in Boothbay Harbor provide an intimate experience filled with personal touches. Hosts greet guests by name and create an atmosphere that feels more like visiting family than booking a room. This level of attention ensures a memorable, comfortable stay.
Historic Charm Meets Modern Comfort 
Each inn has its own story. From restored Victorian houses to seaside cottages, Boothbay Harbor’s B&Bs reflect the area’s history and charm while offering updated amenities like private baths, Wi-Fi, and gourmet breakfasts.
Top Bed and Breakfasts in Boothbay Harbor, ME 
Harbor House Inn 
Located in the town’s heart, the Harbor House Inn welcomes visitors with its inviting porch, cozy fireplace, and tastefully decorated rooms. Guests enjoy a homemade breakfast each morning before exploring the harbor. Whether you’re kayaking, whale watching, or reading on the porch, this inn makes an ideal home base for your coastal getaway.
Topside Inn 
Perched high above the harbor, the Topside Inn offers sweeping ocean views and quiet luxury. Rooms feature plush bedding, private balconies, and modern comforts. Each morning begins with locally roasted coffee and a gourmet breakfast served on the terrace overlooking the water. With its serene setting and thoughtful hosts, it’s no wonder the Topside Inn is among the most sought-after B&Bs in Boothbay Harbor, ME .
Special Touches That Make a Difference 
Many Boothbay Harbor bed and breakfasts offer extra details that elevate the experience. Afternoon tea, homemade pastries, or complimentary wine hours add a touch of indulgence. Hosts also share local insights hidden trails, scenic spots, and favorite seafood restaurants to help guests enjoy an authentic Maine adventure.
Things to Do Near Boothbay Harbor B&Bs 
The area is a playground for nature lovers and culture seekers alike. Visit the Coastal Maine Botanical Gardens for lush landscapes and ocean views. Sail past lighthouses, enjoy fresh lobster at waterfront restaurants, or browse local artisan markets for unique keepsakes. Every experience captures the essence of coastal Maine.
Your Perfect Seaside Escape Awaits 
Whether you’re planning a romantic weekend, a family retreat, or a solo escape, B&Bs in Boothbay Harbor, ME blend comfort, charm, and heartfelt hospitality. With cozy rooms, ocean breezes, and warm hosts, they promise the ideal retreat by the sea. Pack your bags and let Boothbay Harbor’s timeless charm rejuvenate your spirit.

A Paradise Unveiled: Crystal Sands Destin, FL 
by relaxgofishing March 4, 2024 
written by relaxgofishing 
Crystal Sands in Destin, FL, is a hidden gem along the pristine coastline of the Gulf of Mexico. This breathtaking destination is synonymous with tranquility, luxury, and natural beauty. As you step onto the powdery white sands and feel the soft sea breeze, it’s easy to see why Crystal Sands is a cherished retreat for both locals and travelers alike.
A Paradise Unveiled: Crystal Sands Destin, FL
Crystal Sands, located in the heart of Destin, FL, showcases the unmatched beauty of Florida’s Emerald Coast. The allure of this beachfront paradise lies not only in its crystal-clear waters but also in its fine, sugar-white sand. From the moment you arrive, the magnificence of Crystal Sands unfolds, inviting you to relax and embrace the serenity of coastal living.
The Jewel of the Emerald Coast
Known as the “Jewel of the Emerald Coast,” Crystal Sands possesses a charm that’s hard to find elsewhere. The sunlight dancing on the clear waters creates a magical ambiance throughout the day. Whether you’re soaking up the Florida sun or enjoying a sunset stroll, Crystal Sands promises moments that exceed every expectation.
Luxury Living by the Shore
Beachfront Condos and Rentals
If you’re seeking a luxurious beach escape, Crystal Sands offers elegant accommodations that redefine coastal comfort. Beachfront condos and vacation rentals blend sophistication with convenience. Guests can wake to the sound of gentle waves and breathtaking Gulf views.
Morning Bliss by the Gulf
Imagine sipping morning coffee on your private balcony as the sunrise paints the sky – a simple pleasure that feels truly special here.
Revel in Outdoor Adventures 
Beyond the peaceful shore, Crystal Sands invites you to explore its natural playground. The emerald-green waters are perfect for snorkeling or diving. Fishing enthusiasts can charter boats for a day on the Gulf, while kayakers glide through calm coastal dunes. Moreover, nature lovers can enjoy dolphin-watching tours or scenic bike rides along the beach
Family-Friendly Fun
Crystal Sands isn’t just for couples; it’s also a family-friendly destination. The shallow, calm waters provide a safe space for children to splash and play. Building sandcastles, collecting shells, and enjoying beachside picnics create memories that last a lifetime. Because of its relaxed atmosphere, families return year after year.
Exploring Destin’s Culinary Delights
A trip to Crystal Sands is also a treat for food lovers. Destin’s vibrant dining scene reflects its rich coastal heritage. From casual seaside cafés to upscale seafood restaurants, every meal is a celebration of flavor. Don’t miss the fresh catch of the day, prepared by local chefs who bring out the best of Gulf seafood.
Immersive Cultural Experiences
While the beach is the main attraction, the surrounding area offers plenty of cultural charm. A short drive takes you to Destin’s lively harbor, filled with shops, galleries, and entertainment venues. Whether you’re browsing local art, enjoying live music, or watching fishing boats return at sunset, there’s always something to discover.

The Best Destination is Get Away to Danang and You Should See 
by relaxgofishing January 16, 2020 
written by relaxgofishing 
Popularized by the American TV series, China Beach is a stunning, untouched stretch of sand near Danang. With 3,260 kilometers of coastline , longer than the U.S. West Coast, Vietnam is truly a beach lover’s paradise. Once a quiet R&R spot during the Vietnam War, Bac My An better known as China Beach has become one of the country’s most famous seaside destinations.
The Rise of the Furama Resort
Until the Furama Resort opened, China Beach remained a sleepy area filled with small local hotels. Locals enjoyed the cool early mornings and late evenings by the sea. Today, the red-tiled roofs of the Furama peek above palms and pines, offering luxury on this pristine shoreline. The resort alone gives travelers a reason to holiday here.
Easy Access from Major Cities
Danang lies just a 90-minute flight from Hong Kong. Dragonair now runs direct charters, and Thai Airways plans to start flights from Bangkok. Vietnam Airlines is also considering regular service between Hong Kong and Danang. The city’s airport, a legacy of its military past, easily handles large aircraft. From there, guests can ride to the resort in an air-conditioned minibus or take a restored U.S. Army jeep for a breezy 15-minute trip.
Comfort, Style, and Adventure
The resort’s colonial-style buildings sit within landscaped gardens, creating calm away from Danang’s busy streets. Rooms and suites open to private terraces with views of the sea or lagoon. Restaurants and bars face the ocean, welcoming cool breezes.
Adventure seekers can explore the Diana Diving Base , run by European professionals. It offers cave and wreck diving, night dives, and beginner training certified by CMAS or PADI. Guests can also enjoy tennis, mountain biking, or water sports.
Relaxation and Wellness
Those looking to unwind can relax beside the Furama’s terraced freshwater pools and waterfalls. The health club includes a gym, sauna, and steam rooms with expert masseurs. Tai chi sessions, painting classes, and even fortune-telling sessions provide unique ways to unwind.
Exploring Danang and Beyond
In Danang, the Cham Museum displays the world’s finest Cham sculptures carved sandstone pieces worth multiple visits. Don’t miss the colorful Cao Dai Temple , either.
Nearby, four major cultural centers make Danang an ideal base for day trips. Drive 45 minutes southeast to Hoi An , a charming ancient port lined with temples, cafés, art galleries, and markets. Along the way, stop at Marble Mountain , where caves and sanctuaries await atop steep steps.
To the north, the imperial city of Hue lies two hours away, filled with pagodas and temples nestled under leafy hills. Another perfect excursion is to My Son Valley , home to temples of the ancient Champa Kingdom Vietnam’s answer to Angkor Wat.
A Journey Worth Taking
From sunrise over My Son’s ruins to sunset on China Beach, Danang offers a rare mix of culture, history, and relaxation. Whether diving in clear waters or exploring ancient temples, visitors find themselves surrounded by Vietnam’s beauty and spirit.
