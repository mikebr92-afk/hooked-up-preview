Where to Find the Best Fall Foliage in Toronto and Surrounds It’s Time for Some Leaf Peeping! 
by relaxgofishing September 2, 2025 
written by relaxgofishing 
Toronto’s skyline and waterfront are well-known, but when fall comes, the city turns into a show of colours. The parks, ravines, and seaside walks become heaven on earth, all cosy and beautiful.
High Park 
Start with the obvious one. High Park is Toronto is the biggest green space and one of the most popular places to see fall colours. With wide walking trails, wooded paths, and even a little zoo, it offers the best fall experience. A favourite among nature lovers, October is when the maples and oaks transform into a sea of crimson, orange, and yellow.Head to Grenadier Pond for the best views of reflections on the water or walk the forest trails for a more secluded feel. If you’re up for a longer walk, the trails connect across different areas, so you can spend a whole afternoon surrounded by colour.
Trinity Bellwoods Park 
This downtown park is a local hangout. People come here to walk their dogs, play frisbee, or lounge with a coffee. In the fall, the trees along the paths create brilliant canopies that make even a short walk a memorable experience.
The blend of city life and nature is what makes this place fun. Cosy cafes and storefronts are selling all things autumn. Get a latte from a coffee shop nearby, sit down on a bench, and take it all in.If you stay nearby, you can visit whenever you feel like it. Pan Pacific Toronto, often counted among the best hotels in North York, Toronto , is about 30 minutes from the park.
Trillium Park 
Down by the waterfront, Trillium Park offers fall colours with stunning skyline views. The park, situated on the east side of Ontario Place, features trails, open green lawns, and native plants that burst into shades of red and gold in the autumn.
Walk the William G. Davis Trail to enjoy both the lake and the beautiful trees. It’s even more beautiful at sunset, when the colours glow in the soft light and the city lights turn on.
Ontario Place 
Right next door to Trillium Park is Ontario Place, another excellent place to enjoy fall colours. While it’s more known for concerts and events, the grounds have plenty of tree-lined paths that light up in autumn.
The contrast is the best thing about Ontario Place – bright fall leaves against the modern architecture and views of Lake Ontario. Pack a picnic, sit by the water, and you’ll see just how pretty the city can get when the seasons change.
Toronto Harbourfront 
The Harbourfront is a convenient choice if you don’t have much time. Walk along the boardwalks and trails and you’ll find bursts of colour among the trees lining the waterfront. It’s not as wild as the ravines or big parks, but it has the perfect combination of city and natural beauty.
You’ll find lots of things to keep yourself occupied with. You may go leaf-peeping, visit a gallery, take a boat ride, or stop at one of the lakeside cafés. On a calm day, the lake reflects the autumn foliage, making the whole area feel twice as colourful.
Liberty Village 
You might not think of Liberty Village as a place to see fall leaves, but the neighbourhood is full of tree-lined streets, parks and converted old factories – it all looks great in the backdrop of fall colours.
It’s more of a casual, local experience than a grand show of nature, but that’s exactly why you should visit.
Walk around the side streets, get a coffee, and admire how the leaves make the red-brick buildings look brighter.
Glen Stewart Ravine 
Go east to Glen Stewart Ravine if you want to feel like you’re in the wild without leaving the city. This secret treasure is a forested ravine with wooden boardwalks that wind among tall trees. In the autumn, the trees make a colourful canopy, which you don’t want to miss.
It’s a lot quieter than High Park or the seaside, which makes it great for a peaceful walk. The boardwalk trail isn’t too long, but it feels completely different from the busy city.
Scarborough Bluffs 
If you don’t mind a short drive, the Scarborough Bluffs have some of the most beautiful autumn views near Toronto. The cliffs that rise far above the ground look amazing against the autumn leaves, and the vistas of Lake Ontario are amazing.
At the base of the cliffs is Bluffer’s Park, an excellent place for a walk. For the best views, however, head up to top of the bluffs and look over the colourful trees that extend towards the shoreline. Bring your camera – you absolutely don’t want to miss this scene.

Essential Fishing Technology for a Caravan Fishing Trip 
by relaxgofishing December 12, 2024 
written by relaxgofishing 
A caravan fishing trip combines the joy of road travel with the serenity of fishing in scenic locations. To make the most of this adventure, incorporating modern fishing technology can enhance your experience, improve your catch rate, and ensure safety and convenience. Here are some must-have fishing technologies to consider: 
Fish Finders 
Fish finders are invaluable tools for locating fish and understanding underwater environments. These devices use sonar to map the waterbed and display fish locations, water depth, and temperature on a screen. Portable or compact models are ideal for caravan trips, especially if you plan to fish in multiple locations. 
GPS and Mapping Tools 
Handheld GPS devices or smartphone apps designed for anglers help pinpoint fishing spots and navigate waterways. Many of these tools also allow you to mark locations, track your route, and identify nearby boat ramps or facilities. Apps like Navionics or Fishbrain offer detailed maps and real-time data to maximize your fishing success. 
Smart Fishing Rods and Reels 
Modern fishing rods and reels are equipped with sensors to monitor line tension, water depth, and fish activity. Some even connect to smartphone apps, providing data on when and where to cast for optimal results. 
Drones for Fishing 
Fishing drones have gained popularity for reaching hard-to-access areas. They can carry baited lines to distant spots, drop them precisely, and even scout for fish from the air using cameras. 
Portable Aerators and Coolers 
To keep your catch fresh, invest in portable aerators and coolers. Aerators ensure live bait stays active, while high-tech coolers maintain a precise temperature for storing fish. 
Underwater Cameras 
For those who love exploring, underwater cameras provide a fascinating view of fish habitats. They’re perfect for scouting fishing spots or simply enjoying underwater wildlife. 
Equipping yourself with the right fishing technology can transform your caravan fishing trip into a memorable, efficient, and enjoyable adventure. Get more info at website for the latest entertainment.

Catch of the nation: Fernando Jose Cuellar Membreno lists the 5 best saltwater along the Atlantic Ocean 
by relaxgofishing March 22, 2022 
written by relaxgofishing 
For Fernando Jose Cuellar Membreno, fishing is more than just a hobby. It is a family affair.
Fishing is a passion he’s passed down to his three kids. Despite hectic work, school, and social schedules, Fernando Jose Cuellar Membreno and his three kids always make time to get out on the open waters.
Born in El Salvador, Fernando Jose Cuellar Membreno moved to the U.S. at an early age to chase his dreams. He wanted to study, learn business, and build a fulfilling life. Over the years, he created a successful career as a business manager while staying true to his passion for fishing. He teaches his children the same work-life balance. Fishing offers the perfect outlet that strengthens their family bond.
Now living in Buford, GA, just north of Atlanta, Fernando and his family often travel to the coast. Together, they enjoy the best Atlantic saltwater fishing adventures and create unforgettable memories on the water.
With this in mind, Fernando Jose Cuellar Membreno reels in his five favorite fishing spots on the Atlantic Ocean. 
Florida Keys 
Just 15 miles off the coast of Miami, the Florida Keys are a saltwater fishing enthusiast’s own personal oasis. With more than 125 miles of coral islands, anglers can enjoy world-class charters all year long. In addition to the warm weather, the crystal clear waters are littered with highly sought-after species, like tarpon, swordfish, and marlin.
Montauk, New York 
Saltwater fishing evokes images of southern, tropical climates. However, Montauk has established itself as one of the premier fishing destinations in the world. This is due to the large schools of fish that pass through the area as they migrate from Long Island Sound to Maine each year. Bass is the most common catch during late summer and early fall, but off-shore canyons present opportunities for more deep-sea fishing throughout the season.
Cape Cod, Massachusetts 
“Cod” might be in its name, but don’t let that fool you. Cape Cod is home to swordfish, bluefish, tuna, wahoo, and so much more too. It has also earned an outstanding reputation. Yet it’s more than just casting a line. Enjoy the ambiance and attractions of this historical area. With idyllic lighthouses, sandy beaches, and water parks, Fernando Jose Cuellar Membreno recommends Cape Cod as a must-stop for the entire family. 
Galveston, Texas 
Everything is bigger in Texas. This applies to its fish too. Off the Gulf of Mexico, giant kingfish, yellowfin tuna, and flounder populate these waters. But the available species are much more diverse than people give them credit for. What makes Galveston so unique are locations for both inland and off-shore fishing.
Venice, Louisiana 
New Orleans is the marquee attraction, but the real draw might be Venice. This tiny town is situated in the heart of the Mississippi River Delta, making it fertile for fishing. The marshy bayous are overflowing with trophy catches, like black drum, speckled trout, and dolphins. Tucked at the very southeast of the state, it’s a world-famous choice for fishing off the beaten the path.

Assistive Technologies For Disabled People Who Like To Go Fishing 
by relaxgofishing February 16, 2022 
written by relaxgofishing 
Fishing is an activity that people with disabilities can enjoy. For instance, it allows them to relax and connect with family and friends. While physical, intellectual, or developmental impairments can affect daily life, assistive technology has advanced rapidly, making leisure more accessible for everyone.
For many non-disabled people, catching a fish or boarding a boat is routine. However, for a person with a disability, these experiences bring purpose, satisfaction, and personal enrichment. In fact, they can significantly improve mental health and overall well-being.
A wide range of adapted fishing equipment exists. For example, simple devices include rod holders, while advanced tools include motorized or casting reels that release the line with a push of a button. Even without motorized tools, accessible options are available. In addition, wheelchair-mounted rod holders, grip aids, and harnesses for limited arm reach help users fish safely and comfortably.
Assistive technology ensures that people with disabilities can live dignified, active lives. Moreover, the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) in Australia actively supports this goal. The Australian Government recognizes the right of people with disabilities to access devices that support leisure and mental health. As a result, programs like the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) make fishing and other hobbies accessible to all.
The NDIA has a program called the National Disability Insurance Scheme or NDIS. Its specialist support coordination feature can link all persons with disabilities to resources in their areas, including physicians, athletic clubs, support groups, libraries, and learning institutions and information about the support given by each state or local government.
Assistive devices range from simple mats to complex, custom technologies. However, the NDIS may require expert assessment for high-risk or specialized equipment. Participants often submit a clinical report to justify the device. In addition, the NDIS can fund evaluation, fitting, configuration, and training if these services are not included in the device cost. Participants keep devices as long as they need them. Finally, when equipment is no longer needed, it returns to an NDIA-approved service for refurbishment or recycling.
Disabilities should not prevent anyone from enjoying hobbies. For example, with adaptive technologies and government support, people with disabilities can fish, hunt, or engage in other leisure activities fully and safely. Moreover, these opportunities improve confidence, independence, and overall quality of life.

The Deciding on My Ice Fishing Shelter 
by relaxgofishing January 25, 2020 
written by relaxgofishing 
Deciding on my ice fishing shelter became important after years of relying on my friends to bring one out to the pond where I usually fish. As that old, homemade shelter started to fall apart and our schedules no longer aligned, I realized it was time to invest in my own setup to stay warm and dry. My thinking is below.
There were a few criteria I needed this shelter to meet 
Portability
I needed a way to get my shelter up to a pond to ice fish at least once a year, which includes a 6-hour drive. It needed to be something I could not only pack into the trunk of a sedan but carry out to the center of a pond (or farther) on my back or in a sled.
Warmth
Let’s not kid ourselves, the main reason we want a shelter is to stay out of the cold and preserve some warmth. Insulation of the key, and shelters, especially home-made ones, are notorious for letting in that icy breeze.
Accessibility
I wanted multiple entrances, so I could exit the shelter from either side. One reason for this is the weather. If it isn’t all that cold outside, but the wind is murderous, you might want to leave one door open, and the wind-facing door closed, so your shelter becomes more of wind-stop than an insulator.
The second reason is friends. I wanted two exists so we wouldn’t be tripping over each other if we needed to get out and stretch our legs or chase a trip-up flag.
Space
I wanted room for at least one other person, with a bucket/chair. The simple reason, I often have a few friends over, one of which has his own two-person shelter, but we might need room for four.
Darkness and Lighting
I wanted it to be dark inside the shelter, so the ice and snow beneath it would provide the majority of the lighting. Most shelters you purchase will be lined with black fabric on the inside, so this isn’t so much of a concern, but something you may want to check into before you purchase.
The dark lining inside allows you to stare down the hole to sight-fish your jigs inside the shelter. But I love my tip-ups more than sitting in the shelter…so I also needed an abundance of windows to watch flags.
The Final Choice 
There were many options that met the above criteria. It really came down to price, which took out the 2-man Frabil or Clam rectangular shelters that include a poly-foam floor with auger holes (look into the styles, you will see what I mean. So I looked into a pop-up style ice fishing shelter.
Once settling on this size, I knew I would need something labeled as at least a 3-man shelter since it is rare that 3 people would actually comfortably fit, but 2 definitely would. Then see reading reviews, and asking friends. Trust me; you should want to go ice fishing.

Creating Your Own Home Made Trip Ups Fishing 
by relaxgofishing January 24, 2020 
written by relaxgofishing 
Making your own homemade ice fishing trip-up can be fun and rewarding, especially when you build one that actually works on the ice.
Is It Portable?
You’ll need to carry it out to the ice, so make sure it’s not a herculean task to move.
Is It Fail-Safe?
Think about what happens when your device fails. A tip-up can freeze, wind up, or fall through the ice. Plan ways to prevent these problems before you start building.
Is It Effective?
You need to know that it can not only hook a fish but also alert you when a fish bites. Reliability is everything when you’re standing on the ice.
Is It Easy to Use?
Think about set-up and tear-down time. How long will it take to spool the line if a fish runs? The best tip-ups let you set them, then reset them quickly when needed.
Can It Be Replicated?
If your design works well portable, reliable, and predictable you’ll want to make more than one. How easy will it be to rebuild the same trip-up?
Don’t Overthink It
You might imagine the ultimate contraption one that signals a bite, sets the hook, and reels in the fish. Go ahead and try! But while you’re dealing with frozen parts, dead batteries, and broken pieces lost under the ice, I’ll be reeling in fish with my simple three-part trip-up made in under thirty minutes.
The designs you already see catching fish on the ice are your best starting point. They’ve been tested and proven. You don’t need to reinvent the wheel just grab your tools and build something that works.
Borrow from the Best
If your goal is to catch fish, start with what’s already effective. Most of what you need is already available. Fishing line comes on spools. Rods already act as line guides. Even if your idea seems unique, someone has likely tried it before—and there’s usually a reason you don’t see that design used widely.
Dissecting the Ice Fishing Trip-Up
Before creating your own design, look at existing ones closely. Study how they work, what makes them effective, and which parts are essential.
The main functional parts of a trip-up are:
• Spool: Holds the line and lets it run smoothly when a fish pulls.
• Anchor: Keeps the tip-up above the ice so it doesn’t slip into the hole.
• Alarm: Alerts you when the spool releases line. This usually includes a trigger that activates your chosen signal system.

The Best Feed Mixture Horizon for Fishing 
by relaxgofishing January 23, 2020 
written by relaxgofishing 
Every angler knows that bait works best when it stays within a fish’s reach for as long as possible. The longer the bait remains in its zone, the higher the chance of a bite. Since most mixtures sink to the bottom and only a few can stay near the surface or midwater for long, anglers use different feeding methods for bottom, surface, and mid-depth fish.
In simple terms, the closer your fishing line is to the bottom, the less often you need to throw bait. When the mixture settles on the bottom, you can feed less frequently but should increase the amount of initial bait. A strong base helps fish find the feeding area more easily.
Feeding Frequency and Fish Behavior
Frequent tossing of ground bait not only attracts bottom-feeding fish but also spreads scent and particles through the water. This improves visibility and helps more fish notice the feeding spot. Sometimes, frequent feeding even encourages fish to rise toward the surface. Larger, more active fish often chase away smaller ones, creating a clear feeding hierarchy.
However, this approach has limits. Too much feeding can break up the bait, letting the current carry particles away. As a result, inexperienced anglers often struggle to control their feeding patterns, especially in running water where currents can shift without warning.
The Challenge of Accurate Casting
Casting accuracy matters most in places with unpredictable flow, such as man-made channels or competition sites. In calm water, a short or long cast only slightly widens the feeding zone. But in moving water, even small errors let the current spread your bait downstream. You then waste time covering a larger area to find active fish.
Accurate throws also help you hit the right fishing point the spot where bait meets the fish’s feeding depth. Keeping the bait in that position lets you target the most productive zone without constantly adjusting to changes in current.
Finding the Absolute Fishing Depth
“Absolute depth” refers to how far the bait travels from the surface to the fish. Several factors affect this: how often you feed, the size of each portion, the amount of starting bait, and how it mixes with water. When fishing above the bottom, slowly sinking particles must pass through the feeding zone repeatedly. Because each particle sinks at a different speed, some stay longer within reach.
As the depth increases, you should reduce the frequency of throws but increase the size of each portion. The bait must be visible and concentrated enough for fish to notice it even in deep water. Otherwise, it disperses too much and turns into a weak column instead of an inviting cloud.

While Fishing Feeding Tactics is Good Thinking 
by relaxgofishing January 22, 2020 
written by relaxgofishing 
Summer feeding in fishing refers to the practice of using a special feed mixture throughout the entire open-water season. This method helps anglers attract and hold fish in one area for longer periods. The success of summer feeding depends on how you mix, portion, and deliver the bait. By mastering this feeding technique, you can greatly improve both the quality and quantity of your catch.
Factors Influencing Feeding Features
The best feeding strategy depends on several fishing conditions:
• Fishing Horizon (Relative Depth) This describes where the fish stay in the water — near the bottom, in mid-water, or at the surface.
• Water Current The current’s strength, direction, and steadiness affect how the bait spreads and settles.
• Absolute Depth This is the distance from the hook to the surface. It differs from the relative depth because you might fish 30–40 cm from the bottom in one case and 2 m deep in another, even if the total depth is 4 m.
• Bottom Characteristics The bottom’s slope and soil type influence how the bait stays in place. A steep or soft bottom needs a different feeding method than a flat, firm one.
• Distance to the Fishing Spot The farther the bait must go, the more important accuracy and portion size become.
• Fish Behavior Some fish act shy and avoid movement, while others explore actively. Knowing this helps you decide how often to feed.
• Target Fish Size Bigger and older fish are cautious. Feed them carefully and with smaller, precise portions.
• Competition Nearby anglers can affect your results. If they act more skillfully, you may need to adjust your tactics.
• Feeding Activity of Fish Fish feed more or less depending on temperature, time, and weather. Adapt your rhythm to their mood.
• Unwanted Fish Species Sometimes, small or unwanted fish arrive first. Adjust your mix and timing to avoid attracting them.
From all these conditions, anglers can form the most effective feeding tactics for the day. Success depends on adapting your mix, rhythm, and delivery to what’s happening underwater.

The Best Things You Must Have in Your Tackle Box for Fishing 
by relaxgofishing January 21, 2020 
written by relaxgofishing 
Every angler should know the must-have fishing tackle box items that make a day on the water successful and stress-free. Fishing combines patience, skill, and a love of the outdoors, but having the right gear in your tackle box ensures you spend more time catching fish and less time scrambling for supplies. Here’s a list of essential items that every angler should carry.
Needle-Nosed Pliers
A sturdy pair of needle-nosed pliers is an absolute must. Treble hooks on lures can be tricky, and trying to remove them with your fingers is a recipe for disaster. With good pliers, you can safely extract hooks from a fish’s mouth without injuring yourself or the fish. Trust me your hands will thank you, and you’ll avoid unnecessary trips to the emergency room.
Bug Spray
If you plan to fish in areas like Northern Ontario or other buggy regions, bug spray isn’t optional it’s survival. Mid-summer evenings bring swarms of mosquitoes and other insects that can ruin your trip. A strong insect repellent keeps you comfortable and focused on fishing instead of swatting at bugs.
Extra Spools of Fishing Line
Fishing lines can snap unexpectedly, especially when you land a big catch. Carrying extra spools of your preferred line ensures you can quickly re-spool a reel and continue fishing without frustration. Having spares on hand saves time and prevents missed opportunities.
Cell Phone
While fishing is about escaping the daily grind, a cell phone provides peace of mind. Emergencies can happen anywhere, and having a phone ensures that you can call for help if needed. Keep it in a waterproof case to protect it from splashes and accidents.
Assorted Lures and Backups
Lures are the heart of your tackle box, but fish can be finicky. Losing a favorite lure to a snag can disrupt your rhythm. Always carry backups of your go-to lures, so you can continue fishing successfully even if accidents happen.
Assorted Sinkers
Different water conditions and fish species require different weights. Your tackle box should include egg sinkers, split shot, bullet weights, and barrel weights in various sizes. Having options allows you to adjust your setup quickly to match the fishing environment.
Assorted Hooks
Fish come in all shapes and sizes, and you’ll need a variety of hooks to catch them. Carrying four to five different sizes ensures you’re ready for anything from walleye to bass. You never know when a sudden change in targets might occur during a trip.
Jigheads and Twister Tails
Jigheads and twister tails are versatile tools that work in many fishing scenarios. Keep 1/4 and 3/8 ounce jigheads in your favorite colors, along with matching twister tails. They’re perfect for adjusting to fish behavior and water conditions on the fly.
Lighter or Matches in a Baggie
Preparation is key, and a lighter or waterproof matches in a baggie can come in handy in emergencies. Whether you need to start a fire or light a stove during a camping-fishing trip, having a reliable fire source adds a layer of safety.
Spare Spools for Reels
Finally, always store spare spools in your tackle box. Ideally, pre-fill them with line so you can quickly swap out a broken spool. This small step keeps your fishing trip smooth and uninterrupted.
Conclusion
A well-stocked tackle box makes fishing more enjoyable and less stressful. From pliers and bug spray to extra line and lures, these essentials ensure that you’re prepared for anything on the water. Start with these items, and over time, you can expand your collection to match your personal style and favorite fishing spots. Remember, the right preparation doesn’t just improve your chances of catching fish it makes every trip safer and more enjoyable.
