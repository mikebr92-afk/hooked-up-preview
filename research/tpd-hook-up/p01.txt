Hook Up Fishing has access to some of the most pristine fishing locations Far North Queensland has to offer, taking you on an adventure of a lifetime. 
​Using an array of techniques we will be targeting Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Queen Fish, Coral Trout, Spanish Mackerel, Nannygai and many more. 
​Mud Crabbing also available on private charters. 
With his 5.5 meter Cairns custom craft and its purpose built shallow draft, Skipper Glen Shaw can take you to the places the others don't go. 
​Fishing is his passion, with 20 years of experience commercial and charter fishing in the local area. 
From freshwater rainforest and saltwater estuary to the inshore reefs, he knows the grounds and offers a wide range of fishing from novice to the experienced angler. 
The kids will love it too!
