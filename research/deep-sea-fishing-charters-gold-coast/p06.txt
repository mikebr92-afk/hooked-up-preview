Bucks Party Fishing Gold Coast 
Bucks Parties With a Bang!
So you’ve assembled the boys for a wicked weekend away in Australia’s party capital. The Gold coast has a wide variety of night life for the bucks party goer, but what are you going to do with the day light hours? We Offer exciting offshore reef and game fishing trips and Broadwater cruises. 
Weather you have 6 blokes or a group of 30, Gold coast fishing charters can customize a bucks party fishing trip or cruise to make your bucks party on the Gold coast a memorable one.
BUCKS PARTIES, BUCKS DAY CRUISES AND FISHING
We have 2 boats available for exclusive private bucks parties. 
We can organise food, soft drinks, ice, lingerie/bikini waitress, strippers etc at an additional cost or (BYO-please advise when booking). 
Available trips are 5 hours Gold Coast fishing, 9 hours fishing, or 3-5 hour Broadwater cruises. Boats are available 7 days a week all year round.
