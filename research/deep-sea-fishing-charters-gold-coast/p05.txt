Corporate Fishing Charters 
Let Us Customize a Charter to Suit Your Needs.
Gold coast fishing charters specialize in private group charters and cruises for corporate groups, or just a great day out fishing with a group of friends. We own and operate two quality fishing charter boats and can cater for groups off various sizes. With a maximum capacity of 30 people. More boats can be organised if required. We supply all the equipment you need to catch the big one!! Our half day price includes all bait and tackle, cold bottled water and expert crew. Our full day price includes all bait and tackle, cold bottled water and expert crew. Customized catering for corporate groups can be organised on request we have various options available. 
• Nibble platters.
• Buffet lunch.
• Chips and biscuits.
• Soft drinks.
• Alcoholic beverages can also be supplied.
Enjoy a great day out fishing with your work colleague’s and mates, our professional experienced friendly crews will go out of their way to ensure you and your group have a great fishing experience. Charters depart from The Spit pontoon, Muriel Henchman drive Main beach Gold coast, just 10 minutes from Surfers paradise and 60 minutes from Brisbane CBD.
