Full Day Fishing Charter Group Bookings 
Full Days By Private Charter Only
Gold Coast fishing Charters 9 Hour full day charter. By Private Group bookings only. 12, 16 or 28 people. 
Full day Fishing Charter departs at 6:00 am. We head out through the Seaway to the Offshore fishing grounds for a great day of Gold Coast reef and game Fishing. 
9 hour full day fishing trips are perfect for the more experienced fisherman or those that want a few extra hours fishing time, our friendly, experienced, Skipper and deckhand will show you the best techniques for catching a wide variety of bottom dwelling and pelagic fish species. Target species include Cobia, Mackerel, Marlin, Trag, Tuskfish, Snapper, Jewfish, Tuna, Wahoo, King fish, Samson fish, Amberjack, Pearl perch, Tailor, Trevally and many more. 
Inclusions: All bait and fishing tackle, ice cold, bottled water, full tuition, legal fish gutted, gilled and bagged. Buffet lunch available at additional cost. 
Participant Guidelines: We advise all of our clients to listen closely to our experienced crews to ensure your safety while out on the water, we will brief you on safety equipment and how to use it before departure. Once we arrive at fishing grounds our crew will demonstrate and explain safe and correct methods of how to use our deep sea fishing tackle. We advise all passengers to bring a hat and sunscreen for your own protection from harmful UV rays. Our boat is in current Queensland department of transport survey and is fully equipped with all required safety equipment, on board toilet, padded seats and full cover for protection from the elements. 
Departure Time: 6am sharp, please arrive at jetty by 5.40am. 
Weather conditions: We do not fish if strong winds or big seas are forecast. It is very important that you provide a phone number that we can contact you on the night before your trip in case the charter has to be re scheduled or cancelled in the event of bad weather. We only cancel if high winds and/or big seas are forecast. We do not cancel if it’s raining, if rain is forecast we advise that you bring a suitable jacket or rain coat. 
Departure point: The Spit pontoon Muriel Henchman Drive Main Beach 4217 Go past SeaWorld Nara Resort, look for blue sign with boat ramp/Charter boats, turn left at Muriel Henchman drive. Please note, the boat does not live here permanently, its a public pick up jetty, please wait at top of jetty ramp.
