Princess II Fishing Trip 
Hervey Bay 
Join one of Hervey Bay’s most loved fishing charters with the full-day Princess II Fishing Trip. Perfect for families, beginners, and experienced anglers, this relaxed yet exciting day on the water includes everything supplied - bait, tackle, food, and expert crew. You simply step onboard and enjoy an unforgettable day of fishing in the calm waters off Hervey Bay & K'gari.
Highlights 
• 45 ft purpose built fishing vessel with full walk around lower deck (for better fishing)
• Likely caught fish species - local reef fish, snapper, sweet lip, red butterfly bream, cod, mackeral, tuna, trevally.
• Upper deck lounge for relaxation and viewing marine life
• Onshore reef fishing in Platypus Bay
• Live bait tank
• Fish cleaned and iced
• Keep your catch
• An experienced skipper boasting 25+ years of fishing and local knowledge
• Latest technology - navigation & fish finding equipment
• Trolling to and from fishing grounds (80% strike rate)
• On board domestic toilet & hand basin
• BYO esky & drinks if you like (no glass)
Inclusions 
• Bottomless tea and coffee
• Delicious rib fillet BBQ lunch with salads
• Rod, reel & tackle (or bring your own if you prefer)
• Fresh local bait
Times 
• Departs daily (if minimum numbers reached - we carry 5 to 13 passengers)
• Meet at 7.00 AM
• Depart Fishermans Wharf at 7.30 AM
• Return to Fishermans Wharf 3.30 PM
Price 
• All passengers $275
