South Pacific II Charters 
Whitsundays & Lizard Island Fishing Charters
1/37
South Pacific 2 , Major Tom and our crew are back! And we are putting together charter packages for guests in 2025. 
Charters will be based in the Whitsundays in September, using Hamilton Island as the base, before heading to Lizard Island in October and November using Cooktown as a base. 
South Pacific 2 is our 72′ mothership famous for fishing the Qld coast for the last 50 years. She has recently completed a major upgrade and is in great condition. 
Major Tom (32′) and Tom (24′) are our centre consoles that are perfect for the conditions and fishing on the reef. 
We will be reef fishing for the emperor, nannygai, trout and cod. We will also be popping for the giant trevally and trolling for the mackerel and tuna. Depending on conditions we might even target the billfish. 
WHAT IS INCLUDED 
All meals from our chef. Breakfast is a selection of continental and cooked options. Lunch is wraps and sandwiches. Dinner is top shelf and will include seafood options that have been caught. 
We provide all the tackle you will need. This includes game fishing rods, jigging rods, popping rods and stick baiting rods, as well as a wide selection of lures and terminal tackle. 
NOT INCLUDED 
Alcohol which we can order for you. 
Flights. We will organise transfers for Hamilton or Cooktown airports. 
Accommodation in Cooktown. Ribbon Reef trips we recommend arriving day early and staying at the Sovereign Resort Hotel. 
Damage to rods and reels. 
PRICING 
Get in touch for available dates and pricing.
