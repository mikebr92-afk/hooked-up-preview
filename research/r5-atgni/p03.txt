FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
WHAT MAKES EXMOUTH ONE OF THE BEST FISHING DESTINATIONS IN AUSTRALIA?
Exmouth is known for its incredible variety of sport and game fish, with easy access to both Ningaloo Reef and its deep-sea drop-offs and canyons. From marlin and sailfish to coral trout and red emperor, it’s one of the top sport fishing and game fishing destinations in Australia
WHY HAVE CHARTER PRICES INCREASED?
Due to rising fuel costs, we’ve had to adjust our charter rates to maintain the quality of our trips. 
HOW MUCH DOES A CHARTER WITH ALL THE GEAR NO IDEA SPORTFISHING COST?
We offer premium fishing charters in Exmouth with pricing designed for small groups of up to 6 anglers.
Half-Day Charter: $1,800 (total for the the boat, up to 6 anglers)
Full-Day Charter: $3000 (total for the boat, up to 6 anglers)
These prices include all top-quality fishing gear, bait, tackle, lunches, light snacks, soft drinks, water, cleaning/filleting your fish, videos/photos of your charter, and expert guidance from our experienced crew. Whether you’re chasing game fish or enjoying a family day out on the water, you’ll get the full Exmouth fishing experience.
You can check availability and book online via our Booking Page , or contact us directly to customise your trip.
WHAT TYPES OF FISHING CHARTERS DO YOU OFFER IN EXMOUTH?
We offer half-day, full-day, multi-day, and custom fishing charters out of Exmouth, Western Australia.
Whether you’re keen on light tackle sport fishing, chasing big game fish like marlin and sailfish, or just a fun day out on the water, we tailor the trip to suit your style.
WHAT FISH CAN I CATCH ON A CHARTER IN EXMOUTH?
Depending on the time of year, you can catch marlin, sailfish, tuna, mahi-mahi, wahoo, Spanish mackerel, coral trout, red emperor, and more. Exmouth’s waters are alive year-round.
DO I NEED A FISHING LICENCE FOR A CHARTER IN EXMOUTH?
No fishing licence is needed – all our guests are covered under our commercial fishing charter licence. Just show up and enjoy the day on the water!
WHERE DO YOU DEPART FROM?
Our fishing charters depart from Tantabiddi boat ramp. You’ll receive detailed meeting instructions before your charter.
CAN BEGINNERS JOIN YOUR FISHING CHARTERS?
Absolutely! We cater to beginners, kids, and experienced anglers alike. We provide all the gear, instructions, and support you need.
ARE YOUR CHARTERS SUITABLE FOR FAMILIES AND KIDS?
Yes, we welcome families with children. Private charters are a great option for families wanting a personalised day on the water.
WHAT SHOULD I BRING ON A FISHING CHARTER IN EXMOUTH?
Bring a hat, sunscreen, water bottle, sunglasses, and a camera, and sea sickness tablets if required. We supply all gear, bait, ice, and safety equipment. Dress appropriately for sun and sea conditions.
HOW DO I BOOK A FISHING CHARTER?
You can book directly through our website at allthegearnoideasportfishing.com , call us on 0413 392 276, or message us on Facebook or Instagram . We recommend booking in advance, especially during peak fishing seasons in Exmouth.
