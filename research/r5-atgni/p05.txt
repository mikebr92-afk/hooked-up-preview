ABOUT
ABOUT ALL THE GEAR NO IDEA SPORTFISHING
In the sun-drenched coastal town of Exmouth, a fishing adventure awaits those who dare to explore the open waters.
Welcome to All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing, a fishing charter company that embodies passion, perseverance, and the pursuit of dreams. Our story is one of dedication and bold decisions, led by a captain who truly put it all on the line to make his dream a reality.
OUR STORY
All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing was born from a burning passion for the sea and the thrill of the catch. Our captain, a seasoned angler with an unwavering love for fishing, made a life-changing decision to turn his dream into reality. Selling his house and putting everything on the line to purchase the game fishing vessel “Black N Blue”, he embarked on a journey to establish a premier fishing charter in Exmouth.
This bold move was driven by a passion and desire to share the exhilaration of Game fishing with others. Our captain’s dedication and willingness to take risks have paid off, turning All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing into a beloved name among fishing enthusiasts.
OUR HISTORY
The history of All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing is woven with tales of adventure and camaraderie. From its humble beginnings to becoming a sought-after fishing charter, the journey has been remarkable. Our captain’s commitment to providing unforgettable experiences has attracted anglers from near and far, eager to explore the rich waters of Exmouth.
Over the years, we’ve built a reputation for excellence, offering top-notch fishing charters that cater to both seasoned fishermen and novices alike. Our team is passionate about sharing their knowledge and ensuring every guest leaves with memories to last a lifetime.
OUR COMMITMENT TO EXCELLENCE
At All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing, our mission is to deliver exceptional fishing experiences that exceed expectations. We pride ourselves on our attention to detail, personalized service, and a genuine passion for the sport. Our team is dedicated to ensuring your time with us is unforgettable, from the moment you step aboard to the final catch of the day.
Join us for an adventure on the high seas and discover why All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing is the top choice for fishing charters in Exmouth. Let our captain and crew guide you on a journey filled with excitement, camaraderie, and the thrill of the catch.
We invite you to become part of our story and create your own memories with All The Gear No Idea Sportfishing. Book your charter today and experience the adventure of a lifetime.

PLANNING YOUR TRIP TO EXMOUTH
To make the most of your fishing adventure in Exmouth, it’s important to plan ahead. Consider the time of year, as certain species are more prevalent during specific seasons. Booking a personalized charter in advance ensures you secure your preferred dates and have ample time to discuss your goals and expectations with the charter operator.
ACCOMODATION AND TRAVEL
Exmouth offers a range of accommodation options to suit different budgets and preferences. From beachfront resorts to cozy cabins, there’s something for everyone. When planning your trip, consider the proximity to the marina and other amenities to ensure a convenient and enjoyable stay.
WHAT TO BRING
When embarking on a fishing charter, it’s essential to pack appropriately. Sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses are a must to protect against the sun. Comfortable clothing and non-slip shoes are recommended for a day on the boat. Don’t forget to bring a camera to capture the memories of your fishing adventure. We also have cameras running all day on the boat to provide you with memories from your trip.
Don’t forget to follow us on Facebook and Instagram. You’ll stay up to date with news and offers. Plus, you’ll see great photos and footage from our latest fishing adventures!!
