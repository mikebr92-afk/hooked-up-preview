VESSEL
"BLACK N BLUE"
Welcome to the world of luxury fishing charters. Where the thrill of the catch meets the comfort of a high-end game fishing boat.
Meet “Black N Blue,” our custom-built 40ft luxury Black Watch. It’s more than just a boat. It’s a testament to our commitment to providing an exceptional fishing experience.
Located in Exmouth, a prime destination for game fishing, “Black N Blue” is ready to take you on an unforgettable journey. With twin Cummins QSC 540hp engines, it’s a powerhouse on the water.
But it’s not just about power. It’s about the perfect blend of luxury and functionality. State-of-the-art electronics, high-quality fishing gear, and a custom fit-out designed for all Exmouth fishing styles.
“Black N Blue” can accommodate up to 8 guests and 2 crew members. It’s the perfect setting for a day of sportfishing or a relaxing getaway.
Join us as we delve into the features and experiences that make “Black N Blue” a premier choice for luxury fishing charters in Exmouth.
UNMATCHED POWER AND PERFORMANCE
At the heart of “Black N Blue” lie its robust twin Cummins QSC 540hp engines. These engines promise reliable power and efficient performance, ensuring smooth sailing regardless of sea conditions. Their strength is unparalleled, providing both speed and stability for all your fishing excursions.
Our custom 40ft Black Watch game boat handles both calm and challenging waters with ease. This reliability elevates your fishing adventure, making it truly exceptional. Designed with performance in mind, the vessel ensures you reach the best fishing spots swiftly and safely. With “Black N Blue,” every journey is one of power and precision.
CUSTOMIZATION FOR EVERY ANGLER
“Black N Blue” is meticulously tailored to accommodate various fishing styles found in Exmouth. We understand each angler’s needs differ, and our boat reflects this flexibility. From experienced to novice anglers, “Black N Blue” caters to all with ease.
The custom fit-out of our boat adapts to different possibilities. We provide top-tier equipment and high-quality fishing gear, making sure every guest is well-equipped. These customized features ensure that no matter your fishing style, “Black N Blue” is your ideal companion for an unforgettable adventure.
STATE OF THE ART ELECTRONICS
Navigating the waters of Exmouth with precision is crucial for a successful fishing trip. “Black N Blue” is equipped with state-of-the-art electronics that set it apart in the realm of fishing charters. These advanced systems enhance navigation, ensuring you reach prime fishing spots with ease.
Our high-tech fish finding equipment maximizes your chances of landing a prized catch. With the integration of cutting-edge technology, anglers can benefit from real-time data and insights. This intelligent system is a testament to our commitment to providing an exceptional fishing experience, making each journey productive and exciting.
LUXURY AMENITIES FOR A PREMIER FISHING CHARTER
“Black N Blue” doesn’t just offer a fishing trip, it provides a luxurious experience. Onboard, guests enjoy comfort with features often found in premium charters. The vessels elegant interior design creates a relaxing atmosphere that’s perfect for unwinding after a day on the water.
Indulge in top-of-the-line amenities that enhance your adventure. Whether it’s the plush seating or the gourmet galley, every detail is designed for your enjoyment. This dedication to luxury ensures that your time aboard is as comfortable as it is thrilling, making every moment memorable.
DESIGNED FOR THE GAME FISHING ENTHUSIAST
“Black N Blue” offers an unparalleled experience for game fishing enthusiasts. With a custom fit-out, it caters to all Exmouth fishing styles. Whether you’re targeting marlin or tuna, the vessel equips you with high-quality fishing gear tailored for various pursuits.
Novice and seasoned anglers alike will find the environment conducive to both skill development and enjoyment. The vessels sturdy design and stability ensure smooth sailing in any sea condition, while the expansive deck space allows for ease of movement and optimal casting opportunities. Every element is crafted to deliver the thrill and excitement of game fishing at its best.
A CREW THAT KNOWS THE WATER
On “Black N Blue,” you’re guided by an experienced crew with vast local knowledge. They know Exmouth waters like the back of their hand. Their insights lead you to the best fishing spots, ensuring a fruitful trip.
The crew’s expertise extends beyond location guidance. They assist with techniques and tips to catch your dream fish. Safety and guest satisfaction are their top priorities. Their passion for the sea and fishing enriches your charter experience, creating memories you’ll cherish forever. Trust in their skills for an unforgettable adventure aboard this luxury Black Watch game boat.
WHY CHOOSE "BLACK N BLUE" FOR YOUR CHARTER
Choosing “Black N Blue” means opting for excellence. This luxury Black Watch, known for its unmatched performance, combines comfort and functionality. Its renowned Black Watch design promises a seamless experience on the open sea.
Our dedicated crew brings expertise and enthusiasm, elevating each trip into a unique adventure. From novice to expert anglers, every guest receives personalized service. Embrace the thrill of game and sport fishing aboard a vessel designed to exceed expectations. “Black N Blue” stands out not just for its features but for creating unforgettable memories. Make it your top choice for luxury fishing charters.
