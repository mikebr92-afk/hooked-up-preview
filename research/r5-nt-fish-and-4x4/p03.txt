ABOUT NT FISH & 4X4 
NT Fish & 4x4 is a proudly indigenously owned family operated company. 
Our experience comes from 5 generations of knowledge who have forged a living on the mighty Daly River over the past century. 
We have a passion for Fishing, heritage and exploring the Northern Territory. 
Our focus is building a legacy for our children and providing affordable recreational activities for all of our guests. 
We cater for most age groups and experience levels and can accommodate larger groups or provide a more personal experience. 
We specialise in creating an adventure package that is unique and suited to your needs. 
​ ​ ​ 
Daly River our home. 
The Daly River district is one of the major tourist destinations within the Northern Territory. The Aboriginal name for the community is Nauiyu. 
Daly River itself is a picturesque tidal river, home to both freshwater and saltwater crocodiles, and a plentiful source of barramundi. 
Facilities within Nauiyu include an airstrip, general store, Roadside inn and medical clinic. 
There are many fishing and hunting opportunities within the area. 
Daly River has a public boat ramp located on Wooliana road and provides number of camping grounds/caravan parks within the district. ​ 
Daly River is located 223 km south of Darwin via the Stuart Highway and the Daly River Road. It is 110 km south-west of Adelaide River. ​ 
​ 
Our vehicles and vessels. 
We pride ourselves on having some of the best equipment to tackle any adventure you would like during your visit. 
Our vehicles consist of extremely well equipped Landcruiser 300 series offering comfort and touring performance, perfectly accompanied by our rugged late model Holden Colorado equipped with everything you need to go remote. 
​ 
Our Flagship vessel MEDUSA a next level fishing machine, 7.5 meter, 300hp fibreglass Pathfinder. This boat is in 2D survey with the latest in fish finding technology and angling features. 
this vessel is licenced for 5 anglers and is ready to take you on the extended runs to the coastal creeks for prime Barramundi run off fishing. 
​ 
The Opal our second vessel is a very capable Stabicraft 4.5m frontier. Safety is second to none with this vessel the high sides matched with the level floatation ensures your safety on the water. matched perfectly with a 70hp Yamaha this vessel has the versatility to fish tight creeks and navigate the open river with confidence. licenced for 3 anglers we can ensure a fantastic time aboard this well equipped barra boat.
