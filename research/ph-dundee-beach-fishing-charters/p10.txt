FISH WITH THE EXPERTS 
AT DUNDEE BEACH 
Unbeatable local knowledge and experience to find your #BIGFISHNT 
We love what we do, and our passion for adventure and fishing is infectious!​ 
Whether you are a seasoned angler or a novice looking to experience the thrill of catching your first Barramundi, our charters are tailored to meet your needs and provide an unforgettable adventure. 
Bringing unmatched expertise to Darwin's fishing scene! Our team's deep knowledge of Darwin and Dundee Beach's waters and commitment to top-notch charters make us the go-to choice for anglers looking for the ultimate Darwin fishing adventure. 
​ 
You'll be fishing with guides who are just as excited about your trip as you are! 
OUR GUIDES 
With over 25 years combined experience, our expert guides are always keen and helpful in demonstrating tips and techniques of fishing. 
​ 
Experience and results 
DO COUNT 
when your choosing your fishing charter. 
​ 
Don't settle for second best! 
We believe a fishing trip should be about catching lots of what you want to catch and having a great time while you're at it! ​ ​ 
Whether you're planning your first Top End Barramundi Fishing Adventure , looking to bag that Big Red , wanting a Mixed Bag to take home, or looking for a new location out of Darwin, we tailor our Northern Territory fishing charters to suit your requirements. 
​ 
Dundee is easy to get to and we would love to share it with you! 
WHY FISH WITH US 
TRUE LOCALS 
We have the most local knowledge fishing Dundee of all the Darwin fishing charter operators.​ 
​ 
Our professional guides know where and how to find the fish, and know how to look after you.​ 
​ 
Our boats are perfect for local conditions, and we always use the best tackle and quality Shimano equipment to ensure you get the best experience. 
ACCREDITATION 
We are licensed Northern Territory fishing tour operators. 
​ 
Our guides hold coxswain qualifications as well as first aid certificates.​ 
​ 
Our boats are in NT marine survey for charter fishing up to 15 nautical miles offshore. 
SUSTAINABILITY 
We adhere to all bag limits and size restrictions to ensure the future of our fish.​ 
​ 
We practice responsible fishing methods and encourage catch and release of fish surplus to your needs.​ 
​ 
Fish that have a low chance of survival if released will be kept as part of the bag limit. 
The lads always work really hard to get us on to great fish every time.
