AFTER A BIG DAYS FISHING 
Everything you need to refresh and recharge - at your fingertips. 
After a day out on the boat reeling in your big catch, there's nothing better than relaxing by the pool with a drink in hand and retelling the stories of the day! 
REFRESH 
Cool off in our large in-ground swimming pool and enjoy a few drinks after a big day fishing. Our fully equipped kitchen and large outdoor entertaining area is perfect for relaxing while your meals are prepared by our in house chef. 
RECHARGE 
Our Dundee Beach Retreat is the perfect place to 'recharge'. The modern fully air-conditioned rooms offer luxury accommodation for up to 16 guests. Bedrooms are twin-share, with two king single beds, and are equipped with quiet split system air conditioning. 
INDULGE 
Indulge in our fully catered services , including 3 chef prepared meals a day, all included on our extended stay charters with your individual dietary requirements fully catered for. 
Guests will be treated to fabulous meals like our famous Territory Mud Crabs and mouth-watering fresh fish Seafood Nights. 
REJUVENATE 
Our newly renovated bathrooms offer luxury facilites to enable you to rejuvenate in style, and revive for the next day's fishing.
