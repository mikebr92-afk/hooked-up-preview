Our new boat launched November 2020
It’s unbelievably great 
After close to 30 years of loyal and reliable service from our original vessel, Broadbill, it was time for an upgrade. 
The design brief was simple enough, a user friendly, full walk around cabin design that is both great as a fishing platform and for whale and dolphin watching. Add to that list, it needed to be fast with a shallow draft to navigate our shallow bar. 
John Pontifex from Plate Alloy Australia was enlisted to design our boat, Andrew Gatt to build it and Merimbula Mowers and Marine to fit it out.
The result is spectacular. We couldn’t be happier. 
At 11m in length, a 3.7m beam and powered by twin 300hp Yamaha engines it provides a magnificent fishing platform. She gets us to our destination faster than ever before which allows us to go further afield in the same time frame.
We have a toilet and hand washing facilities on board. 
We look forward to getting you out on it soon.
