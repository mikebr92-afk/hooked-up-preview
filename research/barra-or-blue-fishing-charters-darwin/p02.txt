Extended Fishing Charters 
Darwin’s Premier Extended Fishing Charter Company
Get the Barra or Blue Experience
The fishing at Dundee Beach is first class with a great range of species and lots of big hard fighting fish right on your doorstep!
Come chase the sought-after barramundi in our scenic river and estuary systems that are teeming with wildlife. The systems also hold good numbers of big NT mud crabs, Threadfin Salmon and Blackjewfish.
The Bluewater is home to some of the best-eating reef fish in the ocean including Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Bluebone and Golden Snapper. We are happy to have fresh fish cooked for you as you wish and there is also the option to prepare a freezer box if you would like to take some fillets home.
Some of the prized Sportsfish Dundee offers include big Trevally, Tuna, queenfish, cobia and Spanish Mackeral. Sailfish and Marlin are also an option at the right time of year.
We will mix up fishing styles and tactics to what is working best at the time to make sure you have the best experience possible. All our guides are expert fishermen with local knowledge and will ensure you are in the right spot at the right time to maximise your chances. Don’t miss out on your chance to score the catch of a lifetime.

We’ve got your meals & accommodation covered! 
Nestled in the midst of a breathtaking rainforest, our Barra or Blue house boasts a pool with a stunning deck for relaxing and sipping on refreshing drinks.
The house features 5 bedrooms, each equipped with either twin or king beds to accommodate your needs. The house also has 2.5 bathrooms and a fully equipped kitchen, including a BBQ on the rear balcony and a bar with 8 bar stools on the front balcony.
In addition to the main house, there are also two cabins located on the property. These cabins can accommodate up to 3 people each and feature their own bathroom, shower, and kitchen. The house also features a large table and chairs for gatherings.
We have got you covered for all your meals, which includes the preparation of your freshly caught fish. Additionally, mouth-watering chilli mud crabs are available upon request. Limited spaces available.
