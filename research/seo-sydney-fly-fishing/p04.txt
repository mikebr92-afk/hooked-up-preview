FISHING FROTHERS - Kids Fishing Classes 
On boat lure Fishing Classes -ages 12-17 
Lure types, knots and rigs, retrieves, drag settings, reading the water, fish habits, casting techniques, fish fighting techniques, ethical handling, environmental stewardship. 
Do your kids froth over fishing? Mine do! As a father of identical twin boys who love fishing I couldn't think of a greater activity to get them out in the environment and away from screens. That's why I'm running the "Fishing Frothers" lure fishing courses.....on my boat! 
Whilst these are not fishing trips, they are 4 hour windows of opportunity for me to impart my extensive knowledge to youngsters about how to fish lures. By running these classes on the water we will have ample opportunity to see all our discoveries in practical action. 
From types of lures, to knots and rigs, retrieves, teasing the bite, hooking, fighting, reading the water, correct tackle, fish landing and handling, good marine stewardship and more. This will be a perfect springboard for your youngsters to head out onto the water either by boat or on foot and be more successful.
