TOURS & PAYMENTS 
Tours are available in Sydney Harbour or the Pittwater/Broken Bay/Hawkesbury region in Sydneys North. 
Justin can suggest the best area for fishing based on current fishing conditions, weather predictions, and the easiest pick-up location for you. 
​ 
Boat pick-up in Sydney Harbour can be located at: 
- Roseville Bridge boat ramp at Davidson Park State Recreation area, below Roseville Bridge at the end of Healey Way. ($8 parking fee or a national Parks entry pass required) 
​ 
-Mosman Rowing Club pontoons in front of Mosman Rowing Club, The Spit Reserve, Mosman (metered parking or an easy Taxi/uber ride from the city.) Please note, this is not Mosman Rowers which is a different location. 
​ 
In Pittwater, the pick-up location is Newport Public Wharf at the very end of Queens Parade below Newport Hotel (this is a 70-minute easy bus ride from Wynyard station with a bus stop only meters from the jetty). 
​ 
Start times are around 6:00-6:30 am for Spring/Summer/Autumn and Winter is usually  7:30-8:00 am. 
​ 
Tours can be tailored to your personal needs upon request. 
​ 
A 50% deposit is required upon booking confirmation, 7 days notice is required in the event of need to cancel. Full payment is required 7 days before the day of the charter. 
​ 
All equipment and licenses are supplied. 
​ 
All equipment is supplied on a pay if lost or broken basis and although lures and flies are provided Justin does reserve the right to charge for lost lures or flies in the case of adverse losses. 
*All gear charges are at Justin's greatly reduced prices and not at retail cost. 
*All prices include Merchant Credit Card Fee
