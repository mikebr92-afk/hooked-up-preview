HALF DAY CHARTER (5HRS) 
Trip Details 
This is the standard and preferred charter for most of our clients. The charter is for 5hrs from dawn to mid morning or from mid afternoon until dusk. 
We recommend these early mornings and afternoon time slots due to it being the prime fishing time however we can also alter these times upon request.
Depending on time of year we target Bream,Flathead,Whiting,Mulloway,Luderick,Tailor,Salmon,Kingfish,
Squid,Long Tail Tuna and can also add crabbing on request.
Most of the time our fishing will focus on using soft plastic and hard bodied lures however at times we will also fish with bait depending on our clients and the fish.
We provide High end Shimano Rods and Reels for the charter in a variety sizes depending on the targeted species. 
On charter you will also learn about the Port Stephens Marine Park and witness first hand the amazing scenery
and bird/ Marine life that make the Port Stephens waterway so special to fish.
PRICES FROM $200pp
Charter prices include fishing gear,soft plastic lures,bait,bottled water,Ice

FISH PORT STEPHENS KIDS CHARTER (2HRS) 
Trip Details 
A dedicated short fishing kids fishing tour Suitable for under 10 year olds. The aim of this trip is lots of activity rather then catching monsters however we will still spend some of the time targeting something bigger that they can take home too. Kids will learn the basics of fishing
*Learn about Port Stephens great chance of seeing Dolphins Turtles or penguins plus plenty of other wild life
*How to tie knots
*What size lines and leaders to use for what species.
*What lures and bait works best for what fish we are targeting.
*How to correctly cast with minimal effort.
*Times of year to fish for what species.
*How to identify key fish holding structure and features for different species. 
*The influence tide has on fishing different areas.
*A variety of different retrieves for different lures and how each species responds
*How to set your drag and fight a fish properly
* How to handle fish.
Plus loads more!!!
