Reef & Game Fishing – 8 Angler special – PRIVATE Half Day
0 
• Max People : 8 
• All year round Morning Charter - 5:00am – 10:30AM 
• All year round Afternoon Charter - 10:30am – 4:00pm 
• Duration 5 hours 30 minutes 
Reef Fishing available enquire about this adventure now! 
Private Charters are available for Half and Full Day Charters. Get a group of friends together and have some fun fishing the deep reefs.
Departure & Return Location 
The Spit Pontoon, Muriel Henchman Drive, Main Beach, QLD
Map 
Departure 
The boat is not permanently moored at the location so please wait at the pontoon for the boat to pick you up.
Inclusions 
• Top Quality Rods, Reels, Bait & Tackle 
• Soft Drinks and Water 
• Fully qualified, experienced, friendly & professional skipper and deckhand onboard to maximise your fishing experience 
• Latest Fish Finding Technology 
• Safety Equipment 
• Covered Rear Deck 
• Toilet 
Bring with you 
• Sunscreen 
• Hat 
• Sunglasses 
• Alcohol if required 
• Esky for your catch (Please keep this in your car) 
• Seasickness Tablets 
• Camera 
Terms and Conditions 
• NO refunds for late arrivals! If you are on an afternoon charter, allow plenty of travel time for congested traffic conditions. The boat needs to depart on time to ensure valuable paid fishing time for all parties. 
• Times and prices are subject to change without notice. Vessel departs daily and bookings are essential. Most weekend dates book out 4-6 weeks in advance. At times we have availability for smaller groups however it's best to book in early. 
• We do NOT gut, gill or clean your fish, and we legally cannot fillet any species. We recommend you bring an esky to take your fish home if you have a long journey. Eskies are not allowed onboard so please leave them in your car.
