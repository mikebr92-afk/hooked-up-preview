Location 
Find your way to “Fish The Deep” fishing charters Gold Coast!
Fish The Deep 
The Spit Pontoon, Muriel Henchman Drive
Main Beach, QLD
Mobile: +61 416 224 412
1.4kms North of Seaworld along Seaworld Drive (The Spit Pontoon.) Plenty of free parking available. 
Route Option From Brisbane
• From Pacific Highway take exit number 66: Smith Street.
• Follow for 10 minutes to the T-intersection at the end of Smith Street.
• Turn right onto Gold Coast Highway.
• Follow road for 2 km and once you go over The Southport Bridge (over Nerang River) turn into your first street on your left: Waterways Drive.
• Drive along Waterways Drive until you come to the first roundabout. Turn left at this roundabout and head North along Seaworld Drive.
• Follow this Road for 2.5 km and turn into Muriel Henchman Drive on your left. Drive for 160m.
• Click or tap on the map below to open Google Maps for turn-by-turn directions 
Route Option From Surfers Paradise
• From Gold Coast Highway heading North, take the exit for Seaworld & The Spit.
• Drive along Waterways Drive until you come to the first roundabout.
• Turn left at this roundabout and head North along Seaworld Drive.
• Follow this Road for 2.5 km and turn into Muriel Henchman Drive on your left. Drive for 160m.
