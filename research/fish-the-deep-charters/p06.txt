Fishing Techiques
Fish The Deep use a variety of techniques ranging from bottom bouncing and float lining, live baiting and trolling lures and baits. Different methods suit different species.
As with most parts of Australia and the world, the Gold Coast has seasons for various species, however our reef fishing charters run all year round.
Deep sea fishing techniques 
During winter our catches include:
• Snapper
• Pearl Perch
• Rosie Job Fish
• Morwong
• Teraglin Jew Fish
• Amberjack
• Yellow tail Kingfish
• Sampson
• Cobia (Black Kingfish)
• Mulloway
• Tailor
• Flathead
• Cod
• Tusk Fish
• Shark
• Various Tuna
Inclusive of full-day trips only.
During summer our catches include:
• Black and Striped Marlin
• Sailfish
• Mahi Mahi (Dorado or Dolphin fish)
• Spotted, School and Spanish Mackerel
• Wahoo
• Bonito
• Various Tuna
• Teraglin Jew Fish
• Tailor
• Flathead
• Tusk Fish
• Cod
• Shark and many more
Our deep sea fishing charters for large game are seasonal, November – February, this is due to the arrival of the East Australian Currents, with these warm current flows come a variety of pelagic fish including Black, Blue and Striped Marlin, Sailfish, Wahoo, Mahi Mahi (Dorado) and Various Tuna. The Gold Coast has became World famous for its light tackle game fishing, seeing great numbers of small black marlin (20-70kg) tagged and released every season, not to mention the tasty Wahoo, Tuna and Mahi Mahi often encountered.
Species targeted Tackle used 
• For our reef fishing, we supply Spinfisher Spin reels on Ugly Stick 8-15kg Gold series rods.
• For our game fishing, we supply Shimano Tyrnos reels with 15-24kg rods. (Stand up only)
