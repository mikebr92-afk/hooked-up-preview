Montebello Islands Liveaboard Charters & Eco Cruises
Discover a Hidden Paradise in WA’s North West. Escape the routine and explore the stunning coral atolls, historic military sites, and pristine waters of the Montebello Islands aboard Western Australia’s premier wilderness liveaboard platform.
Enquire About Available Cruises ➔
A Breathtaking Offshore Escape in the Heart of North West WA
Located 65 kilometers northwest of Dampier and 16 kilometers north of Barrow Island, the Montebello Islands Marine Park is an unmatched tropical paradise.
Comprising a massive network of pristine islands, shallow coral structures, and thick mangrove inlets, this isolated marine park is a sanctuary for world-renowned fishing grounds and top-tier snorkeling hotspots. Step aboard to witness an explosive marine ecosystem where you can come face-to-face with an incredible array of species, from rich reef residents to pelagic giants, creating a wilderness getaway that will resonate for years to come.
Endless Possibilities in a Remote Marine Sanctuary
From deep waters to sheltered inlets, experience the ultimate tropical escape your way.
Uncover the Wonders of the Archipelago: 
• World-Class Snorkeling & Diving: Deeply immerse yourself in crystal-clear lagoons and vibrant coral atolls teeming with tropical marine life, sea turtles, and colorful reefs.
• Fascinating Human History: Guided eco-tours take you ashore across Hermite, Alpha, and Trimouille islands to discover unique wildlife habitats and historical military test remnants.
• Sheltered Wilderness Exploration: Navigate winding mangrove networks on a quest for succulent mud crabs, or catch fresh squid right off the transom marlin board under a starry night sky.
• Diverse Fishing Experiences: For those looking to drop a line, try your hand at casual reef trolling, jigging, or traditional bottom bouncing for premium table fish like Red Emperor and Coral Trout.
Wilderness Staging From Dampier Harbor
Embark on a thrilling six-night, five-day liveaboard extravaganza along the breathtaking North West Coast of Western Australia.
Our Campaign Logistics: 
• The Seasonal Window: Our remote wilderness expeditions operate strictly in the stable dry season between August and October, when the region experiences exceptionally high clear days, zero humidity, and peak environmental conditions.
• The Staging Route: Your adventure begins with a flight to Karratha, where you’ll depart from Dampier Harbor. Cruise out through the 42 pristine islands of the Dampier Archipelago towards your secluded island anchorage, arriving just in time for a fresh cooked breakfast on your first morning.
• Professional Onboard Service: Leave the logistics behind. Our friendly crew handles all island anchoring paths, deck navigation, custom catering, and assistance so you can focus entirely on the beauty of your surroundings.
Experience Unforgettable Holidays Aboard Livewire
Our Flagship 80-Foot Luxury Platform Built Specifically for Remote Western Conditions
Liveaboard Comfort & Specifications: 
- The Comforts of Home: Livewire is our newest, largest, and most luxurious purpose-built charter vessel. It features fully air-conditioned living spaces, hot showers, private cabins, and shared quarters to support custom groups.
- Expert Owner-Operators: Captains Brandon and Peter are your seasoned skippers. Fully licensed with over 20 years of expertise on the water, they use their intimate local knowledge to skip generic tours and take you directly to secret spots and hidden tropical gems.
- Daily Onboard Catering: Wake up to the rich aroma of freshly brewed coffee before fueling your body with a hearty cooked breakfast. After an action-packed day of exploration, enjoy refreshing drinks on the spacious back deck as the sun dips below the horizon.
Looking for a Hard-Core Angling Campaign?
While Livewire is ideal for all nature lovers, we run dedicated heavy-tackle expeditions for serious fishermen targeting Giant Trevally, Red Emperor, and pelagic giants.

Explore Beyond the Ordinary: Montebello History Tours
A fascinating blend of unique human history and stunning natural resilience.
Take a break from the water to step ashore on Hermite Island, Alpha Island, and Trimouille Island. These historic sands served as ground zero sites for Britain’s secret 1950s atomic weapons tests. Our skippers will share the captivating stories of these military blasts and the islands’ remarkable environmental recovery, allowing you to pay your respects and reflect on the history of the region while walking pristine, untouched sandy beaches.

The Expedition Experience
⚓ Where do we depart from? Our expeditions depart from the Hampton Harbour Yacht Club . Please ensure you arrive at the designated meeting point at least 30 minutes prior to your scheduled departure for boarding and gear checks.
🎣 Can we troll for pelagics? Absolutely. Trolling is a core part of our Montebello expeditions, allowing us to target hard-fighting pelagic species as we travel between the best fishing grounds.
🦀 Do we go mud crabbing? Yes. On the fourth day of your expedition, we incorporate island exploring and mud crabbing into the itinerary, providing a fantastic break from offshore fishing.
Logistics
📡 Is there mobile reception? Connectivity is extremely limited. We recommend preparing for a "digital detox" while on the water. Our vessel is equipped with satellite communication for emergencies to ensure your safety.
⚠️ What happens if weather turns? Safety is our absolute priority. If weather conditions are deemed unsuitable for safe transit or fishing, the Skipper may adjust the itinerary to explore sheltered bays or remain at anchor. We prioritize your well-being in every decision.
