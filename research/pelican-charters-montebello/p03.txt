Livewire: The Pinnacle of Luxury Charter Cruising
~ Pelican Charters Premium Private Bookings ~
Livewire breaks the mold of standard boat hire, offering a massive, multi-tiered sanctuary designed perfectly for group celebrations, corporate river functions, and open-ocean adventures. From the moment you step into her sprawling, custom-fitted interior, you will experience a seamless blend of elite marine capability and five-star relaxation.
🚢 First-Class Comfort on the Water:
⎈ 
Spacious Luxury Living: Relax away from the elements in full, crisp air-conditioned comfort. Guests can wind down in beautifully detailed private cabins or socialize across expansive, open-plan lounge quarters fitted with plush bedding and high-end finishes.
⎈ 
Premium Onboard Amenities: Say goodbye to cramped boat facilities. Livewire features three meticulously clean bathrooms to ensure total convenience for your entire group, complemented by an outer deck vast enough for sun-soaking, mingling, and capturing flawless photo backdrops.
⎈ 
Gourmet Galley & Premium Audio: Take your catering to the next level in a fully equipped commercial kitchen and helm setup, run by our elite local crew. Gather around the grand dining table, stream live matches on the crisp smart TV, or plug into the powerful multi-device sound system.
⎈ 
Endless Cold Storage: Keep the celebration flowing underneath the warm WA sun. Our heavy-duty on-board ice machine and commercial-scale storage iceboxes guarantee a steady, unlimited supply of perfectly frosted beverages.
🌊 Built to Conquer the Elements
Commanding a phenomenal 80 feet of pure water line and driven by muscular twin diesel engines, Livewire delivers rock-solid offshore stability. Originally crafted to withstand rugged deep-sea exploration, she has been masterfully reimagined into a premium cruising platform. No matter the channel or ocean weather, Livewire guarantees an incredibly smooth, safe, and elite voyage across WA’s pristine marine icons.

The Livewire Advantage
⚡ What makes Livewire a standout vessel? Livewire is designed for efficiency and high-capacity performance. With a rating for up to 55 passengers in sheltered waters, she is our go-to vessel for larger corporate functions, big social gatherings, and lively party cruises where space is at a premium.
⚓ Where does Livewire operate best? Livewire excels in river cruising and coastal sheltered-water charters. Her hull design provides an incredibly stable platform, ensuring that even with a full crowd, the ride remains smooth and comfortable.
🎉 Is she ready for parties? Absolutely. Livewire features an open-plan deck configuration that allows for effortless social mixing. Whether you are hosting a sundowner, a corporate networking event, or a birthday bash, she provides the perfect stage.
Booking & Scheduling
📅 How can I secure Livewire for my group? Given her high capacity, Livewire is in high demand for larger group bookings. We recommend checking your date well in advance. Reach out to our team to discuss your guest numbers and vessel availability.
