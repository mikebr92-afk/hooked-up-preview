Full day reef n sport Fishing Charter for individuals 
Our bottom fishing charter starts at 6 am at Cronulla ferry wharf after a quick safety briefing we cruise out of gunnamatta bay down past jibbon bommie and along the national park targeting bonito and kingfish along the way to our fishing grounds where snapper and flatead are our main target but catch plenty of bycatch like mowies, nanniguy, bakers, pearl perch, john dory, pigfish and more. We finish at 1pm and head home to the wharf by 2pm.

Full day reef and sport fishing 
Reef n Sport fishing starts at 6 am and we target snapper, flathead, jew, bream, kings, bonitos, salmon, grouper, john dory and many more we fish bottom and troll for sport fish this trip dose it all and touch's abit of everything a fishing day can jam in to it. and goes for 7 hours bait, tackle licence is supplied.
