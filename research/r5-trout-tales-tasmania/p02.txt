About our Tasmanian fly fishing tours
Our unique fly fishing packages aim to capture what makes Tasmania so special as a trout fishery. We tailor your package to suit your experience level, group size and travel itinerary, ensuring the best possible outcome for your trip.
From sight fishing with a single dry fly to large rising trout in crystal clear highland lakes to wandering up small lowland streams and rivers casting to trout feeding on an array of aquatic and terrestrial insects - our experienced Trout Tales fishing guides will show you why Tasmania is considered to be one of the best fly-fishing destinations in the world.
meet matt - your Tasmanian fly fishing guide
With a lifelong passion for Tasmania's endless wilderness, Tasmanian trout fishing guide Matt combines over 20 years of experience fly fishing in Tasmania with a vast knowledge of local produce as a qualified chef to create unique and personalised packages. Matt's relationships with local accommodation providers also gives some great options to include in your package. 
What's included in your trip?
a tailored experience of tasmania's fly fishing landscape
Tasmania's trout fishery offers a lifetime of fly fishing options to explore, coupled with stunning scenery and an abundance of wildlife to provide an unforgettable wilderness experience.
From the world-renowned fly fishing lakes of the Central Highlands , to the intimate lowland small streams - it's easy to see why Tasmania is on the bucket list of many fly fishers around the world. 
Home to the 2019 World Fly Fishing Championships, it is a spectacular place to experience some of the best trout fishing in the world.
Whether wading along shallow lagoon edges, drifting across weed lined lakes in one of our custom built fly fishing boats or meandering up quiet meadow streams, your Trout Tales guide will be with you every step of the way, giving you the the best possible chance to achieve success on your fly fishing charter while in Tasmania. 
Learn more: Our full breakdown of trout fishing in Tasmania . Accommodation options
Our connections with multiple accommodation providers near our trout fishing locations allows us to incorporate all accommodation and meal requirements into your fly fishing package.
This allows for multi-day trout and fly fishing trips , giving you more time to experience the full range of fishing options, explore different fly fishing techniques and get the most out of your journey to Tasmania.
Tasmanian Cuisine
Your Trout Tales Tasmania Guide, Matt, shares his passion for Tasmania's world class local produce through his experience working as a chef in Tasmania for over 10 years. You will have the chance to sample some of Tasmania's fine local produce during a break from the fly and trout fishing action. 
Our gourmet lunches, snacks and refreshments feature world-class Tasmanian produce, expertly prepared and served, adding another twist to your Tasmanian Trout Tale.
Your fly fishing package with Trout Tales Tasmania includes: 
When booking your Tasmanian fly and trout fishing tour with Trout Tales, we provide:
• Transport to and from the Hobart or Miena areas, unlocking the best trout fishing locations;
• The use of premium quality Stalker Fly Fishing rods, reels, breathable waders, boots, locally tied flies and all other gear required for your trip;
• The use of modern 4wd vehicles and custom built fly fishing boats to access unique locations and give you the best possible chance at catching that elusive brown trout;
• Access to exclusive private waters through our relationships with landowners, providing a range of untouched fly fishing opportunities on lakes and rivers, simply not accessible to the general public;
• Chef prepared lunches and refreshments featuring local Tasmanian produce;
• Experienced, fully accredited and insured TGALT fly fishing Guides for your safety;
• For multi day trips - Lodge or cabin style accommodation including breakfast, evening meals and drinks next to open log fires.
What to bring on your fly fishing trip: 
Here are some handy items and attitudes to bring along to your trip with Trout Tales Tasmania:
• A desire to experience the Tasmanian wilderness and learn more about the intricacies of fly fishing;
• A current Tasmanian angling license ( available through Inland Fisheries Service );
• Personal fly-fishing equipment (optional);
• Warm clothes, hat and polarised sunglasses. Tasmania's weather is unpredictable, we recommend bringing layers of clothing that can be added/taken off, including thermals, merino or fleece mid layers, down jackets and waterproof over layers.
