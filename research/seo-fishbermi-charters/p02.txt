Fishing Charters
We offer shared fishing charters out of Bermagui Harbour year round.
We target general reef fish, Kingfish, Tuna and Sharks.
Our 42ft Randell Flybridge Cruiser is in Survey to carry 18 Passengers and 2 crew, has a comfortable on-board toilet, the latest fishing tackle and marine electronics. We keep numbers below 9 on share charters to allow you maximum fishing time and comfort.
A local experienced crew add value to your fishing experience.
Share charter spots are available on a per person basis. Contact Bermagui Bait and Tackle for more information or click here to Book now 
For further information on group or private Bermagui fishing charters see Exclusive Charters .
