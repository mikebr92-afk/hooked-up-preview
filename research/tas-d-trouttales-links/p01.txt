Tasmanian Fishing Links
Relevant links and resources to trout and fly fishing in Tasmania.
When combined with a professional trout fishing guide, the following information can greatly increase your chance of success during your trip.
Angler Alliance Webcams
The Angler Alliance Tasmania webcams let you check out the current weather conditions, lake levels and angler numbers on up to ten trout fishing lakes and rivers around Tasmania. You can also see what has happened there at various times throughout the day.
Visit the Angler Alliance Tasmania webcams site. 
Anglers Alliance Tasmania 
Anglers Alliance Tasmania are the peak body for freshwater anglers in Tasmania and work closely with the Inland Fisheries Service to provide angler feedback to both fisheries management as well as State and Local Government.
Inland Fisheries Service
The Inland Fisheries Service provide all information regarding regulations and licencing requirements, access points, trout stocking programs and fishery performance data.
