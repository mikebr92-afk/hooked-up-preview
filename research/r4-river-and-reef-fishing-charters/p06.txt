Saltwater Barra Fishing 
Saltwater Barramundi Fishing Charter
Barra Fishing Darwin, Day Tours
(mid May – November)
When tides suit we prefer to spend our time chasing saltwater Barra, Barramundi fishing saltwater tidal creeks and mangrove lined estuaries, sight casting to Barra hunting the flats or the thrill of casting to a big Barra located on live target sonar is what we love and love to show our clients what it is all about.
Most locations are within 2 hours drive from Darwin, some are just on Darwins door step.
• Bynoe Harbour
• Shoal Bay
• Dundee Beach area
• Shady Camp
Tour in Brief
• You will be picked up from your accommodation at 5.00am and transferred to the fishing loction in our air-conditioned 4wd.
• morning tea, lunch and light refreshments are all supplied
• all fishing gear supplied on a replace if lost or broken basis
• our fully customised Barra boat offers a safe & comfortable platform for your days fishing
• expert tuition given as required
• you will be off the water around 1.30/2 pm to return to Darwin by approximately 3.30/4pm
Numbers
minimum of 2, larger groups can be catered for.
