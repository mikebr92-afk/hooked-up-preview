Darwin Harbour Charters 
Half Day – Darwin Harbour Fishing Charter
Our 4 hour Darwin Harbour half day fishing Charters offers great opportunities for those who are time poor to get a taste for the great fishing Darwin has to offer.
Depending on the tides we will suggest what will present the best opportunities.
Darwin Harbour Fishing Options:-
• Barra Fishing
• Mud Crabbing
• Reef Fishing
• Pelagics (Queenfish, Longtail Tuna, Trevally)
Barra Fishing Darwin Harbour is a great option to tangle with a Top End Barramundi. You will fish the harbour arms and estuaries targeting Barra on lures but you just never know what other species you may encounter. Time poor check out this half day Darwin Harbour fishing Charter.
