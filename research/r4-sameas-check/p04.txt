Dining and bars
There are several places to eat and drink on board, offering everything from relaxed meals to quick bites. Enjoy fresh Tasmanian produce at Tasmanian Market Kitchen (TMK), relax with local wines at Bar 7, and unwind in the summery Terrace Lounge Bar or the family-friendly Top Deck Lounge. 
Our onboard shop, The Pantry, also offers freshly prepared sandwiches, snacks, drinks and specialty Tasmanian products.

Our history
Spirit of Tasmania’s fleet has evolved significantly since its inception, with the current vessels, Spirit of Tasmania I and II, offering enhanced passenger and freight capacities. The company is set to further upgrade its services with the introduction of two new purpose-built ships, Spirit of Tasmania IV and V, which will replace the existing fleet in the coming years.
