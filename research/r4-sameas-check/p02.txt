Diver’s Fishing Charters Hobart
Details
Diver’s Fishing Charters Hobart offers shared or private diving and fishing trips, specialising in small groups for unforgettable adventures! 
Whether you’re a beginner trying fishing for the first time, a diver hunting for crayfish and abalone with non-diving friends or family, or simply exploring Tasmania’s famous giant kelp forests and cold-water corals – they’ve got you covered. Fishing enthusiasts can also book private charters to target bluefin tuna, and striped trumpeter, or enjoy deep-sea bottom fishing. 
Just 15 minutes from downtown – all fishing gear, dive tanks, and weights are provided. Step aboard their stunning 11.7m vessel, Kylin, for a luxurious day on the water!
You can bring your own food, or we can arrange catering for you. They’re also happy to recommend BYO-friendly restaurants that can prepare your catch for dinner.

We acknowledge the Tasmanian Aboriginal people and their enduring custodianship of lutruwita / Tasmania. We honour 40,000 years of uninterrupted care, protection and belonging to these islands, before the invasion and colonisation of European settlement. As a destination that welcomes visitors to these lands, we acknowledge our responsibility to represent to our visitors, Tasmania’s deep and complex history, fully, respectfully and truthfully.
We acknowledge the Aboriginal people who continue to care for this country today. We pay our respects to their elders, past and present. We honour their stories, songs, art, and culture, and their aspirations for the future of their people and these lands. We respectfully ask that tourism be a part of that future.
