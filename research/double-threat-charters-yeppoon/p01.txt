HOME: Welcome 
Double Threat Charters Yeppoon 
Set off on an unforgettable adventure with Double Threat Charters Yeppoon, exploring the pristine waters of Keppel Bay and the Southern Great Barrier Reef. From relaxing island cruises to full on reef and deep water fishing, we offer experiences to suit everyone — from first timers to serious anglers.
​ 
With a range of trips from 8 to 36 hours, you can chase Coral Trout, Red Emperor, or Spanish Mackerel — and if the season's right, even hook into a mighty Marlin. Our modern vessel, quality gear, and experienced crew make every charter safe, exciting, and unforgettable.
​ 
Whether it's a day with mates, a team adventure, or a family getaway, we'll tailor the perfect trip for you.
