Fishing in Sydney Harbour with Craig McGill
With over 45 years experience on the water, Craig McGill established Sydney harbour’s first ever guided fishing service in 1992. As a full time fishing guide for 26 years, Craig’s level of experience and intimate knowledge of this intriguing waterway and its abundant fish species is second to none. With Kingfish up to 50lbs and Jewfish up to 80lbs encountered within sight of the famous Opera House, along with a huge variety of other seasonal targets, it’s no surprise Sydney harbour is ranked as a world class fishery, and there’s no better choice of guide than Craig to put you on the fish of your dreams. Species We Target 
Sydney harbour boasts 586 different species of fish not to mention an abundance of squid and crustaceans.
Regular sightings of dolphins, whales, seals, sharks and the harbours resident penguin population make for an enthralling ‘sideshow’ to your days fishing.
KINGFISH
Kingfish are the most sort after fish in the harbour. In recent years their average size has come up considerably and fish over 1m are not uncommon. 
They fight hard and are renowned for breaking tackle, lines and angler spirit. They are dirty fighters often diving into structure to break the line. They are also fantastic eating either cooked or raw. 
Kingies are our speciality and after 26 years of refining technique we offer you your best chance of landing this harbour icon. We pioneered both the use of live squid and stickbait lures to target these fish in the harbour. 
Best season 
Kings are now a year-round option in the harbour 
JEWFISH
Excluding sharks, jewfish are the harbour's largest predator. Their numbers have increased dramatically in the harbour since the banning of commercial fishing and fish to 80lbs are possible. 
They require very refined techniques, paying careful attention to tides, moon, location and bait. 
We have cracked the code and offer you a real chance of tangling with one of these tasty trophies. We use both bait and lures. 
Best season 
Jewfish are at their best from October to July 
OTHER SPECIES
We target a multitude of other species in both summer and winter.
Summer 
Surface fish such as Salmon, Bonito, Tailor and a variety of Tunas.
Flathead, Bream, Hairtail, Amberjack and Samson fish
Winter 
Blackfish, Surgeon fish, Salmon, Flathead, Trevally and John Dory
