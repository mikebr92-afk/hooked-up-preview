This is where to go to catch Barramundi!!!
Imagine this...
Spend a whole day fishing the Territory's most Barramundi laden river. After an action-filled day fishing and posing for all those fishing photos, cool off with a dip in the pool and then sit back and relax with a refreshing cold beer or wine and barbecue of fresh Barra! Have a great nights sleep in the airconditioned comfort of modern self contained cabins to wake up to the sounds of birds and wildlife... and start all over again again!!! 
Could you handle that?
The friendly Daly River Barra Resort is a new family owned and operated business on the famous Daly River, Woolianna Rd, Daly River, Northern Territory. 
Daly River Fishing Competitions include the famous Barra Classic and Barra Nationals Fishing Competitions. The Daly River is the best Barramundi Fishing river in the Northern Territory. 
This wonderful new fishing resort on the Daly River is on Woolianna Road and has direct river access. As part of our service we provide fishing charters, local fishing knowledge and boat hire.
The Daly River Barra Resort is 250kms from Darwin and 260kms from Katherine. Map of Darwin to Daly River Barra Resort 
Dorat road and Daly River road are both bitumen, Woolianna road is also sealed except for the final 4kms which is regularly maintained gravel ensuring easy access to the Daly River.
The Daly River Barra Resort provides large shaded powered or unpowered caravan sites, camping and new air conditioned self contained unit accommodation to tourists and to local Territorians.
For the best barramundi fishing spots come and join us on the Daly River NT Northern Territory.
