Spearfishing Mackay and the Great Barrier Reef 
For the avid diver or snorkeling enthusiast, spearfishing is always a great way to get a feed as well as relaxing from a hard week at work or just taking in the surrounds of the Great Barrier Reef. 
Mackay has some phenomenal diving and spearfishing locations, great visibility and numerous reef species or even pelagic Fish. Grab some mates and this can also be another great day out even combine it to have half day spearfishing and the afternoon sportfishing in and around the bays. 
Use The Expertise Of Our Spearfishing State Champion Mick... 
If you are looking for the ultimate spearfishing experience you can expect to shoot any of your coral reef species or even pelagics such as the Giant Trevally or Spanish Mackeral for the more adventurous spearfisherman. 
With Mick Saunders our local State Champion Spearfisherman , he'll show you some of the finer points and techiques that will help you improve on your catch rate.
