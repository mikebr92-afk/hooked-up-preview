Reef Fishing Charters Mackay & Great Barrier Reef 
Welcome to REEFARI the ultimate Great Barrier Reef fishing adventure. Where we offer an awesome Fishing experience customised to your fishing or sightseeing needs. 
From a full day out reef fishing the outer great barrier reef and beautiful nearby islands, aboard one of our great vessel's. 
Options range from a fast day trip to the outer reef if time is limited, or two to four days fishing the remote outer reefs of the great Barrier Reef on our Steber 41 Gameboat . 
Or join us on the ultimate Reefari Hardline Mothershipping Adventure for 5 to 7 days of fishing uncharted waters up to 200 kilometres offshore. Please don't hesitate to contact us to look after your fishing requirements.
We are here to look after you no matter what your passion is!! Game fishing , sport fishing , reef fishing , fly fishing , shark fishing , spear fishing or even just snorkeling on our beautiful Great Barrier Reef you name it, we can do it. With years of local experience, top skipper and deckies we can't be beaten on service and value. We can customise any boat charter to your particular needs. 
Experience The Ultimate Reef Fishing Adventure With Reefari Fishing Charters... 
As you can see, any special requests can be catered for, from chasing billfish, black marlin or sailfish, or flyfishing for queenfish or tuna. Try game fishing for that massive Spanish Mackeral you've always wanted, or reef fishing for a beautiful Coral trout or Red Emperor. 
The options are endless. You owe it to yourself to see some of Mackay's best kept secrets and experience the Great Barrier Reef and islands at their best.
Experience one of our full day outer reef fishing trips, or we can also do a half day trip if on a tight time schedule. We are in survey for 10 guests, but restrict numbers to 6-8 for fishing, so get a few mates or work colleagues together and book a date. 
If only yourself or a couple of friends, we can also put you on our shortlist and get you out amongst it ASAP!
No Fish Is Too Big...
Check out our Steber 41 and overnight charters for more unbeatable trips with up to 6 guests for unbeatable reef and game fishing locations and adventure. Want the ultimate fishing Charter.
Try 7 days on our Reefari Hardline trips with an 80 foot Mothership. Fishing doesn't get any better!! Now this is Living!!

We offer our fully customised 35 foot Cougar Cat with flybridge, with fast cruising speeds of 25 knots to ensure you fish longer and harder. Time is precious, why waste it travelling. Cougar cats are well known for their legendy Sea keeping ability, with comfortable ride and stability. 
Not to mention the best seat in the house with unbeatable views on the flybridge. This gameboat is fully optioned with all the comforts including Galley, dinette, Stereo, DVD player, refrigeration and much more. Most importantly we have a fully enclosed electric toilet for the ladies.
The best gear guaranteed, brand new shimano TLD 25's with 50 lb braid. Why Braid, ultra thin diameter and no stretch, once you've experienced braid you won't look back and of course, you'll catch more fish!! With all bait and tackle provided, great fishing tips , tackle and gear to help ensure more fish, bigger fish and hopefully that fish of a lifetime. 
Our ultimate day out is the longest day available on the reef with 6am start and back at 8pm offering unbeatable value and a full day out fishing on the Outer Great Barrier Reef .
We dare you to compare our prices. We travel to the best fishing spots up to 60 nautical miles ( 120 Kilometres ) offshore to ensure you get the quality reef fish and fishing you deserve. 
Fishing Harder Faster And Longer... 
Some of the great fish you can expect to catch include Coral Trout, Redthroat Sweetlip, Red Emporer, Spangled Emporer, Trevally, Cod, Spanish Mackerel and many more reef species to numerous to mention. With some great fishing at Stevens Reef recently with some Massive Coral Trout and the Icebox full, guests couldn't be happier. 
See our Hall of Fame fish gallery to be blown away. Put yourself in the picture. If you want more, we can also break the trip up with some great Red Emporer fishing on the way back home on dusk, it just doesn't get any better whilst watching the sunset. 
Our service is second to none, everything provided including some great food on the water, with full buffet lunch including prawns, followed by an afternoon cheese platter, you won't be going hungry. 
With unlimited softdrinks, bottled water, tea and coffee, you'll just need to bring your own Alcohol. We've even got the sunscreen.
Once you've been on a fishing charter with Reefari, you'll be back for more. 
For your convenience we depart from Mackay Marina located directly in front of the Mackay Marina Office and Clarion Hotel. You can't miss us, best location berthed at A1. 
Trips are filling up fast so make sure you get in early to book your favoured day, with weekends extra popular. Please don't hesitate to Contact Greg Reynolds to discuss your trip requirements or you can download our brochure for further information. All major credit cards accepted. Rates include GST and Reef tax.
