Fly Fishing Charters 
Simone with a Mack Tuna , which are in quite prolific numbers off Mackay all year round. Great sport on Fly or Lure casting slugs into feeding schools of these fighting tuna.
Fly fishing around the magnificent waterways of Mackay is yielding some good catches of fish. This is a very specialised and challenging sport with alot of fun and excitement, sight casting to schools of feeding fish.
With numerous Mackeral and Tuna Schools available this is one way to work out your fly fishing gear as per our sports fishing charters.
Another recommendation is fly fishing Scawfell island at Refuge Bay, with post card scenery and a beautiful beach all to yourself. For those that would like to try their hand at Fly casting in the shallows you would be looking at catching such fish as Queenfish, Trevally, Giant Herring, Flathead and many more. 
Even the elusive bonefish has been caught while fly fishing in Refuge Bay, with the perfect reef flats and ideal environment.
If you are looking for the ultimate fishing action tackling some big fish on light line then this is the experience for you.
Amazing stuff.
