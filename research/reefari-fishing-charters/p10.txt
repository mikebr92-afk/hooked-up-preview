Our Charter Boat Rates 
When Time and Speed count to ensure maximum fishing time, you won't be disappointed, with twin 250HP Suzuki's and a cruise speed of 25knots. Our day trips are mainly broken into Outer Islands and Outer Reef fishing Trips with closer Island trips available on request. 
Also we can do overnight trips to outer reefs with limited numbers of 4. Trips can be customised to your requirements, or a variety of fishing methods and locations tried throughout the day, so please don't hesitate to contact us. 
We operate from Mackay Marina aboard Raptor II and are located directly in front of the Mackay Marina Office, Berth A01. The Vessel is a Flybridge Cougar Cat, 10.0m LOA in commercial 2C Survey for 10 guests + 2 crew, although we tend to restrict numbers to 6 to 8 people for fishing. Although 10 is fine for social trips to the islands, sunset cruises, parties and snorkeling trips. 
We can take individual, couples or small groups and organise a combined three or four day boat charter or if you have enough people for a charter we can take a booking to guarantee your required date. Weekends are popular and booked well ahead, so try to get in as early as possible. Get a group together and excellent value when shared between 7 or 8 people!! 
Please BYO alcoholic drinks, with heaps of food, buffet lunch including prawns, smoko's, cheese platter, softdrinks, bottled water, tea & coffee, etc provided. Plenty of bait and tackle provided. 
Rods are new Shimano TLD 15's & 25's with 50lb braid for your fishing pleasure and increased catch. Don't forget your hat and Camera, Sunscreen provided on the boat!!
Shared Charters
Mad Keen to go fishing and can't get a group together, Just yourself or a couple of mates, No Problems!! Join one of our shared Charters and we'll get you out there amongst 'em!! $350 for an outer Reef trip. Limited availability, so call as early as possible and we'll book you in. Same great fishing trips as below.
Inner Island Charter $1950 per Day
Leaving at 8am once again for the nearby islands of Keswick/St Bees, Brampton or Baileys for some fishing or general sightseeing. Travel time of 1 hrs gets you out there in no time. Fishing ceases at 4pm with eta back at Mackay Marina about 5.30pm. Full charter booking required for these Charters.
Outer Island Charter $2200 per Day
Leaving at 7am for the outer islands of Calder, Scawfell and 3 rocks for some great fishing. Travel time of 1 – 1.5 hrs gets you out there nice and quick. Fishing ceases at 4pm with eta back at Mackay Marina at 6pm, another full day out on the water with everything supplied. Full charter booking required for these Charters.
Shipping Channel, Deep Reef & Shoals Charter $2800 Per Day 
Trips leave at 7am for shipping Channel, with awesome fishing the deep reefs and shoals chasing large nannygai and red emperor. Travel time of approximately 1hrs 30mins, with expected return at Mackay Marina at 5 - 6pm. Everything supplied. Up to a maximum of 8 anglers. Please arrive at marina at 0630 for early departure.
Reef Charter $3000 Per Day 
Trips leave at 7am for Parker or Stevens Reef with a travel time of approximately 2hrs 15 mins, with expected return at Mackay Marina at 6pm. Everything supplied. Up to a maximum of 8 anglers. Please arrive at marina at 0630 for early departure.
Ultimate Reef Charter $3300 Per Day
Trips leave at 0600 am for Stevens Reef combining some deep water work chasing nannygai's and Red emperor. Travel time of approximately 2hrs 15 mins. Fishing ceases later for expected return to Mackay Marina at 9.00pm. So an extra full day with 3 hours more fishing the prime time of dusk on the reef and travelling further. Everything supplied. Up to a maximum of 8 anglers recommended. 
Overnight Charter $3000 Per Day 
Even more fishing when one day isn't enough, leaving at 6am, returning at 6pm on the final day of your charter. Let us customize a charter to suit your needs, Fishing the outer reef from two to four days, going further into the outer reef, beyond the range of most private craft. 
Ask about our Weekend Special package, Leaving Friday Afternoon, arriving early that evening to begin your fishing adventure. Check out our "Steber 41" Gameboat for that live aboard charter to the Great Barrier Reef for a fishing trip of a lifetime.
Such Reefs visited might be Cannon, Liff, Warland, Creal, Kindemar and even further. All meals, bait and tackle included and even better fishing, just BYO Alcohol. Numbers are limited to 6 anglers with heaps more fishing space and comfort.
