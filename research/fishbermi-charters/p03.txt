Exclusive Charters
Charter the whole boat for your family, friends or workmates. 
The size and comfort of our boat makes private charters and corporate bookings very enjoyable. Private groups may charter the boat for up to 12 persons if required.
Groups may charter the vessel from Bermagui for anything from Whale Watching to Marlin fishing or simply a day on the water chasing a feed of reef fish.
Price is negotiable for repeat clients and multiple day bookings.
