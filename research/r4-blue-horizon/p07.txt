GAMEX OVERVIEW 
Gamex is Western Australia’s premiere game fishing competition.
Teams can fish the abundant waters off Exmouth in small or large vessels to target over 40 different species of game fish, including six different species of billfish.
Why not Charter the Blue Horizon to compete in the annual competition where people come from all sides of the world.
Includes 6 days game fishing, all gear, team t-shirt, meals, tea, coffee and water supplied. BYO alcohol.
Held annually in March. Contact us for this year's exact dates.

OUR GAMEX HISTORY

Gamex 2023 - Runner Up - boat tag and release Marlin

Gamex 2012 – Champion boat tag and release Billfish (15-24kg line), Champion Skipper

Gamex 1998 – Champion boat tag and release Marlin.
