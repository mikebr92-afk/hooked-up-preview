OUR VESSELS
OUR VESSEL
Custom-Built for Maximum Fishability 
Our vessel isn’t just another boat – it’s a purpose-built fishing machine, designed from the ground up by a commercial fisherman in partnership with a naval architect. Built because no production vessel could meet our standards, this rig is the ultimate offshore weapon – fast, stable, safe, and made to fish hard.
With 650 horsepower, she cruises effortlessly at 21–22 knots and can hit up to 44 knots when needed. Backed by a 1,000L fuel capacity, we can reach distant grounds quickly and confidently.

Every detail has been carefully considered:
Spacious fishing experience 
Takes a maximum of 12 people for a more personal, spacious fishing experience. 
Comfortable seating 
Padded seating for all 12 guests, so everyone rides in comfort.
Soft, cushioned decking 
Stand all day in comfort – no sore knees or feet by the end of your trip.
Rod holders galore 
65 rod holders mean you’ll never be short on space when re-rigging, baiting, or relaxing.
True walkaround cabin 
Half-metre wide access all the way around – no awkward shuffling or squeezing past others.
Flushing marine toilet 
Enjoy privacy and convenience with a full-featured onboard toilet.
Cold drinks and fresh lunch 
A 12V fridge keeps everything cool and fresh during your day out.
Freshwater on demand 
100L freshwater system for rinsing hands, gear, or just cooling off.
Smart transom design 
Cut-out transom for better fish handling and more usable deck space.
Rock-solid stability 
Double-wide chines keep the boat stable and comfortable in a swell.
Top-tier safety gear 
Marine-grade first aid kit, oxygen, defibrillator, and positive flotation.
Pro-level electronics 
$25,000+ in sonar, GPS, and radar to keep you on the fish.
From the hull to the helm, every element is built to maximise performance, comfort, and fishability. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or a first-timer, you’ll immediately feel the difference of a vessel designed by fishermen, for fishermen.
This is where serious fishing begins.
