What we provide

Half Day Charter
Half Day trips cater for those who just want a snapshot of what the area has to provide for approximately 5 hours.
Working with you, we can make time suit your needs but for the majority of summer it will mean an early start in the predawn glow of sunrise and fishing that prime morning bite period and then be back before lunch.
The afternoon session can also be a good time for you to be out on the water watching the sunset and casting surface lures around for hungry Cod as the light fades.
Includes snacks, drinks, fishing licence and all fishing gear
$700 for 1-3 people for the most comfortable trip
Book now 
• 
Full Day Charter
Getting a full day charter (approx. 8 hours) is the best experience for you learn about the fishery the whole day. We start in the early morning and will stop for a break through the heat of the day letting you go have a sleep and do your own thing for lunch and then go again in the afternoon and fish into the evening. This means you are capitalising on the best fishing times on the water.
You will have a lunch break in the middle of the day somewhere on the water or where suits the client and then back into fishing the evening where the fish become more active again and target fish with surface lures or swimbaits.
Includes coffee and light breakfast, lunch or dinner (depending on session) , drinks, fishing licence and all fishing gear
$1100 for 1-3 people for the most comfortable trip

Explore Lake Mulwala tour
We understand the need for some other activities for families and tourists to enjoy while visiting or staying in the Yarrawonga Mulwala region. We can easily organise a short trip or tour in the local area to explore Lake Mulwala or the Murray river and visit some pretty scenic and special spots.
Specialising in sunset tours or sunrise cruises, can work in with alternate drop offs in Bundalong or Yarrawonga. Dinner may be arranged to have on the boat and watch the sunset, or go for a cruise along the lake and drop off for Breakfast at many locations on the foreshore.
You will experience a 2.5 hr guided trip around Lake Mulwala to see some of the sights and sounds of the region. There is some amazing birdlife and everglade areas that are only accessible by boat. We can then drop you off somewhere different and allow you to explore or maybe have something to eat nearby the lake

FAQ's
Do I need to bring any fishing gear? 
No, we provide everything you need out on the water and use the best quality gear
Where is the meeting location? 
The meeting location can change, we mostly launch at Hogans Road Boat ramp but can easily arrange pick up from other locations or your accomodation on the lake edge
How many people can you take?
1-3 people is perfect, any more and it can get a bit cramped and not as comfortable
Is there a minimum age?
No, as long as there is an adult supervising on the boat and safety procedures are followed we are happy to take any keen young kids but older than 7 or 8 years is more suitable
Is there a toilet on the boat?
No, but most of the time we are only a short boat drive from a public toilet which are all around the lake
Do you still go out in rain and rough weather?
Yes, we will still go out in not ideal conditions, as long it is not dangerous, this will be arranged prior to the charter
Do I need to have prior experience?
Not at all, that is what the charter is all about, learning new skills and enjoying your time on the water
What fish might we catch?
We target Murray Cod and Yellowbelly at Lake Mulwala and Redfin if we go other places such as Lake Hume
Will we catch a fish? 
Murray Cod can be a very challenging fish at times and we will do our best to put you in the best possible spot where you will have the best opportunity but this does not guarantee we will catch anything

Extended Trips 
Extended trips away can be a rewarding and exciting way to explore different water ways. There are many different ways to experience trips away such as cabin accomodation, camping under the stars or hiring a houseboat as a mother ship and then fishing from our guiding boat during the prime times. We can chase a range of fish on extended trips as we can target them during the peak time of year such as big winter Murray cod, spring time Yellowbelly, catch a feed of Redfin in summer or chase Australian Bass on surface in the humid summer afternoons.
Get in touch so we can organise and plan a trip for your needs . Extended trips can vary a lot so prices will vary, we look forward to planning a dream trip with you

Sounder Tutorials
Do you want to better understand your sounder? do you want to find out the best settings to find fish.
We have been using live technology for multiple seasons now and have seen both brands (activetarget and livescope) and can take you on a short trip to show you how it works

Get in contact for more information and bookings . $300 for 2 hours
