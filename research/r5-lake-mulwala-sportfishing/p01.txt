The Best Way to Experience Lake Mulwala 
Guided Fishing charter and outdoor tours based in Yarrawonga, North East Victoria. Focusing on fishing for Murray cod on lures in Lake Mulwala. We provide guided Half, Full day and overnight/Extended trips for 1-3 people. 
If you are keen to chase big cod in winter it is a good idea to plan your trip as there are already limited bookings.
Subscribe to our Mailing list to receive up to date information and current fishing reports.
Please Contact us now if you have any questions or how you can plan a trip in the near future, get in quick as bookings are filling fast.
What a Charter provides
We provide fishing charters, fish finder/sounder tutorials and tours of Lake Mulwala or we can cater a trip to suit your needs. We also can organise extended trips to other locations. Spring time is a great time to chase Yellowbelly in places like Lake Hume and Lake Eildon, it is great to chase them on the nice sunny days.
Our mission is to provide you the most memorable, knowledgable and fun trip while providing a safe and professional operation through listening to clients and making every trip suit their style. 
Experience one of the best charter boats around on our 20ft Phoenix Bass boat, 250hp Mercury V8, twin 10 foot Powerpoles and the latest Garmin fish finding electronics.
Chase your Murray cod fishing dream
If you want to fast track your learning experience when fishing for Murray Cod then this is the place for you. Teaching you a multitude of tips and tricks to consistently catch fish in Lake Mulwala that you will be able to use in other lakes and rivers over the country. Whether you just want to catch your first fish or chase that elusive metre plus trophy cod we can make it happen
Best times to Fish at Lake Mulwala
The best times to fish in Lake Mulwala for Murray Cod are almost all year round as the water level stays very consistent. Murray cod closed season is from 1st of September until the 1st of December, however this is a great time to target Golden perch. Summer is a great time of year to chase Cod, the water is warmer and the fish are most active however the early mornings and nights are most productive. Once it starts to cool down around winter it is the time to chase trophy Murray cod, the smaller fish become less active and it is a great time to brave the cold to catch that giant Murray cod. 
Looking for things to do in Yarrawonga Mulwala
Want to try fishing or take the kids out for an action packed day and get them into fishing and the outdoors. We can easily handle kids and make it safe and enjoyable for them. We can also teach you how to get set up and catch fish while camping on the Murray River easily with a few little tips. 
We can take you on a quick tour from the water around the foreshores of Yarrawonga and Mulwala and even up to Bundalong. We can drop you off for a picnic on an island to enjoy the scenery and get away from the busy streets. We can organise a sunrise or sunset cruise on Lake Mulwala for 1-5 people.
We can also arrange local four wheel drive tours of the area with 20 years of local knowledge there is a multitude of interesting areas to see within an hour of Yarrawonga, such as the koalas at Ulupna island, historic trees and relics scattered through the bush, add to this an enjoyable lunch somewhere on the river to fill part of the day.
