Fish Finder Fishing Magazine 
This exciting 304-page biennial edition has full satellite and aerial coverage - there are very few mudmaps. The Fish Finder tells you where rockbars, launch sites and secret fishing holes are. It contains many of the aerial photos sold on Fish Finder's web site. This 2005-06 release includes new areas, extending across the remote Pilbara, Kimberley, NT and Cape York and as far south as Moreton Bay, Qld, and Geraldton, WA. The gloss colour pages include loads of GPS and other useful data. 
Published by Darwin-based fishing writer Matt Flynn. Buy Fish Finder Fishing Book online.
While most other fishing media thumb their noses at you when it comes to giving away the good spots, North Australian Fish Finder tells you where the fish are, in some cases down to the last 5m with highly accurate differential GPS waypoints!

We have about 200 copies left of the North Australian Fish Finder 5, covering the coast from Turkey Beach, Qld, to Karratha, WA.
