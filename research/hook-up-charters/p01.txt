About Us 
GET HOOKED WITH HOOK UP FISHING CHARTERS 
PORT DOUGLAS 
With access to some of the most pristine fishing locations Far North Queensland has to offer, Shawry will take you on the fishing adventure of a lifetime! 
In his brand new for 2023 custom built 7m centre console, with a cruising speed of 30 knots, he will get you to the reef in 30 mins. 
Using an array of techniques we will be targeting Coral Trout, Spanish Mackerel, GT's Nannygai, Red Emperor, Queenfish and many more. Our share charters are predominately bait fishing, for topwater and trolling jump on a private charter. 
​ 
​ 
​ 
Fishing is Shawry's passion, he has over 25 years of experience commercial and charter fishing in the local area. 
From freshwater rainforest and saltwater estuary to the offshore reefs, he knows the grounds and offers a wide range of fishing to suit everyone from novice to the experienced angler. 
The kids will love it too!
