King Fish Fishing in Melbourne 
The most prized sport fish to our local waters is the YELLOW TAIL KINGFISH 
Each summer we are lucky enough for the 
kings 
of the sea to make there way locally for us to catch, turning up in 
huge schools with the warm 
blue water 
that flows down the coast. 
These fish 
can bring a grown man to his knees, Pound for pound one of the toughest fighting fish we have, Each time a Kingfish 
hits the deck its a huge achievement for all to land such an epic fish in our local waters. 
Rated as one of the best eating fish in the ocean 
, the YELLOW TAIL KINGFISH 
has beautiful white flesh and if taken care of correctly provides some of the best Sashimi 
our oceans have to offer. 
We have been chasing these fish in our local waters for many years and know all the tricks to get them to bite on even the hardest days. 
Techniques such as " Jigging , live baiting, Trolling, Top water & many more. 
Jigging 
is a very popular style to target these fish and we have all the gear to get the job done. 
Catching live bait such as Calamari & slimy mackerel 
at the start of the trip makes for a great pre workout before the fun begins! 
A Top Water 
experience casting stick baits and poppers is also available . 
Youre welcome to bring your own jigs/lures and rods to suit.
