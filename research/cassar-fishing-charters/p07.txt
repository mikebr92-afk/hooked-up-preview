Whiting Fishing in Melbourne 
All year round the King George Whiting 
will patrol both Western Port and southern Port Phillip bay and CASSAR FISHING CHARTERS 
will be there running charters in both locations catching these very tasty fish for you! 
Personally one of my favorite fish 
to catch and eat , making the Whiting Taco 
a household name here in Victoria! 
A super exciting fish 
to target with everyone on board with a rod in there hand waiting for that bite, Be quick or you might miss it! 
Bait is prepped for you each trip. 
A very popular fish for its excellent eating quality and tough fighting on our specific light gear. 
All whiting 
will be put straight into a salt water ice slurry keeping them in prime condition for when its time to fillet and eat them.
