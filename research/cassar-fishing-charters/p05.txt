Inshore & Offshore Gummy Shark Fishing Charters. 
We pride ourselves on being the most consistent 
charter operator in targeting and catching BIG GUMMY SHARKS 
both inshore and offshore to our local waters here in Victoria. 
When that rod buckles over and screams off it gets the heart pumping! 
These sharks 
fight super hard so hold on and take your time, Because there is nothing like fresh flake for dinner at the end of a trip with us. 
We start the trip catching fresh bait 
so not long after we leave the ramp you will have a rod in your hand catching fish straight away. 
There are also a huge range of bottom fish we will catch while we wait for a SHARK BITE! 
All Sharks 
will be cleaned and filleted correctly for you upon return to the ramp. 
We target Gummy Sharks 
all year round so don't hesitate to book a trip with us.
