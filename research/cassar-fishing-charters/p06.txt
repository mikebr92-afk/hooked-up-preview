Charter Location Notice
Fish are aways moving, and so are we! 
Locations will change to catch the fish of your dreams! 
Why does location change?
Locations on our meeting point for charters are subject to change, reason for this is fish are always on the move, and so are we! Here at CASSAR FISHING CHARTERS our vessel is on a trailer giving us the power to get to each location easily. we will give you the best opportunity there is to catch fish. Where ever the fish are we will be there.
How we will notify you?
You will receive a secondary confirmation message 
closer to your booking date, giving everyone enough time to organise transport to our locations. Changes will be made due to fish movements, 
Being where the fish are and not where they are not is the key to success!
Still confused? Get in touch
Any questions about locations you are not sure about feel free to get in touch with us to make sure you are at the right location for your fishing charter.
