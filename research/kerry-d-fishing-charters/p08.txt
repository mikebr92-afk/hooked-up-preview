About
At Kerry D Fishing Charters , we’re more than just a fishing business – we’re a family. Run by husband-and-wife team Ben and Jemma, alongside our son Layne, the Kerry D name represents not only our boats, but the pride and passion we have for fishing, community, and the incredible Gulf of Carpentaria.
Fishing isn’t just what we do – it’s who we are. Whether it’s chasing a trophy-sized barramundi in the river or heading offshore for Spanish mackerel and cobia, every trip is about creating memories and sharing the magic of Karumba with our guests. We take pride in offering professional, personalised charters where everyone – from beginners to seasoned anglers – feels welcomed, supported, and part of our extended fishing family.
What makes Kerry D special is the heart behind it. Every smile, every fish landed, every story shared on the deck reflects the dedication and love our family pours into this business. For us, it’s not just about catching fish – it’s about connecting people to the Gulf, its waters, and each other.
From our family to yours, we invite you aboard the Kerry D boats for an adventure you’ll never forget.
Ben & Jemma Probert
