Estuary and River Fishing 
Fishing the Top End , Fishing techniques 
Estuary and river fishing is a popular and diverse style of angling that takes place in transitional water systems where freshwater rivers meet the saltwater of the ocean. These unique environments are known as estuaries, and they create a rich, brackish water habitat that is home to a wide variety of fish species. As the salinity of the brackish water changes with the tides and the amount of freshwater flowing in from the river, this creates a highly dynamic and nutrient-rich environment.
