For over 30 years, Australian Sportfishing Charters has offered multi-day sportfishing in remote East Arnhem Land, Northern Territory.
From the Roper River in the Gulf to the offshore Bromby Islands, English Company Islands, and the Wessel Islands, the mangrove-lined rivers of Arnhem Bay and Buckingham Bay. 
Whether it's the iconic Barramundi or the fast, exciting strike of a Spanish Mackerel, Trevally, Queenfish, or Marlin, the far north sport fishing NT has it all. The prime target is Barramundi, which is exceptionally good in estuaries, headlands, and creeks; but there's many other varieties: Fingermark, GTs, Mangrove Jacks, Estuary Cod, Salmon, and Barracuda. 
Fish from sun-up to sun-down, and you'll be rewarded with delightful food, a crisp, cold beverage, and a hot shower at the end of the day on the mothership, the Iron Lady. She is a 65-foot luxury live-aboard motor yacht – equipped with full air-conditioning, sleeping arrangements for eight guests (three bedrooms, each with ensuite), a large saloon, an open-air deck upstairs and four tenders to cater to smaller, more personalised fishing adventures. 
The lady is used as 'home base' throughout the duration of your fishing expedition with an on-board gourmet chef, a hostess and multiple fishing guides, plus Captain Dan – you couldn't be better looked after during your charter. Book a four, five, or seven-day shared charter or customise your adventure.

4 Day Fishing Charter in East Arnhem Land 
Embark on an exclusive fishing adventure with Australian Sportfishing Charters, the premier choice for guided sportfishing in the remote East Arnhem Land for over 30 years. Immerse yourself in the stunning Top End, a paradise of islands and estuaries celebrated for its breathtaking landscape, pristine waters, and unparalleled fishing opportunities. 
Discover diverse fish species in the unspoilt rivers, headlands, and creeks, home of the iconic Barramundi. The nearby Wessel or English Company islands offer a spectacular change of scenery and virtually unfished waters. 
During the 4-day shared or private charter, the luxurious mothership Iron Lady will be your home with an onboard Chef, Hostess and Fishing Guides. The luxurious Iron Lady is fully air-conditioned and accommodates eight guests (3 cabins, each with an ensuite), a large saloon, an open-air deck for dining upstairs, and 3-5 fishing tenders for a more personalised experience. The expert fishing guides will take you to different locations daily, teaching you about the area and fishing techniques. Whether a beginner or a seasoned angler, you can relax while all your needs are taken care of for during your fully catered charter.

4-7 Day Sportfishing Charters 
Big skies over the Gulf, remote anchorages, the spear of a Marlin piercing out of the inky blue, a streak of colour in turquoise waters as a yellowfin hooks on to your line, crocs on the banks of mangrove-lined estuaries, and inching in the dingy through thick country chasing a Barra—this is Australian Sportfishing Charters.
Book a 4, 5, or 7-day charter aboard the luxury 65-foot motor yacht Iron Lady, where you will enjoy first-class meals from the Chef and Hostess, full air conditioning, sleeping arrangements for 8 guests (3 cabins, all with their own ensuite), a large saloon, an open-air deck upstairs, and four tenders with multiple fishing guides to cater to smaller, more personalised daily fishing adventures. Captain Dan has 27 years of experience sportfishing – you couldn’t be better looked after during your charter.

Roper River Fishing 
Unleash your angler’s instinct and indulge in the thrills of reeling in a variety of prized catches on the Roper River fishing charter. Your charter will begin in Darwin, departing on the Grumman Mallard Amphibian Aircraft and arrive on the back deck of the “Iron Lady” mothership after just two hours.
Once onboard, our chef and hostess serve breakfast. The fishing gear is loaded into skiffs, ready for you to catch your first Barramundi in the NT. The “Iron Lady” becomes your home away from home while your fishing adventure unfolds. Our seasoned guides take you to chosen hotspots and impart local wisdom and advanced fishing techniques.
The Roper River, teeming with fish species, is renowned as one of Australia’s premier fishing destinations. This magnificent river flows into the Gulf of Carpentaria and is navigable for almost 170 km. With its pristine waters and thriving ecosystem, the Roper River attracts fishing enthusiasts worldwide. Captain Dan and the Australian Sportfishing Charters crew know the river intimately. You can be confident that they know exactly where to take you in search of the elusive “metrey” Barramundi, and competition is virtually nonexistent. 
Phone to book a 4-7-day shared or private charter.
