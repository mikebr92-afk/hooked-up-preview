What is Wild Turkey
Meet Wild Turkey — ASFC's Black Marlin fighting boat
Wild Turkey is a 34-foot purpose-built heavy-tackle game fishing vessel operated by Australian Sportfishing Charters. She is the dedicated fishing boat for every Cairns marlin charters , running daily from the mothership Iron Lady during the Black Marlin season between October and December, out of Cairns, Port Douglas, Cooktown and Lizard Island.
Wild Turkey is a fully equipped game boat fit-out with all the bells and whistles to aid your adventure, searching to capture the elusive 1000lb+ fish in the deep blue water of the Great Barrier Reef. With the latest Garmin sounders, chirp transducers and radar to ensure your fishing experience is second to none, Wild Turkey is an easy choice for your next Marlin Sportfishing charters in Queensland, Australia.
For the duration of your charter, the mothership Iron Lady is used as a home base so you can rest assured of a great night’s sleep in comfort after a big day of Billfishing.

What is the Wild Turkey?
Wild Turkey is Australian Sportfishing Charters’ dedicated Black Marlin fighting boat — a 34ft heavy tackle game fishing vessel that fishes daily out of mothership Iron Lady during Cairns marlin charters.
How many guests fish aboard Wild Turkey at once?
Wild Turkey is configured to fish up to 6 guests at a time.
Where does Wild Turkey fish?
Wild Turkey fishes wherever mothership Iron Lady is anchored during a charter — Iron Lady relocates along the Great Barrier Reef, including the Ribbon Reefs, to follow the fish over a typical 4 to 7 day trip.
Has Wild Turkey won any tournaments?
Yes — including the Port Douglas Marlin Challenge (2025, 2024 runner-up, 2019) and the Ribbons Ladies Game Fishing Tournament (2023 winner, 2024 runner-up), among other results.
How big are the marlin caught from Wild Turkey?
Most seasons see Black Marlin estimated at over 800lb, with fish over 1000lb always a possibility on the Great Barrier Reef.
Do I sleep aboard Wild Turkey?
No — guests live aboard the Iron Lady for the length of the charter. Wild Turkey is used purely for fishing each day.
What season does Wild Turkey fish?
The Black Marlin season runs October to December each year, out of Cairns, Port Douglas and Cooktown.
Is Wild Turkey competing in any upcoming tournaments?
Yes — Wild Turkey is entered in the CPGFA 60th Anniversary Black Marlin Tournament, the Lizard Island Black Marlin Classic, and the Port Douglas Marlin Challenge. See the Cairns Marlin Charters page for full event details.
What electronics does Wild Turkey carry?
Wild Turkey is fitted with the latest Garmin sounders, CHIRP transducers and radar, used to locate bait and current lines while working the Reef.
Wild Turkey is the boat behind every Cairns marlin charter?
Paired with mothership Iron Lady as your home base on the Reef, Wild Turkey does the fishing each day of a Cairns Black Marlin charter. For dates, pricing and availability, head to the Cairns Marlin Charters page.
