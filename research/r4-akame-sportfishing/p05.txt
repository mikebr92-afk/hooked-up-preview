Creek Fishing / LiveScope
Creek Fishing & LiveScope Charters in Broome
Broome’s creeks, bay systems and inshore waters are home to some of the most exciting sportfishing in the Kimberley.
Akame Sportfishing offers private Creek Fishing and LiveScope charters targeting species such as barramundi, threadfin salmon, tripletail, mulloway and other inshore sportfish.
LiveScope is real-time sonar technology that allows us to see what’s happening below the surface in real time. Instead of fishing blind, LiveScope helps identify fish, structure and movement in the water, giving anglers a better understanding of where to cast and how fish are responding.
This makes it especially useful when targeting species around structure, bait schools, creek systems and inshore grounds. It does not guarantee a catch, but it gives you a serious advantage by helping put your lure in the right zone.
Each charter is planned around tides, weather, conditions and target species on the day. Whether you’re casting lures, throwing swimbaits, working structure or watching fish react in real time, this style of fishing is hands-on, focused and built for anglers who want a proper sportfishing session.
