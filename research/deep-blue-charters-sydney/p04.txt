Harbour Cruises
Bucks & Hens Parties
Looking for a fun filled exclusive Sydney Harbour Cruise? Join us on our spacious boats and experience the sights and seas of Sydney harbour from the front row.
With open layout, covered areas and plenty of inclusions, our vessels Mystery Bay (48pax) and Mahi Mahi ll (25pax) , can cater for Bucks/ Hens/ Birthdays or any occasion you want.
So grab a bunch of friends and let the party begin!
WHAT’S THE DEAL?
Harbour cruising along with in harbour fishing can be organised. Please give us a call on 0403085672 to discuss.
