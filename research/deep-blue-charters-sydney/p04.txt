NEW YEARS EVE
Want an exclusive Sydney NYE cruise experience? With 2 boats to choose from you can escape crowds and enjoy the spectacular Sydney Harbour fireworks with your mates. 6.5 hour cruise includes a prawn platter and premium BBQ , Soft drinks and BYO Alcohol (no expensive bar!!). Premium location for fireworks viewing for the 9pm and midnight fireworks with a Complimentary glass of bubbles/beer on the midnight countdown. 
Book Now 
Australia Day
From ferry to tall ships races, majestic Naval Ships to RAAF Roulettes with aerobatic displays. Australia Day is full of spectacular events in Sydney Harbour. With so much on offer, you can choose how you want to celebrate your Aussie Day.
