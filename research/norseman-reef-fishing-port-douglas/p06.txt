About Us
The Norseman Team 
Our team are all passionate about fishing tours in Port Douglas. 
Our mission is to give our customers the very best day of reef fishing that they have ever had! 
Our crew are handpicked for their local reef knowledge, skill, customer service and fishing expertise. Join us for one of our Great Barrier Reef Tours in Port Douglas! 
MICHAEL 
OWNER 
Mick had been fishing with Norseman for 22 years before deciding that he wanted to buy the business. Michael lives and works in Melbourne, and has a great team of people running his business for him up in Port Douglas. He’s passionate about providing top-quality fishing tours in Port Douglas! 
• What age did you start fishing? 
• 20
• Where have you fished? 
• Tokyo for Sea Bass, New Zealand for trout and Kingfish, England for Trout, Hinchinbrook and Cardwell for Barramundi, Port Douglas for Coral Trout, Large Mouth Nannygai and Spanish Mackerel, Bermagui for Marlin and big Tuna, Lakefield National park for Barramundi, Karumba for Barramundi, Port Phillip Bay for Snapper, Whiting and Flathead.
• Best thing about fishing? 
• It’s very relaxing and much better than working.
• What’s your favourite style of fishing to do? 
• Lure fishing for Barramundi.
• Best Fishing Trip Ever? 
• Cape York Barramundi Fishing in Lakefield National Park.
• Best Catch Ever? 
• 6 Marlin in one day at Bermagui and a 117cm Barramundi at Lakefield.
• What do you like about Fishing on Norseman? 
• The size and speed of the boat, the friendly helpful crew and the licensed bar!
• What’s your red hot tip for fishing on Norseman? 
• Handlines get the fish up quicker before the sharks can get to them.
KOBE 
DECKHAND /FISHING GUIDE 
• What age did you start fishing? 
• My first fishing memory is actually on the Norseman when I was about 7 years old. Me and my brother caught a massive coral trout.
• Where have you fished? 
• I’ve always gone on fishing charters everywhere I go, in places like Japan, New Zealand, Mauritius, Thailand and all over Australia but I’ve done the majority of my fishing here in Far North Queensland for barramundi.
• Best thing about fishing? 
• The escape! Just love being on the water without a worry in the world.
• What’s your favourite style of fishing to do? 
• Can’t beat casting lures for Barra.
• Best Fishing Trip Ever? 
• It’t too hard to pick one, but it would have to be one of the Lakefield trips I did as a teenager.
• Best Catch Ever? 
• My best ever catch is definitely not my biggest but I’ll never forget it, a 106cm barramundi, fishing Lakefield National Park with my dad, caught in the last hour on the last day of a 7-day trip.
• What do you like about Fishing on Norseman? 
• I really enjoy putting people onto big fish that they usually wouldn’t get to experience and watching the expressions when they feel how hard they can pull.
• What’s your red hot tip for fishing on Norseman? 
• Keep checking your bait every 5 minutes, the little fish can take it without you noticing and you can’t catch fish without bait!
