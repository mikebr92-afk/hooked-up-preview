Sports & Recreation
With sporting facilities, golf courses, aquatic centres and endless opportunities to enjoy the water, Port Stephens is made for getting outside and getting active, whatever your pace.
Get active in Port Stephens, where sport and recreation come with a backdrop of beaches, waterways, bushland and wide-open spaces. Whether you’re teeing off on a championship golf course, enjoying a game of tennis, casting a line or looking for somewhere for the kids to burn off some energy, there are plenty of ways to get out and play.
One of the best things about sport and recreation in Port Stephens, NSW is the setting. Start the morning with a round of golf, spend an afternoon fishing or paddling, take the kids to the skate park or simply find a waterfront reserve and enjoy the outdoors.
On the water
With an extraordinary natural harbour, rivers and ocean coastline, it’s little surprise that much of Port Stephens recreation happens on the water. Go fishing, boating, sailing, kayaking or stand-up paddleboarding, with sheltered bays and waterways offering options for beginners through to experienced adventurers.
Fishing is a favourite local pastime, whether you’re casting from the beach or shoreline, heading onto the rivers or joining a Port Stephens fishing charter for a day offshore.
Swimming & Aquatic Centres
Beyond the region’s beaches, you can make a splash at local swimming and aquatic facilities. Tomaree Aquatic Centre at Salamander Bay and Lakeside Leisure Centre at Raymond Terrace provide options for lap swimming, recreation and family fun.
Golf in Port Stephens
Pack the clubs – Port Stephens offers some fantastic golf courses surrounded by beautiful coastal and bushland scenery. Play a round at Nelson Bay Golf Club, where the fairways wind through native bushland close to the centre of Nelson Bay, or head to Pacific Dunes at Medowie, an impressive championship course set amongst natural wetlands and native forest.
Tennis, Skate Parks & Places to Play
Across Port Stephens you’ll find sporting fields, tennis courts, playgrounds, skate parks and recreation areas where visitors and locals can get active. From kicking a ball around and shooting hoops to skating, scootering or enjoying a family picnic, the region's parks and recreation spaces make it easy to add some activity to your day.

Acknowledgment
Destination Port Stephens acknowledges the Worimi people as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waterways on which we live, work and welcome visitors. We pay our respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. As modern custodians, we embrace the responsibility to protect, celebrate, and share the stories of Worimi Barray (Country). We invite visitors to walk softly, connect deeply, and experience the enduring spirit of this incredible place.
