Explore Moreton Bay, Bribie Island & Pumicestone Passage 
Looking for things to do around Bribie Island or Sandstone Point? Fish Moreton offers a unique way to experience the area, from family sightseeing and scenic cruises through Pumicestone Passage to fishing adventures across Moreton Bay. Departing from Sandstone Point, our charters are an easy option for visitors staying in the Bribie Island area who want to explore the waterways, spot local marine life, enjoy the scenery or simply spend a memorable day on the water.
Meet your Local Skipper 
With over 35 years of experience in fishing charters and cruising, Captain Wally brings local knowledge, experience and a personal touch to every trip. From relaxed family outings and scenic cruises to serious fishing adventures, Wally is focused on making every charter safe, enjoyable and memorable. Meet Captain Wally 
Where do you depart from? 
Fish Moreton departs from Spinnaker Sound Marina at Sandstone Point. 
​ 
How many people are onboard? 
Our fishing charters are limited to a maximum of 8 guests, while private sightseeing and scenic tours can accommodate up to 16 guests.
​ 
Do you go to Moreton Island? 
Yes. Our Moreton Island Swim & Cruise Tour is a relaxed way to explore Moreton Island by boat and enjoy the surrounding scenery and marine environment.
​ 
Do you have a minimum age limit for children? 
No. However, we recommend discussing younger children with the Captain so we can help you choose the most suitable trip.
​ 
What is supplied? 
Fishing rods, tackle and bait are supplied, along with drinking water and soft drinks. Catering can also be arranged on request. 
​
Frequently asked questions 
Based at Sandstone Point, the gateway to Bribie Island and Moreton Bay, Fish Moreton offers fishing charters and scenic boat tours throughout Moreton Bay and the Pumicestone Passage.

Responsible Fishing & Local Knowledge 
Moreton Bay is our backyard, and we want future generations to enjoy it too. Fish Moreton promotes responsible fishing, respectful handling of catch and care for the marine environment. Captain Wally also enjoys sharing his local knowledge of Moreton Bay, Bribie Island and Pumicestone Passage, helping guests learn more about the waterways, local species and responsible fishing practices. 
​ 
Responsible Fishing - Sensible fishing practices and respectful handling of catch. 
​ 
Local Knowledge - Learn about the waterways, fish species and conditions from an experienced local skipper. 
​ 
Respect for Moreton Bay - Enjoy the bay while helping protect the environment that makes it special.
