Humbug Fishing Charters Darwin, Northern Territory! 
Humbug Fishing runs liveaboard and tag-along fishing charters out of Darwin aboard Starfire, a 24-metre catamaran mothership. Charters reach Daly River, Moyle, Shady Camp, Tiwi Islands, and the Kimberley, targeting barra, reef, and pelagic species, for groups of up to 8 or 12 across a minimum of 5 days.
Remote water. No boat ramp queues. No 4pm curfew. You live on the water for the length of your charter, fishing the tides that matter instead of the ones that fit around a shore-based schedule. Our team plans the trip around your group, your dates, and what you want to catch.
Extended Live Aboard Fishing Charters
Live aboard Starfire for the length of your trip. Groups of up to 8 or 12, minimum 5 days away.
Tag Along With The Mother Ship
Fish from your own tender and use our mothership as your base each night.
Explore Our Destinations
Daly River, Moyle, Shady Camp, Tiwi Islands, the Kimberley, and the Timor Box.

Humbug Fishing Boats
We know you have been planning this trip for months, and your mates are counting on you to get it right. We have been doing this on Top End waters for 20 years.
Starfire is the largest charter mothership operating out of Darwin, with our Mother Ship, Brute Force, and two dedicated fishing tenders in survey. Clients regularly book next year’s trip before they step off the boat.
