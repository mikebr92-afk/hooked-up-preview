Wild West Charters has been showcasing the Swan River in Perth Western Australia and the Islands beyond since 2008, based around being able to take people of all ages and abilities out to see the most to travel the furthest offering a comfortable fast smooth and dry ride. Our vessels operate out of Elizabeth Quay in front of the City of Perth. Wild West Charter vessels are highly maintained in AMSA survey and our staff are friendly and motivated. We go the furthest to show you the most.

Amazing Swan River Cruises & Ocean Eco Tours
Daily, we offer Swan River adventure tours where we showcase to our passengers the incredible natural beauty of the Swan River and the amazing river side real-estate. We inform our passengers of Perth’s fascinating history both aboriginal and colonial and we locate the wildlife including Ospreys, Black Swans and our resident pod of Swan River Indo-Pacific bottle nosed dolphin.
We offer a set run to Fremantle right through the river through the working Fremantle port and into Fremantle Fisherman’s Harbour – return daily. This trip also has the option to venture out to see the rarest pinniped in the world the Australian Sea Lion in the eastern cove of Carnac Island also to see the amazing reef systems at the Mewstones and Stragglers rocks and to view the amazing sand dune formations on the northern end of Garden Island.
We offer a charter to catch Western Rock Lobster, where clients experience the Western Australian tradition of catching rock lobster (crayfish) by pulling crayfishing pots and to take the catch home.
Wild West Charters also offers private charters customized to the client’s needs and requirements including transfers out to Rottnest Island, snorkelling tours, birthday parties, events, team building etc. Clients can book a vessel and trust us to offer you the best day out on the water here in Perth Western Australian whether it be in the river or the islands beyond right from the front doorsteps of Perth city – Elizabeth Quay.

Slow Paced Ride 
Wild West Charters offers a slow-paced, daily Swan River adventure tour that showcases the incredible natural beauty of the river and its stunning riverside real estate.
Our jet drive propulsion vessels, with their shallow draught capabilities, navigate the riverbanks and explore secluded coves and hidden moorings, revealing the river’s most beautiful angles.
During the tour, we share fascinating stories about Perth’s history, both Aboriginal and colonial, and help passengers spot local wildlife, including ospreys, black swans, and our resident pod of Swan River Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphins.
