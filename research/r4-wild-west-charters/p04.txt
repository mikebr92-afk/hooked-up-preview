Wild West Charters
Back to See & Do 
Set sail from the heart of Perth with Wild West Charters and explore the Swan River like never before.
Cruise past luxury riverside homes, spot dolphins and ospreys, or venture offshore to rugged islands and vibrant marine life. From high-speed adventure tours to laid-back wildlife encounters, Wild West Charters takes you further to show you more.
Operating since 2008, their fast, comfortable vessels offer smooth rides for all ages and abilities. Join a Swan River tour, journey through Fremantle’s working port, or visit Carnac Island to see the rare Australian sea lion. For a hands-on experience, try the rock lobster tour—pull cray pots and take home the freshest catch.
After something bespoke? Private charters can take you to Rottnest Island, hidden snorkelling spots, or even host your next big celebration—all departing from Elizabeth Quay.
