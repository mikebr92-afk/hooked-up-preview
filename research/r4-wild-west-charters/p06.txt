Private Charter
Ready for a fun and leisurely ride on the water? Our boats have everything you need for a comfortable and enjoyable experience. With features designed to support your relaxation and comfort, such as sun shades and bluetooth speakers, you'll get to take in all of your favourite views safely and cozily.

Infinity 88
An exceptional smooth & stable vessel for both river cruises & tast transters. Our Indo-Pacific Bottlenose dolphins love to play in the wake of waves!
• 14 staged seats and full cover from the sun
• Stereo system with Bluetooth
• Onboard toilet and beautiful teak decks
• Eskies, ice and water

Your Adventure, Your Way
With Wild West Charters, you have the freedom to craft the perfect day on the water. Choose from our range of pre-selected tours, featuring stunning destinations like Rottnest Island, Carnac Island, Elizabeth Quay, and Fremantle, each offering unique sights and experiences. Prefer something more personal? You can customise your trip or create your own itinerary, tailoring your adventure to suit your group’s preferences. The choice is yours—let us help you make it unforgettable!
