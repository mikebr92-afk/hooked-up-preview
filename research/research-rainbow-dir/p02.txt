About
Big Dolphin Fishing Charters is a fishing charter located in Tin Can Bay and Rainbow Beach, Queensland. With a focus on personalised fishing experiences and targeting reef fish and surface pelagic species, they offer an unforgettable adventure for both experienced anglers and beginners.
Led by Rick, the owner and skipper, Big Dolphin Fishing Charters provides top-quality gear and a wealth of knowledge to ensure a successful and enjoyable fishing trip. They specialise in targeting a variety of species including Snapper, Sweet-lip, Red Emperor, Pearl Perch, Tusk-fish, Mackerel, Mahi Mahi, Tuna, Cod, and many more excellent eating reef and pelagic species.
One of the unique features that set Big Dolphin Fishing Charters apart is their use of live bait to attract bigger fish, maximising the chances of landing a trophy catch. In addition to their regular day fishing trips, they also offer extended fishing trips into the evening as well as overnight fishing trips for those looking for an immersive fishing experience.
With an impressive nine years of experience under his belt, Rick has skippered various fishing charters including Aurora 2, a 10-metre Cougar Cat, where he conducted extended overnight fishing trips. He then transitioned to the Baitrunner out of Rainbow Beach, offering day fishing trips. Rick also skippered CWide, a 15-metre cat out of Tin Can Bay, where he specialised in overnight extended fishing trips offshore Fraser Island. In addition to these charters, Rick owns his own Sharkcat, which provides smaller groups exclusive access to some of the best fishing hot spots offshore Fraser Island and Double Island Point.
The team at Big Dolphin Fishing Charters is committed to providing an exceptional fishing experience, ensuring that guests have an exciting and memorable time on the water. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a novice, rest assured that you'll receive the highest level of service and expertise.
To learn more about Big Dolphin Fishing Charters or to book a fishing adventure, visit their website or contact them directly. They can be found at Esplanade, Tin Can Bay QLD 4580, Australia. The charter operates from Monday to Sunday, from 6:00 am to 8:00 pm.
