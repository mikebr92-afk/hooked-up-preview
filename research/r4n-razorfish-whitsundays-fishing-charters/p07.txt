Frequently Asked Questions
EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW BEFORE YOUR FISHING CHARTER
BOOKINGS, CHARTERS & gift vouchers
What fishing charter options does Razorfish offer? 
Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters offers private and shared reef fishing experiences exploring the Whitsunday Islands and surrounding offshore fishing grounds.
Private Fishing Charters:
• Half Day – 5 hours
• Full Day – 8 hours
• Extended Outer Reef – 10–11 hours
Private charters are exclusively for your group, accommodating up to six guests.
Shared Fishing Charters:
• Full Day – 8 hours
Our shared reef fishing charters are ideal for individuals, couples, families and smaller groups, with a minimum of five confirmed passengers required for departure.
All charters are subject to weather conditions and availability. Explore our Private Charters and Shared Charters pages for current pricing and booking options.
How do I book a Whitsundays fishing charter? 
Booking your Whitsundays fishing charter is easy!
You can explore our charter options, view current pricing, check live availability and book securely online 24/7, at razorfishcharters.com.au 
Prefer to book directly? Give us a call on 0437 850 300 to arrange your charter over the phone and avoid online booking fees.
You can also contact our bookings team:
Email: bookings@razorfishcharters.com.au 
Or send us a message through Facebook or Instagram .
For private charters requiring alternative departure locations or special arrangements, we recommend contacting us before booking.
How many passengers can you accommodate? 
Our 7.5m Sailfish Commercial Offshore Catamaran accommodates a maximum of six guests, plus the skipper and deckhand.
Private fishing charters offer exclusive use of the vessel for your group, whether you’re booking for two guests or six.
Shared fishing charters accommodate up to six guests, with individual seats available to book.
Who is welcome aboard Razorfish? 
Everyone is welcome aboard Razorfish, from first-time fishos to experienced anglers!
Our private and shared Whitsundays fishing charters cater to individuals, couples, families with children and groups of friends. Private charters are also ideal for birthdays, corporate outings and special occasions.
Whether you’re new to fishing or an experienced angler, our crew is here to help you enjoy a great day on the water.
Do shared fishing charters require minimum passenger numbers? 
Yes. Our Full Day Shared Fishing Charters require a minimum of five confirmed passengers before departure can be confirmed.
Shared charters accommodate a maximum of six guests, making them a great option for individuals, couples and smaller groups wanting to enjoy reef fishing in the Whitsundays without booking the entire boat.
If your preferred departure hasn’t reached minimum numbers, we’ll contact you to discuss your options.
Can I book a private fishing charter for a special occasion? 
Absolutely! Our private Whitsundays fishing charters are ideal for birthdays, bucks’ parties, corporate outings, family celebrations or simply getting a group of mates together for a day on the water.
With exclusive use of the vessel for up to six guests, you can enjoy a more personalised fishing experience with your own group.
Contact us to discuss your plans, preferred date and any special arrangements before booking.
Do you offer gift vouchers for fishing charters? 
Yes! Razorfish gift vouchers make a fantastic present for fishing enthusiasts, first-time fishos and anyone wanting to experience reef fishing in the Whitsundays.
Our gift vouchers are available for a nominated dollar value and can be redeemed towards private or shared fishing charters.
Vouchers are delivered electronically by email, making them a convenient gift for birthdays, Christmas, Father’s Day and special occasions.
Visit our Gift Vouchers page to purchase yours.
How do I redeem a Razorfish gift voucher? 
Redeeming your Razorfish gift voucher is easy!
Simply visit razorfishcharters.com.au, select your preferred fishing charter and date through our online booking calendar, and enter your unique gift voucher code when completing your booking.
Alternatively, contact our bookings team on 0437 850 300 or email bookings@razorfishcharters.com.au with your voucher details and preferred charter date, and we’ll happily assist.
All bookings are subject to availability and applicable booking conditions. If your selected charter costs more than your voucher value, you can simply pay the difference.
Can I use a gift voucher towards any fishing charter? 
Yes. Razorfish gift vouchers are issued for a dollar value, allowing you to put them towards any of our available Private Fishing Charters or Full Day Shared Fishing Charters.
If your chosen charter costs more than the voucher amount, you can simply pay the difference.
If the charter costs less than your voucher value, any remaining balance can be used towards another eligible booking within the voucher’s validity period.
GET IN TOUCH 
PREPARING FOR YOUR FISHING CHARTER
Do I need previous fishing experience? 
Not at all! Whether you’re picking up a fishing rod for the first time or you’ve been fishing for years, our experienced crew is happy to help.
We provide quality Shimano fishing equipment, guidance and hands on assistance throughout your charter, so beginners and experienced anglers alike can enjoy reef fishing in the Whitsundays.
Our skipper and deckhand are there to help you make the most of your day on the water.
What is included in a Razorfish fishing charter? 
Every Razorfish Whitsundays fishing charter includes:
• Quality Shimano fishing rods and reels
• Bait, tackle and fishing equipment
• Experienced commercial skipper and deckhand
• Bottled drinking water and light snacks
• An iced esky for food and drinks
• Cleaning and filleting of your catch
• Comfortable seating and an enclosed onboard toilet
Our Full Day Private Charters also have an onboard hotplate available for preparing suitable food.
We provide the fishing essentials, so you can concentrate on enjoying your Whitsundays fishing adventure.
What should I bring on my fishing charter? 
To stay comfortable throughout your Whitsundays fishing charter, we recommend bringing:
• A hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
• Comfortable, sunsafe clothing
• Suitable nonslip footwear
• Lunch and any additional snacks you’d like
• Personal medications, including seasickness medication if required
• A camera or phone to capture your catches
You’re welcome to bring your own drinks, including alcoholic beverages in cans only. Glass bottles are not permitted onboard.
There’s no need to bring fishing gear or an esky, we provide both.
Is food and drinking water provided onboard? 
Yes. We provide bottled drinking water and light snacks on all Razorfish fishing charters.
We recommend bringing your own lunch and any additional food you’d like throughout the day, particularly on our Full Day and Extended Outer Reef Charters.
An onboard hotplate is available on Full Day Private Charters for guests who would like to bring suitable food to cook.
An iced esky is also provided to keep your food and drinks chilled.
Can I bring my own food and alcoholic drinks? 
Absolutely! You’re welcome to bring your own lunch, snacks and drinks aboard your Razorfish fishing charter.
Alcoholic beverages are permitted in cans only, with no glass bottles allowed onboard.
We provide an iced esky for your food and drinks, so there’s no need to bring your own.
What brands of fishing equipment do you provide? 
We’re proud to partner with Shimano, Dirty Tackle and Lure Us In, bringing quality fishing gear, tackle and lures aboard our Whitsundays fishing charters.
Our charters are equipped with quality Shimano rods and reels, along with a selection of tackle and lures suited to the reef and offshore species we target.
All fishing equipment, bait and tackle are provided, so there’s no need to bring your own gear. Whether you’re chasing a quality Coral Trout on the reef or a Spanish Mackerel, we’ve got you covered!
FISHING THE WHITSUNDAYS
Where do Razorfish fishing charters depart from? 
Our primary departure location is Cape Gloucester Resort, approximately 45 minutes by road from Airlie Beach.
Alternative departure and pickup locations may be arranged for suitable private fishing charters, including Airlie Beach, Bowen and selected Whitsunday island locations.
Alternative pickups are subject to availability, weather conditions, access arrangements and any applicable additional fees.
If you’re staying in Airlie Beach, on a Whitsunday island resort or elsewhere in the region, contact us before booking to discuss the most suitable departure arrangements.
Where will we go fishing in the Whitsundays? 
Our Whitsundays fishing charters explore a variety of fishing grounds around the Whitsunday Islands, surrounding reefs and offshore waters of the Great Barrier Reef.
Depending on your charter, we may fish island waters, reef drop offs, shoals or deeper offshore grounds.
Your skipper selects the fishing locations based on weather conditions, tides, the charter booked and where the best fishing opportunities are expected.
Our aim is to give you an enjoyable reef fishing experience while making the most of the conditions on the day.
What fish species can we catch in the Whitsundays? 
The Whitsundays and surrounding Great Barrier Reef waters are home to an incredible variety of reef and pelagic fish species.
Depending on the season, fishing grounds and conditions, species we target include:
• Coral Trout
• Red Emperor
• Large Mouth Nannygai
• Sweetlip
• Tuskfish
• Spanish Mackerel
• Tuna
Every charter offers a different fishing experience, and while catches can never be guaranteed, our experienced crew works hard to put you in the best possible position for a memorable day on the water.
All fishing is subject to applicable Queensland fisheries regulations and seasonal closures.
What happens with the fish I catch? 
Any legal fish you choose to keep will be cleaned, filleted and placed on ice by our crew, ready for you to take home or enjoy freshly prepared at a local Airlie Beach restaurant.
Want to turn your catch into an unforgettable dining experience?
Eastwoods Dining offers a sensational catch-to-plate experience, where their chefs can discuss preparation options and transform your freshly caught fish into a delicious restaurant quality meal. This experience should be arranged in advance, so please contact Eastwoods Dining directly for further details and requirements.
For something more casual, Whitsunday Seafood Bar offers the opportunity to have your fresh catch prepared for a classic local fish and chips experience. Contact them directly to discuss arrangements before your charter.
All fishing is conducted in accordance with Queensland fisheries regulations, including applicable size limits, possession limits and seasonal closures. Our crew will guide you through these requirements throughout your charter.
Who operates the fishing charter? 
Razorfish fishing charters are operated by experienced, fully qualified commercial skippers, including owner-operator Kris and skipper Amanda.
Both bring extensive boating and fishing experience to the team, with a strong focus on safety, local knowledge and making every charter enjoyable.
An experienced deckhand will also be onboard to assist with fishing, equipment and preparing your catch, ensuring guests receive plenty of support throughout their Whitsundays fishing adventure.
You can learn more about our crew on our About Us page.
What days do Razorfish fishing charters operate? 
Razorfish operates fishing charters throughout the year, subject to weather conditions, seasonal fishing closures and availability.
Private charters can be booked for available dates, while shared charters operate on scheduled departures once minimum passenger numbers are reached.
We recommend booking in advance, particularly during weekends, school holidays and peak holiday periods.
Visit razorfishcharters.com.au to check our current charter availability and book online, or call 0437 850 300 to arrange your booking directly without online booking fees.
Why choose Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters? 
At Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters, we’re passionate about delivering personalised reef fishing experiences with experienced local skippers, quality equipment and a genuine commitment to making every charter memorable.
Our 7.5m Sailfish Commercial Offshore Catamaran is purpose-built for fishing, featuring twin 200hp Mercury outboards, comfortable seating, an enclosed toilet and plenty of space to fish.
With a maximum of six guests, plus your skipper and deckhand, you’ll enjoy a more personal experience with hands on assistance throughout the day.
We’re also proud to partner with respected fishing brands Shimano, Dirty Tackle and Lure Us In.
Whether you’re chasing your first fish or your next quality reef catch, our crew puts in the effort to make your Whitsundays fishing adventure one to remember.
WEATHER, CANCELLATIONS & POLICIES
What weather conditions prevent a fishing charter from operating? 
Your safety is our priority. Razorfish fishing charters will not operate when marine conditions are considered unsafe, including strong winds, rough seas, storms or other unsuitable weather conditions.
Our experienced commercial skippers closely monitor marine forecasts and assess conditions before each departure.
The final decision to proceed rests with your skipper, taking into account the weather, sea conditions and safety of everyone onboard.
If conditions are unsuitable, we’ll contact you to discuss the available options.
What happens if my charter is cancelled due to bad weather? 
If your Razorfish fishing charter is cancelled by the skipper due to unsafe weather conditions or circumstances beyond our control, you’ll be offered the option to reschedule to another suitable date, subject to availability, or receive a refund in accordance with our booking conditions.
Weather decisions are made with the safety of our guests and crew in mind.
We monitor forecasts leading up to departure and will contact you if conditions are likely to affect your booking.
What is your cancellation policy? 
Private Fishing Charters: Cancellations made less than 7 days before your scheduled departure will result in forfeiture of the charter payment.
Shared Fishing Charters: Cancellations made less than 48 hours before your scheduled departure will result in forfeiture of the full charter payment.
Weather Related Cancellations
If your skipper cancels the charter due to unsafe weather conditions or circumstances beyond our control, you’ll be offered an alternative date, subject to availability, or a refund in accordance with our booking conditions.
What happens if a shared charter doesn't reach minimum passenger numbers? 
Our Full Day Shared Fishing Charters require a minimum of five confirmed passengers to proceed.
If your scheduled departure hasn’t reached the required numbers, we’ll contact you to discuss your options, which may include transferring your booking to another available shared charter date or arranging a refund of your payment.
If you’ve booked using a gift voucher, we can discuss alternative dates for you to use your voucher.
We recommend keeping your plans flexible when booking a shared charter, as departures cannot be confirmed until minimum passenger numbers are reached.
What happens if I'm running late for my fishing charter? 
We ask all guests to arrive at their confirmed departure location at least 15 minutes before their scheduled charter departure.
Our Shared Fishing Charters depart on schedule to ensure all guests enjoy their full time on the water.
Guests arriving more than 5 minutes after the scheduled shared charter departure time will be considered a No Show. The charter will depart without late guests, and the booking will be 100% non-refundable and non-transferable.
If you’re running late or having difficulty finding your departure location, please contact us immediately on 0437 850 300 .
We recommend allowing plenty of travel time, particularly when travelling from Airlie Beach to Cape Gloucester Resort.
Are Razorfish gift vouchers refundable or transferable? 
Razorfish gift vouchers are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
Gift vouchers are transferable, allowing you to pass your voucher on to another recipient if you’re unable to use it yourself.
Vouchers can be redeemed towards our Private Fishing Charters or Full Day Shared Fishing Charters, subject to availability and applicable booking conditions.
For assistance with transferring or redeeming your gift voucher, please contact our bookings team on 0437 850 300 or email bookings@razorfishcharters.com.au .
