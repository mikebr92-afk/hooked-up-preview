PRIVATE FISHING CHARTERS IN THE WHITSUNDAYS
EXCLUSIVE CHARTERS. QUALITY FISHING. YOUR DAY, YOUR WAY.
Looking for a private fishing charter in the Whitsundays? Get your own boat, your own experienced skipper and a full day of fishing planned around your group with Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters.
Whether you’re chasing your next personal best, getting the family out fishing or organising a trip with mates, our private charters cater for up to six guests aboard our 7.5m Sailfish offshore catamaran. No crowds, no sharing with other groups, just your crew, quality Shimano fishing gear and local knowledge of the Whitsunday Islands and Great Barrier Reef.
Choose from a 5-hour Half Day Charter, an 8-hour Full Day Charter or our 10–11-hour Extended Outer Reef Charter for those wanting to venture further offshore. Your skipper will plan the fishing around the conditions, your group’s experience and the opportunities available on the day.
Our standard departure point is Cape Gloucester Resort, with private fishing charters from Airlie Beach and Bowen also available by arrangement.
FISH THE WHITSUNDAYS & GREAT BARRIER REEF
From the sheltered waters around the Whitsunday Islands to productive offshore reefs, shoals and the Outer Great Barrier Reef, there’s plenty of opportunity to get amongst some quality fish.
Depending on the weather, tides and fishing conditions, we target species including Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Large Mouth Nannygai, Sweetlip, Tuskfish and Spanish Mackerel. Whether we’re bottom fishing over reef and shoal country, working jigs or trolling for pelagic species, our experienced local skippers know how to make the most of the conditions.
All fishing gear, bait and tackle are supplied and we’ll clean, fillet and bag your catch ready to take home. Whether you’re a seasoned fisho or heading offshore for the first time, we’ll help make it a day worth remembering.
FISHING Locations
Whitsunday Islands, inshore and offshore reefs, shoals and the Outer Great Barrier Reef.
DEPARTURES
Cape Gloucester Resort, with Airlie Beach and Bowen available by arrangement.
WEATHER CONDITIONS
Fishing grounds and departure times are confirmed based on weather and sea conditions.
