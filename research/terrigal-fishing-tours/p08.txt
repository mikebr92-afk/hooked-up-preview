About Terrigal Fishing Tours
Skye 1
Take 3 For The Sea 
Charter Fishing NSW 
Eco Tourism Australia 
Terrigal Fishing Charters are small family boat charter business located on the NSW Central Coast. Providing high quality charters with quality boats and equipment and trained and experienced crew. Our aim is to give you the best day on the water possible for your chosen day.
Respect and care for our ocean
We aim to make minimal impact on our environment in everything that we do. Supporting the principles of sustainable fishing so that our fisheries resources can be enjoyed for generations to come. Using environmentally friendly fishing equipment and techniques. Our parent business, Terrigal Ocean Tours is certified by Ecotourism Australia in recognition of our sustainable business practice, marine education, and contributions to the local community. We participate in Take 3 for the sea groundswell program which aims to reduce the impacts of tourism on the environment.
We acknowledge and respect the Darkinjung aboriginal people as the traditional custodians of these lands and waters. The Darkinjung people of the Central Coast celebrated the Humpback Whale “Torongnul” and the Stingray “Stingaree” as some of their totems. Our area is rich in cultural and natural history.
Our Crew
Andy was born and raised on the NSW Central Coast and has had a passion for fishing his entire life. He has been in the charter fishing industry for 12 years, with over a decade of experience fishing both the inshore and offshore waters and reefs of the Central Coast. A qualified and experienced commercial skipper who has worked locally on the Central Coast, in the Northern Territory and on the Great Barrier Reef guiding sports fishing, game fishing, reef fishing, and fly-fishing charters in the pristine waters Australia has on offer. 
Annabel is an experienced deckhand and host. She is a key contact for marketing and bookings and makes sure that things run smoothly on the beach. She is currently studying Coastal and Marine Science at the University of Newcastle.
Andrew is an experienced offshore mariner with experience in boat charters in local and international waters. A former Officer in the Royal Australian Navy.
