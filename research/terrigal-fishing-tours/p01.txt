Central Coast Fishing Charters
Come fishing on-board the Central Coasts premier charter boats with Terrigal Fishing Tours. We are a locally, family owned and operated fishing charter business based on the beautiful Central Coast of New South Wales. We operate from both Terrigal and Hardys Bay. Located just 60 minutes north of Sydney.
Our professional and friendly crew aim to provide you with the best charter experience on our fully equipped, purpose-built fishing vessel, Skye 1. We cater for both experienced anglers and people new to the sport. If you are new to fishing our crew will be happy to assist you and give you a range of skills to take home.
We also provide for Corporate Groups, Whale Watching, Party Cruises and Kids Fishing Charters.

Fishing Charters on the NSW Central Coast
You can book the boat for your own group. A Private Charter is a great idea for corporate functions, birthdays, parties or anytime you want to get a group of friends together to enjoy fishing on the NSW Central Coast. A private fishing charter options give you exclusive use of vessel and allows for flexible departure times. We also offer shared charters, if you dont have enough in your group to book the whole boat we can team you up with others to fill a charter.
We target a range of great species including
Snapper, Flathead, Kingfish, Mahi Mahi, Trevally, Teraglin, Jewfish, Tuna, Blue Morwong, Bonito and Perch. Using our years of experience to select the best fishing grounds based on
the season and the weather forecast for the day. 
We supply quality rods & reels and high-quality bait.
You can bring your own equipment if you prefer.
All you need to bring is your own food & drink, hat,
camera & sunnies. Also bring along a cooler bag to take your cleaned fish home.
