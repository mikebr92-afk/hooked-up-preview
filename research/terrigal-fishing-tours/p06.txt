Gift Vouchers
You can purchase fishing charter gift vouchers on this page. We provide online gift vouchers for shared charters. Please contact us if you require a gift voucher for a private charter.
No Expiry Date – our gift cards do not expire.
NOTE – WE ONLY RUN FISHING CHARTERS IN SUMMER MONTHS DECEMBER TO APRIL 
Reef & Bay Fishing Gift Voucher
Shared Charter departing Hardys Bay Wharf at 6:45am. We will be fishing offshore reefs, targeting a range of species including Snapper, Flathead, Bream, Taylor, Kingfish. Estuary options available if the wind blows up in the afternoon.
Buy a Gift Voucher Reef & Bay Fishing Charter 
Kids Fishing Charter Gift Voucher
Our Broken Bay charters depart from the delightful seaside village of Hardys Bay. If conditions are suitable, we can head to the remote offshore reefs of the Bouddi Peninsula and their bountiful fish. Alternatively, we can stay within the bay for some calm water fishing around the headlands and estuaries. A great days fishing and we very rarely have to cancel as we have more weather options.
This is a great option for special events and groups as it is less weather dependent and very unlikely to be cancelled due to weather. Target species include; Snapper / Flathead / Bream / Jewfish / Kingfish and Tailor.
