About
If you're seeking an unforgettable adventure on the water, look no further than 1770 Creek2Reef Fishing Charters. This fantastic operation features a robust 10.3m Cougar Cat boat, perfect for accommodating various activities. Tony and Sandy are exceptional hosts who prioritize your experience, ensuring seamless communication even during challenging weather forecasts. Their expertise shines through as they navigate to stunning locations like Lady Musgrave Island.
Why you should go
1
• Experience snorkeling at the reef with a chance to see whales on the way
2
• Enjoy fishing trips where dolphins swim around the boat
3
• Personalized tours to Lady Musgrave for a unique adventure

Know before you go
• Book in advance as spots are limited due to small group sizes
• Consider doing a combination trip like half-day fishing/half-day snorkeling for varied experiences
• Follow weather forecasts closely for optimal trip planning as hosts provide valuable advice based on conditions
• Engage with Tony and Sandy for tailored experiences based on your preferences
Reviews
Creek2Reef Fishing Charters, run by Tony and Sandy, offers an unforgettable experience combining snorkeling at the reef, whale watching, fishing trips with dolphins swimming around the boat, and personalized tours to Lady Musgrave. Reviewers praise the hosts' hospitality and knowledge, highlighting their flexibility in activities like trolling and bait fishing. The smaller boat size ensures a more intimate experience even during peak seasons. Review score
