About
Reef Fishing Charters 1770 is an esteemed fishing charter business operating out of the 1770 Marina in the scenic Central Queensland & Southern Great Barrier Reef Region. With their newest and fastest charter vessel, Southern Comfort, they offer unforgettable offshore reef fishing experiences. The company is locally owned and operated, providing guests with a genuine and authentic Queensland fishing adventure.
Reef Fishing Charters 1770 specialises in targeting a variety of prized fish species, including Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Red Throat, Grassy Sweet Lip, Spanish Mackerel, and Nannygai. They have a unique keep your catch policy, allowing guests to bring their own esky to store their freshly caught fish. Additionally, they can connect guests with a local fish filleting service at a reasonable price.
The crew at Reef Fishing Charters 1770 are experienced, friendly, and dedicated to ensuring that guests have an incredible time on board Southern Comfort. They have extensive knowledge of the best fishing spots in the Southern Great Barrier Reef and will go above and beyond to make the charter a highlight of visitors' trips to 1770 and Agnes Water. The crew is always attentive, ready to assist with baiting hooks, serving drinks, and taking care of guests' catches.
At Reef Fishing Charters 1770, they don't just offer a fishing charter, they provide an experience that guests will cherish for years to come. Along with reef fishing, they also offer the option of combining fishing with snorkelling on their fully equipped, purpose-built vessel, Southern Comfort. The crew has over 10 years of experience and can provide coaching to new and young anglers, ensuring that everyone has a chance to land their prized catch.
Reef Fishing Charters 1770 has a skilled crew consisting of Skipper Brent and Deckhand Charlie. Both have been fishing the local waters for over 7 years and have extensive knowledge of the best fishing spots in the area. Skipper Brent has a passion for hunting prized catches, while Deckhand Charlie brings years of experience and local knowledge of reef fishing. With their combined expertise and friendly approach, guests are guaranteed to have an exceptional charter experience.
For booking inquiries, guests can contact Reef Fishing Charters 1770 through their website or call their business and after-hours number. Their booking office is located at 72 Streeter Dr, Agnes Water QLD 4677. It is worth noting that they highly recommend using Shimano gear for the best fishing experience.
With outstanding reviews on TripAdvisor, Reef Fishing Charters 1770 has garnered a reputation as one of the best fishing charters in the 1770 area. Guests rave about the professionalism of the crew and the variety of fish species they catch. The company is committed to providing a top-notch fishing experience and continues to receive positive feedback from satisfied customers.
In summary, Reef Fishing Charters 1770 is the go-to fishing charter service in Seventeen Seventy. With their state-of-the-art vessel, experienced crew, and in-depth knowledge of the Southern Great Barrier Reef, they offer an unforgettable fishing adventure. Whether guests are experienced anglers or first-timers, Reef Fishing Charters 1770 guarantees an incredible and memorable experience on the waters of the stunning Great Barrier Reef.
