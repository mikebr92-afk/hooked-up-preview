Good to know FAQs
What types of trips do you offer?
We offer a variety of experiences to suit everyone. Whether you're looking for a relaxing Rottnest Island day trip or an action-packed game fishing experience in Perth, we have the perfect trip for you and your crew.
How many people can join a trip?
Our private boat charters can accommodate up to 8 passengers for Rottnest day trips. For game fishing trips, we allow up to 6 anglers to ensure there is ample uncrowded space and an exceptional fishing experience.
What's included in the fishing trips?
Our charters include everything you need for a perfect day on the water. We provide fishing equipment on game fishing charters and access to our boat's top-tier amenities, including a sound system and air-conditioned saloon. All alcoholic drinks are strictly BYO, and we'll provide water and soft drinks throughout the day.
How do I book a trip?
Booking your trip with Game Time Charters is simple! Head over to our Booking Page , choose your desired trip, and secure your spot. If you have any custom requests or special arrangements, feel free to contact us directly. We're always happy to help.
