Fishing News 
Bring on 2023 
By Kurt Rowlands 
&bull; 
February 18, 2023 
Welcome to our first newsletter for 2023. Firstly, on behalf of the Fish's Fly and Sportfishing team we would like to thank everyone for there support in 2022. We had a very busy year running 4 guide boats with some notable captures from Permit, BB's, Tuskies , Barramundi and all the usual species Cape York is famous for.
Welcome to the new site 
By Kurt Rowlands 
&bull; 
February 21, 2020 
Thanks for stopping by! During the season, which don't forget starts in April, we'll be posting weekly fishing updates so you can keep track on just how good the fishing is up here in Weipa. If there's anything in particular you'd like to know about or see more of, please get in touch via the site, or via Facebook or Instagram. Happy fishing! Alan
