[ OVERVIEW ] 
Your Dream Fishery
Cape York is renowned for its extraordinary diversity. It’s not uncommon to spend a week here and encounter 30 different species on fly, making the fishery as rewarding for first-time saltwater fly anglers as it is for seasoned veterans. 
While the variety is remarkable, Cape York also offers some of the most technical and challenging fly fishing in the world. The region is the undisputed global stronghold for the endemic T. anak Permit. If there is one place on earth to tick this species off on fly, this is it. The original founder of the business, Alan “Fish” Philliskirk, was the first person to land a Permit on fly in Australia, and since then the Permit fishery here has grown into a world-class destination. 
From sight fishing ultra-shallow, mangrove-lined mud flats for Barramundi and King Threadfin Salmon, to stalking crystal-clear flats for Blue Bastards, Blackspot Tuskfish, and T. anak Permit, through to offshore bait balls being torn apart by a wide range of pelagic sportfish, the opportunities are endless. 
No matter the conditions or your experience level, there is always something happening, and always another shot waiting.

There's fly fishing and then there's Weipa, where the sheer variety of species and fishing styles turns every day into something completely different from the last.
Species
55+ Species. All Environments. 
Giant Trevally • Barramundi • Permit • Queenfish • Golden Trevally • Spanish Mackerel • Longtail Tuna • Cobia • Milkfish • Sailfish • Mangrove Jack • Fingermark • Giant Herring • Grey Mackerel • Threadfin Salmon • and many more.
Sight-fish pristine flats. Work the estuaries. Chase pelagics offshore. Follow manta rays for golden trevally and cobia.
This is tropical fly fishing at its absolute best. 
Food & Drink
We Handle the Meals. You Handle the Fish. 
Forget meal planning, grocery runs, and cooking after a long day on the water. Our all-inclusive packages include quality, freshly prepared meals throughout your trip, breakfast to fuel your early starts, packed lunches for full days on the boat, and hearty dinners to recap the day's action.
All dietary requirements catered for. All meals sorted. All you need to focus on is the next cast.
Transfers
Airport Transfers Sorted 
Skip the hire car headaches and taxi wait times. Simply send us your flight details, and we'll arrange complimentary airport transfers between Weipa Airport and your accommodation, both arrival and departure.
One less thing to organise. One more reason to relax and focus on the fishing ahead.From individuals, to large groups, we can cater for all airport transfer requirements. 
Lodging 
Accommodation to Suit Every Budget and Preference 
Whether you want an all-inclusive lodge experience with a private chef, a comfortable motel with bar and restaurant, or a self-contained option where you control your own schedule, we've got you sorted. From waterfront lodges to budget-friendly cabins, we work with Weipa's best accommodation providers to ensure you're comfortable, well-fed, and ready to fish hard.
Tell us your preference, and we'll handle the rest.
Your Itinerary
Day 0: Arrival in Weipa
Airport Collection: 
Our shuttle service will meet you at Weipa Airport upon arrival. We recommend the 12:25pm flight, allowing time to address any baggage issues or flight delays, and providing an opportunity to settle in before your first fishing session.
Pre-Accommodation Stops: 
If you wish to purchase groceries, beverages, or other supplies, simply advise your driver and they can arrange a brief stop en route to your accommodation.
Guide Briefing: 
Following check-in, one of our guides will meet with you to provide a current fishing report and trip overview. For guests arriving on the afternoon flight, this briefing occurs in person. For later arrivals, the briefing is conducted via phone prior to your departure for Weipa.
Evening Schedule: 
• Early flight arrivals (12:25pm): Time to prepare gear and relax before dinner
• Late flight arrivals: Dinner service shortly after check-in, with gear preparation time to follow
• 
Accommodations 
Your driver will collect you from the Airport and drop to your selected accomodation.
Fishing Days 
Our packages are fully customisable so this can be as long or as short as you like!
Morning: 
Breakfast is served between 6:00-6:30am, with guides collecting guests at 7:00am for departure.
On the Water: 
All meals, snacks, and beverages are provided throughout the day. Guests with specific dietary requirements should notify us in advance, these will be communicated to your guide prior to departure. Fishing sessions typically conclude by 4:00pm, depending on conditions and fishing activity.
Evening: 
Guests staying at the Fishing Lodge will be welcomed back with a charcuterie board or sashimi platter, followed by entrée service at 6:00pm and a full dinner with dessert. Guests accommodated elsewhere are welcome to dine at their chosen restaurant at their preferred time.
• 
Additional Details 
Please ensure you notify us of any dietary requirements 
Last Day: Departure
We will arrange your airport transfer based on your flight departure time. The shuttle service will collect you from your accommodation and transport you directly to Weipa Airport, ensuring a smooth departure.
• 
Additional Details 
All of your logistic details will be sorted prior to the beginning of your trip.
