Enjoy the boat to yourself on a Private Charter in Top End! 
Experience personalized service & an unforgettable full-day fishing charter with our expert guide – max 4 guests!
Come join us for a safe, exciting day out on the water! Our professional and experienced guide will begin with safety briefing and take you to the right spot that offer the greatest success, according to the tide and season. Hop aboard for an unforgettable experience! 
The fish you catch can be kept or released at your choice. Many guests like to keep and take their catch home with them. If so, we’ll clean and fillet for you to take home. Please note that our crew strictly complies by fishing rules in the Northern Territory.
Full-day private charters spend a full 8 hours on the water using techniques including trolling, casting hard body lures, and casting soft plastics. Based on tide conditions, our guide will choose the best plan to target different species across two charter styles:
• Remote & 4WD – 10-11 hours trip 
Head off the beaten track to remote region, combining a 4WD adventure with a full day of fishing in pristine surroundings — embrace the nature, fishing, and spot wildlife including crocodiles and abundant wild birds.
• Darwin Harbour – 8 hours trip 
Spend most of your energy on the fishing with a full day on the waters of Darwin Harbour, offering easy access and a variety of species on offer depending on the tide.
On the way home, our guide will clean or fillet your catch and bag it up for you to take home!
Perfect for small groups — couples, friends, and families are all welcome.
Fishing Option 
• Darwin Harbour — Fish for Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Jewfish, Golden Snapper, Coral Trout, Spanish Mackerel, Tuna and a range of reef species in the estuary, or arrange a crabbing day, and keep an eye out for crocodiles along the harbour.
• Billabong Fishing — Target freshwater Barramundi, Saratoga, Tarpon, and Freshwater Cod, while spotting crocodiles, Jabiru, Eagles, Kites, Buffalo, and other abundant wildlife along the way.
• Dundee Reef Fishing / Finniss River — Chase Golden Snapper, Black Jewfish, Coral Trout, Emperor, Spanish Mackerel, and more across pristine reef grounds. Surrounded by crystal-clear waters and untouched coastline, you’ll enjoy action-packed fishing while taking in the breath-taking Top End scenery.
• Bynoe Harbour — Enjoy saltwater estuary fishing for Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, and Crabs, set against spectacular river scenery.
Note: Fishing location depends on the tide and weather condition, we will let you know prior the tour.

🔗 Meet our guides/vessel 
When you book with Barra Private Tours, you’re not just booking a boat — you’re booking into a crew. Our fleet runs three vessels, each helmed by a guide with over a decade of experience reading NT water, and all built to the same premium standard: 6m+ Australian-grade hulls, powerful outboards, advanced navigation, and LiveScope fish-finding technology for when the barra go quiet and you need to see what they’re not showing you.
We didn’t build this fleet to cut corners — we built it so more anglers can get on the water during our busiest season, without ever settling for a lesser experience. Same capacity. Same standard of care. Same NT knowledge running deep in every guide’s blood. What changes is the flavour of expertise each guide brings to your day on the water.
