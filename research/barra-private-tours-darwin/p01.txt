Go With the Flo
Welcome to Barra Private Tours! We bring you the ease and flexibility of being able to book a full day private barra fishing tour in different locations.
Barra Private Tours is a fishing charter in Darwin, Northern Territory, Australia. We offer private tours for corporate groups or families; tours can be designed for your individual needs. Captain, Flo, will use his angling expertise to make sure you have an amazing time on the water.
