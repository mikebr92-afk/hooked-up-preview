Barra Private Tours, based in Darwin, Northern Territory, Australia, specialises in tailored private charters for individuals and corporate groups.
Led by their experienced captain, a long-time fishing enthusiast, they are dedicated to providing the ultimate fishing experience on the water.
They strive to help clients catch their own fish while sharing their passion for fishing, guiding with dedication, and creating unforgettable moments that make every catch special. Witnessing clients land their first barramundi and sharing in their joy and excitement is what drives them.
Their 6.1 matre Australian Master Marine vessel, equipped with a 250 HP outboard engine and state-of-the-art navigation tools, including LiveScope for detecting fish movement, ensures an exceptional fishing adventure along the region's rivers and coastal creeks. Accommodating up to four passengers, our boat offers a comfortable and memorable experience.
Whether you're a beginner or an advanced angler, their skilled skipper will share expert techniques, find prime fishing spots, and work alongside you to catch your dream fish. Barramundi, a prized species known for its size, fighting spirit, and flavour. Catching one is a truly thrilling experience.
Your guide will clean and fillet your catch, so you can take home a delicious memento of your adventure.
Join them for a personalised, one-of-a-kind fishing trip with Barra Private Tours. Book your adventure today—you won't be disappointed!

Tours available
Full Day Private Charter 
Experience personalized service & an unforgettable full-day fishing charter with our expert guide – max 4 guests!
Come join us for a safe, exciting day out on the water! Our professional and experienced guide will begin with safety briefing and take you to the right spot that offer the greatest success, according to the tide and season. Hop aboard for an unforgettable experience!
When you reach your first location, your guide will provide you all the instruction and support needed to catch barramundi. Both casting and trolling might be part of your day.
The fish you catch can be kept or released at your choice. Many guests like to keep and take their catch home with them. If so, we’ll clean and fillet for you to take home. 
Please note that our crew strictly complies by fishing rules in the Northern Territory.

Multi Days Private Charters 
Get ready for a truly unique experience, head to the Mary River National Park in the Northern Territory during the Runoff season.
Boasting a breathtakingly river system, the park is home to a wealth of wildlife and, a thriving population of barramundi. A place where more people catch their metery than most other places in the Territory.
What's included?
🏷️Fishing: chasing metre-plus Barra on our 6.1m custom Barra boats with the options to go for reef fishing chasing large Jewfish.
🏷️Private Charter: which you will book the whole boat and our guide, Floran, just for you and your mates.
🏷️Transport: We'll pick you up from Darwin hotel in the early morning.
🏷️Accommodation: Stay at Remote Fishing Lodge, in an air-conditioned room provides restful nights.
🏷️Fishing Gears: High quality fishing equipment and best quality lures with Livescope to target fish more precisely.
🏷️Experienced guide Owner/Operator by a true barra enthusiast who's called the Northern Territory home for over 10 years. He's taken his love for fishing and crafted it into a career to share with fellow anglers!
🏷️Meals: Continental/ Full breakfast; Lunch on board; Dinner at an air conditional restaurant (Lodge), selection of diverse and flavourful meals.
