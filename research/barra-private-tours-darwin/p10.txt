🔥 NT Runoff Extended Fishing Trip 2027 🔥 
Get ready for a truly unique experience, head to the awe-inspiring Mary River National Park in the Northern Territory during the Runoff season.
This is the prime time for catching large barramundi. As the floodwaters recede, barramundi return to the river systems, congregating in channels and creeks.
Boasting a breathtakingly large floodplain and a river system lined with lush forests, the park is home to a wealth of wildlife and, most notably, a thriving population of barramundi. A place where more people catch their metery than most other places in the Territory.
Everything is taken care! Just let us know where to pick you up and drop you back in Darwin airport/ hotel.
What’s included? 
🏷️ Fishing : chasing metre-plus Barra on our 6.1m custom Barra boats with the options to go for reef fishing chasing large Jewfish.
🏷️ Private Charter: which you will book the whole boat and our guide, Floran, just for you and your mates.
🏷️ Transport : We’ll pick you up from Darwin hotel in the early morning.
🏷️ Accommodation : Stay at Remote Fishing Lodge, in an air-conditioned room provides restful nights, in preparation for the day’s exciting pursuits.
🏷️ Fishing Gears: High quality fishing equipment and best quality lures with Livescope to target fish more precisely.
🏷️ Experienced guide Owner/Operator by a true barra enthusiast who’s called the Northern Territory home for over 10 years. He’s taken his love for fishing and crafted it into a career to share with fellow anglers!
🏷️ Meals: Continental/ Full breakfast; Lunch on board; Dinner at an air conditional restaurant (Lodge), selection of diverse and flavourful meals.
Fishing destinations 
Shady Camp, located on the Mary River, approximately 2.5hrs drive from Darwin . In this location, the freshwater habitat converges with the saltwater tidal river, creating an ideal haven for large barramundi, particularly after the monsoonal rains of the tropical wet season.
• Distance : The trip from the Shady Camp boat ramp to the mouth of the Mary River is approximately 20-25 kilometers (12-15 miles).
• Time : Depending on the boat’s speed and tide conditions, it generally takes 45 minutes to an hour to reach the river mouth. Tides and water levels can significantly impact travel time, so it’s important to plan according to the tides.
Why Choose Multiday Package 
🔑 Extended fishing time , increasing your chances of catching trophy fish by exploring multiple spots over different conditions. It provides flexibility to adjust for weather or slow fishing days and allows you to target different species.
🔑 All inclusive , our packages include accommodation, meals, and expert guides, creating a hassle-free experience. You’ll enjoy more time to relax, bond with others, and immerse yourself in the scenic surroundings.
🔑 Improve your fishing skills , it’s a great opportunity to improve your fishing skills and make the most of your adventure without the rush of a single-day trip.😊
