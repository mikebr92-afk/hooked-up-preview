Unforgettable Fishing Experiences in Noosa
Noosa, located on the Sunshine Coast, is famous for its outstanding deep sea and offshore fishing in Queensland. With easy access to prime fishing locations and a mild climate year-round, Deepsea Fishing Co. Noosa is your premier choice for discovering the area’s abundant waters. Our expert guides are dedicated to providing an unforgettable experience, leading you to the best fishing hotspots. Our custom-built Catamarans ensure that you’ll enjoy the ultimate fishing adventure in comfort.

About us 
Come aboard and enjoy the ultimate fishing adventure!
Unmatched fishing experience
Join us for an unforgettable day of deep sea fishing on some of Queensland's finest fishing grounds. Our fleet of vessels is equipped to provide the perfect charter experience, whether you're an experienced angler or a beginner - no prior experience is needed!
Refreshments available
Enjoy a selection of refreshments, including bottled water, snacks, and meals which may include morning / afternoon tea or lunch based on the duration of your fishing charter.
All equipment provided
We supply everything you need for a great day of fishing, including rods, reels, bait, and tackle. You're welcome to bring your own fishing gear as well. Our crew will expertly clean, ice, and package your freshly caught fish, making it easy for you to take home.
Vessels designed for comfort
Our vessels are purpose-built commercial catamarans specifically designed for deep sea and offshore fishing. Featuring the largest back deck fishing platform in Noosa and comfortable seating inside, they also include a private toilet for your convenience. 
Where to find us
Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa operates from Noosa Marina, located at 2 Parkyn Court, Tewantin – Noosa, Sunshine Coast, Queensland, established in 1989. Our vessels have been navigating the waters of the Sunshine Coast for generations, and our crew consists of seasoned experts in the Noosa fishing charter industry.
Noosa is home to some of the best deep sea fishing in Queensland
As a third-generation offshore fishing charter operation established in 1989, we depart from Noosa Marina in Tewantin. Clint Ruster and our skilled crew are experts in the Noosa fishing charter industry, bringing years of experience and a deep respect for the ocean to every charter. Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa offers deep sea and offshore fishing charters that promise a memorable experience you won’t forget!
