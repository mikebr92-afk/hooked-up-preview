Frequently asked questions
What types of fishing charters do you offer? 
We offer half-day (5 hour), three-quarter day (7 hour), and full-day (9 hour) deep sea and offshore fishing charters.
What are the departure times for the charters? 
• 
5 Hour Charter: 6:30am to 11:30am and 12:00pm to 5:00pm 
• 
7 Hour Charter: 6:00am to 1:00pm 
• 
9 Hour Charter: 6:00am to 3:00pm 
How much do the charters cost? 
• 
5 Hour Charter: $245 per person 
• 
7 Hour Charter: $295 per person 
• 
9 Hour Charter: $345 per person 
What should I expect during the fishing charter? 
You can expect an exciting adventure where you’ll fish in Noosa’s abundant waters and target a variety of species, including Coral Trout, Snapper, and Mackerel. Our experienced crew will be on hand to assist you throughout the trip.
Do I need prior fishing experience? 
No prior fishing experience is required! Our expert crew will guide you and provide all the necessary equipment.
What is included in the charter? 
Our charters include bait, tackle, water, packaged snacks, and morning / afternoon tea or lunch (depending on the duration of your charter). We also clean and prepare your catch for you to take home.
How long does it take to reach the fishing grounds? 
Travel times to our fishing grounds vary based on the charter duration. For half-day charters, it’s approximately 20 to 40 minutes, while for three-quarter and full-day charters, it can take 1.5 to 2 hours each way.
Can I book a charter for a large group? 
Yes! We welcome individual bookings as well as group and exclusive charters.
How can I purchase a gift voucher? 
Our gift vouchers are valid for 3 years from date of issue and can be purchased here or by contacting us directly . They make a wonderful gift for any occasion!
How can I contact you for more information or to book a charter? 
You can reach us by phone at 0411 222 749 for inquiries or to book your fishing charter.
