Booking & Refunds
Booking and refund policy
A deposit of $500.00 is required on all exclusive bookings and the balance is payable the day before the charter.
This is fully refundable should the weather or other factors force Deep sea fishing co Noosa to change the date.
Exclusive deposits are non refundable but can be used as a credit for another days fishing. 48 hours notice required.
A credit card is required on all individual bookings and the balance is payable on the day. This is fully refundable should the weather or other factors force Deep sea fishing co Noosa to change the date.
48 hours notice is required for all cancellations or the full amount will be charged to the credit card.
Confirmation
Confirmation will only be given by phone the evening before by 4.00pm as weather dictates this. 
Should I book ahead?
Yes. We operate seven days a week (except Christmas Day) but holiday periods and weekends are very popular and should be booked well in advance.
Gift Vouchers expire three years from date of issue and can be extended under deep sea fishing co Noosa’s discretion.
Gift Vouchers are not redeemable for cash.
Safety and Weather Policy
Marine weather forecasts can change during the day and overnight. We will endeavor to inform you via text or phone and email if any decision to cancel a charter is made by 4pm. Please ensure your phone is switched on.
Children under the age of 12 must be accompanied by an adult and become their responsibility whilst on board the boat.
Only wine and beer (in cans) are permitted to be taken and consumed on the boat in MODERATION. The skipper has the authority to ban drinking alcohol at any time to enable a safe boating situation to be maintained for all on board.
If you breach this policy and an early return to shore results from unruly behavior you will be liable for compensation to the other passengers on the boat. Private charters will not be entitled to any refund. Anyone who turns up to attend a charter inebriated or under the influence of drugs will not be allowed to board.
