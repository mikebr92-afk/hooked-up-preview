Deep Sea Fishing Co is Noosa’s Top offshore fishing charter!
Est 1989 we are leaders in the fishing charter industry & still the original owners 
Experience the best deep sea fishing charter in Noosa. 
Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa is the original owners and longest established deep sea/offshore fishing charters in Noosa on the Sunshine Coast, being a 3rd generation offshore fishing charter operation. As the original and highly reputable leaders in the fishing industry, our CATCH 22, DEEPSEA 7 & DEEPSEA 11 Series Cougar Catamarans built to handle any deep sea/offshore fishing conditions in comfort. As a seasoned fishing experts, we have years of expertise to ensure your upcoming fishing trip is one to remember. 
Unforgettable Fishing Experiences in Noosa
Noosa on the Sunshine Coast is renowned for exceptional deep sea/offshore fishing in Queensland. With convenient access to prime fishing grounds and a year-round mild climate, Deepsea Fishing Co. Noosa is the top choice for exploring the rich waters. Our expert guides ensure an unforgettable experience, taking you to the best fishing spots. Our custom-built CATCH 22, DEEPSEA 7 & DEEPSEA 11 3600 Series Cougar Catamarans guarantee the ultimate fishing experience.
WHERE TO FIND US
Deep Sea Fishing Co Noosa aboard CATCH 22, DEEPSEA 7 & DEEPSEA 11 are located at Noosa Marina. 2 Parkyn Court Tewantin – Noosa Sunshine Coast Queensland EST 1989. Our boats have been fishing the waters of the Sunshine Coast for generations and our crew are experts in the Noosa fishing charter industry.
