Deep sea fishing co Noosa a true offshore experience 
Fishing the local and wider reefs off Noosa species expected to catch snapper, pearlies, sweet lip , Moses perch, Cobia, mackerel, tuna, and many more species.
Equipment Provided
Rods, reels, bait and tackle is all supplied onboard, you are welcome to bring your own fishing gear. Crew will also clean, ice and bag your freshly-caught fish ready for you to take home.
Our vessels are set up for comfort and performance – plenty of deck space 
Vessels CATCH 22 & DEEPSEA7 & DEEPSEA 11
3600 series (11 metres) commercial catamaran built especially for deep sea fishing, offshore fishing and any fishing charters. These boats have the biggest back deck fishing platform in Noosa, lounge inside for seating. (private toilet onboard)
Fishing deep sea isn’t guesswork – decades of local experience 
Perfect for everyone 
• Families and first timers 
• serious anglers
• Groups and celebrations
No experience needed just bring yourself and a sense of adventure 
Come aboard CATCH 22, DEEPSEA 7 & DEEPSEA 11 experience a unforgettable day of deep sea fishing on some of Queenslands best fishing grounds. For a great day on Noosa’s open water. (no experience needed)
Why do our customers keep coming back 
• Friendly professional crew
• Top quality gear included 
• Clean safe well maintained boats
• A genuine Noosa Sunshine Coast Queensland experience 
Refreshments Available
Bottled water, snacks – Morning / afternoon tea / lunch depending on your fishing charters duration.
Noosa on the Sunshine Coast is renowned for exceptional deep sea/offshore fishing in Queensland. With convenient access to prime fishing grounds and a year-round mild climate, Deepsea Fishing Co. Noosa is the top choice for exploring the rich waters. Our expert guides ensure an unforgettable experience, taking you to the best fishing spots. Our custom-built CATCH 22, DEEPSEA 7 & DEEPSEA 11 3600 Series Cougar Catamarans guarantee the ultimate fishing experience.
