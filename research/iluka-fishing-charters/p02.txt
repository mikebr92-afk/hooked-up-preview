MV Lady-N
We take you where the fish are!
Owned and operated by Peter Tate, out of Gladstone Queensland.
MV Lady-N is a luxuriously appointed 60ft Custom Built Charter Boat
We offer a unique fishing experience around the Capricorn Bunker Group and Swains Reef.
Whether it’s a 3 day charter or 7 day, we aim to make your getaway fishing trip one to remember! We look forward to meeting your every fishing requirement Lady-N offers extended trips from 7-10 days to Swains Reef and Shoalwater Bay Trips, also minimum of 3-day trips around the Capricorn Bunker Group and Shoals. All bookings are up to 8 passengers. All charters come complete with an experienced skipper and Deck Hand so you just have to relax and enjoy some of Queensland's best fishing spots.
