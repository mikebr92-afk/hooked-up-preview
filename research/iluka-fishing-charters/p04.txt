MV Lady-N
the Capricorn Bunker Group and Swains Reef
Vessel name – MV Lady-N, QLD reg 3327 QC
MV Lady-N is a luxuriously appointed 60ft Custom Built Charter Boat
We offer a unique fishing experience around the Capricorn Bunker Group and Swains Reef. 
Whether it’s a 3 day charter or 7 day, we aim to make your getaway fishing trip one to remember!
