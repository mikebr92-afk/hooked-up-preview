Fishing Charters
the thrill and excitement of a Swains Reef fishing adventure!
Iluka Fishing Charters
We can take you to where the fish are, on our Swains Reef Charters!
Climb aboard the The MV Lady-N for some the best Gladstone Charter Fishing on offer! Whether you are fishing for the very first time, are a seasoned fisherman, or you would just like to see what the charter fishing excitement is all about, let Iluka's Gladstone fishing charter take you there to find out!
Peter Tate, owner and operator of Iluka Fishing Charters is dedicated to providing you with a charter fishing experience of a lifetime. The crew are skilled in Swains Reef charter fishing and will do their best to keep you busy catching big fish. Peter and the crew aim to deliver a fun and friendly trip for you and all onboard the Lady N.
Pete, is an experienced skipper with a wealth of knowledge, and a terrific attitude! Peter has an exhaustive knowledge of the area and its’ marine life, and is ably supported by his skilled crew who will always be ready to assist you with all your fishing needs.
If you have not had much experience with fishing, don't worry, the friendly crew from Iluka fishing charters will help you learn the skills needed to reel in a great fish from the sea! and are always willing to teach you as much or as little as you would like. Everyone is welcome aboard our Gladstone fishing charter -- from novice to experienced fisherman, to corporate groups and families.
If you want to get out on the water to chase those elusive big fish or maybe just keep busy with more light tackle charter fishing action, then you found the right fishing charter company to get you into the best fishing trips on the waters of the Swains Reef. Whether you’re fishing, snorkeling or diving, chasing big Red Emperor, the fighting Trevally, or the highly prized Coral Trout, or just taking some time out to relax, Iluka Fishing Charters aim to make your charter fishing trip one to remember! 
Iluka Fishing Charters can arrange for you and your mates to embark on an fishing trip filled with the thrill and excitement of a true Swains Reef charter fishing adventure! - the type of deep sea fishing you and your mates have dreamt of experiencing! * All fishing charter trips subject to weather conditions.
What we offer
Iluka Charters caters for smaller groups up to 8 passengers; group bookings less then 8 are based of a daily rate for the vessel.
All bookings will be confirmed when a $2000 deposit is pain at least three months prior to departure. Balance is payable on arrival or can be paid directly into out account.
All towels n linen supplied any special dietary needs or medical conditions of concern need to be notified at least 3 weeks prior to departure.
All bate and EMC chargers plus GST are included in the price.
All fish caught will be filleted and frozen in packs or kept whole at your request.

What to bring
*Fishing gear and tackle
*Wet weather gear
*Hat, sunscreen, sunglasses
*Swimming gear, snorkels, spears (if you want to go snorkelling or spear fishing)
*DVDS, CDS, 
*Clothes that can get fishy and a bag to put the dirty ones in.
*BYO alcohol (alcohol can be arranged to be onboard on arrival
but we need to be notified 2 weeks before departure and it is to be paid for on arrival.)
Note: for an additional cost, we can also supply fishing gear.
