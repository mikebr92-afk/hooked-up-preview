About
Central Coast Reef and Game Fishing Charters is a sports club based in Terrigal on the beautiful Central Coast of New South Wales, Australia. Founded in 1996, this club offers fishing charters for deep-sea reef fishing, game fishing, and whale watching. The club has built a fine reputation for providing top-quality vessels, excellent customer service, and high safety standards. Their crew consists of experienced and qualified individuals who are dedicated to ensuring a great day out on the water for all members and guests.
Whether you are a fishing enthusiast, looking for a fun family or friends get-together, or planning a work bonding session, Central Coast Reef and Game Fishing Charters has the perfect fishing experience for you. They offer a wide range of fishing charters to suit everyone's preferences. From deep-sea reef fishing to game fishing, you will have the opportunity to catch sportfish and saltwater species such as Marlin, Yellowfin Tuna, Albacore, Snapper, and Jewfish.
The club prides itself on its fully equipped and well-maintained boat, which is ideal for deep-sea fishing. The boat is modern, fast, and equipped with all the necessary amenities, including a shower, toilet, galley, canopy, and seating deck. Rod holders, a live bait tank, fish cleaning tables, and insulated fish and bait boxes are also available, ensuring you have everything you need for a successful day of fishing. The club's crew is always ready to assist with fishing techniques and will even clean and ice your catch, making it ready for you to take home.
Safety is of utmost importance to Central Coast Reef and Game Fishing Charters. The club provides all necessary safety equipment and strictly adheres to safety practises to ensure all members and guests have a safe and enjoyable fishing experience.
If you are interested in booking a fishing charter with Central Coast Reef and Game Fishing Charters, it is recommended to make a booking in advance, as spots can fill up quickly. The club's contact information, including the phone number and email address, can be found on their website. The club is conveniently located at Fisherman's Beach, The Haven, Terrigal, and they provide a map for easy navigation.
Central Coast Reef and Game Fishing Charters invites you to join them for an exciting and memorable day of fishing on the beautiful Central Coast. With their experienced crew, top-quality vessels, and a variety of fishing options, they guarantee a fantastic fishing experience for all members and guests.

Frequently Asked Questions
What fishing and activities do you offer? 
We run deep sea reef fishing, game fishing, and leisure charters including whale watching. You can join a reef fishing charter to catch sportfish and saltwater species.
Where do the charters depart from and what’s included? 
Charters run from the Haven, Terrigal on the Central Coast. Our crew looks after you on the water, and we assist with fishing techniques while providing facilities like a toilet, shower, galley, and fish cleaning setup.
How do I book and what do I need to know? 
Bookings are a must, and you can find pricing, terms, and directions on our Info page. No licence is required.
