About Central Coast Reef & Game Fishing Charters
Central Coast Reef & Game Fishing Charters offers reef fishing, game fishing and whale watching trips departing from Terrigal Haven. Join an experienced local crew to target species including snapper, kingfish, marlin and tuna, with all fishing gear provided and no fishing licence required.
