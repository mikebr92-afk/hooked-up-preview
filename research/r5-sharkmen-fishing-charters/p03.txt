ALL YEAR ROUND
Looking for a unique corporate experience that your team will actually remember?
At Sharkmen Fishing Charters, we specialise in tailored corporate fishing experiences designed for businesses of all sizes. Whether you are rewarding staff, entertaining clients, building team culture or celebrating company milestones, we can create a day on the water to suit your business needs.
With two professionally operated vessels accommodating up to 10 guests per boat, we can host both small and larger corporate groups in a relaxed, exciting and highly engaging environment.
Our experienced skippers and deckhands ensure your group is looked after from start to finish, regardless of fishing experience level.
For larger corporate groups, Sharkmen can also coordinate additional vessels through a select network of trusted charter operators that we have worked closely with over the years. This allows us to cater for larger scale events and group bookings requiring more than 20 guests while still maintaining a high standard of organisation and experience.
