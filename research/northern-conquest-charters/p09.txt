About Conquest Charters
3 
At Conquest, we’ve spent over 20 years taking keen adventurers out to experience some of the best fishing on the East Coast of Australia. With our deep local knowledge, top-tier boats, and a crew that knows how to look after you, we don’t just run fishing charters—we create unforgettable experiences. 
We specialise in Great Barrier Reef fishing charters, from half-day trips to multi-day liveaboard expeditions, departing from Yeppoon, Queensland. Our liveaboard vessels are designed for comfort, so you can focus on reeling in trophy fish by day and unwinding in a relaxed, well-equipped setting by night.
Chasing big Pelagics? Keen on a reef fishing adventure? Prefer to mix it up with some island hopping? Or maybe you’d like to take over a boat for a special event? However you like to explore the water, Conquest has you covered.
Vessels
Our Boats 
At Conquest, our boats are built for adventure. Whether you’re fishing, island hopping, or just soaking up the beauty of the Great Barrier Reef, our vessels provide the perfect balance of comfort, safety, and performance.
With full marine park registration, we can take you anywhere, giving you access to some of the most incredible waters on the East Coast.
Boat
Vanuatu 
“Vanuatu” is a 58′ Conquest vessel, purpose-built for fishing in total comfort and safety. It features:
 
State-of-the-art electronics – Advanced fish-finding and navigation equipment.
 
Air-conditioned cabins – Stay cool and comfortable after a big day on the water.
 
Spacious shower & desalination unit – Plenty of fresh water for showers and toilets.
 
Expansive rear deck – Designed for maximum fishing space.
 
Air-conditioned saloon – The perfect spot for a game of cards or to kick back and relax.
 
Plenty of freezer & fridge space – Four freezers and two fridges ensure your catch is well cared for.
 
Catch processing on extended trips – For trips over 72 hours, we fillet, bag, and freeze your catch for you.
 
Fully catered meals – Quality cooked meals and non-alcoholic drinks are provided, with dietary needs catered for.
 
Capacity :
• Extended trips: 8–10 guests 
• Day & overnight trips: Up to 20 guests 
Boat
San Miguel 
“San Miguel” is a 65′ vessel, purchased in 2014 as the ultimate platform for extended trips. With a length of 18.2 metres and a beam of 5.0 metres, it offers:
 
Comfortable accommodation – Room for up to 10 guests (all linen supplied).
 
Convenient facilities – Two showers & two toilets (no morning queues!).
 
Spacious top deck – Ideal for relaxing, catching the breeze, or even casting a line.
 
Premium catch storage :
• 2.5m³ of freezer space + snap freezing .
• Fish are bled in a saltwater tub, chilled in a saltwater brine, then filleted and snap-frozen to maintain the highest quality.
• Styrofoam airline-approved boxes are supplied for transport, or road freight can be arranged.
 
Fully air-conditioned – Comfort throughout the cabins and bridge.
 
Power outlets in cabins – Perfect for CPAP machines (keep the snoring down!).
 
Well-equipped galley & BBQ – Taste the magic of Chef Roly’s cooking.
 
Large water storage & desalination system – Plenty of hot water for showers.
Staff
Our Crew 
Operations Manager | Cook | Deckhand | Skipper in the Making
Benny Costa 
Born to fish, Forced to Ummm Fish! 
With over 25 years fishing the Great Barrier Reef as a commercial Coral Trout and charter fisherman, these days Benny specialises in fishing for big Reds and Giant Coral Trout. 
Ben is also our resident cook, keeping lines tight and stomaches full. 
When he’s not on deck helping put our guests onto the fish of a lifetime, or cooking up a storm, Ben runs his own business in Townsville, is currently in the process of obtaining his skipper’s tickets. 
“In my spare time, I’m always fishing. I love chasing big Barramundi, Mangrove Jacks and other land based monsters in some of the wildest spots in North Queensland. I’ve got the Best office in the world.”
Skipper | Professional Fisherman
Daniel Price 
Daniel Price — better known as “Cocky” — is a skipper who backs his confidence with capability. A professional fisherman through and through, Dan knows
where the fish live and more importantly, how to make them bite. If there’s a patch holding Red Emperor or Coral Trout, he’ll find it. He’s not the kind of skipper who hides in the wheelhouse either. When it’s time to get amongst it, Dan’s on the back deck — gaff in hand, helping crew, filleting fish, and making sure every guest is dialled in properly. Mechanically sharp, there’s not much this man can’t fix. Engines, electrics, hydraulics — if something stops working, Dan’s already halfway through sorting it. Confident? Absolutely. Competent? Without question. He’s got strong customer skills, keeps the vibe steady offshore, and knows San Miguel like it’s an extension of himself. One minute he’s smouldering over the sounder looking for fish, the next he’s in a pink tutu stirring the crew up — and five minutes later you’re hooked up to the biggest fish of your life. Dan the Man. If there’s fish to be found, he’ll have you hanging on.

Senior Deckhand | Coxswain | Pro Fishing Back Deck Specialist
Luke Currie 
Luke Currie, 39, is the kind of senior deckhand you want beside you when things get serious offshore. Raised on a Queensland cattle station until he was 19,
Luke figured out early that while the land was good — the water was better. He’s been pro fishing ever since, from mud crabbing in the Northern Territory to live Coral Trout operations on the Great Barrier Reef. Luke has worked trawlers and fishing charters from the Sunshine Coast to Cairns and everywhere in between. He knows his fish, his tackle, his gear — and exactly what it takes to run a professional back deck. As a qualified Coxswain with diesel fitting experience, he’s not just there to gaff fish. In the unlikely event something needs attention, Luke is the bloke you want on hand. Calm, capable and mechanically sharp. But here’s the other side — Luke’s also the crew jokester. He can read a bite, run a deck and keep clients dialled in — and five minutes later have the whole boat laughing. He lightens the mood when the weather’s up, keeps the energy steady on long runs, and somehow still makes it look effortless. Serious fisherman. Solid operator. Good for morale. Built tough. Built reef smart. Built Conquest.

Our Mission & Values 
At Conquest, we’re all about epic ocean adventures—fishing, exploring, and making memories out on the water. We take pride in running smooth, well-planned trips where you can relax, chase big fish, and soak up the best of the Great Barrier Reef. With a focus on safety, sustainability, and top-notch service, we make sure every trip is one to remember.
