Careers
3 
Imagine an office where the horizon is your only boundary and the pristine, world-heritage waters of the Great Barrier Reef are your playground. At Conquest Charters, this is our reality. Operating from Yeppoon, we are one of Queensland’s premier fishing and charter operators, offering guests the adventure of a lifetime.
We are built on a foundation of teamwork, professionalism, and providing unforgettable experiences for every guest who steps aboard. Our crew is the heart of our operation, and we are committed to fostering a supportive, safe, and dynamic environment where you can grow your skills and build a rewarding career on the water.
We’re always on the lookout for exceptional individuals who share our passion. We are searching for crew who are:
 
Passionate about the ocean , fishing, and the world-renowned ecosystem of the Great Barrier Reef.
 
Customer-focused , with a natural ability to connect with people and provide outstanding service.
 
Team players who are reliable, hardworking, and thrive in a collaborative environment.
 
Safety-conscious , with an unwavering commitment to procedure and the well-being of our guests and crew.
 
Professional and enthusiastic , taking pride in their work and representing the Conquest Charters brand.
Staff
Our Crew 
Operations Manager | Cook | Deckhand | Skipper in the Making
Benny Costa 
Born to fish, Forced to Ummm Fish! 
With over 25 years fishing the Great Barrier Reef as a commercial Coral Trout and charter fisherman, these days Benny specialises in fishing for big Reds and Giant Coral Trout. 
Ben is also our resident cook, keeping lines tight and stomaches full. 
When he’s not on deck helping put our guests onto the fish of a lifetime, or cooking up a storm, Ben runs his own business in Townsville, is currently in the process of obtaining his skipper’s tickets. 
“In my spare time, I’m always fishing. I love chasing big Barramundi, Mangrove Jacks and other land based monsters in some of the wildest spots in North Queensland. I’ve got the Best office in the world.”
Skipper | Professional Fisherman
Daniel Price 
Daniel Price — better known as “Cocky” — is a skipper who backs his confidence with capability. A professional fisherman through and through, Dan knows
where the fish live and more importantly, how to make them bite. If there’s a patch holding Red Emperor or Coral Trout, he’ll find it. He’s not the kind of skipper who hides in the wheelhouse either. When it’s time to get amongst it, Dan’s on the back deck — gaff in hand, helping crew, filleting fish, and making sure every guest is dialled in properly. Mechanically sharp, there’s not much this man can’t fix. Engines, electrics, hydraulics — if something stops working, Dan’s already halfway through sorting it. Confident? Absolutely. Competent? Without question. He’s got strong customer skills, keeps the vibe steady offshore, and knows San Miguel like it’s an extension of himself. One minute he’s smouldering over the sounder looking for fish, the next he’s in a pink tutu stirring the crew up — and five minutes later you’re hooked up to the biggest fish of your life. Dan the Man. If there’s fish to be found, he’ll have you hanging on.
“Big Trout. Big Reds. Big smiles.”
“Loves walks on the beach and Red Coral Trout.”
“If there’s fish around, he’ll sniff them out.”
“Knows San Miguel like the back of his hand.”
“Not afraid to rock a pink tutu.”
Senior Deckhand | Coxswain | Pro Fishing Back Deck Specialist
Luke Currie 
Luke Currie, 39, is the kind of senior deckhand you want beside you when things get serious offshore. Raised on a Queensland cattle station until he was 19,
Luke figured out early that while the land was good — the water was better. He’s been pro fishing ever since, from mud crabbing in the Northern Territory to live Coral Trout operations on the Great Barrier Reef. Luke has worked trawlers and fishing charters from the Sunshine Coast to Cairns and everywhere in between. He knows his fish, his tackle, his gear — and exactly what it takes to run a professional back deck. As a qualified Coxswain with diesel fitting experience, he’s not just there to gaff fish. In the unlikely event something needs attention, Luke is the bloke you want on hand. Calm, capable and mechanically sharp. But here’s the other side — Luke’s also the crew jokester. He can read a bite, run a deck and keep clients dialled in — and five minutes later have the whole boat laughing. He lightens the mood when the weather’s up, keeps the energy steady on long runs, and somehow still makes it look effortless. Serious fisherman. Solid operator. Good for morale. Built tough. Built reef smart. Built Conquest. 
“If the rods are bent, life’s good.”
“Fishing saved me from becoming a pornstar.”
“Raised on a QLD cattle station. Built for the water.”
“From NT mud crabs to GBR live coral trout.”
“Jokester. Mood lifter. Back deck weapon.”
