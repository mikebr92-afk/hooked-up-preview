Island & Reef Adventures
3 
Adventure
Sightseeing & Island Tours 
Experience the breathtaking beauty of the Great Barrier Reef, Keepel Bay Area, and Magnetic Island with our sightseeing and island-hopping tours. Whether you’re after a relaxing cruise, a snorkelling adventure, or a day exploring hidden beaches, we’ll make sure your trip is one to remember.
Itineraries 
 
Magnetic Island Escape – Cruise the bays, stop for a swim, and enjoy a picnic on a secluded beach.
 
Reef & Island Combo – Explore the reef in the morning, then unwind on a tropical island in the afternoon.
 
Sunset Cruise – A laid-back evening on the water with stunning sunset views.
Customize
Custom Charters & Private Events 
Looking for something unique? We can tailor a trip to suit your group’s needs—whether it’s a relaxing day on the water, a multi-day adventure, or something extra special.
Cruises, Hire & Parties 
When most people think of Northern Conquest Charters, they picture world-class fishing. But did you know we also hire our vessels for private events? If you’re looking to celebrate in style, why not swap the traditional function room for a floating venue with unbeatable views?
Popular events include:
 
Bucks & Hens Parties – Celebrate with a private boat, good mates, and the perfect backdrop.
 
Birthday & Christmas Parties – Gather your friends and family for an unforgettable day or night on the water.
 
Corporate Cruises – Host your next work event, team bonding trip, or incentive reward in a unique and memorable way.
We often cruise the sheltered bays of Magnetic Island, offering calm waters, scenic views, and plenty of space to relax and enjoy the party.
Science
Marine Services & Research Support 
Northern Conquest Charters also provides professional marine services to government agencies and private enterprises. Our vessels have been used for a variety of projects, including:
 
Defence Science Research – We’ve provided support services for the RAAF for several years.
 
Environmental Research – Mother ship support for AIMS research and collection projects for over 7 years.
 
Cruise Ship Transfers – Reliable and professional transport services.
 
Marine Logistics & Transport – Including crane-assisted loading/unloading.
FAQs
Island & Reef Adventures - FAQs 
What happens if the weather is bad?
If conditions aren’t safe, we’ll either reschedule your trip or provide a credit for a future date. Travel insurance is recommended for any additional costs.
What should I bring?
We provide meals and drinks (alcohol availble to purchase), but you’ll need:
• Swimwear and towel
• Sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses
• Light clothing and a warm layer
• Enclosed footwear if exploring islands
Are your trips suitable for families?
Yes! Our island-hopping and reef adventure trips are family-friendly. Just let us know if you’re bringing kids so we can ensure their comfort and safety.
Can I snorkel or swim on the trip?
Absolutely! We can provide snorkelling gear on request, or you can bring your own, and our crew will guide you to the best spots.
Do I need to be a strong swimmer?
Not at all. We have flotation devices available, and there are plenty of shallow spots for relaxed snorkelling.
Is there food on board?
Yes! All trips include quality cooked meals, snacks, and non-alcoholic drinks. We can cater to dietary needs—just let us know in advance.
Can I book a private charter?
Definitely! We offer private boat hire for special events, corporate trips, or customised itineraries. Get in touch, and we’ll put together the perfect experience for you.
Explore the Great Barrier Reef with us and we promise great fishing and an epic adventure!
