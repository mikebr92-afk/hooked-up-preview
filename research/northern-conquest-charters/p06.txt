Island and Reef Adventures FAQs 
What happens if the weather is bad?
If conditions aren’t safe, we’ll either reschedule your trip or provide a credit for a future date. Travel insurance is recommended for any additional costs.
What should I bring?
We provide meals and drinks (alcohol availble to purchase), but you’ll need:
• Swimwear and towel
• Sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses
• Light clothing and a warm layer
• Enclosed footwear if exploring islands
Are your trips suitable for families?
Yes! Our island-hopping and reef adventure trips are family-friendly. Just let us know if you’re bringing kids so we can ensure their comfort and safety.
Can I snorkel or swim on the trip?
Absolutely! We can provide snorkelling gear on request, or you can bring your own, and our crew will guide you to the best spots.
Do I need to be a strong swimmer?
Not at all. We have flotation devices available, and there are plenty of shallow spots for relaxed snorkelling.
Is there food on board?
Yes! All trips include quality cooked meals, snacks, and non-alcoholic drinks. We can cater to dietary needs—just let us know in advance.
Can I book a private charter?
Definitely! We offer private boat hire for special events, corporate trips, or customised itineraries. Get in touch, and we’ll put together the perfect experience for you.
Fishing Quest FAQs 
What happens if the weather is bad?
Safety is our top priority. If the weather is unsafe for departure, we’ll reschedule your trip or offer a credit toward a future booking. We recommend travel insurance to cover any non-refundable costs.
When should I arrive at the boat?
We ask that you arrive half an hour prior to departure for the Welcome Tour and Safety Briefing.
What do I need to bring?
We provide all bait, tackle, and meals, and alcohol (available for purchase) but you should bring:
• Sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses
• Enclosed footwear
• Light clothing and a warm layer for cooler evenings
• Any personal medications
Can I bring my own fishing gear?
Absolutely! You’re welcome to bring your own rods, reels, and tackle. We also have high-quality fishing gear available for hire.
What happens to the fish I catch?
For day trips, you can take your cleaned fish home in your own esky. On extended trips (72+ hours), we fillet, bag, and freeze your catch for you. We also offer airline-approved Styrofoam boxes for transport.
What is the minimum age for kids?
For offshore fishing trips, children must weigh at least 32kg (typically around 10 years old) and must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
What if I get seasick?
If you’re prone to seasickness, we recommend taking medication before departure. Once on board, fresh air, staying hydrated, and keeping your eyes on the horizon can help.
What if I need to cancel my trip?
Deposits are non-refundable, but you may transfer your spot to someone else or rebook for a future trip. Cancellation policies vary based on notice given—see our Terms & Conditions for details.
