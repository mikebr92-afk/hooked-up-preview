The Ultimate Guide to Fishing the Great Barrier Reef 
The Great Barrier Reef is one of the world’s most spectacular fishing destinations, offering an incredible variety of species and fishing experiences. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or a first-timer, here’s everything you need to know about fishing in this legendary location. 
Why Fish Out of Townsville? 
While there are many departure points along the Great Barrier Reef, Townsville offers some key advantages over other harbours: 
• Less Fishing Pressure: The reefs we visit are further offshore and not as heavily fished, meaning bigger and more abundant catches. Compared to areas near Cairns and Port Douglas, where tourism is high, our fishing grounds remain relatively untouched. 
• Direct Access to Prime Locations: Townsville provides a strategic launch point to both the inner and outer reef systems, giving anglers more variety. This means we can target everything from shallow coral reefs to deep-sea drop-offs in a single trip. 
• Consistently Good Conditions: The waters off Townsville tend to have fewer interruptions from extreme weather patterns compared to northern ports. Being further south along the reef means fewer tropical cyclones and better year-round fishing. 
Best Fishing Environments 
• Reef Fishing: Target species like Coral Trout, Red Emperor, and Nannygai around vibrant coral structures. 
• Deep Sea Fishing : Venture further offshore for Jobfish, Goldband Snapper, and Trevally. 
• Pelagic Fishing: Chase fast-moving giants like Spanish Mackerel, Tuna, and Marlin. 
Best Times of Year 
• Summer (December – February): Great for Marlin and GTs but can be hot and stormy. 
• Autumn (March – May): Calmer seas and excellent reef fishing. 
• Winter (June – August): Peak season for Red Emperor and Nannygai. 
• Spring (September – November): Ideal for Mackerel, Tuna, and mixed bag fishing. 
What to Expect on a Conquest Trip 
• Expert local guides who know the best fishing spots. 
• High-quality fishing gear and tackle. 
• Comfortable liveaboard or day-trip experiences. 
• Freshly prepared meals and full onboard facilities. 
For more information about the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park and sustainable fishing regulations, visit the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority website : https://www.gbrmpa.gov.au .
