Offshore Fishing Charters in Agnes Water & 1770
Offshore Fishing
Discover world-class offshore fishing in the 1770 region, where local charters offer tailored adventures for anglers of all skill levels. Whether you’re after reef fishing, estuary trips, or a day out on the water with friends, you’ll find the perfect fishing experience right here.
The waters off Agnes Water and 1770 are renowned for their rich marine life and pristine fishing grounds. With a range of experienced fishing charters, you can choose from half-day, full-day, or custom trips to suit your style—reef fishing, estuarine adventures, or even a sunset cruise.
Reef Fish & Dive 1770
Caters for 2–10 passengers | 3100 Noosa Cat: SOUTHERN COMFORT
The only local operator offering SCUBA diving as well as fishing. The Southern Great Barrier Reef is famous for both hard and soft corals, and the waters here are home to Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Spanish Mackerel, and more. Reef Fish & Dive has a “keep your catch” policy and can even arrange local filleting for your fresh haul.
Phone: 07 4974 7906 | info@reeffishanddive1770.com.au | reeffishanddive1770.com.au
Hooked On 1770 Fishing Charters
Caters for up to 7 passengers | 33ft Clayton Gallant: HOOKED ON 2
Hooked On 1770 provides all fishing equipment or you can bring your own. They specialise in reef fishing but also cater for trolling and sport fishing. The deep-water reefs off 1770 are teeming with Nannygai, Sweetlip, Cod, Mackerel, Coral Trout, and more. Offshore rocks and trawler wrecks offer exciting opportunities for big catches all year round.
Phone: 0428 94 1770
Whether you’re a keen angler or a first-timer, offshore fishing in Agnes Water and 1770 is an unforgettable experience.
Check-In: 2:00pm
