Unreel Fishing Charters is one of the most renowned and reliable fishing charters in Melbourne when it comes to catching fish.
We operate in and around Port Phillip Bay, Western Port Bay, Port Fairy, Portland, Apollo Bay and surrounding areas, consistently providing our clients the best fishing Victoria has to offer.
Our focus at Unreel Fishing Charters is all about catching fish because that is what we do best. Our commitment to our customers is to maintain affordable rates, whilst delivering the best fishing available in our waters.
We are a no frills operation and we operate on the good old fashioned principles of hard work, honesty and friendship. We have a reputation for going further, staying longer and trying harder to make sure that we catch our target fish for our customers.
Meet the Team
• 
James Rogers
FOUNDER & HEAD SKIPPER
Fishing isn't just James' job—it's his passion and way of life. Having spent countless hours on the water from a young age, he turned his lifelong dream into reality when he founded Unreel Fishing Charters, allowing him to share the excitement of fishing with others every day.
Known for his tireless work ethic and commitment to his customers, James has built a reputation for working harder, staying longer and doing whatever it takes to find fish. His friendly nature, local knowledge and genuine love of fishing ensure every guest enjoys an unforgettable experience on the water.
• 
Tristan Rogers
SKIPPER
As the eldest son in the Rogers family, Tristan has spent his life on the water and developed a passion for fishing from a very young age. An exceptional fisherman and experienced skipper, he has earned a reputation for consistently finding fish and delivering outstanding results for his clients.
Known for his friendly personality, willingness to help and incredible eye for spotting fish activity, Tristan is highly respected within the fishing industry and a favourite among customers. His local knowledge, professionalism and passion for fishing help ensure every trip is both successful and memorable.
• 
Dylan Rogers
SKIPPER
Few people are as passionate about fishing as Dylan. A qualified skipper and dedicated angler, he spends countless hours on the water chasing fish, exploring new locations and constantly improving his craft. Fishing isn't just something he enjoys—it's what drives him.
With a great sense of humour and a genuine love for helping others succeed, Dylan brings plenty of energy to every trip. His determination to find fish, combined with his extensive time on the water and successful fishing YouTube channel, make him a valuable member of the Unreel Fishing Charters team.
• 
Bailey Rogers
DECKHAND
Having grown up around boats and fishing, Bailey has been part of the Unreel Fishing Charters family from day one. While still completing his schooling, he joins the crew during school holidays and busy charter periods, bringing plenty of enthusiasm, energy and a genuine passion for fishing.
Friendly, approachable and always willing to lend a hand, Bailey enjoys helping guests make the most of their day on the water. When he's not fishing, he loves turning the day's catch into a great meal, with a passion for cooking seafood that rivals his passion for catching it.
• 
Nino Rogers
CUSTOMER RELATIONS
Nino is the friendly voice behind Unreel Fishing Charters, managing all bookings, enquiries and customer communications to ensure every trip gets off to the best possible start.
Passionate about providing exceptional customer service, Nino is dedicated to helping guests feel welcomed, informed and excited about their upcoming fishing adventure. Whether you're booking your first fishing charter, organising a group trip with mates, or chasing a bucket-list Southern Bluefin Tuna, Nino is always happy to answer questions and help find the best option for your day on the water.
