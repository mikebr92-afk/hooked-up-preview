Take On One of Victoria's Hardest Fighting Sport Fish
Yellowtail Kingfish are undoubtedly one of the most exciting and challenging fish found in Victorian waters. Renowned for their explosive strikes, powerful runs and incredible fighting ability, kingfish provide an unforgettable experience for anglers looking for the ultimate fishing challenge.
Kingfish generally arrive in Victoria from January through to April, bringing with them some of the most exciting sport fishing available in our waters. When conditions line up, anglers can experience explosive surface action, powerful runs and unforgettable battles with these incredible fish.
Why Fish for Kingfish?
Kingfish are built for speed and power. Their aggressive nature and incredible strength make every hookup an unforgettable experience, with many anglers describing kingfish as one of the hardest fighting fish they've ever encountered.
When conditions are right, kingfish can provide spectacular action, smashing live baits and lures before charging for structure in an attempt to break free.

How to Target Kingfish
At Unreel Fishing Charters, we use a variety of proven techniques to target kingfish, including jigging, casting stick baits, live bait fishing and slow trolling squid.
There is no doubt that few fish pull harder than a kingfish. Their strength, speed and determination are second to none, and once hooked they will test both angler and tackle from start to finish. Every hookup is an adrenaline-filled battle you'll never forget.
Trip Duration
Our Kingfish Charters are full-day adventures, with trips running for up to 10 hours. The extended time on the water allows us to travel to productive fishing grounds, locate active fish and maximise your chances of experiencing the excitement of targeting Yellowtail Kingfish.
Charter Locations
We operate kingfish charters from a range of Victorian locations, including:
• Stony Point
• Sorrento
• San Remo
• Port Welshpool
• Port Fairy
• Portland
Our departure location will depend on where the fish are holding and the time of season, ensuring we can provide the best possible opportunity for success.
