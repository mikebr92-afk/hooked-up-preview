As a beginner to barramundi fishing our charter with Carpentaria Barra Fishing was my 4th experience over
the past 10years and first time in Karumba. Having only caught a few smaller barramundi in the past, I really
needed Gavin’s expertise when I unexpectedly hooked a big one. Gavin knew it was a good size and yet
calmly provided advice to me over the hour it took to bring this 143 cm barra in. Without his guidance and
boat skills this story would have been about the “one that got away” ! I would recommend Carpentaria Barra
fishing to anyone, not just for my fish but for a really enjoyable day out as Gavin goes above and beyond
your expectations as an excellent fishing charter operator. A truly memorable “1.43 metre day” and
experience.
Christine K
