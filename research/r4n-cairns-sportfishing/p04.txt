What type of fish do we encounter on our trips?
There's so many fish species in the world famous, fully protected waters of Queensland’s Great Barrier Reef
Trip dependent - we expect to encounter: 
Estuary
• Barramundi
• Golden Snapper
• Salmon
• Mangrove Jack
• Grunter
• Queenfish
• Bream
• Moses Perch
• Mudcrabs
• Estuary Cod
Outer and inner reef
• Large Mouth Nannygai
• Red Emperor
• Gold Spot Cod
• Giant Trevally
• Coral Trout
• Sweetlip
• Long Nose Emperor
• Spangled Emperor
• Fingermark
• Spanish Mackerel
• Queenfish
• Golden Trevally
• Cobia / Black Kingfish
• Various Reef Species
Heavy/light tackle Marlin & Pelagic
• Black/Blue Marlin
• Sail Fish
• Wahoo
• Yellow Fin Tuna
Catch and release policy
Being environmentally conscious we encourage catch and release on coastal reefs and estuaries.
We ask you to please remember: All trips are subject to weather, and we reserve the right to transfer to coastal reef/estuary alternatives.
Your financial reef charter investments (prices) are subject to change and trips can be cancelled with a full refund provided if the minimum numbers aren’t reached.
