Frequently Asked Questions
Since 2004 our fully experienced team are North Queensland’s premier fishing and island tour professionals and encourage you to take a look at these frequently asked questions
Can I come alone?
Absolutely – please check our availability in regard to shared charters. This allows you to get the very best value while still being able to enjoy some of the most incredible fishing found anywhere in the world.
Can I book the whole boat?
You certainly can book the entire boat for your family, friends, corporate group, or even if you just fancy the whole thing to yourself. Please get in contact with us if you have specific requirements.
Are there preferred locations?
Each trip is unique. Weather and water conditions vary on a day-to-day basis, which means that we calculate each route individually and customise each trip to provide the very best experience for our passengers.
How do I book a charter?
For your perfect fishing trip, remember to book with us by phoning Gavin. 
Where do you depart from?
We depart from the local Boat Ramp. It’s easy to find, but if you’re having trouble just let us know and we’ll be able to direct you.
What time do you depart?
½ Day Charters hours are 7am-11.00 am and then 12:00-400 pm.
Full day Private Offshore Sport-Fishing takes place on the outer reef and, generally speaking, unless under specific conditions, we will aim for a 7 a.m. departure to get a full day’s fishing in.
What about insects?
Usually, given the locations we choose and the conditions, insects are generally not a problem. If, however, on the off-chance they become an issue for you, we are well stocked with insect repellent, which we are more than happy to provide to keep you comfortable.
How do I pay?
We can take full payment via phone/email for all charters. Please note that all coastal/offshore trips are subject to weather conditions and passenger/employee safety absolutely must come first at all times.
Are night charters available?
They certainly are – night fishing is fantastic fun! We cater our service to you, so if you have any particular requirements, please contact us and we’ll be glad to discuss arranging something to suit your needs.
What should I bring?
We would say that necessities include a well-covering hat, sunglasses, and some appropriate strength sunscreen. Whatever else you decide you want to bring is up to you! We will provide ice, cold water, and soft drinks throughout the day. If you have any special drinks and nibbles you want to enjoy, please bring them along to make the most of your day.
Do I need fishing experience?
Not at all! All you need is the desire to learn. Even if you’ve never picked up a rod before, our friendly team will have you casting like a professional in absolutely no time at all.
Can I cancel?
There is a strict cancellation policy for private and shared charters.
If you have any other questions, please contact us!
