Fishing Charters 
Our charters operate mainly in South East Tasmania , from Triabunna , Dunalley and Eaglehawk Neck, other locations by request . We cater for up to 5 people and all equipment is supplied. We supply all quality Penn, Fin-Nor & Alvey Reels and all tackle is supplied (you are welcome to bring your own gear if you wish). 
You will need to bring your own food and drink , sunscreen, hat, warm clothes, camera and a sense of adventure. 
We also cater for families & children. 
Charter Costs 
Bottom & Reef Fishing (all year round) 
Location: Departing Orford or Dunalley fishing around Maria Island 
Times: 
• Half Day - depart 7.00–7.30 am, return 12.00–1.00 (5 hours) 
• Full Day - depart 7.00-7.30am, return 2.30–3.30 (8 hours) 
Cost: 
• Half Day $600* 
• Full Day $900* (GST Included) 
Numbers: Maximum of 5 people 
Species captured: Flathead, perch, gurnet, squid, striped trumpeter, salmon, gummy sharks, kingfish, mackeral & couta. 
Game Fishing (March - August) 
Location: Departing Eaglehawk Neck or Port Arthur 
Times: Full Day - depart 7.00-7.30am, return 2.30–3.30 (8 hours) 
Cost: Full Day $900* (GST Included). Half days not available. 
Numbers: Maximum of 5 people 
Species captured: Albacore, Striped Tuna, Yellowfin & Bluefin. If the tuna are not around we can bottom fish for flathead, perch, striped trumpeter, gurnet, couta & squid. 
* All Prices are a flat rate for the boat so if price quoted is $900 and you have 4 people in your group it is $225 each. If you can't get a group together we can try and organize a group for you.
