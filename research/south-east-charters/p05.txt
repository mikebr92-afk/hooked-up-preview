The Boat 
PHANTOM 
“PHANTOM” is a locally built (by Mariner Aluminium ,Tasmania) 26 foot Aluminium boat powered by twin 115 HP fourstoke Yamaha outboards. 
It has a Garmin GPS/Sounder combo and GME VHF and AM/FM radios. Toilet and padded seating in the cabin as well as in the deck area. 
It is licenced by AMSA (Australian Maritime Safety Authority) to carry 5 passengers and has a current safety management certificate.
