Night Crossing Fishing Adventures operates from GLADSTONE, QLD, 4680 
"Friday to Friday" fishing charters ex Gladstone to Swain reefs, Saumarez Reef, Shoal Water Bay area & remote Island Head Creek. 10 day minimum fishing trips to the Coral Sea including Marion, Frederick, Kenn and Wreck reefs. The “MV Night Crossing” accommodates between 16 and 18 anglers.
