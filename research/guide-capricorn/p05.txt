GLADSTONE, QLD, 4680

The ‘MIKAT’ is a combination of ‘catamaran’ hull design and engine power offer a much shorter time to the off-shore destinations which can be 120 –150 nautical miles from port of Gladstone.
Inclusions:
* Accommodation includes linen & bath towel
* All meals
* Tea, Coffee, Milo, Cordials and Snacks
* Starter bait provided & fresh bait prepared. 
Bring your own:
* Personal Fishing Equipment
* Swimming /Snorkelling gear
* Travel Insurance
* Catch cleaned, packaged & frozen
* Assistance with rigs etc.
* Sunscreen.
