Red Tag Trout Tours is a Tasmanian specialist river and stream fly fishing guiding service also providing introductory Fly Fishing Workshops. Ray Curran (owner-operator) has over 25 years of experience of wild trout fly fishing around Tasmania, with specific emphasis on rivers and streams of the Midlands. He caters for all levels of fly fishers from the absolute beginner to the widely experienced angler. 
Tours range from full day, to the client's requested duration and include where necessary, all gear, accommodation, all meals and refreshments, transport and fishing licenses, plus a complimentary (KOCPIT) fly fishing manual with all introductory workshops.
In addition, Red Tag Trout Tours can also advise, quote and supply fly fishing outfits and accessories.
Red Tag Trout Tours is based in Hobart so we can collect clients from the greater Hobart area and or the Hobart airport or by arrangement. All your guiding needs can and will be catered for.

Right on the edge of the beautiful Derwent River, all that’s between this incredible home and the Hobart CBD is water! Set in a quiet, affluent suburb this spacious, comfortable, modern home has outstanding river views. Down stairs are two double bedrooms both with Queen size beds sharing a large bathroom.

House on the Hillside Howrah
Hobart
$650 - $1200
Modern open-plan holiday home set over two levels with a separate private self-contained studio/granny flat. A home where you can just sit back and relax and take in the amazing views of the mighty Derwent River, across to Hobart City and up to Mount Wellington. 
Situated in a quiet, affluent Hobart suburb of Howrah this spacious, comfortable, modern home has room for all the family (6 bedrooms) including a separate studio for additional guests. A convenient 15-minute drive to the CBD and Hobart Airport.
Stylish, spacious home truly promotes a feeling of peace and invites you to simply relax into the super comfortable space.
The home sleeps up to 12 guests, with six bedrooms. The master bedroom boasts a king-sized bed, en-suite with, shower, toilet and dual vanities, as well as a walk-in wardrobe. There are three spacious bedrooms with Queen size beds and another with a King single bed with a pull-out trundle ideal for the kids. All bedrooms are well appointed, with built in robes using the highest quality and professionally laundered linen, all bedrooms benefit with an additional bathroom with large vanity, separate shower and spa bath.
Key features ducted heating/cooling throughout, and a dedicated study space with a desk and ergonomic chair. There is everything you could ever want or dream for. Close to local parks, shops, beach and transport.

The Shoreline Hotel Accomodation
Hobart
$155 - $180
Hobart Shoreline Hotel is 5 minutes' walk from the Howrah Recreation Centre and Sunshine Tennis Club. It is 15 minutes' drive from Hobart CBD (Central Business District). There are shops and restaurants within 5 minutes' walk. 
The Shoreline Bistro features a range of dishes such as pizza, steak and chicken schnitzel. It is open for lunch and dinner daily. The Cafe Bar serves breakfast each morning and offers snacks and a variety of wine.
All rooms feature a flat-screen TV, a desk, a refrigerator and tea/coffee making facilities. Some rooms offer a sofa bed. Each room has an en suite bathroom with a shower, hairdryer and free toiletries.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Tasmanian Aboriginal people and their enduring custodianship of Lutruwita / Tasmania.
We honour the uninterrupted care, protection and belonging to these islands, skies and waterways, before the invasion and colonisation of European settlement.
As part of a tourism industry that welcomes visitors to these lands, we acknowledge our responsibility to represent to our visitors Tasmania's deep and complex history, fully, respectfully and truthfully.
We acknowledge the Aboriginal people who continue to care for this country today.
We pay our respects to their Elders, past and present.
We honour their stories, songs, art, and culture, and their aspirations for the future of their people and these lands.
