FISHING CHARTER FAQ 
Everything you need to know before you step on board. Can't find an answer? Give Mal a call.
Captain Mal
40+ years on Port Phillip Bay & Bass Strait
Still have questions? Mal's happy to chat through the details before you book.

Do I need a Fishing Licence? 
Yes, unless exempt, the Victorian Government requires all anglers, including clients on fishing charter vessels, to be in possession of a current Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence . Under 18 or over 70 years old, pensioners, and seniors cardholders don't need a fishing licence.
How much does it cost to book a Fishing Charter? 
Prices vary according to your destination. Bay charters (Snapper and Mixed Bag) are $200 per person. Offshore Tuna charters start from $300 per person. All charters include rods, reels, tackle, bait, and full guidance from Mal. You just need to bring your fishing licence, food, drinks, and sunscreen. See our charters for full prices.
Do I need to organise a whole group to go fishing? 
No, we understand it's not always possible to organise a whole group. Find a suitable date and make the booking, and we will try and fill the remaining spots for you through our booking system. If you'd prefer a private experience, you can also book the whole boat exclusively for your group.
What is the maximum number of people allowed on board a charter? 
We can host up to 8 passengers, for offshore fishing trips or around the bays. This keeps the experience personal, uncrowded, and gives everyone plenty of rod space.
Do I need to pay a deposit to book a Fishing charter? 
No deposit: full payment is taken online when you book. Pick your charter, date and departure, enter your details, and pay securely through Stripe on the last step. Your spot is held as soon as the payment goes through, and Mal confirms the booking by email within 24 hours.
• Snapper and Mixed Bag charters: $200 per person on a shared charter, or $1,550 to book the whole boat for 8.
• Offshore charters: $1,800 for 6 people, or $2,400 for 8.
There is nothing more to pay on the day. Visa and MasterCard are accepted, and gift vouchers can be applied at checkout.
Are kids welcome? 
Yes, when supervised by an adult. We suggest children from 6 years of age, as they are able to cast and reel in a catch. Bay charters (Snapper and Mixed Bag) are the best fit for kids. For safety reasons, a minimum age of 12 years applies on offshore Tuna charters due to the longer duration and open ocean conditions. All junior anglers must have an appropriate fishing licence unless exempt.
Where do you launch from? 
• Carrum (Launching Way)
• Hastings (Hastings Boat Ramp)
• Mornington (Schnapper Point)
• Rye (Rye Boat Ramp)
Bay charters usually depart from Carrum or Hastings, depending on the charter and conditions on the day. Offshore Tuna charters leave from Hastings at 6:00 AM, all year round. Mal will confirm the exact departure point when your booking is confirmed. See where we depart for maps.
Do we take our catch home? 
Yes. We will provide bags for your catch, and please leave an esky in your car to keep your catch fresh on the way home.
What do I need to bring on the day? 
Warm clothes, a hat, sunscreen, a spray jacket, a camera, and leave an esky in your car to keep your fish cool on the way home. Bring your Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence , food and drinks for the trip, and non-slip footwear. All fishing gear is provided: rods, reels, tackle and bait are included.
Food? 
You are welcome to bring food onto the boat (no bananas). Please note there are no heating facilities to warm food.
Do you supply lifejackets, first aid, etc? 
Yes.
What if the weather is bad on the day I have booked? 
Safety is the top priority. If conditions are deemed unsafe, Mal will contact you as early as possible and you will be given the opportunity to reschedule your fishing charter. If no other day is suitable, a full refund can be organised. Offshore Tuna charters are particularly weather-dependent due to Bass Strait conditions.
Do you clean our catch? 
No.
Can I bring bananas on the boat? 
No.
Do you offer gift vouchers? 
Yes. Gift vouchers start at $50 and can be put towards any charter, so they suit birthdays, Father's Day, Christmas or any excuse to get someone on the water. Buy one on the gift card page , pay by card, and it is emailed straight to the recipient with a copy to you. Or call Mal on 0418 769 384. To use a voucher, enter the code at the checkout when booking online, or give it to Mal over the phone. Vouchers are valid for three years and any balance left over stays on the voucher.
How far in advance should I book? 
The peak Snapper season (October to December) books out fast, so we recommend booking as early as possible, ideally weeks or months in advance. Tuna charters are also popular and fill up quickly. Off-peak Mixed Bag charters generally have more availability but booking ahead is always recommended. Online bookings close 24 hours before departure, so you can't book today's charter or tomorrow morning's on the day. If you're after a last-minute spot, call Mal on 0418 769 384 and he may still be able to fit you in.
General Fishing Charter FAQ 
Frequently asked questions can vary depending on the location, type of fishing, and the specific charter company. However, here are some common fishing charter questions that you might encounter, with our answers.
1. What is a fishing charter? 
This question is often asked by people who are new to fishing charters. A fishing charter is a service provided by a captain and crew who take you out on their boat to fish in specific areas. It's a way for individuals or groups to enjoy a fishing experience without owning their own boat.
2. What types of fishing charters are available? 
Charters can vary in terms of the type of fishing they offer, such as deep-sea, freshwater, fly fishing, or inshore fishing. Customers often want to know what options are available. See our charters .
3. How much does a fishing charter cost? 
This is a common question, and prices can vary widely depending on location, duration, type of fishing, and the size of the group. It's important to ask about the cost upfront and whether it includes equipment, bait, and licences.
4. What is included in the charter fee? 
Customers often want to know if the charter fee covers everything they'll need, including fishing gear, bait, licences, and any additional amenities like snacks or drinks.
5. What should I bring on the charter? 
Charter operators may provide a list of items customers should bring, such as sunscreen, sunglasses, appropriate clothing, and any personal items they might need.
6. Do I need a fishing licence? 
Regulations regarding fishing licences can vary by location and the type of fishing you plan to do. The charter operator should be able to provide guidance on this. See the Victorian Fisheries Authority .
7. How long is the fishing trip? 
Customers often want to know the duration of the fishing trip, whether it's a half-day, full-day, or overnight excursion. Our trips range from 4 hours to 8 hours.
8. Can I keep the fish I catch? 
Charter operators usually have rules and regulations regarding catch limits and whether you can keep the fish you catch. It's essential to clarify this beforehand. On I'm Hooked Fishing Charters, you are able to keep the fish, limited to the bag limits set by the Victorian Fisheries Authority.
9. Is there a minimum or maximum group size? 
Some charters have restrictions on the number of people they can accommodate, so it's essential to inquire about group size limitations. I'm Hooked Fishing Charters take a maximum of 8 on Port Phillip and Western Port Bays. Bass Strait charters are based on 6 people.
10. What is the cancellation policy? 
Understanding the cancellation policy is crucial in case you need to change your plans due to unforeseen circumstances. Please check our policies .
11. Is the charter family-friendly? 
Families with children often want to ensure that the charter is suitable for all age groups and if any special accommodations are available. If you're fishing with children, we recommend our charters on the Bays.
12. What happens in case of bad weather? 
Customers should inquire about the charter's policy regarding bad weather, rescheduling, or refunds if the trip needs to be cancelled due to adverse conditions. Please check our policies .
13. Are there any restrictions on bringing alcohol or food? 
Some charters may have rules about bringing alcohol or outside food and beverages. It's essential to know the policy in advance. Please check our policies .
14. Can I bring my own fishing equipment? 
Some anglers prefer to use their own gear. Charter operators might have policies about bringing personal equipment. At I'm Hooked Fishing Charters we do allow anglers to bring their own fishing gear.
These are just a few common fishing charter questions, and the specific answers can vary depending on the location, regulations, and individual charter company policies. It's always a good idea to contact the charter operator directly for the most accurate and up-to-date information. Do you have a fishing charter question not covered by this page? Feel free to use the contact form and ask Mal directly.
