What's Included 
We provide almost everything you need for a successful day on the water. Just bring yourself and your fishing licence.
Bait & Tackle
Top quality gear and fresh bait provided for all target species.
Safety Gear
Life jackets and comprehensive safety equipment on board.
Expert Guidance
Mal provides coaching for beginners and tips for pros.
Bags for Your Catch
Legal-size fish are yours to keep. We bag them for the trip home; leave an esky in your car.
Comfortable Vessel
Weather protection and marine toilet available.
Local Knowledge
40+ years of experience finding the best spots.
What to Bring 
• 
Appropriate clothing for the conditions (layers are best) 
• 
An esky (cooler) to take your catch home 
• 
Your own food and drinks (strictly no glass on board) 
• 
A valid Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence

Gift Vouchers
A Day On The Water, From $200 
Gift vouchers start at $200 and can be put towards any charter. They make a great present for the angler in your life — birthdays, Father’s Day, Christmas or just because. Vouchers never go to waste: the recipient picks their own date.
Order one online or call Mal, and he’ll email it straight to the lucky angler. To use a voucher, enter the code at the checkout when you book online, or read it to Mal over the phone. Vouchers are valid for three years and any balance left over stays on the voucher for next time.
