Well worth the experience
I am not usually inclined to give reviews unless it has exceeded my expectations, however, this occasion has done exactly that. Firstly, Captain Gavin was extremely forth giving in every circumstance. He is, in my opinion, very knowledgeable with everything to do about fishing, location, types of bait/lures, methods and what he does not know most likely would not be worth knowing about anyway. I brought my wife with me on the trip (and even though he would not let me throw her overboard) she had a great time as well. I must thank Gavin for the time he persisted with her and showed her basics of fishing- Better man than me! Captain Gavin never let up on trying to find us places which would hold fish and I fully understand the different variables involved with such a past time. We caught a couple of fish and saw some wild crocodiles but all in all day was fantastic. If you get a chance book this service as we will again (minus the Wife).
Thank you Captain Gavin you made my day - Tim and Rakta
