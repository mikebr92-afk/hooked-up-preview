Cairns sportfishing charters
Experience one of our exciting Cairns Great Barrier Reef fishing charters and/or reef experiences
No visit to Cairns’ Great Barrier Reef would be complete without embracing one of our exciting fishing and/or reef experiences departing from: Tingira St. Cairns (plenty of parking)
½ Day Private Estuary @ 7.00 am depart
4Hrs: $880
1 to 4 Passengers (additional $220 for 5th person). Includes tackle/bait & soft drinks. Perfect for family, friends, or corporate needs.
½ Day Private Estuary @ 12.00 pm depart
4Hrs: $880
1 to 4 Passengers (additional $220 for 5th person). Includes tackle/bait & soft drinks. Perfect for family, friends, or corporate needs.
¾ Day Private Estuary @ 7.00 am depart
6Hrs: $1320
1 to 4 Passengers (additional $330 for 5th person). Includes tackle/bait & soft drinks. Perfect for family, friends, or corporate needs.
Full Day Private Reef Adventure @ 7.00 am depart
8Hrs: $1900 (weather permitting)
Maximum 4 Passengers only. Includes tackle/bait & soft drinks (bring your own lunch). Perfect for families wanting a more relaxed experience.
Full Day Private Top Water Sport-Fishing @ 7.00 am depart
8Hrs (dock to dock) $1900 (weather permitting)
Maximum 2 Passengers. Includes tackle/bait & soft drinks (bring your lunch). NOTE: terminal tackle losses charged extra per loss.

8Hrs: (dock to dock) $3200 (weather permitting)

NOTE: Our 10mt. Game Boat is an amazing experience
