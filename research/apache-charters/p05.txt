“Big Boat – Big Fish – Big Trip” 
Come aboard this bucket list fishing trip, fishing remote locations west of Dirk Hartog Island and the Zuytdorp Cliffs in the fish abundant waters of the Indian Ocean, anchoring each night in the protected waters of Turtle Bay, Cape Inscription or Shelter Bay and Steep Point. 
With 25 years experience fishing Shark Bay, only Apache has the capability and knowledge to get you to the remote fishing spots in the Dirk Hartog waters where very few fisherman can go! Because the fishing industry in which we operate is highly regulated by Fisheries and is sustainable we are still able to offer our fishing clients up to 20kg of demersal fish on our live aboard extended fishing tours in the Gascoyne and Pilbara regions of Western Australia.

Trip Itinerary 
• 
Sunday 3pm 
The Lady Grace is available for boarding at 3pm at the Denham Jetty, Knight Terrace Denham. Once all guests are boarded and gear is loaded, the Lady Grace will cruise west for 3 hours from Denham to South Passage for a safe anchorage where the on-board chef will serve tonight’s dinner. Just anchoring in protected waters between Steep Point and Dirk Hartog Island gives the opportunity for an early start tomorrow for the best fishing grounds.
• 
Day 1 - Monday 
After a nice hot cooked breakfast, it’s time to pull up the anchor and depart nice and early for the best fishing grounds. Fishing from many remote locations this week, Steep Point, Dirk Hartog Island, Turtle Bay, Dampier Reef, West of False Entrance and Crayfish and Thunder bays. We head into remote waters where it’s unlikely you will see other boats. Anchorage each night is in protected waters of Turtle Bay or South Passage. While you are busy fishing, are crew are on standby to assist in any way they can, meanwhile the cook is busy cooking up a feast for when you get hungry. The drinks and are cold, the showers are hot, and you don’t have to worry about a thing, just fish, fish fish.
• 
Day 2 - Tuesday 
After a big, cooked breakfast, its straight into fishing, bottom bouncing for the massive Pink Snapper, Red Emporer, Coral Trout, Rankin Cod and heaps more species –– it’s easy to catch over 16 different species in one day in the waters of Shark Bay.
• 
Day 3 - Wednesday 
Breakfast, fishing, morning tea, fishing, lunch, fishing, beers, fishing, fishing, dinner, fishing, beers. Snappers, Red Emporer, Coral Trout, Rankin Cod, Coronation Cod, Spanish Mackerel, Chinamon Cod, Tomato Cod, Spangled Emporer, Sweetlips, Cobia, Tuna, The Fishing Today Is Epic.
• 
Day 4 - Thursday 
Pretty much just like day one, day two and day three, it’s all about FISHING!
• 
Day 5 - Friday 
The last day it’s all about FISHING FISHING FISHING. Then it’s back to Denham in the late arvo. Head over to the pub for a few bevies. Dinner is not supplied on last night. Stay on the boat tonight then depart by 8am Saturday morning for your trip home.
• 
Day 6 - Saturday 
Your fish have been packed into your esky, now its time for the short journey home. Disembarking is at 8am.

Why choose Apache Charters?
As the same owner/operator for 25 years, we are busy, very experienced and our vessel spends more time out fishing up North than it does in port (we leave Fremantle in March and don’t return till the end of November). What does this mean for you? With more spots and thousands of marks on the plotter which is also constantly being added to, you have more options and ultimately… more fish! Our boat, the Lady Grace is also a serious blue water fishing vessel, expertly set up to fish, and to fish hard but still fitted with plenty of comfort for when you’re not fishing.

The Vessel 
Lady Grace - 70ft fully equipped live aboard vessel 
The Lady Grace has been specially fitted out for the best fishing and live aboard experience. The vessel is used 9 months of the year for 6 night live aboard charters and 3 months for our corporate River Cruises and Boat Charters.
Before every season, the Lady Grace is completely fitted out with the necessary changes to ensure the vessel is fully equipped with specialised seasonal fishing equipment and gets transformed into Live Aboard mode. The vessel is licensed for 70 persons on our river trips, so to only have 12 passengers on our north west fishing trips means there is plenty of room for everyone to enjoy a week of bucket list fishing.
