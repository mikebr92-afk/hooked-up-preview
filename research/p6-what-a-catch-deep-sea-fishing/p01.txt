THE VESSEL
About the Prospector
Our purpose built vessel, Prospector , is fully surveyed to carry up to 16 passengers, plus crew, for offshore fishing charters, private parties, or scenic tours. Designed for comfort and safety, Prospector features a fully enclosed toilet and a fresh water wash hand basin, ensuring a pleasant experience for all guests on board. Whether you're seeking a thrilling fishing adventure or a relaxing tour, Prospector provides the perfect setting for an unforgettable Mooloolaba fishing charter.
