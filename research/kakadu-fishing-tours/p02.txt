About Kakadu Fishing Tours 
At Kakadu Fishing Tours, we live and breathe the Top End. As a family-owned and operated business, we offer personal, small-group fishing experiences in one of Australia’s most spectacular and culturally significant regions. Based in the heart of Kakadu National Park, our tours are designed not just to catch fish—but to create stories that last a lifetime.
With years of local knowledge and passion for the water, our professional guides take you to the best hotspots for Barramundi and other iconic species, navigating the tides, seasons and weather to ensure the best chance of success. We operate in pristine, remote locations where nature is untouched, wildlife is abundant and every cast could be your catch of a lifetime.
Whether you're an experienced angler chasing a trophy Barra or a first-time visitor looking to experience true Territory fishing, we’ve got you covered.
Why fish with us? 
✔ Local expert guides 
✔ Small group charters – max 4 guests 
✔ Quality gear and top-of-the-line boat 
✔ Authentic NT experience 
✔ Family-owned, personal service 
✔ Operating in world-famous Kakadu National Park
Come join us on the water, disconnect from the busy world, and experience fishing the way it should be—wild, real and unforgettable.
Book your adventure and let Kakadu Fishing Tours take you where the fish are.
Looking forward to hearing from you!
Mitch & Anni
My wife Jodi and I have owned and operated Kakadu Fishing Tours since 2004. We moved to the N.T from Adelaide with our 2 children Maddie and Ryan in 2003 to pursue a more relaxed and enjoyable lifestyle. The opportunity to purchase this well established business in Kakadu felt like it was ‘meant to be’.
I grew up in Darwin and moved to Adelaide to finish school in my teens. I regularly visited family living in Jabiru from 1991 onwards and this is where I developed my obsession for Barra fishing. As a keen fisherman in South Australian waters I couldn’t believe the contrast when fishing in Kakadu. The adrenalin fuelled fishing this area has to offer still excites me to this day. The ability for me to share this with people from all over the world is without doubt ‘my perfect job’.
Shannon Summerton
Kakadu Fishing Tours
RUN OFF FISHING CHARTERS ( March-May )
Run-Off Barra Fishing – Limited Season, Peak Action 
The run-off season is when Kakadu fishing truly comes alive. As floodwaters recede, Barramundi gather in the drains, creeks and floodplains, creating prime conditions for big strikes and high catch rates. This short window offers some of the best Barra fishing of the year, with trophy-size fish commonly landed.
Our experienced guides know exactly where the fish are feeding during this time, giving you the best chance of landing that unforgettable catch.
Run-off charters are extremely limited due to demand and seasonal timing. 
Bookings are now open – secure your spot early & send us an enquiry to learn more. Don’t miss out on the peak of the fishing season!

DRY SEASON CHARTERS ( june- october )
Discover Kakadu’s dry season fishing at its best with our guided Barramundi tours. Located just upstream of the renowned Cahill Crossing, this section of the East Alligator River offers the rare opportunity to target both freshwater and saltwater Barra—where our record catch measured an impressive 135 cm. Set beside the striking Arnhem Land Escarpment, the river delivers not only great fishing but unforgettable scenery. Expect to share the water with Kakadu’s resident Saltwater Crocodiles and be surrounded by remarkable birdlife as you cast.
Whether you’re chasing your personal best Barra or simply soaking in the raw beauty of the Top End, our tours deliver more than just a day on the water—they provide a true Kakadu experience.
Join us, escape the everyday, connect with nature, and fish the Territory the way it’s meant to be — wild, real, and unforgettable.
