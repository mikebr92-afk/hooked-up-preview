Run Off Fishing Charters
RUN OFF FISHING TOUR 
RUN OFF EXTENDED CHARTERS 
$2380 per day for up to 4 people.
TOUR DATES 
We offer full day ‘run off’ tours from March to Early May Fishing the large Kakadu rivers.
Tour Details 
Run-Off Barra Fishing – Limited Season, Peak Action 
The run-off season is when Kakadu fishing truly comes alive. As floodwaters recede, Barramundi gather in the drains, creeks and floodplains, creating prime conditions for big strikes and high catch rates. This short window offers some of the best Barra fishing of the year, with trophy-size fish commonly landed.
Our experienced guides know exactly where the fish are feeding during this time, 
giving you the best chance of landing that unforgettable catch.
Run-off charters are extremely limited due to demand and seasonal timing. 
Bookings are now open – secure your spot early & send us an enquiry to learn more. Don’t miss out on the peak of the fishing season!
Tour Details 
Kakadu has some of the best ‘run off’ fishing available in the Top End and we are one of only a few guided fishing companies able to access this. We are also the only company based full time in Kakadu and who fish Kakadu exclusively. We can cater for groups of up to 4, fishing from 1 to 7 days. We can customize your experience to suit and even organise accommodation within Jabiru (Kakadu). Jabiru is a short 2.5hr drive from Darwin taking you away from the crowds fishing out from Darwin. You can also ask about fishing from a ‘live aboard’ Mother Ship experience for almost exclusive access to the East Alligator River during the ‘run off’.
