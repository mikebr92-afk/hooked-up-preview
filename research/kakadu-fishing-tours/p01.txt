WELCOME TO KAKADU FISHING TOURS 
Kakadu Fishing Tours offers you an exhilarating day Barramundi fishing on some of Kakadu’s breathtaking waterways. We offer half day, full day and extended charter options.
From the floodplains of the mighty South Alligator to the sandstone gorge country of the East Alligator and any number of secluded billabongs in between. You will experience some of the best Barramundi fishing the Top End has to offer.
With rod in hand absorb the sights and sounds of this ancient landscape, abundant with birdlife and Kakadu’s famous Saltwater Crocodiles. All from the safety of your custom built 2011 6m barra boat. Kakadu Fishing Tours is one of a select few operators who meet the stringent criteria required to fish in Kakadu. We are also the only private operator who lives and fishes in Kakadu exclusively.
A professional guide will assist both beginner and experienced angler. Tours depart ex Jabiru in Kakadu.
RUN OFF FISHING CHARTERS ( March-May )
Run-Off Barra Fishing – Limited Season, Peak Action 
The run-off season is when Kakadu fishing truly comes alive. As floodwaters recede, Barramundi gather in the drains, creeks and floodplains, creating prime conditions for big strikes and high catch rates. This short window offers some of the best Barra fishing of the year, with trophy-size fish commonly landed.
Our experienced guides know exactly where the fish are feeding during this time, giving you the best chance of landing that unforgettable catch.
Run-off charters are extremely limited due to demand and seasonal timing. 
Bookings are now open – secure your spot early & send us an enquiry to learn more. Don’t miss out on the peak of the fishing season!

DRY SEASON CHARTERS ( june- october )
Discover Kakadu’s dry season fishing at its best with our guided Barramundi tours. Located just upstream of the renowned Cahill Crossing, this section of the East Alligator River offers the rare opportunity to target both freshwater and saltwater Barra—where our record catch measured an impressive 135 cm. Set beside the striking Arnhem Land Escarpment, the river delivers not only great fishing but unforgettable scenery. Expect to share the water with Kakadu’s resident Saltwater Crocodiles and be surrounded by remarkable birdlife as you cast.
Whether you’re chasing your personal best Barra or simply soaking in the raw beauty of the Top End, our tours deliver more than just a day on the water—they provide a true Kakadu experience.
Join us, escape the everyday, connect with nature, and fish the Territory the way it’s meant to be — wild, real, and unforgettable.
