About Us
Who is Audacity?
In the simplest of terms Audacity is a family business.
Established in 2019 (about a month before the pandemic) by two unlikely partners. 
Adam Finlay, an experienced and expert fisherman with his own Barra guiding business and a passion to grow into the reef sports fishing arena.
Matt Purcell, a Brisbane doctor with a lifelong passion for all things related to the water and no idea about the requirements of a fishing charter business.
The idea for Audacity was born at Camp Island where Adam and Matt found themselves enjoying a fine G&T on the verandah while watching schools of baitfish being hunted by everything from GT’s to Spanish Mackerel. That verandah is clearly a superb place for creative thinking as, by the end of the weekend, the boys had the roadmap for a business and the requirements for our flagship vessel.
Adam and Matt were joined in planning by their wives Siew and Leah. The business took shape and was launched with a 37’ Kevlacat (Audacity) , Adam as chief skipper ably assisted by Les and Jordan.
"That’s Audacity, a business yes - but mostly its a family with a passion for the water and life outside."
In the last 4 years the Audacity fleet has grown.
We now have our Flagship – Vanguard and Pilgrim – our inshore and estuary fishing vessel. 
We operate our day charter operation year-round out of Breakwater Marina in Townsville.
We know fishing and we’re the most prolific day charter in business regularly bagging out on Reds from our well managed grounds.
Over the last 2 years we’ve been growing the Audacity experience options.
We now also run multiday trips from either Lucinda or Camp Island Lodge. These trips are the best of everything we have to offer, great company, great food and lifelong memories.
The Audacity family has grown too
…and some of the faces have changed.
We still have Adam and Siew teamed with Leah and Matt. 
We now also have our brilliant skipper Bryce and his partner Ashley.
In addition we have a rotating roster of Audacity Team members running our deck…
Ahoy captains
Meet the crew
Audacity Charters Townsville and Central Queensland home to a professional and enthusiastic crew who aim to provide the best Townsville and Rockhampton fishing trips every time you come aboard.
We have built up an honest reputation as being a dependable and hospitable crew, and we are genuinely passionate about what we do.
Adam Finlay
Primary Skipper & Co-Founder of Audacity Charters,
Townsville & Central Queensland
Read Bio 
Adam’s earliest memories of fishing are from Murray Island in the Torres Strait, where as a baby, he went reef flat fishing with his father. His parents and adopted Island family were fishing fanatics, so it was inevitable that he was going to grow up to be a fisherman. 
When Adam’s family moved to Townsville after he turned six, he quickly learned the local fishing systems, and has been catching fish in the area since.
As a teenager, Adam’s name became well known in the Townsville and Proserpine area for his knowledge of fish species, tides, and his uncanny ability to catch fish. Despite this, after leaving school, his professional career didn’t start in the recreational fishing industry, but at the Townsville Casino. Despite working crazy hours, Adam (where he) continued to spend countless hours, days, and weeks honing his fishing skills in the local area. (He) Adam (quickly) made his move into the fishing industry as a deckhand and subcontractor, and shortly after, decided to carve out his own path by creating his first company – AFS Sportsfishing. Adam’s initial focus was to help his punters catch their dream fish—Barramundi a metre or longer. In the following years he fine tuned his skill with catching other inshore species, such as Fingermark and Spanish Mackerel.
While chartering at Camp Island in 2018, Adam met Matt Purcell. There was an instant appreciation and insight into what could be achieved if they worked together, and Audacity Charters was founded the following year.
Adam now combines his years of knowledge as a deckhand on large reef charters, and his own knowledge of local fishing to deliver a personalised charter. Adam tries to create a feeling of fishing with family and friends—a culture that has made Audacity Charters popular today.

Deckhand

Hey I’m Deacon — aka Deacs, Ranga, or Big Deac — deckhand aboard Vanguard I might be one of the younger crew, but don’t let that fool you. I’ve been fishing since I could walk. It runs strong in my family — you could say I’ve got saltwater in my veins. Working on Vanguard brings together two of my favourite things: chasing quality fish and spinning a good yarn. Whether it’s rigging up, gaffing the big ones, or sharing tips to help you land your best catch, I’m here to make sure every trip is one to remember. I look forward to making sure your next story isn’t about the one that got away. 
Matt Purcell
Co-founder and Business Management of Audacity Charters
Read Bio 
Matt is the Audacity Brisbane connection, though his connection to NQ goes back to his childhood with regular family road trips including an epic Christmas holidays to his uncle’s place in Tully.
Matt didn’t start out being interested in fishing but has had a lifelong association with the water and an enduring love of anything that floats. Initially attracted to Scuba diving, Matt became a Dive Master for a dive shop in Brisbane and spent as much time on the waters of Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast and Gold Coast as possible.
In 2018, as one of the owners for Camp Island Lodge, Matt asked Adam to visit the island to do some guiding for guests. That weekend saw the beginning Adam and Matt’s friendship. It also saw quite a few local oysters, the creation of “oyster’s Camp Island “ and a lot of Gin and Tonic.
Since then, from 2018 to now Matt, his wife Leah and daughters Emma and Sarah have enjoyed the rollercoaster ride of small business, charters and vessel ownership.
“I’m learning all the time and looking forward to whatever the next chapter has in store!”
Siew-Ling Perkins
Administration
Audacity Charters Townsville
Read Bio 
Siew-Ling has always supported Adam’s dream of running his own fishing charter. As she get sea sick on the open water, she knew she wouldn’t be able to help him directly on charters so has been quietly helping out “behind the scenes” in-between her work as a Veterinary Nurse.
As Audacity continues to grow, Siew-Ling is taking on more administration for the business and will most likely be the person you will be speaking to on the other end of the phone or replying to your emails. 
Siew-ling also has photography and videography as her hobby. So when she isn’t photographing pets (a little side passion), she is going to be working on getting some great footage to help promote everything Audacity so be prepared to show your Audacity smile on charter!
