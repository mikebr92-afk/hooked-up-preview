Explore the beauty of the of the North Queensland Coast Line and Great Barrier Reef.
Our personalised Escapes are made by you.
Audacity Townsville, the home of our enthusiastic and professional crew from Audacity Charters, invite you to create an Escape with us to explore our home region. Your Escape could incorporate the spectacular Great Barrier Reef, the beautiful Hinchinbrook Hinterlands, you can book your own island at tip of the Whitsundays with Camp Island or you can spend your days estuary fishing through the beautiful Yeppoon and Rockhampton regions.
We have built our reputation as being a honest and reliable fishing charter company with crew that are friendly and engaging. We want to take the opportunity to show you why we love our region and why it is the must see and must do destination along the East Coast of Australia.
Contact us now to start planning your Escape away! 
We can’t wait to meet you!

Townsville Fishing Charters
Audacity Townsville has built the reputation of having the best crew and a fast and capable 55ft Austral to get you to the Great Barrier Reef.
Vanguard, our flagship, will have you comfortably fishing nonstop for coral trout, red emperor and nannygui. We may even do a troll for mackerel if the timing is right. Our crew will engage you in friendly banter throughout the trip to make you feel like you’re fishing with friends.
All your fish will be tagged, bled and stored in our ice slurry eskies to ensure the best eating quality fillets for the table when you get home.
If you are looking to immerse yourself in an fantastic seagoing fishing adventure, then our crew are ready to provide the best fishing event you will have ever experienced!
