About Estuary Escapes Darwin
BriaN wishES to welcome you to MY owner operated, personal fishing Escapes business. come with ME on your next Fishing Escape in the Northern Territory.
Estuary Escapes offers both half and full day fishing Escapes in the Darwin region. We offer day Escapes and extended fishing Escapes throughout the year, targeting Barramundi and other tropical estuary species.
Estuary Escapes, a fun day on the water for anglers of all levels from novice to experienced competition angler. We show inexperienced anglers how to fish with a variety of tackle and techniques.
Estuary Escapes holds a Fishing Tour Operators Licence for the Northern Territory. Your fishing guide, Brian, is an accredited fishing guide.
We chase both freshwater and saltwater species and use all types of tackle and methods to suit the anglers experience and preferred species.
I offer Barramundi Run off Escapes from late February to May. With other Escapes available over the rest of the year until early December, depending on the wet season.
I had a boat built specifically to target barramundi and other estuary sports fish.
MV Reel Escape is a Sea Adex Typhoon 6. It has been built to 2D survey requirements. Safety is our priority in remote fishing escapes. MV Reel Escape is fitted with:
Satellite phone
Dual Garmin Ecomap 95 SV PLUS Chart plotter/Sounders.
Garmin Livescope LVS34
Fusion Sound System
Zip wake active trim tabs
Suzuki 175 HP Outboard.
72-inch 80lb Min Kota Terova Electric motor with GPS Spot lock.
4 comfortable padded swivel seats for clients.
95lt Waco fridge / freezer for food safety and cool drinks and snacks.
Two shade umbrellas
Porta-potty / umbrellas for privacy for our female clients.
Electric Anchor system
Fresh water Deck wash to cool you down if it’s too hot
Salt water Deck wash
