The Trip 
Catering groups of up to 6 people
Get away with a group of mates on a remote Northern Territory fishing charter.
These fully guided Live aboard charters can be booked with a group as small as 3 with a maximum of 6 and you can opt to have the boat to yourselves.
Depending on the time of year and fishing preferences you will be head west of Darwin fishing the NT West Coast or head east and fish the Western Arnhem land area.
Heading west of Darwin offers up some great Barra fishing also some option for some inshore reef and pelagic fishing. A trip west also opens up a shorter 3 or 4 day Dundee and beyond option,
The Arnhem Land area to the east offers predominantly Barra fishing with some reef fishing options.
Escaping the rat race and the crowds is what mothership charters are all about.
Inclusions 
• Transfers 
• All fishing gear supplied on a replace if lost or damaged basis 
• Lunches, morning tea, breakfasts and cooked dinners whilst on tour 
• Linen & Towels

Options
• 5 days - NT West Coast or Western Arnhem Land 
• 3 or 4 days - Dundee Beach and Beyond 
5 days - NT West Coast or Western Arnhem Land 
6 night / 5 day Charters
Departing Darwin early am you will travel that day to the fishing location, have a comfy nights sleep at anchor and when you awake you will be ready to fish. After your 5th days fishing you will travel overnight arriving in Darwin in the early afternoon. This charter gives you 5 full days of fishing.
Location
Western Arnhem Land or NT West Coast
The Mother Ship
• Beagle Gulf is a 47ft Steber
• Air-conditioned accommodation for up to 6 guests
• Freezer facilities
• Large back deck
• Cel-fi booster (reception can vary)
• Back deck BBQ
• Desalinator with unlimited fresh water
Target Species
Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Black Jew, Golden Snapper, Mangrove Jack, Trevally, Mackerel, Tuna and more.
3 or 4 days - Dundee Beach and Beyond 
3 or 4 days
Departing Darwin late afternoon you will travel to Dundee Beach to rendezvous with the mothership, maybe sneak in a sunset fish, have a comfy nights sleep at anchor and when you awake you will be ready to fish.
Location
Dundee Beach & Beyond (Perron Islands, Daly RIver Mouth, Cape Ford, Littel Moyle)
