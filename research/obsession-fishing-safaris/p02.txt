Darwin Barra Fishing Charters & Light Tackle Sport Fishing
Obsession Fishing Charters are a Darwin based Guided Barra fishing Charter business specialising in Darwin Barra Fishing & Bluewater Fishing Charters.
Whilst Darwin and the Northern Territory are world renowned for their Barra fishing Obsession Fishing Safaris also specialise in Bluewater Sport Fishing Charters.
Species available on a Darwin Fishing Charters with Obsession include but are by no-means limited to:
• Barramundi (Barra)
• Queenfish
• Golden Snapper
• Threadfin Salmon
• Spanish Mackerel
• Saratoga
• Jew Fish
• Giant Trevally
• Tuna… and more
