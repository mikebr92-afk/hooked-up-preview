The Trip 
The Run Off occurs at the end of the Top End wet season, when the flood waters are subsiding the Barra fishing really fires up. The Run Off is one of the best times to experience Barra fishing with trophy fish over the magic metre mark being a real possibility.
We fish the Run Off on the Mary River at Shady Camp. Shady Camp has a reputation for producing great Barra fishing during the Run Off and has accounted for many trophy Barra of the years.
Accommodation on these tours is lodge style, all with clean comfortable airconditioned rooms.
All meals & accommodation are included in the package and tours are available from 2 to 7 days.
If you are serious about Barra fishing you should do yourself a favor and book a Run Off Barra Fishing Tour.
All itineraries are subject to change without notice due to seasonal weather, tides and fishing conditions.
