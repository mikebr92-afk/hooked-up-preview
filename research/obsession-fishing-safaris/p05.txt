The Trip 
Our half day Darwin Harbour fishing charters are tailored to suit the tides, weather conditions, time of year and target species to ensure we produce the best results every time.
We will suggest the best options for you when you are looking to book.
Taking tides and conditions into account, you may target Barramundi (Barra), Queenfish, Golden Snapper, Threadfin Salmon, Spanish Mackerel, Jew Fish, Trevally and Tuna just to list a few.
Mud crabbing is also an option if you are chasing a feed of Darwin Harbour Mud Crabs.
Darwin harbour half day charters include use of tackle and fishing equipment and light refreshments. BYO food, soft drinks & alcohol (cans only)
Obsession can cater for all size groups. Trips require a minimum of 2 people however get in touch if you don’t have a mate to go with and we can see where we have available seats.
All itineraries are subject to change without notice due to seasonal weather, tides and fishing conditions.
