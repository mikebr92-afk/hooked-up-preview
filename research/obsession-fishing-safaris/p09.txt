About Us 
Obsession Fishing Safaris are a Darwin based Guided fishing business. Whilst Darwin and the Northern Territory are world renowned for their Barra fishing Obsession Fishing Safaris also specialise in Bluewater Sport Fishing and Fly Fishing.
Obsession is a custom build 6.3m plate Barramundi fishing boat. The obsession is a large vessel with a small stern console leaving the rest of the vessel pure fishing platform meaning it can comfortably fish 4 anglers plus your guide.
Equipped with a electric motor to ensure you can get into just the right spots to quietly stalk and sight cast the cautious of fish.
Powered by economical, quiet and reliable 4 stroke outboards with ample under floor fuel storage making long range trips to remote fishing destinations a pleasure.This boat is fully surveyed to N.T standard and features an inflatable life raft and life buoy.
Equipped with state of the art navigational equipment , Lowrence side imaging, Chartplotter Sounder GPS, EPIRB and VHF radio.
Combine this with our local knowledge and you’ll catch fish in comfort and safety.
Other features of “Obsession” include swivel seats, gas cooker, kill tank, live bait tank, bilge pump and large umbrellas leaving the sides of the boat open for casting.
“Obsession” is a pleasure to fish from or simply sight seeing; croc and bird watch or just relax and enjoy the Top End’s unique waterways.
The Boat 
• 6.5 metre (20”) Plate customised Barra fishing boat
• raised front casting platform
• 150 HP 4 stroke outboard
• 300L (72 US Gal) under floor fuel storage
• state of the art navigational equipment 
• Humminbird SideScan Sounder/GPS
• VHF radio and EPIRB
• swivel seats
• retractable drink holders
• kill tank, live bait tank
• retractable sliding canopy
• Minn Kota 80 pound Bow mounted Electric motor
