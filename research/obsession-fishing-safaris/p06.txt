The Trip 
If screeming reels on the bluewater is your thing this is the charter for you. We love seeing our clients hooked up to a top end pelagic sport fish.
We will target Queenfish, Spanish Mackerel, Trevally and Tuna just to list a few.
The Darwin harbour charter include use of tackle and fishing equipment , morning tea, lunch & light refreshments.
Obsession can cater for all size groups. Trips require a minimum of 2 people however get in touch if you don’t have a mate to go with and we can see where we have available seats.
All itineraries are subject to change without notice due to seasonal weather, tides and fishing conditions.
