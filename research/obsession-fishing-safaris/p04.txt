The Trip 
Barra day charters are offered in the Top End’s remote coastal creeks, scenic billabongs, and harbours.
Each trip is tailored to suit the tides, weather conditions, time of year to ensure we produce the best results every time.
We pick up from Darwin around 5am and have you back around 3.30pm
note:- times may vary depending on location and how the day goes
Whilst onboard your Darwin Barra fishing adventure you will encounter many of our iconic top end wildlife. Keep an eye out for the massive Saltwater Crocodiles, Jabiru and Whistling Kites to name a few.
All trips include use of tackle and fishing equipment , transfers from your accommodation to fishing location, morning tea, lunch and light refreshments.
Obsession can cater for all size groups.
Trips require a minimum of 2 people however get in touch if you don’t have a mate to go with and we can see where we have available seats.
All itineraries are subject to change without notice due to seasonal weather, tides and fishing conditions.
