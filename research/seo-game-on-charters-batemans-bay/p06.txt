Game On Charters is located in the iconic Batemans Bay marina.
Offering modern well equipt vessels with an extensive range of charters to choose from including reef fishing, game fishing, snorkelling, whale watching, scenic and sunset cruises. 
Charter options are part day, half-day, and full-day and cater for beginners up to the hard-core fisherman. Game On Charters allows B.Y.O. so if you wish, you can enjoy a drink while soaking in the surroundings.
Whether you're a fishing enthusiast a novice or simply want to snorkel or enjoy the pristine waters in the Batemans Bay area, Game On Charters has an amazing on-water experience waiting for you.
The vessels are fully equipped with all the latest safety gear, electronics and fishing equipment.
You can also put together a custom charter targeting the activities you want in all in the one trip.
