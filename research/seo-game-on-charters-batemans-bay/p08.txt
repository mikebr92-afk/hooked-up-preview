Welcome to Game on Charters Batemans Bay: Premium Sunset river cruises
Book Now 
Book Now 
Immerse yourself in the tranquil beauty of the Clyde River with the premium Sunset Cruise brought to you by Game on Charters Batemans Bay. Our sunset cruise takes you on an enchanting journey through calm waters and stunning natural scenery, transporting you to a place of pure magic and serenity.
Discover the Breathtaking Clyde River
Our Sunset Cruise on the Clyde River has been carefully designed to give you the best of the Clyde River. Over the span of 2 hours, you can witness the breathtaking views that the Clyde river and Batemans Bay have to offer. As one of the leading operators in Batemans Bay along with many years of experience, we ensure that every cruise experience will exceed your expectations.
Your Comfort, Our Priority
At Game on Charters Batemans Bay, we place your comfort at the forefront of our services. Our Sunset Cruise on the Clyde River offers air-conditioned comfort onboard, making for a cosy and comfortable experience. If you prefer a more panoramic view, our flybridge on our luxury cruiser lets you experience the sun setting over the horizon from a bird’s eye view.
Customised Options and Personalised Experiences
We are flexible in our cruise options. If you wish to explore Batemans Bay or the Clyde River, we provide customised solutions according to your preferences. Our aim is to provide you with a memorable and personalised experience, showcasing the best of what our region has to offer.
Gourmet Meals and Comfortable Dining Options
To elevate your experience, Game on Charters Batemans Bay also offers catered meals. We partner with local restaurants to provide our guests with fine dining options that suit your needs, we also have a B.Y.O policy, so you can enjoy a casual drink with friends or family while basking in the tranquil surroundings of the Clyde River.
Book Your Clyde River Sunset Cruise Today
Embark on an unforgettable journey with Game on Charters Sunset Cruise on the Clyde River today. Experience the sheer beauty of the stunning scenery, peaceful waters, and the magical sunsets. Contact us to reserve your place on our next cruise and soak in the pure magic of the Clyde River.
