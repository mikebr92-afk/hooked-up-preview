Our Charters 
Reef Fishing
Game On Charters Batemans Bay offers half and full-day reef fishing charters, both shared and private. Our experienced crew will have you fishing the best spots catching a variety of species, such as Snapper, Morwong, King Fish, and Bonito
Learn More 
Game Fishing
Experience some of the South coast's best game fishing on our full-day private charters. Our crew's expertise and our well-equipped luxury game cruiser ensure a memorable trip chasing that fish of a lifetime.
Learn More 
Deep Drop Fishing
Our full-day deep drop fishing charters are both exciting and rewarding. Equipped with the latest electric reels, quality rods, tackle, and the latest Garmin electronics, our experienced crew will ensure you have the best chance and catching that monster from the deep.
Learn More 
Whale Watching
Game On Charters Batemans Bay offers stunning whale watching tours (seasonal). Observe the awe-inspiring Southern Right and Humpback whales in their migratory route off Batemans Bay on our comfortable and luxurious Sea Bee cruiser.
Learn More 
Sunset Cruise
Enjoy an enchanting 2.5-hour sunset cruise along the Clyde River or Batemans Bay. Experience breathtaking views, stunning scenery, and serene waters from the comfort of our luxury cruiser. Gourmet meals and BYO drinks options are also available.
Learn More 
Tollgate Island Dolphin Cruise
Join us for an exceptional dolphin watching experience around the peaceful Tollgate Islands. Our Dolphin Cruise offers you a chance to observe these playful creatures up-close in their natural habitat.
Learn More 
Clyde River Cruise
Discover the tranquil beauty of the Clyde River on our immersive cruises. Our knowledgeable and experienced crew ensures a comfortable journey, unveiling the picturesque landscapes of the waters 
Know More 
Snorkelling
Experience the thrill of exploring the vibrant marine life beneath the crystal-clear waters of Batemans Bay with our 2.5hr guided Snorkelling Adventures. We ensure your safety and an unforgettable underwater experience.
Know More 
Tollgate Islands and Surrounds
Uncover the secrets of the South Coast's hidden gem as you explore the untouched paradise of Tollgate Islands and its Surroundings with Game On Charters Batemans Bay.
Know More 
Located in the iconic Batemans Bay marina, you have the chance to charter our 36ft Riviera Platinum Game cruiser or our 42 ft Westcoaster centre cab. We offer comfortable vessels with all the bells and whistles to make your trip enjoyable.
