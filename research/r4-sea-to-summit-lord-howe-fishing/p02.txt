Enjoy the day on "Noctiluca" fishing or sightseeing around spectacular Lord Howe Island and Ball's Pyramid. Book a private tour and spend a day climbing the iconic Mt Gower. Every guided tour is a wealth of information on Island history, with stories along the way. This includes expertise on birds, fish, geology & photography. Sea to Summit Expeditions was established in 1992 by Jack and wife Cindy.
Jack is a 5th generation Islander and a 3rd generation mountain guide, following in the footsteps of his grandfather, Jack Snr & Father Ray. Jack spent his childhood roaming the mountains or out fishing, any chance he got with family and friends. On Ray's retirement from the mountain Jack took over to continue the family tradition. Since then, in 1992 Jack has completed over 2400 guided trips to the summit. Lord Howe Island's most experienced & long running Mt Gower guide.

Sightseeing at Ball's Pyramid. 
Ball's Pyramid is the tallest monolith out of the water in the world. It is an iconic rock stack rising 552m out of the water, 23km from Lord Howe Island. 
Our Bird Watching at Balls Pyramid tour is not just a boat ride. Your journey will be filled with a wealth of information including Birds, Fish, Geology, and Local History.

Mt-Gower Day Walk-Climb, Nature Tour 
Mt Gower is an iconic peak at the southern end of Lord Howe Island, rising 875m from sea level. Considered a medium to hard trek. The walk is around 14km return and takes about 8.5 hours to complete, so you need to be in good health and have no knee or back problems. Limited private tours only..
