Beaches & Waterways 
An afternoon of mates, fishing and BBQing on the river? Great fishing spots and boat ramp locations? 4WD Tours? Sorted. You can thank us later :)

and boat ramp locations? 4WD Tours? Sorted. You can thank us later :) 
Fishing around the region
We've rounded up some of the top spots to wet a line here.
Gone Fishin' 
Journey
Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters
Journey
Port Macquarie Fishing Charters
Boating & Fishing Gear
Check out Jordan's Boating Centre and Settlement Point Boat Shed for BBQ Boat Hire, tinnies, e-boats, kayaks, SUPs and fishing gear hire. 
Keen fishos will also want to check out Ned Kelly's Bait & Tackle . They speak fluent 'fisho', know the waterways inside and out, and post detailed fishing reports every week.
4WD Adventures
Based in Port Mac, the team at Aus 4X4 Tours offer tours suitable from all wheel drive low clearance vehicles to extreme bucket list adventures. Experience the natural history, forgotten history and culture.
Beach Driving 
You can purchase a Beach Driving permit to tackle our golden stretches of coastline. Check out the beach driving map here .
Every August, we host the Mid North Coast Caravan, Camping, 4WD, Fish and Boat Show at Wauchope Showground, with thousands of enthusiasts coming from all over to check out all the latest gear.
