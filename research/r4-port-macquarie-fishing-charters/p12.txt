Art, Culture & History 
The Glasshouse
Proudly situated in the heart of our CBD is this majestic architect designed building, housing a state-of-the-art 588 seat theatre, Regional Art Gallery, shop, Visitor Information Centre, conferencing facilities, a foyer, bar and studio. The basement features heritage and archaeological displays dating from the 1820s.
The venue regularly attracts national and international acts, from Opera and ballet through to pop icons. You can book a 'behind the scenes' tour of this fascinating building and discover the inner workings of the theatre.
Visit the Glasshouse 
Visitor Information Centre 
The Glasshouse is home to our Visitor Information Centre providing a wealth of knowledge on attractions, tours, and more! Stop in and ask the lovely Tourism Ambassador volunteers for tips on things to do during your stay. 
Glasshouse Regional Gallery 
Featuring many exhibitions throughout the year of contemporary, indigenous, historical, traditional, design and sculptural artworks; the Glasshouse Regional Gallery program aims to support and provide professional development opportunities to regional artists within the local community.
Touring exhibitions provide access to quality artists and cultural experiences not otherwise available within the region. The Gallery also provides a range of education opportunities directly associated with the exhibition program.
Visit the Regional Gallery 
What's On
Be sure to check the Glasshouse theatre and show bookings. Plan ahead as shows do book out! An annual catalogue of shows from world-renowned live entertainment companies from a vast array of genres including classical and contemporary dance, opera, live comedy, short film and educational children's entertainment, to name a few. 
Shop
The Glasshouse Shop is home to exquisite glassware, handcrafted jewellery, corporate gifts or something for that extra special person in your life. Choose from individually designed ceramic objects, local produce or small homeware items.
