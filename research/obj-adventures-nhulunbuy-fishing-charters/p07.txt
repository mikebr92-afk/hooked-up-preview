FAQ 
Nhulunbuy and Gove Peninsula Fishing Charters 
1) Does Nhulunbuy fishing charters rely on the tides? 
No OBJ Nhulunbuy Fishing charters can leave on any tide. The Melville Bay Boat Ramp and fishing facilities in Nhulunbuy, East Arnhem Land are great in any tides. If you are at the Boat Club this can be difficult in very low tides which is not very often. 
2) Can children go on the OBJ fishing charter? 
Children are welcome on the Fishing Charters as long as they are under parental supervision at all times and are over the age of 5. T he charter boat has a large canopy to protect them from the hot Nhulunbuy sun, there is stairs at the front and rear to help them get on and off the boat. The experience is for everyone young and old. 
3 ) What is the maximum number of anglers on the boat? 
OBJ Adventures, Nhulunbuy Fishing Charters can take up to 8 anglers on the charter boat. We do have a minium of 4 needed to run a charter. 
4) Can I book as a single angler for a Nhulunbuy fishing charter? 
We can take a booking for a single angler, however we need 4 anglers to run a charter. A single angler will not be able to book a fishing charter online however you can contact us and we will marry you up with a fishing charter already going or If there is no charter booked we will do our best to find you a goup to join. 
5) What if it rains? 
East Arnhem (Nhulunbuy) is in the tropics so we do encounter rain, which usually makes the water calm and the fish bite. With our charter boats having a full canopy we are able to fish and stay relatively dry. If it is too dangerous that it would make the charter unsafe, we will postpone the departure till the weather clears or cancel the trip. If this happens we will contact all anglers prior to departure time and organise refunds or rebooks on the next available charter. 
6) Is there any additional costs once we are on the charter? 
No other costs envolved on our charters however if you are on the Exculsive Island Fishing Charter there is a bar on the island so you can purchase drinks/ cocktails whilst at lunch at your own expense. 
7) What accommodation is around Nhulunbuy/East Arnhem Land? 
There is a number accommodation types to choose from including camping , r esort style , lodges and motels . Visit East Arnhem land for booking options around East Arnhem Land. 
8) Can I bring alcohol on the fishing charter? 
You can bring alcohol on board, we do ask that angles try to bring cans where possible. Please note that you do require a permit to purchase take away alcohol in Nhulunbuy and the usual RSA rules apply. 
9) Is food included on the charter? 
The fishing charters are BYO lunch and snacks. We do provide eskies to store any food or drinks bought along. If you are o n the Exclusive Island Charter l unch at the res ort is included 
10) Where does the fishing charter leave from? 
We do leave from Melville Bay Road boat ramp Nhulunbuy near Sea Swift however we can also do pick ups from the Boat Club (AKA Gove Yacht Club). Please let us know in advance if you wish to be picked up from another location. 
11) Can I bring my own fishing gear on the charter? 
If you have a lucky fishing rod or lure bring it along. Just let us know you are bringing them so we can make sure we have room. 
12) What else can you see whilst on the water in East Arnhem Land 
There is an abundance of wildlife in Arnhem Land. We often see pods of dolphins, manta rays, turtles, sea snakes and many other varieties of sea life. We have even had a whaleshark come to check out the boat. 
13) What can I catch on the fishing charter in East Arnhem Land? 
There is a wide variety of fish in the gulf, so there is everything from Mackerel and Giant Trevally to Coral Trout, Golden Snapper, Red Emperior, Blackjew fish and loads more. It will depend on tides and also on the moon phases as to what is going to be best for that day. 
14) Do I need a fishing licence or permits on the charter? 
No in the Northern Territory there is no recreational fishing licence requirements. However we still have size and bag limits that we must adhere too. East Arnhem Land does require you to have a general permit to be in Nhulunbuy however we do not require a permit for fishing charters. If you are joining the Exclusive Island Charter the permit for Bremer Island is included in the price. 
15) What if I need to cancel the fishing charter? 
If you cancel 14 days prior to the charter you will receive a full refund. If cancelled within 14 days but 72hours prior it is a 50% refund. If it is within the 72hours there is no refund. 
1 6 ) What if i have never fished before? 
Our experienced and knowledgeable captain and crew will take you to the best fishing spots, and provide all the equipment you need to catch the big one! Whether you're a beginner or an experienced angler, we cater to all skill levels. 
17) How do you make your charter sustainable? 
We are committed to sustainability in our operations by utilising an electric motors to reduce emissions and help to protect the fishers habitat, recycling materials, and using water jugs to cut down on single-use plastics. These practices reflect our dedication to environmental responsibility and resource efficiency. 
18) How do I book a private charter? 
To book a private charter wheather full day or half day please contact us direct on 0455460277 or email book@objadventures.com.au
