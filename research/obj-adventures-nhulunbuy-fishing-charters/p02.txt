OBJ Adventures Nhulunbuy, Gove Fishing Charters 
Come fishing in Arnhem Land 
Nhulunbuy, Gove Peninsula Fishing Charters 
Ready to explore the beautiful waters of NHulunbuy and the Gove Peninsula? Whether you’re after a serene day on the water, or an action-packed fishing adventure, our charters are designed to suit all budgets and experiences. Follow us on social to see the latest catches and customer stories! 
Half Day Fishing Charter Nhulunbuy 
• Departure: Leaves Melville Bay Road boat ramp at 7 AM. 
• Duration : Up to 4 hours on the water. 
• Includes: All fishing gear (bait, tackle, rods). 
• Bring: Snacks and drinks. 
• Cost: $350 per person. 
• Optional: Pickup from the Boat Club upon request tide dependant. 
Exclusive Island Full Day Fishing Charter 
• Departure: Leaves the boat ramp at 7 AM; returns around 3 PM. 
• Highlights: Includes a delicious lunch at Banu Banu Retreat. 
• Includes: All fishing gear (bait, tackle, rods). 
• Cost: $495 per person. 
• Optional: Pickup from the Boat Club upon request tide dependant. 
Full Day Fishing Charter Nhulunbuy 
• Departure: Leaves from Melville Bay Road boat ramp at 7 AM. 
• Duration : Up to 8 hours on the water. 
• Includes : All fishing gear (bait, tackle, rods). 
• Bring: Lunch and drinks. 
• Cost: $480 per person. 
• Optional: Pickup from the Boat Club upon request tide dependant. 
Full Day offshore fishing adventures 
• Departure: Leaves the boat ramp at 7 AM; returns around 3 PM. 
• Activities: Fishing and exploring up to 20 miles off the coast. 
• Includes: All fishing gear (bait, tackle, rods). 
• Bring: Lunch and drinks. 
• Cost : $595 per person. 
• Optional: Pickup from the Boat Club upon request tide dependant. 
Nhulunbuy Sunset Harbour Cruise 
• Departure: From the Boat Club Boat ramp. 
• Duration: Up to 2 hours. 
• Experience: Enjoy a serene journey around the stunning Gove Harbour as the sun sets. 
• Contact Us for booking and more information. 
Private Charter Nhulunbuy 
• Details: Customise your fishing charter for up to 8 anglers. Perfect for private events, team-building, or a personal getaway. 
Contact Us to plan your private charter experience.
