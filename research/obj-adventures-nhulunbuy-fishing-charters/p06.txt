Experience extraordinary fishing charters along the stunning coastline of the Gove Peninsula with OBJ Adventures Nhulunbuy Fishing Charters.
Whether you prefer a full-day excursion, a half-day adventure, or a more intimate experience, they cater for all your needs and skill level. Their seasoned skipper boasts 25 years of expertise navigating these waters, ensuring you access some of East Arnhem Land's finest fishing locations. The boats are AMSA surveyed and outfitted with all required safety equipment. 
Operating out of the pristine waters and wild landscape in Nhulunbuy, Northern Territory, their family-owned company specialises in unforgettable, eco-friendly fishing adventures. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a first-time adventurer, we're here to help you catch everything from Spanish Mackerel to Red Emperor .

Tours available
A Full Day Fishing Charter 
Set off with the experienced guides to explore the diverse and abundant fishing grounds that East Arnhem Land is renowned for. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a newcomer to the sport, their guides are dedicated to ensuring an enriching and tailored experience for all.
The full-day charter allows for a comprehensive fishing experience, providing ample time to explore various fishing grounds and maximize your chances of a bountiful catch. Your day begins with a 7am departure from the Melville Bay Road Ramp, as the rising sun bathes the surroundings in a warm, golden glow and return to the Melville Bay Road Ramp as the day draws to a close, enriched with memories of a full day immersed in the untamed beauty of East Arnhem Land.
They want to ensure you have a comfortable and enjoyable experience, so please remember to bring your lunch and hat. Additionally, feel free to bring your favorite drinks and snacks to keep you energized. Everything you need for fishing is provided.
This is a great fishing charter option for those die hard fishers. 
Show more 
A Half Day Fishing Tour 
The half-day fishing charter in Arnhem Land is the perfect way to connect with nature, unwind, and create memories that will last a lifetime.
Whether you're an experienced angler or a first-time fisher, the half-day charter caters to all skill levels. You'll have the opportunity to reel in a variety of species, from the iconic black jewfish to the feisty golden snapper. As you cast your line and feel the excitement of a tug, you'll be surrounded by the untouched wilderness, creating an unforgettable backdrop for your fishing excursion.
This charter leaves the Melville Bay Road boat ramp at 7.00am and will spend to up to 4 hours on the water around Nhulunbuy. 
They want to ensure you have a comfortable and enjoyable experience, so please remember to bring your own hat to shield yourself from the sun. Additionally, feel free to bring your favourite drinks and snacks to keep you energized. Everything you need for fishing is provided.
Please call OBJ Adventures for single bookings.

An Exclusive Island Fishing Charter 
Embark on a journey like no other, as the sun rises over the pristine waters off East Arnhem Land, your day begins with an exhilarating fishing excursion. Our experienced guides will lead you to the region's most coveted fishing spots, promising an abundance of marine life and the thrill of reeling in your catch of the day.
Following the morning fishing adventure, we head to the amazing Banu Banu Retreat. Here, you'll be treated to a gourmet lunch that transcends the boundaries of ordinary dining. Delight your palate with a culinary experience crafted from the finest local ingredients, served against the backdrop of breathtaking views. To enhance your island escape, the retreat also provides the perfect setting for a few refreshing cocktails—a fitting way to cap off your day of adventure and relaxation. 
Show more 
Offshore Adventure 
A full day offshore adventure in East Arnhem Land takes you deep into some of the region's most remote and untouched waters. 
It's not just about the fishing—the scenery is ever-changing and wild, offering a real sense of isolation and connection to the natural environment. It's a day shaped by the sea, the land, and the quiet rhythm of the remote tropics.
