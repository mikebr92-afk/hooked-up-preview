Cairns’ only guided walk and wade freshwater fishing experience. 
Target iconic species like Jungle Perch and Sooty Grunter on foot, casting surface lures and flies in crystal-clear rainforest streams. 
This is a truly immersive tropical light-tackle experience, perfect for anglers chasing something different. 
The Experience
• Walk-and-wade fishing through tropical rainforest creeks • National Parks access under unique guiding permits • Topwater action all day – fish aggressively hit surface lures and flies • Warm water wading – no waders required • Swim in the stream to cool off between sessions • All tackle provided – or bring your own if preferred • Pick-up available from Cairns CBD, Port Douglas & Mission Beach • Locations vary depending on weather and river conditions (up to 1hr 45min drive) • Moderate fitness required – walking over uneven terrain
Trip Options
Half Day: 6:30am – 12:00pm
Full Day: 6:30am – 4:00pm
Includes lunch (full day), snacks, water and electrolytes.
What to Bring
• Wading boots or sturdy shoes suitable for rocky streambeds • Hat, long-sleeve shirt, sunscreen and polarized sunglasses • Waterproof your camera if bringing one • Waterproof backpack available to borrow if needed
Tackle Overview
Spin
4lb mono, 1000-size reel, 2–4kg rod Small surface poppers and unweighted plastics
Fly
5–6wt rods with floating line (shooting heads preferred)
Hoppers, large dries, foam bugs, small streamers and poppers (up to size 1/0)
Long casts and fast strips – far more aggressive than trout fishing.
Custom multi-day rainforest adventures available on request.
This is a seasonal trip available April – December only .
Bookings and Enquiries 
Private Fishing Charters & Nature Tours 
Tropical Sportfisher is a locally owned fly and sportfishing charter based in Cairns, Far North Queensland. Led by experienced local guides, we specialise in guided barramundi fishing and light tackle lure and fly fishing across Cairns, Port Douglas, and Mission Beach .
We run private day trips for up to 2 anglers per boat, and offer multi-day remote adventures for small groups, including mothership and heli-access options.
Our trips include river, estuary, jungle perch, and inshore bluewater fishing. We also offer wildlife cruises , croc spotting, birdwatching, and private nature or photography trips to secluded beaches, waterfalls, or tropical islands.
Whether you’re chasing fish or exploring wild places, our charters are tailored to your interests and the conditions. Get in touch to experience the best of Tropical North Queensland with a local guide.
