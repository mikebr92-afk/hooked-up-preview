FV Gadget.
FV Gadget our estuary / coastal vessel, is a 6m Hooker Pro Guide that is fitted with the latest Garmin 8416 unit partnered with Panoptix Livescope Forward which is an amazing tool for scouting and fishing out ahead of your cast. FV Gadget is customized specifically to fish and target Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Coral Trout, Spanish Mackerel, Finger Mark with a lot of other by catch species in the mix!

Welcome to Gone Fishing, Cooktown, Australia
Gone Fishing offers guided fishing charters and wildlife tours around Cooktown, Cape York and on the Great Barrier Reef, Australia. Fish with lures or live-bait for barramundi, coral trout, tuna, mackerel, jacks, trevally and Queenies. With over 15 years experience fishing locally, we pride ourselves on our personalised, professional and consistent fishing charters.
