What a Catch Fishing Charters 
Terms & Conditions of Booking 
Mooloolaba, Sunshine Coast, Queensland 
At What a Catch Fishing Charters, we're passionate about providing safe, enjoyable and memorable experiences on the water. 
As a family-owned and operated business, we appreciate your understanding that offshore fishing charters are subject to weather conditions, minimum passenger numbers and operational requirements. 
Please read the following Terms & Conditions carefully before making a booking. By confirming a booking with What a Catch Fishing Charters, you acknowledge and agree to these conditions, subject to your rights under Australian Consumer Law. 
1. Bookings & Payments 
1.1 Individual / Shared Fishing Charters 
• 
A valid credit or debit card is required to secure an individual or shared charter booking. 
• 
Payment will be processed in accordance with the payment arrangements communicated at the time of booking. 
• 
Customers are responsible for ensuring their contact information and payment details are accurate. 
• 
All bookings are subject to suitable weather conditions, minimum passenger requirements and operational availability. 
1.2 Private / Exclusive / Full Boat Charters 
• 
A deposit equal to 50% of the total charter price is required to secure all private, exclusive or full boat bookings. 
• 
A private charter booking is not confirmed until the required deposit has been received. 
• 
The remaining balance is payable no later than the day before the scheduled departure, unless otherwise agreed in writing. 
• 
The person making the booking is responsible for communicating these Terms & Conditions to all members of their group. 
2. Customer Cancellations & Refunds 
2.1 Individual / Shared Charters — 48-Hour Cancellation Policy 
• 
Customers must provide a minimum of 48 hours' notice before their scheduled departure time to cancel their booking without incurring a cancellation fee. 
• 
Cancellations received less than 48 hours before departure will incur a cancellation fee equal to 100% of the booking value , subject to applicable consumer law. 
• 
Failure to attend a confirmed charter (no-show) will also result in the full booking amount being charged, subject to applicable consumer law. 
• 
Where a valid card has been provided to secure the booking, the applicable cancellation fee may be charged to that card. 
• 
Requests to reschedule within 48 hours of departure are subject to availability and approval by What a Catch Fishing Charters. Rescheduling is not guaranteed and does not automatically waive cancellation fees. 
2.2 Private / Exclusive / Full Boat Charters — 7-Day Cancellation Policy 
• 
A 50% deposit is required to secure a private or full boat charter. 
• 
Cancellations received more than 7 days before the scheduled departure time will receive a full refund of the deposit. 
• 
Cancellations received 7 days (168 hours) or less before the scheduled departure time will result in forfeiture of the 50% deposit as a cancellation fee, subject to applicable Australian Consumer Law. 
• 
The cancellation fee reflects the advance reservation of the vessel, crew allocation, administration and potential loss of other bookings. 
• 
Requests to reschedule within the 7-day cancellation period will be considered at our discretion and are subject to availability. A change of date is not guaranteed. 
• 
Any cancellation fee will be applied only to the extent permitted by law, having regard to the circumstances and the reasonable costs and losses associated with the cancellation. 
2.3 How to Cancel 
All cancellation requests must be communicated directly to What a Catch Fishing Charters by telephone or written message. 
Cancellation notice is calculated from the scheduled departure time, not the time of booking or payment. 
3. Weather Conditions & Minimum Passenger Requirements 
All bookings are subject to suitable weather and sea conditions, minimum passenger numbers and operational requirements. 
3.1 Weather & Sea Conditions 
• 
The safety of our passengers and crew is our highest priority. 
• 
The skipper has final operational authority to determine whether weather and sea conditions are suitable for the charter to proceed safely. 
• 
Weather assessments may consider wind, swell, tides, visibility, forecasts and other relevant marine conditions. 
• 
What a Catch Fishing Charters reserves the right to cancel, postpone or make reasonable changes to a charter where conditions are considered unsafe or unsuitable. 
• 
Weather conditions can change unexpectedly. Cancellations may occur at short notice, including on the day of departure. 
3.2 Minimum Passenger Requirements 
• 
All shared fishing charters are subject to minimum paying passenger numbers being reached. 
• 
If minimum passenger requirements are not met, What a Catch Fishing Charters reserves the right to cancel or offer an alternative departure. 
• 
Customers will be notified as soon as reasonably practicable if a charter cannot proceed due to insufficient numbers. 
• 
Minimum passenger requirements may vary depending on the vessel, trip duration and operational costs. 
3.3 Cancellations by What a Catch Fishing Charters 
If we cancel a charter due to unsuitable weather, insufficient passenger numbers, mechanical issues, safety concerns or other operational circumstances preventing the trip from proceeding, customers will be offered: 
• 
A full refund of all payments made for the affected charter; or 
• 
The opportunity to reschedule to another available date. 
Customers will not forfeit their deposit or incur a cancellation fee when What a Catch Fishing Charters cancels their charter. 
3.4 Charter Confirmations 
We endeavour to contact customers the afternoon before their scheduled charter to confirm weather conditions, departure details and operational arrangements. 
Customers should ensure they are contactable and check for any updates before travelling to the departure location. 
A charter confirmation does not guarantee departure if conditions subsequently deteriorate. 
4. Departure Times, Late Arrivals & No-Shows 
• 
Customers must arrive at the designated meeting location by the check-in time provided in their booking confirmation. 
• 
Departure times are scheduled and may be adjusted due to operational requirements. 
• 
Customers who arrive late or fail to attend may miss their charter. 
• 
The vessel is not required to delay departure for late passengers. 
• 
No refunds will ordinarily be provided for missed departures caused by customer lateness or non-attendance, except where required by law. 
• 
Customers are responsible for their own travel arrangements, parking and arriving at the correct departure location. 
5. Safety & Passenger Responsibilities 
The safety of everyone on board is paramount. 
• 
All passengers must follow the instructions of the skipper and crew at all times. 
• 
Passengers must participate in safety briefings and comply with all vessel safety procedures. 
• 
Appropriate footwear and clothing must be worn. 
• 
Passengers must remain within designated passenger areas and use equipment as instructed. 
• 
Running, unsafe behaviour, interference with vessel operations or misuse of equipment is prohibited. 
• 
The skipper may refuse boarding or direct a passenger to cease participating in activities where reasonably necessary for safety. 
Passengers should advise us before departure of any medical condition, disability, mobility limitation or other circumstance that may affect their ability to safely participate or require assistance. 
We will make reasonable efforts to accommodate individual needs, subject to vessel safety requirements and operational limitations. 
6. Children & Supervision 
• 
Children must be accompanied and appropriately supervised by a responsible adult. 
• 
Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising children throughout the charter. 
• 
Children must follow all safety instructions and wear appropriate safety equipment when directed. 
• 
Participation by young children may be subject to vessel suitability, weather conditions and the skipper's safety assessment. 
• 
Please discuss the age and needs of children with us before booking. 
7. Seasickness, Personal Comfort & Early Returns 
Offshore fishing takes place in an open marine environment, and vessel movement is to be expected. 
• 
Passengers are responsible for considering their own susceptibility to motion sickness and preparing accordingly. 
• 
We recommend seeking advice from a pharmacist or healthcare professional regarding suitable seasickness prevention before departure. 
• 
Seasickness or personal discomfort does not automatically entitle a passenger to a refund once a charter has commenced. 
• 
If a passenger voluntarily chooses to stop fishing or requests an early return for personal reasons, a refund is not ordinarily provided. 
• 
The skipper retains authority to return to port early where necessary for passenger welfare, safety or operational reasons. 
• 
Any refund entitlement arising from a shortened or interrupted charter will be assessed in accordance with Australian Consumer Law and the circumstances involved. 
8. Fishing Activities, Catch & Regulations 
Fishing is an outdoor activity influenced by weather, seasons, tides and natural conditions. 
• 
While our experienced crew will make every reasonable effort to provide an enjoyable fishing experience, fish catches are not guaranteed . 
• 
Fishing locations, methods and target species may change depending on conditions, fishing activity and applicable regulations. 
• 
All fishing activities must comply with Queensland fisheries laws, including relevant size limits, possession limits, protected species requirements and seasonal closures. 
• 
The skipper and crew have final authority regarding the retention or release of fish to ensure compliance with applicable regulations. 
• 
Passengers must follow crew instructions when handling fish, hooks, knives, fishing lines and other equipment. 
• 
Legal catches may be retained by passengers, subject to applicable regulations and vessel procedures. 
• 
Fish cleaning assistance may be provided at the crew's discretion, depending on time, conditions and operational requirements. 
9. Fishing Equipment, Damage & Loss 
What a Catch Fishing Charters provides fishing equipment for use during scheduled fishing charters. 
• 
Passengers must use all fishing equipment responsibly and in accordance with crew instructions. 
• 
Reasonable wear and tear arising from normal use is expected. 
• 
Customers may be held responsible for the reasonable repair or replacement costs of equipment that is deliberately damaged, misused, negligently damaged or lost through failure to follow reasonable crew instructions. 
• 
Charges will reflect the reasonable cost of repair or replacement, taking into account the age and condition of the equipment. 
• 
Customers will be informed of any proposed charges and provided with an explanation of the damage or loss. 
10. Alcohol, Smoking & Passenger Behaviour 
We aim to maintain a safe, welcoming and enjoyable environment for all passengers. 
• 
Responsible consumption of BYO alcohol may be permitted, subject to applicable laws, vessel rules and the skipper's approval. 
• 
Glass bottles and glass drinking containers are not permitted on board. 
• 
Passengers must comply with any alcohol restrictions communicated before or during the charter. 
• 
Excessive intoxication, aggressive behaviour, harassment, abusive language or conduct that endangers others will not be tolerated. 
• 
Illegal drugs and prohibited substances are strictly forbidden. 
• 
Smoking and vaping are permitted only if expressly authorised by the skipper in a designated area, where lawful and safe. 
• 
The skipper may refuse boarding or end a passenger's participation where reasonably necessary to protect passengers, crew or the vessel. 
• 
Where participation is ended due to serious misconduct or a safety breach caused by the passenger, a refund will not ordinarily be provided, subject to applicable consumer law. 
11. Changes to Itinerary & Charter Duration 
• 
Fishing locations, departure times, routes and activities may be adjusted to account for weather, tides, safety, fisheries regulations and operational conditions. 
• 
The skipper will determine the most appropriate fishing locations based on conditions on the day. 
• 
Charter durations are approximate and may include travel to and from fishing grounds. 
• 
Reasonable changes to fishing locations or activities do not automatically entitle passengers to a refund. 
• 
Where a material change prevents the agreed service from being provided, customer rights and any remedies will be determined in accordance with Australian Consumer Law. 
12. Gift Vouchers & Booking Credits 
• 
Purchased gift vouchers are valid for a minimum of three years from the date of purchase or supply, unless a lawful exception applies or a longer validity period is stated. 
• 
Gift vouchers may be redeemed toward eligible fishing charters, subject to availability and booking conditions. 
• 
Gift vouchers are generally not redeemable for cash, except where required by law. 
• 
Customers are responsible for booking their charter within the applicable voucher validity period. 
• 
All charter bookings made using gift vouchers remain subject to weather conditions, minimum passenger requirements and the relevant cancellation policies. 
• 
Promotional vouchers and booking credits may have separate conditions, which will be clearly communicated when issued and must comply with applicable law. 
• 
Where a charter is cancelled by What a Catch Fishing Charters, the customer will retain their applicable refund or rescheduling rights regardless of the original payment method. 
13. Personal Belongings 
• 
Passengers are encouraged to secure their belongings while on board. 
• 
The marine environment presents risks of water exposure, movement and accidental loss. 
• 
Customers should take appropriate precautions when bringing phones, cameras, sunglasses and other valuables. 
• 
What a Catch Fishing Charters is not responsible for loss or damage arising solely from a passenger's own actions or failure to secure belongings, except to the extent liability arises under applicable law. 
14. Inherent Risks & Liability 
Participation in offshore fishing and marine activities involves inherent risks, including but not limited to: 
• 
Vessel movement and changing sea conditions. 
• 
Slips, trips and falls. 
• 
Seasickness and exposure to weather. 
• 
Contact with fishing hooks, lines, equipment and marine life. 
• 
Other risks ordinarily associated with offshore recreational activities. 
Passengers acknowledge that these activities involve risks and agree to follow all reasonable safety instructions. 
Nothing in these Terms & Conditions excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer guarantee, statutory right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified. 
Any limitation of liability will apply only to the extent permitted by applicable Australian law. 
15. Photography & Promotional Material 
Photographs and videos may occasionally be taken during charters for promotional purposes, including use on our website and social media channels. 
We respect the privacy of our passengers. Customers who do not wish to appear in promotional photographs or videos should advise our crew. 
We will seek appropriate consent before using identifiable images of passengers for promotional purposes, including parental or guardian consent where required for children. 
16. Website Use, Privacy & Intellectual Property 
• 
Information provided through our website and booking communications is used to manage bookings, communicate with customers and operate our services. 
• 
Personal information will be handled in accordance with applicable Australian privacy laws. 
• 
We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and payment details. 
• 
Website content, photographs, logos, branding and written material belonging to What a Catch Fishing Charters may not be reproduced or used commercially without permission, except where permitted by law. 
17. Complaints & Dispute Resolution 
We value our customers and aim to resolve any concerns fairly and promptly. 
If you have a concern regarding your booking or charter experience, please contact us directly so we have an opportunity to investigate and respond. 
Any disputes relating to cancellations, refunds, fees or service delivery will be considered in accordance with these Terms & Conditions and applicable Australian Consumer Law. 
18. Changes to Terms & Conditions 
What a Catch Fishing Charters may update these Terms & Conditions from time to time. 
The current version will be published on our website at www.whatacatchfishing.com.au . 
The terms applicable to a confirmed booking will generally be those provided or made available when the booking was accepted, unless a lawful variation is subsequently agreed. 
19. Governing Law 
These Terms & Conditions are governed by the laws of Queensland, Australia, and applicable Commonwealth legislation. 
Nothing in these conditions limits any statutory rights or remedies available to customers.
