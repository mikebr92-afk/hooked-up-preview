FAQs
Got questions about our Mooloolaba fishing charters? We’ve answered the most common ones below to help you plan your deep sea fishing trip with What a Catch . From what to bring to what you’ll catch, here’s everything you need to know before you hop aboard. 
How many people can come on board? Our vessel, Prospector , is surveyed to carry up to 16 passengers plus crew.
Do I need fishing experience? Not at all! Our experienced crew will guide you every step of the way—no experience necessary.
What should I bring? We recommend bringing a hat, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a camera. You’re welcome to bring your own snacks and drinks too (no glass, please). We’ll provide all the fishing gear and safety equipment.
Is there a toilet on board? Yes! Prospector is equipped with a fully enclosed toilet and a fresh water hand basin for your comfort.
Can I keep the fish I catch? Absolutely! Any legal-sized fish you catch are yours to keep.
What if the weather is bad? We keep a close eye on the forecast and will only run trips if the conditions are safe. If we need to cancel due to weather, we’ll reschedule your trip or offer a full refund.
Can I book a private charter? Yes! Private charters are perfect for families, parties, or corporate groups and give you full flexibility over the itinerary.
How do I book? To book a trip, just give us a call. Because our trips are weather dependent, we confirm bookings the afternoon before departure.
Where do we depart from? We depart from the Mooloolaba Marina—details and directions will be provided when you book.
