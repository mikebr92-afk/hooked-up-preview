The Vessel
Meet Prospector – our 12-metre Cougar Cat and your ultimate deep sea fishing vessel in Mooloolaba. Purpose-built for offshore reef fishing on the Sunshine Coast, Prospector is fast, stable, and ready to take up to 16 guests to the best fishing spots out wide. Equipped with modern marine electronics, full safety gear, and a private onboard bathroom, she offers a comfortable, reliable platform for all-day fishing adventures. Whether you're chasing snapper, pearl perch, or just enjoying the sea breeze, Prospector is the perfect boat for your Mooloolaba fishing charter.
