ABOUT US 
ABOUT US 
Yamba Fishing and Charters are here to cater for all of your fishing adventure and sightseeing needs. 
Glynn is a passionate fisherman and has fished the Yamba, Evans Head and Wooli areas for the last 20 years. Glynn will take you on an unforgettable journey in his 9 metre 3100 Noosa Cat with twin Yamaha 250 motors which are more than enough to shoot across to the horizon and fill the esky with fresh fish. 
Yamba Fishing and Charters have more than enough room to accommodate comfortably up to 8 persons per trip. 
All fishing gear is supplied which is made up of heavy duty rods and Penn and Shimano fishing reels all fitted with braid line to ensure you the very best chance on landing your catch. 
​ 
Remember when you book with Yamba Fishing and Charters you are supporting a small business.
