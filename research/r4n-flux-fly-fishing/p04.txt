About Us
Flux Fly Fishing, formerly Weipa Fly Fish was built on two decades of chasing Cape York's most challenging species and we're just getting started.
Led by Kurt Rowlands, our team of experienced guides have spent years learning every flat, every tide, creek bend, and offshore reef that makes Weipa one of Australia's premier fly-fishing destinations. We don't just guide fishing trips, we live and breathe this fishery. 
With 55+ species within reach and fishing styles ranging from sight-casting on pristine flats to battling big fish offshore, we've designed our guided trips to suit every angler, from first-timers to seasoned fly fishers. All-inclusive packages mean you can focus on what matters: the next cast, the next strike, the next story.
This is remote fishing done right. Professional. Passionate. Unforgettable.
This is Flux. 
Driven by passion
Since taking ownership, Kurt's focused on one thing: making sure every client gets the trip they want for themselves. That means investing in the best guides, keeping the fleet in top condition, and fishing these waters year-round to stay ahead of what's working. Kurt's not managing from a desk, he's on the boats, in the estuaries, and constantly learning this fishery.
Contact Us
We are lucky enough to be supported by some of the best brands in the industry. These companies make our jobs a lot easier, and we only put our name to gear we can trust and rely on.
