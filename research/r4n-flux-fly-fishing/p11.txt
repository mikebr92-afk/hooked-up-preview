WEIPA LODGE BASED 
• Season: April – November
• Duration: 4 days and up
• Group size: up to 10 guests (more with enough notice)
• Base: Weipa, Cape York
The Lodge
This is the trip Weipa Fly Fish guests have known for 20 years. Each day starts with a cooked breakfast before heading out on the water, and ends back at the lodge with canapés and a three-course dinner prepared by our private chef. Every guest has a private room with ensuite. For groups wanting more space, private villas next door are available, and you're still welcome at the lodge for every meal.
Enquire now
Whats included: 
• Private chef: cooked breakfast, canapés and three-course dinner every night
• Private room with ensuite
• Optional private villas next door, with full use of the lodge for dining
• Trips from 4 days, tailored to your group
