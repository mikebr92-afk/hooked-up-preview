THEY SAY A BAD DAYS FISHING BEATS A GOOD DAY AT THE OFFICE EVERY TIME. 
THEYRE RIGHT, BUT IN THE TOP END, THERES NO SUCH THING AS A BAD DAYS FISHING!
With chartered tours in and around some of the most awe-inspiring remote areas of the Northern Territory, Spring Tide Safaris will give you the opportunity to snag that elusive fifty pounder.(Barramundi) 
So, whether its an aquatic battle with the big one, or a slightly less hectic experience you're after, you've come to the right place. 
Spring Tide Safaris will guide you to the front door of that meter club so you can experience the aggressive Barramundi strike. 
Our main target species is the mighty barramundi, however in the Northern Territory we have variety of other saltwater sport fish including Jewfish, Tuna, Golden Snapper, Trevally, Spanish & Spotted Mackerel, Coral Trout, GTs and more. 
Spring Tide Safaris caters for Day Trips, Overnight Trip, Extended mother ship safaris during and after the run-off season. Our fishing tours and charters range from one to ten days. 
In the Northern Territory we have available some of the best barramundi and blue water sports fishing in the world. We provide our anglers the opportunity to fish all the major rivers in the Northern Territory and also experience the explosive Kimberly fishing. 
Location: Mary River, Finnis River, Daly River, Adelaide River, Darwin Harbour, Bynoe Harbour, and many more remote locations where Barramundi has never seen an angler before. 
What to bring: Cool protective clothing, hat, sunscreen, insect repellent, towel, camera and film, and a smile. 
Let us know: Any allergies or food dislikes and any medical conditions. 
To be noted: All safaris are fully guided and all-inclusive. Fishing tackle is supplied on a replace if lost basis. Facilities provided are suitable for bush conditions. Visitors participate at their own risk - No Liability Accepted. 
Spring Tide Safaris will always provide you with an opportunity to fish with the head guide ( Kaleem Micky Qaiser) who has extensive knowledge of the territory waters and fish habitats. Feel free to contact us for further information on charter availability. 
The Boat 
*6.5 metre (20") Plate customised Barra fishing boat 
*raised front casting platform 
*175 HP 4 stroke Yamaha outboard 
*340L (72 US Gal) under floor fuel storage 
*state of the art navigational equipment 
*SIMRAD, Sounder, GPS 
*VHF radio and EPIRB 
*swivel seats 
*retractable drink holders 
*kill tank, live bait tank 
*retractable sliding canopy
