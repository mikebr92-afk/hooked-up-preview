Fishing Charters Merimbula
List of Fishing Charters throughout Merimbula
Reel Affair Fishing Charters – 1800 233 247
Can carry up to 12 passengers up to 30 nautical miles offshore. Purpose-built 38-foot fly bridge Mariner Cruiser with twin diesel engines fitted with the latest electronics and fish finding equipment. Ideal for Game, Reef, Deep-Sea fishing and Whale Watching.
Headland Fishing Charter – (02) 6495 1134
Headland Charters has 17 years experience in local and surrounding waters. Charters include Deep Sea Fishing (Trumpeter, Ling, Morwong, Snapper, Shark), Game Fishing (Black, Blue, Stripped Marlin, Yellow fin tuna & Albacore), Shark Fishing & King fishing (Mako, Thresher. Hammerhead)
Departure Times
Deep Sea Fishing – 7am till 4pm
Game Fishing – 7am till 4 pm
King fish & Shark Fishing – 7am till 4 pm
departs from the public jetty opposite Lakeview Hotel Merimbula.
Rathlin II Fishing Charters – 0428 489 229
State of the art fish finding equipment including chirp sounder technology – the best for finding fish – with a 3kW transducer. Crew have been commercially fishing for 25 years so you are in good hands.
The boat is a 41ft steber, custom built from the ground up for maximum space for fishing.
Fishpen Charters Merimbula – 0404 024 399 
Family owned and operated for 23 years. Five Hour Reef and Three Hour Family Fishing trips. Can arrange private charters for groups of up to ten people. These can vary in duration from two hours up to a full day charter.
Trips departing daily
5 hour reef trips
3 hour reef trips
Family trips
Five Hour Reef and Three Hour Family Fishing trips. Can arrange private charters for groups of up to ten people. These can vary in duration from two hours up to a full day charter.
Merimbula Marina Ocean Adventure Tours – 02 6495 1686 
A brand new boat – a comfortable, high powered 20 passenger eco RIB. Merimbula Marina have adventure tours, fishing charters and discovery cruises leaving everyday.
Game Fish Merimbula’s – 0414 882 639 
Focus on game fishing and love nothing more than chasing Marlin and Tuna.
Aussie Fish Estuary Adventures – 02 6495 9902 
This vessel is a fully customized 6.1 meter-fishing machine. 90 litre Live well (to keep caught fish alive for weighing etc),Four fully cushioned seats for added confort,dual consoles, Larger forward casting deck (this will ensure that there is plenty of room for anglers to fish on the front of the boat. Specially designed for casting lures and fly fishing). Full carpeted decks (comfortable on the soles of the feet).
