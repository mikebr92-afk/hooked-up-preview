Barra Brain / Launching later this year
Fifteen years of barra, in your pocket.
Barra Brain is everything Watty has worked out guiding the Top End — how to read the water, when to go, what to throw, and what to do once the fish is alongside. One app, no fluff.
Register for first access &darr; 
What it is
The thinking behind the fishing
Most barra content shows you the catch. This is the part before it — the decisions a guide makes about where to be, and when, and why. Get that right and the fish look after themselves.
Where & When
Reading a system through the year: the run-off, the dry, the build-up, and what each one changes about where fish sit.
Wind & Weather
What actually matters on the day, what to ignore, and when to move rather than wait it out.
Time & Tide
Which tides move fish and which ones waste a day. The timing Watty plans his own trips around.
Techniques & Tactics
Working structure, bait and current — lure choice, retrieve and where to put the cast.
Those four are the framework. You can read the short version free in The Four Pillars — Barra Brain is where they get worked through properly, with real sessions as the examples.
What's in it
Six parts, built from the way a trip actually runs
Nearly forty lessons, recorded and written up over years of guiding — including the whole Top End Barra Secrets program, which is now folded in and won't be sold separately again.
01
Getting your head right
What the Top End is actually like to fish, and the expectations worth having before you go.
02
Finding barra
Reading country and water, and narrowing a big system down to the few spots worth a cast.
03
Techniques & tactics
How to fish the spot once you're on it — approach, presentation, and when to move on.
04
Lures & tackle
What Watty runs and why, plus the gear that quietly costs people fish.
05
Before & after the catch
Hooking, landing, handling and release — the bit most people learn the hard way.
06
Bonus lessons
The odds and ends that don't fit anywhere else but are worth knowing.
Who it's for
Anyone who can't get up here as often as they'd like
Trip once a year
You get a week up north and you want to make it count rather than spend three days working the place out.
Locals with a boat
You fish the Top End regularly and want to stop repeating the same average sessions.
Coming on a safari
Turn up already understanding what we're doing and why. You'll get far more out of the week.
Already bought Top End Barra Secrets? You keep your access. Nothing to do — it moves across with you.
