The Top End Barra Tides Cheat Sheet
Every Darwin tide for 2027, colour-coded so you can see the good ones coming.
Tides decide more about a Top End barra trip than gear ever will. This is the planner I use to line up the best sessions of the year — so you rock up when the fish are on, not when they're not.
What's on the sheet
• Every Darwin high and low for 2027 — times and heights, month by month
• Each tide cycle colour-coded: neap , building and waning 
• Moon phases marked, so you can see the big tides coming weeks out
