Hooked On 1770 Offshore Fishing Charters - Our Vessel 
Hooked On 1770 
Offshore Fishing Charters 
Our Vessel 
Hooked on 2 is a 33ft (9.7m) Clayton Gallant fibreglass monohull vessel, built to survey in 2004. It is powered by a 450hp Volvo Penta Diesel engine. This fast, comfortable boat provides a dry ride with seating both inside the cabin, and at the rear of the boat.
The fishing area is fully undercover, providing invaluable protection from the sun and weather.
Hooked on 2 is equipped with the latest electronics, live bait tank and large ice boxes for your catch. It also has a custom built hydraulic anchor winch, which makes repositioning of the boat to fresh fishing spots a fast and efficient exercise.
Hooked on 1770 Adventure Tours limits charters to a maximum of 7 fishermen, to ensure enough space is available for everyone to enjoy comfortable fishing and travelling.
There is both fore and aft steering stations to improve your skippers efficiency during the charter.
The boat also features an enclosed toilet, accessed from the fishing area.
