About Us 
Our charters operate mainly in South East Tasmania , from Orford , Dunalley and Eaglehawk Neck, other locations by request . 
Michael Kent has been operating his own charter boat off South East Tasmania for almost 20 years. 
Michael has a superbly equipped and locally built 26 foot Aluminium boat powered by twin 115 HP fourstoke Yamaha outboards, specifically customised for charter fishing. 
With Michael's vast local knowledge, charter clients can catch tiger flathead, striped trumpeter, tuna, ocean perch, jackass morwong, Sand Flathead, cod, leatherjacket, wrasse, barracouta, gurnard, gummy shark, calamari, yellowtail kingfish and snapper (depending on seasons).
