South East Charters offers tailored day or half day fishing or marine animal watching tours departing from Orford, Eaglehawk Neck or other locations by request.
South East Charters operates a 7.5 m twin outboard vessel fully equipped for deep sea, game or estuary fishing, all fishing equipment is supplied.
If you're going marine animal watching, you can expect to see whales, dolphins and seals. Tours operate subject to weather conditions. Meals are not included.

Unique Alpaca Pod
Orielton
$233
This is a hosted stay through Hipcamp, below is the information provided by the Host regarding their offering.
Toffeemont Alpacas Farm Stay is a fully working accredited Alpaca farm located in Orielton 5.6 kilometres from Sorrell on the way to the sunny east coast. 
The Paca pod offers a cosy space for 2 tastefully designed with Peruvian decor. Furnishings are created from alpaca fibre as well. The Pod has a comfortable queen bed, a continental breakfast for two, a private deck with a BBQ and an open fire pit (weather and restrictions permitting) for use. A separate private bathroom with access to a guest laundry completes the amenities. 
This is an interactive farm with regular Alpaca tours running. Complete your luxury stay with an add ons.

Accommodation 
Toffeemont Alpacas
Orielton
$193
This is a hosted stay through Hipcamp, below is the information provided by the Host regarding their offering.
This isn't just a place to sleep, it's a proper farm experience. If time allows, they'll take you out for a quick 20-minute alpaca feed. Want the full experience? Book a guided farm tour with at least 24 hours' notice and get up close with the whole herd. Before you head home, pop into the farm shop for cosy hand-dyed alpaca yarn and knits made from our very own herd (perfect souvenirs, just saying).
Sleeps: 2 adults, 1 queen bed. Bathroom: Your own private bathroom (not shared with day visitors!) in the amenity building, just a short stroll up a gentle ramp from the pod. Kitchen bits: No full kitchen, but you'll find a microwave, kettle, toaster, fridge, and all the plates, bowls, glasses, mugs, cutlery and utensils you'll need. Cooking: Fire up the BBQ on your veranda and enjoy dinner with a side of alpaca views. 
Small on space, big on charm, and seriously heavy on fluff therapy. Come for the alpacas, stay for the alpacas.

Enjoy a true country escape at Toffeemont Alpacas Farm Stay, a working alpaca farm in Orielton, just 5.6 km from Sorell on the way to Tasmania's sunny east coast. 
The cosy Paca Pod for two offers a queen bed, light breakfast, private deck with BBQ and fire pit (weather permitting), private separate bathroom (detached) guest laundry, and plenty of peaceful farm views with friendly alpacas to meet.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Tasmanian Aboriginal people and their enduring custodianship of Lutruwita / Tasmania.
We honour the uninterrupted care, protection and belonging to these islands, skies and waterways, before the invasion and colonisation of European settlement.
As part of a tourism industry that welcomes visitors to these lands, we acknowledge our responsibility to represent to our visitors Tasmania's deep and complex history, fully, respectfully and truthfully.
We acknowledge the Aboriginal people who continue to care for this country today.
We pay our respects to their Elders, past and present.
We honour their stories, songs, art, and culture, and their aspirations for the future of their people and these lands.
