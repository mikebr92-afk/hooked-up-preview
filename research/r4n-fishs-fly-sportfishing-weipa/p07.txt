GETTING TO WEIPA IS EASY. 
With daily flights to Weipa from Cairns and linking with most of our major cities, Weipa is one of the most accessible remote locations in Australia. 
Let us know your flight details and we'll happily arrange a transfer from Weipa airport to your accommodation, and return, so you don't have to worry.
