basic stuff about our 6.5hr - SHARED sydney fishing charters trips...
days of operation
We operate Fishing charters (7) DAYS per week from Cronulla. 
Our LOCATION
Cronulla Public Wharf is approx 20min via road from Sydney Airport and there is plenty of non timed parking along Tonkin Street which is an easy stroll from the wharf. For those that wish to use public transfer, Cronulla Train Station is approx 200m away -> [CLICK HERE FOR GOOGLE MAPS DIRECTIONS] 
Car PARKING
There is plenty of free untimed street parking available along Tonkin Street, where the wharf is located. Once parked, it’s just a short 100-metre walk down to the wharf where the Bundeena Ferry departs. 
SHARED Charters for Individual Bookings
We run shared 6.5-hour deep sea fishing charters every Saturday and Sunday from 5:30am to 12:00pm, with individual seats available — perfect for solo anglers or small groups of 1-15 guests. Price: $260 per person -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
PRIVATE Charters for Group Bookings
We run a range of adventure-packed private fishing charters to suit most budgets, starting from $1,440 for up to 8 guests. We offer 4-hour, 5-hour, and 6.5-hour charters with a variety of departure and return times available. -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
Offshore V's Deep Sea Charters.. which is Better ??
Both charters use very similar rods, reels, line classes, and baits, and both are deep sea fishing trips targeting the same offshore species in the open ocean with Sydney’s leading offshore fishing charter. The main difference is how far we can travel — the shorter 4 to 5-hour charters naturally limit travel distance compared to the 6.5-hour trips, which allows for more time to reach wider grounds and fish longer. Both charters consistently catch plenty of quality fish, but if you’re curious about the difference… maybe try both and see for yourself. 
How Many people can go on Fishing Charter
All SHARED Deep sea charters are limited to a maximum of 15 passengers, and for PRIVATE Deep sea, Offshore, and Mahi Mahi charters you can come alone or bring a group of friends. You’ll need to choose a boat that suits your group size, as each boat has its own maximum capacity: ReelFisher (max 8), Nitro (max 10), BlueCat (max 15). 
Which Boat to Choose
All boats used across our range of Sydney fishing charters are modern, clean, and safe, with the main difference being the size of each boat and its passenger capacity. Start by working out how many guests you have, then choose the boat that best suits your adventure -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
MORNING Fishing Charter TIMES & AVAILABILITY
We offer a range of start times and charter lengths for Deep Sea and Mahi Mahi fishing trips, including (5:30am–12:00pm), (7:00am–12:00pm), and (8:00am–12:00pm). For custom charters, please contact us and we’ll provide a tailored quote 
AFTERNOON Fishing Charter TIMES & AVAILABILITY
WE can arrange Afternoon Charter on WEEKDAYS ONLY... If you wish to arrange an Afternoon private fishing charters in Sydney, please contact us with and we can provide you with a custom QUOTE..... 
ONLINE Availably and Pricing - Accuracy
We can confirm that all availability calendars and pricing on our website are live, fully up to date, and accurate in real time. You can book your fishing trip with confidence directly through our online calendars today. 
Age Limits
SHARED fishing charters and MAHI MAHI fishing charters have a minimum age of 12 years old, while PRIVATE Deep Sea and Offshore charters have no age limit. 
Areas we service in Sydney
Based in Cronulla, a suburb of the Sutherland Shire, we welcome happy customers every week from all over Sydney, as well as many interstate visitors, overseas guests, and tourists. 
How much does a fishing charter cost in Sydney?
Shared deep sea trips are $260 per person | 4hr offshore fishing trips range from $1,440–$1,760 | 5hr offshore fishing trips range from $1,560–$1,800 | 6.5hr deep sea fishing charters range from $1,760–$3,380 | 6.5hr Mahi Mahi fishing charters range from $2,200–$2,950. 
Is there a SECURITY DEPOSIT Required
No initial deposit is required to secure your Sydney fishing trip booking, however a credit or debit card must be held on file. This card will only be used in accordance with our Cancellation & Damage Policy, available on our website -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
When is FULL PAYMENT Required
Two (2) days before your Sydney fishing charter, we’ll contact you to confirm how you’d like to settle your account. At that time, you can choose cash at the wharf, EFT, or credit card — whatever is easiest for you -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
Frequently Asked Questions - FAQ's
Got a question? Check out our Frequently Asked Questions page — chances are your question has already been asked by others about their Sydney fishing trips -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
Our CANCELLATION & DAMAGE POLICY explained
Please be aware of our cancellation and damage policy for all PRIVATE and SHARED Deep Sea and MAHI MAHI fishing trips in Sydney (Cronulla). If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before booking online -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
When will fishing charter be called - ON or Off ?
For all fishing charters with us, we will contact you by TEXT and EMAIL between [4:30pm and 5:30pm] the DAY BEFORE your charter to confirm whether your SHARED or PRIVATE trip is (going ahead) or has been (cancelled) due to weather -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
If Charter Cancelled Due to 'BAD Weather' ?
If your charter is cancelled due to POOR WEATHER, you can choose to RESCHEDULE. If you are (unable to reschedule), ZERO payment will be required or taken from your card -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
WHAT HAPPENS IF IT RAINS
Light rain is not a problem, as we have undercover enclosed areas to keep you warm and protected. We recommend bringing a spray jacket if rain is forecast. However, if the forecast includes heavy rain, strong winds, or large seas, we will not go out for safety reasons and you will be able to rebook for another day. 
Can you Change the number of passengers for a booking
On PRIVATE fishing charters, yes — you can change passenger numbers on your booking. |Step 1| secure your boat and date so you can invite your friends. |Step 2| let us know your final numbers 24–48 hours before the charter. For SHARED charters, passenger numbers cannot be changed....you are only able to book 'more seats' if they are available ONLINE... 
What fish can you catch on a Sydney fishing charter?
Target species on our DEEP SEA and OFFSHORE Sydney fishing charters include snapper, morwong, pigfish, flathead, bonito, kingfish, trag, salmon, and many more. Both deep sea and offshore trips are focused on putting fish in your esky to take home for dinner. On MAHI MAHI trips, the main target species are mahi mahi and marlin -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
Summer Fishing (November - April)
As the warmer water hits Sydney fish species such as Kingfish, Aust Salmon, Bonito, Mahi Mahi, Marlin all start to become active as well as the all year species such as Snapper, Morwong, Perch, Flathead. etc. Fishing during summer is generally a mixture of trolling lurers, fishing with live bait and bottom bashing with pilchards on the reef and sand -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
Winter Fishing (May - October)
The colder months are really good for REEF fishing... the Snapper, Morwong, Perch, Pigfish, Trag all become active as does the Flathead on the sand -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO] 
Fish Sizes + Bag Limits
Current (NSW Fisheries) rules for sizes and bag limits can be found here -> [CLICK FOR MORE INFO] 
Fishing Techniques
Depending on conditions and the time of year, our fishing charter crew in Sydney will use a combination of techniques including dead baits (pilchard and squid), trolling lures, and jigging at certain times. 
Fishing EQUIPMENT
On every Fishing Charter run, ALL Fishing equipment including - Rods, Bait, Licences, Safety Gear, etc, are all included. All guests only need to turn up with their required Food and Drinks for day and appropriate clothing for the time of year. 
No Fishing Experience is Required
No experience is required for any of our fishing charter trips. Every deep sea, offshore, and Mahi Mahi Sydney fishing charter is led by experienced skippers who have been fishing the waters off Sydney for over 20 years. 
BYO Drinks
All fishing charters are BYO drinks... If you wish to bring Alcohol no worries although please bring CANS…. NOT BOTTLES... Please feel free to bring a small drinks Esky onboard that you can also use to put your fish in at the end of the charter. 
CAN YOU KEEP YOUR FISH
Yes, you get to keep what you catch, and we’ll gut, clean, and bag it for you to take home. However, we encourage you to only take what you plan to eat so we can help protect our oceans for future generations. 
DO WE CLEAN FISH FOR YOU ?
The crew will gut, scale, and bag your fish for you to take home. However, we do not fillet fish — all fish must be brought back to the wharf with head and tail attached so Fisheries can measure them if required. 
WHAT TO BRING
All fishing equipment is supplied, but you’re welcome to bring your own favourite rod or bait if you prefer. Please bring your own food and drinks, and wear appropriate clothing for the time of year. As a guide, we recommend a hat, sunglasses, a warm jumper (just in case), and wet weather gear if rain is forecast. You’re also welcome to bring a small esky onboard for your fish at the end of the charter. 
MOTION / Sea SICKNESS
You’ll be out on the open ocean, so it’s always best to come prepared. We recommend visiting your chemist or doctor for seasickness tablets or remedies. Options include Travelcalm, Kwells, or natural remedies like ginger. Make sure you choose NON-DROWSY tablets.

We offer fishing gift vouchers to suit all budgets and experience levels. Our basic shared fishing charter voucher is $260 per person. For private fishing charters in Sydney, prices start from $1,440 for up to 8 guests and go up to $3,380 for up to 15 guests -> [CLICK HERE FOR MORE INFO]
