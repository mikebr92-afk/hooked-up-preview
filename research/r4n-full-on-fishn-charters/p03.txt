Got questions? Full On Fish’n Charters has you covered! 
With 30+ years of local expertise, we offer tailored fishing adventures in the Hinchinbrook Channel. From day trips to all-inclusive packages, we handle everything—just cast a line and enjoy the thrill!
Got a question that’s not answered below? Head over to our contact page, and Nathan will get back to you – faster than a barra hitting a lure.
What kind of boat will we be fishing on? 
You’ll be fishing from a custom-built 6-metre vessel , equipped with the latest state-of-the-art Garmin electronics . It comfortably accommodates up to six anglers , ensuring a safe and top-tier fishing experience .
Can beginners join a fishing charter? 
Absolutely! Whether you’re an experienced angler or a first-time fisher , we tailor the trip to your skill level to ensure an exciting and rewarding experience .
What should I bring on my fishing trip? 
• Sunglasses 
• Large hat 
• Camera (for those brag-worthy catches!)
• Sun-smart clothing 
• Sunscreen 
• Lunch and refreshments (for day trips)
• BYO alcohol (Please drink responsibly)
What time do we start and finish? 
There are no set start or return times . We go when the fishing is best , and we stay until you’ve had enough —it’s all about making sure you get the most out of your trip!
Is there a bag limit on your charters? 
We are a responsible charter business and, whilst enjoying the environment we live and work in, we also realise that it is our responsibility to look after it as well. 
Reasonable and maintainable bag limits will be enforced by the charter operator, Nathan, who will discuss this with you on your charter.
Can I catch Barramundi all year round? 
Queensland Barramundi closed season is between 1st November and 1st February making them off limits. All other species are available to be caught.
Do you provide transport? 
As part of our four day packages, we offer return transfers to/from Townsville Airport for those arriving by plane. For all other packages, give Nathan a call on 0429 332 566 to discuss your requirements.
If you’re driving yourself, just head to the Dungeness boat ramp in Lucinda for your departure.
Where do we stay for multi-day charters? 
Lucinda offers a wide range of accommodation options for visitors, ranging from caravan parks through to self-contained cabins.
Click to view accommodation options on our Packages page. 
Is all fishing gear provided? 
Yes! We supply all rods, reels, bait, ice, and tackle —so you just need to bring yourself!
What types of fish can we catch? 
Depending on the season and location, you can target species like:
• Barramundi
• Mangrove Jack
• Threadfin Salmon
• Finger Mark
• Trevally
• Queenfish
Do you provide filleting services? 
Depending on the charter, Nathan will happily provide filleting services – please discuss with Nathan before or during your charter.
Ready to wet a line with us?
Don’t just dream about landing a trophy catch—make it a reality!
Get in touch and secure your place for an epic day on the water.
