Pearl Tours 
Broome is the home of the South Sea Pearl, the finest in the world. Take a visit to a pearl farm at Willie Creek or Cygnet Bay by helicopter, aircraft, bus or self-drive. Alternatively take one of the tours in Chinatown to learn about the history of Broome and the art of culturing pearls. The finest pearl jewellery is on display in the many pearl boutiques and shops around Chinatown.
Call us for personalised service from the Broome Visitor Centre team on +61 8 9195 2200 
“ The pearl is the queen of gems, and the gem of queens
- Grace Kelly
Did you know??
Australia’s pristine oceanic conditions are acknowledged as the source of the world’s finest and largest pearls. 
See how cultured pearls are farmed on a pearl farm tour!
Whether you're looking to purchase a pearl, discover the intriguing history of Broome's Pearling industry or learn about the operations of a Pearl Farm, we have you covered. Can't make it up to a pearl farm? Ask us about local pearling experiences in Broome's Chinatown and our museums and historical locations are a great place to learn more!
