About
There is no better place on earth to catch wild brown trout, this is fishing mecca, where the conditions can be challenging one day and exceptional the next. Our guided fly fishing trips are tailored for you. We live and breathe Tasmania’s trout waters, and have a truly world class team with international fly fishing experience.
Based at Miena Village in the Central Highlands, we can access over a thousand lakes and streams. That’s right – we live in paradise. Fishing the Central Plateau can include airport pickups and accommodation options. Stalking trout in our pristine lakes, overnight wilderness experiences, river fishing and use of our many boats are all within your reach, whatever the experience.
Catering for individuals or groups we plan an enjoyable experience taking into account your fishing capability, what’s biting, the seasonality, what the weather is doing and where you want to be based. This is your experience – we want it to be memorable and no two days are the same. We also provide lowland fishing from Hobart and Launceston, we plan fishing tuition and casting lessons as well as overseas adventures with our valued clients. No deposit is required, but bookings are subject to availability.
