About
Welcome to Tasmania Fly Fishing. We offer professional guided fly fishing experiences across Tasmania, from Launceston and Northern Tasmania to the Central Highlands, East Coast, North West Coast, Hobart and Southern Tasmania, as well as the beautiful Currawong Lakes Private Fishery.
Whether you’re picking up a fly rod for the first time or you’re an experienced angler looking to explore Tasmania’s renowned trout waters, our team can tailor an experience to suit you. Choose from half-day, ¾-day and full-day tours, with multi-day fishing experiences also available.
Our guided experiences can include river and lake fishing, wading and boat-based options depending on location, conditions and your preferences. Quality fly fishing equipment and tuition can be provided, making our tours equally suitable for beginners wanting to learn and experienced anglers wanting to make the most of their time in Tasmania.
We can also cater for larger groups, corporate outings and customised fishing experiences, drawing on our team of guides to create an enjoyable time on the water.
Ready to go fly fishing in Tasmania? Contact our friendly team on 0407 334 415 and we’ll help you plan an experience to suit you.

The Team
Scott has been guiding fly fishers in Tasmania since 2005 and brings more than two decades of guiding, coaching and fly fishing experience to Tasmania Fly Fishing.
Tasmania Fly Fishing also works with a carefully selected team of experienced guides across the state. We match guides to each booking based on location, fishing style, experience level and individual requirements, allowing us to cater for everything from first-time fly fishers through to experienced anglers, individuals and larger groups.
Our focus is simple: putting the right guide with the right client to create a quality Tasmanian fly fishing experience.
