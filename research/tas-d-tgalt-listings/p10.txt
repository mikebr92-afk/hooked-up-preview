About
Experience fly fishing heaven in Tasmania with a unique Trout Tales guided tour.
Offering a range of single and multi-day tours across the state for anglers of all experience levels, Trout Tales packages capture what makes Tasmania so special as a trout fishery. From sight fishing with a single dry fly to large rising trout in crystal clear highland lakes to wandering up small streams and rivers casting to trout feeding on large terrestrial insects, Trout Tales guides aim to show you why Tasmania is considered to be one of the best fly-fishing destinations in the world.
The diversity of fly-fishing options in Tasmania make it the perfect place to spend a few days, exploring a range of lakes, lagoons, rivers and streams. Let Trout Tales organize everything for you, with all-inclusive guiding, accommodation, and food packages of up to a weeklong available.
Trout Tales packages also provide the opportunity to sample some of Tasmania’s world class produce, with lunch packs featuring expertly prepared local ingredients. Our accommodation providers also take pride in using fine local meats, seafood, cheeses, and other delicacies for you to try during your stay.
