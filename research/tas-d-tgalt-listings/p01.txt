About
TAILORED to YOUR NEEDS 
That’s Red Tag Trout Tours promise to you.
Red Tag Trout Tours has been operated for 25+ years by Owner/Operator Roger Butler who has been fly fishing Tasmania’s wild trout rivers, streams and lakes for over 50 years.
In 2021 Ray Curran joined Red Tag as House Guide with over 30 years of fly fishing experience
Red Tag Trout Tours conducts all-inclusive guided wild trout, fly fishing tours and introductory fly fishing workshops.
From one day to extended, accommodated packages, everything is included -even the evening’s relaxing drink over dinner!
Red Tag’s Point of Difference! We are a Tasmanian specialist rivers & streams fly fishing operation for wild trout enthusiasts.
We offer a personal service, so your experience starts from the moment you make contact with us.
The people who plan your trip are the guides who deliver the product.
• Guided fly fishing tours on Tasmania’s finest fly fishing rivers, streams, lakes.
• Fly fishing tuition – Red Tag’s KOCPIT theory
• Options from one-day to extended tours, workshops
• Gear and equipment assistance, sales and advice.
Red Tag is accredited with industry, TGALT Inc., and tourism (TICT)
Roger is a Life Member of TGALT.

Red Tag Trout Tours was originally established and operated for 25+ years by Owner/Operator Roger Butler who has been fly fishing Tasmania’s wild trout rivers, streams and lakes for over 50 years.
Ray Curran joined Red Tag in 2021 as House Guide and, on Roger’s retirement at the end of the 2022-23 season Ray has become the new owner operator of Red Tag Trout Tours. Ray has over 30 years of fly fishing experience and over 45 years overall fishing Tasmanian trout waters.
