marina gem
Experience the perfect getaway at Marina Gem, a charming accommodation nestled near the vibrant D’Albora Marina in beautiful Nelson Bay. This inviting retreat offers guests easy access to stunning waterfront views and an array of recreational activities, including boating, fishing, and water sports. With its modern amenities and comfortable living spaces, Marina Gem is ideal for couples seeking a romantic escape or families looking for adventure. Enjoy leisurely strolls along the marina promenade, where you’ll find delightful cafes, boutique shops, and local attractions just moments away
After a day of exploring Port Stephens’ pristine beaches or embarking on unforgettable fishing charters with Pacific Blue Charters, return to your cozy haven at Marina Gem for relaxation. Whether you’re sipping coffee on your private balcony or unwinding by the poolside after an exciting day out, this hidden gem promises an exceptional stay that will leave you longing to return
