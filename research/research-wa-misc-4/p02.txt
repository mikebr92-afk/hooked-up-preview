TITLE: Catalpa Charters | Tour | Rockingham | Destination Perth
DESCRIPTION: Catalpa Charters is more than a business - it’s a family legacy. Their connection to Rockingham situated along the coast 40 minutes from Perth Western Australia runs deep, interwoven with decades of local and maritime history. From the fishing industry to the rescue missions that have helped shape our identities, the name “Catalpa” resonates with stories of courage and heritage. Generations of family have sailed these waters, cultivating a profound understanding of the region and a passion for preserving its natural beauty and marine ecosystem. Catalpa Charters' vision is rooted in creating a platform where people can connect with the ocean’s endless beauty while appreciating the ecological importance. Experience the magic of Perth’s pristine coastline with thrilling Western Rock Lobster catches, to spectacular sunset cruises, and private charters designed for family and friends to create lasting memories as the Catalpa Charters crew bring everyday ocean adventures to everyone. Western Rock Lobster Catch and Keep Tour: Step into the shoes of a fisherman and experience the thrill of a three-hour Rock Lobster- catch and keep tour. Take home a share of the catch up to eight Western Rock Lobster. With years of experience in Rockingham’s fishing industry, our team guides you through the process of identifying, catching and appreciating this prized crustacean. Beyond the excitement, these tours provide a fascinating glimpse into the sustainable best practices we use to preserve marine life for future generations. Sunset Cruises: Imagine embarking on a three-hour sunset cruise aboard Venture 111 relaxing with family and friends, taking in the sweeping views of Western Australia’s turquoise coastline as we escape the ordinary for an extraordinary afternoon with Catalpa Charters. Watching the sun dip beyond the horizon, painting the sky a cocktail of colours and casting hues across the Sound towards the causeway and Garden Island. Where every wave tells a story unveiling the tapestry of rich maritime history and natural marine beauty. The sea breeze directs our course beyond the shores to a gateway of adventure and twilight to remember. Private Charters: Escape with catalpa Charters and create a three-hour Indian Ocean Adventure. Let us navigate a memorable day on the ocean for your next social event, work function or celebrating with family and friends while exploring Western Australia’s iconic waterways as we cruise along Rockingham’s spectacular coast. Cockburn Sound Regatta: The Cockburn Sound Regatta has historically been one of Western Australia’s premier events and, with it’s sheltered waters, stunning beaches and proximity to Fremantle and the metropolitan area, Hillary’s and Mandurah, it’s easy to see why it has become such an iconic event and priority on every sailor’s calendar. From start to finish their four-hour tour follows a tapestry of bright sails that fill the bay amongst the thrills of the race and Rockingham’s spectacular backdrop.
