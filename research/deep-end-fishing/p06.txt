9 Hour Reef Fishing Charter
SUNSHINE COAST REEF FISHING TRIPS OFF MOOLOOLABA 
CALL US TODAY TO BOOK YOUR FISHING CHARTER – 0499 044 458 
Our Sunshine Coast 9 hour Reef & Light Game Fishing trip is our most popular trip. We take you to the best offshore fishing grounds in the deep water 50+ metres where you can expect to catch some of the best fish the Sunshine Coast has to offer. This trip is for the experienced fisherman who is wanting a fantastic day’s fishing, which may include a combination of Reef Fishing & Light Game. This fish we will be targeting are Snapper, Pearl Perch, Tuskfish, Parrotfish, Cod, Coral Trout, Jewfish, Emperor, Sweetlip, Kingfish, Amberjack, Spanish Mackerel, Dolphin Fish (Mahi Mahi), Wahoo, Cobia, Marlin, along with many other local species found off the Sunshine Coast .
Includes Drinks & Light Refreshments. BYO rod welcome
