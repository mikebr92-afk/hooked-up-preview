Deep End Fishing Charters
THE BEST FISHING ADVENTURE YOU’LL HAVE ON THE SUNSHINE COAST 
CALL US TODAY TO BOOK YOUR FISHING CHARTER – 0499 044 458 
A FISHING EXPERIENCE TO REMEMBER
Taking a fishing charter on the Sunshine Coast with us you will be taken to the best fishing grounds for the catch of a lifetime. – It’s more than ‘just fishing’ – you’ll discover the scenic splendour of the Pacific Ocean as you fish off-shore from our stable twin-hull sea fishing boat. At different times of the year you may also spot dolphins, whales & sometimes even sharks adding to the excitement of your trip.
Passenger Numbers – The vessel carries a maximum of 7 passengers per trip so you’ll enjoy an exclusive fishing experience with full support from our friendly and professional crew.
Private Charter Hire with The Deep End Crew is available for game fishing, special occasions or corporate fishing trips.
ALL YOUR FISHING GEAR IS INCLUDED: 
• Quality Rods & Reels
• Tackle – hooks, line, sinkers & lures
• Bait & ice
• Trip fishing licenses
• Refreshments
• Fish cleaning & bagging
• Instruction & advice (if required)
Glen Hayden is the owner and skipper of Deep End Fishing Charters. Glen’s passion and experience with fishing is second to none and you’re assured of a first-class experience on all of our tours. 
Join us aboard MV Gunbarrel, 9 meter Marlin Broadbill Charter Vessel Power Cat, powered by twin 200hp Yamaha engines to provide a fast and stable ride to the fishing grounds from estuary to deep sea. 
Gunbarrel was custom designed Charter Vessel for fishing and Diving with a spacious covered fishing deck and is fully equipped for a quality fishing experience. 
Carrying a maximum of seven passengers, you won’t feel over crowded and can enjoy a private fishing tour with plenty of space to fish and relax. 
GLEN HAYDEN
