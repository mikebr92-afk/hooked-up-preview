SUNSHINE COAST ESTUARY & RIVER FISHING CHARTERS 
CALL US TODAY TO BOOK YOUR FISHING CHARTER – 0499 044 458 
Targeting all the river species that make the Estuary their home, such as Mangrove Jack, Bream, Flathead, Moses Perch, Cod, and the elusive Sunshine Coast Barramundi, and many other species.
On these trips cold water, soft drink and light snacks are provided.
You are most welcome to bring your own fishing rod, soft plastics & lures to use.
Estuary / River Fishing 
MAX 7 PERSONS. 
On our estuary / River trips we stay in calm waters of the Mooloolah river and the tributaries that run off the main river.
On these trips cold water, soft drink and light snacks are provided.
Cost: 3 hr $80 per person or 5 hr (Price & Availability on request) 
Preferred Fishing Package
5 Hour Offshore Reef Fishing Day Trip 7 Hour Offshore Reef Fishing Day Trip 9 Hour Reef & Light Game Fishing 12 Hour Offshore Reef Fishing Day Trip 8 Hour Night Fishing Trip Estuary or River Fishing 
Please note this is a booking enquiry. We will contact you to confirm availability. 
Terms & Conditions: All trip bookings are subject to sea & weather conditions.
GENERAL INFORMATION
All trips depart from and return to Mooloolaba Marina on the Sunshine Coast.
Advance booking requires a deposit of 50% to secure your date.
Should your trip be cancelled due to sea and weather conditions and is not rescheduled your deposit is fully refunded.
