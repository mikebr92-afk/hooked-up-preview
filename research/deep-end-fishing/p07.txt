12 Hour Reef & Light Game
MOOLOOLABA SUNSHINE COAST REEF FISHING TRIPS 
CALL US TODAY TO BOOK YOUR FISHING CHARTER – 0499 044 458 
Our 12 hour Reef & Light Game Fishing Trip is the ultimate fishing experience. We take you to the best offshore fishing grounds in the deeper water 50+ metres where we can put you in the hot seat and experience a truly unique fishing adventure which man include a combination of:
• Reef Fishing
• Deep Sea
• Light Game
We monsters we will be targeting Snapper, Pearl Perch, Tuskfish, Cod, Jewfish, Emperor, Sweetlip, Kingfish, Anberjack, Spanish Mackarel, Wahoo, Dophinfish (Mahi Mahi), Cobia, Marlin, along with many other species commonly found off Mooloolaba and the Sunshine Coast.
Morning and Afternoon trips are both available (weather permitting)
Includes Drinks & Lunch BYO Rod welcome
12 Hour – Reef & Light Game Fishing Trip 
MAX 7 PERSONS – ON A SHARED CHARTER. 
Includes use of rods, tackle, bait, trip license & light refreshments
Cost: $320 per person 
Departs 5/6am
Preferred Fishing Package
5 Hour Offshore Reef Fishing Day Trip 7 Hour Offshore Reef Fishing Day Trip 9 Hour Reef & Light Game Fishing 12 Hour Offshore Reef Fishing Day Trip 8 Hour Night Fishing Trip Estuary or River Fishing 
Please note this is a booking enquiry. We will contact you to confirm availability. 
Terms & Conditions: All trip bookings are subject to sea & weather conditions.
GENERAL INFORMATION
All trips depart from and return to Mooloolaba Marina, Sunshine Coast.
Advance booking requires a deposit of 50% to secure your date.
Should your trip be cancelled due to sea and weather conditions and is not rescheduled your deposit is fully refunded.
