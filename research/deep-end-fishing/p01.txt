DEEP END FISHING CHARTERS 
Anytime of the year is great for deep sea fishing in Mooloolaba, Sunshine Coast. You’ll find a variety of fish species on our Half-Day Deep Sea Reef Fishing trips taking you up to 6 nautical miles offshore, or take a Full-Day Deep Sea Fishing Tour and spend more time fishing the deep sea reefs or head out to deeper waters to target light game (if prescheduled). Night fishing trips also available.
