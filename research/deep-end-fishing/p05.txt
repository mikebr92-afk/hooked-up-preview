7 Hour Reef Fishing Charter
MOOLOOLABA SUNSHINE COAST FISHING TRIPS & CHARTERS 
CALL US TODAY TO BOOK YOUR FISHING CHARTER – 0499 044 458 
Our 7 hour Sunshine Coast reef fishing trip we head to the deeper reefs of the Sunshine Coast, fishing in 50m of water. These deeper waters produce good quality fish all year round. This is for the fisherman who just wants a little bit more and to experience the hard fight of a bigger fish.
We will be targeting Snapper, Pearl Perch , Tuskfish, Coral Trout, Cod, Moses, Perch, Jewfish, Trag Jew, Emperor and Sweetlip. Also Kingfish, Amberjack, Dophin Fish (Mahi Mahi), Spanish Mackerel, Wahoo, Cobia, along with many other species.
Includes Drinks & Light Refreshments BYO Rod welcome
Visit the Sunshine Coast for Mooloolaba’s best deep water reef fishing charters.
