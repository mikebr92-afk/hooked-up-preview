Estuary / River Fishing 
MAX 7 PERSONS – ON A SHARED CHARTER. 
On our estuary / River trips we stay in calm waters near the river mouth where the ocean meets the river to the tributaries that run off the main river.
On these trips cold water, soft drink and light snacks are provided.
Cost: 3 hr $80 per person or 5 hr (Price and availability on request)

GENERAL INFORMATION
All trips depart from and return to Mooloolaba Marina on the Sunshine Coast.
Advance booking requires a deposit of 50% to secure your date. Should your trip be cancelled due to sea and weather conditions and is not rescheduled your deposit is fully refunded.
