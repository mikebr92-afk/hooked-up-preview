Night Fishing
SUNSHINE COAST NIGHT REEF AND ESTUARY FISHING TRIPS & CHARTERS 
CALL US TODAY TO BOOK YOUR FISHING CHARTER – 0499 044 458 
This is the best of the best at night when the real big ones come out to feed. If you want your arms stretched or you love fishing and looking for that most memorable fishing trip, and a great night leaving you wanting more, this trip is for you. This trip is only bottom-bashing, targeting all the night predators including Snapper, Pearl Perch, Cod, Tuskfish, Moses Perch, Emperor, Sweetlip, Jewfish, and many other popular species found near the Sunshine Coast.
Drinks & Lunch included. BYO rod welcome.
Night Fishing 
8 Hour – Night Fishing Trip 
MAX 7 PERSONS – ON A SHARED CHARTER. 
Includes use of rods, tackle, bait, trip license & light refreshments
Cost: $250 per person

Terms & Conditions: All trip bookings are subject to sea & weather conditions.
GENERAL INFORMATION
All Sunshine Coast Fishing trips depart from and return to Mooloolaba Marina.
Advance booking requires a deposit of 50% to secure your date.
Should your trip be cancelled due to sea and weather conditions and is not rescheduled your deposit is fully refunded.
