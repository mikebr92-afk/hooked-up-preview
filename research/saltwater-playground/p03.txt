Choose your experience 
Full-day fishing CHARTER 
Join us on a full-day fishing charter in Hervey Bay as we explore the western waters alongside K’gari (Fraser Island), venture through the middle of the bay, and fish the productive southern gutters. Our wide-ranging trips cover a large area to maximize your chances of landing trophy fish.
Whether you’re after reef species or pelagics, our expert guides tailor the experience to give you the best opportunity for a memorable catch in one of Australia’s premier fishing destinations.

breaksea topwater
fishing CHARTER 
Our Topwater fishing charters to Breaksea Spit, Fraser Island are all about raw power and surface-smashing action. We make the run to this remote fishery to target giant trevally and Spanish mackerel , fishing some of the most productive water in the region. Watching these fish explode on topwater lures is pure adrenaline and something every angler needs to experience. With some of the largest giant trevally in the country living in these waters, this is topwater fishing at its absolute best.
