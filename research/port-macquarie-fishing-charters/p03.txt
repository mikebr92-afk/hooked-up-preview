Port Macquarie deep sea Fishing Charters
Come deep sea fishing off beautiful Port Macquarie with Port's most experienced and successful deep sea fishing charter operators Ocean Star.
Locally owned and operated, Port Macquarie Fishing Charters would like to welcome you aboard the Ocean Star, a 40ft custom Randel charter fishing boat.
The Ocean Star is superbly equipped with the latest electronics, comfortable seating and also a private toilet (unlike our competitors).
Deep Sea Fishing off Port Macquarie means we regularly catch Snapper, Dolphin Fish, Pearl Perch, and Kingfish. Come along and enjoy a memorable and rewarding experience.
Some of the wildlife you may see includes dolphins and sea eagles and if you pick the right time of year to come aboard we regularly see the beautiful humpback whales on their migration too.

What's included with your Ocean Star trip?
Brett and the crew look forward to welcoming you aboard the Ocean Star for a truly fun day out for everybody. We depart between 5.00am - 6.00am for a 6 hour trip and the cost is :
Adults $200 - 
Children 10 -16yrs $190- 
Your charter fare includes:- 
* Quality rods and overhead reels.
* All bait and tackle.
* Snacks and drinks
* Expert help and advice.
* Personalised customer service.
* Onboard facilities include a private toilet
* Your catch is cleaned, iced and packed.
* Free parking.
* FREE PICK UP AND RETURN.
(individuals or groups catered for)
All you need to bring is your hat!
Gift vouchers are also available - gift an experience! Suitable for those going for the first time or experienced anglers.
