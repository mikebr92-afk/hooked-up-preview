The crew of the Ocean Star Port Macquarie
Your skipper Brett is a true born and bred Port Macquarie local.
He grew up fishing and surfing the waters of the Mid North Coast so if anyone should know where the fish are biting it is him!
Brett first started working on fishing charters in Port Macquarie back in 1986 and its that experience that will help to make your trip as successful as possible.
So why not come deep sea fishing off beautiful Port Macquarie with Port's most experienced and successful deep sea fishing charter operators Ocean Star.
John with keen fisherman Dr Harry
