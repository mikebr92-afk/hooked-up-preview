Yamba Fishing and Charters are here to cater to all of your deep sea fishing adventures and sightseeing needs.
Glynn Bitton your skipper, is the owner, and operator of Yamba Fishing and Charters and is an experienced and passionate fisherman. Glynn has fished the Yamba, Evans Head and Wooli areas for the last 20 years and will take you on an unforgettable journey in his nine-metre 3100 Noosa Cat with twin Yamaha 250 motors which are more than enough to shoot across to the horizon and fill the esky with fresh fish. 
Yamba Fishing and Charters have more than enough room to accommodate comfortably up to eight persons per trip with pickup locations either in Yamba or Iluka.
All fishing gear is supplied for you which is made up of heavy-duty rods and Penn, Abu Garcia and Shimano fishing reels all fitted with the braid line to ensure you the very best chance of landing your catch. Included also are fishing licenses, bait and a great time.
Individual, Half and Full Day Charter bookings available, contact them for more details on how they can customise the best experience for you.
Remember when you book with Yamba Fishing and Charters you are supporting a small business.
