WHAT TO DO AND BRING 
​ 
• 
Weather Check 
Please phone Glynn by 6 PM the night before your trip to confirm weather conditions and departure time. 
Please arrive 15 minutes before scheduled departure time to allow for completion of passenger manifest and safety brief. 
Glynn's Mobile: 0407 540 038 
Julie's Mobile: 0401 564 052 
​ 
• 
Clothing and Footwear 
Please bring warm/suitable clothing, you can always take layers off, but you can't put it on if you don't have it. Comfortable footwear is required. 
​ 
• 
Sunscreen, sun hats and sunglasses are recommended. 
• 
Food, Drinks and Alcohol 
Please bring your own food & drinks. We have an icebox & refrigerator for your use.
​ ​ 
Alcohol is allowed in moderation but not recommended, please check with your skipper before the trip for more details. 
​ 
Alcohol before your trip is a recipe for sea sickness, even in moderation. If you are unsure about sea sickness, please consult your local pharmacist or doctor. 
• 
Sea Sickness 
Alcohol before your trip is a recipe for sea sickness, even in moderation. If you are unsure about sea sickness, please consult your local pharmacist or doctor. Remember we are going offshore. 
• 
Fish Transportation 
Your fish will be stored on board on ice. It is important for you to transport your fish home on ice, in an esky. PLEASE LEAVE YOUR ESKY IN YOUR CAR . 
You may wish to freeze a bottle of water the night before to keep your esky cool and save having to stop for ice on the way home. 
​ 
For more information or any enquiries please call Julie 0401564052 or Glynn on 0407540038
