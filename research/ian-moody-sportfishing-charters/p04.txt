TITLE: Ian Moody Sportfishing Charters | Tours | Queensland
DESCRIPTION: Ian Moody Sportfishing Charters offers guided inshore and estuary fishing charters around the Hinchinbrook region. 

Specialising in large Barramundi, Threadfin salmon, Fingermark etc. Bookings for 2 minimum to 4 maximum anglers.  All different styles of fishing catered for and all equipment supplied.  

Custom 5.7 metre Barra boat with the latest in electronics and very stable. Plus a custom 7.6m sportfisher for the Great Barrier Reef.
