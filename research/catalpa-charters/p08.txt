Welcome Aboard 
Catalpa Charters is more than a business - it’s a family legacy. Our connection to Rockingham runs deep, interwoven with decades of local history and maritime history. From the fishing industry to the rescue missions that have helped shape our identities, the name “Catalpa” resonates with stories of courage and heritage.
Generations of our family have sailed these waters, cultivating a profound understanding of the region and a passion for preserving its marine ecosystem. Our vision is rooted in creating a platform where people can connect with the ocean's endless beauty while appreciating its ecological importance.
Marine Wildlife & Education 
Rockingham’s marine waters are home to an array of incredible wildlife, from dolphins gliding through the waves to the diverse amount of bird species found nesting in our sanctuary zones and thriving fringing reefs. Our charters will take you up close and personal to this vibrant and unique marine ecosystem, offering opportunities to witness nature’s beauty while learning more about it’s delicate balance above and below the waves.
