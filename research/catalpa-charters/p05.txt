Who we are

Experience the magic of Rockingham's pristine waters with Western Australia's most passionate marine adventure family. From thrilling lobster catches to sunset cruises, we invite you to step aboard and embrace the ocean dream we live every day.

The Catalpa Rescue: A Historical Anchor
Rockingham’s maritime history is tied to the legendary Catalpa Rescue—a tale of bravery and determination that underscores the values we hold dear. This historical event inspires us daily, reminding us of the deep connection between people, the sea, and the stories that shape coastal communities. We bring this spirit to every voyage we undertake, blending history with the thrill of discovery.
Local Knowledge and Generational Expertise
Our foundation lies in the rich tapestry of local knowledge passed down through generations. Having grown up surrounded by the ocean's rhythm, our family knows Rockingham's waters like the back of our hands. This expertise allows us to take guests to hidden gems, ensuring they experience the best of this unique coastal region.
We emphasise the importance of safety and environmental stewardship. By combining industry experience and hands-on knowledge, we create adventures that are not only memorable but also responsible. Our commitment to sanctuary zones and sustainable fishing practices reflects our dedication to preserving the marine environments we cherish.

Marine Wildlife & Education 
Rockingham's waters are home to an array of incredible wildlife, from dolphins gliding through the waves to diverse bird species nesting in sanctuary zones and thriving fringing reefs. Our charters take you up close to this vibrant marine ecosystem, offering opportunities to witness nature's beauty while learning about its delicate balance above and below the waves. 
Education Meets Exploration 
We are passionate about delivering marine education through interactive and fun experiences. Whether identifying marine species or understanding sanctuary zones' significance, our tours encourage curiosity and leave guests with a deeper appreciation for the marine world. 
Nature's Beauty: The Heart of Rockingham 
Rockingham is renowned for its stunning natural scenery, from sparkling turquoise waters to pristine beaches. It is a sanctuary for wildlife, offering a window into the ecological richness of Western Australia's coastline. Every charter is an opportunity to bask in this beauty, immersing yourself in an environment that feels both peaceful and exhilarating.
