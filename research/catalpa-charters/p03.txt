Whale Watching Tour 
Come hear the Whale Song and venture into the heart of the sea. 
September to November 2026 - 3 Hours
Per Person: $99 Adult + $75 Child 
This spring, Catalpa Charters is proud to launch Rockingham, WA’s only accredited whale watching charter boat tour —a brand new 3-hour adventure where history and whale song meet.
Aboard Venture 111 , we’ll cruise beyond the islands and towards the whale highway , where the Indian Ocean hosts the breathtaking humpback migration on their journey south to Antarctica. Expect unrestricted views , luxury and sheltered seating , and a friendly, knowledgeable crew sharing fun facts and local stories as you search for the giants of the deep .
We also honour the powerful legacy of the Catalpa —a whaling ship once sent here both to hunt whales and to rescue men. Today, Catalpa Charters carries that history forward with pride and conservation at heart, capturing memories and creating our own epic tales—protecting these magnificent ocean travellers along the way.
Tour details 
• Season: approx 10 Sept – 10 Nov (end date depends on whale activity)
• Duration: 3 hours 
• Time: 9:00am – 12:00pm 
• Arrive: 30 minutes prior (strictly) 
• Capacity: Limited to 20 passengers 
• Includes: Morning tea, bathroom facilities onboard
• BYO: Yes

Preparing For Your Tour
Everything You Need to Know
Embarking on a tour with Catalpa Charters promises an unforgettable experience, blending adventure with the tranquility of the sea.
• 
Getting to Rockingham 
Rockingham is a coastal city located in Western Australia, approximately 40 kilometers south of Perth. Renowned for its pristine beaches and marine adventures, it serves as the departure point for Catalpa Charters.
• 
Parking & Meeting Point 
• Parking facilities are available near the departure point
• Arrive 15 mins early 
• Meeting point Val St jetty adjacent Cruising Yacht Club Rockingham
• Departure from the end of Val St Jetty look for the sign
• Usually at a dock or marina close to our operations center
• 
What to Bring 
Each seat has storage for personal items
• Sunscreen and sunglasses for UV protection
• Hat or cap for additional sun protection
• Refillable/Reusable water bottle to stay hydrated
• Any medications, make crew aware 
• Camera or smartphone to capture stunning views and wildlife
• 
What to Wear 
We recommend bringing a warm layer as temperatures can drop on the water.
• Light, breathable clothing for sunny conditions
• Windbreaker or jacket for cooler ocean breezes
• Non-slip footwear (sneakers or boat shoes) for safety aboard
• Strictly no healed shoes 
Important Information for Passengers
Boarding: 
Access is via The Cruising Yacht Club, Rockingham jetty, with a 5-step gangway. Please note, this may limit accessibility for some passengers.
Seating: 
Our vessel features three tables seating six guests each and one table for two, all with cooler tables for easy access to ice and drinks.
Weather Policy: 
Charters run daily unless weather conditions make travel unsafe. In such cases, guests will be contacted to arrange a rebooking.
