Hervey Bay 
Discover why Hervey Bay is one of Queensland’s most loved natural coastal destinations. Nestled between World Heritage-listed K’gari (formerly Fraser Island) and the Great Barrier Reef, Hervey Bay is a natural playground for adventure, relaxation and unforgettable wildlife encounters. From calm, turquoise waters and endless sandy beaches, to the world’s first Whale Heritage Site , this is where you can snorkel the best reef in the world (as declared by Lonely Planet), explore ancient rainforests, and watch majestic humpback whales up close.
Experience Hervey Bay connects you with the best local tours and attractions — including K’gari 4WD adventures & Kingfisher Bay Resort stays, Great Barrier Reef snorkelling trips at Lady Elliot Island , and award-winning Whale Watching cruises (mid July to October). Start planning your Hervey Bay escape today.
HERVEY BAY EXPERIENCES 
K'gari (Fraser Island) 
You’ll never forget K’gari (meaning Paradise - named by the indigenous Butchulla Traditional Owners ) and once you experience it, you'll be left with a very special connection. There is nowhere else like it. World-Heritage listed K'gari is the world’s largest sand island and is situated just off Hervey Bay with easy access via River Heads (45 min ferry cruise ). It stretches for 123 km along the Great Sandy Strait and Wide Bay. There are a range of fantastic marine and land-based tours ranging in duration and style that will ensure you have an unforgettable K’gari Experience!

Lady Elliot Island - Great Barrier Reef 
We have one of the best kept secrets to share about Australia’s Great Barrier Reef . You don’t have to travel for days to the far northern tropics to experience it with thousands of other tourists. The cooler and less disturbed Southern Great Barrier Reef has been voted by Lonely Planet as the world's best snokelling! Lady Elliot Island is easily accessible from Hervey Bay with a short 40-minute scenic flight. The hard and soft corals, and marine life (home of the manta ray & significant turtle breeding sites) at Lady Elliot Island will truly delight and amaze you! Day trips and longer stays available.
Traditional Custodians
We acknowledge the Butchulla people, the traditional custodians of this land and we pay our respects to their Elders past and present. We extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. 
Experience Hervey Bay acknowledges Butchulla lore: 
• What is good for the land comes first 
• Do not take or touch anything that does not belong to you 
• If you have plenty, you must share
