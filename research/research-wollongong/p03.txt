Tours
The best way to discover Wollongong is with someone who knows it inside out. From foodie experiences and behind-the-scenes attractions to unforgettable adventures and fascinating local stories, there’s a tour for every kind of traveller.
Go beyond the guidebook as you uncover hidden gems, meet passionate locals, and gain a deeper understanding of the region. Explore Wollongong’s rich industrial past and the industries that continue to shape the city today, or simply sit back and let someone else show you the highlights.
However you like to explore, you’ll find there’s always something new to discover.
