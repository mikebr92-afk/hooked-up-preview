Adventure and Outdoors Tours 
Set out on the ultimate fishing adventure with Shell Cove Offshore Adventures and Shell Cove Offshore Fishing Adventures, where premium offshore and inshore experiences meet the breathtaking NSW coastline. 
Departing from Shellharbour Marina, their charters blend expert seamanship, top-quality gear, and genuine local knowledge to deliver a day on the water that’s seamless, exciting, and truly unforgettable.
Whether you’re targeting snapper on the reefs, chasing kingfish offshore, or simply enjoying a well-run charter with a friendly, experienced crew, they focus on what matters most safety, professionalism, and an exceptional time on the water. With fast access to prime fishing grounds and a personalised approach to every trip, you’ll spend less time travelling and more time doing what you came for fishing.
You are welcome to bring your pet if you book a private group adventure. 
During migration season, we also offer exclusive whale watching experiences.
With Shell Cove Offshore Adventures, every day is a fishing day!
Services
Shell Cove Offshore Adventures Fishing Tour
Shell Cove Offshore Adventures offers a premium fishing experience, departing from the heart of Shellharbour Marina.
Their vessel will take you straight to the reefs where the big fish are found.
