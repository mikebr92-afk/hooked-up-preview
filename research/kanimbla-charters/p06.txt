Where to find us
Kanimbla Charters operates from Rosslyn Bay Boat Harbour, Yeppoon, Queensland, Australia. See the interactive map below.
• Yeppoon is approximately 700kms north of Brisbane QLD.
• The closest airport is Rockhampton Airport which is serviced by Qantas and Virgin Airlines, with a flight time from Brisbane of approximately 1 hour.
A Yeppoon information sheet, including details of available accommodation, will be provided when you book.
