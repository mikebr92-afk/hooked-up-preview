ABOUT REEL CONTAGIOUS CHARTERS
Featuring the new Kelacat 3000 Hard Top Express 
Specialists in creating a fishing experience to suit the specific needs of groups and individual skill level. We can cater for a range of fishing techniques in the one outing determined by fishing conditions, currents or water quality and temperature. Based in Port Stephens, the fishing hot spot on the east coast of Australia.
Our core business is to provide every charter with a comfortable, quality vessel, offering a rewarding, enjoyable and safe experience at sea.
The operators have extensive experience in tutoring, with boat safety and are experienced in estuary, reef and game fishing.
Luxury Fishing on Reel CONTAGIOUS
A Kevlacat Hard Top Express 3000, powered by twin 300hp Mercury Verados.
2C Survey for a maximum of 8 Passengers & 2 Crew.
An amazing fishing platform with the capability of a fast and safe fishing experience.
Fitted with an extensive array of SIMRAD navigation and fish finding Technology.
