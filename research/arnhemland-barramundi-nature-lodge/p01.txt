Latest News From The Barra Lodge 
Celebrating 21 Years of Arnhem Land Barramundi Lodge 
2025 marks the 21st anniversary of Arnhem Land Barramundi Lodge — a place that has always been more than a fishing destination. For over two decades, the lodge has been woven into the floodplains, rivers and salt arms of north-central Arnhem Land, shaped by country, culture and a shared passion for one of Australia’s most iconic fish. What began as a remote fishing camp has become a destination defined by adventure, respect for land and sea, and unforgettable barra moments.
Email from Chris & Anne Carter 
Heading home today after a memorable trip (March 2025). Highlights being a personal first 108cm Barra. My wife then followed with her first ever Barra on Sunday then a 102cm on Monday. Not sure how often you get two metre plus fish on the same boat, let alone caught by husband and wife! Needless to say, we were over the moon and the envy of the camp. Special shout out to our guide Lance who was a wonder to be with and taught us so much. So it was a great trip. Probably doesn’t get any better! – Chris Carter

The NT Runoff (late Feb-early May)is a spectacular time to visit the Arnhem Land Barramundi Lodge. The landscape has regenerated with post Wet Season lushness and the fishing can be extraordinary with cricket score barramundi catches the norm. The Lodge luxury bush accommodation is fully air-conditioned with ensuite bathroom facilities. The in-house, Lodge chef inspired mouth-watering meals of fresh local barramundi and mud crab are first class. The Lodge boasts a team of dedicated, experienced fishing guides plus friendly house staff and Lodge Manager to ensure your stay is a memorable one. In addition to wifi access, the Lodge has a licensed bar that features a range of quality wines, spirits and a good selection of beers that are served icy cold.
