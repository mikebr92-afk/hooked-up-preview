Deep Sea, Reef & Game Fishing Charters
Gold Coast – Tweed Heads
Cushy Fishing Charters is a local- family owned business providing offshore Fishing Charters on the Tweed Coast since 1996.
Our boat Reef Hunter is a 36ft STEBER Flybridge Utility with an extra large fishing deck.
Our boat REEF HUNTER CFB 12840 is surveyed for 22 passengers however for our clients comfort we like to keep the crew numbers to a maximum of 8 passengers on all charters.
In survey for 14+2 offshore / 18+2 inshore. 
However we like to keep the passenger numbers down to give our customers plenty of deck room, resulting in a more enjoyable and relaxed experience. 
We recommend 6 passengers maximum on our charters. 
We use fresh bait, good quality fishing rods, reels and tackle. Beyond the exceptional fishing opportunities, our charters provide an opportunity to create lifelong memories. Whether you're celebrating a milestone or bonding with colleagues, our commercial boats offer a platform to forge lasting connections while indulging in the thrill of the chase. Your experienced skipper and crew are professional in providing you a Fun and Safe day offshore fishing. Whether you're an experienced angler or its your very first time, everyone is welcome and we are happy to guide you and share our fishing knowledge with you.
We can take you reef fishing all year round, targeting Snapper, Pearlies, Venus Tusk, Teraglin, Moses Perch, Spangled Sweetlip and Maori Cod are just some of the species you can expect to catch.
We will show you how to live bait for the Big Ones! Targeting Kingfish, Cobia, Mulloway, Amberjack and Samson Fish. We offer Game Fishing Charters during the Summer months (December to March) Targeting Marlin, Wahoo, Mahi Mahi, Mackeral and Tuna. With our luxurious and well-equipped charter boats, you can fish in style and comfort. Book your charter today and let us create an exceptional fishing experience for you. For a great day out offshore fishing give Kyle a call on 0418 631 076.
