COMMERCIAL WORK BOAT FOR HIRE
Cushy Fishing Charters is your premier destination for unforgettable offshore fishing adventures. Whether you're an experienced angler or a novice looking to reel in the big ones, our professional crew and state-of-the-art boats are here to provide you with an exhilarating fishing experience.
Don't have your own boat? No problem! We offer boat hire specifically designed for fishing enthusiasts, Film and TV crews, Surveyers and many other professions. Hire a boat and enjoy the freedom to explore the open sea at your own pace. We have the following vessels available for hire: 
• Commercial Marine Operations Support Vessel
• Offshore Crew Transfers and Supplies 
• Films and TV Documentaries 
• Artificial Reef Survey Vessel (ROV) 
• Sea Drone Survey Vessel
• Offshore Sediment sampling
Our commercial boats undergo regular maintenance and are equipped with state-of-the-art safety features, including navigation systems, life-saving equipment, and communication devices. We ensure that you can focus on your fishing pursuits with peace of mind.
Book your adventure with Cushy Fishing Charters today.
