Reef and Game Fishing Charters
Cushy Fishing Charters the Deep Sea, Reef and Game Fishing Specialists
Welcome to Cushy Fishing Charters, your premier destination for an unforgettable fishing experience in NSW, and the Gold Coast. Cushy Fishing Charters has been Fishing Gold Coast waters and providing Gold Coast Fishing Charters and Tweed Heads fishing charters for over 20 years. 
Fishing the best reefs that the Gold Coast, Tweed Coast and Bryon Bay regions have to offer.
It is only a short boat ride from our departure point on the Tweed River to the open sea, with the closest reefs laying within 3 kilometres from the Tweed River Entrance and expanding in sections, east to the Continental Shelf. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a novice, our Fishing Boat Charter services are tailored to meet your every need. Embark on an exciting adventure with our experienced crew, who will guide you to the best fishing spots in the deep sea. Our Deep Sea Fishing Charters offer you the opportunity to reel in the biggest catches and create lasting memories. Our charters are strategically located in renowned fishing grounds, offering access to abundant fish populations and diverse species. Explore the bountiful waters under the guidance of our experienced crew, who possess in-depth knowledge of local hotspots and seasonal fishing patterns. Discover the abundant marine life off the coast of NSW while enjoying the comfort and luxury of our state-of-the-art fishing vessels. Book your Fishing Charter with us today and make your fishing dreams a reality.
