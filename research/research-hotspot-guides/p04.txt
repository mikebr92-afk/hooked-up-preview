Burnett Heads
Burnett Heads is what happens when a fishing village decides not to grow up.
Thongs on, shoulders down, worries off. The river rolls out to the reef, the boats slip in and out of the port, and nobody’s in much of a hurry about anything. 
Grab fish and chips, wander the marina, stroll out to the lighthouse, or find a spot to watch the water and pretend you’ve got nowhere else to be. Mon Repos’ turtles are just up the road, the sea breeze does wonders for the soul, and somehow the day disappears without you noticing. 
Stay a few hours or stay the night. Either way, you’ll get why people keep coming back. 
portside
adventures
Drop a line in the Burnett River and see what bites, or just wander the port and soak up the calm. When hunger hits, swing by the Lighthouse Hotel for a cold one and a classic feed, this is where locals unwind and swap yarns. If you fancy something a bit different, Monsoon Aquatics run hands-on coral farm tours right here, and for a proper reef fix, Lady Musgrave Experience takes off straight from the port.

Queensland Rum Day is this Saturday 17 October, and that`s a day worth raising a glass to 🍻😎
Home to the iconic Bundaberg Rum and Kalki Moon Distilling Company, our region knows a thing or two about crafting world-class spirits.
This year, Bundaberg Rum and Kalki Moon have joined fellow distilleries from across the state to create a special limited-edition collaboration rum, bringing together some of Queensland`s finest drop-makers in one bottle.
And because no celebration is complete without a bit of Bundy spirit, Bundaberg Rum Distillery is getting behind the day with a community celebration at the Distillery. Expect distillery tours, giveaways and prizes, $5 Bundy Bangers, yard games and plenty happening around the site throughout the day.
Whether you`re a seasoned rum drinker or simply keen to soak up the atmosphere, it`s a chance to celebrate the people, passion and pioneering spirit behind Queensland rum.
Cheers to legends. Cheers to local. Cheers to Queensland Rum Day.
