South East Charters offers tailored half or full day game, estuary or deep sea fishing tours departing from Orford, Dunalley or Eaglehawk Neck. All fishing equipment is supplied. 
All tours operate subject to weather conditions. Tours depart around 7 am to 7.30 am and return around 1.30 pm or 3.30 pm. Meals are not included.

Orford Coastal Retreat by Peppy Beach Retreats
Orford
$320 - $600
Relax with your friends and family at this modern Hamptons-style seaside retreat.
Just a two-minute walk to the pristine Millingtons beach or leafy park with playground. Lounge in the glorious al-fresco dining area and cook your own woodfired pizzas. Let the kids loose on the table tennis table in the rumpus/ bunk room.
You won't want to leave, but this amazing property is the ideal base to explore further up Tasmania's stunning east coast. 
This property was quite literally built to highlight the best aspects of seaside living and allow you to maximise your precious time on Tassie's east coast. 
Enter the property to explore the flowing open-plan and contemporary Hamptons-inspired interior. With polished concrete floors, a modern island kitchen, and plenty of indoor entertaining space, this purposely designed interior is both functional and stylish. 
But why spend time indoors when you can relax in a hammock in the spacious backyard and al-fresco dining area? This manicured garden and private area comes complete with a large concrete woodfired pizza oven, two dining areas, fire pit and plenty of grass for the kids and dogs to enjoy. 
Put simply, Orford Coastal Retreat was specifically designed to relax and share quality time with friends and family. You could not ask for a better layout for an incredible stay on Tasmania's east coast.

Accommodation 
Sunways Orford
Orford
$400
Just a stone's throw from the Prosser River in the heart of Orford, and only a five-minute walk to the beach, Sunways is the perfect coastal escape for couples or families. Sleeping up to four guests across two comfortable queen bedrooms, this welcoming retreat blends character, charm, and modern comfort.
Recently refreshed with a stylish new kitchen that flows into the open-plan living and dining area, Sunways offers modern convenience while retaining the soul of its near 100-year-old bones. It's a home that wears its history proudly—original floorboards, layers of wallpaper, and quirky imperfections that tell a story of generations past. Every creak, tilt, and whisper adds to its unique personality, and that's exactly what makes it so special.
This isn't a house that tries too hard. Instead, it invites you to slow down, settle in, and embrace its charm. They've kept it warm, comfortable, and filled it with the little things they love—so when you stay, you'll feel right at home too.
Their hope is that your time at Sunways is about pressing pause: long, lazy mornings, slow strolls to the beach, shared meals, and quiet moments that turn into treasured memories. Sunways doesn't just offer you a place to stay—it insists you relax and savour every second.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Tasmanian Aboriginal people and their enduring custodianship of Lutruwita / Tasmania.
We honour the uninterrupted care, protection and belonging to these islands, skies and waterways, before the invasion and colonisation of European settlement.
As part of a tourism industry that welcomes visitors to these lands, we acknowledge our responsibility to represent to our visitors Tasmania's deep and complex history, fully, respectfully and truthfully.
We acknowledge the Aboriginal people who continue to care for this country today.
We pay our respects to their Elders, past and present.
We honour their stories, songs, art, and culture, and their aspirations for the future of their people and these lands.
