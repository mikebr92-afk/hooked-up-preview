All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Trips are weather led — Scott makes the call on the morning. MV Fin is commercially surveyed and operates under a Lord Howe Island Marine Park permit.
How booking works here
The weather decides. Rather than selling dates months ahead and cancelling half of them, we set the week’s operating days on the Sunday before and open bookings then.
Send us your dates and who is coming. We will add you to that week’s list, and on the Sunday you will get an email with the trips running, the times, and a link to book and pay.
It means a little less certainty in advance. It also means when we say a trip is running, it is running.
Trips leave from the Lord Howe Island jetty. Departure times move through the week, because Fin can’t get out of the lagoon on a low tide — so the time comes with your Sunday email rather than being fixed in advance.
Weather, cancellations, children, seasickness and what to bring are all covered in the FAQs .

What swims here
Lord Howe sits where warm water meets cool, with reef and deep ocean close together. Tropical and temperate species share the same water, on and around the southernmost coral reef in the world.
Kingfish are the main event. Wahoo, yellowfin, trevally and deep water species come into it depending on the season and the day.
