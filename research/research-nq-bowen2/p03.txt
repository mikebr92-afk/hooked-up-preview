Horseshoe Bay, Bowen 
Beyond Expectation
A coastal town perched on a headland overlooking Queensland’s Whitsunday Islands, Bowen is a beach-lover’s paradise with country town hospitality. 
Discover
Home to eight secluded, palm-lined beaches and a slower pace where even the turquoise waves seem to take their time rolling onto shore, here every day feels made for the water. Just offshore, fringing coral reefs invite snorkellers and divers into another world. Fishing is the local pastime. The seafood is some of the freshest you’ll taste, including tiger prawns and mud crab, unloaded daily at the harbour and served in local restaurants only minutes away.
