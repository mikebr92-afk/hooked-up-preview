It's not all about fishing 
At Fish Moreton, we offer more than just a rod and reel. Cruise through the stunning waters of Moreton Bay and the Pumicestone Passage. Whether you're spotting dolphins, sharing a relaxing day on the water with loved ones, or taking in the golden glow of the bay at dusk—our tours are designed for unforgettable moments, both on and off the line.
