Explore Moreton Bay, Bribie Island & Pumicestone Passage 
Looking for things to do around Bribie Island or Sandstone Point? Fish Moreton offers a unique way to experience the area, from family sightseeing and scenic cruises through Pumicestone Passage to fishing adventures across Moreton Bay. Departing from Sandstone Point, our charters are an easy option for visitors staying in the Bribie Island area who want to explore the waterways, spot local marine life, enjoy the scenery or simply spend a memorable day on the water.
Meet your Local Skipper 
With over 35 years of experience in fishing charters and cruising, Captain Wally brings local knowledge, passion and a personal touch to every trip. Whether you are heading out for a family day on the water or a serious fishing adventure, Wally is dedicated to making every charter a memorable experience. Meet Captain Wally 
Where do you depart from? 
Fish Moreton departs from Sandstone Point Marina, Spinnaker Drive, Sandstone Point. 
How many people are onboard? 
We only take maximum 8 fishing and up to 16 on sightseeing tours.
​ 
Do you go to Moreton Island? 
Yes, our Moreton Island Swim and Cruise Tours is ideal for anyone who would like to discover Moreton Island by boat.
​ 
Do you have a minimum age limit for children? 
No. However, we recommend discussing younger children with the Captain so we can help you choose the most suitable trip.
​ 
What is supplied? 
Water & soft drinks are supplied along with suncreeen. Catering can be supplied upon request. All rods, tackle and bait are supplied.

Our Impact 
Positive Changes
Fish Moreton is committed to sustainable fishing practices and environmental conservation. Our efforts focus on promoting responsible angling and preserving marine ecosystems.
