Snapper & Gummies
Charter Fishing in Lakes Entrance this winter has been great with plenty of Snapper all the way through.
Lakes Entrance is one of the only places in Victoria we can catch these magnificat fish all year round plus many other reef species with a few Gummy Sharks thrown in.
The Weather is warming up and the water temperature is rising so this only means one thing Big Snapper and Gummy Shark are about to come on the chew.
Lakes Entrance summer is fast approaching and the fishing is hotting up so best book early to avoid disappointment.
Keep an eye on our FB page for latest catches.
Cheers Captain Tony Kemna
