GIPPSLAND LAKES 
The Gippsland Lakes is Australia's largest coastal lake system and one of Victoria's most productive bream and flathead fisheries. Lake King, Lake Victoria, Lake Wellington and the Mitchell, Nicholson and Tambo Rivers provide more water than any angler could explore in a lifetime. The secret is knowing which part of the system to fish, and why. 
WHY FISH WITH A PROFESSIONAL GUIDE 
Scale is the challenge at the Gippsland Lakes. The sheer size of the system means unguided anglers often spend their day covering unproductive water. A local guide puts you on active fish efficiently and helps you understand how tides, wind and season interact across the different lakes.
TARGET SPECIES 
Black Bream · Yellowfin Bream · Dusky Flathead · Salmon · Estuary Perch · Tailor · Trevally · Luderick · 
BEST SEASONS 
Summer (Dec-Feb) 
Topwater fishing for bream, flathead active on shallow banks 
Autumn (Mar-May) 
Trophy bream season, consistent lure fishing across the system 
Spring (Sep-Nov) 
Active perch and aggressively feeding fish across all species 
Winter (Jun-Aug) 
Deep water vibe fishing, large schooling bream in channels 
KEY LURE TECHNIQUES 
Crankbaits, blades and topwater baits for bream. Paddle-tail plastics and soft vibes for flathead. Twitching jerkbaits and topwater baits during low light for estuary perch. 
THE CHARTER EXPERIENCE 
The Gippsland Lakes suits groups wanting variety — multiple species, multiple techniques and multiple environments all within a single day on the water. It's the most accessible of all the East Gippsland systems for first-time charter clients. 
Gippsland Lakes is 4 hours from Melbourne.
