About Us 
Not only is Dan (your charter captain) an exceptional angler, but he is also a patient teacher. Whether you're a novice angler looking to learn the ropes or a seasoned pro seeking to refine your technique, Dan is there to offer guidance, tips, and tricks to help you improve your skills and increase your chances of landing that trophy catch. 
When you embark on a fishing adventure with Dan, you can trust that you are in capable hands. His professionalism and passion for fishing create a welcoming and enjoyable atmosphere for anglers of all levels. 
At Calm Water Fishing , we specialise in providing exceptional fishing tours on the Tweed River. Our extensive knowledge of these regions' ecosystems ensure a successful and enjoyable fishing trip every time. 
Nestled in picturesque surroundings, the Tweed River offers a haven for anglers seeking a tranquil fishing experience. Each location boasts its own unique charm, with calm waters, stunning natural scenery, and abundant marine life. Cast your line and test your skills as you target a variety of fish species, including mangrove jack, flathead, bream, and more. 
Join Dan and the Calm Water Fishing team for an unforgettable fishing tour. Book your adventure today with Calm Water Fishing, and let Dan share his expertise and love for fishing with you.

Our Fishing Gear 
​ 
When you choose Calm Water Fishing, you can rest assured that you will have access to the best fishing equipment available. We believe that using top-quality gear enhances your chances of landing that trophy catch and provides an overall enjoyable fishing experience. We spare no expense in sourcing and maintaining our equipment, so you can focus on the thrill of the catch. 
Complimentary Equipment on Our Tours. As part of our guided fishing tours, we provide all the necessary equipment to ensure you have a seamless and hassle-free experience. From high-performance rods and reels to sturdy fishing lines and tackle boxes, we have everything you need to tackle a wide variety of fishing conditions and target different species. 
Pay-Per-Loss Lure Policy. While we take pride in offering complimentary equipment, it is important to note our pay-per-loss lure policy which applies to hard body lures ONLY . Fishing in diverse environments comes with its challenges, and occasionally, lures may be lost or damaged during the fishing excursion. In such cases, we kindly ask our guests to reimburse us for the cost of the lost lures on a pay-per-loss basis. This policy allows us to continue providing top-notch equipment and ensures that future anglers can enjoy the same level of quality. 
Our experienced fishing guides are available to assist you in selecting the right equipment for your fishing needs. Whether you are a seasoned angler or a beginner, we are here to offer advice and recommendations to enhance your fishing experience. We understand that each angler has unique preferences, and we are dedicated to tailoring our equipment offerings to suit your individual requirements. 
**Pay-per-loss lure policy which applies to hard body lures only. A ll soft plastics and other lures are included in the charter price.
