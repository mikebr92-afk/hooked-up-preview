Why Choose Calm Water Fishing On The Tweed River? 
1. Expert Guides. Our experienced fishing guides have an intimate understanding of the Tweed River's ecosystem and know the best spots to find various species of fish. They will share their tips, tricks, and techniques to help you maximise your fishing success. 
2. Personalised Experience. We pride ourselves on offering personalised fishing tours tailored to your preferences. Whether you're after a specific species or want to explore a particular area, we will customise your charter to meet your needs. 
3. Quality Equipment. We provide high-quality fishing equipment, including rods, reels, bait, and tackle, to ensure you have the best chance of landing your prized catch. 
4. Spectacular Scenery. The Tweed River is renowned for its breathtaking natural beauty, with lush greenery, (mostly) crystal-clear water, and stunning sunsets. Prepare to be captivated by the scenery while enjoying a day on the water. 
5. Family-Friendly. Our fishing charters are suitable for anglers of all ages and skill levels. Whether you're planning a family outing, a corporate retreat, or a solo adventure, we can accommodate your group and ensure everyone has a fantastic time.
