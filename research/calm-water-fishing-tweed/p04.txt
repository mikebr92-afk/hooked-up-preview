Fishing The Broadwater

Are you ready to embark on an unforgettable fishing adventure amidst the stunning waters of the Gold Coast's Broadwater? Look no further than Calm Water Fishing, where we specialise in providing exceptional guided fishing tours that cater to both novice anglers and seasoned fishing enthusiasts. Whether you're seeking a thrilling fishing expedition or a relaxing day on the water, our experienced team and top-quality equipment ensure an unforgettable experience for all. 
​ 
At Calm Water Fishing , we are passionate about sharing our love for fishing and the breathtaking beauty of the Broadwater. With years of expertise in the industry, our professional and friendly team is committed to delivering the ultimate fishing charter experience. We pride ourselves on offering personalised service, ensuring that each guest receives the attention and guidance needed to make the most of their fishing adventure. 
Nestled within the pristine Gold Coast region, the Broadwater boasts abundant marine life and a variety of fish species that make it a prime fishing destination. The Broadwater offers a diverse range of fishing opportunities with heaps of different species for you to catch. Our experienced charter captain, Dan possess extensive local knowledge, ensuring that you fish in the best spots and make the most of your time on the water. 
Ready to experience the thrill of fishing in the Gold Coast's Broadwater? Booking your adventure with Calm Water Fishing is simple and convenient. Explore our website to learn more about our charter options, pricing, and availability. Our friendly team is always ready to assist you with any questions you may have or provide recommendations based on your preferences.

Why Choose Calm Water Fishing On The Broadwater? 
• 
Expert Guides. Our skilled fishing guides are passionate about fishing and are dedicated to ensuring you have an enjoyable and successful fishing experience. They will share their tips and tricks, teach you new techniques, and provide valuable insights into the local fishing scene. 
• 
Quality Equipment. We provide high-quality fishing gear, ensuring you have the best tools at your disposal. Our well-maintained boats are equipped with state-of-the-art navigational systems and safety equipment to guarantee a safe and comfortable trip. 
• 
Personalised Service . We understand that every angler has different preferences and skill levels. Our guides will tailor the trip to meet your specific needs, whether you're a beginner looking for guidance or an experienced angler targeting a specific species. 
• 
Memorable Experiences. Beyond the fishing, our charters offer a chance to relax, unwind, and create lasting memories. Enjoy the stunning natural scenery, observe local wildlife, and relish the camaraderie that comes with a shared fishing adventure. 
• 
Family-Friendly. Our fishing charters are suitable for anglers of all ages and skill levels. Whether you're planning a family outing, a corporate retreat, or a solo adventure, we can accommodate your group and ensure everyone has a fantastic time.
