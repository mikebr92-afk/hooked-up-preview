JIGGING AND POPPING
For those who enjoy a more hands-on approach, jigging and popping offer a unique fishing experience. Jigging involves using lures that mimic the movement of prey fish, enticing larger fish to strike. Popping, on the other hand, involves casting surface lures and creating a splash that attracts fish to the surface. Both techniques require skill and patience, rewarding anglers with the thrill of the catch. You would be targeting species like Giant Trevally, Spanish Mackerel, Red Bass, Rankin Cod, Goldband Snapper, Coral Trout, Red Emperor, and many more reef dwellers.
DEEP DROPPING
Deep dropping is perfect for those seeking to explore the depths of the ocean. This technique involves the use of electric reels and fishing at great depths. Targeting species such as Ruby Snapper, 8 Bar Cod, and other deep water bottom dwellers. With the help of advanced equipment and the expertise of our experienced guides, deep dropping can be an incredibly rewarding experience.
