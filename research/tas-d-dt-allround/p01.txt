Tasmania Fly Fishing – Guided Trout Fishing Tours
Tasmania Fly Fishing offers guided trout fishing experiences across some of Tasmania's most scenic rivers and lakes, based out of Launceston.
They specialise in introducing people to fly fishing, with most clients either completely new or looking to build confidence in a new environment. Half-day, three-quarter day and full-day tours are available, with all equipment, catering and transport provided.
The tours operate across a range of locations, including local rivers, highland lakes, and the private waters of Currawong Lakes. Each day is tailored to suit conditions and client experience, with a strong focus on learning, enjoyment, and time on the water.
Fly fishing in Tasmania is about more than catching fish — it's about clear water, wild trout, and slowing things down. Whether it's your first cast or your first time fishing Tasmania, they aim to make the experience relaxed, practical, and memorable.

Nightcap at Riverside Hotel
Launceston
$129 - $399
With everything you need and nothing you don't, you will have no reason to leave Nightcap at Riverside. Delight in the comfort of an on-site restaurant, licensed bar and kitchenette facilities.
After a day of playing hard (or not), sink into your premium King Koil bed featured in each of their contemporary rooms. Like all Nightcap Hotels, they offer an on-site bistro, bar and bottle shop, free unlimited 24-hour wifi, with all rooms featuring a flat-screen TV, refrigerator, desk, heating, air-conditioning and tea/coffee making facilities.
The only thing that could make your stay better is a refreshing drink over a game of billiards in their friendly bar. And if the deal wasn't sweet enough already, they will even throw in a free drink on arrival just to see you smile.
Nightcap at Riverside Hotel is situated on West Tamar Rd, 4.7 kilometres from Launceston city centre within close proximity to Cataract Gorge, Penny Royal World, the Gunpowder Mill and Launceston Lakes Trout Fishing Complex. It is also the gateway to the many attractions of the Tamar Valley and the wine route!
Just 20 minutes from Launceston airport, Nightcap at Riverside Hotel is a family orientated establishment offering true Tasmanian hospitality.

Peppers Silo Hotel
Launceston
$199 - $1059
Peppers Silo Hotel is one of Tasmania's most distinctive luxury accommodation experiences, located on the banks of kanamaluka / River Tamar in Launceston. Originally built in the 1960s as grain storage silos, this iconic waterfront landmark has been transformed into a sophisticated hotel that seamlessly blends industrial heritage with contemporary design.
The hotel features premium guest rooms and suites within the original heritage silos and modern North Tower, offering spacious accommodation, modern comforts and views across the Tamar River, Cataract Gorge and the surrounding landscape. Warm textures, natural tones and thoughtful design create an authentic connection to Tasmania's unique environment and sense of place.
Dining is a highlight of the guest experience. Grain of the Silos, led by Food Director Massimo Mele and Executive Chef Thomas Pirker, celebrates Tasmania's renowned food culture through seasonal menus showcasing local farmers, growers, fishers, makers and winemakers. Grain Cafe offers specialty coffee, breakfast, lunch and house-made treats in a relaxed waterfront setting, while Woody's Barrel Bar features Tasmanian wines, craft beers, premium spirits and cocktails overlooking the river.
Guests can also unwind at Silo Day Spa, enjoy conference and event facilities, or explore nearby attractions including the Seaport Precinct, Cataract Gorge Reserve and the Tamar Valley Wine Region. Peppers Silo Hotel delivers a uniquely Tasmanian experience combining exceptional hospitality, local flavour and rich heritage.

La Banque
Launceston
$240 - $345
Welcome to La Banque, a stunningly renovated 1930s Art Deco apartment with three bedrooms, once home to a bustling bank and now your stylish retreat in the heart of Launceston.
Just minutes from the CBD, you'll be surrounded by a vibrant array of cafes, restaurants, and shops. Explore local parks, historic sites, and cultural attractions all within walking distance, making it easy to immerse yourself in the city's energy. Whether your interest lies in sports or the arts, La Banque is just a short walk from UTAS Stadium and the Star Theatre (cinema), both only a stone's throw away.
Living Room: Relax in a spacious living area filled with natural light, featuring elegant furnishings and décor that echo the opulence of the 1930s.
Bedrooms: Unwind in one of the two cosy bedrooms with queen-sized beds, complete with sumptuous linens and period-inspired accents for a restful night's sleep. A third bedroom forms a study space to work or just sit and read – and contains a lush ottoman that folds out to a double bed if required for additional guests.
Kitchen: Cook and dine in a fully equipped kitchen, blending modern amenities with a touch of retro flair. Enjoy meals at the charming breakfast nook and dining table.
Off-street parking included.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Tasmanian Aboriginal people and their enduring custodianship of Lutruwita / Tasmania.
We honour the uninterrupted care, protection and belonging to these islands, skies and waterways, before the invasion and colonisation of European settlement.
As part of a tourism industry that welcomes visitors to these lands, we acknowledge our responsibility to represent to our visitors Tasmania's deep and complex history, fully, respectfully and truthfully.
We acknowledge the Aboriginal people who continue to care for this country today.
We pay our respects to their Elders, past and present.
We honour their stories, songs, art, and culture, and their aspirations for the future of their people and these lands.
