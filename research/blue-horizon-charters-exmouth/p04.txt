ADVENTURE OVERVIEW 
Join us on an Island fishing adventure.
Get a group together or join us for our 3-5 day fishing trips up to Long Island, Thevenard Island or Barrow Island. Trips can be from 3 to 5 days.
Get onboard and enjoy some of the best fishing grounds the north west has to offer. Bottom bouncing for Red emperor, Rankin Cod, Coral Trout to name a few as well as trolling for Mackerel, Sailfish or have a go at deep dropping for Ruby Snapper, 8 Bar cod or Goldband snapper. Then at the end of each day anchoring up around the islands to relax and have dinner and rest up before the next day of fishing.

ITINERARY 
The boat departs the Exmouth Marina at 5am.
On the way we throw the lures out and trawl for Sailfish, Marlin, Mackerel and Tuna. Once arriving at the fishing grounds we will bottom bounce for the rest of the day before heading into the islands where we will drop the anchor for the night for dinner.
Each we have a cooked breakfast before heading out to the fishing grounds for some more bottom bouncing, trolling or deep dropping. The Island have some great snorkeling spots for spear fishing and catching crays also walking the beaches flicking poppers for GT's. On the last day depending on our location we either fish till around mid day, then throw the lures back out and trawl our way home arriving back in Exmouth marina around 1700. Depending on the duration of the trip depends on how far north we go. On the 3 day trips we head about as far as Thevenard Island and on the 4-5 days we will go as far as Barrow Island. We can tailor the trip to suit your group.
