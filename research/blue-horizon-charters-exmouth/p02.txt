DAY TRIP OVERVIEW 
With the continental shelf only 12 kms off the coast, the North West Cape offers some excellent fishing and an abundance of fish species in the waters that surround Exmouth. So why not get out there and discover them for yourself.
Exmouth is recognized as a premier game fishing destination. Enjoy a day on the pristine water game fishing or bottom fishing on the Blue Horizon, a luxury vessel equipped with the latest electronic equipment, 240 V power and air-conditioned.
Although catching a great fish is only luck, our crew will do their best to make sure you don’t go home empty handed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
How much is a fishing day trip in Exmouth? Expand 
Adults are $360, children 14 and under are $340 and observers are $110. Two people sharing a line is $500 for one adult and one child, or $550 for two adults. You can also book the whole boat for the day for $4,000. Prices are in Australian dollars and the booking page always shows the current price.
What is included in the price? Expand 
Everything: tackle, bait and ice, plus water, tea, coffee and lunch (BYO alcohol), and transport to and from your accommodation. We also clean, fillet and bag what you catch so it is ready to take home.
What time does the day trip start and finish? Expand 
We pick you up from your accommodation at 6.30am and drop you home between 4.30 and 5.00pm. It is a full day of about 10 hours.
Do I need fishing experience? Expand 
No. If you have not fished before, we will teach you.
Can I hire the boat just for my group? Expand 
Yes. You can join a group trip or book the Blue Horizon exclusively for your own group for the day.
Where do you fish? Expand 
The continental shelf is only 12 km off the coast, so there is plenty of fishing close to Exmouth. Where we go on the day depends on the wind, tides and current, and where the skipper thinks you will catch the most.
