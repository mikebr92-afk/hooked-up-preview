Pelican Charters in Western Australia is a luxury boat charter company that specialises in exploring the waters around Perth and beyond. 
Boat hire, boat rentals for cruising the Swan River, visiting Carnac and Rottnest Island, or exploring other destinations around Perth. Their boats are suitable for groups of various sizes.
They provide private cruises on luxury vessels that can accommodate up to 55 passengers. for sightseeing, parties, or corporate events.
The fishing charters offer deep-sea fishing adventures to various locations like the Abrolhos Islands and the Montebello Islands. They provide multi-day live-aboard fishing trips and target a variety of fish species.
Overall, Pelican Charters is a good option for those looking for a luxurious and personalised boating experience in Western Australia, whether it's for fishing adventures or scenic cruises.
