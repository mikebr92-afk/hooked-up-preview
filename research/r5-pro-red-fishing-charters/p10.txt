What you need to know about Victorian Fishing Licenses 
Please note: Fishing Licenses are to be brought with you on the day of departure and must remain with you.
You don't need a fishing licence if you're: 
*Under 18 years or over 70 years*
*A person identifying as Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander*
*A Victorian or interstate equivalent Seniors Card holder*
*A Veterans Card — Victoria holder*
*A Veterans' Affairs Pensioner or TPI-coded Repatriation Health Card holder*
*A Commonwealth Pensioner Concession Card holder, coded either DSP, DSP Blind, AGE, AGE Blind or CAR*
For more information please and to acquire your licence

Why Fish With Pro Red Fishing Charters? 
With over 49 years of local fishing experience, Captain Jayson Turner and the team at Pro Red Fishing Charters know exactly where to find fish throughout Port Phillip Bay, Westernport Bay and Bass Strait. Whether you're an experienced angler chasing a trophy snapper or trying fishing for the first time, you'll receive friendly guidance and support throughout your trip.
Fish aboard the Barracuda , a purpose-built 10.4 metre walkaround charter vessel designed to provide plenty of room for everyone on board. The boat is equipped with modern fish-finding technology, quality fishing equipment and all the bait and tackle needed for a successful day on the water.
Comfort and safety are a priority on every charter. The vessel features onboard toilet facilities, weather protection and a spacious layout, allowing you to enjoy your fishing experience in comfort.
While snapper are the main target during the season, the action doesn't stop there. Depending on the time of year and conditions, you may also encounter species such as gummy sharks, flathead, King George whiting and calamari, making every trip unique.
Pro Red Fishing Charters caters for individuals, private groups and corporate bookings, with options available for groups of up to 12 people aboard the Barracuda. Larger groups of up to 20 people can also be accommodated through affiliated vessels, making Pro Red an excellent choice for work functions, Christmas parties, fishing clubs, family outings and group events.
Safety is a top priority on every charter. Our vessel is equipped with all required safety equipment, and a full safety briefing is provided before departure so you can enjoy your day on the water with confidence.
