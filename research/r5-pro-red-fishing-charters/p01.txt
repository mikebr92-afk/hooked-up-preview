Expert Fishing Charters In 
Melbourne & Mornington Peninsula 
We aim to provide the most professional, and fun, fishing trips in Southern Victoria. 
We run our Melbourne, Port Phillip Bay and southern coast fishing charters from Carrum, Mornington, St Kilda and Rye. Over in Westernport, we depart from Hastings, Stony Point and Corinella.
Our aim is simple: a safe, relaxed and professional fishing charter for Melbourne locals and visitors who love getting out on the water. Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned angler, you'll feel right at home aboard.
We target a wide range of species, including snapper, kingfish, King George whiting, flathead, squid, gummy sharks and elephant sharks - and head out of Portland for bluefin tuna in season. Whether you're chasing snapper in Port Phillip Bay or bluefin tuna off Victoria's southern coast, our promise is to work hard to give you a true fish-of-a-lifetime experience, and a fun, enjoyable and memorable day on the water.

Meet Captain 
Jayson Turner 
Captain Jayson Turner brings more than 51 years of fishing experience to every charter and holds a Master 5 qualification. Having spent decades fishing Melbourne's bays and Victorian offshore waters, Jayson knows where the fish are and how to give anglers the best chance of landing a memorable catch.
Jayson was originally from Tasmania fishing from the age of 5 catching various saltwater and freshwater fish and diving from the age of 7 for Crayfish and Abalone
Over the years, Pro Red Fishing Charters has helped clients catch some impressive fish, including snapper exceeding 10.8kg in Port Phillip Bay and over 9kg in Western Port, kingfish over 11kg, gummy sharks to 25kg, and southern bluefin tuna ranging from 8kg to an incredible 111kg off Portland.
Whether you're targeting snapper, king george whiting, squid, salmon, kingfish, sharks, or southern bluefin tuna, Jayson combines local knowledge, experience, and a passion for fishing to deliver an enjoyable and rewarding day on the water.
