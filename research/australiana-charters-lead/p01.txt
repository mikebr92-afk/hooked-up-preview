Stop auDA’s Policy Changes: Protect Australian Domains & Small Businesses
Stop auDA’s Policy Changes: Protect Australian Domains & Small Businesses
The Issue
auDA, the organisation that sets the rules for Australian domain names, has accepted recommendations from its Licensing Rules Review that will change eligibility requirements for . com.au and . net.au domains.
https://www.auda.org.au/public-impact/have-your-say/policy-panels/au-licensing-rules-review-2025/ 
Specifically Recommendation 2, which deletes subparagraph (f) from section 2.4.4 (2) of the .au Licensing Rules.
This decision strikes a direct blow to around 2.7 Million . com.au and . net.au domain registrations out of 3.4 Million, 80% of the namespace, that will now require changes just to remain eligible to renew and keep those web addresses. 
Under this new policy, every domain registered will strictly require a registered Business Name that exactly matches each domain name, whereas previously having a single active ACN or ABN was sufficient.
If this change goes ahead: 
-Creates a new tax on small business, costing Australian businesses OVER $500 MILLION immediately to fix current registrations, and over $200 million every year to maintain.
-2.7 Million existing domains names will need a New Business registration from ASIC or a new Trade Mark from IP Australia.
-2.7 Million existing domains will require a Correction done at the Registrar to the new Business Name or Trade Mark.
-Registering new . com.au or . net.au will have the same extra costs, will require a unique Business Name or Trade Mark to register the new domain.
-The confusion caused will result in many existing domains to be ineligible for renewal and will results in many small business to lose their existing domains.
-Severely damages trust in . com.au and places an impossible burden on major Registrars (like GoDaddy, VentraIP, Webcentral, Help.com.au , CrazyDomains etc.) to assist hundreds of thousands of clients and process massive numbers of Change of Registrants.
-Many existing domain owners will be vulnerable to lose their domains, as anyone performing a WHOIS lookup will find that 4 in 5 domains are no longer compliant. This opens the door for mass submissions of domain deletion complaints that the Registrars and Regulators are not capable to process, allowing long-established domains to be deleted from real end users with existing websites.
You can check if your domain is no longer eligible under the new proposed policy here: https://www.help.com.au/au-eligibility 
We call on auDA to: 
-Stop the implementation of Recommendation 2 and ask the auDA Board to keep Section (f) of the policy.
-Stop forcing unnecessary costs and operational damage onto Australian business owners and domain holders.
-Maintain and protect the integrity and stability of the . com.au and . net.au namespace.
Please sign this petition to make your voice heard, protect small businesses, and put a stop to these harmful changes to Australia's domain name infrastructure.
