Product vision Everything an everyday person needs to change their community
We’re building an AI campaign assistant that supports people through every step of creating the most effective public advocacy campaign for any issue.
Guiding principles What we believe
01 Everyone should be able to raise their voice and be heard
Our platform gives people a voice to help shape their own community.
02 Change starts local
The biggest changes often start with one person, one problem and the neighbors who back them.
For petition starters A campaign copilot, personalized to your issue
It starts with the best possible petition. Then the copilot stays with you — helping you share in the right places, rally supporters, get the media’s attention and reach the person who can actually say yes.
Campaign assistant 
The crosswalk outside our elementary school has no light. Two kids were hit this year, and I walk my son across it every morning.
Thank you for sharing that. Here’s the path most likely to work: 1
Your ask A traffic signal at the school crosswalk before next school year 
2
Decision maker City Director of Public Works — we’ll notify them for you 
3
Who to bring in The school PTA, your ward’s council member and local parent groups

Every step of the way From one person’s story to a decision maker’s desk
An everyday person gets every single thing they need to help shape their community.
01 
Tell your story
Describe the problem and why it matters to you, in your own words.
02 
Create the best possible petition
Find the most effective solution and a clear ask, then shape it into a petition that persuades.
03 
Share in the right places
Get guidance on exactly where your supporters already are, online and off.
04 
Rally your supporters
Keep followers engaged with updates, asks and moments that build momentum.
05 
Engage the media
Pitch local reporters with a story they want to tell.
06 
Reach the decision maker
We reach out to them automatically, and prepare you for the conversation when they engage.
For supporters Everyone can give their time and talents
Supporters will be able to do much more than sign. Whatever they can offer, our platform will help them have real impact on the campaigns they care about.
Chip in
Fund the campaigns that matter to you.
Share
Spread the word with friends and neighbors.
Record your story
Add your voice with a short video.
Put up yard signs
Make the campaign visible on your street.
Organize the community
Join the starter’s team and bring neighbors together.
Show up at city council
Speak up where the decisions get made.
Your community is waiting.
Start with your own words. We’ll help with everything after that.
