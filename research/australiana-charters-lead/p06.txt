Press office The stories behind the world’s largest platform for change
Our press team loves connecting journalists with the people using Change.org to start, grow and win campaigns on the issues they care about.
Email press@change.org
Journalists
Want to speak with a petition starter or a member of the Change.org staff team? Email press@change.org .
Decision makers
On the receiving end of a petition and want to respond? Get in touch. Your response is published on the petition and emailed to its signers.
How we can help What our press team can do for you
Connect you with petition starters
Put you in touch with the people who started petitions on Change.org.
Surface national trends
Spot major national trends and fast-growing petitions before they break.
Provide petition data
Share data on petitions, such as where signers are from and how fast a campaign is growing.
Offer expert comment
Analysis from leading experts on people power, online campaigning, and digital politics and engagement.
Coordinate with brands and influencers
Help brands and influencers create petitions for important causes.
Provide speakers
Change.org leadership and movement-building petition starters, available for keynotes, panels and events.
