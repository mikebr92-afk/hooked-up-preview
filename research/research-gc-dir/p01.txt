Gold Coast Fishing Charters Overview
There are many Gold Coast fishing charters , in fact there are 20 fishing charters operators in the Gold Coast area. Don't be overwhelmed by choice - we are here to help you find the option that suits you best. The fishing charters listed below target reefs, estuaries and waterways in the Gold Coast region all year round. Additionally offshore operators target trolling grounds during the summer months.
The amazing array of Gold Coast fishing locations allow you to target Snapper, Pearl Perch, Cobia, Spotted Mackerel, Spanish Mackerel, Marlin, Tuna, Mahi Mahi (Dolphin Fish), Amberjack, Samson Fish, Kingfish, Pearl Perch, Wahoo and various species of sharks.
Half, three-quarter and full day fishing trips are available for groups of between two and 20 anglers. Trips start from only $90 per person and can be customised to suit beginners, expert angers and everyone in between. Local residents, holiday makers and international travellers are all welcome on Gold Coast fishing charters. Individuals, private groups, bucks/hens parties and corporate fishing charters can all be catered for.

Our highly motivated, experienced owner/skippers are committed to providing the best possible Gold coast fishing tour every time.
