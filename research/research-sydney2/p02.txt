What’s on
What’s on
Experience the perpetual brilliance of the Sutherland Shire with a dynamic lineup of events throughout the year. Immerse yourself in unforgettable performances that transport you to another world.
The Sutherland Shire beckons you to step out, explore, and revel in the vibrant tapestry of experiences. It’s time to embrace the excitement and play in the rhythm of this lively community!
Discover what's happening in the Sutherland Shire
Sutherland Shire Council acknowledges the Dharawal speaking people, traditional custodians
of the land, and pays respect to elders past and present. Council extends this respect to all
Aboriginal people living in or visiting Sutherland Shire.
