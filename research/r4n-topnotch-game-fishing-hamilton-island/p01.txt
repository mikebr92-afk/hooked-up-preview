HAMILTON ISLAND PREMIER SPORTS / REEF FISHING CHARTERS
Topnotch Game Fishing is based in the stunning Whitsunday Islands and anchored at the prestigious Hamilton Island Marina. At the helm of this dynamic business is the seasoned team of Captain Russell and Marlene Hicks. The Hicks duo have passionately explored the Whitsunday region for over a decade now and offer their guests a diverse array of Hamilton Island fishing charters. Their commitment to excellence is evident in the impeccable upkeep of their two vessels, The Pirate and Moonshine , both maintained to the highest marine standards. Topnotch Game Fishing beckons you to embark on full-day or multi-day fishing excursion, with their liveaboard fishing trips proving very popular.
Professional Fishing Guides and Topnotch Equipment 
Our experienced guides bring their knowledge of the local waters and a passion for fishing to ensure your safety and success. We provide high-quality fishing gear (rods, reels, lures & assorted tackle) from SHIMANO Australia to enhance your chances of landing the big one with unforgettable memories. Don’t miss the opportunity to experience the thrill of fishing in one of the world’s most stunning locations.
Captain Russell and Marlene Hicks are proud members of the Whitsunday Game Fishing Club
About Topnotch
Hamilton Island’s premier sports and reef fishing charters. Owned and operated by Captain Russell & Marlene Hicks, the backbone of Topnotch Game Fishing. Choose from a variety of fishing charter options from 4-hour, 6-hour, 8-hour day charters to extended liveaboard fishing charters at the world heritage-listed Great Barrier Reef.
