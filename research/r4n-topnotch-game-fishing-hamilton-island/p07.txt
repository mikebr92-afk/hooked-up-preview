Whitsundays Sailfish
Fishing Charters 
Hamilton Island | Light-Tackle Sportfishing in Paradise 
If you’ve ever dreamed of chasing lightning-fast sailfish across the turquoise waters of the Whitsundays, you’ve come to the right place. Topnotch Game Fishing offers premier Whitsundays Sailfish Fishing Charters, targeting these incredible speedsters on light tackle aboard “Moonshine” 40 Black Watch.
With more sailfish caught in recent seasons than ever before, the Whitsundays is fast becoming a must-fish location for anyone chasing the ultimate sportfishing thrill.
Book Online Now 
Why Target Sailfish in the Whitsundays?
• Known as the fastest fish in the sea, reaching speeds up to 110 km/h
• Incredible acrobatics and aerial displays once hooked
• A bucket-list catch for anglers around the world
• Found year-round in the Whitsunday waters – Peak season August to December
• Our crew specialises in finding these sleek predators, giving you the best chance to experience the magic of a sailfish strike.
Sailfish The Indo-Pacific Sailfish (Istiophorus platypterus) is found in the tropical waters of the Coral Sea
The Topnotch Difference
When you book a Whitsunday sailfish fishing charter with us, you’re not just hiring a boat — you’re joining a passionate team with decades of fishing experience.
Fish aboard “ Moonshine” 40ft Black Watch. 
Tournament-grade Shimano tackle.
Marlin Magic lures, Tantrum lures and JB lures.
Experienced skipper & deck crew who know these waters inside out.
Options for a full-day 8-hour fishing charter.
Tag-and-release fishing to help preserve this iconic species.
When is the Best Time to Catch Sailfish?
Sailfish can appear in the Whitsundays throughout the year, but we see them more consistently during certain seasonal runs. If you’re planning a trip specifically for sailfish, chat with our team for the latest updates — we’ll guide you on the best time to book your Whitsunday sailfish fishing charter for maximum success.
Book Your Sailfish Adventure
Don’t just dream about catching a sailfish — make it happen! Book your Whitsunday sailfish fishing charter today with Topnotch Game Fishing and experience the fastest fish in the ocean, right here in one of the world’s most beautiful destinations.
