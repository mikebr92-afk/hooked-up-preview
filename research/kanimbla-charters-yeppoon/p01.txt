The premier extended charter experience
We invite you to join us on our comfortable, spacious and well appointed 25m catamaran Kanimbla as we cruise the magnificent Great Barrier Reef.
Kanimbla Charters operate 7 and 10 day charters to the Swains Reefs and The Coral Sea. The Kanimbla is also available for charter anywhere along the QLD coast.
We pride ourselves on offering superior service with the aim of providing a memorable fishing or diving holiday for our guests.
Kanimbla Charters is owned and operated by Brad & Narelle Watts. Together, with our experienced crew, our success as a team has ensured that our company has grown into a market leader since it began in 1997.
