Get out there 
Catch a feed of fresh fish to take home for lunch! The bountiful Sapphire Coast has some of the best inshore and offshore fishing in NSW.​ Snapper, morwong, kingfish, leatherjacket and flathead are all caught in the pristine waters surrounding Merimbula. 
With years of experience operating in the waters of the Sapphire Coast, we combine local knowledge with high-quality equipment and well-maintained vessels to deliver a reliable and professional experience. 
Our charters are led by experienced local fishermen who know the conditions, seasons and productive fishing grounds across Eden and Merimbula, ensuring you are guided to the right locations at the right time. 
Quality fishing rods and reels provided. No fishing licence required. Fish cleaned and bagged for you to take home and enjoy! 
About our fishing charters 
Our fishing charters operate out of Merimbula from December to July. We offer shared or private charters and cater to all skill levels, from beginners to advanced. All equipment and fishing license included. 
Vessel and equipment 
This charter is on our 14-metre shallow draft vessel, 'Sensational II'. She is in AMSA survey for 50 people however we fish a maximum of 12 people to ensure you have plenty of space. With three 425hp Yamaha engines we get you out to the fishing grounds in comfort and speed. There is an onboard toilet and a canopy to keep the sun off, however there may be wind and salt spray so bring a weatherproof jacket. We are committed to minimising our impact upon the environment, our engine emissions are extremely low and our fuel burn is also very efficient. 
All equipment provided including quality fishing rods and reels. 
Fish species 
If you are looking for that special variety of table fish, be assured that the Sapphire Coast has them. The local reefs are plentiful all year round with snapper, kingfish, morwong, tiger and sand flathead, trevally, nannygai and leatherjacket, not to mention the many varieties of wrasses and gropers that are a bottom bouncers delight. 
The skipper has a variety of reefs to choose from and will take you to the ones that fish best given the weather conditions. 
Fishing instructions and assistance 
The difference between a good tour and a great Merimbula fishing charter is in the quality of the skipper and the deckhand that you have on the day. We take pride in offering the best services to our customers onboard every charter whether that involves professional fishing instruction, or just the right local advice to ensure that you have the best catch possible on your charter. 
Our skippers will gladly provide instruction to beginners to help you get a handle on this great ocean sport as all our Merimbula fishing charters and tour options are geared toward deepening your connection with the ocean. 
What are the best things to do in Merimbula? 
If fishing is not what you're after, 
whale watching on the Sapphire Coast runs from May to November and offers a different experience. You can also try our 
wildlife cruises that run from Merimbula and Eden, which run year-round and target a range of local species. These are good alternatives when 
fishing charters in Merimbula are not your main focus. 
Please be advised that our whale spotting kelpie may be on board. If you have an allergy or this poses a problem for you, please contact us. 
Sea sickness prevention 
This tour takes you out into the open ocean so we recommend sea sickness prevention. It's better to come prepared! Eat sensibly before you travel and ask your pharmacist about sea sickness tablets. 
Sunglasses and hat 
To avoid the glare off the water and sun protection. 
Shoes 
Please wear flat, covered shoes. 
Food 
Bring any food you may like whilst out at sea. 
Warm clothes 
It is typically colder out on the water than on land. 
Weatherproof jacket 
This is an adventure tour and you may get wet. 
Camera 
To capture those magical moments!
