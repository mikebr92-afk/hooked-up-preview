Experience Whale Watching on the Sapphire Coast 
Every year, the Sapphire Coast plays host to one of the most incredible natural spectacles on the planet. 
Tens of thousands of humpback whales pass the coastline during their annual migration, forming the highlight of our Whale Watching Cruises in Eden, Merimbula and Bermagui from May to July (northern migration) and August to November (southern migration).
