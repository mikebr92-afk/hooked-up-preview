Unreel Fishing Charters
Unreel Fishing Charters offers fishing charters in Port Phillip Bay, Western Port Bay and other Victorian coastal locations. The operator describes itself as a no-frills charter business focused on helping guests catch fish, with James Rogers as founder and head skipper, supported by a family-based crew including Tristan Rogers and Dylan Rogers.
Charter options include snapper, King George whiting, calamari, sharks, bluefin tuna, kingfish, reef fishing and tuition charters. Departure points vary depending on the target species, weather and fish availability, so trips can leave from a range of ramps including Carrum, St Kilda, Mornington, Black Rock, Hastings, Stony Point, Sorrento, Rye and Frankston, while some offshore trips also depart from places such as Port Fairy and Portland.
Location:
Various departure points across Victoria. Common Port Phillip Bay and Western Port Bay departure locations include Carrum, St Kilda, Mornington, Black Rock, Hastings, Stony Point, Sorrento, Rye and Frankston.
Opening Hours:
Bookings are available Monday to Saturday all year round. For current details and enquiries see the official contact page: www.unreelfishingcharters.com.au/contact-us .
Cost:
Charter prices vary depending on the trip type, duration and departure location. For current prices see the official prices page: www.unreelfishingcharters.com.au/charter-prices . Gift vouchers are also available.
