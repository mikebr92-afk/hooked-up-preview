I'm Hooked Fishing Charters (Mornington Peninsula)
I'm Hooked Fishing Charters is run by Mal, a qualified charter operator, who is passionate about fishing and brings a positive attitude to his charters. His customers describe the experience as fun, friendly and enjoyable. Years of experience means Mal can bring you to the best fishing spots. He is happy to help beginner through to experienced anglers.
Our fishing charter service is your local gateway to unforgettable fishing adventures. Our expert guides are well-versed in the best fishing spots on the Mornington Peninsula, Western Port Bay, Port Phillip Bay or Bass Straight, ensuring you'll have a day filled with excitement and great catches. Whether you're a novice or a seasoned pro, embark on an unforgettable fishing journey with us.
Our fishing charters target assorted species in Port Phillip Bay, Western Port Bay, Bass Strait and Portland fishing grounds. The local Melbourne fishing charters are typically 5 to 6 hours in length, whilst offshore charter in Bass Straight are typically 7 - 8 hours in length. 
Charter Options
Local Departure from Carrum, Hastings, Rye, Mornington. 
Snapper Fishing Charters 
Season: August to April. Our Snapper dedicated season is October to December. The main snapper fishing season begins in August (offshore), or in September in the bays (Western Port and Port Phillip), but snapper can be caught in winter.
Snapper, Whiting & Calamari Fishing Charters 
Season: All year. Mixed Fishing charters commence from January 1st through to 30th September. We fish according to which fish are biting at the time to try and make sure you bring home a good catch.
Squid Fishing Charters 
Season: All year. 
Whiting Fishing Charters 
Season: Traditionally it is considered December to April. However, you can catch King George whiting all year round in our local waters within Western Port Bay and Port Phillip Bay.
Kingfish Fishing Charters 
Season: January to April. The main kingfish fishing season begins in January either in or outside the heads on Port Phillip Bay and Westernport Bays. Kingfish Fishing charters commence from January 18th.
Tuna Fishing Charters 
Season: In Victoria, outside the heads of Port Phillip Bay, the season is February to May. When departing from Portland, Victoria, the season is June to August. Tuna Fishing charters commence from January 18th. 
Shark Fishing Charters 
Season: All year. Shark Fishing charters commence from January 18th. For more info on how to catch gummy sharks read here.
Operating Hours:
Available weekdays and weekends
Cost:
Prices start from $180 per person.
