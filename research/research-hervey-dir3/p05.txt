Fishing Hervey Bay
Fishing in Hervey Bay is influenced by seasonal changes, tidal movements, and wind conditions. The warmer months are ideal for targeting Barramundi and Mangrove Jack in the estuaries, while the cooler months offer excellent opportunities for chasing reef species offshore. Tides play a crucial role in baitfish activity and predator feeding patterns, with dawn and dusk high tides often producing the best results. Moderate winds are generally favorable, but strong winds can make offshore fishing challenging.
The land surrounding Hervey Bay features a mix of sandy beaches, mangrove-lined creeks, and rocky headlands. These diverse terrains provide varied fishing opportunities, with easy access to popular land-based spots. Many locations offer well-maintained walking paths and fishing platforms, making them accessible to anglers of all abilities.
Boat access to Hervey Bay is excellent, with multiple boat ramps and marinas providing access to the bay's numerous fishing grounds. From small tinnies to larger offshore vessels, a wide range of boats can navigate the calm waters of the bay. Charter services are also readily available for those seeking guided fishing experiences.

Landbased
• Urangan Pier: A historic pier extending into the bay, offering opportunities to catch a variety of species, including Whiting, Flathead, and Mackerel.
• Enzo's On The Beach: Fish the beach gutters at high tide for Whiting and Bream.
• Torquay Beach: Target Flathead and Whiting along the sandy stretches, especially during dawn and dusk.
• Scarness Jetty: A popular spot for catching Bream, Trevally, and Squid.
• Point Vernon: Fish off the rocks for Tailor, Mackeral and Dart.
Offshore
• The Artificial Reef: Located a short distance from the mainland, this reef teems with life and is a great spot for targeting Snapper, Sweetlip, and Cod.
• Wathumba Banks: A shallow sand bank that holds a variety of species. Great for targeting Mackerel, Tuna and Trevally.
• Outer Banks: Further offshore, these banks offer opportunities to catch larger reef species such as Coral Trout and Emperor.
• Fraser Island's Western Side: Fish the gutters and drop-offs along the western side of Fraser Island for a chance at landing some quality reef fish and pelagics.
• Rooney Point: Located on Fraser Island, this is a great spot to target Tuna and Mackeral.
Local Bait Tips
• Yabbies: Pumped at low tide on the intertidal sandflats around the bay.
• Prawns: Net along the creeks and estuaries during outgoing tides.
• Squid: Jigged around the Urangan Pier and other jetties at night.
Boat Ramps
• Urangan Marina Boat Ramp (Hervey Bay): Located inside Urangan Boat Harbour off Buccaneer Drive, Urangan QLD 4655. Managed by the Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR) Queensland and Fraser Coast Regional Council. Features a premier high-capacity 4-lane concrete boat launch ramp equipped with dual central floating universal access pontoon jetties. Protected inside harbour breakwaters, providing safe deep-water launching into Hervey Bay, Great Sandy Strait, and K’gari across all weather conditions. Facilities feature an extensive bitumen car-and-trailer carpark, public restrooms, stainless-steel fish cleaning station, vessel wash-down bay, marine fuel wharf, VMR 406 basecamp, and 24-hour security lighting. Free public access.
• Gatakers Bay Boat Ramp (Hervey Bay): This boat ramp is at Gatakers Bay, off Corfield Street, Hervey Bay QLD 4655. It is owned by the Department of Transport and Main Roads and managed by Fraser Coast Regional Council.
