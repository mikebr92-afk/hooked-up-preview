Hervey Bay fishing charters 
We offer 3 different charter options on our center Cab 23 foot Noosa Cat and we can take a private booking of up to 5 people. 
Please view our charter options and Terms & Conditions before booking your charter.

7Hr CHARTER (3/4 DAY)
This is a awesome day out on the water and we concentrate our efforts sportfishing and baiting, closer to home. Areas we fish are the bay Islands, outer sand banks, Great Sandy Straits, inshore reefs and wrecks. If you are wanting to sportfish with ultralight tackle we can use 3kg line. We will target Golden Trevally, Queenfish, Diamond Trevally, Giant Herrings, Snapper, Tuna, Grunter, Mackerel, Reef Fish and more.
We suggest booking this charter on “Beenleigh II ” If you prefer sportfishing, with a selection of lures and softplastics. We can still use bait and other techniques if you wish. Fish in comfort on-board “Beenleigh II” where we use techniques such as baiting, live baiting, for Sweetlip, Grunter, Trevally, Cod, Blackall, Mackerel, Coral Trout and many other varieties of fish. But if you’re group is small then we can still focus on sportfishing with a selection of lures and softplastics.
Price
7 hr private charter for up to 5 people $2000
9Hr Charter
The better the weather, the further we can travel on the day (if your group chooses to) in search of tasty Reef Fish or Pelagics. Areas we target are the Southern, Northern and African Gutters that are situated wider offshore, isolated reefs, wrecks, wonky holes, Platypus Bay, Hervey Bay and the islands. This is a fantastic adventure day out exploring and fishing these pristine waters that Hervey Bay has to offer and if your lucky we may be cruising only a stones throw away from the majestic western side of Fraser/K’gari Island to or from our fishing grounds.
Prices
9 hr private charter for up to 5 people $2500
9 hr shared charter for up to 5 people $500 pp

Fishing Methods
We use a assortment of traditional and sportsfishing methods to give the anglers their best chance of coming home with a impressive box of tasty fish.
The methods we focus on are:
Baiting/ Float Lining
If you’re chasing quality Reef Fish such as Coral Trout, Scarlet Sea Perch and the prized Red Emperor along with Snapper and Mackerel to name only a few species, then this method can see some great colours being added into the fish box.
Jigging
Jigging for Reef Fish, Spanish Mackeral, Coral Trout, Dino sized Cobias plus a vast variety of Trevally species and Mackerel can be a extremely exciting and effective.
Trolling
Towing around rigged whole dead baits and lures for pelagics fish such as juvenile Black Marlin, Large Spanish Mackerel, Snapper, Tuna, Reef Fish and more.
This gets the adrenaline racing when the drag start screaming.
Popping/ Stickbaiting/ Softplastics
This is our favourite way to fish the bait balls on the surface that are being harassed by Spanish Mackerel, Tuna, Spotty and school Mackerel, Golden Trevally, Cobia, Queenfish and more.
The best way to explain this is
pop, twitch BOOM!!!
Breaksea Spit (Not currently available)
On this extreme fishing charter we will be fishing the top eastern side of Fraser/K’gari Island, Breaksea Spit and beyond using a combination of the techniques listed above for big Red Emperor, Green Jobfish, Rosy Jobfish, Tuskfish, Pearl Perch, Gt, Wahoo, Spanish and a extremely vast combination of both Northern and Southern species.
This charter is for the experienced fisher.
Shark Fishing – Tag & Release
If you wish to on your private charter, why not test your strength and skills on a wide variety of Sharks. We are tagging and releasing to help gather the much needed information in order to keep their numbers at a sustainable level. This option not only gets the anglers adrenaline racing but also gives you the chance to help out with sustainable fishing for the future.
