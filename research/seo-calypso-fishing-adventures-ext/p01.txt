Join Calypso Fishing Adventures for one of the most exciting challenges to face any keen angler, game fishing! 
Located in beautiful Port Stephens, let the Captain and crew take you on an adventure with charters arranged to suit all budgets and abilities.
Calypso Fishing Adventures is fully licensed and surveyed with the Captain and crew having over 30 years' experience in game fishing. You do not need a fishing license to fish with them, they have taken care of all of this for you.
Their fully equipped 47 foot sports fishing vessel can handle the waters surrounding Port Stephens and take you to the right places to fish for Black, Blue, and Striped marlin. With all equipment supplied, all you need to bring is your sense of adventure.
Want a family day out in comfort and style? Why not include a trip to Broughton Island located a couple of miles offshore and 30 minutes north of Tomaree Headland, past the islands that make Port Stephens the iconic maritime destination it is and renowned worldwide. Here they can organise a barbecue lunch on the peaceful and idyllic island for the whole family to enjoy.
At the end of the day, they return you to their resort mooring with windswept hair and memories to last a lifetime.
