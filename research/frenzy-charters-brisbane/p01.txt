Our Brisbane based reef fishing charters cater for individuals & group bookings.
Whether you are looking for a family fun day fishing, or an all out fishing adventure, we will make every effort to see that your Brisbane fishing charter will be one you will long remember and soon want to repeat.
Departing: Wyvernleigh Close, Manly (William Gunn Jetty) just 20 minutes from Brisbane CBD and 60 minutes Surface Paradise Gold Coast.
Pick up available Stradbroke Island (Amity Point jetty).
WHAT TO EXPECT
Year round our bread-and-butter target species are Snapper, Pearl Perch, Tusk Fish and other reef dwellers.
During the summer months, conditions may not be favourable for fishing the reef. (combination of strong East Coast Current and northerly winds).
On the rare occasions we are not able to fish the bottom, pelagic fish will be our target (Dolphin Fish, Wahoo, Mackerel, Marlin and Tuna)Trolling is not every one's cup of tea, but nothing beats the sound of screaming reels.(not for you, avoid booking during the warmer months)Venturing out to the continental shelf may also be possible on NICE days (or on request for private charters) to try our luck on the deep-water semi submerged FADs.

TOUR DESCRIPTION 
Lunch will be served during the day: 
All day free icy cold soft drinks and bottled water. 
Afternoon tea consisting of seasonal fruit and biscuits.
All fishing equipment and bait is included (guests welcome to bring along own gear).
Both ladies and gents separate toilets (full size) on-board.
Fishing vessel is 11.5 metres long and participants can fish from bow to stern, offering more deck space than our competitors.
Commercial registration, licenced food vendor and fully insured for 12 passengers, .
The huge covered deck area and the stability our vessel offers will go a long way to make your Brisbane fishing trip an enjoyable one.
Your individual catch is placed in ice slurry on capture to avoid any risk of spoiling. Fish are kept whole ( not gutted ) and prepared for cooking at home.
Fishing Brisbanes offshore reefs from Cape Moreton (Deep Tempest) to Northern Gold Coast ( Cathedrals).
