WHATS INCLUDED IN THE FISHING TRIP 
Fishing Tackle
We supply everything you need for success on the water, from proven tackle setups to premium bait, so you can focus on fishing while we maximise your chances of a solid catch.
Fish storage & Refreshments
We’ve got plenty of onboard storage to keep your catch in top condition, including large eskies and chiller bags for fish of all sizes. Bottled water is provided, and we supply fish bags so you can take your catch home with ease.
Perfect for All Experience Levels
Whether it’s your first time on the water or you’ve been fishing for years, our charters are designed to suit everyone. We provide guidance where needed and give experienced anglers the freedom to fish their way, making it an enjoyable trip for all.
