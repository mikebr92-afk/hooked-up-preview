Frequently Asked Questions
What Is the Best Bait for Port Douglas? 
Live bait like garfish, or small tuna works best for big game fishing in Port Douglas. 
What’s the Best Time for Top Water Fishing? 
Early morning and late afternoon are ideal for topwater action when fish are most active, however they do bite through the day depending on tidal conditions. 
What Conditions Are Good for Topwater Fishing? 
Calm seas with low wind, and active feeding times create perfect topwater conditions. 
Where to Fish in Port Douglas? 
Great spots include the reef edges, offshore flats, inshore flats, remote gravel patches and near rocky outcrops where predatory fish hunt.
