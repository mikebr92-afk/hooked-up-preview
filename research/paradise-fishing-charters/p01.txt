Our Fishing Trips
We offer game fishing trips to suit all from first timers to seasoned anglers. We are happy to take single person or group bookings up to 10 persons. 5 hour deep sea/reef, 9 hour deep sea/reef, game fishing, specialised deep sea jigging, or soft plastic charters, or even purely sightseeing, witnessing humpback whales or island resort visits. Just call for information for the ultimate beach fishing Gold Coast experience. All charter options include bottled water. Don’t forget your camera! 
• 5 hour Deep Sea/Reef option 
This option is suited to all.
Here, we travel to reefs up to 25 kilometres from the Seaway which are up to 65 metres deep.
These areas are great to catch Snapper, Cobia, Mackeral, Marlin, Tuna, Wahoo, Mahi Mahi and shark.
We use a combination of bottom baits, float baits, live baits and soft plastics.
• 7 Hour Deep Sea/Reef option 
For the more experienced fisher, or for those who want big fish!
We travel to the distant reefs up to 50km off shore and 100 meters deep.
Here you can catch Snapper, Amberjack, Samson fish, Kingfish, Pearl Perch, Job fish, etc.
We use cut fish and squid baits, live baits, and deep sea jigs here.
Better eat your Weet-Bix for this one!
• 9 Hour Deep Sea/Reef option 
Ok, this one is more for the experienced fisher, or for those who want big fish!
