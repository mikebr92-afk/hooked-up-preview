WEIPA FLY FISHING LET’S GET WILD 
Weipa fly fishing charters, catering to everyone from the novice to experienced caster is a specialty service of Far North Fishing Weipa. Owner and guide Kurt Gane has been fly fishing with industry legend Peter “Windy” Wilson, Australian Fly Casting Champion, many times. This allows him to put his best foot forward when it comes to guiding fly charters.
Over his more than 3 decades of fishing, he has gained extensive knowledge of the best times, tides and locations to target different species of fish. If you want a wild and wonderful fly fishing charter, you’ve found it! Contact Far North Fishing Weipa today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au

To maximise space and experience, our 4-day 5-night Weipa fly fishing charters accommodate a maximum of 4 guests, per charter. That’s 2 per boat, with 2 experienced fly fishing guides.

THE GEAR YOU NEED FLY FISHING WEIPA
Wondering what to bring, for your exclusive Weipa fly fishing charter? Here’s some of our top tips and suggestions for gear.
• 8 or 9 weight rod with an intermediate line for fishing coastal flats.
• 8 or 9 weight rod with a floating line for casting popper flies over weed beds and drains.
• 9 or 10 weight rod with a sinking line for blue water fishing or floating line for surface action. 
• White clouser 1/0 hook, pink things, crab and shrimp flies will see you through but feel free to bring your favourites. 
Far North Fishing Weipa have all the sizes of leader required. If you have any queries or concerns, contact us today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au
