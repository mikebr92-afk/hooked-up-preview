Cruises, Sailing and Water Tours 
Experience blue water sport fishing and reef fishing from the pristine waters of the Ningaloo Reef and Exmouth Gulf to the deep blue canyons of the Nor-West Shelf onboard the luxury 15-metre vessel, ‘Teraliza’.
They offer mixed group day trips, private charters and live aboard tours staying three-days and two-nights onboard their vessel.
The Captain “Whitey” has over 35 year’s experience to share on the tours, along with the crew to assist you throughout your tour.
Services
Private Day Charters
$3300 
Evolution Fishing Charters offers exclusive Private Charters, meaning your booking only onboard.
Experience blue water sport fishing and reef fishing from the pristine waters of the Ningaloo Reef and Exmouth Gulf to the deep blue canyons of the northwest shelf onboard the luxury 15-metre vessel, ‘Teraliza’.
You can custom this tour to suit you, which may include fishing for reef or sport fish, swimming and snorkelling. Bring your snorkelling gear if you’d like to explore the shallow reefs (weather permitting). Quality fishing equipment is supplied, and lunch is provided.
The Captain and crew onboard will assist you throughout your tour

Live Aboard Tour
From $13990 to $16990 
Stay on board the vessel ‘Teraliza’ for three days and two nights for the ultimate fishing experience. Onboard, you can explore the many Islands to the North of Exmouth, including Surrurier Island, Bessiers Island, Peak Island and Muiron Islands. 
Your fishing expedition can be tailored to suit you. Typically, Day 1 includes targeting reef species, such as Red Emperor, Rankin Cod, Coral Trout, Spangled Emperor, Saddle Tail, Sea Perch and Gold Band Snapper. Try a variety of baits, jigs and soft plastics. Explore the coral bombies casting stick baits for Spanish Mackerel, Golden Trevally, Giant Trevally and Queen Fish.
Day 2 – head out deep-dropping targeting Ruby Snapper, Flame Tail, and Grey Band Cod. Snorkel the shallow reefs, and explore the remote islands.
Day 3 – troll for Sailfish, Black Marlin, Blue Marlin (in season) Wahoo, Mahi Mahi and Tuna.
The Captain and crew onboard will assist you throughout the tour.
