Contact the Queensland Government 
Call Triple Zero (000) for emergencies: Police, Fire or Ambulance
On this page
Phone
General enquiries
Call 13 QGOV ( 13 74 68 ).
International callers: +61 7 3022 6100 (+10 hours UTC).
If it is an emergency, call Triple Zero (000) or dial 106 if you are using TTY. 
Interpreter services
If you need an interpreter, call 1800 512 451 .
National Relay Service
If you are d/Deaf, hard of hearing, or have a speech/communication difficulty, contact us through your preferred NRS call channel and ask to call 13 74 68 .
Online chat
Chat live with our customer service team during business hours.
8 am to 5 pm (Monday to Friday)*
*Subject to change and not available on public holidays.
