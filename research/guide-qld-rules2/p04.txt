Recreational fishing
Recreational fishing rules 
Understand Queensland’s fishing rules, including size and possession limits, protected and no-take species, and the type of gear and activities allowed.
Responsible fishing 
The future of our fisheries relies on them being used in a sustainable way. Help make sure we have fish for the future and be a responsible fisher.
Qld Fishing 2.0 app 
Download the free Qld Fishing 2.0 app to learn more about your catch, check the rules, receive reminders, find fish aggregating devices (FADs), boat facilities, stocked impoundments and artificial reefs, buy a permit to fish in stocked dams or weirs, and more.
Fishing in dams and weirs 
Dams and weirs that are stocked with native fish specifically for recreational fishing.
Report illegal fishing, abandoned fishing gear and fish habitat damage 
Use this online form to report illegal fishing, abandoned fishing gear and fish habitat damage in Queensland waters.
Fish species guide 
Fish species found throughout Queensland
Marine pests 
Prevent marine pests establishing in Queensland.
Fish aggregating devices 
FADs are human-made structures anchored offshore that attract fish, making them easier to catch so you can have a great fishing experience.
White spot disease alert 
Understand the fishing and movement restrictions in South East Queensland and learn how to identify and report this serious disease.
Using bait responsibly 
Learn how to catch and use bait responsibly to protect Queensland’s waterways and prevent the spread of marine diseases.
Decontaminating fishing equipment and vessels 
Find out how to clean and decontaminate your fishing gear and vessels to stop the spread of marine pests and diseases.
