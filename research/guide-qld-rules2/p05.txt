Illegal fishing activities
Download the Qld Fishing 2.0 app for the latest fishing rules.
You can't:
• sell, trade or barter fish without an appropriate licence (illegal marketing of fish poses a threat to the legitimate seafood market, food safety and sustainability of fish stocks)
• jag or foul-hook fish
• use explosives, poisons or electrical devices to catch fish (divers can only use a powerhead on a spear gun to defend against sharks)
• interfere with authorised aquaculture activities
• obstruct lawful commercial fishing operations, or damage or interfere with fishing gear (including removing fish)
• possess or carry prohibited gear in closed waters unless the gear is stowed and secured
• remove, damage or interfere with Fisheries Queensland markers or signs
• use fishing gear (including nets) across a waterway or navigation channel if it makes more than half its width impassable to a boat or fish.
Report
If you suspect someone is fishing illegally:
• call the Fishwatch Hotline on 1800 017 116 
• or
• report online .
