Private Boat Charter - Rottnest Island
Enjoy a spectacular day on the water travelling out to Rottnest Island onboard the 70ft ‘Lady Grace ’. 
Perfect for your Summer Boat Party, Hens Party, Corporate Staff Christmas Party or simply for a memorable day out with friends & family. 
Lady Grace is fully equipped with everything you need for a comfortable day onboard and our highly experienced & knowledgeable crew will ensure you have an enjoyable day.
Departing from Fremantle, at either 9am or 10am your trip over to Rotto will take about an hour, then depending on the day we can head to the Army Barracks and drop the anchor or we can cruise past Bathurst, Longreach, The Basin and Geordie Bay to our favourite Parakeet Bay. Once we have anchored, we can take you ashore at Parakeet Bay on our tender (please request when booking your charter - landing fees may apply). On the beach, you can play cricket or just sit back and soak up the sun.
The waters are crystal clear and are perfect for swimming and snorkelling and we do have a few sets of snorkelling equipment, sup boards and inflatables on board for you to use.
In the afternoon, enjoy the cruise back to Fremantle for your 4pm or 5pm finish.

Departures 
When booking we will confirm charter start/finish times and departure points. You can choose from the following locations:
• East Street Jetty , East Street, Fremantle (plenty of parking, about 500m from the Left Bank Hotel)
• Sardine Jetty, Mews Road Fremantle (plenty of parking, about 500m from Little Creatures)

Catering 
We can arrange food catering for you or you can BYO food. If you bring your own BBQ food, our crew will cook it for you.
You can also arrange to bring your caterers onboard. You will need to provide caterer contact details so we can touch base with them before the event (no oven on board).
Drinks are BYO (talk to us about the delivery options we have available). Eskies, ice & glasses are supplied.

Set up prior to charter 
Included in your charter hire is a 15 minute window before your charter, where our vessel will be at the departure jetty for you. At this time, we suggest any catering, drinks, ice be delivered for our crew to load your drinks and ice into our iceboxes and handle your catering. At this time, the event organiser is welcome to board vessel to decorate if needed. However, the rest of the party will need to wait on the jetty till the departure time. Once the crew and skipper are in place and ready to assist guests, then boarding can begin. If you require additional, set up time, please request when booking as a surcharge will apply.
• 
Safety 
Our charter company has been operating successfully for over 20 years. Safety is paramount on our vessel. We have strict licensing requirements with AMSA, Dept of transport, DPAW, Swan River Trust, Rottnest Island Authority, Liquor and Gaming, ACMA, DPI, and a few other government departments. Our company operates under a AMSA Safety Management sys-tem, which our crew adhere to. Our vessel is inspected annually by Dept of transport and is in survey for 1B, 1C and 1D.
Our crew have first aid training and are RSA accreditation and will be following RSA guide-lines on charters.
We will need a completed passenger manifest prior to the vessel departing. The form will be emailed to the organiser with charter paperwork.
At the start of each charter, our skipper will conduct a safety briefing.
• 
Drink and Liquor licencing 
On all charters the drinks are BYO but we have a great delivery service for your drinks. The best this is you only pay liquor store prices! When you make a booking with us, we will email you Liquor Barons Melville’s drinks price list, order with Liquor Barons 28 days before your event and they will deliver to the boat COLD drinks 15 minutes before departure. Our crew will meet them and unpack the drinks and load the ice and drinks into our onboard ice boxes. We recommend 50kg of ice for a 4 hour charter. You will need to pay Liquor Barons direct, but we can organise the delivery. Shooters and full bottles of spirits are not allowed, no drinking games etc.
Our crew RSA trained and will be following RSA guidelines on all charters.
We are a registered BYO vessel and as such when our clients are chartering our vessel for a private event such as birthday, anniversary, wedding, corporate staff party, hens party, staff Christmas party or
any celebration where you are supplying alcohol complimentary and are NOT SELLING the alcohol to your guests, then these boat functions are EXEMPT from liquor licensing regulations regardless of
the duration and number of guests. However, you will need to get a liquor licence if your company is supplying liquor to on board guests that are from another company, supplier or client which is then considered commercial gain and a licence is then required. its only a form to fill out and a small fee to the RGL. We can help if you have any queries.
This information has come direct from the Racing and Gaming Department, so please feel free to call our office if you are still unsure.
• 
Waitressing 
We do not supply wait staff on all our charters, it depends on the catering menu that you have selected in order to receive complimentary wait staff.
If you would like us to provide waitressing staff, to serve your food and drinks, please request when booking. $30.00 per waitress per hour will be charged.
However, we do not believe that waitressing staff if necessary if you have chosen the BBQ menu or buffet style menus.
