FISHING TRIPS 
COFFS HARBOUR FISHING CHARTERS
Fish with the No1 Fishing Charter in Coffs Harbour. Come out and enjoy a great day on the water while you are visiting the Coffs Coast. It doesn’t matter if you are a beginner or an experienced fisherman, we take out smaller groups and offer daily fishing trips to cater for everyone.
Fishing Trips for the whole family including the kids, work groups or even if you are on your own, we offer single bookings on mixed charters.
The Coffs Coast has tremendous all year-round fishing and we supply all the fishing gear to fish the reefs and islands around Coffs Harbour.
Coffs Harbour Fishing Charters has local experienced skippers and crew, we have brand-new custom-built boats with full sized toilets.
If you want to catch a snapper, pearlies and flathead on the reefs, Kingies and mackerel around the Islands or head out wider chasingMarlin and Mahi-Mahi we will be happy to show you how good the fishing is on the Coffs Coast.
