OUR CHARTERS
4 Hour Calm Water Fishing - Pumicestone Passage
Join Salty Fishing Charters for a fun and relaxed 4-hour family-friendly fishing adventure through the stunning Northern Pumicestone Passage . Departing from Pelican Waters Marina , this calm water trip is perfect for kids, beginners, or anyone looking to enjoy a scenic and safe day on the water. You’ll target estuary species like bream, flathead, and whiting with help from our experienced local skipper. Our custom-built vessel offers a smooth ride, shaded comfort, and plenty of space to fish. All gear, bait, and safety equipment are provided.
Opt for our afternoon session and enjoy incredible sunset views while casting lines —the perfect backdrop for family photos. Our vessel is fully licensed , so adults can purchase a chilled drink as the sun dips behind the Glass House Mountains.
Create memories, catch fish, and soak up the coastal magic. Book your spot today and share the salty fun with the whole crew!

7 Hour Offshore Fishing
On this trip we visit the 50m to 60m country West of the Barwon Banks mainly targeting bottom species. 
Fishing the deeper reefs off Caloundra up to 15 mile offshore chasing species such as snapper, pearl perch, amberjack, rosy jobfish, tusk fish and more!

9 Hour Offshore Fishing
The best chance at catching a great feed of fish from the Barwon Banks. 
On this trip we head over 40km offshore (weather pending) to the Barwon Banks or further depending of the day. We fish ground rarely touched by smaller vessels and you can expect a variety of species to fill the esky. Approx 1 hour to 1.5 hour travel each way.

12 Hour Offshore Fishing
The Ultimate fishing trip chasing those trophy fish. 
On this trip we do the miles to get the smiles. Depending on the weather, we can end up as far North as the NE end of the Hards, almost in line with Rainbow Beach. We target a diverse range of species anywhere from 50m depth out to 100m.
