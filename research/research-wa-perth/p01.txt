Perth Fishing Charters

» List of Perth Tours 
» List of Perth Accommodation 
The fishing charters that depart from Perth offer some of Australia’s finest fishing experiences that you will ever have. With these waters that fringe the coastline of Perth you can enjoy a range of different types of fishing from deep sea fishing, reef fishing, drag fishing, game fishing and spear fishing. The crew on board the fishing charters know only the best places to take you where you will be reeling in the catch of the day in no time at all. There are fishing charters that cruise just off shore through to charters that let you fish in waters up to 200m in depth where you can fish for saddle back cods, ruby snapper, hapuku, dhufish, queen snapper, red emperor, trevally, Samson fish and more. With reefs, artificial reefs and locations like Rottnest Island , the Montebello Islands and the Abolhos Islands all being just some of the fishing locations that the charters leave to, you really will be in for some great fishing adventures.
There are day fishing charters, overnight and live aboard tours that you can start from Perth. If you wish to fish the locations such as the Montebello Islands and Abolhos Islands these are all live aboard trips where you can travel from anywhere up to 5 days and have everything included from bedding, excellent air conditioned accommodation, lounge area, TV, fishing equipment and bait, all fish is cleaned for you, fully qualified skipper and crew and transfers to and from your mainland accommodation in Perth . Alcohol is always BYO on many of the charters so all you need to take along with you will be your clothes, drinks and plenty of sunscreen for there will be beautiful sunny days ahead of you. Day fishing charters are great for the whole family and all you need to take along will be yourself and you will get much enjoyment out of seeing the kids reeling in their first catch! There is also the Swan River snaking its way through the city of Perth that you may like to enquire about with estuary and river fishing charters that you can embark upon from Perth.
A lot of the fishing charters also offer other inclusions into their price as well such as diving and snorkelling for anyone who wants to see what lies beneath the water level and between the months of July and October you may even be able to see the migrating whales as the pass by. Dolphins are always a common sight while out upon the ocean waters so it will certainly be an experience that you will never forget. With great company, fun filled adventures and plenty of wildlife and marine life to see along the way, a fishing charter is a great way to spend a day in Perth. The luxury vessels are available for private use too, for large groups who want the boat all to themselves as well as being hired out for private functions and social and corporate events.
Night fishing is always a great adventures to join and many fishing charters do this throughout the year which can be a different and exhilarating experience for all. As the sunsets you depart the harbour for the open waters and head for a secret location where you drop in a line or two and wait for the big fish to come out. Whatever you pleasure or desired fishing charter may be, you will find it in Perth, as this city has not only some of the best fishing in Australia but some of Australia’s most sought after attractions. It is a city with it all, so come and arrange your place on one of the fishing charters for a time you will never forget!
