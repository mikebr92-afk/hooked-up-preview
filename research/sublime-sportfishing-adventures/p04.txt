Port Douglas Sportfishing - Reef & Wreck (Galeforce 5.5m) max 3 anglers
The Great Barrier Reef off Port Douglas offers some of the best reef fishing anywhere in the world
Customise your choice of fishing style with Sublime Sportfishing Adventures.
Whether you want to target GT on poppers, mackerel and tuna on light tackle, catch a feed of reef fish, or jig plastics and knife jigs over the wrecks; the options are endless.
Sublime Sportfishing Adventures can tailor to almost any individual requirement and because we are a small business, we can customise your trip to suit your needs. Prices are for whole boat private charter, maximum 3 persons. These trips depart from the Port Douglas public boat ramp on Ashford Avenue.
What will it cost?
We strongly advise you to BYO hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, long sleeve shirt, light lunch (on 3/4 and full days), camera and sense of adventure
Full day Private Charter $1200
Book now – Pick a date and book online! 
Weather Permitting for Galeforce 5.5m 
Please note this coastal sportfishing trip on the Galeforce is subject to favorable weather conditions, with our back-up option being the Daintree River fishing option above.
Upgrade to Apsro – 40’ Blackwatch 
Alternatively, why not upgrade to Aspro, our 40’ Blackwatch game boat for a full day, all weather, reef sportfishing option.
All charters on the Galeforce include
Quality tackle, lures/ bait
Drinking water and soft drinks
Insect repellent
Basic wet weather gear
BYO tackle optional
Hours are a guide only and can be varied to suit anglers, tides and best fishing times
Please note quality lures, rods and reels are supplied by Sublime Sportfishing on a ‘replacement cost if lost or broken’ basis, while hooks, bait and leader are available at no cost
All trips are subject to weather conditions, and we reserve the right to cancel a trip if the weather poses any risk to passengers or crew.
