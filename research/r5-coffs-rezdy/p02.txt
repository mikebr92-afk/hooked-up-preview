CHARTERS
Book your Coffs Harbour Fishing Charter Online
Coffs Harbour Fishing Charters is a locally owned deep sea charter. Coffs Harbour Fishing provides a day out on the water like no other for you and your mates. First timers to the extreme professional all welcome. Our chartered fishing trips take you to Coffs Coast best spots.
Coffs Fishing Charters
We use the best, state of the art equipment to get the most out of your charter. All our fishing gear is high quality and specific to the type of fishing we are doing. We only use gear that we would personally use ourselves.
We have two boats ready a 12mt Steber Custom Flybridge and a 10mt Leisurecat
12mt Steber Custom Flybridge
5 years old and has all the luxuries for a days Gamefishing out on the shelf and beyond.
A proven fish raiser. She is powered by twin 440hp motors cruising comfortably at 24knots its an easy run out to the shelf
10mt Leisurecat
Brand new and recently commissioned is set up for all types of fishing. A big open cockpit for casting, jigging and trolling. A Massive live-bait tank has been installed
Powered by twin Suzuki 300APXX outboards goes like a rocket, giving you more fishing time out on the water.
