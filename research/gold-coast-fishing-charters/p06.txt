Private Charters
Ensure your special occasion is one to remember with a private fishing charter. 
Want to book a private charter? Gold Coast Fishing Charters want to come to the party! The Fat Hooker crew would like to give you a memorable day out on the water. 
Birthday Parties

Bucks Parties 
Having trouble with bucks party ideas? Why not get the boys together and send the groom off with a fishing trip he’ll never forget.
Team Building 
Whether you’re entertaining important clients, holding a corporate event or contemplating something different for this year’s Christmas party. Private fishing charters are a great team building activity to give everyone a chance to network and have fun together.
