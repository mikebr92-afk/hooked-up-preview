Gold coast fishing 
At Gold Coast Fishing Charters we live the adventure everyday! When fishing on the Gold Coast, you will enjoy the personalised fishing experience the Fat Hooker crew provide!
Full or half day fishing charters on the Gold Coast mean you can pack as much excitement into your day as possible, or leave some time free for other things.
Our Gold coast Fishing options cater for beginners through to expert anglers chasing both reef and big game fish!
We provide everything you need to enjoy your day so let our friendly expert crew spoil you! Onboard our boat ‘Fat Hooker’ you’ll use only the best gear available, the latest fish-finders and navigation technologies. Our crew also specialise in corporate, custom and special occasion charters so contact us about your next Bucks party or birthday adventure.
