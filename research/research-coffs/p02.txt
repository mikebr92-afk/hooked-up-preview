Description
Cougar Cat 12 fishing charters offers visitors to Coffs Harbour a unique fishing or whale watching experience. Cougar is a 12 metre long, wide bodied luxury catamaran, purpose built for fishing and whale viewing, with a cruising speed of 25 knots, powered by twin turbo diesel, ensuring a very safe and stable cruise. Cougar has spacious and safe bow viewing areas and two decks, allowing 360 degree views. There is an easy access toilet on main deck. Your local skipper and crew (who have fished the seas in and around Coffs Harbour for over 20 years) will take you to their most prized fishing spots. You'll have a great day out on Cougar Cat 12 - not only will you enjoy the fishing - you may even see whales (migration season) and dolphins.
