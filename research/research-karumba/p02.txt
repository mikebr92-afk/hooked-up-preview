TITLE: Kerry D Charters | Tours | Queensland
DESCRIPTION: Kerry D Fishing Charters is a family-owned and operated tour company based in Karumba, Queensland, offering unique and professional fishing experiences in the stunning Gulf of Carpentaria. With a passion for fishing and the local waterways, we provide private or shared charters that cater to all skill levels – from first-timers to seasoned anglers – ensuring everyone enjoys a fun, safe, and memorable day on the water.

Their two custom-built boats are designed for comfort and functionality, featuring shaded seating, walk-around decks, and onboard toilets, as well as being able to take groups of up to 10 people per boat. Whether you're chasing a personal best or just soaking in the views, they tailor each trip to suit your needs.

Beyond just fishing, they're about creating experiences – sharing local knowledge, exploring scenic coastal areas, and giving guests a real taste of life in the Gulf. Their professional crew is committed to delivering top-quality service and ensuring every charter is enjoyable and rewarding.

Kerry D Fishing Charters is more than a tour – it's a chance to connect with nature, learn new skills, and make lasting memories with friends, family, or fellow fishing enthusiasts. Join them in Karumba and experience the difference of fishing with passion.
