Take Pleasure in a Fun Charter Fishing Trip in Swain Reefs
Experience the reef like never before on an exciting charter fishing trip in Swain Reefs with up to eighteen of your best friends, family, and even co-workers. Our experienced crew will take you on a week-long trip to a remote area that is 120 nautical miles away from the shore. Our fishing trips help you catch different types of reef fish like the coral trout and red throat emperor while gazing at the migrating whales, turtles, and dolphins that swim in the majestic seas.
You will always come back with a great catch and will be able to take home clean and processed fish so that you can cook up a great meal for you and your guests. During your charter fishing trip in Swain Reefs, we will also treat you to generous meals and en-suite cabins that you will feel comfortable and relaxed. You can trust in the services that we offer since we adhere to and follow all guidelines and regulations established by the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority, the Department of Primary Industries and Fisheries, and the Australian Maritime Safety Authority.
We offer a completely customisable experience of a lifetime that you will remember for years to come. In fact, we take pride in being an industry leader when it comes to reef fishing. On your next charter fishing trip to Swain Reefs, you can look forward to enjoying a ride on a luxury vessel, “MV Night Crossing” that will depart from Bryan Jordan Drive and will take you and your guests out to the great barrier reef for the ultimate in relaxation and pleasure.
