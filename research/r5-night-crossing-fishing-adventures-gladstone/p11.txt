Experience Charter Fishing in Gladstone Like Never Before
Gladstone Coral Charters is a third-generation family-owned-and-operated fishing charter that has been in business for forty years. We take pride in providing the opportunity for you and up to eighteen of your friends, family, and co-workers to enjoy a week-long fishing adventure to a remote location that is 120 nautical miles away from the shore. Not only can you catch all the fish that you want but you can take in the beautiful scenery and set sail to some of the most relaxing and majestic areas around when you take a charter fishing trip in Gladstone.
Why not book a charter fishing trip in Gladstone so that you can catch some of the finest fish like coral trout and red throat emperor while watching dolphins, turtles, and migrating whales swim alongside the boat? We are an industry leader in reef fishing and provide en-suite serviced cabins and generous meals for you and your guests. Our crew is experienced and adheres to all regulations set by the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority, the Department of Primary Industries and Fisheries, and the Australian Maritime Safety Authority.
We provide all the bait that you need so that you are guaranteed to catch some of the best fish. For a whole week, you can live aboard the luxury mother ship “MV Night Crossing” where you will not have to worry about cooking, cleaning, or processing your fish. Get excited about booking a charter fishing trip in Gladstone so that you can sit back and enjoy the finer things in life on your fishing adventure.
