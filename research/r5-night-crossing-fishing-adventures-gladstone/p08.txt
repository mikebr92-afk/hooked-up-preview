Enjoy a Riveting Reef Charter Trip in Gladstone
MV Night Crossing is a third-generation family-owned-and-operated reef charter company dedicated to providing exciting fishing trips. We have forty years of experience in charter fishing at the great barrier reef and bring you up to 120 nautical miles offshore. With us, you will be able to enjoy a personalised experience with a maximum of eighteen guests.
Make your charter trip something memorable with a group of your family and friends. Our company is a leader in reef fishing and employs an experienced crew with five crew members on board during your reef charter trip in Gladstone. You can enjoy a fun and relaxed fishing holiday on our luxury fishing vessel in a remote location of your choice.
When you take a reef charter trip in Gladstone, you will be able to fish the swains from one of our six custom-built fishing dories. You won’t have to worry about cooking or cleaning and will be able to sit back, relax, and enjoy fishing the way you envisioned. While you fish, you can take in the beautiful sites and watch as different marine life like whales, dolphins, and turtles pass by to investigate.
Whatever you catch, you can take home and cook up for a delightful meal. You can trust in our expertise since we are regulated by the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority, the Department of Primary Industries and Fisheries, and the Australian Maritime Safety Authority. We will clean and process your fish and make it easy for you to enjoy whatever you catch on your reef charter trip in Gladstone.
