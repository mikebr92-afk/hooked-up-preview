Take a Reef Charter Trip to Swains Reef
If you are looking for a nice getaway, look no further than MV Night Crossing that will take you 120 nautical miles away from the shore to a remote location so that you can enjoy the fishing and the beauty of the ocean. Invite your friends and family to join you on a reef charter trip to Swains Reef to kick back and have fun fishing without having to worry about cooking, cleaning, or navigating your way to the best fishing locations around. We are a third-generation Australian family-owned-and-operated charter company that has been in business for forty years.
We operate a fishing vessel that departs from the Bryan Jordan Drive, and we offer to take you on a six to seven-day fishing excursion. During your reef charter trip to Swains Reef, you will be able to enjoy an experience of a lifetime and have generous meals and en-suite serviced cabins tailored to meet your needs. While you fish, you can enjoy the beautiful scenery and even watch dolphins, turtles, and whales.
We make sure to follow and adhere to all regulations set by the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority, the Department of Primary Industries and Fisheries, and the Australian Maritime Safety Authority. You can enjoy all the fish that you catch without having to clean or process them since we do all the hard work for you. Find the peace of mind that you want when you go fishing during your reef charter trip to Swains Reef.
