MV NIGHT CROSSING
Speccifications:
Length – 24 mt
Roll Control System
Beam – 7mt
Latest electronic Navigation and Safety Equipment
Speed – 9-10 knots
Fully Air-Conditioned
Crew – 4-5
6 x 4.7mt Fishing Dories /Tenders
Passengers – 16-18
Large Snap Freezer
Satellite TV Foxtel
USB DVD CD Player
The “MV Night Crossing” Gladstone’s only purpose designed and built live aboard fishing charter vessel . She has spacious living and deck areas. A large walk on walk off, water level fishing platform with safety rails on her stern. A Deck store room for storing all the fishing gear.
She has a large dining and bar area with hotel style bar fridges, 2 x keg tap facilities with cold water filtered on tap also. Perfect for a cool drink relaxing after a big day’s fishing. The bar is stocked with softdrinks, mixers, tea, coffee, biscuits and cake 24/7.
The accommodation is 9 x 2 berth roomy air-conditioned cabins with ensuites, which are fully serviced daily. All cabins have 2 x large single beds, vanities and 240v power. We provide all your linen and bath towels.
6 x Custom Built Fishing Dories/Tenders (title)
The 6 x Custom built fishing dories/tenders are very comfortable and safe. They are a 4.7mt fibreglass boat with 18HP outboards. Equipped with
VHF radios Dedicated Tracking System monitored by the mothership
Echo sounder/fish finder Good Quality Anchor and Rope
Gaff Comfy Seats
Fish measure Large Fish Esky with pilchards bait and ice included
Rod holders All Mandatory Safety Equipment
To operate our fishing dory/tenders you will require a recreational ship master license (speedboat ticket). All Australian State and Territory Licenses are excepted.
