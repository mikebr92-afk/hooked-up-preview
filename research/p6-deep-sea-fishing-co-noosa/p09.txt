Fishing Charters Noosa
Deep Sea Fishing Charters Noosa
Fishing charters Noosa at their finest aboard one of our 3 vessels Catch22 – DeepSea7 – Deepsea 11 Join Noosa’s #1 deep sea fishing charter on an experience of a lifetime! With over 30 years of experience fishing up and down the Sunshine Coast, Clint Ruster and his deep sea fishing crew can take you on a journey to find the best fish to catch Noosa. You are guaranteed to have an amazing day on the open water and enjoy Noosa’s amazing scenery. We offer 1/2, 3/4 and full day charters to choose from. It can be a great day out for families to enjoy or a group of mates! 
Bottled water, soft drinks and snacks are available and even tea/lunch depending on the duration of your trip! All rods, reels, bait and tackle is supplied onboard but you are also welcome to bring your own fishing gear. We will clean, ice and bag your freshly-caught fish to take home with you and to top it all off, no experience is needed! What are you waiting for? Give us a call on 0411 222 749 or email us at info@deepseafishingco.com.au today to book your noosa fishing charter!
