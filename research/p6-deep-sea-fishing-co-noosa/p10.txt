Noosa Fishing Charters
Noosa fishing charters offer some of the best offshore fishing experiences on the Sunshine Coast!
Clint Ruster and his deep sea fishing crew are 3 rd generation fishermen and can help you catch the amazing fish Noosa has to offer. Noosa has been a hot spot for deep sea fishing for years and it won’t disappoint. Now is the time to book your ½ day (5 hour), ¾ day (7 hour) or full day (9 hour) fishing charter. Perfect for a group of friends, or even a family day out in Noosa!
Bottled water, soft drinks and snacks are available and even tea/lunch depending on the duration of your trip! All rods, reels, bait and tackle is supplied onboard but you are also welcome to bring your own fishing gear. We will clean, ice and bag your freshly-caught fish to take home with you and to top it all off, no experience is needed! What are you waiting for? Give us a call on 0411 222 749 or email us at info@deepseafishingco.com.au today to book your noosa fishing charter!
Check out our Instagram page for more pictures of our amazing fishing charter experiences in Noosa.
