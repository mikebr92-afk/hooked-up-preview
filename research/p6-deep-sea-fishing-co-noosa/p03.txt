About Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa
Established in 1989, we are the leading pioneers in the fishing charter industry and proud to remain the original owners. 
Discover the Legacy of Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa: Experience Adventure on the Water
Welcome to Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa, where generations of fishing expertise meet unparalleled adventure on the waters of Queensland’s Sunshine Coast. Established in 1989, we are a proud third-generation offshore fishing charter operation, dedicated to providing unforgettable experiences for fishing enthusiasts of all skill levels. 
Our commitment to quality and excellence has made us a leader in the fishing charter industry. With over three decades of experience, our knowledgeable team, led by Clint Ruster, is passionate about sharing the thrill of deep sea fishing with you. We believe that the combination of expert guidance and stunning natural surroundings creates an exceptional fishing adventure.
Explore the Rich Fishing Heritage of Noosa
Noosa is renowned for its rich marine environment and diverse fish species, making it a premier destination for deep sea and offshore fishing. Whether you’re looking to catch Coral Trout, Snapper, Mackerel, or even Sailfish, our experienced crew will take you to the best fishing spots, ensuring that you have a successful and enjoyable day on the water.
We operate a fleet of custom-built, comfortable vessels equipped with modern amenities for your convenience. Each charter is tailored to accommodate groups of friends and families, offering half-day, three-quarter day, and full-day options to suit your schedule.
Commitment to Excellence and Sustainability
At Deep Sea Fishing Co. Noosa, we not only prioritize your fishing experience but also emphasize respect for our oceans. We practice sustainable fishing methods to ensure the preservation of our marine ecosystem for future generations.
Join us for an adventure on the beautiful waters of Noosa that you won’t soon forget. We look forward to welcoming you aboard and sharing the joy of fishing in one of Australia’s most beautiful coastal regions!
We also run Noosa Whale Watching Tours
Noosa Whale Watching Tours, proudly owned by Deep Sea Fishing Co (established in 1989), offers an unforgettable marine experience on the crystal-clear waters of Noosa. With over 30 years of expertise in delivering exceptional coastal tours, we’re dedicated to providing intimate and eco-friendly whale watching adventures that bring you closer to the majestic humpback whales (and dolphins!) during their annual migration.
