Barunguba Montague Island Tours
Take a trip to the island with your family
You haven’t had a holiday in Narooma unless you’ve joined us on one of our Barunguba
Montague Island Tours! 
Take a trip out on our comfortable boats. Around 8km offshore,
BarungubaMontague Island 
is one of the most popular attractions on the South Coast. This island is pristine, unspoilt
and surrounded by the bluest waters you’ve seen. Montague Island Nature reserve is also home
to penguins, a resident seal colony and has rich historic links to indigenous culture,
Narooma and the South Coast boating industry.
Barunguba Montague Island is approximately 80 hectares in size and its lighthouse has been
in operation since 1881. On your excursion you’ll discover the secrets of the island
– we know a few, we’ve been taking visitors on Barunguba Montague Island tours
since 1983! All tours involve a 25 minute boat trip, and the same trip back again. On the
way back we may stop for dolphins or whales which can extend the time.
We’ll take you to see the resident seal colony, and along the way you may
encounter pods of migrating whales (Sept to Nov),
vast schools of friendly dolphins, basking sunfish, manta rays, or acres of pelagic fish.
Barunguba Montague Island is well known for its colony of Little Penguins. It’s home to
around 10,000 pairs of them! These birds return to their burrows each evening after a busy
time fishing in the waters in and around the island. This nightly migration can be viewed on
the evening tour to the Island between September and late January.

This package includes the boat cruise and guided island exploration without any swimming or snorkeling, making it ideal for guests who prefer to stay dry while experiencing the island's natural and historical highlights.

Barunguba Montague Afternoon Tour
(with Snorkelling)
The afternoon snorkel tour combines the island experience with the chance to get in the water with Montague Island's resident fur seals. Arrive at 12:30pm for a 1:00pm departure.
You'll land on the island for a guided tour of the heritage-listed lighthouse and lightstation, then enter the clear sheltered waters to snorkel alongside curious, playful fur seals. All equipment is included (wetsuit, mask, snorkel, and fins). Participants should be confident swimmers as snorkelling takes place in open water.
