Overview
MRW Retreat is the perfect base to explore the lush wetlands and the incredible wildlife of the Northern Territory. Mary River is a spot well-known for fishing, croc-spotting, birdwatching, bushwalking and four-wheel driving. 
The waterways of the Mary River wetlands are made up of lagoons, canals, and billabongs – they’re home to a diverse range of birdlife, prize barramundi and the incredible saltwater crocodile.
Mary River is the ultimate place to base yourself for your Northern Territory adventure. Choose from safari tents, powered or unpowered sites.

Cancellation Policy
Booking Policy
All reservations require a bond (deposit) to be paid at the time of the reservation. Without a bond, we are unable to make you a reservation. The minimum bond payable is equal to one night’s tariff. Any changes to reservations or cancellations will incur an administration fee of $39.00. All cancellations must be made directly with the park in writing. Upon cancellation, monies paid can be credited to a client account for future stays. Any money transferred for future stays at the parks will only be valid for 12 months from the original reservation date. No credit or refund will be available for early departures or cancellations once arrived. A credit cannot be exchanged for a refund.
Off-Peak Periods
Cabins
Cancellations received more than 14 days prior to the reservation date, will receive a refund of monies paid minus an administration fee. Cancellations made within 14 days from the reservation surrender the bond (one nights tariff). Any remaining monies can be refunded or applied to a client account. Cancellations made within 48 hours of the reservation, surrender all monies paid.
Sites
Cancellations received more than 48 hours in advance will receive a full credit or refund. Cancellations received within 48 hours of the reservation will surrender all monies paid.
Peak Periods
Cabins & Sites
Full payment may be required by a designated date; this will be supplied upon the reservation (approx. 30 days for peak and selected peak periods). Cancellations made before the designated full payment date will receive a refund of all monies paid minus an administration fee. Cancellations made after the designated full payment date surrender all monies paid.
Third Party Bookings
Booking/cancellation policies for 3rd party bookings may vary depending on the specific website. Please check the booking conditions included in the terms and conditions of the booking website.
