Byron Bay Charter prices
Byron Bay Charters operate seven days per week, weather permitting. Specialising in group bookings and private charters.
Full-day Fishing Charter
Please contact us for pricing 
We offer discounts for group bookings.
Hours: 6am–2pm 
Charter includes:
• All fishing gear
• Lunch
• Fishing permits, if required
• Complementary softdrinks and water
• Ice to put in your esky
On demand, you tell us when you would like to go Whale Watching
• Complementary soft drinks, water, tea and coffee
• As with all charters appropriate clothing for the day is advised
Booking terms and conditions: 
• 100% payment on booking the Charter. Mastercard and Visa welcomed, a
surcharge for all credit card bookings will apply.
• To cancel a booking we require 7 days notice to receive a full refund. We charge a
cancellation fee of 100% after that time. If we cancel the charter for safety reasons (weather and Bar conditions ) we will reschedule or refund your payment.
• All bookings will be confirmed by Byron Bay Charters by 7pm the night before departure, when all weather updates and sea conditions have been assessed.
• As with all our Charters, group bookings are welcomed, we can make up the numbers for people with individual bookings as well.
