Fish the Kimberley Region
Kimberley, by boat
Humbug Fishing runs liveaboard and tag-along charters into the Kimberley region of Western Australia, operating under a WA fishing charter licence in addition to its NT tour operator licences. Charters run aboard Starfire, a 24-metre catamaran mothership, reaching destinations across the East and West Kimberley coast.
Can you fish the Kimberley with Humbug Fishing?
Yes. Humbug Fishing holds a WA fishing charter licence, alongside its NT bluewater and barra tour operator licences, which allows charters to run into the Kimberley region. Not every Top End operator holds the WA licence required to fish these waters. You still need to pay a permit fee, and we arrange that for you so it’s easy to deal with.
Enquire about your next adventure! 
How do you fish the Kimberley with Humbug Fishing?
You can fish the Kimberley two ways. Live aboard Starfire on an extended liveaboard charter, with the mothership as your base for the length of the trip, or bring your own boat on a tag-along charter and tie up alongside Starfire overnight. Our team works with you to plan the trip around your dates, your group, and the species you want to target.
Where do Kimberley charters go?
Humbug Fishing runs charters to fishing grounds across the East and West Kimberley, including Bouganvillea Point, Montgomery Reef, The King George, The Berkeley, Cape Londonderry, and The Mitchell. Each location fishes differently depending on the tide and the season, and our team will help match your dates to the right ground.
How does booking work?
• Get in touch and lock in your dates. Call or email with your preferred dates, group size, and whether you want liveaboard or tag-along.
• We sort everything. Once your booking is confirmed, we handle the provisions, gear, and logistics for the charter.
• Fish the Kimberley coast. Days on the water across remote Kimberley fishing grounds, based aboard Starfire for the length of your charter.
