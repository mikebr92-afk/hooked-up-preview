It is with great sadness that we share the passing of Robert Smith, the heart and Captain of Smithy Fishing Charters.
He was not only a beloved Husband, Son, Father, Brother, and Friend, but also a passionate fisherman who loved sharing the ocean with so many of you. 
Due to this heartbreaking loss, all upcoming charters will be cancelled.
We sincerely apologise for the disruption and appreciate your understanding during this difficult time. We will be in touch directly with affected clients.
Thank you for your kindness, support, and patience as we navigate the days ahead. 
Kind Regards, Smithy Fishing Charters 
Welcome to Smithys Fishing Charters - The Longest Running Fishing Charter on the Sunshine Coast!
DEDICATED TO EXCLUSIVE FISHING CHARTER TRIPS FOR SMALL GROUPS
No tangling up with 12 strangers! Have the boat to yourselves!
17/9/25 First Available Day. 16/11/25 First Available Weekend Day. 
Snapper & Pearl Perch Closed Season 15/7-16/8
Fish the offshore waters of the Sunshine Coast aboard the charter boat “Capricorn” with your local owner operator skipper Robert Smith, known locally as “Smithy”. Operating for over 18 years, he is now the longest continuously working charter boat businesses out of Mooloolaba. 
Centrally located at Minyama on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland we can tailor a trip to suit everyone including individuals, groups, corporate or bucks parties, end of year or Christmas work trips..
We specialise in offshore deep sea, reef, game, sport, jigging and soft plastics for up to 5 people . All fishing equipment, tackle and bait supplied. With changes to the laws in Queensland we can now offer exclusive whale watching, dolphin and birdwatching trips for up to 7 people in a group.
We are fully licensed to fish the “Barwon Banks”, “The Hards” & “The Shelf” and all points in between including many wrecks.
We use and recommend Gary Howard Custom Rods and Okuma rods and reels.
Contact Robert on 0407 574 868 or email info@smithysfishing.com.au to book.
