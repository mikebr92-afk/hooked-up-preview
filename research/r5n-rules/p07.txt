Tinaroo Falls Dam
You need a Stocked Impoundment Permit (SIP) to fish at this dam.
On this page
About this dam
• Tinaroo Falls Dam Road, Tinaroo, Qld 4872 (about 14km north-east of Atherton)
• Part of Barron River
• Owned by Sunwater , which provides information on: • activities and facilities
• fishing vessel requirements
• dam alerts.
Stocked species
• Barramundi
• Sooty grunter
Who needs a permit
All persons 18 years and over must hold a permit when line fishing in SIPS impoundments.
Children and teenagers under 18 years do not require a permit.
Cost
• $13.60 per week
• $64.58 per year
• $46.45 per year if you have a: • Queensland Government Seniors Card
• Pensioner Concession Card, Health Care Card
• Repatriation Health Care Card (Gold Card).
On 1 July 2026 most of our fees and charges increased by 3.4%, in line with the government's indexation policy.
Buy your permit
• Online .
• Phone 1300 575 359 . • Phone 13 25 23 for general enquiries.
• In store at: • all Queensland Australia Post outlets 
• these New South Wales Australia Post outlets • Ashford LPO 
• Casino Post Shop 
• Casino West LPO 
• Inverell Post Shop 
• Moree Post Shop 
• Yetman LPO 
• Boggabilla LPO 
• Kyogle LPO 
• Tenterfield LPO. 
Receiving your permit
The permit is:
• emailed and/or texted to your phone when purchased: • online
• at selected sub-agent outlets
• over the phone.
• emailed within 24 hours when purchased: • over the counter at an Australia Post outlet.
When you go fishing
When fishing in a stocked impoundment, you must:
• carry your permit (digital email, digital SMS, paper receipt) with you
• produce it upon request from the Queensland Boating and Fisheries Patrol.
Dam management
• Tablelands Fish Stocking Society 
• Tablelands Regional Council
