Duration of Crayfish Tours is dependent on many factors including location of the pots, weather conditions and time of season. Whilst we do our utmost to catch the crayfish for you we cannot guarantee that there will be crayfish in every pot pulled. 
We are licensed to catch up to 80 crayfish but under Fisheries WA bag limits you are only allowed to keep up to 8 crayfish per person. 
Please go here for reference ~ http://rules.fish.wa.gov.au/Cray limits
