Whale Watching Adventure
Caloundra, Sunshine Coast
SEE THE WHALES AT CALOUNDRA
Experience One of Nature's Greatest Spectacles
Every winter the waters off Caloundra become one of Australia's greatest natural theatres as thousands of magnificent humpback whales migrate along the famous Humpback Highway.
Join Salty Fishing Charters for an unforgettable whale watching adventure departing from beautiful Pelican Waters Marina. Our experienced local crew will take you offshore to witness these incredible giants in their natural environment while sharing their knowledge of whale behaviour, migration and the unique marine ecosystem of the Sunshine Coast.
Whether it's a whale launching into a spectacular breach, a gentle mother guiding her calf, or the unmistakable sound of a whale exhaling beside the boat, every trip delivers memories that last a lifetime.
Operating during the annual whale migration season from June through October, our tours offer a truly authentic marine wildlife experience only minutes from the coastline. The Sunshine Coast sits directly on Australia's famous "Humpback Highway", where thousands of humpback whales travel north to breed and later return south with their newborn calves, making this one of the country's premier whale watching destinations.

Why Choose Salty Fishing Charters?
Unlike many tourism operators, we're not just seasonal whale watchers.
Our skippers have spent decades working professionally on these waters as commercial fishermen, charter operators and marine professionals. We understand the local weather, currents, wildlife and offshore environment because this is where we work every day.
Our experience means we know where whales are most likely to be travelling while always operating safely and respectfully around these magnificent animals.

A More Personal Whale Watching Experience
One of the biggest differences between our whale watching tours and many others operating around Australia is simple—we deliberately keep our numbers small.
Our vessel carries a maximum of just 12 guests , creating a far more personal, comfortable and immersive experience than the large commercial tourism vessels that can carry dozens, and sometimes hundreds, of passengers.
When you're whale watching, space matters. 
With only 12 guests onboard, everyone has room to move around the vessel, enjoy uninterrupted views and position themselves for the best photographs without fighting through crowds or standing shoulder-to-shoulder with other passengers. 
There are no long queues for viewing areas, no scrambling to find a position at the rail and no feeling like you're simply another number onboard. 
Instead, you'll enjoy a genuine small-group wildlife experience where every guest has the opportunity to see, photograph and fully appreciate these magnificent animals. 
Our vessel has been purpose-built for offshore operations and has spent countless hours working the waters off Caloundra. Unlike many vessels designed primarily for sightseeing, our boat was built to handle offshore conditions safely and comfortably while providing excellent visibility around the entire vessel. 
The open deck layout allows guests to spread out and view whales from multiple angles, increasing your chances of witnessing those unforgettable moments when a whale surfaces unexpectedly beside the boat, breaches on the horizon or lifts its tail before diving. 
Being aboard a smaller vessel also creates a much stronger connection with the ocean itself. You'll be closer to the water, closer to the wildlife and closer to the action. Many guests describe the experience as feeling more like heading offshore with experienced locals rather than joining a commercial tourist attraction. 
Our small group numbers also allow our crew to spend more time with each guest. Whether you have questions about whale behaviour, migration patterns, local marine life or simply want help spotting wildlife, our crew are always available to share their knowledge and passion for the ocean. 
With fewer people onboard, everyone can hear the commentary, engage in conversations and enjoy a far more interactive experience. 
For photographers, the advantages are significant. Fewer passengers mean more freedom to move around the vessel, clearer sight lines and greater opportunities to capture those once-in-a-lifetime images of breaching humpback whales, mothers and calves, dolphins and the stunning Sunshine Coast coastline. 
Our experienced skipper continuously positions the vessel to maximise viewing opportunities while adhering to all responsible whale watching guidelines. Because of our smaller group size, guests can quickly move from one side of the vessel to the other when whales change direction or suddenly appear nearby. 
The result is a whale watching experience that feels relaxed, personal and authentic.

What you may see...
Humpback whales are renowned for their spectacular surface displays.
During your cruise you may witness:
Breaching 
The unforgettable moment when a 40-tonne whale launches completely clear of the water before crashing back into the ocean. 
Tail Slapping 
Powerful tail strikes on the water's surface that can be heard hundreds of metres away.
Pec Slapping 
Whales raising their enormous pectoral fins before slapping the surface.
Spy Hopping 
A curious whale lifting its head vertically out of the water to investigate its surroundings.
Mother & Calf Interactions 
Later in the season mothers often return south with their newborn calves, creating some of the most heartwarming wildlife encounters imaginable.
