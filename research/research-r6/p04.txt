The Eden Community Access Centre is a community owned not-for-profit organisation operating under a management committee. The Access Centre is a member of the Local Community Services Association, which connects rural and regional communities.
We provide a range of services to our local community, individuals, businesses and community service providers.
*The Eden Community Site wishes to acknowledge and show our deepest respect for the original shared custodians of this land, the Yuin and Monaro people. We would like to extend that respect to Elders, past present and emerging.
