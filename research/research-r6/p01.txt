About
Request. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a beginner, our fishing education programme can cater to any skill level. Our experienced crew will provide guidance and assistance throughout the trip to ensure that everyone has a great fishing experience.
For those looking for a different kind of adventure, we offer exciting whale watching charters. Witness the breathtaking southern migration of the Humpback Whales and get up close and personal with these majestic creatures. Our tours also allow you to spot other marine wildlife such as dolphins, seals, and seabirds. We even offer homemade pastries and refreshments on our whale watching tours.
If you're a fan of sports fishing, we have charters specifically tailored for you. Spend a thrilling 8 hours on the water, targeting species such as Kingfish, Snapper, and Tuna. Our experienced crew will provide all the necessary gear, including a combination of live bait, jigs, and bait. Enjoy a homemade morning tea to keep you energised throughout the day.
For the ultimate game fishing experience, look no further. Our game fishing charters offer a full day of adrenaline-filled action, targeting species such as Marlin, Tuna, and Mahi Mahi. Join our share charters or book a private charter for a more exclusive experience.
If birdwatching is your passion, we offer private charters dedicated to birdwatching. Explore the stunning coastal and marine habitats of Eden and spot a variety of bird species in their natural habitats. Our knowledgeable crew will provide insights and information about the different bird species you encounter.
At Freedom Charters, we also offer unique Ashes to Sea Scattering services to provide a respectful and meaningful way to bid farewell to your loved ones. Choose our 3-hour charter to scatter ashes at sea and create a memorable and peaceful tribute.
Our charter prices are affordable and offer great value for money. We have both shared and private charters available to suit your preferences. For reef fishing, our shared charters start at $150 per adult for 5 hours, while private charters start at $1,000 for 5 hours. Our whale watching tours start at $95 per adult for 3 hours, with special family rates available.
To make a booking or inquire about our departure times, please contact us at 0415 602 446 or email us at chrisscatt@bigpond. Com. Our friendly team will be more than happy to assist you with any questions or special requests you may have.
You can find us at 28 Cosham Close, Eden NSW 2551, Australia. We are conveniently located in Eden Harbour on the South Coast of NSW, offering easy access to plentiful reefs and the open ocean.
Choose Freedom Charters for an unforgettable experience on the water. Our friendly and experienced crew will go above and beyond to ensure that your day is memorable and enjoyable. Join us for a fishing charter, whale watching tour, birdwatching excursion, or any other adventure of your choice. Experience the freedom of the ocean with Freedom Charters.
