Aquilla Fishing Charters
Great outdoors / beaches 
Things to do 
Tour 
Transport and Tours 
Aquilla Fishing Charters is an award-winning fishing charter operator in Wollongong. Enjoy a fishing adventure as they take you to the seas in one of the most stunning areas of New South Wales. 
With three generations of experience in Wollongong, they know some of the best fishing spots that will guarantee you an unforgettable day on the water. 
No fishing licence is required. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or just starting, they offer the perfect trips that are catered to individual skills and interests. 
Aquilla Fishing Charters provides high-end fishing gear, bait, a delicious sausage sizzle, and your catches of the day – gutted and cleaned, ready for you to take home. Just bring your enthusiasm for fun (and beers), and let their experienced crew take care of the rest.

Endeavour Dr, Wollongong

Bar Lina Wollongong 
Bar Lina Wollongong 
Inspired by Lisbon’s bustling tabernas, Bar Lina brings fire-fuelled Iberian dining to the heart of Wollongong. Expect bold Portuguese and Basque flavours, flame-kissed plates, natural wines and a soundtrack spanning Prince to Biggie. Cosy, lively and full of character, it’s made for good food, wine and long nights.

UOW Science Space 
UOW Science Space 
Discover the wonders of science at Science Space, NSW’s only dedicated immersive science experience. Explore more than 90 hands-on exhibits, live science shows, workshops and an advanced planetarium. Plus, step into the new Energy Futures Zone to discover 25 interactive exhibits exploring the future of energy. 
Find Out More 
Harbourfront Seafood Restaurant 
Harbourfront Seafood Restaurant 
A Wollongong favourite for over 30 years, this waterfront dining spot sits on the edge of Belmore Basin, serving fresh local produce with harbour and coastal views. Choose from the daily seasonal Market Menu or à la carte, with lunch and dinner available seven days a week.
