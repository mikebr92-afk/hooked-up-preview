Go Fish Yamba Charters
Fishing & Whale Watching Adventures from Yamba
Experience the waters of the Northern NSW coast aboard ‘Achiever’, our 15-metre charter vessel. Join us for offshore fishing charters targeting a variety of prized species, or experience the annual humpback whale migration on one of our seasonal whale watching cruises.
IMPORTANT BOOKING NOTICE
Captain Frank will be away from 8 October to 5 November 2026. 
During this time, Kevin will be running private group fishing charters only. 
Regular bookings resume from 6 November. 
ABOUT US
Established in 2022, Go Fish Yamba Charters is a local father-and-son business based in Yamba, NSW, offering offshore fishing charters and seasonal whale watching cruises.
Join us aboard Achiever , our 15-metre charter vessel, for offshore fishing adventures along the Northern NSW coast, targeting everything from snapper and pearl perch to kingfish, mackerel, mahi mahi and more.
During humpback season, we also offer whale watching cruises, giving you the chance to experience these incredible animals along the Yamba coastline.
Local knowledge. Quality gear. Plenty of room. Great days on the water. 
What to bring?
• Hat and sunglasses (suncream is supplied) 
• Sensible footwear, spray jacket, extra layers for cold weather
• We supply water, soft drinks and snacks, but you are welcome to pack your own food and drinks for the morning. We are based at Yamba Shores Tavern, where they serve great food and beverages. 
• Please keep bags size to a minimum. 
• Please have an empty esky in your vehicle to put your fish in when back in port. We have plastic bags if needed. Ice is available at the Tavern bottle shop if needed.
