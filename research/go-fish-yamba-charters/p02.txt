About Go Fish Yamba Charters 
Established in 2022, Go Fish Yamba Charters is a family-run, local business born and bred in Yamba, NSW. Run by a father-and-son team with a lifetime of experience on the water, we’re passionate about sharing the incredible coastline and offshore fishing grounds around Yamba with locals and visitors alike. 
Offshore Fishing 
Our fishing charters explore the waters from Evans Head to Sandon and east to the continental shelf , targeting a wide variety of prized northern NSW species including snapper, pearl perch, mulloway, kingfish, mackerel, mahi mahi and more . 
Whether you're an experienced angler or stepping aboard for your first offshore fishing trip, we aim to provide a relaxed, enjoyable and memorable day on the water. 
All fishing gear is supplied, with quality Shimano rods and reels spooled with braid , giving every passenger the equipment they need to get stuck into the fishing. 
Meet "Achiever" 
Our 15-metre (49ft) Alufarm vessel, Achiever , is one of the largest fishing charter boats on the NSW North Coast. 
Powered by a Volvo Penta D12 650HP engine, Achiever offers plenty of room, stability and comfort offshore, with capacity for up to 12 passengers . Her size and spacious layout allow everyone to fish comfortably without feeling crowded. 
Whale Watching 
During the annual humpback whale migration, we also offer seasonal whale watching cruises from Yamba. 
Our afternoon cruises head offshore between Woody Head and Angourie , giving passengers the opportunity to see humpback whales, dolphins and the spectacular coastline from the water. 
With a maximum of 12 passengers, our whale watching tours offer a more relaxed, small-group experience aboard Achiever. 
Local Knowledge. Local Family. Local Waters. 
Go Fish Yamba is more than just a charter boat. We're a local father-and-son operation with a genuine connection to Yamba and the ocean surrounding it. 
From chasing quality fish offshore to watching humpback whales cruise along our coastline, our goal is simple — to get you out on the water, have a great day and give you an experience worth remembering. 
Come aboard Achiever and see Yamba from the water.
