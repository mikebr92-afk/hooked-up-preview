Why Fish With Us 
We pride ourselves on EFFORT and PASSION, making every experience personal, private and one to remember. Each adventure remote and one of a kind - visiting world heritage listed rainforests, coastal beaches, Tinaroo Dam and rivers with scenery you have to physically see to comprehend. 
All charters are owner-operated, backed by a money-back guarantee with expert guides who live and breathe fishing with one goal - to bring your fishing dreams to life. 
We currently hold a master permit covering from the Daintree River to the Hinchinbrook Channel and every river in between. This gives us pure flexibility to operate where conditions are best, guaranteeing an amazing experience time and time again.

Meet the Team 
• 
Brodie Quaas
FOUNDER & HEAD FISHING GUIDE 
Deciding to go all-in on his dream, Brodie turned his love for fishing into his livelihood. Moving from NSW to QLD with no experience on catching Barramundi, he committed himself to learning every river, creek, and body of water in the surrounding areas of Cairns. Having now caught more barra’s than he can count, Brodie’s passion now lies in giving you the fishing trip of a lifetime!
• 
Charlie Hard
RISING STAR FISHING GUIDE ‍
Charlie is the definition of passion when it comes to Barramundi fishing! If he’s not working, sleeping, or eating, you bet he’ll be out fishing for Barra’s somewhere. Which is exactly why he’s the perfect fit for your charter. His passion is next to none and you can trust he will deliver a fun day on the water!
• 
Sophie Price
ADMINISTRATION 
Having caught a few decent Barramundi herself, Sophie knows what it’s all about, which makes her the perfect person to run the back end of the organisation. From making a booking to enjoying one of her famous chicken wraps on the boat, she’ll make sure your trip runs smoothly and that you are well looked after.
CancelLation policy 
• Weather Cancellations: If a charter is cancelled by Tropic Barra Adventures due to unsafe or adverse weather conditions, guests will receive a full refund or may choose to reschedule at no additional cost.
• Guest Cancellations: If a guest cancels a booking for any reason, a voucher equal to the amount paid (including deposits and full payments) will be issued. This voucher can be used to reschedule a future charter date .
• Late Cancellations: If a booking is cancelled within 21 days of the scheduled charter date and a new date cannot be arranged, all payments will be forfeited .
Frequently Asked Questions 
• 
How many guests can you take? 
We have 4 vessels and 4 guides prepared to make each trip private and personal! Each boat can take up to 2-3 guests. For group bookings 10 people max.
• 
Do you take group bookings? 
Yes! Up to 10 guests over 4 boats.
• 
What makes us different? 
It’s not just what we do—it’s how we do it that sets us apart. Every trip is personal and tailored to our guests. We pride ourselves on effort and passion, backing every trip with a money back guarantee.
• 
How can I contact you? 
You can reach us anytime via our contact page or email. Or to discuss a multi day trip or group booking, wer’e always just a phone call away.
• 
Where should we stay? 
Depending on where you’re fishing, we have multiple accommodations we recommend in Cairns, Port Douglas, Tinaroo and Mission Beach.
