GO FURTHER. FISH LONGER. JUST DOON IT.​ 
​ 
If barramundi is what brought you to the Top End, why stop after one day? 
Our multi-day barramundi trips are built for anglers who want more time on the water, more water to explore and more chances to find that fish of a lifetime. 
With multiple days on the water, we can take our time, follow the conditions and focus on what the barra are doing. 
From sight casting barra and casting tight to structure to covering productive water with the right lures, every day is about putting you in the best position to land a Top End barra. 
​ 
YOUR TRIP. YOUR TARGETS. YOUR ADVENTURE. 
The beauty of a multi-day trip is having the time to adapt. 
Conditions change. Tides move. Water levels rise and fall. Barra switch up where they're holding and how they're feeding. 
With several days on the water, we can adjust our approach, explore different areas and keep chasing when the bite is on. 
And when that big barra finally eats, you'll know exactly why you came all this way. 
​ 
BUILT AROUND THE BARRA 
No two trips are exactly the same. Your days on the water will be shaped by the season, conditions, tides and what the barra are doing. 
We'll put the time in, work the water and chase them where they're holding. 
Expect plenty of: 
• 
Sight casting barra 
• 
Lure fishing 
• 
Exploring remote Top End waterways 
• 
Early starts and long sessions 
• 
Big hits, hard fights and hopefully a few fish of a lifetime 
​ 
GO FURTHER WITH A MOTHERSHIP 
For anglers wanting to take their multi-day adventure even further, there is also the opportunity to fish via mothership, including opportunities around the Tiwi Islands during the Build Up. 
​ 
Fishing from a Mothership gives you more time on the water and the ability to reach further, more remote fishing spots that are harder to access on a standard day trip. 
​ 
Spend more time fishing and less time travelling, then come back to the mothership to relax and enjoy fully cooked meals with everything taken care of for you. 
Everything is supplied, including an onboard cook, so you can focus on what you came here to do: fish.
