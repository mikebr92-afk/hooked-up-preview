What is Port Douglas VIP?
Get first access to VIP event tickets, special dining packages, last minute accommodation and limited
access experiences by joining VIP.
VIP Members will be given the opportunity to accumulate Port Douglas points when booking
accommodation, travel, tours, dining and more each visit to Port Douglas. These points will enable
savings to be made on the visitor's next trip to Port Douglas.
