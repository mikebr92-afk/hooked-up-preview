Fully Inclusive Dundee Beach Fishing Charter Packages
Book a 2-7 day full inclusive extended fishing charter staying at 'The Beach House” or 'Oasis”.
We are the local Dundee Beach Fishing Charter Community: established in 2002, Anglers Choice have been fishing the water off Dundee for over 22 years and live locally at Dundee Beach.
Experienced Guides: Our highly experienced team knows the best remote spots Dundee Beach has to offer. From barra, to reef to pelagic action, we'll take you where the fish are biting.
Vessels: We offer 6 custom built vessels all in 2C Survey ranging in size from 7.2 m to 9.2m and can accommodate solo anglers or larger corporate fishing charter groups.
Accommodation: We have the two best properties at Dundee Beach. Our Beach House offers unbeatable views over the Timor Sea and our 2nd property Dundee Oasis truly is a tropical Oasis.
The Food: Come for the fishing and stay for the food! Our two fabulous Chefs whip up delicious cuisine using quality local ingredients. Seriously great food.
Our Team: Our staff are deadest legends.
One Stop Shop: Our aim is to make this process an easy one! Airport pick ups, transfers, accommodation, gourmet meals, stunning accommodation, daily room serving including a washing service - not to mention stocked icey beer eskys! We really do take the stress away from your Dundee Beach Fishing Charter.
So what are you waiting for? Get in touch and let's reel in some big fish and great memories with a multi day fishing adventure.

Our Fishing Packages 
Learn more about our fishing packages below. 
Multiple Day Packages
We offer multiple-day packages that are personalised to the experience you want. This can include Barra Fishing, Sport Fishing, and Reef Fishing, all within the same charter. There's no better way to experience premier fishing at Dundee Beach. 
Beachfront Accommodation
Enjoy true beachfront accommodation at the Anglers Choice beach house. In addition to a comfortable and relaxing coastal location, we provide an on-site chef who will arrange all your meals. 
All Inclusive
Simply put - we take care of everything! This includes transport to and from Darwin. Just arrange flights to the airport and we'll handle the rest. We're all about offering genuine hospitality and a relaxed, stress-frree fishing package. 
The Anglers Choice Way
We pride ourselves on offering the best hospitality in the Top End. Our clients come as customers and leave as great friends.
It is common to find the fishing guides helping customers learn new knots and techniques in the lounge after dinner or flicking through photos and downloading images of a great days fishing.
Your pre-purchased beer is packed down on ice in big eskies on the balcony each day and substantial quality meals are served on the balcony each morning and night.
Nothing is too much trouble at the Beach House. We look forward to welcoming you soon at Dundee Beach and sharing with you the little piece of paradise we call home.
