THE LIFE OF BRIAN! There are many elements that can affect barra fishing including rain, wind, temperature and tides.
After two past unsuccessful trips to Darwin (not with Anglers Choice), Brian was desperate to catch a barra! He originally booked to join us at Dundee Beach to fish in March but the wind was playing havoc and there was no way we were risking a 3rd trip in harsh conditions. We both agreed we should postpone his fishing charter till April. 
Fast forward 30 days and we pick up a very excited Brian from the Darwin airport. The pressure was on. It was on Brian who felt he was jinxed and it was on us and Guide Josh to help Brians dreams come true. Brian had also refused to eat barramundi until he landed one himself. Fresh Barra was on the menu that night, was Brian going to starve? That was the question on everyone’s lips.
We are very PLEASED to announce that Brian has a full belly and joined the Barra Club with this very solid 90cm saltwater barra!!! He also landed some very respectable threadfin salmon around the 90cm mark.
Did we mention that this was Birthday celebration? Happy Birthday Legend, you earned that beer!

Anglers Choice Supporting Movember & Mens Mental Health Since 2003, Movember has funded more than 1,250 men’s health projects around the world, challenging the status quo, shaking up men’s health research and transforming the way health services reach and support men.
Movember is one of our chosen charities and each year we donate a Darwin fishing package for a staff member or customer to raffle to raise funds for this worthy cause. This year our fishing guide Ian and some of his crazy generous mates ( https://www.facebook.com/themoustacheskibrothers ) rode jet skis from Newcastle to Harvey Bay (1200km) raising just over $30, 000 for the Movember Foundation. Great effort team Moustacheski and we are proud to be a part of your achievements! The lucky raffle winners were Bill and Mel and they joined us at Dundee Beach last week to claim their fishing charter prize. Mel was overall boat champion landing some very nice fish including some solid barramundi. #movember

• 09/04/2024 - Emma Cartwright 0 Comments 
Million Dollar Fish: Fishing for a Fortune Did you know that TWO of Anglers Choice past customers have been lucky enough to go home $10,000 richer by landing a red tagged barra! How cool is that! We are now excited to announce that the time frame to catch the Million Dollar Fish has been extended and the odds are better than ever!
The Million Dollar Fish competition draws fishing enthusiasts to Dundee Beach from all corners of Australia. Here’s what you need to know about its extension:
1. Season 9 Extension:
• The NT government has extended Season 9 of the Million Dollar Fish competition until 30 April 2024. 
• This means that eager fishers have more time to cast their lines and try their luck at reeling in the elusive Million Dollar Fish.
Prize-Tagged Barramundi:
• In Season 9, over 100 prize-tagged barramundi have been released into five incredible fishing regions in the NT: Darwin, Dundee, Kakadu, Arnhem Land, Katherine, and the Tiwi Islands. 
• Among these, there are now 80+ red-tagged fish worth a whopping $1 million swimming in the Top End waterways.
• Additionally, lucky anglers can also snag goodies from the Lucky Prize Pool.
Year-Round Excitement:
• While the official season runs from 1 October to 31 March, the eight fish carrying tags worth $1 million remain active year-round for those who registered for Season 9. 
• So, whether you’re a seasoned pro or a novice angler, grab your mates and head to the NT for a fishing charter of a lifetime at Dundee Beach.
• Remember, it’s not just about the cash prize; it’s about the thrill of the catch, the camaraderie, and the unforgettable memories created along the way with the team at Anglers Choice.
