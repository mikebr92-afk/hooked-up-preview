BOOK YOUR ADVENTURE TODAY 
Get onboard with Offshore Charters WA and come and catch some fish! We supply all the fishing gear, bait, tackle, cold water and ice. Our charters are swell/wind based and the times of departure can vary. We operate under a strict fish for the future policy and the current limit is 2 demersal fish per person. Once boat limit has been achieved the fishing charter will cease. If you have booked for the fish/cray combo tour sit back and relax while we pull the pots for you and give you an educational tour of the crayfish lifecycle and rich history of the West Australian Coast fishing industry.
