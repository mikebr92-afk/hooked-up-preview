Our fishing charter boats
We provide comfortable, safe and enjoyable charter boat fishing to the Great Barrier Reef and deep sea. Our fishing charter boats include Blue Whaler a 42-foot Randell, Aqua Cat a 48-foot Randell and the latest edition to our fleet, Serranidae a 55-foot Conquest.
Our vessels are fitted with the latest in electronics, safety and fish finding equipment – over 4000 GPS fishing marks! And are fully equipped with an undercover decking area, seating for all passengers, swim platform and full-size toilet facilities.
Serranidae 
Serranidae is a 55ft Conquest and is surveyed to carry up to 25 passengers. For comfort, shared charters are capped at 14 guests, while private charters are best suited to around 15 guests. Additional guests may be accommodated on private charters by prior arrangement, with an additional per-guest charge.
She features a large, covered deck, interior and exterior seating, and toilet facilities onboard. Serranidae cruises comfortably at 14 knots.
Aqua Cat 
Aqua Cat is surveyed to carry up to 23 passengers. For comfort, shared charters are capped at 14 guests, while private charters can accommodate up to 15 guests. Additional guests on private charters may be accommodated by prior arrangement, with an additional per-guest charge applying.
She cruises comfortably at 14 knots and features a large, spacious covered rear deck. Aqua Cat is a 48ft Randell, providing a stable and comfortable platform for fishing trips.
Blue whaler 
The Blue Whaler is surveyed to carry up to 23 passengers, however, for comfort we cap the number to 12 on share charters and Private Charters. The Blue Whaler boasts a comfortable interior. She has a cruising speed of 14 knots and is very stable for her size. Blue Whaler is a 42ft Randell.
