The Cairns Reef 
Fishing Crew 
A family-owned business, fishing is in our blood. Learn all about who we are and our history. 
Cairns Reef Fishing is a multi-award-winning, Australian-owned, family business with over 15 years of experience operating in the stunning waters off Far North Queensland. We are proud to have been recognized with awards from both the Best of Queensland Award Program and TripAdvisor, placing us in the top 10% of operators and experiences.
Our fleet of fully equipped, purpose-built vessels—Serranidae, Aqua Cat, and Blue Whaler—are staffed by a long-term, dedicated crew, all of whom undergo rigorous training to uphold the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and customer service. This ensures that every trip is not only enjoyable but unforgettable. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or a first-timer, our charters are suitable for all experience levels, and we welcome families, with trips recommended for children aged 10 and up.
At Cairns Reef Fishing, fishing is in our blood, and we are committed to providing the best possible experience on the Great Barrier Reef. For more information or to book your next adventure, give us a call at 0427 400 027 or email us at [email protected] . We look forward to welcoming you aboard!
Ready for your Cairns fishing trip? Discover the ultimate adventure with Cairns Reef Fishing. Expert crew, top-notch gear, and prime spots await. Book now for an exhilarating day on the reef!
