Cairns reef fishing 
CAIRNS REEF FISHING 
Come fishing with us! Cairns Reef Fishing is Far North Queensland’s leading fishing charter. We take pride in giving our guests an enjoyable deep-sea fishing experience at the Great Barrier Reef on board our fully catered, purpose-built charter boats. 
Book Now 
Shared charters 
Our all-inclusive Full Day Shared Reef Charter is an awesome day out on the beautiful waters of the Great Barrier Reef chasing some of our iconic reef species. 
MORE 
Private charters 
Enjoy the luxury of having your own skipper and crew on a Private Reef Fishing Charter. Enjoy a full day on the Great Barrier Reef with the option to snorkel.
MORE 
Our Fishing Gear 
A full range of quality fishing gear is provided, including our preferred supplier Shimano, as well as handlines for those who, like our crew, prefer this method. We hand make all our own rigs and are constantly updating our fishing methods and equipment. Our knowledgeable crew can also happily show you how to use all of the gear.
