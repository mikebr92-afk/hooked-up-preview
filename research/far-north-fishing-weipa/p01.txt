WEIPA’S BEST FISHING IS WAITING FOR YOU 
Weipa’s best fishing is what we deliver. It’s a big claim, backed up with lots of big fish, bigger smiles and a swag of 5-star customer ratings. Our range of premium, four-day and five-day Weipa sports fishing charters, cater to all styles of fishing and experience levels. We’ll chase a variety of fresh and saltwater species, including Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Mangrove Jack, GTs, Permit, Blue Bastards, Tuskfish, Trevally and many more; up the river, across the flats and out to sea. 
This is where some of Australia’s finest fighting and eating fish live, and from novice to experienced angler, we’re committed to delivering Weipa’s best fishing trips and charters for you. Whether it’s your first visit north, or you make regular expeditions to our frontier town, we won’t let you down. Contact Far North Fishing Weipa today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au

WEIPA’S BEST FISHING FAR NORTH FISHING 
Far North Fishing Weipa is the preferred fishing charter operation in Weipa. With 100% 5-star social media reviews and repeat, return clients, we’re growing more popular by the day and in 2021, we’re offering more customised charters than ever before.
As well as delivering Weipa’s best fishing, our four-day and five-day charters are fully inclusive. From airport transfers, to accommodation, meals, boats, fishing guides, snacks and refreshments, we can do it all, so you don’t have too.
Our custom charters are tailored to meet your needs. From different fishing styles, to preferred catch and locations, we’ll work harder to deliver it in style. Our itineraries and times are flexible, because when you book a charter with us, you’re booking the boat for your exclusive use. Early start with early finish, can do.
