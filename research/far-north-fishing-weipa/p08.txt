WEIPA SPORTS FISHING AS GOOD AS IT GETS 
Weipa’s sport fishing is second to none. This remote township, located on the western cape of the Gulf of Carpentaria and east cape of Cape York Peninsula, is bordered by two big rivers running out to sea. This enables us to deliver a very versatile and exciting fishing experience for our guests. There’s a huge variety of fish species living here, many of which grow to their biggest size. 
You could be chasing metre Barras and Mangrove Jacks up the rivers in the morning and jigging up Fingermark and Tuskas in the blue water, that afternoon. Whether you’ve got a specific fish to check off your list or are just looking to have an experience like no other, we’ve got you covered. Contact Far North Fishing Weipa today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au 
4 DAY CHARTERS
Far North Fishing Weipa have 4-day, 5-night sports fishing charters. Maximum of 6 guests per charter. 3 per boat, plus 2 experienced fishing guides. BYO rod & reels or use ours.

5 DAY CHARTERS

WEIPA SPORTS FISHING
Some of the most sought after species for sports fishers are found here. Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Mangrove Jack, Queenfish, Tuna, Mackerel, Trevally and many more.

BARRA FISHING
Barramundi fishing Weipa is as good as it gets! We catch Barras everywhere from the freshwater lagoons and creeks, to the rivers and estuaries, and all the way out to our coastal beaches and rockbars.
