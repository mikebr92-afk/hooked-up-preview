WEIPA FISHING CHARTER WITH US 
Weipa fishing charters that are customised to suit your ability and requests, is what we specialise in. Premium four-day and five-day charters, fishing from Weipa every day. Our pristine waterways, from the remote rivers and lagoons, to the flats and blue water, are teeming with some of the world’s best game fishing species and at Far North Fishing Weipa, we love nothing more than seeing our customers land their trophy fish. 
We live the spirit of adventure up here. Our country is remote. A place that is only accessible by boat or plane for a few months of the year. The population is small and the waters are an unspoiled, untapped fishing paradise. We’ll show you the best bits in our premium Weipa fishing charters, which include accommodation. Contact Far North Fishing Weipa today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au

WEIPA FISHING OUR CHARTERS 
Far North Fishing Weipa specialise in Weipa sports fishing charters and fly fishing charters, customised to your fishing ability and requests. When you book a charter with us, you’re booking the boat exclusively and have a say in where we go and what style of fishing you’d like to do. 
As our Weipa fishing charters are fully customisable, we can easily adapt our schedule to suit the bite and conditions. We can arrange an early continental breakfast and be on the water by daylight, to get home earlier for a relaxing afternoon. Or we can do a later pickup, and fish into the sunset and twilight. 
Far North Fishing Weipa will adjust its schedule to suit your preferences, ensuring you get the best possible chance at catching some great fish, while having an awesome time. We pride ourselves on our customer service, making guests feel comfortable and welcome. We’ll try our very best to give you an unforgettable experience, that leaves you wanting more. 
FAR NORTH FISHING WEIPA CHARTERS 
We specialise in premium, 4-day and 5-day fishing charters. Our itineraries are flexible to suit the weather, tides and your preferences. An example day with us, might play out like the following.
Fishing Itinerary
• 6:30am – 7:15am: Breakfast.
• 7:15am: Your fishing guide collects you.
• 9:30am – 12pm: Morning tea and lunch are provided but we don’t have set times. We ask you if you’re hungry and go with the flow.
• 4pm – 4:30pm: Arrive back at the ramp. 
• 4:45pm: We’ll drop you off at your accommodation, where you’ll be greeted by a platter and cold beer. This is your time to sit back and reminisce on the day you just had, recharging to do it all again tomorrow.
Transfers to and from the airport are included in our packages, and once you have landed you will be taken directly to your accommodation to settle in. For more information contact Far North Fishing Weipa today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au 
Please note: pending availability, we can sometimes accommodate 1 or 2 day charters. Contact us for more information.
