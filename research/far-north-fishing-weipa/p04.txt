WEIPA FISHING CHARTERS FIVE DAY TRIPS 
Five-day fishing charters Weipa maximise our ability to showcase the diversity of locations, fishing styles and the variety of fish, that thrive in the warm waters of the western Cape. These premium charters include exclusive use of our custom-built fishing boats, experienced local fishing guides, accommodation, meals and transfers. When you book a Far North Fishing Weipa charter, it’s all about the fishing, in amazing locations, which have had little touch from the rod and reel. 
The fish are big and plentiful, with lots of fight. Your arms will be sore and your smile wide, at the end of every day on the water. Our guides are absolutely committed to exceeding your expectations, with service and support. We love nothing more than making your fishing dreams come true. Contact Far North Fishing Weipa today, to book one of our five-day fishing charters. Call 0417 098 732 or email fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au

WEIPA FISHING CHARTERS FIVE DAYS / SIX NIGHTS 
Our Weipa five-day fishing charters are all about maximising your fishing, experience and comfort when in Weipa. Our township is a small outpost on Queensland’s western cape, home to approximately 3,500 people. It sits on the shores of Albatross Bay, between the Mission and Embley Rivers. What do we do here? Fish! The warm waters of our estuary rivers and the Arafura Sea are bursting with over 40 species of fresh and saltwater fish, just waiting to be caught by you.
We’re the most popular Weipa fishing charter operator and take care of all of the details for you. From the accommodation (see options below) and meals, to experienced guides, boats, rods and reels. There’s a range of fishing styles available, including sports and fly fishing, and you’ll be impressed by what’s on the bite. 
We keep our numbers small (maximum of 6 guests for sport fishing charters and 4 guests for fly fishing) and the experience big. When you choose Far North Fishing Weipa you can expect premium service, delivered with a smile. For more information on our Weipa five-day fishing charters, contact us on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au 
FISHING CHARTER ACCOMMODATION OPTIONS 
Far North Fishing Weipa are pleased to offer our guests a choice of three accommodation options, subject to availability. You can expect comfortable beds, good showers and great tales from your time in Weipa. 
The Fishing Lodge is the premium choice and comes with its own private chef. It’s a two-storey property overlooking Albatross Bay, with 5 bedrooms and spacious communal areas, including a large deck. 
There are also self-contained beachfront villas, which can accommodate up to 4 guests each. Or a range of motel rooms, with various bedding configurations. Weipa accommodation books out early, so confirm your choice quickly to secure your preferred place.
