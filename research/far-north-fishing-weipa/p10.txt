MOTHER SHIPPING WEIPA FISHING CHARTERS 
Far North Fishing Weipa specialise in accommodated fishing charters from Weipa, however, we also offer mother shipping charters. Our mother shipping charters start and end in Weipa, with your floating hotel being a 65ft Power Cat. This one was designed and developed for long range cruising and reef exploration, delivering a comfortable ride in fully air-conditioned comfort. Our fishing boats are with us all the way, getting you to where you need to be, to catch the big ones. 
These charters can be booked for a minimum of 4 days and a maximum of 6 guests. All meals, accommodation, fishing guides and transfers are included, you just need to book your flights to Weipa. As with all our charters, we’ll customise the style of fishing and destinations, to suit your preferences. For more information contact us today on 0417 098 732 or email fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au

MOTHER SHIPPING FISHING WEIPA CHARTERS 
Weipa mother shipping fishing charters tailored to your style, interest and itinerary, that’s what we deliver to our guests. To maintain our standard of premium charters, we’ve partnered with another local business to run mother shipping charters.
During your day of fishing, you’ve got the same great Far North Fishing Weipa guides, boats and advice, but at the end you retire to the mother ship for relaxation and refreshments. 
The 65ft vessel has 4 staterooms, each with ensuite, communal dining and lounge, with wide wrap-around decks. She comes with her own chef, so you’ll be kept in good stock during your mother shipping charter with us. For more information, contact us on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au 
THE MOTHER SHIP FEATURES & SPECIFICATIONS 
Built in 2009 this vessel has been kept in immaculate condition and was designed specifically for long range cruising, the waters of the Great Barrier Reef and Cape. 
The bedding configuration varies across the 4 staterooms. There’s 1 King (or 2 Singles), 1 Queen, 2 Double and 3 Singles. All are air-conditioned for your comfort. 
She’s kitted out with the latest electrics and navigational equipment, with redundancies including onboard water maker and back-up generators.
