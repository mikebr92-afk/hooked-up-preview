Weipa Fishing 
Weipa fishing is awesome. As you can see from the photos across our website, the fish up here are big. They’re also bountiful, from our freshwater rivers and lagoons, to coastal beaches, flats and inshore reefs. At Far North Fishing Weipa our charters accommodate all fishing styles, including sports fishing and fly fishing, and all experience levels. 
Our premium charters are inclusive of guides, accommodation and meals. We generally specialise in 4-day and 5-day charters, but can also accommodate longer charters if needed. We keep our numbers small and experience big. We look forward to taking you fishing in Weipa. For more information contact us today on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au 
SPORTS FISHING
Weipa is one of Australia’s premium sports fishing destinations. You could be chasing metre Barras and Mangrove Jacks up the rivers in the morning and jigging up Fingermark and Tuskas in the blue water, that afternoon.
