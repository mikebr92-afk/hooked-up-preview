PRESS RELEASE
Port Douglas Launches Official Travel Planner App 
Port Douglas, QLD | 29 August 2025
Visitors to Port Douglas now have a new way to plan their tropical getaway with the launch of the Port Douglas Travel Planner App, the official mobile guide to the region.
The free app, available for iOS and Android, puts the very best of Port Douglas in the palm of your hand. Featuring a live Four Mile Beach cam, instant access to live availability for tours and experiences on the Great Barrier Reef and in the Daintree Rainforest, restaurant reservations, accommodation pricing, events, shopping, and local news, the app is designed to make visiting Port Douglas easier and more memorable.
“Port Douglas has always been known as the gateway to two World Heritage Areas, the Great Barrier Reef and the Daintree Rainforest,” said Sam Cullen, publisher of Port Douglas Magazine and creator of the app. “Now, travellers can have all the essential tools in one place — whether it’s booking a reef trip, checking what’s on, or simply seeing the beach conditions live before they go.”
The app integrates seamlessly with the Travel Planner website (tourismportdouglas.com.au), offering users a streamlined way to research and book local tours, keep up with events, and even shop for unique Port Douglas souvenirs.
