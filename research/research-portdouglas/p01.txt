About the business
Welcome to Out n' About Sportfishing Port Douglas 
In need of some Reel Therapy?

4 Hours Tour
Morning: 8:00am-12:00pm or
Afternoon: 12:30pm-4:30pm)
------- 
Port Douglas is home to some of the most diverse fishing experiences from the Great Barrier Reef to river and estuary systems. 
The team at Out n About Sportfishing have a Stabicraft 609 and a 6.0 metre Amara Centre Cabin, both very well appointed, safe, roomy and comfortable to get you where you need to be fast maximising your time out with line in the water! They can cater for groups of up to 12 guests in total, across 2 boats. Perfect for wedding parties, corporate groups or large families, tours can be designed for your individual needs. 
Both boats are equipped with top shelf rods and reels and as they both have high sides prove to be a family friendly day out on the water even for the little ones. Live/fresh bait, refreshments, insect repellent and wet weather gear is supplied on all trips for passenger comfort. 
With expert guides, quality gear and plenty of great marks no matter what the weather is doing they can get you out n about and onto some awesome fish. 
They cater for estuary trips chasing barra, jacks, salmon, fingermark, tarpon and queenies whilst out on the reef trips coral trout, red emperor, cobia, nannygai, Spanish mackerel, giant trevally and a host of other species. 
Specialising in shared and private charters, half day, full day or even night fishing they can customise a charter to suit you. 
Give Ash a call on 0409 204 985 and check out their website at www.outnaboutfishing.com.au
