Interested in advertising? 
Published three times a year in January, May, and September, PORT DOUGLAS Magazine prints 20,000 copies per issue, with each edition enjoying a full four-month circulation. 
Locally, it is distributed across Port Douglas, Daintree and Cairns areas in hotel rooms, lobbies, cafes, and waiting areas as the ultimate holiday read.
Beyond the region, visitors are encouraged to take copies home to share with friends and family, while our Sydney print partner handles direct distribution to travel agents and wedding expos nationwide.
