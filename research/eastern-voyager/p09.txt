Whether you’re an individual, small group or 20 people, you’re welcome to book with us. Our trips are based on a minimum of 15 and maximum of 20 people. As we may receive emails or phone calls from people wanting to reserve a spot, we will need to confirm your booking based on a first-in-first-served priority.
You can either book online here, or email us directly at bookings@easternvoyager.com.au . This trip is ideal if you would like to return to the Swain Reefs or experience it for the first time. It’s a great opportunity to share stories and meet new friends who love fishing. The price shown here is per person for an eight day/seven night trip to the Swain Reefs.
If you would like to join a waiting list for any availabilities that come up throughout the season, please fill out the Enquiry Form below. We will be able to get back in touch with if any dates become available. Otherwise, you can contact 0436 640 197 , or email us at bookings@easternvoyager.com.au .

Overview
Your booking will need to be confirmed with our bookings officer first in case any offline bookings have been made on the same day (on a first-in-first-served basis ). Once we confirm your booking is available, a non-refundable deposit of $750 per person is required to confirm your booking.
Once processed, we can then look at setting up a payment plan if required (usually four pre-arranged payments) to pay the balance of the trip off by six (6) weeks before the expected departure date.

What to bring
• No Travel Insurance included - please organise your own.
• Fishing gear - you must bring your own, as none is provided. (A suggested list of tackle can be found on our Useful Links tab.)
• Tackle World Gladstone - 07 49723 692 offer (advice and terminal tackle) & Compleat Angler Gladstone (offer advice, terminal tackle to full set up if required) 07 4972 7283 
• Beverages
• Poly airline boxes are available for your flight home, charges apply (notification prior or on departure applies).
