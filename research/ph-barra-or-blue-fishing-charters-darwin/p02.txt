About
Barra or Blue is Darwin’s premier Fishing charter company
Get the Barra or Blue Experience
Take the Darwin fishing trip you’ve always wanted with the help of Barra or Blue Fishing Charters. We have all the boats, rods, reels, equipment and the best guides you need to have a great day on the water.
At Barra or Blue Fishing Charters, we can cater for any of your inland or offshore fishing requirements including Barra fishing and Bluewater fishing. The owner, Lincoln is a Fishing Guide with over a decade of experience and has the local knowledge required to help you angle your next catch. You have the freedom to customise your fishing charter to any waterway within a 200km radius of the Darwin area, just ask us and we will cater to your preferences.
With the best fishing guides and vessels in the Territory, Barra or Blue Fishing Charters has everything covered, so you can focus on hooking into your catch of a lifetime. Browse our bookings page for your next fishing charter.

Meet The Team
Lincoln 
Lincoln is an experienced Fishing Guide who caught his first Barra at age 3 growing up on the banks of the Ord River. He has been fishing in the Territory for over a decade and knows the waters like the back of his hand. Lincoln will make sure you have an enjoyable day and is happy to pass on much or as little as you require. Barra fishing is Lincoln’s forte and he has a strong passion for putting people onto his favourite species of fish.
Kurt 
If a Billfish is on your bucket list then Kurt is your man. He has won multiple fishing comps across the top end as a skipper and champion angler including a career highlight winning all 3 Territory Billfish tournaments in one year, 2020!
Born in the Kimberley Kurt grew up fishing and has travelled back and forward to the NT chasing Barra to Billfish for over 20 years.
