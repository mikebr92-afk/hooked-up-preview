COMING OCTOBER 2026 
If you’re looking for unforgettable Great Barrier Reef fishing trips, we run action-packed adventures that put you right in the middle of some of Australia’s most incredible offshore fishing grounds! From reef edges and open blue water to hidden coastal hotspots, every trip is built around chasing quality fish in epic locations. Whether you’re completely new to fishing or already obsessed with it, our guided fishing charters in Queensland make it easy to relax, enjoy the experience, and get stuck into the action from the moment you step onboard. 
Offshore Fishing Trips on the Great Barrier Reef 
Our offshore fishing trips on the Great Barrier Reef are all about big scenery, hard-fighting fish, and unforgettable moments on the water. Expect explosive surface action, powerful reef species, and exciting offshore encounters while fishing one of the most famous marine environments in the world. Every Great Barrier Reef fishing tour is different depending on the conditions and season, which keeps every trip exciting and full of surprises. 
Charter Options to Suit Your Adventure! 
We offer a range of charter options so you can build the fishing trip that suits you best. From quick day trips to longer multi-day adventures, there’s something for everyone looking to experience a true Great Barrier Reef fishing charter. We also offer personalised experiences, including family fishing trips in Cairns , father-and-son fishing adventures, and fully tailored private trips for those wanting a more exclusive experience. Our private fishing Charters in Cairns are perfect for families, groups of mates, or anyone wanting a more immersive day on the water! 
Great Barrier Reef Fishing Charters & Offshore Adventures 
Our Great Barrier Reef fishing charters open up a whole different side of Far North Queensland fishing. Alongside reef fishing, we also run exciting offshore adventures that showcase why we’re known for some of the best deep-sea fishing charters in Queensland. Whether you’re targeting reef species, exploring offshore waters, or simply soaking up the scenery, these trips deliver the perfect mix of adventure, excitement, and unforgettable fishing moments! 
Explore More Than Just the Reef 
While the reef is a major highlight, our charters also explore some of the best fishing destinations across Far North Queensland. Trips can take you through Cairns , Port Douglas , the Daintree River , and inland to Lake Tinaroo for trophy barra fishing . This variety means you can experience everything from offshore reef fishing to remote rainforest rivers all in one unforgettable adventure. 
Book Your Fishing Charter 
Whether you’re planning a family getaway, a father and son fishing trip , or an all-out offshore adventure, Tropic Barra Adventures has a trip to match. From reef and offshore experiences to river and barra fishing, our charters deliver unforgettable days on the water across Far North Queensland!
