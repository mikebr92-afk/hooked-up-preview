If you’re looking for unforgettable Daintree River fishing, Tropic Barra Adventures delivers action-packed trips through one of the wildest and most scenic fishing locations in Far North Queensland! Surrounded by ancient rainforest, winding creeks, and untouched waterways, every trip is built around adventure, incredible scenery, and chasing quality fish in remote country. Whether you’re completely new to fishing or already hooked, our guided fishing in The Daintree makes it easy to get out there and enjoy the action from start to finish! 
Barramundi Fishing Daintree River Adventures 
Our barramundi fishing in the Daintree River is all about explosive strikes, tight structure fishing, and unforgettable moments on the water. Expect hard-fighting barra, aggressive mangrove jack, queenfish, jungle perch, and more, depending on the season and conditions. Every Daintree fishing charter is different, which is exactly what makes fishing in this region so exciting! From calm river drifts to fast-paced sessions when the bite turns on, there’s always something happening out here. 
Charter Options to Suit Your Adventure! 
Our one-day options are perfect if you’re after a quick but unforgettable day on the water, similar to a one-day fishing charter in Cairns. If you want to explore more of Far North Queensland, our longer adventures, similar to a multi-day fishing tour in Cairns , allow you to fish multiple river systems and really experience the region. For anglers chasing trophy fish, our giant barra fishing charters focus on targeting metre-plus barramundi in some of Queensland’s most iconic fishing spots! 
Reef & Offshore Fishing, Including the Great Barrier Reef 
It’s not just rivers and barra… Alongside our Daintree fishing tours, we also run reef and offshore charters , giving guests the chance to explore the incredible waters of the Great Barrier Reef . These trips offer a completely different style of fishing, targeting reef and offshore species while surrounded by one of the most spectacular marine environments in the world! Whether you’re after river fishing or offshore excitement, there’s a trip to match your adventure. 
Explore More Than Just the Daintree 
While the Daintree is one of our most popular fishing destinations, our fishing charters also operate across other incredible Far North Queensland locations. Trips can take you inland to Lake Tinaroo for trophy barra fishing, down towards Cairns , or up through Port Douglas and the surrounding coastal waters. This variety means no two trips are ever the same, and every adventure brings new scenery, new species, and new fishing opportunities! 
Book Your Fishing Charter 
Whether you’re after a relaxed day exploring rainforest waterways or an action-packed barra adventure, our guided fishing charters in Queensland deliver unforgettable days on the water. From river fishing to reef and offshore adventures, Tropic Barra Adventures offers the ultimate river fishing charter experience right here in the Daintree.
