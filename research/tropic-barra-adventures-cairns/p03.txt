Whether you're planning a quick day on the water or the fishing trip of a lifetime, we have a charter to suit you. 
Choose from action-packed one-day adventures, extended multi-day expeditions exploring some of Far North Queensland's best waterways, or our famous giant barra fishing charters for the chance to hook a trophy-sized barramundi! As a specialists in guided fishing charters in Queensland , every trip is made to give you the best possible fishing experience, no matter your skill level.
Our charters are perfect for family fishing trips and unforgettable father-son fishing trips, giving everyone the chance to experience the thrill of fishing together. From a relaxed river-fishing charter through stunning rainforest scenery to an exciting guided river-fishing tour chasing barra and other iconic species, our experienced guides make every trip fun, welcoming, and packed with adventure for anglers of all ages!
Looking for the right charter? Choose from our range of adventures, including: multi-day fishing tour in Cairns , one-day fishing charters , and private fishing charters in Cairns .
