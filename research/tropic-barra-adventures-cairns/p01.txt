Our Fishing Trips in North Queensland 
We offer unforgettable fishing trips in Far North Queensland’s most iconic locations, including Lake Tinaroo , Cairns , Port Douglas , the Daintree River , and areas near the Great Barrier Reef . Adventurers can choose from a range of charters, including one-day adventures, extended multi-day trips, and specialised Giant Barra charters targeting trophy-sized barramundi! 
With private river charters and small group sizes, every trip delivers a fully immersive experience that allows anglers and families alike to explore remote rivers, estuaries, and offshore waters. Whether you’re chasing barramundi in rainforest-lined waterways or heading out for reef and offshore fishing, each charter is designed to maximise time on the water while enjoying the incredible scenery and our fishing boat tours in Queensland! 
full day river charterS 
Our fishing tours from Port Douglas and fishing charters in Cairns offer some of the most stunning scenery you will ever see... coastal runs, world heritage-listed rainforests, and simply incredible landscapes that you will not find anywhere else in Australia. 
The fishing? Even better! Our saltwater days see some epic numbers of Barra when the stars align, sometimes seeing 50 + Barramundi in a single day's fishing, Queenies on topwater showcasing insane aerial displays, and white knuckled, tight country Mangrove jack pursuits and crystal clear jungle drifts for some of the most spectacular sight casting jungle perch and sootie grunter fishing, Australia has to offer! 
To top this off, we have EXCLUSIVE access to the only place in Far North Queensland where Saratoga are accessible.

tinaroo giant barramundi Fishing tours 
We have INSANE success rates on meter plus giant barramundi and have proudly been stamped on the map as one of Australia’s best Giant Barramundi fishing charters, with our results speaking for themselves. 
In 2025, we saw 168-meter-plus barramundi hit the deck in both the fresh and salt, 12 of which were in excess of 120cm and a new record of a 132cm MONSTER on our LAKE TINAROO GIANT BARRAMUNDI fishing charters, as seen on IFISH .
