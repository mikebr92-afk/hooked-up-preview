Darwin’s Premier Reef, Sport & Barra Fishing Charters
Offshore Boats, winner of the 2024 Tour & Transport Operator Northern Territory Brolga Tourism Award, is Darwin’s premier Barra, reef and sport fishing charter operator. We pride ourselves on delivering exceptional service and unforgettable fishing experiences. Our team of expert local guides operates three high-speed, custom-built vessels, all fully compliant with 2C AMSA commercial survey standards. With a focus on customer satisfaction, safety, and passion for fishing, we offer you a top-tier, fishing adventure. Whether you’re an experienced angler or a first-timer, our goal is to provide you with fishing experience in the stunning Top End that you will remember for years to come.
Check out our reviews for Darwin Fishing Charters on our Facebook or TripAdvisor .

About us
Winner of a 2024 Brolga NT Tourism Award, Offshore Boats is Darwin’s premier reef and sport fishing charter operator. We take pride in providing the best fishing experiences for our guests, whether you are a pro of a first-timer. Our team’s commitment to customer service, local knowledge, quality catering, and passion for recreational fishing, along with our high speed, comfortable, offshore vessel, has led to our 5-star reputation.
