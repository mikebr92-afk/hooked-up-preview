Screw Loose Fishing Charters
Welcome to Screw loose Fishing Charters. 
Family owned & operated business that has been running fishing charters in Lakes Entrance for over 15 years. The Skipper of Nellie is Brick Carstairs who has been a fisherman in Lakes Entrance his whole life. Originally a commercial fisherman, Brick turned to charters in 2000 and has never looked back.
Vessel: Nellie
The vessel is originally from NSW, Sydney that was being run as a charter boat. Brick purchased the boat in 2000 & drove it down the coast where the boat was continued to be used for the same purpose. The vessel has been upgraded in 2017 to have 2 new 200 horsepower Honda output motors.
Nellie is a 37 foot catamaran that can hold up to 21 passengers. Toilet facility is located on board. 
Minimum of 6 adults are required for charter to take place.
All bait and tackle are supplied on board during the crabbing and fishing experience.
