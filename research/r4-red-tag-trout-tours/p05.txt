The ongoing adventures of a passionate wild trout fly fishing guide based in Tasmania,
Australia's best trout fishing destination.
Ray Curran guides for his one-man guiding operation Red Tag Trout Tours which caters to flyfishers from all over the world, who share a common goal: getting a wild brown trout to hand.

Tour Options
Ray Curran, owner operator of Red Tag Trout Tours since 2023, has been fly fishing Tasmania’s wild brown trout rivers, streams and lakes for over 34 years but has been a trout fisherman since 1975. Since 2021 he had been the House Guide for Roger Butler as he got to know Roger’s clients, rivers and streams and customs prior to taking over Red Tag with Roger’s retirement in 2023.
Ray specialises in the traditional fly fishing techniques of stalking/walking these waters introducing his clients to feeding/sighted wild trout predominantly using the dry fly, nymphs and dual rigs.
His passion is his job and his aim is to have all his clients experience the excitement and success of taking their own wild brown trout (or rainbow) especially on the (dry) fly.
Red Tag Trout Tours conducts all-inclusive guided wild brown trout, sea-run and rainbow trout fly fishing tours throughout Tasmania. In addition we run a choice of introductory fly fishing workshops and corporate fly fishing programs.
These can be single days to an extended (choice of) accommodated packages! (Even the evening’s relaxing drink over dinner is included).
Red Tag’s Point of Difference! We are Tasmania’s only specialist rivers & streams fly fishing operation for wild trout tours and teach the stalking / wading skills required to succeed in such fly fishing environments through our introductory fly fishing workshops or one on one tuition.
Tasmania’s Point of Difference! Tasmania’s guided trout fishing industry is guide driven not lodge dominant. This is because our industry has been developed by individuals who are passionate about our fish, the fishery and Tasmania’s environment through the value added services we offer our clients.
Red Tag Trout Tours is no exception and prides itself on quality service and exclusive, intimate and productive wild trout fly fishing experiences.
We offer a personal service so your experience starts from the moment you make contact with us — the person who plans your trip is the guide who delivers the product.
So what do we offer?
• Fully guided fly fishing tours – specialising in rivers, streams and lake margins for Tasmania’s wild brown trout, rainbows and sea-runners. Traditional fly fishing styles of stalking and presenting your fly to the sighted/feeding wild brown trout.
• From one day to multi day tours, it’s your choice to look at rivers/streams and or private lakes or a mix of both.
• Top-quality fly fishing on Tasmania’s finest fly fishing rivers and streams throughout the season.
• Fly fishing tuition – Learning to fly fish with options for one-day or extended workshops.
• Gear and equipment assistance, sales and advice.
Our all inclusive packages cover:
• All fly fishing gear, including waders, as required.
• Choices of approved accommodation.
• Fully catered with local produce and homemade treats.
• Hot and cold refreshments, with a beer at the end of the day and dinner wine.
• Fishing licenses as we are a licence agent for the Inland Fisheries Service.
• Transport & transfers [to/from airport or greater Hobart area].
• Fly tying introductory workshops to our top flies.
• Red Tag Trout Tours is fully accredited with both industry, TGALT Inc., and tourism (TICT) authorities.
On the waters I guide, dry fly (after early October) is the norm, not the exception and if you like your own space, light gear, a variety of river and stream conditions from small runs to large pools, fly fishing to beautiful small wild browns up to 4lb+ surprises and want to give it a go, bring your own magic wand — or use ours — and have some fun.
