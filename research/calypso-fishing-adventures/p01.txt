Welcome Aboard Calypso Fishing Adventures 
Calypso Fishing Adventures — proudly operating as Marlin Fishing Australia — delivers world-class offshore fishing experiences aboard the iconic Calypso , a 47-foot O’Brien built specifically for serious blue-water action.
From first-timers chasing a dream, to seasoned anglers ticking off bucket-list species, every single trip is run with professionalism, safety, and passion at the core.
More than the fishing
Each charter is about more than the fishing — it’s the stories told, the friendships made, and the memories that last a lifetime!

World-class Marlin Fishing from 
Far North Queensland to Central NSW
We operate as a roving fishing operation , giving us the freedom to fish areas well beyond the reach of most charter operators. From the remote waters of Far North Queensland — including Cairns , Cooktown , and Lizard Island along the Ribbon Reefs section of the Great Barrier Reef — through to the relative hustle and bustle of New South Wales’ premier fishing grounds at Port Stephens , Calypso is built around chasing billfish during their peak seasons.
Across these locations, you’ll be targeting all species of billfish — including giant black marlin well in excess of 1,000 lb , juvenile and mid-range black marlin, striped marlin, and heavy-tackle blue marlin .
Led by Tim Dean , the crew can cater for every style of marlin fishing, including heavy tackle, light tackle, fly fishing, and spearfishing , tailoring each trip to suit both the angler and the fishery.
With decades of on-water experience, we are equally comfortable guiding a first-time anglers through their first billfish encounter or working alongside highly experienced anglers looking to refine techniques, target specific species, or fish at the highest level.
