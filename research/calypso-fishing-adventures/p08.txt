Lizard Island Black Marlin Fishing 
Lizard Island: Where Luxury and Black Marlin Fishing Meet
Lizard Island is one of the most famous destinations in the world for black marlin fishing , and is widely regarded as synonymous with giant black marlin on the Great Barrier Reef.
Alongside Cairns , Lizard Island has historically helped define Australia’s international reputation as a premier heavy-tackle marlin destination.
And for generations of anglers and crews, fishing the reef near Lizard Island represents the pinnacle of the sport.
A Long-Time Favourite of the Professional Marlin Fleet
Historically, Lizard Island has been one of the professional marlin fleet’s most favoured bases , largely due to its proximity to the most legendary of all Ribbon Reefs.
Just offshore lies Ribbon Reef No. 10 , widely considered the most famous and productive of the Ribbon Reefs for the giant black marlin. At 18 miles in length, this reef has numerous hot spots and collectively they have produced countless iconic captures. Number 10 Ribbon Reef remains a benchmark location for serious marlin anglers worldwide.
Being based at Lizard Island allows vessels to fish these prime grounds with minimal transit time — maximising time in the strike zone during peak black marlin season.
The Rock: Where History and Location Align
Often referred to simply as “The Rock,” Lizard Island is instantly recognisable within the marlin fishing world. Its rugged granite landscape mirrors its reputation — remote, powerful, and purpose-built for blue-water fishing.
The island is home to a private island resort , offering a rare combination of isolation and access. This balance has made Lizard Island black marlin fishing uniquely appealing to professional crews and experienced anglers alike.

Frequently Asked Questions 
When is the best time to fish for the giant black marlin 
The peak giant black marlin season in Cairns runs from September to December, with October and November traditionally the peak of peak season offering the highest chances of grander‑class fish.
Multi‑day charters are strongly recommended during peak season to maximise opportunities on the reef. 
How many days of fishing do you recommend? 
For anglers travelling to Cairns specifically to target giant black marlin, 3 to 5 days is considered a minimum timeframe. This allows for weather variability, fish movement, and the natural rhythms of the fishery — without rushing the experience.
It also gives us the flexibility to move with the fish, stay on the bite, and focus on the areas producing the best action at the time. Quite simply, the more days you fish, the greater the opportunity for a once-in-a-lifetime fish.
