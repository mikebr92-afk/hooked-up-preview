Perth's Top Fishing Spots
Looking for the best fishing spots in Perth? From tranquil rivers and freshwater lakes to scenic coastal waters, Perth offers a wide variety of fishing locations perfect for every angler. Whether you're an experienced fisherman or trying your luck for the first time, there’s no shortage of incredible places to cast a line.
Fishing in Perth offers a variety of catches, from herring and tailor to crabs, mussels, and crayfish. With its pristine waters, Perth is a top spot for recreational fishing. For a bigger adventure, local fishing charters and boat tours take you into the Indian Ocean for unforgettable deep-sea fishing experiences.
To help protect local fish stocks and marine environments, make sure you're familiar with Western Australia's recreational fishing rules , including any licence requirements, fishing restrictions and catch limits, before heading out. Below, you'll find some of Perth's top fishing locations, as recommended by locals who know the best spots to relax and enjoy the great outdoors.
