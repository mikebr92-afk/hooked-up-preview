OUR VESSELS
Purpose-Built Boats for Private Sportfishing Charters
Akame Sportfishing operates two vessels to suit different styles of private sportfishing around Broome. Whether you’re working the creeks, fishing the bay, casting topwater lures, fly fishing or heading out wide when conditions allow, each charter is matched to the boat best suited to the day, the target species and the type of fishing you want to do.
Ocean Whaler 5.65​
The Ocean Whaler 5.65 is a versatile sportfishing vessel well suited to Broome’s creek, bay and inshore fishing.
Its size and layout make it ideal for more technical fishing styles, including creek fishing, LiveScope sessions, lure casting, fly fishing and working tighter water where manoeuvrability matters.
This vessel is commonly used for private charters focused on species such as barramundi, threadfin salmon, tripletail, queenfish and other inshore sportfish.
PMY 233 Formula​
The PMY 233 Formula is the larger vessel in the Akame Sportfishing fleet, built for bigger days on the water and heading out wide when conditions allow.
With more space and capability, this boat is well suited to private charters chasing pelagic species, game fish and more open-water sportfishing opportunities around Broome.
The PMY 233 Formula gives Akame the flexibility to access a wider range of fishing grounds, making it ideal for clients wanting to target bigger fish, cover more water or plan a more specialised charter.
