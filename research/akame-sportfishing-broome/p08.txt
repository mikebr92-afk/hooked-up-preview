Topwater Fishing
Topwater Fishing Charters in Broome
Topwater fishing is one of the most visual and exciting ways to fish Broome’s waters. Akame Sportfishing offers private topwater charters for anglers who want surface strikes, active casting and a more energetic style of sportfishing. 
These sessions can target species such as queenfish, trevally, tuna, mackerel and other fast-moving sportfish, depending on the tides, weather and fish movement on the day. Topwater fishing is all about reading the water, working lures properly and making the most of opportunities as they come up. 
It’s a hands-on style of fishing suited to anglers who enjoy casting, movement and the chance of explosive surface action.
