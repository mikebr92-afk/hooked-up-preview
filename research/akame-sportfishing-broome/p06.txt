Remote Charters
Remote Charters Charters in Broome
Akame Sportfishing offers private remote charter options for anglers wanting to get further away from the usual fishing grounds and experience more of what the Kimberley has to offer. 
These charters are planned around access, tides, weather, target species and the style of fishing you want to do. 
Depending on the trip, remote charters may include creek systems, inshore grounds, remote coastline and more isolated fishing locations. 
They’re best suited to anglers looking for a more tailored and adventurous sportfishing experience, with the flexibility to plan the day around the conditions and the fishing opportunities available. 
Every remote charter is private and built around your group, your goals and the type of water you want to fish.
