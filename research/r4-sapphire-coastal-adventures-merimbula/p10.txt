FISHPEN CHARTERS (BROADBILL) AT MITCHIES JETTY 
• 
• 
• 
Mitchies Jetty is a Merimbula icon, the colourful shack a hub of activity, especially in the summer months when jumping off the jetty is a favourite with little kids and big kids. 
​ 
Head out on a Fishing Charter to test your reef or game fishing skills or go Whale Watching during the season with an experienced skipper on the "Broadbill". 
​ 
Hire a SUP or kayak and explore the beauty of the lake with a unique perspective from the water - you'll be sure to spot some marine life and have a wonderful few hours. 
​ 
Probably the most loved and photographed building on the Sapphire Coast, Mitchies Jetty has been a meeting place and family destination since the 70's and is just as popular as ever!
