SAPPHIRE COASTAL ADVENTURES 
• 
• 
• 
Get out on the water and see whales, dolphins and seals or go fishing with the pros at Sapphire Costal Adventures. Departing from Bermagui and Merimbula Marina Jetty, pop into the office over the water to see the friendly team or view tours and book online. 
​ 
Sapphire Coastal Adventures (formerly Merimbula Marina) is a small family owned business with a passion for whale watching. The Southern Migration in Merimbula is their favourite time of the year as so many whales pass close to the shore and the whale watching is sensational! 
Outside of whale season you can take a (delete Scenic Dolphin Cruise) Merimbula Ocean Wildlife Adventure which is a wonderful way to see the spectacular Sapphire Coast and sight a variety of marine life.
Fishing charters operate year round with (Delete: daily 5hr Reef Fishing Charters, 3hr Family Fishing during school holidays and during Game Fishing Season, you can take a 8hr Game Fishing Charter.) 3hr Reef Fishing Charters our speciality. These are ideal for both beginner and experienced anglers.
Private charters can also be organised by contacting their office or enquiring online.
