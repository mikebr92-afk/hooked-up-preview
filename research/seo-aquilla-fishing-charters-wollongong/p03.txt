Whole boat charters 
Want to hire the whole vessel just for yourself and your mates? For a personal experience of a private charter, this exclusive option is perfect to suit any occasion including corporate events, private functions, bucks or Christmas parties. We can accommodate up to 22 passengers, but for your safety and comfort, each charter is capped at 17 passengers to fish at any one time.
For individual bookings in a shared charter experience, check out our Per Person Charters . 
pricing 
A whole boat hire for up to 22 passengers (17 fishing spots):
$3,600 
A whole boat hire for up to 10 passengers:
$2,800 
A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the remaining balance due the day before your scheduled charter.
Note: Our vessel can hold up to 22 passengers, but we only allow 17 to fish at any one time for safety reasons. For groups larger than 17, people would need to take turns to fish.
Public holidays will incur a 15% surcharge 
charter schedule for 2026 
🗓 Operating Days : Thursday to Tuesday 
🕕 Time : 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM (Back in the harbour between 12:30-1 PM)
🚫 Closed Days :
• Every Wednesday (except in December & January)
• Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year’s Day, 2 January
highlights & inclusions 
• Departing from the historic Wollongong Harbour, we fish anywhere from off the beach to 10 miles offshore, between Stanwell Park and Shellharbour.
• Top of the range modern gear are supplied, including Shimano Saragosa 10,000 spin reels and Daiwa Beef Stick Z 10-20kg rods, along with bait, sinkers and fishing licence per person.
• Coffee, tea, and a delicious (beef) sausage sizzle for breakfast.
• We cover a vast array of fishing grounds targeting Snappers, Morwong, Wrasse, Flathead, Leatherjacket, Eastern Pigfish and more.
• Experienced crew, including a skipper and deckhands, for assistance throughout the trip.
• Your catches of the day are gutted and cleaned for you to take home. Fish filleting is not provided.
• Unisex toilet onboard.

conditions 
• A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the remaining balance due the day before your scheduled charter.
• Passengers must be at least 8 years old to hold their own rod.
• There is no wheelchair access to the boat. Passengers must be able to descend and climb a vertical ladder of 3 to 5 steps from the berth to embark and disembark the boat.
• Please be advised that there is no seating available on the deck while fishing. Guests are required to stand while fishing. However, ample seating is provided in the cabin and the sheltered area of the deck for your comfort.
• Our charters operate rain or shine. In the event that the weather is deemed too dangerous or unfavourable for a safe fishing experience, such as thunderstorms, high winds and/ or strong swells, our skipper will make the decision to cancel the charter. See below for more info on our cancellation policy.
• BYO food and drinks are allowed, including alcohol in moderation.
• All prices shown are in Australian Dollars and include GST, card surcharges and booking fees.
cancellation & reschedule policy 
• All bookings are transferable but strictly non-refundable for changes of mind. Cancellations made by you will result in forfeiture of the deposit. 
• Illness, injury or other personal circumstances do not automatically entitle you to a refund or reschedule. Our standard Change & Cancellation Policy will apply.
• Weather conditions can be unpredictable and can change at any time, which is out of our control. As a result, cancellations usually occur the day before or the morning of your scheduled charter . Kindly take this into consideration when booking accommodation specifically for our charter. We highly recommend checking with your accommodation about their policy on last minute changes and cancellations.
• Cancellations made by us due to bad weather or mechanical issues, you will be entitled to one of these options:
• Reschedule to another day
• Get a refund (or receive a Return Voucher if a gift voucher was used for the original booking)
• Receive a Return Voucher that is valid for 3 years, equivalent to the value of your booking 
• Complimentary reschedules are offered up to 10 days before the charter. Any changes made after this time will result in forfeiture of the 50% deposit.
• For full Booking Terms & Conditions, click here .
Seasickness Disclaimer 
Seasickness can occur during our charters, even on calm days. The motion of the ocean is unpredictable and affects everyone differently. We strongly recommend taking preventative measures if you’re prone to seasickness. Over-the-counter remedies, natural options like ginger, or consulting your doctor for advice can make a big difference in your comfort during the trip.
Please understand that the decision to return to the harbour earlier than scheduled due to seasickness will be at the discretion of the group. No refunds or compensation will be provided in such cases. Our crew will do their best to assist, but preparation is key to ensuring a great day on the water.
By booking with us, you acknowledge that seasickness is a potential risk and accept responsibility for taking necessary precautions.
