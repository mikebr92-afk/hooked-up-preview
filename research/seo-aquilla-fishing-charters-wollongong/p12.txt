Corporate resources
Destination Wollongong offers a range of corporate resources. This information includes various online resources developed for our community, individuals and organisations who are searching for information to help promote our region. Wollongong unashamedly and boldly promotes itself as Sydney’s playground and a vibrant place to visit. With our laid back coastal lifestyle, a range of adventure experiences, and events that indulge into the arts, culture and technology, it really is the best of ALL worlds.

Featured Partners
Towradgi Beach Hotel 
Towradgi Beach Hotel 
From delicious food and luxurious function spaces to family-friendly activities and live entertainment, Towradgi Beach Hotel has been a South Coast favourite for decades. Whether you're gathering with friends, bringing the family or chasing a good time, there's something for everyone at TBH. 
Find Out More 
Symbio Wildlife Park 
Symbio Wildlife Park 
Meet koalas, kangaroos, red pandas and more at Symbio Wildlife Park. Get up close with friendly animals, join keeper talks or book a behind-the-scenes experience. With a splash park, adventure playground, picnic areas and café, it’s a wild day out for the whole family. 
Find Out More 
WIN Entertainment Centre 
WIN Entertainment Centre 
From big-name concerts and comedy to family shows and Illawarra Hawks games, WIN Entertainment Centre brings the region’s biggest live events to Wollongong. With capacity for up to 6,000 people, it’s also a versatile venue for conferences, exhibitions, gala dinners and more. 
Find Out More
