Our Guides
Without a doubt the best variety of species and most diverse fishery anywhere in the country. Come fishing in Weipa with one of our experienced guides. 
Kurt Rowlands 
OWNER/OPERATOR 
After three years guiding in Hervey Bay, Kurt made the move north to Weipa in 2022; a decision he calls the best he’s ever made. While guiding full-time, he worked behind the scenes alongside Bargy, and when the opportunity arose to take over the business, he couldn’t say no. Kurt brings relentless passion, enthusiasm, and drive to every trip and plans to channel that same energy into growing and evolving the operation, always with the client experience front and centre.
Scott Hackett
guide 
Scotty is our true jack-of-all-trades guide and one of the fishiest blokes you’ll ever meet. He thrives in the chaos of the guiding world, and if he’s not on the water with clients, he’s fishing himself, researching new areas, refining techniques, or honing his fly designs. With unmatched passion and work ethic, Scotty embodies exactly what it takes to be a top-tier fly-fishing guide.
Alan “Fish” Philliskirk
guide 
The original founder of the business, Alan “Fish” Philliskirk is a household name in the Australian and international fishing scene. A true pioneer of fly fishing on the Western Cape, Fish is widely respected across the industry. Most notably, he became the first person in Australia to land a permit on fly in the late 1990s. He brings a lifetime of experience, knowledge, and perspective to the team.
Drew Bolton
guide 
A Weipa local through and through, Drewy has spent more time on the water than on land. His intimate knowledge of the Western Cape has been earned through years of exploration, leaving no creek, flat, or gutter unexplored. Calm, capable, and endlessly reliable, Drew simply gets it done, no matter the conditions. We’re incredibly lucky to have him on the team.
Liam Van Der Merwe
guide 
Our resident South African guide, Liam joins us after four years guiding in the Seychelles on Cosmoledo, along with several years guiding Yellowfish in South Africa. For his age, his experience is exceptional. Known for his infectious positivity and deep knowledge of flora and fauna, Liam’s passion is contagious. A talented fly tier and casting instructor, you’ll step off his boat having learned far more than just how to catch fish.
Kiel Jones
guide 
Kiel’s passion for fly fishing runs as deep as it gets. Joining us seasonally from the Millbrook Lakes operation as well as hosting several overseas trips annually, he truly lives and breathes the sport. An exceptional fly tier and casting tutor, Kiel’s calm, steady nature makes him an absolute pleasure to spend time with on the water. We’re stoked to have him as part of the team.
Caitlyn Rowlands 
admin 
The backbone of the operation. Caity keeps everything running smoothly behind the scenes, juggling business management alongside family life, gym sessions, and the many hats she wears daily. Without her, the business simply wouldn’t function! We’re incredibly lucky to have her steering the ship.
We are lucky enough to be supported by some of the best brands in the industry. These companies make our jobs a lot easier, and we only put our name to gear we can trust and rely on.
