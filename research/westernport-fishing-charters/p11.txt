Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence
Please note when fishing in Victorian waters recreational fishing licence is required. Westernport Fishing Charters does not sell them nor holds any responsibility if you don’t have one on your possession when on a fishing charter. Please visit the following site for all the information you require as well as buying a Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence before you go out on a Charter Fishing trip.
https://vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/fishing-licence
