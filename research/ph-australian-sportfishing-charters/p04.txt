Meet the iron Lady– ASFC's Multi-Day Fishing Mothership
Every Australian SportFishing Charter is built around the Iron Lady boat. The Iron Lady is home base — where you sleep, eat, and return to after a great day of fishing, wherever that day takes you.
Iron Lady is equipped with full air-conditioning, sleeping arrangements for 8 guests –3 cabins all with their own ensuite, fresh water for showers, a large saloon, a large open-air deck upstairs and fishing tenders(skiffs) to cater to smaller, more personalised daily fishing adventures with fishing guides.
Iron Lady mothership boat is your private floating lodge in the heart of the wild . 
Home base, wherever the fishing takes you
The Iron Lady isn’t just transport between fishing spots — she’s genuinely comfortable to live aboard. She’s a boutique liveaboard experience for the serious specialist.

What is the Iron Lady?
The Iron Lady is a 65ft liveaboard fishing mothership boat / vessel operated by Australian Sportfishing Charters. She is the mothership for every ASFC charter, based in Arnhem Land, NT, and Cairns, QLD.
Do you fish directly from the Iron Lady?
No — the Iron Lady serves as your live-aboard home base. Guests fish each day from smaller tenders, or the custom game-boat Wild Turkey, returning to the Iron Lady each evening for meals and accommodation.
Is there air conditioning on the Iron Lady?
Yes — the Iron Lady is fully air-conditioned throughout, including all cabins and common areas, which is genuinely important given the tropical climate of Northern Australia.
Who is part of the crew on the Iron Lady?
Every charter includes Captain Dan, a gourmet chef, a hostess, and experienced fishing guides — a full crew dedicated to looking after guests throughout the trip.
How many guests does the Iron Lady sleep?
The Iron Lady sleeps up to 8 guests across 3 air-conditioned cabins; Two 2-birth and one 4-birth, each with a private ensuite bathroom.
Where is the Iron Lady based?
From February to October, the Iron Lady mothership operates out of Nhulunbuy in East Arnhem Land, NT. From October to December, she relocates to Trinity Park near Cairns, QLD, for the marlin season.
How long is a typical Iron Lady Charter?
Most charters aboard the Iron Lady run 4 to 7 days or more, giving guests multiple days of fishing while living aboard in comfort.
Is the Iron Lady suitable for beginners?
Yes. The Iron Lady and her crew cater to all experience levels, from complete beginners to seasoned anglers, with guides providing instruction and support throughout.
Ready To Come Aboard?
The Iron Lady is the mothership for both our Arnhem Land and Cairns marlin charters. Choose your fishery and enquire — we’ll take care of everything else.
