About
Welcome to KC's Fishing Charters! KC's Fishing Charters offers an exciting and unforgettable fishing experience in Coffs Harbour, Australia. The charter is operated on the “Canopus,” a 41ft custom Steber boat specially equipped for blue water fishing. The vessel is equipped with the latest GPS and fish finding technology, comfortable seating, and a cruising speed of 12–14 knots.
Casey, the owner/operator, and the crew of KC's Fishing Charters are dedicated to providing personalised customer service and ensuring that every guest has a rewarding day out on the water. With quality rods and overhead reels included in your charter fare, you can trust that you'll have the right gear to target a variety of fish, including Pearl Pearch, Snapper, Jewfish, Trag, Flathead, and Kingfish.
KC's Fishing Charters offers a range of fishing adventures to suit every angler's preference. For those looking for a half-day excursion, the Half Day Reef Fishing charter is available from 6 am till 12 noon, priced at $180 per person. If you're up for a full day of fishing, the Full Day Reef Fishing charter runs from 6 am till 4 pm and is priced at $350 per person. For larger groups, the Whole Boat Fishing charter allows up to 10 people to fish from 6 am till 12 noon for $1800 per boat.
For a deeper fishing experience, KC's Fishing Charters offers the Full Day Deep Drop Fishing charter. This charter is available by arrangement and allows up to 6 people to fish from 6 am till 4 pm. The pricing for this charter is available upon inquiry. Additionally, the Deep Reef Experience charter is perfect for a group of up to 8 people, with fishing available from 6 am till 3 pm at a price of $2600 per boat.
If you're looking to spot some majestic whales, KC's Fishing Charters also offers Whale Watching charters, departing twice daily, unless when fishing. This experience is priced at $60 per person.
At KC's Fishing Charters, customer satisfaction is a top priority. Your catch will be cleaned, iced, and packed, ensuring that you can take home your fresh catch to enjoy. With expert help and advice readily available, you'll have the support you need to make the most of your fishing adventure.
To stay updated with news and highlights from KC's Fishing Charters, be sure to “Like” their Facebook page. For more information or to make a booking, reach out to Casey Rose, the owner/operator, via email at casey@kcsfishingcharters. Com. au.
Discover the abundance of marine life in the beautiful waters of Coffs Harbour by joining KC's Fishing Charters. The team looks forward to providing you with a memorable and exciting fishing experience.
