Cast and Catch Fishing Charters
Cast and Catch is a Fishing Charter service based in Alligator Creek, Queensland and launches from Hay Point. Operating the "Obsession," a 24ft custom-built boat that provides the ultimate fishing experience the region has to offer for all levels of anglers. From Spanish Mackerel to Coral Trout, book your next fishing adventure!

About Us
Based in Alligator Creek, Queensland, just 30 minutes south of Mackay and launches from Hay Point for Fishing Charters. Cast and Catch Charters operates “Obsession”; A custom-built 24 Foot vessel built by sea prowler boats, it is in 2c survey for 6 passengers, comfortable fishing with 4, with the latest in technology specifically designed to couple comfortability with utility for the local experience of sport fishing the region has to offer.
Powered by two 150 EFI Mercury 4 strokes to get you quickly and safely to your fishing destination. Cast and Catch Fishing Charters make every effort to make your experience of a day's fishing with us as hassle-free as possible. Therefore we provide you with everything you need for a superior fishing experience by including all your bait and quality Shimano tackle, if you wish to use your own tackle, by all means, bring it along on the day.
Your comfort and safety are also important. Our vessel is equipped with all the mandatory safety equipment, sat phone for 24/7 communications, a private toilet, and cabin space. In addition to this, our tours provide lunch, morning and afternoon tea, fruit platters and water.

WEATHER PERMITTING
Unfortunately we don’t control the weather, if we did we’d be fishing everyday!
Also please be aware coastal forecasts are not accurate representations of weather conditions off shore.
REGULATIONS
We uphold all fishing regulations including all fish come back whole. This means nothing can be filleted on board by Law.
SAFETY
Safety is important to us! If we feel something or SOMEONE will compromise it in any way, we maintain the right to deny passengers on board. This includes but is not limited to person/s who are intoxicated. For this reason you may bring alcohol but a limit of one 6 pack per person will be in effect.
Please keep in mind that we maintain the right to make last minute cancellations on Charters if weather conditions are not to our standard. We take ours and your safety very seriously.
