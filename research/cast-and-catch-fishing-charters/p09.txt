About QLD2032
QLD2032.com is an independent record of Queensland's infrastructure and procurement: what was disclosed, lodged, decided and awarded, compiled daily from primary sources and attributed to each one. It is built for the businesses, estimators, developers and agencies working on the state's pipeline.
Every figure traces to a row in a file an agency or register published. Corrections go on a dated public changelog within 24 hours.
What it covers
• LotScan: property intelligence for any Queensland address, across several layers.
• Shadow Pipeline: daily changes in 13 council development registers, with the distance to Games venue sites.
• Pipeline Radar: the Queensland Government's forward procurement pipeline.
• Directory: licensed contractors cross-matched across the QBCC, WorkSafe Queensland's asbestos and demolition registers and the Suitable Operator Register, with Overture Maps open data.
• Live Tenders: open Queensland tenders, read live from QTenders.
Our story
We registered qld2032.com in July 2019 and opened the @qld2032 account on X in December 2019. The site came back in November 2025, and the record has been built on it since: compiled daily from primary sources, every figure dated, corrections within 24 hours.
QLD2032 is built and run by Chris S, working with AI tools on Google Cloud.
The @qld2032 account on X, opened December 2019. 
How it's built
QLD2032.com runs on Google Cloud: Compute Engine for the site, BigQuery for the data and the Gemini API for enrichment. The site is plain PHP with no content management system. It is free to use; partner feeds are licensed.
Independence
QLD2032.com is not owned by a builder, developer, council or the Queensland Government. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games, the Australian Olympic Committee, the International Olympic Committee or any official Olympic body. Our use of "2032" refers to Queensland's future growth, not any specific event.
