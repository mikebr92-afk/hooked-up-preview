Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters
All types of Reef, Sport and Game Fishing in the unspoiled waters of Rainbow Beach, Double Island Point and Fraser Island.
Double Island Point Fishing Charters
Departing from Carlo Point, Rainbow Beach Road, Qld, 4581
Greg Pearce – Owner/Operator
PHONE: 0417 073 313
EMAIL: doubleislandfishingcharters@hotmail.com
Established in 2008, we offer a personalised, professional charter service specialising in smallergroups of 1-5 people however can cater for up to 12 on our 10m custom built Southern Plate built vessel. Our vast experience in these waters give you the best opportunity to hook that fish of a lifetime.We Fish the rich, unspoilt offshore waters off Double Island Point and Fraser Island with top quality rods and reels all supplied aboard our fully Australian surveyed vessel.We can arrange a trip to suit any level of experience and also provide tuition in bar crossing and all aspects of reef, sport and game fishing.

Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters
Located at Carlo Point, which is at the end of Carlo Rd, Rainbow Beach.
PHONE:0439 775 070
EMAIL:info@rainbowbeachfishing.com.au
Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters takes you offshore from Fraser Island, Rainbow Beach and Double Island Point. You will encounter species such as Snapper, Pearl Perch, Red Emperor, Sweetlip, Cobia, Parrot Fish and of course seasonal species such as Mackeral, Amberjack, Wahoo and others. Don’t forget the sighting of Whales May – October. Longest operating charter business in the Rainbow Beach area.
With 25 years experience fishing in these waters we can definitely put you on the fish.

Rainbow Escape Charters
33 Satinwood Road, Rainbow Beach Qld 4581
Dave and Suzie
PHONE:0428 868 087
EMAIL:rainbowescape@outlook.com
Rainbow Escape Charters depart from Bullock Point Boat Ramp, Bullock Point Rd Inskip Point, Rainbow Beach Qld. All charters have a 4am departure time. All fishing equipment, bait and tackle is supplied by Rainbow Escape Charters. You are welcome to bring your own equipment if you desire. Rainbow Escape Charters will provide morning/afternoon tea, lunch, and non-alcoholic beverages. All charters are subject to weather conditions. In the fortunate event of a charter reaching the Qld recreational fishing bag limits, the 250 litre esky filling, or unforeseen weather conditions, charters may return early. You will receive a confirmation call 12 hours prior to departure. Feel free to contact us 48 hours in advance if you are worried about the weather.

Keely Rose Deep Sea Fishing Charters
Rainbow Beach Qld 4581
PHONE:0439 863 150 | 0407 146 151
EMAIL: edkimmy@bigpond.com
Experience some of the best deep sea fishing in Queensland on the many unspoiled reefs surrounding Rainbow Beach, Double Island Point and Fraser Island on the Keely Rose. Catch an abundance of spectacular fish on a full day deep sea fishing charter departing beautiful Rainbow Beach. Our crew of professional fishermen have fished the waters off Rainbow Beach, Double Island Point and world heritage listed, Fraser Island exclusively for the past 30 years. We will take you offshore to the best fishing in Rainbow Beach. Rainbow Beach, Double Island Point and Fraser Island waters produce an abundance of quality reef species such as Snapper, Pearl Perch, Parrot, Coral Trout, the mighty Red Emperor and many other table and game fish species. Don’t miss out on one of Queensland’s best deep sea fishing charters on the awesome “Keely Rose”.
