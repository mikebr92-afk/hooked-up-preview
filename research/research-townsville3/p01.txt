Fishing Charters Townsville. com. au - Townsville Charters 
TOWNSVILLE, QLD, 4810
Welcome to Fishing charters townsville.com.au. This listing details Townsville departures. Come and join us as we fish the warm tropical waters of North Queensland. The beauty and abundance of the tropics is just breathe taking. Be prepared to embark on the fishing adventure of a life time that is sure to leave you with bountiful memories, exaggerated tales and an addictive need to try it all again. 
Fishing charters townsville.com.au has had great success fishing the creeks,the Great Barrier Reef and every where in between. Barramundi, Mangrovejack, Grunter,Threadfin Salmon, Bream and Whiting are all found in these creeks while just offshore puts us amongst Mackerel, Tuna, Trevally, Coral Trout, Emperor, Cod, Fingermark and the list goes on and on.
The North Queensland coast of Townsville is littered with islands and the fishing is world class. Spending a day out trolling the shoals and many wrecks for Spanish Mackerel will definitely get that adrenalin pumping or drop a fresh squid bait down to the depths to entice a tug of war with speicies such as Coral Trout, Emperor, Fingermark Bream and Nannygai. To make it all more interesting, you never know what you will catch. Cobia, Mahi Mahi, Tuna ,Wahoo, Sailfish and even Marlin are found in these waters.
The tropical reefs of Queensland are abound with fish life and to try and list them is impossible but trying to catch them is an adventure worth pursuing.
We welcome you to browse our site for full details on our tours and taking your first step to a North Queensland Fishing adventure.
