CHARTER INFORMATION & PRICING (please scroll down for schedule & availability) 
4-HOUR REGULAR SCHEDULED CHARTER (max 20) 
Adults (16yrs+): $110 
Kids (10 -15 years): $55
Kids (Under 10): Free
2-HOUR SHORT SCHEDULED CHARTER (max 20) (only during school holidays/public holidays) 
Adults (16yrs+) : $80
Kids (10 -15 years): $40
Kids (Under 10): Free
Please note: for safety purposes, the following maximums apply to scheduled 2hr, 4hr & Gone Fishing Day offer charters:
For every u/10 child there must be an accompanying adult.
For every 2 10-15yrs children there must be an accompanying adult. 
A maximum of 1 u/10 child and 1 10-15yrs child can be accompanied by one adult. 
3-HOUR SNAPPER FISHING CHARTER – September-March/April only 
Adults (16yrs+): $110 
Kids (12+ years): $110
Kids (Under 12): UNSUITABLE
GONE FISHING DAY SPECIAL: For one day only, all 3hr charters on Sun 18th October, will be discounted to the price of a 2hr charter ($80 for adults, $40 for children aged 10-15yrs, and children under 10 free). Exceptionally, on this day, a fishing licence will NOT be required.
6-HOUR GUMMY SHARK & SNAPPER/WHITING FISHING CHARTER (Snapper season Sept-Mar/Apr) 
Adults (16yrs+): $150
Kids (12+ years): $150
Kids (Under 12): UNSUITABLE
4-HR KIDS’ SCHOOL HOLIDAYS FISHING CLINIC (max 8 children per clinic) 
Cost: $160 per child
Includes: telescopic fishing rod with reel & 150pc tackle set, 2 hr tutorial on rigging, casting, and knot-tying, 2 hr bay charter, all food & drinks, goodie bag
Adults free of charge if not fishing ($55 per adult, if fishing) 
Please note: to participate in any fishing clinics, the following ratios apply:
• 1-2 children aged u12yrs = 1 adult to accompany
• Up to 3 children aged 12-17yrs = 1 an adult to accompany
• Limit of 1 adult per child
Registration required . 
SENIORS’ CLUB: Offers a 20% discount on the adult ticket price of a 4hr scheduled charter to any adult aged 60 years and over who is a Seniors cardholder. Seniors’ club charters run 10am-2pm on the 9th of every month, except for May, due to Mother’s Day, when the charter is run on the 10th May instead. All charters include all bait, tackle, a good selection of hot and cold food, snacks, hot drinks, water and softdrink and all fish cleaned and filleted. Please note: charters that are unable to run due to the weather, will be rescheduled. 
LADIES’ CLUB : Offers a 20% discount on the adult ticket price of a 4hr scheduled charter to any female participant over 18 years old. Ladies’ club charters run 10am-2pm on the third Tuesday of every month (except January) and are designed to fit within school hours. All charters include all bait, tackle, a good selection of hot and cold food, snacks, hot drinks, water and softdrink and all fish cleaned and filleted. Please note: charters that are unable to run due to the weather, will be rescheduled. 
HOLIDAY KIDS’ CLUB : 3-day fishing club for children aged 12-17yrs held during the school holidays. Day 1: 3-hr charter, Day 2: 4-hr charter, Day 3: 6-hr charter. All charters include bait, rods, hot drinks/icypoles to start, a great selection of hot & cold food, water, juice & softdrink, a sweet, and fish cleaned for you. Children are not accompanied by parents/carers during this time in order for the children to foster friendships. Total cost: $250.00. Limited spots. Please email info@geelongfishingcharter.com.au to register. 
2-HR KIDS’ BIRTHDAY FISHING CHARTER (starting from $550) – please email info@geelongfishingcharter.com.au for information 
FISHING CHARTER INFO: 
Our regular charters are very family-friendly and include:
*all bait and tackle (fishing rods, reels, etc..)
*an excellent selection of food including BBQ sausages in bread, salad & fruit snack packs and lamingtons or donuts
*tea/coffee/hot milo to start & healthy snacks
*all legal-sized fish caught by you, cleaned, filleted and bagged (bag limits apply)
This year, in order to reduce onboard baggage, non-alcoholic drinks (soft drinks and bottled water) will be included with all charters. Please DO NOT bring any cans or bottles of any drink with you on board (glass, tin or plastic). Lollies, chocolates, chips, raincoats, handwarmers, hats and stubby holders are also available for purchase on board. The licensed bar is open between 9am-8pm Sun-Thur and 9am-11pm on Fri & Sat.
Fishing charters depart from and return to “Wangim Walk”, bayside of the Wharf Shed Cafe at the end of Yarra Street, Geelong 3220 (go through the glass doors and on to the walkway). 
You are requested to arrive at the departure point at least 5 minutes prior to scheduled departure time.
Fish you may catch on a charter include: flathead, snapper (pinkie if undersized), Australian Salmon, Rock Cod, garfish, (snotty and silver) Trevally, Tailor, Gurnard, whiting (silver or King George), snook, banjo shark, Port Jackson shark, Gummy shark, 7-gill shark, and School shark.

Gift vouchers are still available and we can be contacted by leaving a voice or text message on 0491 713 236, by email at info@geelongfishingcharter.com.au or FB/Insta messages on social media.
Should your preferred date be fully booked, you may join a waitlist by texting the name, number of people in the booking, and preferred date to 0491 713 236. Any last minute availabilities (due to COVID/ cancellations) will be posted on our Facebook page if there are no waitlisted bookings for that date. 
** Anyone looking to re-book a charter (that has been postponed due to poor weather conditions or Covid restrictions), but cannot find dates suitable above, is welcome to book any time in the future that suits. There is no expiry on dates. 
What a great way to spend a day or evening!
Please note: *pricing and scheduling is subject to change *minimum numbers are required for each charter and poor or dangerous weather conditions will result in a charter being postponed or cancelled *no one booking can take up more than 50% of full capacity on scheduled charters according to the restrictions in place at the time *halal food is only available during a private charter *fishing licences are also required for people aged 18-69, unless exempt – visit https://vfa.vic.gov.au/recreational-fishing/fishing-licence to purchase prior to participating in a charter.
BOOKINGS ESSENTIAL and can be made by calling or texting Tracy on 0491 713 236, messaging via FB messenger on the FB page, instagram, or emailing info@geelongfishingcharter.com.au. Bookings for dates using a valid gift voucher number issued by Geelong Fishing Charters are accepted by simply quoting the number of the voucher however such bookings are still bound by the cancellation policy of 72 hours.
VOUCHERS FROM BOOKING AGENTS :
We no longer accept any vouchers from third party booking agents, unless by prior arrangement. 
PAYMENTS:
Places are only confirmed once payment has been received.
PAY ID:
Payments can be now made via PayID using our telephone number: 0491 713 236
AFTERPAY:
Afterpay will be available for all bookings when the online booking system is launched (Sept 2026) as well as in person, by prior arrangement. 
EFTPOS:
Payments can be made over the phone via credit card. Please bring the credit card used for payment with you on the day of the trip to be sighted.
BANK TRANSFER:
Payments can also be made via bank transfer, provided they take place within 72 hours of the booking. Please contact us for the details. Once you have made the payment, please text a screenshot to 0491 713 236. 
SMOKING/VAPING:
• Cigarette smoking is permitted on board the vessel (at the rear).
• Due to the increased risk of vape pens exploding, we no longer permit their use or possession on board. Please DO NOT bring your vape pen with you on the boat. 
Prices and schedule valid 01/01/2025 (and subject to change without notice)
Cancellation Policy: 72 hours’ notice is required in order for a credit to be provided for any booking. Cancellation within this period forfeits the place and any monies paid towards a charter, unless a replacement can be found at short notice. 
• The vessel and the tackle provided is completely disinfected and sanitised between charters
• Running water & soap is available in the toilet, wet (disinfectant) wipes, and concentrated sanitiser gel is available widely on board
*Buckets of salt water and fresh towels are provided for each family/household group
• Refund Policy: For refunds, please provide bank details (name, BSB, account number) and allow up to 3 weeks for processing.
