WELCOME TO GEELONG FISHING CHARTERS
Fishing is an activity the whole family can enjoy! So, take a break from screens, work, school, and everyday stresses to join us and spend some quality time outdoors on the gentle waters of Corio Bay.
To make things easier, we at Geelong Fishing Charters, have taken all the hassle out of going fishing so that you can just relax and enjoy the day - especially when kids under 10 are free of charge!
Perfect for:
*birthdays
*anniversaries
*family reunions
*bucks/hens celebrations
*Xmas staff parties
*enjoying the company of some close friends
*getting away on your own
Why not ask us about our other specialty fishing charters which include:
*Gummy Shark Fishing Charters (max 12)
*Snapper Fishing Charters (max 12)
*NEW!!! 8-hr ALL-INCLUSIVE Fishing Charters (max 12)
*Private Charters (max 20)
There are some very happy snaps of some of the lovely people who have already joined us on our Facebook page! Like our page to get all the schedule updates!
