Gift Vouchers
Gift vouchers from Geelong Fishing Charters make the ideal present, perfect for any age! For Father’s Day, birthdays, Christmas or even a pick-me-up gift to yourself, they are the perfect choice.
Our fishing charters are so much fun, very family-friendly, excellent value and a great experience!
Gift vouchers are available for any amount, are fully-transferable, and have no expiry dates. They are subject to weather conditions and availability, and not redeemable for cash. Currently, they are sent out by post so you have a tangible item to give, however in the case of last-minute purchases, we are also able to email a copy of the voucher instead.
If you have been lucky enough to receive a gift voucher and would like to redeem it, simply call us on 0491 713 236 to book a place, quoting your gift voucher number. On the day of your charter, please present your voucher upon embarkation. Please note: all charters are subject to weather conditions and Covid-19 restrictions in place at the time.
Buy your gift vouchers now! Call Tracy/ Brian on 0491 713 236 today!
