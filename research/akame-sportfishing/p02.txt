Creek Fishing & Bay Fishing - Broome
Lure trolling and casting, throwing swimbaits and fly casting
Barra
Chase barramundi through Broome’s creeks and mangrove systems using lures, swimbaits and LiveScope to target fish around structure.
Threadfin Salmon
Target threadfin salmon in Broome’s creeks and bay waters, casting lures and soft plastics around bait schools, gutters and tidal movements.
Triple Tail
Sight-cast to tripletail hanging around floating debris, structure and current lines in Broome’s inshore waters.
Mulloway
Target mulloway in Broome’s creeks and inshore waters, working deeper holes, channels and structure with lures and soft plastics.
LiveScope finds the fish
Akame Sportfishing use only quality gear to get you onto your target species. We use Garmin Live Scope to find the fish so when you’re casting you know you’re in the right spot!

Inshore Sport Fishing - Broome
Metal jigs, soft plastics, casting top water, troll and fly casting
Spanish mackerel
Target Spanish mackerel around Broome’s inshore and offshore waters, casting topwater lures or trolling bait schools, reefs and current lines.
Long tail tuna
Chase longtail tuna around Broome, casting lures into feeding schools and working bait-rich waters for fast runs and hard fights.
Cobia
Target cobia around reefs, structure and bait schools off Broome, using lures and jigs to chase these hard-fighting fish.
Sailfish
Head out wide off Broome to target sailfish, using trolling and other game fishing techniques to find these fast-moving billfish.
Queenfish
Chase queenfish around Broome’s inshore waters, casting topwater lures and working bait schools, current lines and coastal structure.
Trevally
Target trevally around Broome’s reefs and inshore waters, using topwater lures, stickbaits and jigs to chase these hard-fighting sportfish.
Coral Trout
Target coral trout around Broome’s reefs and rocky ground, casting soft plastics, jigs and lures around structure.
Bluebone
Chase bluebone around Broome’s shallow reefs and rocky inshore grounds, targeting fish holding close to structure.
Our Vessels
Akame Sportfishing operates two vessels to suit different styles of private sportfishing around Broome.
The 5.65m Ocean Whaler is ideal for creek, bay and inshore fishing, offering a stable and comfortable setup for lure casting, LiveScope sessions, fly fishing and targeting species such as barramundi, threadfin salmon and tripletail.
The PMY 233 Formula is the larger vessel in the fleet, suited to bigger days on the water, game fishing, remote charters and heading out wide when conditions allow.
Each charter is matched to the most suitable vessel for the day, depending on the weather, tides, target species and the type of fishing you want to do.
