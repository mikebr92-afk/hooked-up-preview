Local commercial Fishing Guide, David Patterson owner/operator of the original Daintree River Fishing tour, has 30 years experience living and fishing on the Daintree River, Snapper Island, Wrecks and Local Reefs.
Specializing in family/small group, personalized fishing/nature and crabbing tours.
7 metre catamaran hull, Twin 115 Suzuki outboards. Safe, stable and shady. Member Trip Adviser.
PRICES UP FRONT, BOOK DIRECT WITH THE FISHERMAN AND SAVE 
Inclusions: Fishing tours include quality bait, rods, reel and tackle – bring a hat, sunscreen, food and drinks. Raincoats provided in wet weather.
Departures: All tours depart from the public wharf near the Daintree River Ferry Crossing, 315 Cape Tribulation Road, Lower Daintree QLD48
Removable canopy for lure and fly fishing.
Rates are valid to 31 March 2026 (Australian Dollars).
