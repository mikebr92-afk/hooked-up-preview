What We Offer 
Half Day Fishing 
Our 
half-day fishing trips are perfect for beginners and seasoned anglers alike, offering an exciting chance to explore the Whitsundays' fishing grounds. We provide all the gear, refreshments, and expert guidance to make your morning or afternoon on the water unforgettable. 
Full Day Fishing 
Join us for a 
full-day fishing adventure where you can immerse yourself in the beauty of the Whitsundays and target prized fish species. With extended time on the water, our charters ensure you enjoy a comfortable and rewarding experience, complete with top-quality equipment and support. 
Private Charters 
Our 
private charters offer a tailored experience, whether you're fishing, sightseeing, or celebrating a special occasion. With custom itineraries and exclusive access to our premium boats, we create a day designed just for you and your group. 
Sightseeing Tours 
Discover the breathtaking landscapes and marine life of the Whitsundays on our 
sightseeing tours . Our seasonal trips provide the perfect opportunity to immerse yourself in nature's beauty, complete with expert commentary and unforgettable scenery. 
Snorkelling & Fish Feeding 
Experience the magic of the Whitsundays with 
snorkelling 
and fish feeding tours. Explore vibrant reefs, meet tropical fish up close and enjoy an unforgettable day on the water. 
Whale Watching Tours 
Enjoy an unforgettable whale watching experience as humpbacks cruise through the Whitsundays. Tours run twice daily from 9am–12pm and 1pm–4pm, with a maximum of 18 guests per trip. Ideal for families, nature lovers and wildlife enthusiasts. 
Whitsunday Fishing Charters whale watching tours operate outside the (POM) Plan of Management for the protection of whales inside the POM.

Why Choose Us for Your Next Adventure? 
Embarking on an open-water adventure is an unforgettable way to explore the beauty of the Whitsundays. We take pride in delivering exceptional experiences that combine fun, safety, and comfort, ensuring every moment on the water is memorable. 
• Experienced Crew : Our professional team is dedicated to ensuring your safety, comfort, and enjoyment throughout your trip. 
• Custom-Built Boats : Our charter boats are designed for performance and equipped with everything needed for an exceptional adventure. 
• All-Inclusive Service : We provide top-quality gear, refreshments, and everything you need for a hassle-free experience. 
• Tailored Experiences : Whether you're fishing, sightseeing, or celebrating, we customise your adventure to suit your needs. 
• Catch & Keep: 
Take your catch home—we'll clean, fillet, and bag your fish before you head ashore.
