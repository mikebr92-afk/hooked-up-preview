Explore the Whitsundays Underwater 
Discover the best of the Whitsunday Fishing Charters' 
snorkelling and fish feeding experiences. Offering both half-day and full-day options, we take you to some of the region's most stunning island locations where crystal-clear waters, vibrant marine life and breathtaking coral gardens await. Our tender boat ensures safe and easy access to prime snorkelling spots around the Whitsunday Islands. 
Whether you're a seasoned snorkeller or trying it for the first time, this tour is a must-do. All guests are supplied with stinger suits for protection during the stinger season, ensuring a comfortable and safe experience. As part of your adventure, you'll also enjoy feeding tropical fish, giving you the unique opportunity to get up close with the marine life that calls the Whitsundays home. Book your snorkelling and fish feeding tour today and see why Whitsunday Fishing Charters is the top choice for unforgettable marine adventures in the Whitsundays. 
Don't miss this adventure. Call us at 
0457 822 553 to book your fish feeding and snorkelling tour in the Whitsundays today!

Meet the Marine Life Up Close 
At Whitsunday Fishing Charters and Day Tours, we make exploring the underwater wonders of the Whitsundays easy, safe and exciting. 
• Half-Day & Full-Day Options: 
Choose the perfect tour length to suit your schedule and sense of adventure. 
• Tender Boat Access: 
Enjoy seamless trips to secluded snorkelling and fish feeding spots around the Whitsunday Islands. 
• Stinger Suits Provided: 
We supply stinger suits for your safety and comfort during the warmer months. 
• Unforgettable Marine Life Encounters: Feed tropical fish and experience snorkelling amongst vibrant coral reefs and clear blue waters. 
Create lasting memories with Whitsunday Fishing Charters and Day Tours. Book your snorkelling adventure in the Whitsundays today and explore the beauty beneath the surface.
