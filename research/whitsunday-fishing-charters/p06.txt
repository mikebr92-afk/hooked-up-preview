Get the Most Out of Your Getaway 
Explore the stunning Whitsundays on our whale watching tours, where every moment offers a glimpse of natural beauty. From vibrant marine life to iconic landscapes, our tours provide a unique way to connect with this world-famous destination. When you book with us, you'll be able to view: 
• Pristine Beaches : Admire the white sands of famous beaches like Whitehaven and the turquoise waters surrounding them. 
• Vibrant Coral Reefs : Marvel at the colourful coral reefs teeming with tropical fish and marine life. 
• Lush Islands : Take in the greenery and dramatic landscapes of the Whitsundays' picturesque islands. 
• Seasonal Marine Life : Spot migrating whales, turtles, and other marine creatures during their seasonal visits. 
• Stunning Sunsets : Enjoy unforgettable views as the sun sets over the pristine waters and islands. 
• Whale Sightings: Between May and August, keep your eyes peeled — you might just catch the unforgettable sight of a migrating whale. 
Ready to experience the natural wonders of the Whitsundays? Contact us today to book your sightseeing tour and create memories that will last a lifetime. 
*Whitsunday Fishing Charters whale watching tours operate outside the (POM) Plan of Management for the protection of whales inside the POM.

Frequently Asked Questions 
• When is the best time to see whales in the Whitsundays? 
Whale season runs from May to August, when migrating whales pass through Whitsunday waters. Our whale watching tours are timed perfectly to give you the best chance of unforgettable sightings, alongside pristine beaches, coral reefs and lush island scenery throughout your journey.
• What will I see on a sightseeing tour? 
You'll admire Whitehaven Beach's white sands, vibrant coral reefs teeming with tropical fish, lush island landscapes and seasonal marine life like turtles and whales. Many tours also showcase spectacular sunsets over the water, making for a truly memorable Whitsundays experience.
• What should I bring on a tour? 
We recommend sunscreen, a hat, comfortable clothing and a camera to capture the scenery. Our boats are comfortable and our crew will ensure you have everything you need for an enjoyable, relaxed day exploring the Whitsundays.
