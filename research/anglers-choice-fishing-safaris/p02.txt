Are you looking for a one day fishing charter and ready to BOOK NOW ? Please see our range of available charters below. All one day fishing charters depart Dundee Beach boat ramp at 7:30am and return at 3pm. Morning & afternoon tea, lunch, soft drinks, water and all fishing tackle is supplied. Your guide will also prepare and package your fish and airline approved boxes are available. We specialise is small group quality charters and our friendly fishing guides will help you every step of the way.
If the date you wish to book is not available please get in touch as we might be able to open up extra dates to accommodate you. 
Anglers Choice also offer fully inclusive multi-day fishing packages that cater for large groups, corporate events, families or solo anglers. Please use our contact us to enquire about our extended fishing adventures staying at our beautiful private Beach House.

Reef and Sportfishing Day Trip (Maximum 8 anglers) - Shared Charter
• Maximum 8 anglers
• Our stunning 9.2 m reef fishing boat Waverider features a walk around deck and large canopy. Head to the reefs wide of Dundee Beach chasing big pelagic fish such as Spanish Mackerel, Trevally, Cobia and Tuna then hit the reefs for all your favourite reef species from Jewfish, Bluebone, Coral Trout to Red Emperor plus more.

Reef and Sportfishing Day Trip (Maximum 6) - Small Group Shared Charter
• Maximum 6 anglers
• We operate four, 7.4 m charter boats that are custom built to fish at Dundee Beach. Small group fishing charters allow plenty of space and flexibility to target a good mix of reef and sportfish using different techniques including casting, jigging, trolling and bottom bouncing.

Barramundi Day Trip – Shared or Private Charter
• Maximum 6 anglers
• Barra fishing charters are available on our 7.4 m custom built boats. Vessels are clean and uncluttered and are the ideal platform for casting for barra. Please contact our team to discuss best tides for barramundi fishing. Crabbing is also available when tides permit.

Reef and Sportfishing Day Trip (Maximum 10) – Full or Half Day Charter
• Maximum 10 anglers
• Catering for groups of up to 10 anglers Lily Reign provides plenty of space to cast for pelagics or bottom bounce for reef fish. Fish the inshore reefs for jewfish, golden snapper, tricky snapper, coral trout, bluebone and cod or cast some lures and hook into some Tuna, Queenies and Trevally. A fun day for groups and families.
