Northern Territory Fishing Charters Dundee Beach
With Anglers Choice Fishing Safaris, you can enjoy the complete fishing experience all from one location. We run an extensive range of Northern Territory fishing charters from our base at Dundee Beach - including barra, sport and reef fishing. The Anglers Choice Beach House is your home away from home and you will not only love the NT Fishing but you will also love the true Top End hospitality that we provide. 
The Best Fishing on Dundee Beach
Dundee Beach is located 165km southwest of Darwin. We have access to some of the most difficult to reach and sought-after fishing grounds in the region. Our 7.4m custom sport fishing boats can get to places which other NT fishing charters simply cannot reach. We are fishing NT waters daily and have been operating our charters at Dundee Beach for 15 years. 
Species Diversity in the Northern Territory
The Northern Territory is renowned for the famous NT barra. We sure love our barra fishing, but Dundee Beach also offers first class deep sea fishing and sport fishing options! Book a one day fishing charter or an extended fishing package to catch your NT barra. You can also expect to catch big jewfish, golden snapper, Spanish mackerel, cobia, queenfish, trevally, coral trout, red emperor, tuna, sailfish and more.

Darwin Reef Fishing
This is the best reef fishing charter near Darwin! Fish the reefs wide of Dundee Beach that are out of range for the many charter companies that depart Darwin harbour. Book now for first class reef fishing at Dundee Beach. 
Darwin Sport Fishing
