The Pelican: Perth’s Ultimate Luxury Party Vessel
~ Custom-Built Excellence by Pelican Charters ~
Welcome aboard The Pelican , a magnificent 62-foot custom jet boat specifically engineered to deliver the finest private charter experience in Western Australia. Blending high-performance engineering with boundless onboard space, she is the ultimate platform for groups of up to 48 guests looking to celebrate in absolute comfort and unforgettable style.
🚢 Premium Onboard Features & Amenities:
⎈ 
Expansive Entertainment Deck: An incredibly wide, flush deck layout leaves maximum room for mingling, dancing, and relaxing. Bask in the glorious Western Australian sunshine or catch the refreshing ocean breeze with plenty of space for everyone on board.
⎈ 
Full Galley & Onboard BBQ: Take advantage of our fully equipped modern kitchen area or fire up the massive guest-ready barbecue. Perfect for everything from quick party platters to a sizzling gourmet cruise feast.
⎈ 
Flexible BYO Setup: Bring your own premium selection of drinks and refreshments. We provide plenty of large, commercial-grade eskies and ice coolers scattered throughout the vessel to ensure your drinks remain ice-cold from departure to sunset.
⎈ 
High-End Entertainment Hub: Set the exact soundtrack for your celebration using our premium, smartphone-friendly sound system. Multi-angle flat-screen TVs are also strategically mounted to showcase custom videos, slideshows, or live sport.
⎈ 
Five-Star Captain & Crew: Relax completely while our highly skilled, professional captain and attentive crew manage all the navigation and safety. We pride ourselves on delivering a smooth, seamless, and completely custom itinerary on every single voyage.
🌊 Tailored for Every Occasion
Whether you are planning a high-energy hens or bucks night, a sophisticated corporate event, a relaxed family gathering, or a pristine sunset cruise along the Swan River and beyond, The Pelican seamlessly adapts to your vision. It isn’t just a day on a boat; it is a premium venue that puts you completely in control of your entertainment, dining, and scenic destination landmarks.

Why Choose The Pelican?
⚓ What makes The Pelican special? The Pelican is our pride and joy, designed specifically for versatility on the water. Whether it's a calm day on the river or an excursion to the islands, she offers a perfect balance of stability, space, and comfort for groups up to 48.
🛋️ Is there plenty of space for my group? Absolutely. She features expansive deck areas that are ideal for social gatherings, corporate presentations, or simply taking in the spectacular WA coastline. The layout is designed to encourage flow and interaction between your guests.
💨 How does she handle on the water? Built to commercial standards, The Pelican provides a smooth, reliable ride. Our crew maintains her to the highest safety and performance levels, ensuring your experience is both thrilling and stress-free.
Booking & Availability
📅 How do I request The Pelican for my date? Due to her popularity, we recommend booking well in advance, particularly for weekend and summer dates. Check our availability calendar or contact our office directly to discuss your preferred timing.
