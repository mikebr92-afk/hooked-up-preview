Frequently Asked Questions
🎶 Can we play our own Christmas carols or party playlist? It’s your end-of-year celebration, so the soundtrack is entirely up to you! Our onboard sound systems are fully Bluetooth-ready so you can easily sync your phone and play your custom holiday mix. If you are hiring a professional DJ or band for your Christmas party boat charter, just have them touch base with our office beforehand to confirm our connection specs and power setup.
🎄 What are the rules for festive decorating? We love a festive boat decked out for the holidays, but we need to keep the vessel in pristine condition for the next guests. You are welcome to put up Christmas decorations, banners, and tinsel, provided you use non-damaging adhesive tape (no masking tape that leaves a sticky residue, please!). Strictly Prohibited: No confetti, glitter, or artificial snow sprays—they are notoriously difficult to clean up and can damage the vessel’s finish or the marine environment.
🧊 Can you help with ice and drinks storage? We provide massive commercial-grade eskies on board for all your BYO festive drinks and catering. To save your team the heavy lifting on the day, we can arrange for ice to be pre-delivered straight to the boat for $110, provided we receive your request at least 48 hours before departure.
🍾 Can we bring our own Christmas catering and alcohol? Yes! Our Swan River Christmas cruises offer flexible BYO options. You are welcome to bring your own spread of festive food, platters, and drinks. We provide the eskies and fridge space; you provide the cheer. (Note: If you are organizing professional catering or a grazing table delivery through a third party, let our crew know so we can coordinate drop-off logistics).
⏰ What is the latest our evening Christmas party can run? To comply with local Swan River navigation regulations, local noise management guidelines, and crew safety hours, evening party charters generally wrap up and conclude at the jetty by standard local curfew times (typically 10:00 PM for evening slots, though our daytime festive cruises offer plenty of flexibility for soaking up the summer sun).
🎅 Can we host Secret Santa or presentations on board? Absolutely! Our spacious layouts and comfortable seating make it easy to gather the team for a Secret Santa gift swap, end-of-year award presentations, or speech-making while cruising past the Perth city skyline.
🧹 What happens after the Christmas party wraps up? Your team is here to celebrate a massive year, not clean up! Once your group disembarks at Barrack Street or Elizabeth Quay, our professional crew handles all deck scrubbing, rubbish collection, and general tidying to get the boat shipshape.
