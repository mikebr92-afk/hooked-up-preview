Swan River Charter FAQs & Guidelines
Review our frequently asked questions regarding local riverway guidelines, BYO setups, and on-water policies to ensure your upcoming private cruise is flawlessly coordinated.
⚓ 
What are the guidelines for decorating the charter boat?
We welcome custom decorations for your event, provided they are applied using non-marking, gentle painter's tape to protect our woodwork. To keep our vessels in immaculate condition for subsequent private functions, we maintain a strict prohibition on glitter, metallic confetti, silly string, and sparklers. These items present heavy environmental clean-up hazards for the local Swan River ecosystem and can cause aesthetic damage to our boat finishes.
⚓ 
Can we orchestrate our own music and entertainment?
Absolutely. Our vessels are equipped with zone-controlled, high-output marine speaker configurations featuring seamless Bluetooth integration so you can stream your own playlists directly. If you intend to hire a live acoustic musician or a professional local Perth DJ for your river cruise, please have them consult our management office ahead of time to verify our deck power outlets and spatial equipment parameters.
⚓ 
How are BYO alcohol and catering logistics managed?
We provide full BYO freedoms so you can completely customize your refreshments and food. Our crew supplies massive commercial chest eskies and refrigerated units to store your beers, ciders, wines, and non-alcoholic drinks. For food, you can utilize our onboard commercial barbecues for a self-managed cookout, drop off your own prepared grazing boards, or hire an independent local catering provider to manage your food service on the water.
⚓ 
What time constraints apply to evening river charters?
To ensure full compliance with regional West Australian navigation regulations, strict local riverway noise management guidelines, and maritime crew hour safety limits, our evening social charters wrap up at the designated jetty by our routine 11:00 PM curfew. Daytime booking durations offer completely flexible starting windows depending on seasonal daylight.
⚓ 
Do we need to clean up the vessel after our cruise?
Not at all. Your only job is to host your guests and celebrate. Once your group safely disembarks at your chosen Perth or Fremantle jetty, our professional deckhands handle all structural cleaning, rubbish sorting, and general sanitation to restore the charter boat to pristine condition.
