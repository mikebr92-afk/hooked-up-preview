YOUR GUIDE JUSTIN DUGGAN 
Dive deep into the heart of Sydney's aquatic wonders with one of Australia's leading Fly and lure fishing specialists, Justin Duggan. 
For Justin, guiding anglers is not just a profession; it's a passion. With a global footprint, he has mastered the art of fishing across diverse species and terrains and with bother Fly and Lure. His credentials as a certified Casting Instructor, combined with his vast teaching experience, make him an unparalleled guide in the flyfishing realm. Whether you're a local or visiting from abroad, Justin's reputation as a top-tier guide is well founded. 
Justin's expertise isn't limited to the water. His background as a Zookeeper and Wildlife Photographer adds a unique dimension to every tour. As you navigate Sydney's vast waterways and National parks, Justin's insights into the local wildlife and natural beauty enhance the experience. His regular guiding stints, including the mesmerising waters of Australia's Cape York Peninsula, are a testament to his dedication in expanding his knowledge and enhancing his clients experiences . 
Justin has worked in the fishing industry as a Radio presenter, writer, photographer and public speaker. 
When he's not guiding, Justin's passions extend to photography and music, showcasing his talents as a freelance drummer. Justin is Married with two beautiful identical twin boys.
