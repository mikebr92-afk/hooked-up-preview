CASTING TUITION 
If you have a desire to catch fish and be a successful fly angler, there is simply no way to do it with poor flycasting skills. Becoming a competent caster is only a lesson 
away with Justin as your coach. 
Turning up to fish with only one style of cast in your bag of tricks is like turning up to a game of golf with only one club. Let Justin teach you the skills to tackle short casts, long casts, accuracy, dealing with the wind, casting big flies and so much more. Whether Freshwater or Saltwater, Justin can fine tune your game in a clear, concise and friendly coaching session. 
Justin is one of Australia's most experienced International Federation of Flyfishers certified instructors. Private tuition means you will receive the best in personal attention to help learn or improve the art of flycasting. 
With Justin you are not just learning from a passionate caster but someone who knows how to apply your casting in real fishing situations, not in a park. Lessons are held on Sydney's Northern suburbs at a mutually negotiable time, Often on weekend mornings or afternoons. 
Weekday bookings are possible and more frequent during daylight savings afternoons. Cost is $150 for 1.5 hours. Beginner to Advanced. 
Remember, even Tiger Woods has a swing coach!
