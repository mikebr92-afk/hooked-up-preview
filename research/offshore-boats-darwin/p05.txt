Corroboree Billabong Barramundi Fishing Charters
About Our Corroboree Billabong Barramundi Fishing Charters
Journey deep into the breathtaking Mary River Wetlands on our full-day Barramundi fishing charter to Corroboree Billabong. This isn’t just a fishing trip; it’s an immersion into an ancient landscape where the pursuit of Barramundi, Saratoga, and Tarpon unfolds amidst an astonishing array of wildlife. Witness colossal crocodiles, grazing buffalo, agile wallabies, and a symphony of over 280 bird species, including majestic Jabiru and dancing Brolga.
Your day commences with a pick up from a central location at about 6.15am. A comfortable 1hr 15min drive brings us to the Billabong. Spend the day casting into productive waters, surrounded by the raw, untamed beauty of the Top End. We’ll return to Darwin at about 4pm, full of incredible memories.
With our expert guides by your side, equipped with premium gear and unparalleled local knowledge, you’re set for an exceptional day on the water. Uncover the magic of Corroboree Billabong – a true Barra fishing haven. Book your Top End adventure today!
