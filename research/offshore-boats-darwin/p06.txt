Mud Crab Charters
Half Day (4 hour) Mud Crab Charters
If you’re looking to experience the iconic Northern Territory Mud Crab scene without committing to a full day, our half-day (4-hour) mud crabbing charters are the perfect choice. Set in the productive waters of Darwin Harbour, this trip is all about soaking pots, exploring mangrove-lined creeks, and chasing those great-eating Territory Mud Crabs.
Using our fast vessels, we’ll efficiently reach prime crabbing grounds where we set and check pots based on tides, recent catches, and local knowledge. Our team carefully plans each trip around the best conditions to give you the highest chance of success. Charters operate in both morning and afternoon sessions, depending on the tides. Just let us know your preference when booking.
While we always put in the effort to find quality crabs, results can vary as conditions change day to day. That said, there’s nothing quite like the anticipation of pulling a pot and seeing what’s inside. Your guide will walk you through the process, from setting pots to handling and measuring crabs, making it a hands on and enjoyable experience for all skill levels. We’ll also have snacks and cold drinks onboard to keep you comfortable throughout the trip.
To top it all off, you can take your fresh catch to a local restaurant we partner with where it can be professionally prepared for you, an ideal way to finish the day with a true “catch to plate” experience.

Embark on an unforgettable full-day (8-hour) mud crabbing adventure, designed to give you the best possible opportunity to experience the Northern Territory’s iconic mud crab fishery. Led by highly experienced local guides, each charter is carefully planned around tides, weather, and seasonal conditions to maximise your chances of success.
Depending on the conditions, we explore some of the region’s most productive crabbing grounds, including Darwin Harbour, Bynoe Harbour and Shoal Bay, and surrounding mangrove-lined creeks and estuary systems. Your day begins at approximately 6.30am with a friendly welcome and comprehensive safety briefing at the Darwin Boat Ramp.
Once on the water, your guide will walk you through the full crabbing process from setting and positioning pots to checking, measuring, and handling crabs. It’s a hands-on experience suitable for all skill levels, with plenty of excitement as each pot is pulled. Throughout the day, you’ll be well looked after with plenty of snacks, cold drinks, and a satisfying lunch. You’re welcome to keep your catch, and our crew will prepare your crabs ready to take home. All crabbing is conducted in accordance with Northern Territory regulations and size limits, ensuring a responsible and sustainable experience.For those wanting to mix things up, we can also include a bit of Barramundi fishing in between checking pots. This optional extra adds another exciting element to your day and makes the most of your time on the water.To top it all off, you can take your fresh catch to a local restaurant we partner with where it can be professionally prepared for you, an ideal way to finish the day with a true “catch to plate” experience.
