Our Charter Vessels
No expenses spared!
At Offshore Boats, we have 4 high speed fishing charter vessels. All 3 vessels have a cruise speed of 30 knots, this allows us to reach some of the best, most remote fishing destinations in the NT swiftly from Darwin.
All of our vessels are also fitted with comfortable seating and canopys, offering you protection from from rain and sun when needed, reducing heat stress and fatigue. We also have a private, flushable toilets in the forward cabin of each vessel.
Vessel 1
400hp Denis Walsh Centre Cab – ‘Sea Star’
Our 8 metre custom build centre cab walkaround, ‘Sea Star ‘ is powered by twin 200hp Mercury Seapro outboards with a cruise of 30 knots. It is in 2C survey, for 6 guests plus 2 crew. 
Reliable, fast and comfortable
Sea Star is also fitted with the following equipment
• Simrad 12″ chartplotter
• Simrad Autopilot
• Simrad Halo 20+ Radar
• Lowrance HDS 12″ Live Pro
• Lowrance Active Target Live Sonar transducer – specifically for targeting Barramundi
• Minn Kota Ipilot electric motor
• Stereo with bluetooth
• Electric anchor winch
• 600 litre fuel tank
• Full length canopy
• Flushable toilet in the private forward cabin
• 2x 60 litre fridge/freezers
Vessel 2
400hp Westerberg – ‘Reel Work’
Our 8 meter Westerberg, ‘Reel Work ‘ is powered by twin 200hp Mercury Seapro outboards. It is in 2C survey, for 8 guests plus 2 crew. However, we price our shared charters to carry a maximum of 6 guests on this vessel, to ensure we provide a premium, small group experience. For private charters we can carry up to 8 guests no problem. With a cruise speed of 30 knots, this allows us to reach some of the best, most remote fishing destinations in the NT swiftly from Darwin.
Reliable, fast and comfortable
The Westerberg is also fitted with the following equipment
• Simrad 16″ chartplotter in the cabin
• Simrad 9″ on the deck for our guests

• Outriggers

About us
Winner of a 2024 Brolga NT Tourism Award, Offshore Boats is Darwin’s premier reef and sport fishing charter operator. We take pride in providing the best fishing experiences for our guests, whether you are a pro of a first-timer. Our team’s commitment to customer service, local knowledge, quality catering, and passion for recreational fishing, along with our high speed, comfortable, offshore vessel, has led to our 5-star reputation.
