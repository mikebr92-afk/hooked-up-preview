The Journey
Your Charter Day by Day
Every Arnhem Land, Far North sport fishing charter departs from Gove Harbour (Nhulunbuy). You either make your way by pre-organised taxi from the airport to the Iron Lady located in Gove Harbour, or take the 20 min charter flight from Gove Airport to Arnhem Bay where the Iron Lady and her crew await. Once onboard, let the Iron Lady take over.
01. Departure Day
Board the Iron Lady at Gove Harbour. Get settled into your cabin, meet the crew, and brief over drinks as we steam out on the tide into East Arnhem Land. Dinner aboard as the coastline fades behind you. Or make your way to Air Frontier’s hanger Gove , board the charter flight to Arnhem Bay where the Iron Lady awaits.
02. First Morning on the Grounds
Wake up anchored in remote wilderness. Enjoy a delicious cooked breakfast and coffee how you like it. Guides load the skiffs with tackle. Fish finders on, tide checked — your first session begins.
03-06. Fishing Days
Each day your guides plan the session around tides, conditions, and what you want to target. Move between environments — rivers, estuaries, reef, mangrove edges, or offshore islands. Return to the Iron Lady for lunch and in the evening cold drinks, nibbles, a hot shower and a delicious meal as the sun sets — enjoying the camaraderie.
Last. Return to Gove
The Iron Lady departs the fishing grounds in time for a comfortable return steam. We arrive at Gove Harbour with time for the transfer to the airport and your flight home. Or stay onboard after fishing on the last day then the next morning catch the charter flight back to Gove in time to catch your flight home — with photos, stories, and memories to last a lifetime.
Your Itinerary, Your Way
Six environments. One charter.
Unlike fixed itinerary tours, your Arnhem Land fishing charter is designed around what you want to fish. Tell Captain Dan your priorities, and he’ll build the right mix of environments and techniques around the tides and season.
Estuary Fishing
Where rivers meet the sea, a diverse mix of species gather. Estuary sessions produce Barramundi, Queenfish, Giant Trevally, Salmon, and Black Jewfish.
River Fishing
The NT’s vast open river systems are the heartland of Barramundi country. Fish the bends, snags, and run-off zones where big Barra hold on the tide.
Reef Fishing
The reefs off East Arnhem Land hold Coral Trout, Golden Snapper, and Spangled Emperor among others — a complete change of pace from estuary fishing.
Mangrove Sight Fishing
Cast into the shadows of ancient mangrove edges for Barramundi and Mangrove Jack. Requires precision and timing — intensely rewarding when it comes off.
Island Fishing
The Wessel and English Company Islands offer virtually unfished waters. Remote headlands and rocky points hold big fish that rarely see a lure.
Fly Fishing
Arnhem Land is one of the world’s premier destinations for fly fishing for Barramundi, Permit, Blue Bastards, and a wide array of palegic species. Our guides are experienced fly fishing instructors.
Not sure what mix is right for you? Ask Captain Dan — he’ll design an itinerary around your experience level and priorities.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Contact Us 
Where do charters depart from?
All Arnhem Land charters depart either from Gove Harbour, or Gove Airport, Nhulunbuy, NT. Clients either make their way from Gove Airport or fly to us in Arnhem Bay — simply fly into Gove and we take care of the rest.
Can I book as an individual or do I need a full group?
We offer both private full-vessel charters (ideal for groups of up to 8 on the Iron Lady) and “shared” dates for individuals or pairs. Contact us to see our upcoming availability.
Do I need to be an expert angler?
Not at all. While we host many seasoned pros, our guides are experts at teaching fishing techniques, and other important details for the varied types of fishing and locations that we offer. We tailor the experience to your skill level.
What is included in the charter?
We provide a true all-inclusive luxury experience: delicious meals, air-conditioned accommodation, 3 cabins with ensuites, professional guiding, and high-end fishing tackle. You only need to order your drink requirements prior & bring your personal items and a sense of adventure.
Does Australian Sportfishing Charters practice sustainable fishing?
Yes, Australian Sportfishing Charters, believe in responsible fishing practices that ensure the preservation of the marine environment for future generations. We only keep what fish we would eat onboard each day, that are within the required size, and ensure all fish are well and healthy before releasing. If you’re looking to support sustainable fishing efforts and contribute to the conservation of the region’s marine biodiversity, look no further than our Arnhem Land fishing charters.
What should I pack?
Light, long-sleeved sun-smart clothing, a wide-brimmed hat, polarised sunglasses, sturdy sandals or reef booties, high-SPF sunscreen, and any personal medications. The climate in Arnhem Land is warm and humid during the season. We’ll send you a full packing list on booking.
What fishing gear is provided?
We supply high-quality Shimano rods and reels, soft plastic lures, and all the tackle needed for the day. Gear is provided on a replace-if-lost basis. You’re welcome to bring your own tackle — just check with us about suitability first.
What makes Australian Sportfishing Charters one of the best NT fishing charters?
Access and experience. We run multi-day, liveaboard trips deep into East Arnhem Land aboard our mothership, the Iron Lady — remote water most NT fishing charters can’t reach, targeting barra in rivers and estuaries few anglers ever fish.
Do I need a fishing license in the NT?
No — a recreational fishing license is not required in the Northern Territory. We do strictly adhere to NT Fisheries possession limits to ensure sustainable fishing practices.
What's the best time of year for barramundi in Arnhem Land
We fish February through October. When fishing in NT the run-off season (March–May) produces the highest fish numbers as floodplains drain into the rivers. However, fish are plentiful in this nearly untouched Top End region. October, before we move to Cairns for the marlin season, can also see very aggressive feeding as temperatures build.
Can I choose what type of fishing we do?
Absolutely — that’s the point. You can fish rivers, estuaries, reef, offshore islands, mangrove edges, or fly fish for Barramundi. Captain Dan will design an itinerary around your priorities, experience level, and the tides. Just tell us what you’re after when you enquire.
Is there internet access on the boat?
Yes. The Iron Lady is equipped with Starlink satellite internet, giving you mobile phone coverage and reliable communication even in the most remote parts of Arnhem Land.
How is crocodile safety managed?
Safety is our absolute priority. All guides are trained in Crocwise safety practices. We fish from stable, well-equipped skiffs and enforce strict protocols when guests are near the water at all times.
Can I take fish home?
We are primarily a catch-and-release operation focused on sustainable fishing.
Are the Wessel Islands included?
Depending on tides, weather conditions, and your itinerary preference, we do steam to the Wessel and English Company Islands. These remote waters are virtually unfished and offer exceptional diversity — including GT on poppers and a range of reef species. If you’d prefer to focus on river and estuary fishing, we can design your charter around that too.
