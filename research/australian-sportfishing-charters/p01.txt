Arnhem Land: The Barramundi Frontier
Join us for a 4–7 day remote expedition into the untouched wilderness of the Northern Territory. Living aboard the Iron Lady , our 8-guest luxury mothership, you’ll explore river systems, estuaries, and offshore islands that few ever see.
• Target Species: Barramundi, GTs, Spanish Mackerel, and Coral Trout.
• The Experience : Guided tender fishing with professional crews and gourmet onboard dining.
Explore Arnhem Land Charters 
Cairns: The Giant Black Marlin Capital
Step aboard Wild Turkey for the world’s premier heavy-tackle challenge. Based in Cairns during the peak season, we offer liveaboard access to the outer edge of the Great Barrier Reef, where the world’s biggest Marlin roam.
• Target Species: Giant Black Marlin, Tuna, and Wahoo.
• The Experience: A purpose-built game boat designed for the ultimate heavy-tackle battle.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Contact Us 
Where do charters depart from?
Our Arnhem Land expeditions depart from Nhulunbuy (Gove) in the Northern Territory. Our Giant Black Marlin charters depart from either Cairns, Port Douglas, Cooktown, or Lizard Island, Queensland. We provide detailed arrival instructions upon booking.
Do I need to be an expert angler?
Not at all. While we host many seasoned pros, our guides are experts at teaching fishing techniques, and other important details for the varied types of fishing and locations that we offer. We tailor the experience to your skill level.
What is included in the charter?
We provide a true all-inclusive luxury experience: delicious meals, air-conditioned accommodation, 3 cabins with en-suites, professional guiding, and high-end fishing tackle. You only need to order your drink requirements prior & bring your personal items and a sense of adventure.
Can I book as an individual or do I need a full group?
We offer both private full-vessel charters (ideal for groups up to 8 on the Iron Lady) and “shared” dates for individuals or pairs. Contact us to see our upcoming availability.
Does Australian Sportfishing Charters practice sustainable fishing?
Yes, Australian Sportfishing Charters, believe in responsible fishing practices that ensure the preservation of the marine environment for future generations. We only keep what fish we would eat onboard each day, that are within the required size, and ensure all fish are well and healthy before releasing. If you’re looking to support sustainable fishing efforts and contribute to the conservation of the region’s marine biodiversity, look no further than our Arnhem Land fishing charters.
