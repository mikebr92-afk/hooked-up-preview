2026 Barramundi Fishery Update
Barramundi Fishing in the NT is evolving: NT gillnet ban updates
• The Northern Territory Government is currently reforming the commercial barramundi fishery , including a planned phase-out of gillnets by 2027 and a new management framework expected by 2028 . This will protect the environment and boost recreational or sportfishing.
• Electronic monitoring with camera systems on commercial vessels is also being expanded to improve catch reporting and sustainable barra fishing.
• The good news is that recreational fishing rules and opportunities remain unchanged for anglers visiting the Top End.
What are the best seasons & locations for learning how to catch Barramundi fish in NT?
Barramundi can be caught year-round in the Northern Territory , but the fishing improves dramatically depending on the season:
• Wet Season (Jan–mid March): Big fish move into flooded systems.
• Run-off (March–April): Considered the best barra fishing NT as bait and fish are flushed from floodplains into rivers.
• Dry Season (May–September): Stable weather makes this the most comfortable time for fishing charters.
• Build-up (October–December): Barra feeds aggressively ahead of the wet-season storms.
Although barramundi is found throughout the northern coastal waters and down the Queensland coast, there is no fishing like that in the remote East Arnhem Land. This area is virtually unfished and definitely unspoilt. Our barramundi season in Arnhem Land runs from February to October, with the peak season being from February to June. For this reason, run-off barramundi fishing is often booked months in advance. The water temperature is warmer during this time, and the barramundi are more active. Don’t let the off-season deter you from planning your Arnhem Land fishing trip, as there are always plenty of fish in this location. When it comes to locations, the East Arnhem Land NT is a Barramundi fishing paradise . Buckingham Bay and Arnhem Bay are renowned for their barramundi population . These locations offer a mix of rivers, headlands, coastal creeks and estuaries, providing diverse and exciting fishing opportunities.

Barramundi Fishing Gear: What you actually need?
The right gear and equipment are crucial for successful barra fishing trips. Here are some essential items you’ll need to pack:
• Fishing Rods and Reels: Opt for a sturdy spinning or baitcasting rod and reel combo capable of handling barramundi’s size and power. A medium to heavy-duty rod with a strong backbone and good casting distance is ideal.
• Fishing Line: Choose a braided fishing line with a high breaking strength to withstand the barramundi’s powerful runs and jumps. A line with a test strength of 30 pounds should be sufficient.
• Hooks and Lures: Use a variety of hooks and lures to entice barramundi. Popular lures include soft plastics, hard-bodied lures, and surface lures. Experiment with different colours, sizes, and actions to find what works best in the fishing location and conditions.
• Leader Material: Barramundi have abrasive mouths, so using a strong leader material is essential to prevent break-offs. We recommend monofilament leaders with a breaking strength of 40 to 60 pounds.
• Tackle Box: Organise your hooks, lures, leader material, and other accessories in a tackle box for easy access and storage.
• Sun Protection: Don’t forget to pack sunscreen, a hat, polarised sunglasses, and protective clothing to shield yourself from the harsh Australian sun.
• Refreshments and Snacks: Bring plenty of water and snacks to stay hydrated and energised during your fishing trip.
• Camera: Capture your fishing memories with a waterproof camera or a smartphone in a waterproof case.
Check local fishing regulations and obtain the necessary permits before your trip. Being prepared with the right gear will increase your chances of landing a barramundi and ensure a comfortable, enjoyable fishing experience.
What are Techniques and Tips for Catching Barramundi?
Catching Barra requires a combination of skill, knowledge, and a bit of luck. Here are some techniques and tips to help you increase your chances of success:
• Choose the Right Time: Barramundi are most active during the runout tide and at the start of the run-in tide; having these coincide with early mornings or late afternoons is ideal. Plan your fishing trips accordingly to coincide with these feeding times.
• Cast Near Structure: Barramundi are ambush predators that hide near structures like fallen trees, rocks, and mangroves. Cast your lures or bait close to these structures and let them sink before retrieving them.
• Vary Your Retrieve: Experiment with different retrieval techniques, such as slow and steady, fast and erratic, or a combination of both. Barramundi can be finicky, so changing your retrieve can entice a strike.
• Target Tidal Movements: Barramundi often feed during tidal movements, especially during the falling tide and the beginning of the rising tide. Plan your fishing trips around these tidal movements to improve your chances of catching barramundi.
• Pay Attention to Water Temperature: Barramundi are more active in warmer water temperatures . Use a fish finder or a temperature gauge to locate areas with optimal water temperatures.
• Be Patient: Barramundi can be elusive and challenging to catch. Patience is key. Stay focused, observe your surroundings, and be prepared to wait for the right moment to strike.
• Practice Catch and Release: To preserve the barramundi population and ensure sustainable fishing, practice catch-and-release whenever possible. Handle the fish with care and release them quickly and safely.
Applying these techniques and tips will increase your chances of hooking onto a barramundi and creating a memorable fishing experience in East Arnhem Land, NT .
