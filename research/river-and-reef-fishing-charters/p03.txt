Live aboard mothership Dundee Beach & Beyond Barra, Reef, Pelagics 
Dundee Beach Fishing Charters
Live Aboard Mothership
*** NEW FOR 2026 *** 
New for 2026 we are excited to be able to fish the Dundee Beach area and further west to the mouth of the Daly, Cape Ford and beyond utilising the 47 foot mothership Beagle Gulf.
Available as a 3day/3night or 4days/4nights you will get to experience fishing Dundee that can only be offered on a live aboard.
When Bluewater fishing you will target Trevally, Queenfish, Mackerel and Tuna, reef fishing can yield Jewfish, Snapper and Cod. Crab pots are also available (on request, prior to the trip), if a feed of mud crabs tempts the palate.
Barramundi fishing tidal creeks & rocky headlands casting feeder creeks with lures or flies, casting snags or trolling, is all on offer.
Numbers
• up to 6 people fishing out of 2 guided boats, smaller private charters also catered for
The Mothership
• Beagle Gulf is a 47ft Steber
• Air-conditioned accommodation for up to 6 guests
• Freezer facilities
• Large back deck
• Cel-fi booster (reception can vary)
• Back deck BBQ
• Desalinator with unlimited fresh water
Target Species
Barramundi, in the rivers & estuaries , Queenfish, Mackerel and Tuna on the inshore Blue water and Jew fish and Golden snapper on the reefs just to name a few.
Availability
late May– late September
Includes
• All fishing gear supplied on a replace if lost or damaged basis 
• Bedding 
• Light refreshments 
• Lunches, morning tea, breakfasts and cooked dinners whilst on board

• Personal Toiletries
