Live Aboard Charters 
Live Aboard
Mothership Charter
Get away with a group of mates on a remote Northern Territory fishing charter. These fully guided Live aboard charters can be booked with a group of 4 or 6 securing the boat for just your group.
Depending on the time of year and fishing preferences you will be head west of Darwin fishing the NT West Coast or head east and fish the Western Arnhem land area. Heading west of Darwin offers up some great Barra fishing also some option for some inshore reef and pelagic fishing, The Arnhemland area to the east offers predominantly Barra fishing with some reef fishing options.
6 night / 5 day Charters 
Departing Darwin early am you will travel that day to the fishing location, have a comfy nights sleep at anchor and when you awake you will be ready to fish. After your 5th days fishing you will travel overnight arriving in Darwin mid morning. This charter gives you 5 full days of fishing.
The Mothership
• Beagle Gulf is a 47ft Steber
• Air-conditioned accommodation for up to 6 guests
• Freezer facilities
• Large back deck
• Cel-fi booster (reception can vary)
• Back deck BBQ
• Desalinator with unlimited fresh water
Location
• Western Arnhemland
• NT West Coast
Target Species
Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Black Jew, Golden Snapper, Mangrove Jack, Trevally, Mackerel, Tuna and more.
