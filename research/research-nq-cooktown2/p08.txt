Discover Lizard Island
Lizard Island is the northern most island resort on Australia’s Great Barrier Reef - 240 kilometres north of Cairns.
Situated 240 kilometers north of Cairns and 27 kilometers off the Queensland coastline, Lizard Island is a one-hour scenic flight from Cairns, traveling over a 200 kilometers of the Great Barrier Reef. It is here that you will gain your first glimpse of the beauty, scale and diversity of the reef. It is truly a breathtaking way to arrive at the island.
Lizard Island’s amazing location directly on the Great Barrier Reef means that the reef is literally on your door step. So close and convenient for you to explore straight off the beach or by taking a short boat trip to the inner and outer reefs. This is just one of our natural wonders that makes Lizard Island like nowhere else.

Tailored to your every desire
Your stay at Lizard Island Resort is tailored to your every desire with your experiences of choice paired with personalised service.
All stays are packaged with luxury offerings that include:
• Luxury accommodation
• Gourmet meals daily
• Unlimited non-alcoholic beverages
• A fine selection of wine, beer, spirits, Champagne and cocktails
• Your own in-room mini bar, restocked daily
• Complimentary use of motorised dinghies
• Morning or afternoon refreshments pack for private beach outings
• Unlimited use of stand-up paddle boards
• Unlimited use of clear view sea kayaks
• Snorkelling gear for the duration of your stay
