About Us
Here at Blue Reef Fishing Charters Sydney we simply love what we do. 
Our deck hands and crew have been hand picked and are all fish mental - We live and breath fishing! Our crew is highly experienced in all aspects of fishing, whether were catching snapper, kingfish, marlin or any other fish species, they are happy to share and demonstrate techniques to help you become a better fisherman. 
Vessel Strike Back
StrikeBack UVI 423790 - CFB/FBNO 4132 
The MV 'Strike Back is a purpose built 45ft charter fishing boat powered by a 460hp turbo diesel 18lt power plant. Boasting a huge rear deck, marlin/swim platform, seating for all passengers, she certainly terns heads in the charter world. With a modern and sleek design and being purpose built for off shore work, she's a great vessel at sea and is superb in rough condition. A very spacious vessel and comfortably can fish 14 passengers with room to spare. 
Strike Back charter vessel is fully Licensed and Insured, compliant as per AMSA and NSW RMS Maritime regulation. Survey Permit UVI 423790. 
We are a certified NSW charter fishing vessel. FBNO 4132 
Our Vessel's offer:
• A full flush house style toilet
• Seating for up to 25 passengers
• Live bait tank
• Sun/rain cover
• Rod Holders
• 150lt cold storage
• Fish finder/Satellite navigation
• Modern electronics
• Fresh drinking water
• Over 1000 GPS marked reefs
• Quality Shimano and Penn tackle
• 25 years experience
What you Catch, You Keep!
Target Species: Snapper, Kingfish, Morwong, Perch, Flathead, Nannygai, Jewfish, Mahi Mahi, Marlin, Tuna, Sharks, and much more...
