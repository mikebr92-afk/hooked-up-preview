With over 25 years of experience, Blue Reef Fishing Charters is among Sydney’s most experienced charter fishing companies. We offer deep sea reef fishing, game and sports fishing charters, harbour cruises, and buck parties to those who are looking for an exciting day out in the blue.
Welcome to Blue Reef Fishing Charters Sydney. We are the first choice for anyone looking for the best fishing charters that Sydney has to offer. We boast a passionate crew and thoroughly enjoy introducing amateur and experience fishermen alike, to the world of deep-sea fishing. Come with us on an unforgettable fishing adventure—we guarantee you’ll be hooked the moment we set sail.
Game Deep Sea Fishing Charters Sydney
Whether you’re looking to enjoy local Sydney harbour fishing, or game sea fishing; you can experience it all with Blue Reef Fishing Charters.
Of course, fishing wasn’t always practiced for fun. In fact, it’s an ancient survival activity that dates back somewhere in the region of 40,000 years. You can imagine how the earliest fishermen might have reacted if they were told that today, people would be getting together and fishing for fun. That said, even when fishing for survival, we can imagine that it offered a similar therapeutic feeling that it does today.
At Blue Reef Fishing Charters, we understand that there are many types of fisherman who enjoy a wide variety of fishing techniques. Some people prefer spearing and angling, others netting and trapping; then there’re those who favour a calm and relaxing day fishing beside a lake, catching and returning the occasional fish for sport, or scouring the seas for monster fish to take home for later consumption. Thus, we offer a variety of fishing charters to cater to everyone’s taste.
From Fishing Charters Sydney Harbour To Deep Sea Fishing
Deep sea reef fishing in Sydney offers fishermen a way to experience their favourite hobby in a truly unique way. We will set sail and take you out for a stunning day trip on the wide-open ocean, fishing in depths of up to 150m. What’s most exciting about this activity—for those who’ve never experienced deep-sea fishing—is the fact that you never know what you’re going to find! Groper, Cod, Snapper, Perch, Morwong, Pig Fish, Nannygai and Pearl Perch just to name a few and many more delicious and unusual fish can be hooked and cooked.
We have built up a solid reputation over the years as being a friendly, knowledgeable and experienced deep-sea fishing charter in Sydney—so, if its reliability that you’re looking for, then Blue Reef Fishing Charters is the crew for you. Our crew will provide all of the required gear, all that you need to bring is sunblock, and plenty of food and drink to remain hydrated (landing monster fish is thirsty work).
