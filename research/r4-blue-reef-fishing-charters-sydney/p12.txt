Game Fishing Trips
We run our game fishing trips all year round, and they last for a full day—one that you’ll remember for the rest of your life. We kick things off at 7am, and then pack it up again at 5pm.
Depending on the season, we will chase a variety of pelagic game fish. For example: through the winter months (June-Oct), it is peak time for hunting Shark and Tuna—targeting Yellow fin and Albacore Tuna, and Mako Sharks.
During the spring and summer months (Nov-May), we go after Marlin! You can land Black, Blue, and Striped Marlin by trolling heavy game lures. We will also go after Mahi Mahi, Wahoo, Short Billed Spear Fish, and certain species of shark.
If you’ve never done game fishing before, then prepare yourself for a supreme battle with some of the most incredible fish! The smaller Marlin still weigh between 70 & 80 kg, which is more than the average human male! So, you can imagine what it must feel like to successfully hook and land a larger Marlin, weighing in at over 200kg!
