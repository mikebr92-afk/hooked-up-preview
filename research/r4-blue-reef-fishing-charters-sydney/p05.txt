Sports Fishing Trips
The main aim of our sports fishing trips is to land the larger predatory and pelagic fish, like the Mahi Mahi, Marlin, and Kingfish. Depending on the conditions, we light troll to 9-mile, 12 mile, or long reef for Marlin and Tuna, and then live bait and jig for Kingfish or Mahi Mahi. We will even do a spot of reef fishing as well, to maximize your daily catch. In addition to that, we fish around Sydney’s FAD’s (artificial fish aggregating devices)
For the modern angler, our sports fishing trips are a ‘reel’ adrenalin rush, so be sure to bring your cameras to catch you and your friends in the action.
If you want a 'reel' challenge and would like to know what it feels like to land a monster Kingfish, then you’ll love our Jigging charters! The 12-mile reef is home to some of the hardest fighting sports fish in the game, are you up to the challenge?
We run one trip per day from 6:00am – 1pm (7 hours) and an extended trip from 6:00am - 3:00pm (9 Hours).
Our sports fishing charter caters to a maximum of 8 anglers.
