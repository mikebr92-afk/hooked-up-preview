Brook Islands Snorkelling Safari
Venture out to the Brook Islands, a human exclusion zone, which we have a special use permit for only 50 visits per year. This is a once in a lifetime opportunity for a day trip to explore coral reefs that you would normally have to travel two days to the continental shelf to see.
Thank you to Prith and the team at Vertical Pixel for creating this video. 
Private.
Exclusive.
Only one trip per week.
This is a once in a lifetime opportunity not to be missed.
Highlights
• Discover the richness and diversity of the coral reefs with world renowned marine biologist Dr Adele.
• Snorkel at Honeymooners’, Grandpa’s, Dugong’s Meadow and Neptune’s Marbles.
• Observe the wildlife that calls these waters and islands home; turtles, dugongs and sea birds.
• Witness the spectacle of sea bird migration to this critical breeding habitat between October and March.
• Enjoy our signature seafood picnic lunch on a secluded beach at the Three Sisters.
• Create memories that will last a lifetime.
Brook Islands Snorkelling Safaris depart Mission Beach. This 6 hour experience is weather dependent. 
$1000 per person with a minimum of two and maximum of six guests. $350 surcharge for pickups at Bedarra Island. Commencing September 2022 fuel surcharges will be applied. Commencing 1 July 2023 there is a Marine Park Environmental Management Charge of $8.00 per person.
We provide
• State of the art Cressi F1 soft seal masks and snorkels
• Reef safe sunscreen
• Thermal rashies or stinger suits depending on the season
• Underwater camera and complementary photographs
What to bring
• Polarized sunglasses
• Hat
• Swimmers and towel
• Jumper/jacket in winter
• Your sense of adventure
