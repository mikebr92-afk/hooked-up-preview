Family Island Safari
Explore seas surrounding the Family Islands. All you need to bring is your sense of adventure and a towel.
The 3-hour safari includes one hour of marine biologist guided snorkelling on the fringing reefs that surround the islands. Or perhaps you prefer a bit of light catch and release fishing. Or maybe you just want to explore some secluded beaches and go for a dip. The boat is yours so you set the itinerary. Your adventure will conclude with a light snack as we cruise back to Mission Beach. Upgrade to the 4-hour safari and there’s time for two activities.
A 5-hour tour includes our signature seafood lunch on a secluded beach. We cater to all food requirements. We have food sensitivities so we understand and will look after your dietary requirements.
About half of our guests end up having so much fun they extend their safaris by 1 or 2 hours once they get out on the water.
