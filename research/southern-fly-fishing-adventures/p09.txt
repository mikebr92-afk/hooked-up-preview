WEDDINGS & CELEBRATIONS 
Stunning backdrops are just the tip of the iceberg. These services will help you plan the celebration of your dreams. 
Ellen May Photography
CELEBRATE WITH US 
Take your event to the next level with one of our professional event services. Bright & Surround's local businesses are here and on call to ensure your wedding, birthday, engagement, hens or bucks weekend runs smoothly.
From catering and cocktails, marquees and flowers, to your own private butler or wedding planner, we have the experts to take the stress out of planning so all that's left is for you to enjoy your special day.
We have no shortage of stunning locations for your event. Explore our range of large estates like Feathertop Wines , Gapsted Estate , and Spring Spur , or talk to an accommodation booking agent about intimate luxury holiday homes.
Many outdoor locations have panoramic views of our National Parks , while our churches, restaurants and halls offer plenty of choice for you to make memories that will last a lifetime.
