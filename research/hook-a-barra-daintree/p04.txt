GO BEHIND THE SCENES BARRAMUNDI FARM TOURS
Our Barramundi farm tours take you behind the scenes of the multi award winning Daintree Saltwater Barramundi Farm. Discover how our award-winning Barramundi is sustainably raised, during this fully guided tour. We cast the net wide to show you just how we hand-rear Australia’s best farmed Barramundi and best of all, it ends with the most delicious tasting plate featuring a seasonal array of Barramundi produce.
We keep the numbers small on our premium Gourmet Barramundi Farm Tours, and the experience big. You’ll explore the farm and discover the story of Barramundi from fingerling to plate. See how our fish are raised, learn about sustainable aquaculture and get closer to the fish and people behind one of Australia’s favourite species. You’ll get to feed the barra, check-in at the growing ponds have a masterclass on how to fillet a fish. A 1-hour journey of fishy discovery.
Barramundi Farm Tours FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
THE DAINTREE SALTWATER FARM TOUR 
What’s included in the Barramundi Farm Tour / What do we do?
The Daintree Saltwater Farm Tour is a premium, small group tour lasting 1 hour. It includes a meet and greet and walking tour around the various ponds and locations on the Daintree Saltwater Barramundi Farm. As a part of the tour, you get to feed some of the barramundi in our ponds, where you’ll see them race, jump and fight for food. It gives you a first-hand view of the energy and enthusiasm of the iconic Barramundi. Next step is some catch and release fishing, to check the weight and health of the fish. So good!
Time to taste. We’ve prepared a gourmet tasting platter, showcasing our award-winning produce prepared in a range of styles. Sashimi. Battered. Grilled. Smoked.
Departure times and farm experiences may vary, depending on the farm’s seasonal workload. Click here to check availability or give us a call (07) 4098 7117. Advance bookings are essential. 
What topics are covered on the barramundi farm tour?
Farm operations and location, including details on the amazing Daintree Rainforest and River, which is where we get our pristine saltwater from. Water standards, checks, balances and recycling. Food conversion ratio for barramundi. Farm equipment. The lifecycle of barramundi on our farm, from arrival as 10cm fingerlings in batches of 12,000, to growing and hand harvesting. Fish filleting and of course, eating.
Is the tour undercover? / What should I wear?
The Daintree Saltwater Farm Tour is both undercover and in open air. It is recommended you wear a hat and long-sleeved sun shirt, with appropriate sun protection. Comfortable walking shoes are also recommended.
What facilities do you have on-site? 
We have plenty of on-site car parking and covered tables and chairs. There is a viewing tank, where you can see some of our rare Speckled (Panda), Albino and Golden Barramundi – as a part of your tour, our crew will demonstrate their feeding techniques here. We have a café that specialises in showcasing our beautiful barra in burgers, fish and chips, gyoza and even sashimi. Cold drinks are available to purchase, as is a unique selection of barramundi souvenirs and merchandise. There are toilet facilities for guests.
BARRAMUNDI FARM TOUR TIMES, COST & LOCATION
How much do the 1-hour Daintree Saltwater Farm Tours cost?
Adult $77.00 | Child [1-14 years] $67.00 . Option to purchase additional produce to take home, for a fishy feast.
Please note the general admission price is for any members of your family or group that are visiting but not participating in the farm tour. The general admission price allows you to see our viewing tank, which is home to some rare Albino, Speckled and Golden Barramundi, and enjoy our on-site facilities.
What time do the barramundi farm tours start?
They usually commence at 9am, but departure times and farm experiences will depend on the farm’s seasonal workload. Click here to check availability or give us a call (07) 4098 7117.
Do I need to book my tour in advance?
Yes. Advance bookings are essential. 
Where are you located and how do I get there?
Hook-A-Barra is located on the Daintree Saltwater Barramundi Farm, which is Lot 3, Vixies Road, Wonga Beach, Queensland. We are about a 90 minute drive from Cairns, or about 30 minutes north of Port Douglas. Guests need to self-drive, as there is no public transport available at this time. NB: If you are staying in Port Douglas, then our 4-hour Port Douglas barramundi fishing tour might be for you – it includes transfers, a farm tour, 1 hour of barra fishing and lunch.
