CAIRNS BARRAMUNDI TOUR FARM TOUR, FISHING & LUNCH 
This Cairns barramundi tour ticks all the right boxes! Guaranteed encounters with the iconic Aussie Baramundi, from seeing how they are farmed and raised for sustainable market supply, to casting a line for some reel spinning action at the Barra Pond, you can do it all – it even includes lunch at the Hook-A-Barra kitchen. This full day, 8-hour Cairns barramundi tour includes transfers to and from your accommodation, to our property at Wonga Beach near the Daintree. You’ll be picked up in our Barramobile at approximately 8.15am depending on your location and taken on the scenic Great Barrier Reef drive past Port Douglas, to Wonga Beach. Enjoy interpretive commentary on the way, with stops at culturally significant sites and a Welcome to Country. 
When you get here, it’s straight into a private tour of the Daintree Saltwater Barramundi Farm. This place is legendary and has been producing award winning barra for over a decade. Stop in at the baby barra ponds and learn about sustainable farm practices, before watching a hand harvest. From there it’s over to the Barra Pond where you’ve got 1 hour to try and land a big one. Our expert guides will give you some top tips and please note it’s catch and release fishing. The next stop is lunch, and you can choose classic fish and chips or a tasty barra burger. These 8 hour tours run Monday to Saturday (not Sunday), with pick-ups from Cairns at 8.15am and 8.30am northern beaches. Note: If you don’t want to do the Farm Tour, which is highly recommended, then there’s the option for 2 hours of barra fishing. This needs to be pre-arranged, so contact us on (07) 4098 7117.

WHAT’S INCLUDED IN THE CAIRNS BARRAMUNDI TOUR WITH HOOK-A-BARRA?
The exclusive 8-hour Cairns Barramundi Tour with Hook-A-Barra includes all the below.
• Free transfers to and from Cairns and Hook-A-Barra on the Barramobile.
• Welcome to Country and Interpretive Commentary along with the way, with stops at culturally significant sites.
• 1 Hour – Interpretive Farm Tour of the Daintree Saltwater Barramundi Farm.
• 1 Hour Barra Fishing – With assistance and help around the ponds.
• Fish Feeding.
• Lunch at the Hook-A-Barra Kitchen – Hook-A-Barra burger or fish and chips.
Note: If you don’t want to do the Farm Tour, which is highly recommended, then there’s the option of ‘swapping’ this for 1 extra hour of barra fishing (giving you 2 hours fishing). This would need to be pre-arranged though, so make a note in your booking or contact us on (07) 4098 7117.
WHEN DO THEY OPERATE AND HOW LONG ARE THEY?
The Cairns barramundi tour with lunch operates daily, 6 days a week (Monday to Saturday – not Sunday). They are 8 hour tours, from pick-up to drop-off in Cairns. Approximate times below.
Cairns Pick-Ups and Drop-Offs
• 8.15am pick up Cairns – Arrive at Hook-A-Barra 10.30am – 1.45pm return to Cairns at approx 4pm.
WHAT SHOULD I WEAR?
It is recommended you wear a hat and long-sleeved sun shirt, with appropriate sun protection. Comfortable walking shoes are also recommended.
DO I NEED TO BRING MY OWN FISHING GEAR? CAN I BRING MY OWN FISHING GEAR?
Please note we prefer to not allow personal rods and tackle to be used in our ponds, because they create a bio-security risk to our entire farm. We provide everything you need for catch and release barramundi fishing, and equipment hire is included in the tour price. Hook-a-Barra has a huge range of quality gear, including a specialist selection of lures, with everything from hard bodied minnows to soft plastics, poppers and paddle tails. We even have a fly-fishing option for expert anglers.
CAN YOU SHOW ME HOW TO FISH?
Yes. Our passionate and knowledgeable crew provide guidance and tuition on all our equipment. The fishing component of our Cairns barramundi tour includes an introduction to barra fishing, rod and tackle setup and the assistance of our expert fishing guides around the pond. If you have any questions feel free to ask our friendly staff.
CAN I KEEP WHAT I CATCH?
Yes, you can keep what you catch, but this is in addition to the tour price. We charge $17.50 per kilo and can provide ice and eskis to take your fish home. We even have delicious fresh fillets and recipe cards.
WHAT FACILITIES ARE ON-SITE?
There is a viewing tank, where you can see some of our rare Speckled (Panda), Albino and Golden Barramundi – as a part of your tour, our crew will demonstrate their feeding techniques here. You will have lunch from the Hook-A-Barra Kitchen, either a fresh Barra Burger or classic fish and chips. Cold drinks are available to purchase, as is a unique selection of barramundi souvenirs and merchandise. There are toilet facilities for guests.
DO I NEED TO BOOK MY TOUR IN ADVANCE?
Yes, advance bookings are essential.
