Freedom Charters
Operating from Eden Harbour on the South Coast of NSW, Freedom Charters offer a range of charters to suit everybody, including Reef Fishing , Whale Watching , Sports Fishing , Game Fishing , Birdwatching , Marine Wildlife Tours and Ashes to Sea Scattering .
Our aim is for you to have a great experience on the water, and our friendly and experienced crew will make your day unforgettable.
