Game Fishing
Game Fishing
Come aboard and experience the thrill of a lifetime chasing the mighty Marlin, Yellow Fin, Albacore and Striped Tuna. Broadbill, and Sharks.
Our game fishing season starts in November and runs through to the end of July. Trolling lures, live baiting and cubing for tuna. Eden has some of the best water on the South Coast with a high success rate per trip. The boat is available on a full day hire basis with accommodation packages to suit every angler.
We also do combination reef and game packages which have proved to be very successful. We support tag and release game fishing, but of course it is always the anglers choice if they decide to capture their catch. Recreational fishing licences included in the price. We use quality game rods, reels and tackle.
We can provide lunch on day trips. We can also cater for any dietary requirements if we are advised prior; nuts, gluten free, etc.
Contact us for departure times as this may vary due to daylight savings.
