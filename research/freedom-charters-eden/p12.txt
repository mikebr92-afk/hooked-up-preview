Marine Wildlife Tours
Join us on a Marine Wildlife Tour. Dolphins, seals, sea eagles, pelicans, fairy penguins, humpback whales (in season).
See the amazing marine life around Eden and at the same time take in some of Eden's sights from the water - Boyd's Tower, the Chipmill, Edrom Lodge.
We take a maximum of only twelve people on each tour, so you are guaranteed excellent viewing of all marine life from all aspects of the boat, including foredeck & flybridge. Great for photography and no jostling for a view.
Private tours and group bookings available. Contact us for times as this may vary due to daylight savings.
See prices for details.
We can provide lunch on day trips. We can also cater for any dietary requirements if we are advised prior; nuts, gluten free, etc.
