South West Rocks Fishing Charters
Welcome aboard their 36-foot fishing charter vessel Sea Scout II set up with state-of-the-art fish-finding equipment.
Try your hand at reef, sport, or game fishing. 
Let their experienced skipper and deckie make your fishing trip a memorable day out, both Andrew Trappel and Brad Jenkins have a tonne of experience, ranging from float baiting for huge snapper to live baiting for Marlin.
