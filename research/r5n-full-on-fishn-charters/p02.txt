Experience the Best of Hinchinbrook Fishing 
At Full On Fish’n Charters, Nathan Kemp delivers unforgettable guided fishing adventures in the World Heritage-listed Hinchinbrook Channel, one of North Queensland’s premier fishing destinations. 
With over 18 years of experience, Nathan , your locally born and bred fishing guide, knows these waters inside out—giving you the best chance of landing trophy barramundi, mangrove jack, fingermark, and more. His deep understanding of tidal movements, seasonal fish patterns, and the best fishing spots ensures every trip is both productive and exciting.
Whether you’re a seasoned angler chasing the elusive barramundi or a first-time fisher looking for a hassle-free experience , Nathan’s expert guidance and local knowledge take the guesswork out of finding fish. He’ll provide insider tips on lure selection, casting techniques, and the ideal fishing conditions to help you maximize your chances of a successful catch.
Why Choose Us? 
• Expert Local Guide: With a lifetime of experience fishing the Hinchinbrook Channel, Nathan ensures you’re in the right place at the right time. His knowledge of seasonal fish patterns and tidal movements gives you the best opportunity to hook a barramundi.
• State-of-the-Art Fishing Gear: Our custom-built 6-metre vessel is fully equipped with Garmin LiveScope and premium tackle, allowing us to pinpoint the best fishing spots in real time. 
• Tailored Charters for All Skill Levels: Whether you prefer lure fishing, live baiting, or fly fishing, Nathan customizes each trip to suit your style and experience level. 
• Multi-Day Fishing Packages: Extend your adventure with accommodation in Lucinda or Dungeness.
• All-Inclusive Experience: High-quality rods, reels, bait, tackle, meals, and refreshments are all provided—just bring yourself and your sense of adventure! 
Let’s Go Fishing!
If you’re searching for the best Hinchinbrook Barramundi Fishing Charter, look no further. Nathan Kemp and Full On Fish’n Charters combine local expertise, top-tier equipment, and a passion for fishing to give you the ultimate North Queensland fishing experience.
