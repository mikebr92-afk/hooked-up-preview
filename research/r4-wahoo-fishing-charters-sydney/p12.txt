The harbour hoodlum Kingfish charters off Sydney
Few fish get anglers as fired up as the yellowtail kingfish. Powerful, fast and famous for busting you off in the reef, the humble kingie is Sydney’s premier light-tackle sportfish, and we chase them right around Sydney Harbour and along the coast, wherever the big models are holding.
Wahoo Fishing Charters has more than 30 years reading these waters, and we know the reefs, wrecks, washes and moorings the kings patrol. With modern kingfish jigs, quality live-bait gear and a crew that lives for the bite, we put you in the box seat, whether it is your first kingie or your personal best.

Know your quarry Meet the yellowtail kingfish
Unmistakable with their blue-green, torpedo-shaped bodies and bright yellow tails, yellowtail kingfish are built for speed and power. Hook one and hang on, they run hard and dirty for the nearest structure.
10-70kg
Typical kingfish size, and the true monsters push past two metres long.
Sep-Dec
Peak season is spring into early summer, though kings are catchable year-round.
No.1
Sydney's premier light-tackle sportfish, pound for pound a genuine bruiser.
How we fish Jigging and live baiting
Kingfish respond to a few proven methods, and we run whichever is firing on the day to get you connected.
Jigging the structure
Drop a metal jig down over the reefs, wrecks and washes and work it back with sharp lifts. It is fast, physical and seriously addictive, and one of the deadliest ways to tempt a big kingfish. We supply modern jigging outfits and show you the technique.
Live baiting
Slow-troll or drift a live yellowtail past the structure and wait for the rod to buckle over. Live baiting is the classic big-king method, and the strike, when it comes, is heart-stopping. All the live-bait gear is on board and ready to go.
Your skipper Knows where the kings hold
Kingfish are creatures of structure, and finding them comes down to local knowledge. George has spent more than 30 years learning every reef, wreck, wash and mooring around Sydney where the big kings hold, and exactly how to tempt them.
A champion angler, George and the crew set you over the fish, coach you through the jig or the live bait, then talk you through the fight. That brutal first run for the reef is where kings are won and lost. When he says he will put you on them, 40 five-star Google reviews back him up.
