Pricing questions Fishing charter cost FAQs
The questions we get asked most about what a Sydney fishing charter costs.
How much does a Sydney fishing charter cost? 
Prices start from $1,200 for a half-day sport fishing charter and go up to $2,700 for a full day of game fishing. Charters are priced per boat, not per person, and all rods, reels, bait and tackle are included, so the more anglers you bring, the better the value.
Is a fishing charter priced per person or per boat? 
Per boat. You hire the whole vessel and crew for your group, so splitting the cost between mates makes it great value. Across the two boats we cater from 1 to 12 anglers, so get in touch with your numbers for a quote.
What's the cheapest fishing charter? 
Our most affordable option is a half-day sport fishing charter on On Point, from $1,200 for up to 4 anglers. Split between the crew, that's great value for a day on the water.
Are there any hidden costs? 
No. The price includes all rods, reels, bait and tackle, and fuel is included. Gourmet catering is the only optional extra, available on request.
Do I need to pay a deposit? 
There's no deposit just to enquire. We'll confirm payment details once your date and boat are locked in. Please note our cancellation policy: a 50% fee applies within 21 days of the charter, and 100% within 14 days.
How many people can share the cost? 
Up to 8 anglers on Allie Hunter and up to 4 on On Point, so a full boat splits the charter price nicely. Across the fleet we cater from 1 to 12 anglers.
What's included in the price? 
Complimentary top-of-the-range Shimano rods, reels, bait and tackle, fuel, and onboard toilet facilities. You're welcome to bring your own food and drinks, or add gourmet catering on request. See the full list of inclusions above.
