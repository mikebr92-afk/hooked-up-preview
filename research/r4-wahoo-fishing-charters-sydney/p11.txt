The ultimate boys' day out Bucks party charters on Sydney Harbour
Looking for a bucks day the crew will talk about for years? A private bucks party boat hire on Sydney Harbour is hard to beat. Get the lads aboard, crack a few cold ones and spend the day chasing hard-fighting fish on the Harbour and out through the Heads, with the city skyline as your backdrop.
We take care of the lot, the boat, the gear, the good spots and the local know-how, so all you have to do is round up the crew and turn up. Whether the groom is a keen angler or has never held a rod, everyone gets amongst the action, and the drinks stay cold in the fridge all day.

How it works You bring the crew, we bring the rest
A bucks charter with Wahoo is about as easy as a day out gets. Here is how it comes together.
Leave the fishing to us
We supply all the gear, know exactly where the fish are biting and coach anyone who needs a hand. Half day or full day, harbour or offshore, we tailor the trip to what your crew is after and put you onto some genuine sportfish.
Drinks, catering and entertainment
Bring your own esky, or let us sort a gourmet catering and drinks package so no one goes hungry, with heaps of fridge space to keep them cold. We can also organise the extras that make a bucks party, from food and drinks packages to topless girls available to join your boat fishing trip. Just tell us what the boys are after.
Your skipper In safe hands, and on the fish
A great bucks day comes down to two things: staying safe on the water and actually catching fish. With George at the helm you get both. A champion angler with more than 30 years on Sydney’s water, George knows exactly where to put the crew onto the action.
He and the team keep the day running smoothly and the rods bending, so the groom and the crew have a ripper time from the moment you leave the dock. When he says he will put you on the fish, 40 five-star Google reviews back him up.
