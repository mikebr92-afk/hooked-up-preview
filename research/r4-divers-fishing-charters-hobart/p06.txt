Our team & boat
Diver's Fishing Charters Hobart is Tasmania's premier fishing charter and boat tour company. Founded by experienced mariners with deep local knowledge of Tasmanian waters, our company has built a reputation for providing safe, enjoyable, and productive fishing and boating experiences. Our vessel is fully equipped with modern safety equipment, quality fishing gear, and comfortable amenities to ensure every trip is a great experience. We take pride in our team of skilled captains and crew members who bring years of professional maritime experience and a genuine love for Tasmania's ocean environment. Based in Hobart, we have privileged access to some of the best fishing grounds and most scenic coastal areas in southern Tasmania. From deep sea fishing adventures to leisurely sunset cruises, we offer a range of experiences tailored to different interests and skill levels.
The team 
• 
Our team is made up of experienced divers who are passionate about fishing, including ex-dive instructor, technical divers, and dive master.
We personally scout each fishing and dive spot to ensure the best possible experience for our guests. All our crew members are also fully qualified commercial skippers, prioritizing safety and professionalism. We offer a variety of on-water services, including inshore and offshore fishing, diving, seasighting, scientific research, corporate events, and filming, tailored to meet your needs. CONTACT US
The boat 
• 
The Big Kylin is a 13.1-meter alloy plate vessel designed and built to AMSA survey standards in 2024.
It can accommodate up to 30 passengers and is fully insured, ensuring a safe and enjoyable journey. Certiﬁed to operate within 30 nautical miles from shore, the boat is equipped with radar and a night-vision camera for smooth night navigation. Inside, the Big Kylin offers a spacious layout with advanced navigation systems and modern amenities, including two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a water heater, oven, induction cooktop, dishwasher, two refrigerators, a washing machine, a marine generator, and an onboard karaoke system. All safety equipment meets Marine Safety 2C survey requirements, giving you complete peace of mind on every trip.
