CAIRNS PRIVATE FISHING CHARTERS
Why not hire the whole vessel for yourself, a group of mates, a family get-together, meeting or a team building exercise, this is what we call a Cairns Private Fishing Charters . Add into your fishing charter from Cairns , your fish target species, your preferred fishing location; and why not bring along your own personalise fishing rods/reels/lures to help make your own personalise Cairns Private Fishing Charter .
Three locations to choose from
• Trinity Inlet from Cairns for estuary fishing 
The open waters, creeks and deep channels of the Cairns Estuary System are a paradise for anglers and sports-fisher alike. Trinity Inlet also known as The Cairns Harbour is a State Marine Park , this natural habitat has numerous deep holes, mudflats, snags and drop-offs making it a great location to catch a variety of fish species. Let us show you our backyard with your very own personalised Cairns Private Fishing Charter .
• Mulgrave and Russell Rivers and Mutchero Inlet for river fishing 
Only 40 kms south of Cairns, situated near Queensland`s highest mountain, Mt Bartle Freer, nestled amongst Russell River National Park has some of the most breath taking scenery in Far North Queensland. During the months of May, June, July and August this can fish extremely well with Giant Queenfish and Giant Trevally moving around the river system. let us show you one of our favourite places on your Cairns Private Fishing Charter .
• Reef fishing on the Great Barrier Reef from Cairns 
On the reef edges and bommies, with the low tide exposing, the sun shining, you cast your popper or lure, the battle begins! Giant Trevally commonly referred to as GT `s as natural hunters, this is the ulitmate Sportsfishing on the Great Barrier Reef from Cairns in Queensland. Bring out the natural hunter in you, yes, you will have to work hard all day, and so will we! Not only will they continually smash poppers, they will ambush deep diving lures, in, under and around structures; all done with great speed, hard fights and mind blowing runs. To add to the fun, the GT maybe on it`s own or in schools, shoulder charging through the water in pursuit of your popper /lure. While conditions have to be righ, is it your turn to experience the mighty GT or GT`s caught on Popper ….Let the fun begin! for your very own personalised Cairns Private Fishing Charter .

Reef-Bottom Bouncing Fishing Private Charter 
From 6.30 am to 3.00 pm (app.)
Live-bait and / or dead bait up to 4 people on the Great Barrier from Cairns.
Only a short distance from the Cairns Marlin Marina your fishing charter may start with the gathering of live bait before travelling into the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park . Multiple locations are selected for bottom bouncing based on tides, weather conditions, current movement and target fish species . Bottom bouncing simply means dropping baited hook with heavy sinkers in the water; this is a productive way of fishing. Let us know before your Cairns Reef Fishing Charter if you have a preferred target fish species (happy to help out with the bucket list!).
