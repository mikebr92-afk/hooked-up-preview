Cumberoona 
YARRAWONGA
The Cumberoona is a traditional and Australian Paddle Wheeler with all the sites and sounds of years gone by. 
Fully restored by the Knowles family and gracing the waters of the mighty Murray River and Lake Mulwala, on the Victorian and NSW border of Australia. 
It is with great pleasure that they invite you to join them for a leisurely Lake cruise with two large decks that can seat up to 100 passengers, a viewing balcony to engine room operations, interior viewing windows to underwater paddles. It makes for a A great floating venue for a function or Group charter to experience a bit of Australian history first hand, aboard the newly restored paddle steamer. 
Sit back, relax and enjoy refreshments from the fully licensed bar, kiosk and eatery 'The Paddle Box Kitchen'. Offering a seasonal set menu and Lake cruise packs for lunch, afternoon tea and dinner. All whilst you are captivated by the sights of Lake Mulwala and the Murray river and enhanced by the sounds of the paddle wheels. Lake Cruises operating at their mooring on the Yarrawonga Foreshore, perfect for residents and visitors to enjoy her once again. 
Call today to book an unforgettable cruising experience while you sit back, relax in comfort, enjoy the magnificent views and friendly hospitality of a local family owned and operated business.

Bullanginya Dreaming 
BAROOGA
FOR A 10% DISCOUNT AT THE CHECKOUT, USE THE CODE "YARRA10" 
Nestled along the tranquil banks of Bullanginya Lagoon in Barooga, NSW, ‘Bullanginya Dreaming’ promises an unforgettable journey through the Indigenous history of the region. This immersive experience, featuring a mesmerizing laser light show, beautifully blends Bangerang Indigenous insights with the elemental forces of light, water, and fire, creating a captivating spectacle that transcends time. More than just a visual feast, Bullanginya Dreaming is deeply rooted in community involvement and cultural empowerment, aiming to strengthen the bond between the local community and its Indigenous heritage.
The Luna Light Journey produced by LASERVISION consists of 12 Activations, each intricately designed to embody and convey distinct stories and beliefs. At Bullanginya Dreaming, we’re committed to crafting an enriching sensory experience that fosters a deeper connection between the Cobram Barooga Community and the vibrant Indigenous culture woven into the fabric of the region. Through collaborative artistic endeavors with Bangerang creatives, our installations celebrate the intersection of art, community, and culture, inviting visitors to explore and engage with Indigenous history and traditions.
Visitors traverse a 1.8 km immersive night-time walking track, encountering 12 unique activations that tell distinct stories. We recommend allowing 4-5 minutes per activation to fully experience each cycle, making the total journey approximately 60-90 minutes. These captivating installations not only engage the senses but also educate about the significance of local flora and fauna, the profound connection to the land, and the enduring Indigenous legacy. With artful storytelling and multimedia presentations, Bullanginya Dreaming showcases the rich stories, perspectives, and ties to the land inherent in Indigenous identity and culture, fostering a deeper appreciation and understanding for all who venture into this magical realm.

Shelford Shooting 
MULWALA
Nestled on the edge of Lake Mulwala, Shelford Shooting offers a unique regional tourism experience that combines outdoor adventure, expert instruction, and country hospitality. Led by three-time Australian Olympic shooter James Willett, visitors have the rare opportunity to experience clay target shooting under the guidance of a world-class athlete in a safe, professionally managed environment. 
A standout attraction in the Yarrawonga-Mulwala region, Shelford Shooting caters to tourists, groups, corporate events, sporting clubs, family celebrations, and visitors seeking an unforgettable experience beyond the traditional Murray River activities. No prior shooting experience is required, with all equipment, safety briefings, and coaching provided to ensure an accessible and enjoyable experience for participants of all skill levels.
The facility features a world-class clay target shooting range offering a variety of disciplines, including Trap, Skeet, DTL, Universal Trench, and Ball Trap. Located in a picturesque rural setting, Shelford Shooting provides visitors with a distinctive blend of sport, skill development, and friendly competition that complements the region's reputation for outdoor recreation and adventure.

The Classic Drive Co 
The Classic Drive Co is a trusted personalised chauffeur service providing professional, comfortable, and reliable transportation throughout North East Victoria, servicing Bundalong, Yarrawonga, Wangaratta, Wodonga, Rutherglen, Echuca, and surrounding regional communities.
Known as "Your Regional Ride," The Classic Drive Co offers tailored transport solutions for a variety of occasions, including winery tours, weddings, restaurant transfers, race days, school formals, debutante balls, and regional sightseeing tours. Every journey is designed to provide clients with a seamless, stress-free experience, combining exceptional customer service with a commitment to safety and professionalism.
As a proud member of the Wedding Car Association, The Classic Drive Co maintains high industry standards and is dedicated to delivering quality service for wedding and special event transportation. Clients can book with confidence, knowing that both the company's vehicles and drivers are accredited through Safe Drivers Victoria, demonstrating a strong commitment to passenger safety, compliance, and professional conduct.
Whether transporting wedding parties through the picturesque Rutherglen wine region, providing elegant arrivals for school formals and debutante balls, or creating bespoke winery and regional touring experiences, The Classic Drive Co prides itself on offering a personalised service tailored to each client's unique requirements.
Services Include:
• Personalised chauffeur services
• Winery and cellar door tours
• Wedding transportation
• Restaurant drop-off and pick-up services
• Horse racing and event transfers
• School formal transportation
• Debutante ball transportation
• Regional and sightseeing tours
• Private charters and special occasions
Serving clients across Bundalong, Yarrawonga, Wangaratta, Wodonga, Rutherglen, and Echuca, The Classic Drive Co has established a reputation for dependable service, attention to detail, and memorable travel experiences throughout regional Victoria.
