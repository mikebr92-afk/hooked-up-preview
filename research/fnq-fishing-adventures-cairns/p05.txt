Spend a full day fishing the Great Barrier Reef with FNQ Fishing Adventures on a private reef fishing charter designed for families, first-time anglers and experienced fishermen alike.
Our Cairns reef fishing charters and Port Douglas reef fishing charters take you into productive coral reef systems where a huge variety of tropical species can be caught throughout the day.
From colourful coral trout to hard-fighting red emperor and nannygai, you never really know what might come over the side next.
One of the best parts of a full-day reef charter is the variety. Every reef, bommie and patch of ground can produce something different, making it a great option for anglers who simply want to spend the day catching fish and enjoying the Great Barrier Reef.
Throughout the day we use a range of fishing techniques depending on the conditions and what is working best. As we tailor each charter to your specific requirements, this can include bottom fishing with bait, reef fishing with light tackle and jigging . 
Our private reef fishing charters from Cairns and Port Douglas are ideal for small groups wanting a more personalised day on the water. Your group has the boat completely to yourselves and is o perated by experienced local skippers who know exactly what species to target for every tide, moon phase and time of year. Offshore fishing trips to the Great Barrier Reef does not get more personal or professional than this. 
Whether you are introducing the kids to reef fishing, taking the family out for something different or travelling to Far North Queensland specifically to fish the Great Barrier Reef, we tailor each fishing charter to suit your experience level. All gear, tackle, lunch and drinks included . 
If you are looking for a private Cairns fishing charter , Port Douglas fishing charter or a relaxed full-day fishing experience where the whole group can get involved, our Full Day Reef Charter is one of the best ways to experience fishing in Far North Queensland.
