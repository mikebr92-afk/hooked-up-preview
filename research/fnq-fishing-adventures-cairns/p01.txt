PRIVATE FISHING CHARTERS ON THE GREAT BARRIER REEF 
CAIRNS - PORT DOUGLAS - GREAT BARRIER REEF 
At FNQ Fishing Adventures, our passion lies in pursuing incredible fish and exploring the wild locations Far North Queensland is famous for.
From explosive topwater GT fishing and hard-pulling reef species to black marlin, giant barramundi , our Cairns fishing charters are built around one thing — unforgettable days on the water.
Fish the Great Barrier Reef on one of our Cairns reef fishing charters , casting topwater poppers at trophy Giant Trevally or fishing the depths for coral trout, red emperor and nannygai. Troll for pelagic’s like black marlin, sailfish and Spanish mackerel, Experiencing some of the best reef fishing Cairns and the Great Barrier Reef have to offer.
We also offer Cairns barramundi fishing , giving anglers the chance to chase one of Far North Queensland’s most iconic sportfish throughout the region’s tropical rivers and estuary systems.
Whether it’s your first fishing trip or you’re a seasoned sportfisher chasing the trip of a lifetime, this is Great Barrier Reef fishing and Far North Queensland sportfishing at its best.

We offer unforgettable reef fishing charters across Far North Queensland, fishing some of the region’s most iconic waters from Cairns and Port Douglas to the Great Barrier Reef.
Choose from full-day adventures, extended multi-day trips and specialised reef and river fishing charters, tailored to the fishing and conditions.
Whether you’re taking the family out for an unforgettable day, chasing your first reef fish, or you’re a seasoned sportfisher hunting trophy GTs , every trip is a private charter with a maximum of 4 anglers — giving you more space, more fishing time and a personalised experience on the water.
OUR FISHING TRIPS IN FAR NORTH QUEENSLAND
TOPWATER GT CHARTERS 
Our fishing charters from Port Douglas and Cairns fishing charters take anglers into some of the most spectacular and remote coral reef systems in Far North Queensland.
Topwater GT fishing is high on every serious angler’s bucket list — and for good reason. Watching a trophy Giant Trevally explode on a popper or stickbait, boatside is one of the most brutal and exciting experiences in sportfishing.
On the right day, our GT fishing charters in Cairns and Port Douglas can produce incredible numbers of fish, with some sessions seeing 30+ Giant Trevally encountered in a single day . Add Spanish mackerel smashing surface lures with insane aerial strikes, crystal-clear reef edges and some of the most spectacular sight-casting opportunities Australia has to offer, and you have a day of fishing that is very hard to forget.
But this isn’t just about catching a GT — it’s about experiencing the Great Barrier Reef topwater fishing at its most exciting. From casting poppers across untouched reef edges to watching packs of Giant Trevally charge a lure at your feet, FNQ Fishing Adventures offers specialised topwater GT fishing charters from Cairns and Port Douglas for anglers wanting to experience one of Far North Queensland’s most iconic sportfish in one of the most spectacular fishing environments on earth.

GREAT BARRIER REEF FISHING 
If you want a day where everyone gets involved , this is the trip. Our Cairns reef fishing charters take you out onto the Great Barrier Reef to fish some incredible reef country for coral trout, red emperor, nannygai and so many more species. 
Using a mix of bottom fishing, bait fishing and jigging , these trips are all about dropping into productive reef systems and never knowing what might be waiting below.
It’s some of the most enjoyable and family-friendly reef fishing Cairns has to offer , making it the perfect fit for first-time anglers, families and experienced fishermen who simply want a private fishing charter day on the Great Barrier Reef catching quality fish.

LIGHT TACKLE MARLIN CHARTERS 
There’s nothing quite like watching a spread of lures or skipping garfish working behind the boat, waiting for that reel to explode into life. Our Cairns light tackle game fishing charters chase juvenile black marlin, sailfish, Spanish mackerel, wahoo and mahi mahi across the waters of the Great Barrier Reef.
Trolling reef edges, current lines, you never know what will appear next — a black marlin launching clear of the water, a sailfish tearing across the surface or a big Spanish mackerel hitting at full speed. black marlin fishing in Cairns is famous for fast runs and spectacular aerial action, while the incredible variety of pelagic species makes every day different. 
Whether you’re chasing your first billfish or simply want an action-packed day trolling for pelagics off Cairns , this is Great Barrier Reef sportfishing at its most exciting.

FULL DAY ESTUARY CHARTERS 
Our fishing tours from Port Douglas and fishing charters in Cairns offer some of the most stunning scenery you will ever see... coastal runs, world heritage-listed rainforests, and simply incredible landscapes that you will not find anywhere else in Australia.
The fishing? Even better! Sometimes seeing massive numbers of Barramundi in a single day's fishing, Queenies on topwater showcasing insane aerial displays, and white knuckled, tight country Mangrove Jack pursuits and crystal clear jungle drifts for some of the most spectacular sight casting Jungle Perch and Sootie Grunter fishing, Australia has to offer!
To top this off, we have EXCLUSIVE access to the only place in Far North Queensland where Saratoga are accessible.

LAKE TINAROO GIANT BARRAMUNDI 
We have INSANE success rates on meter plus giant barramundi and have proudly been stamped on the map as one of Australia’s best Giant Barramundi fishing charters, with our results speaking for themselves. 
In 2025, we saw 168-meter-plus barramundi hit the deck in both the fresh and salt, 12 of which were in excess of 120cm and a new record of a 132cm MONSTER on our LAKE TINAROO GIANT BARRAMUNDI fishing charters, as seen on IFISH .
