Experience the speed, chaos and excitement of light tackle game fishing in Cairns .
Our light tackle billfish charters target some of the most exciting pelagic species found around the Great Barrier Reef, including juvenile black marlin, sailfish, Spanish mackerel, wahoo and mahi mahi .
With a spread of lures or skipping garfish working behind the boat, there is ALWAYS that anticipation that something could appear at any moment.
Cairns is famous around the world for marlin fishing. Juvenile black marlin fishing is all about fast runs, aggressive strikes and spectacular aerial displays. Watching a black marlin appear behind the boat, attack a lure and launch clear of the water is an experience that is hard to forget. 
Throughout the day we work productive reef edges, current lines, bait concentrations and pelagic highways looking for active fish. Trolling allows us to cover water efficiently and gives anglers the chance to encounter a wide range of species. One minute the spread might be quiet, and the next a reel is screaming as a black marlin, sailfish, Spanish mackerel, wahoo or mahi mahi attacks.
That unpredictability is a huge part of what makes Great Barrier Reef sportfishing so addictive.
FNQ Fishing Adventures specialises in private fishing charters , giving your group the boat to yourselves for the day, which allows us to tailor the fishing around your experience level and what you want to target.
Beginners are welcome, while experienced anglers can enjoy the challenge of targeting powerful pelagic fish on lighter tackle.
Our light tackle billfish charter combines some of the most exciting fishing in Far North Queensland with the incredible surroundings of the Great Barrier Reef.
From watching a marlin light up behind a lure to seeing a sailfish carve across the surface or a Spanish mackerel launch into the air, every strike has the potential to become the highlight of the trip.
Whether you are chasing your first black marlin , have always wanted to catch a sailfish, or simply want an exciting day trolling for pelagic fish on the Great Barrier Reef, we can shape the day around your group and your goals.
