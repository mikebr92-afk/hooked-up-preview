Cairns river barramundi fishing is one of the best ways to experience the wild side of Far North Queensland. 
FNQ Fishing Adventures also offers dedicated river barramundi charters operated by Tropic Barra Adventures , giving anglers access to tropical rivers, mangrove-lined estuaries and rainforest waterways where barra, mangrove jack, queenfish and other iconic northern species thrive.
These trips are built around lure fishing, casting structure, working snags and exploring productive river systems with a guide who specialises in Cairns barramundi fishing . From tight-country strikes deep in the mangroves to surface action and hard-fighting fish in open water, every river can offer something different.
Whether you’re a first-time angler or an experienced lure fisherman, our Cairns river fishing charters are a great way to experience a completely different side of Far North Queensland fishing — with all barramundi trips operated by experts Tropic Barra Adventures.
