Experience the excitement of fishing along the stunning Sunshine Coast with our half-day fishing trip (approx 5 hours)!
Mooloolaba Half Day Fishing Charter
5 hours 
$225 
Up to 12 People
Experience the thrill of offshore fishing on the Mooloolaba Half Day Fishing Charter. Perfect for beginners, families and keen anglers alike, this 5-hour fishing adventure explores the productive reefs and coastal waters off the Sunshine Coast with an experienced local crew. 
With quality fishing gear, bait and refreshments included, all you need to do is step aboard and enjoy a memorable morning chasing a variety of local fish species. 
Tour Highlights 
Fish the Sunshine Coast’s Offshore Reefs 
Head offshore from Mooloolaba to fish productive reefs between Coolum and Currimundi, including the famous Caloundra 7 Mile Reef. 
Target a Variety of Local Species 
Depending on the season and conditions, you may catch Snapper, Pearl Perch, Sweetlip, Tuskfish, Cod, Mackerel and more. 
Perfect for Beginners & Experienced Anglers 
Whether you’re trying offshore fishing for the first time or have years of experience, the friendly crew are there to help every step of the way. 
Experienced Local Crew 
Receive expert advice on fishing techniques, assistance with baiting hooks and fish cleaning so you can take your catch home. 
Relax & Enjoy the Adventure 
Take in the stunning Sunshine Coast coastline while enjoying an exciting morning on the water. 
Details & Prices 
Prices: $225.00 per person 
Duration: 5 hours 
Departure Time: 5:30am 
Departure Location: Mooloolaba, Sunshine Coast QLD 
Age Requirement: Suitable for ages 6+ (children must be accompanied by an adult) 
What’s Included 
• 5-hour offshore fishing charter 
• Quality fishing gear, bait and tackle 
• Experienced skipper and crew 
• Complimentary drinks and water 
• Fish cleaning service 
Good to Know 
• Please arrive 15 minutes prior to departure. 
• Suitable for beginners and experienced anglers. 
• Bring a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen and non-slip footwear. 
• Seasickness medication is recommended if you’re prone to motion sickness. 
• You’re welcome to bring additional snacks (no glass bottles). 
• Tours operate subject to weather and sea conditions. 
Book Your Mooloolaba Half Day Fishing Charter 
Spend the morning exploring the Sunshine Coast’s productive offshore fishing grounds with an experienced local crew. Whether you’re hoping to catch your first fish or add another great day to your fishing adventures, the Mooloolaba Half Day Fishing Charter offers a fun and memorable experience on the water.
