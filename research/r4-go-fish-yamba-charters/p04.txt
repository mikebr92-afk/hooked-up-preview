Fishing Charters

Mixed 8hr Charter
$250 per person
Max 10 Fishermen
8 hours 5:30am - 1:30pm
Individual bookings or smaller groups are welcome to join mixed group charters, offering a great opportunity to enjoy a full day on the water without needing to fill a private boat. Within the 8-hour trip, you can expect to spend approximately 4 to 6 hours fishing, with the remainder allocated to travel between prime fishing spots around Yamba. This arrangement maximises your time on the water and increases the chance of a rewarding catch, all while sharing the experience with fellow anglers.

Private 6hr Charter

12 fishermen max
6 hours 5:30am - 11:30am
For groups seeking exclusive use of the vessel, this is an ideal choice. Perfect for birthdays, bucks and hens parties, work functions, Christmas celebrations, or gatherings with family and friends. the 6hr option is more suited to the groups that are just after a shorter lengthen trip allowing them to be back for midday to enjoy any other plans they have for the afternoon. Pricing for charters can vary depending on a range of factors, including 15% public holiday rates, fluctuations in fuel prices, and demand during peak periods. On public holidays, increased demand often results in higher rates to accommodate the busy schedule and additional operational costs. Additionally, changes in fuel prices can directly impact the overall cost of running the vessel, which may be reflected in the charter price. It is advisable to check current pricing and availability in advance, as rates may be adjusted to reflect these variables.
