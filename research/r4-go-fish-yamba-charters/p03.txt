Kevin (Owner/Vice Captain)
Kev is a lifelong fisherman from Yamba with 40 years of experience in prawn trawling, fish trapping, long lining, and lobster trapping. He knows the ocean very well. A family man, he has passed his love of the sea to his son Frank. 
Frank (Captain)
Frank, a lifelong Yamba local, is always in or near the water, surfing or fishing. He has been the Head Skipper on Achiever since 2022 following his father's path.
Shane (Deckhand)
Shane, skilled in many areas, has years of experience running north coast charters. A good man with great jokes!
