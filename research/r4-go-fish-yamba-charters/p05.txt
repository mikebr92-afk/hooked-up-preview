• 
Where and what time do we meet in the morning? 
We meet up at Yamba Shores Tavern at 5:30am for a quick departure. We’ll have a pick-up point available at Iluka boatshed if requested.
• 
What happens if the weather is bad on my booked date?
We keep a close eye on the most detailed forecasting websites and will inform you if there is cancellation. Please provide your contact details when booking so we can inform you!
• 
Is there a toilet on board?
Yes, the ‘Achiever’ has a toilet on board.
• 
Are food and drinks supplied?
We will have water, soft drinks, sandwich & snacks available free of charge. Bring food & beverages if you wish. Yamba Shores Tavern has a great lunch menu available on return to port. 
• 
Can I bring alcohol?
Yes, we allow BYO, but NO GLASS. Please drink responsibly. 
• 
What happens if I get sea-sick?
We cannot legally supply seasick/nausea medication. If you are unsure PLEASE see a pharmacist beforehand. Take a tablet the night BEFORE or an hour prior to boarding the charter.
• 
Can I bring my own fishing gear?
You’re more than welcome to!. 
• 
How many people can I take on the charter?
A maximum of 12!
• 
What should I do with the fish I catch?
Please have an esky of some sort, with ice, in your vehicle. Ice is available at the Tavern bottle shop if needed.
