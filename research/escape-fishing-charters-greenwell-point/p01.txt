Welcome to Escape Fishing Charters
Escape Charters operates out of Greenwell Point, which is two hours south of Sydney and 20 minutes north of Jervis Bay.
Our boat is a 15 metre West Coaster which provides an excellent fishing platform. 
All gear is provided whether you are reef fishing, game fishing or deep water fishing.
Your skipper - Gary Crocker - has had over 20 years experience fishing these waters off the beautiful NSW South Coast.
We offer reef fishing for up to 12 anglers, targeting such species as snapper, morwong and flathead.
