About Escape Fishing Charters
Escape Charters operates out of Greenwell Point, which is two hours south of Sydney and 20 minutes north of Jervis Bay.
Our boat is a 15 metre West Coaster which provides an excellent fishing platform.
All gear is provided whether you are reef fishing, game fishing or deep water fishing.
Your skipper - Gary Crocker - has had over 20 years experience fishing these waters off the beautiful NSW South Coast. 
We offer reef fishing for up to 12 anglers, targeting such species as snapper, morwong and flathead.
Kingfish are a favourite and you can use soft plastics, or work a jig or use live bait.
Deep sea fishing is available chasing those deep water species such as blue eye cod, hapuka and gemfish.
But game fishing is Escape's speciality, so if you want to get stretched fighting a pelagic, such as a yellow fin tuna,
a tail walking marlin or just go head to head with a shark, they are just waiting for you to book a day with us.
We do encourage tag and release with our anglers for most species.
Overnight trips are available and the boat is ready for tournament fishing. 
Your skipper - Gary Crocker - has had over 20 years experience fishing these waters off the beautiful NSW South Coast.
