THE ULTIMATE 'BUCKET LIST' EXPERIENCE
Join Sam (Birchy) Birch on a hunting, fishing and camping 10 Day Tagalong Tour along the spectacula Oombulgurri Track in the remotest part of the Kimberley. On this amazing journey you will embark on the most extreme 4WD Tagalong Tour ever. Along the way visit swimming holes, freezing fresh water springs, culturally sacred massacre sites and ancient rock-art paintings. There'll be plenty of time along the way to throw live baits and lures in the pristine waters and learn how to fish and hunt Birchy's way.

Tours departs from Home Valley, Gibb River Road and include: 
Extreme 4-Wheel-Driving through spectacular Country
Camping beneath starry skies
Fishing at the best locally known spots
Hunting (bush turkey and cow)
Catch & cook-off
Campfire yarns, storytelling and cultural history
Swimming in waterholes & fresh water springs
Visit ancient rock art sites with cultural interpretation
Visit sacred and historic massacre sites
Last, but not least... good laughs & great company!
