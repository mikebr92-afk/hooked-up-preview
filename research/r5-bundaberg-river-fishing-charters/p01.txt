Your Guide 
Bundaberg River Fishing Charters is captained by myself, Troy Howes, and I absolutely love fishing. 
Let me provide you a great day on the water, and show you a few spots in Bundaberg worth dropping a line in. Fishing experience isn't necessary, so if you don't know a rod from a reel, you've come to the right place. Even if you have never been fishing before, no worries, that's not mandatory, but having a great day is! 
After spending a day on the water with me at Bundaberg River Fishing Charters, you will be hooked on fishing and have memories for a lifetime. 
Family Fun 
There's nothing better than spending a day on the water catching fish and creating lasting memories with the family. We pride ourselves in running family friendly fishing charters that can accommodate just about any age. Create a memory today! 
Corporate Days 
Fishing is a fun option for a corporate outing or a family reunion. While we can only have 4 passengers on board, we can provide a private personal service for you and your team.
