About Us
Home / About Us 
Evans Head Fishing Charters – Operating Since 2005!
Mick McGillivray has been operating his own commercial boats off Evans Head for over 25 years.
By popular demand, Mick purchased a 38 foot Randall, specifically customised for charter fishing.
With Mick’s vast local knowledge of the Evans Head reef system, charter clients are regularly bagging out on Pearl Perch and taking home some great Snapper, Jewfish, Samson Fish, Kingfish and Trag, etc.
Check out The Great Packages for Group Bookings – accommodation available.
What To Bring On A Fishing Trip In NSW?
• Hat, sunglasses and sun-cream
• a hand towel, sensible footwear and spray jacket
• a thermos if you like, and your food and drinks for the 6 hours
• please keep bags size to a minimum 
Please bring an empty esky to put you fish in, to take home at the end of the day. If you want to clean or fillet your fish at the cleaning tables provided, you will also need knives and packaging for your fillets.
