EVANS HEAD DEEP SEA FISHING CHARTERS TERMS & CONDITIONS
Tentative Bookings are held in good faith for 2 weeks
Confirmation is required on bookings within this time frame.
A deposit is required to confirm your booking.
$500 required per single day group bookings.
$1000 deposit is required for weekly packages.
Deposits can be paid by Cash, Direct Deposit or Eftpos (Eftpos will incur a 1.75% surcharge)
Deposits will be refunded in the event of cancellation due to the weather.
Deposits can be transferred to another suitable date if charter is cancelled due to weather
Deposits will not be refunded due to not showing up or late cancellations
In the event you must cancel late notice, deposit will be refunded if date can be rebooked.
Individual bookings ($170 per day) must be paid in full prior to arrival 
Confirmation of departure time is confirmed the afternoon prior to your trip.
Failing to respond to confirmation may result in loss of seat.
Excessive drinking is not permitted, and will result in cancellation of charter.
Accommodation booked for packages is made in good faith, although we are not held responsible for changes made in holiday homes or availability of holiday homes.
Personal property is the sole responsibility of the client. 
Although our catch history is excellent, and it is in our best interest to catch our clients plenty of fish, we cannot control the feeding habits of wild caught fish and therefore cannot guarantee what we catch each trip. 
Photos taken during each charter may be used on our website. It is your responsibility to advise us if you do not wish to have your photo taken.
