EAST CAPE MOTHERSHIP 
OVERVIEW
Great Barrier Reef flats and remote mainland coast, reachable only by mothership.
• Season: October – December
• Duration: 5-6 days fishing
• Group size: 6 anglers, twin share 
• Base: on board Argo, 27 m yacht
• Fly into Horn Island
Trip Info
From October to December we move to the other side of the Cape. We fish the flats of the Great Barrier Reef and a stretch of remote mainland coast that you can only reach by mothership. You live aboard Argo , a 27-metre yacht, fly into Horn Island to join the boat, and spend 5-6 days fishing water that very few anglers ever see.
• On board Argo , 27-metre yacht
• 5-6 days fishing
• Fly into Horn Island to join the boat
• GBR flats and remote mainland coast, accessible only by mothership
[ THE MOTHERSHIP ] 
Life aboard Argo
Argo is a 27-metre motor yacht built for extended trips to remote coast. Anglers share twin cabins, and some cabins have their own ensuite. At the end of the day you come back to an air-conditioned saloon with a bar and a chef's meal at the dining table. Custom 5.5m carbine skiffs run you from the mothership onto the fishing grounds, and there's unlimited WiFi on board if you need to check in from home.
• 27 m motor yacht
• Twin share cabins, ensuite options
• Chef on board, commercial galley
• Air-conditioned saloon and bar
• Unlimited WiFi
• Laundry on board
