WEST CAPE MOTHERSHIP 
OVERVIEW
Remote western rivers and the Torres Strait, fished from a 27-metre mothership.
• Season: July - September
• Duration: 5-6 days fishing
• Group size: 6 anglers, twin share
• Base: on board Argo , 27 m yacht
Trip Info
Some of the Cape's best water is too remote to reach from land. For three months a year we go after it, living aboard Argo , a 27-metre yacht, and working the remote western rivers and the Torres Strait. Six anglers, twin share in the boat and cabins, with each day's fishing chosen for the tides and conditions. Fly into Horn Island from Cairns and we take it from there.
• On board Argo , 27-metre yacht
• 6 anglers, twin share (boat and rooms)
• 5-6 days fishing
• Fly Cairns → Horn Island to join the boat
• Access to the remote western rivers and the Torres Strait
[ THE MOTHERSHIP ] 
Life aboard Argo
Argo is a 27-metre motor yacht built for extended trips to remote coast. Anglers share twin cabins, and some cabins have their own ensuite. At the end of the day you come back to an air-conditioned saloon with a bar and a chef's meal at the dining table. Custom 5.5m carbine skiffs run you from the mothership onto the fishing grounds, and there's unlimited WiFi on board if you need to check in from home.
• 27 m motor yacht
• Twin share cabins, ensuite options
• Chef on board, commercial galley
• Air-conditioned saloon and bar
• Unlimited WiFi
• Laundry on board
