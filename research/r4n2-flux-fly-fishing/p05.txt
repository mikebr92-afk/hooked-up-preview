WEIPA, QUEENSLAND 
The Accomodation
The best fishing trips are the ones where everything just works. Transfers on time, comfortable accommodation sorted, meals handled, and nothing to worry about except the next cast. Our all-inclusive packages take care of airport transfers, accommodation, and dining, while our custom options let you build exactly the trip you want.
Full package or fishing-only. Budget-conscious or premium experience. We'll make it work.
Accomodation Options
Weipa Fishing Lodge
Located on the northern boundary of the caravan park overlooking the mouth of the Mission river with a wonderful ambiance with sensational views. The Fishing lodge is competitively priced, has 5 rooms, can accommodate up to 12 people and is serviced by a private chef looking after your exclusive menu.
Learn more
Albatross Bay Resort
The Albatross is the oldest accommodation in Weipa it has hotel style facilities with bar and restaurant and bistro. The Alby is within walking distance of the main Weipa shopping area and 5 minute walk to the golf and bowls clubs.
Learn more
Beachfront Villas
The beachfront villas can accommodate up to 4 people and have cooking facilities and water views and are a great budget option if you prefer to look after your own meals.
Learn more
Weipa Caravan Park
100m from the Weipa shopping centre and close to the post office and tackle shop in the middle of Weipa. 48 powered sites and self contained cabins and swimming pool. Great for the budget fisherman.
Learn more
We are lucky enough to be supported by some of the best brands in the industry. These companies make our jobs a lot easier, and we only put our name to gear we can trust and rely on.
