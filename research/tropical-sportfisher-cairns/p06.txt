The Fishing Options 
Escape the crowds and fish the wild rivers, rainforest streams and tropical islands of the Cassowary Coast.
These private guided trips give anglers the opportunity to experience the full diversity of tropical sportfishing.
Barramundi fishing in coastal rivers to jungle perch in World Heritage rainforest creeks and topwater fishing around tropical islands.

The Fishing
Over several days we explore a range of environments across the Cassowary Coast.
River & Estuary Barramundi, mangrove jack, queenfish and river GTs.
Blue water & Tropical Islands (Weather Permitting) Topwater fishing, bait balls and reef edges around tropical islands.
Rainforest Backcountry Walk and wade crystal clear streams for jungle perch and sooty grunter.
Every trip is private and guided, with itineraries adjusted daily to suit conditions.
Boat Options
19ft Sportsman – “Tropic Star” Ideal for island fishing, larger rivers and exploring the coastline.
4.8m Barra Tracker Perfect for shallow rivers, creeks and estuary systems.
Both vessels are equipped with LiveScope sonar to locate and target barramundi in real time.
Accommodation
Trips are based from Mission Beach , with comfortable poolside accommodation, great dining options and easy access to multiple river systems.
After a full day fishing we usually return around 5pm , giving you time to relax before dinner.

Private Fishing Charters & Nature Tours 
Tropical Sportfisher is a locally owned fly and sportfishing charter based in Cairns, Far North Queensland. Led by experienced local guides, we specialise in guided barramundi fishing and light tackle lure and fly fishing across Cairns, Port Douglas, and Mission Beach .
We run private day trips for up to 2 anglers per boat, and offer multi-day remote adventures for small groups, including mothership and heli-access options.
Our trips include river, estuary, jungle perch, and inshore bluewater fishing. We also offer wildlife cruises , croc spotting, birdwatching, and private nature or photography trips to secluded beaches, waterfalls, or tropical islands.
Whether you’re chasing fish or exploring wild places, our charters are tailored to your interests and the conditions. Get in touch to experience the best of Tropical North Queensland with a local guide.
