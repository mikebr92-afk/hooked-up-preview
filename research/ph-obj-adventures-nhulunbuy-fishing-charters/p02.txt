East Arnhem Fishing Charters 
Welcome to OBJ Adventures, where your journey into the heart of East Arnhem Land begins. Operating out of the pristine waters and wild landscape in Nhulunbuy, Northern Territory, our family-owned company specialises in unforgettable, eco-friendly fishing adventures. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or a first-time adventurer, we’re here to help you catch everything from Spanish Mackerel to Red Emperor .

About OBJ 
OBJ Adventures Nhulunbuy Fishing Charters is an aboriginal-owned and operated business that provides unforgettable, sustainable fishing charters departing from Nhulunbuy, exploring the pristine waters of East Arnhem Land. Choose from full-day, half-day, exclusive island charters, sunset harbour cruises, or offshore fishing adventures to match every budget. Our goal is to create lasting memories for each guest. 
We believe in protecting our environment and practice catch and release to ensure future generations can enjoy the same beautiful waters. 
We take pride in working together, blending our unique strengths and passions to create exceptional experiences 
We strive for excellence in every tour, ensuring that every guest leaves with fantastic memories and a new appreciation for nature. 
As locals, we invest in our community, participating in and sponsoring local events and conservation efforts.
