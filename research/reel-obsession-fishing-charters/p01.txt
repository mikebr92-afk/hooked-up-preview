Welcome to Reel Obsession Fishing Charters 
If you are obsessed with fishing or would simply like to try, there is no better place than aboard the Nevlin! 
Reel Obsession Fishing Charters specialise in catering for groups of up to 10 passengers, we offer both In-shore and Off-shore charters. 
We use the latest technology in fish finding and GPS equipment also fishing using moon phases and tide changes to your advantage. 
Reel Obsession Fishing Charters is based at Flinders Victoria the gateway to Westernport and Bass Strait
