Welcome aboard The Nevlin! 
33 ft long with an aluminium hull 
powered by a Volvo Penta 230HP super/turbo charged engine 
holds up to 1000 litres of fuel 
top speed is 20 knots and can cruise at 14 knots 
The Nevlin
The most important things needed on any boat, tea and coffee facilities and BBQ for BYO food (by prior arrangement) 
When you are out on the boat we will make sure you can have a good meal
. . . . and what could be better than a hot drink!
At Reel Obsession we understand that its the small things that can make a fishing trip even better. We believe we have covered the small things with such additions 
Large central preparation and gutting table
Preparations tables on all rails & fresh water wash taps
Ample rod retainers on all rails and above deck
Ample storage space & plenty of comfortable seating
. . . . sometimes the best seat on the boat
Ever been fishing and can’t quite remember the good spot you went to last time? Well not a problem with Reel Obsession. We use state of the art navigation equipment that not only uses GPS mapping but we can also mark your good spots on our weigh point list so next time you come along with us we can simply call up the last spots you went to and go back. 
Remember the spot?
Easy and enjoyable fishing
At Reel Obsession we put our passenger’s safety at the top of the list with life vests, EPIRB, 12 man life boat and 2 two way radios .
