About
Charters is a renowned fishing charter and boat rental service based in Tweed Heads, New South Wales. With over 20 years of experience, Cushy Fishing Charters has been serving customers from the Gold Coast and NSW, offering unforgettable fishing experiences.
Located just a short boat ride away from the Tweed River entrance, Cushy Fishing Charters provides access to some of the best reefs in the Gold Coast, Tweed Coast, and Byron Bay regions. Their prime location allows for a diverse range of fishing opportunities, catering to both seasoned anglers and novices.
Embark on an exciting adventure with Cushy Fishing Charters' experienced crew, who will guide you to the best fishing spots in the deep sea. Their deep sea fishing charters offer the chance to reel in impressive catches and create lasting memories.
Cushy Fishing Charters is strategically located in renowned fishing grounds, ensuring access to abundant fish populations and diverse species. Their experienced crew possesses in-depth knowledge of local hotspots and seasonal fishing patterns, guaranteeing a successful and thrilling fishing experience.
With their state-of-the-art fishing vessels, Cushy Fishing Charters combines comfort and luxury to create the ultimate fishing adventure. You can explore the bountiful waters off the coast while enjoying the top-notch facilities and amenities provided on their boats.
In addition to fishing charters, Cushy Fishing Charters also offers other exciting services, such as whale watching during the winter months. Witness the beauty of humpback whales as you take part in an amazing experience alongside your fishing trip.
Cushy Fishing Charters is conveniently located at the Tweed Marina, River Terrace, Tweed Heads NSW, making it easily accessible from various areas. It is just a short distance from the Gold Coast International Airport, the Brisbane CBD, Byron Bay, and Surfers Paradise.
Led by Master class V skipper Kyle Cush, Cushy Fishing Charters ensures that every customer has a safe and fun day out on the water. Whether you're an experienced angler or a first-time fisherman, their team welcomes everyone and is always happy to help and share their fishing knowledge.
Don't miss the opportunity to embark on an unforgettable fishing charter adventure. Book your trip with Cushy Fishing Charters today and turn your fishing dreams into reality.
