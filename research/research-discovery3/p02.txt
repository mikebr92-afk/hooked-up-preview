3 Yamba Rd, Yamba, NSW, 2464 Reel Time Fishing Charters, located in Yamba, Australia, offers deep sea fishing charters and whale watching cruises, operating daily as weather permits. The charters focus on quality reef fish such as pearl perch, snapper, mulloway, and mackerel.
The custom-built 36ft multi-cata boat, Odin, is equipped with top-of-the-line Shimano fishing rods and reels. The charters accommodate small groups to enhance the fishing experience, ensuring participants catch fish while enjoying the beautiful surrounds of Yamba.
