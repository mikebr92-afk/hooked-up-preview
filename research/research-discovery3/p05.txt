Sea Lord Howe
Explore Lord Howe Island with us from the water. We offer sightseeing, fishing and snorkelling tours. Sea Lord Howe is a locally owned business run by Brad Wilson, a 6th generation islander and has spent his childhood learning and experiencing everything that is Lord Howe Island.
All of our tours are run on Lulawai, a 37ft custom built cabin cruiser specifically designed for the island. It was built on Lord Howe in 1968 and launched in 1969 for the late Roy Wilson. The vessel was completely refitted in 2024 to accommodate modern tours, Lulawai is equipped with a full bathroom, spacious seating inside and out on comfortable cushioned seats, an easy set of stairs to enter and exit the vessel, plenty of room to walk around and two knowledgeable crew members at all times.
We offer four main tours:
• Around the Island Scenic Boat Cruise
Circumnavigate the island with Lulawai as she cruises along magnificent volcanic cliffs, gently floats beneath spectacular mountains, get up close to the Admiralty Isles and the thousands of seabirds nesting there. During the 2 hours you are joining us, two crew members with extensive knowledge about the island will point out landmarks, native wildlife as well as answer any questions you may have about our beautiful island.
• Sunset Fishing Trip
What better way to end the day than with a sunset fishing trip! Head out into the lagoon to calmly enjoy the sunset before chasing various species suited to all anglers. This is an excellent opportunity to enjoy the island during sunset from its best angle. This trip especially suits families or couples who would enjoy a relaxed environment but still wish to leave Lord Howe Island with a fishing experience. Whether you wish to relax with a glass of wine on the front deck or exclusively fish from the back this an opportunity not to be missed.
• Half or Full-Day Outer Reef Fishing Trip
Want to chase the fish of a lifetime? Join us for either a half or full day fishing aboard the Lulawai! Explore the outer reefs with a rod in your hand as we attempt to catch kingfish, trevally, wahoo and other large game species. Bookings essential as spaces are limited to 5 POB, don’t miss out on this once in a lifetime experience. Charters for larger groups also available.
• Choose Your Own Adventure Day
Can’t find the tour you are looking for? Limited time, but want to try everything? Sea Lord Howe is here to help you get the most out of your holiday! If you have a particular experience in mind please come and talk to us. Charter the boat and plan your own adventure. We are happy to combine your group or your family’s needs during your charter. We can take you snorkeling as well as sightseeing, fishing or why not enjoy an amazing sunset from a prime location. All you have to do is ask. Full or half day charters available.
