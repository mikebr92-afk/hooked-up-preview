MV Belle Chase
Join us aboard “Belle” for a half or full day adventure in the fish rich waters that surround our beautiful Island.
Custom built for Lord Howe, Belle Chase is a big, beamy 36’ Cruiser that caters for both the experienced angler and family groups alike.
With a limit of six passengers on any one trip, there’s plenty of elbowroom on her spacious deck, as well as a comfortable cabin in which to relax between bites. (There’s also an enclosed toilet)
All tackle and bait is supplied, just bring along a hat and some sunscreen and enjoy the ride.
If you’d like to join us, please call 02 6563 2025, and we’ll let you know available times.
Alternatively, you can email us at info@bellechasefishing.com.au
Charter Fishing Prices 
Belle operates around Lord Howe itself, along with longer trips* to the famous Balls Pyramid which lays some 15 klm’s south of the Island.
Lord Howe Island Shelf Charters 
4 hour shared trip $300 per person
5 hour Shared trip $350 per person
Balls Pyramid Shelf Charters 
8 hour shared trip $450 per person
We also offer Exclusive Charters, with pricing available upon request.
* Being further afield, we offer an extended chartering time that will allow you to enjoy both the fishing and the scenic wonder of this world famous landmark.
