About Us 
Experienced fishing guide
Luke has been a professional mariner for just under 20 years. He has guided fishing trips in 16 countries and fished, surfed, freedived and dived in over 20 others, hosting many world leaders and celebrities alike. He was a master and engineer working on international luxury superyachts after running watersports and fishing guiding operations on 2 islands in North Queensland (Dunk and Brampton Islands). 
Great location
Having lived all over the world, Luke moved to Bundaberg to start his family in what he describes as one of the best places on Earth. There is gorgeous coral reef with all the associated tropical marine life that is met with species of fish found in more southern waters. 
Unlike our northern neighbour, The Town of 1770, Bundaberg has a well maintained deep water port that is not subject to tidal restrictions and does not have a designated bar crossing. 
It is only 4.5 hours drive from Brisbane, or less than an hour flight with world class fishing and diving right on Bundaberg's doorstep! 
maritime trainer
Luke is an accredited Boat Training Provider with the Qld Dept of Transport and Main Roads. He holds a Certificate in Training and Assessment and a passion for sharing his seafaring knowledge.
