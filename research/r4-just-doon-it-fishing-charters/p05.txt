About Us 
JUST DOON IT is a lifestyle company founded from our love for fishing and the outdoors. With Jayson's passion for fishing and Kate's hosting and business skills, we aim to inspire people to embrace the thrill of outdoor adventures. 
At JUST DOON IT, we create memorable fishing experiences where everyone can step outside, have fun, and 'JUST DOON IT.' 
We're Jays & Kate, a couple of adventure-loving "Dooners" who live to explore and share amazing places & experiences with others. 
Whether it's discovering hidden gems or finding the best fishing spots, we love getting out there and showing you how to make the most of life. 
Our motto? "Just Doon It" – live FULL OUT, chase down fun, and get "Hooked on Livin'." 
​ 
What do we actually do? 
​ 
Kate will host you at our campgrounds, ensuring you're comfortable whether you're camping or caravanning, while Jays will take you on exciting fishing trips. 
​ 
Whether you're with friends, a partner, or family, we welcome everyone to join us and dive into the adventure!

THE FISHIN' SPOTS 
SHADY CAMP 
On the Mary River National Park, NT, is a top spot for ​barramundi fishing. Located 120 km from Darwin, it's famous for its ​tidal barrage and diverse fishing opportunities. Surrounded by ​wildlife and stunning outback scenery, it's a cracking spot for ​Dooners during the Run Off season. 
DALY RIVER 
The Daly River, located about 220 km southwest of ​Darwin, is a renowned fishing destination, especially for ​barramundi during the Run Off season. Known for its wide, deep ​waters and stunning riverbanks, the fishing and the scenery are ​equally as amazing. This location is famous for fishing competitions ​like the Barra Nationals and Girls Gone Fishing 
DUNDEE BEACH 
Located 140 km southwest of Darwin, is a top ​destination for barramundi fishing charters. Known for its tidal ​rivers and creeks, the area provides excellent opportunities to hook ​trophy-sized barramundi.
