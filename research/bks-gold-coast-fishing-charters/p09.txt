ALL are extremely welcome on our Gold Coast fishing charters, from first-timers to experienced anglers, sightseers, Mum, Dad, kids, grandparents, the boys’ or the girls’ day out. Your skipper and deckhand will gladly assist you in a safe, fun and friendly atmosphere.
Your boat for your fishing adventure is a fast, stable, 10m cougar cat with twin 250hp Yamaha outboard engines, ample seating inside and out, a shade roof and a toilet.
We supply quality Penn and Shimano rods and reels in both overhead and spin as well as left-handed outfits. On our overheads, we use no stretch, feel-every-bite braided fishing line, to give you the best chance of feeling the bite and catching that fish. We fish with bait on unweighted and weighted rigs all year, as well as casting and trolling lures during the pelagic season (Nov – June).
