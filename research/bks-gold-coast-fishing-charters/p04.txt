Offshore Reef Fishing Charters
Mixed group and private half day fishing charters on the Gold Coast.
Join us for a half day fishing charter. This is a 5 hour fishing adventure, departing Runaway Bay Marina at 6am and 11:15am, 7 days per week (weather permitting).
The target species on our half day fishing charter on the Gold Coast depend on the season, but include snapper, school/spotted/spanish mackerel, cobia, jewfish, teraglin, tailor, flathead, tusk fish, perch, cod, tuna & more.
Flying solo, or in a smaller group?
Join a mixed group half day Gold Coast fishing charter! Just book your spot/s and we’ll build the rest of the trip (12 maximum).
Your boat for your half day Gold Coast fishing charter...
is a safe and stable fishing platform, as used by many rescue authorities.
‘Reef Cat II’ is a 10 meter/33ft Cougar catamaran with fishing areas on both the front and back decks and is licensed to carry 12 guests. Built to government survey regulations, survey safety equipped, and fitted with modern fishing electronics, she is a fast, stable, twin hulled, wide bodied, twin engine boat with ample seating under a large roof area. She is powered by two quiet and smokeless 250hp Yamaha four-stroke engines.
Reef Cat II is a purpose built fishing charter boat and therefore has a large , insulated icebox to chill your catch.
For your comfort , there is an enclosed and modern electric flushing, highly sanitized , private toilet in the forward cabin. It is complemented with an electric fresh running water tap with basin, liquid soap, mirror and disinfectant deodorizers.
Visit the “ Your Crew ” page to meet the crew who will welcome you on board the charter boat and assist you throughout your trip.
Do you have a larger group, or just want more casting room? Check out our private charters!
Book a private half day fishing charter for your group, and bring anywhere from 1 to 12 people.
PERFECT for the boys/girls day out, a friendly fishing competition, work groups, Christmas parties, birthdays, hens parties, bucks parties, the annual family get together.. or any occasion!
Inclusions on all half day fishing charter and whole boat trips are:
Quality left or right-handed Penn and Shimano rods and reels, bait, tackle, sunscreen, soft drinks, water, biscuits, lollies, tips and assistance from your skipper and deckhand.
It’s your choice to keep, release or share your legal fish. Your kept fish are bled, gutted, iced down and bagged.
