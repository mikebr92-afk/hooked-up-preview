FAQs
So you have questions about Gold Coast fishing tours?
You’ve come to the right place! Answers to our most frequently asked questions about our Gold Coast fishing tours can be found below.
Reef Cat II, your boat for your adventure 
All are welcome! Bring the family, kids 6+ 
Tap the questions to see the answers
WHAT IS THE COST? 
The 5-hour ocean reef fishing trip starts at $200 per person. Up to date costs for specific trips can be found here .
Follow us on Facebook & instagram for access to discounts & special offers.
HOW DO I ENQUIRE, BOOK AND PAY? 
A 50% deposit is required to make a booking.
Click here 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for information, pricing, availability and to make a booking or buy an e-gift card.
You can also call us on 0414293034 between 9am and 5pm. Bec & Kaleb both crew the boat as well as manage the bookings, so please send us a text or email (bk@bksfishing.com.au) if you don’t get hold of us on the phone.
WHICH DAY DO I BOOK AND DO WE FISH IN UNSAFE OR RAINY WEATHER? 
We recommend that you book your trip on a day early in your timeframe so that you have time to reschedule in the event we have to cancel due to weather (strong wind and rough seas). 
Rain isn’t usually a problem for us. We don’t cancel/reschedule if rain is forecast or if it is raining. There is often less rain at sea than on the land, and cloud cover often improves the catch rate on many species.
It’s always a good idea to bring a raincoat.
DO WE RECEIVE A REFUND IF THE TRIP IS CANCELLED? 
In the event that we have to cancel your fishing charter, you will be given the choice to receive a refund, or reschedule to another day.
If you are using gift cards, your gift cards will be revalidated and ready to use at your convenience. 
I HAVE NEVER BEEN FISHING BEFORE, IS THAT A PROBLEM? 
Not at all, our friendly crew will assist you until you have a good fishing technique in a friendly team atmosphere. First time and experienced anglers, sightseers, Mum, Dad, kids, grandparents, work parties, boy’s or girl’s day out, all are extremely welcome.
ARE KIDS ALLOWED TO COME? 
Yes! We pride ourselves on our family friendly experience and strong reputation for being the best Gold Coast charter for kids. Kids from the age of 6 and up are more than welcome to come along on our Gold Coast fishing tours.
Kids do need to be accompanied by an adult please.
IS ALCOHOL PERMITTED? 
We are a family friendly experience and have zero tolerance for drunk behaviour. We understand the enjoyment in a casual drink shared between friends while wetting a line, but we are not a party boat, and we do have some rules in place to ensure an enjoyable and safe experience for our passengers and crew. The following rules are strict, and apply to both mixed group and private charters:
• No spirits.
• Maximum mid strength (1 standard drink per can), maximum 6 cans per person.
• Cans only.
• Any person who is drinking before or during boarding, or is deemed by the crew to be intoxicated, will not be permitted to board.
• Any signs of intoxicated, unsafe or rude behaviour will result in the trip being terminated prematurely with no refunds offered.
IS SMOKING PERMITTED? 
We are a family friendly experience and we often have kids on board. 
The normal QLD smoking laws apply for our vessel. There is a section on the vessel, away from non-smokers, where smoking is permitted.
On occasion, smoking will not be permitted (usually due to ocean conditions), if you MUST be able to smoke, please book a private charter.
WHAT IS INCLUDED? 
Quality left or right-handed Penn and Shimano rods and reels, bait, tackle, sunscreen, soft drinks, iced water, biscuits, tips and assistance from your skipper and deckhand.
Your legal captures can be released, shared or kept by you, your kept fish remain whole, are bled, gutted, iced down and bagged.
HOW MANY PEOPLE DO YOU TAKE? 
Our boat, Reef Cat II, can take 12 passengers and 2 crew. 
On our mixed group charters, we aim to take a maximum of 10 passengers. This gives you a more quality trip, with plenty of room for all of your fishing fun. 
We take a maximum of 12 people on our half day private charters. 
WHAT ARE THE MINIMUM NUMBERS REQUIRED TO BOOK? 
We need a minimum of 6 people to go ahead with a charter, but not to worry as we build the group and rarely have to cancel for low numbers.
Booking for our Gold Coast fishing tours as a solo fisho, twos, threes, etc is no problem.
In the event we do not reach our minimum required numbers, you will have the choice to either reschedule or receive a refund.
WHERE DO WE BOARD THE BOAT AND AT WHAT TIME? 
The departure location for our Gold Coast fishing tours is the Runaway Bay Marina, 247 Bayview St, Hollywell, QLD, 4216. 
This is only 12km north of the middle of Surfers Paradise, or 70km south of Brisbane City.
From Brisbane, take exit 57 (Hope Island Rd) and follow your GPS to Runaway Bay Marina.
We ask that you aim to arrive 15-20 minutes prior to your booked charter time.
IS THERE CAR PARKING AVAILABLE? 
Yes. Free parking is available on site. Please make sure you read the signs carefully and only park in a designated car space.
WHAT SHOULD I BRING? 
• Your own bottle of water, as well as something pre-packaged and ready to eat (muesli bar, sandwich, packet of chips/biscuits, fruit etc).
• Wear practical clothing that can get wet, dirty and fishy, and bring a spray jacket/raincoat all year round. In winter, wear layers so you are warm, but able to remove layers as the sun warms things up.
• Enclosed shoes are a great idea, but it’s your choice on what footwear you wear. Keep in mind your shoes are likely to get wet.
• Any important personal medications (eg. Asthma puffer, epi pen).
• Camera, hat and sunglasses.
• A bag to keep all of your belongings together in the cabin.
• An empty esky/cooler to leave in your car to take your fish home in after we put them in a plastic bag.
HOW FAR DO WE TRAVEL TO SEA? 
On the 5 hour Gold Coast fishing tours (our most popular trip), we go up to 11 kilometers offshore. Fishing locations are decided on the day, and depend on the weather, target species and what’s been on the bite.
HOW LONG IS THE BOAT DRIVING TIME? 
We can fish anywhere from 2 – 20km from the Gold Coast Seaway. We usually aim to be fishing within 30 minutes of departure.
Some days we don’t need to move from the first spot, other days we might have to search a bit more. It depends what the fish are doing.
Most fishing spots are only 10 minutes apart.
WHAT DO WE CATCH, WHERE DO WE FISH AND WHAT WEATHER CAN WE EXPECT? 
• We fish all year round, mainly over rocky reefs from 2-20km from the Seaway.
• We fish depths from 15 to 70 meters.
• Target fish are Mackerel, Cobia, Kingfish, Jewfish, Tuna, Snapper, Teraglin, Tusk Fish, Tailor, Flathead, Cod, Perch, Marlin, Mahi Mahi, Wahoo and many other mixed reef fish.
• As a general rule, we fish where we had the best bite on our last trip.
• Fishing techniques are varied between weighted paternoster rigs, float lining dead baits, live bait, strip bait/fillets of freshly caught legal size bait fish, high speed metal lure spinning and lure trolling – whatever is working the best on the day or days prior.
• If unfamiliar with those terms, don’t let that “fish speak” worry you, we gladly assist/coach as required.
• Mid Autumn, winter, and early spring often have the most comfortable mild weather, with day temperatures around 20-25deg Celsius. Late spring and summer have typical tropical weather, often with cooling afternoon sea breezes giving approximately 30 degrees day temp. In summer and autumn, we encounter more pelagic fish like Mackerel, Marlin, Wahoo, Mahi Mahi. Winter and Spring are our best times for Snapper, Cobia/Black Kingfish and Tuna. Other listed species can be caught all year round.
CAN I KEEP THE FISH THAT I CATCH? 
Yes, you choose to either release, keep or share your legal captures. Kept fish are gutted and iced down, plastic bags are provided if required.
It is a good idea to leave an esky/cooler/bucket in your vehicle to put your bagged fish in after the trip.
CAN I BYO FISHING GEAR? 
• Yes, you can bring your own fishing gear and food/drinks, sunscreen etc on our Gold Coast fishing tours. If your fishing gear is not suitable to work in with the rest of the fishing plan, the crew will let you know and you will be able to use our gear.
