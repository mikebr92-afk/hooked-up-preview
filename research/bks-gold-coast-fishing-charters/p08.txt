Terms and Conditions
We understand, things happen! We always try our best to work with you in the event that you have to cancel. Please see our cancellation policy below.
GENERAL CANCELLATION POLICY
Customer’s responsibility:
Cancellations must be made directly with BKs Gold Coast Fishing Charters office, by phone (0414293034) or email (bk@bksfishing.com.au). The customer is responsible for ensuring the cancellation has been received and responded to by BKs Gold Coast Fishing Charters.
More than 30 days prior to booking:
Any cancellations or changes requested with a minimum of 30 days notice are eligible for a full refund, credit or to reschedule. No fees apply.
Less than 30 days prior to booking: 
Cancellations requested with less than 30 days notice will be subject to the following policy:
• If the cancelled spots do not get filled, the customer will not be eligible for a refund or credit. For the best chance to receive a refund or credit, the customer should advise of a cancellation with as much notice as possible. BKs Gold Coast Fishing Charters will then put the order “on hold”, making the spots available. If the spots fill, the booking will be cancelled, and a refund will be processed to the original payment method and/or gift cards will be made valid again. In the case that the spots do not fill, the customer can choose to either attend the original booking (or send someone in your place) or to lose their deposit/payment/gift cards, including any remaining balance that is due.
Less than 1 day (24 hours) prior to booking: 
When a cancellation is made only 1 day (24 hours) prior to a booking, or the customer does not attend on the day, the customer will not be eligible for a refund or credit, and will be charged for any remaining balance that is due/gift cards will be redeemed/cancelled.
GIFT CARDS
GIFT CARDS ASSOCIATED WITH CANCELLED BOOKINGS:
Gift cards are non-refundable under any circumstance. If the customer is eligible for a refund or to reschedule, associated gift card/s will be revalidated until the original expiry date. In the event that the customer is not eligible for a refund or to reschedule, associated gift card/s will be redeemed/cancelled.
ALCOHOL POLICY
Any person who is drinking before or during boarding, or is deemed by the crew to be intoxicated, will not be permitted to board and no refunds or reschedule options will be offered.
Any signs of intoxicated, unsafe or rude behaviour will result in the trip being terminated prematurely with no refunds or reschedule options offered.
We are a family friendly experience and have zero tolerance for drunk behaviour. Please see our FAQs “IS ALCOHOL PERMITTED” here , for more information.
WEATHER & LOW PASSENGER NUMBERS
WEATHER CANCELLATIONS:
A cancellation due to weather must only be made by BKs Gold Coast Fishing Charters, and not by the customer. Weather confirmations/cancellations are emailed by 5pm the day before the charter. If a charter is cancelled due to weather, the customer will be given the choice to reschedule or receive a refund and/or gift cards to be made valid again.
UNEXPECTED WEATHER FORCING A CHARTER TO END EARLY:
In the event of a charter being ended 1-3 hours early due to weather, the customer will receive a discounted return trip based on how many hours were completed.
In the event of a charter being ended 4-9 hours early, a pro-rata hourly rate will be calculated based on the rate paid per person, and the customer will receive a refund for the hours that were not completed. 
MINIMUM OPERATING NUMBERS CANCELLATIONS:
BKs Gold Coast Fishing Charters have a minimum operating number of 6-8 passengers (depending on various factors). In the rare event the minimum has not been met, a cancellation email will be sent, and the customer will have the choice to reschedule or receive a refund and/or gift cards to be made valid again.
