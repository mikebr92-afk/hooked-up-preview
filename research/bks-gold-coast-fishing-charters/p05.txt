Meet The Crew
Meet the BKs Gold Coast fishing charter skippers!
A personal goal of your fishing charter crew is to see everyone so happy with their guided fishing charter that they come back again.
Captain Bec (“B”) with a Spotted Mackerel 
Captain Kaleb (“K”) with a Spanish Mackerel 
Captain Chris with a Jewfish 
Your skippers, Bec, Kaleb & Chris, June 2012 
Your boat for your trip is a fantastically safe and stable fishing platform, as used by many rescue authorities, it is a 10 meter/33 foot, Cougar Catamaran, named “Reef Cat 2”.
The fishing charter skipper will get you to the fishing grounds safely, and along with the deckhand will assist you in a fun and friendly atmosphere.
Reef Cat II is licensed to carry 12 guests, plus the skipper & deckhand. She is built to government survey regulations therefore is survey safety equipped, and fitted with the latest fishing electronics. She is a fast, stable, twin hulled, wide bodied, twin engine boat with ample seating under a large roof area, undoubtedly the perfect fishing vessel for your adventure.
Additionally, of particular note for the ladies, there is an enclosed, modern, electric flushing, highly sanitized, private toilet in the forward cabin. It is complemented with an electric, fresh running water tap with basin, liquid soap, mirror and disinfectant deodorizers.
A blast from the past, Becky the decky and her Dad (BK the original), early 2000s
