Why Choose Us For Your Fishing Charter on the Gold Coast?
Highly Experienced Crew 
Captains Bec & Kaleb each have more than 20 years fishing, whale watching, scuba diving and charter experience on the Gold Coast and beyond.
Along with their crew, they are passionate anglers with a strong focus on providing safe and quality fishing charters & whale watching tours.
All Experience Levels Welcome 
From first timers to experienced anglers, sightseers, Mum, Dad, kids, grandparents, the boys' or the girls' day out, all are extremely welcome on BK's Gold Coast Fishing Charters and gladly assisted by your skipper and deckhand in a safe, fun, friendly atmosphere.
Safe, Stable & Fast Fishing Boats 
Your boats for your adventure, Reef Cat II & Black Betty, are well maintained, safe, stable & fast fishing boats with reliable, quiet Yamaha 4 Stroke outboard engines, ample seating, quality electronics, cold ice box, live bait tank, casting deck & more.
Quality Equipment, Bait & Lures 
We supply rods & reels from quality brands like Penn, Tackle Tactics, Samaki & Okuma. 
We source quality frozen and fresh/live bait, as well as proven lures which we keep a large supply on board. Our crew will happily teach you how to use the fishing gear.
Complimentary Inclusions 
We provide cold soft drinks & water, as well as sunscreen.
Your kept fish are bled, iced & bagged for you.
Any photos/videos taken by the crew during the charter can be sent to you.
Spend More Time Fishing 
We provide MAXIMUM fishing time for you.
Our fast, stable vessels are powered by quiet, smokeless Yamaha 4 stroke outboard engines, getting you to the fishing grounds quickly, comfortably and safely.
