About
Tasman Spirit Charters is a fishing charter located in Port Arthur, Australia. With their 10m Fast Cat boat, licenced for up to 10 people, they offer an unforgettable fishing experience for both locals and tourists alike.
Whether you're a seasoned angler or a beginner, Tasman Spirit Charters has you covered. They specialise in Tuna, deep-sea, and bay fishing, providing all the necessary gear for a successful fishing trip. Just bring your enthusiasm and get ready to cast your line into the pristine waters of Port Arthur.
In addition to fishing, Tasman Spirit Charters also offers seal sightseeing and dive tours. Experience the beauty of the local marine life and witness these magnificent creatures in their natural habitat. It's an opportunity to create lifelong memories and gain a deeper appreciation for the incredible aquatic life in the area.
Tasman Spirit Charters understands that everyone has different schedules and preferences, which is why they offer both full and half-day charters. Whether you have a few hours to spare or want to spend an entire day on the water, they have the perfect option for you. Their experienced and friendly crew will ensure that your time aboard is enjoyable and catered to your needs.
For bookings and inquiries, don't hesitate to contact Brian Douglas, the knowledgeable captain of Tasman Spirit Charters, at 0408 293 201. He can provide you with all the information you need to plan your fishing adventure or seal sightseeing tour. As a passionate fisherman with extensive knowledge of the Port Arthur area, Brian guarantees an unforgettable experience for all guests.
Tasman Spirit Charters is committed to providing a safe and enjoyable fishing experience. Their boat is well-maintained, equipped with top-of-the-line safety gear, and operated by a professional crew who prioritise your safety and comfort. With Tasman Spirit Charters, you can relax and focus on reeling in your next big catch.
When searching for a fishing charter in Port Arthur, look no further than Tasman Spirit Charters. With their dedication to customer satisfaction, exceptional service, and stunning natural surroundings, they are the ultimate choice for an unforgettable fishing experience in Australia. Book your adventure today and get ready to create lasting memories on the Tasman Sea!
