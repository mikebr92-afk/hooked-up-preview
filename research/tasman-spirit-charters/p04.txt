About
Contrary to commercial fishing, which is fishing for profit or subsistence fishing, which is fishing for survival, sport fishing is a recreational form of fishing. Typically, fishing is done with a rod, reel, hooks, line and a wide range of baits. Competitive fishing is a physical sport in which teams compete against each other to catch the biggest fish in their allotted category.
