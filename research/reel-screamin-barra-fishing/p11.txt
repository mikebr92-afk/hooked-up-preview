Barra Fishing at its best 
Runoff Barra Fishing Charters
Runoff Fishing Charters run from Mid February through to Mid May. The best time to target big barra, you will be casting the coastal creeks and gutters or trolling the river mouth. Our Barra extended charters run from 3 – 7 days during this time. We head out the night before so you can relax and get a good night’s sleep.
We offer small group charters up to 4 per boat to ensure client comfort. Also, if you need tuition on fishing you will have one-on-one time with your guide in the art of fishing for barra. Whether you are a seasoned angler or just starting your fishing career by the end of the charter you will be casting a rod like a pro and would have picked up some tips along the way. 
Charters are based at Point Stuart Wilderness Lodge, conveniently located 20km away from Shady Camp Boat Ramp.
