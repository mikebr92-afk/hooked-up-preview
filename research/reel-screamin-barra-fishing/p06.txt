Mud Crab Tour
Half Day Tour 
Cook your Catch Mud Crab Tour Darwin
Join us on a half-day tour and do it like a local for a mud crab tour. Our Mud Crab experiences on Darwin Harbour and Shoal Bay all within 10 mins drive of Darwin City.
Tours include complimentary transfers from your accommodation in an accredited Tourist Vehicle with your guide. Once on the vessel, your guide will give you a safety briefing and what to expect throughout your tour. Morning/Afternoon tea and light refreshments will be available on board as you cruise the harbour to one of the many mangrove-lined creeks.
As you meander through the mangroves up a creek to your destination everyone will have the opportunity to pull a pot along the creek. We wait a bit and then it is time to pull your pots. Always an exciting time to see what you have. 
Ocean to Plate Dining Experience
Join us on a memorable tour and indulge in a delightful culinary experience. After your tour, take your prepared crabs home or savour them at the renowned Red Dragon, located on the picturesque Stokes Hill Wharf. Talented chef, Hong, will prepare your crabs with one of five delectable flavours –
• Tamarind Sweet and Sour
• Ginger and shallots
• Salt and Pepper
• Curry
• Chilli
Treat yourself to the legendary flavours of the Red Dragon and make your crab feast an unforgettable experience.
Inclusions
Discover the ultimate local pastime with Darwin Mud Crab Tours, complete with a range of exclusive inclusions.
• Benefit from convenient transfers from your accommodation,
• indulge in snacks and light refreshments including refreshing water while aboard our vessel.
• We provide all the necessary crabbing gear and bait, ensuring you have everything you need.
• Bring your own beverages and keep them chilled in our iced esky.
