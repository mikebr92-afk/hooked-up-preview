Pickup Locations

We can pick you up from any location in Sydney Harbour, but to maximise your fishing time and minimise travel, we recommend these four wharves:
Manly Wharf
Manly Wharf
Manly Wharf 3, our home port is located next to the main ferry terminal in Manly, adjacent to NSW’s ONLY cook your catch ocean to plate experience at the new waterfront popular Felons Bar, offering great sunset views and a lively atmosphere. Just head past Felons Manly, and you’ll find wharf 3 easily.
Details & Map 
• 
• 
Balmain – Thames Street Wharf
Balmain – Thames Street Wharf
Located in the heart of Balmain, this wharf is our most popular pickup point. Free parking is available on Mort Street, making it a convenient option. Plus, it’s only a 10-minute trip to prime fishing spots.
Details & Map 
Mosman Wharf
Mosman Wharf
Situated at the end of Avenue Road, Mosman, this location is perfect for those in the northern suburbs. While parking is available, it’s limited, so plan ahead.
Details & Map 
• 
• 
Casino Wharf
Casino Wharf
Located in Pyrmont Bay, opposite The Star Casino on Pirrama Road. Paid parking is available under the casino at affordable rates. This is a great central pickup point for those coming from various parts of Sydney.
Details & Map 
Alternative Pickup Locations
Alternative Pickup Locations
We can also arrange pickups from Manly Wharf, Lavender Bay, Balmoral Wharf, or other locations upon request. Keep in mind, we typically pick up from one location per trip to ensure maximum fishing time. For details or custom arrangements, contact us today!
Details & Map 
• 
• 
Rose Bay Wharf
Rose Bay Wharf
Found in Lyne Park, Rose Bay, on New South Head Road directly opposite Elanora Street. This wharf offers parking and quick access to Sydney Heads, meaning more time spent fishing and less time travelling.
