Private Fishing Charter around Whitsunday Islands
Enjoy a 6-hour private fishing charter exploring some of the Whitsundays’ best fishing locations aboard our 10.8m vessel, Stryker. Featuring quality Shimano fishing equipment and tackle, including Jewel jigging rods and Shimano SWA 8000 HG fishing reels. 
Operating from 7:00 AM to 1:00 PM. 
Pricing is $2,950 for a private charter of up to 10 passengers. 
• 
Included with Every Private Charter
• 
• Lunch – Yummy Wraps
• Bottled water & Ice ( BYO Alcohol in cans only please)
• All high quality equipment (Shimano rods, reels and line)
• Bait provided
• All fish filleted and packed ready for you to take home or to a local restaurant to cook for you
• 
What to Bring
• 
Hat, sunglasses, camera, snacks, and any alcohol you would like to bring (cans only). During winter, we recommend bringing a jacket or jumper.
• 
Meeting Point
• 
Meet at Coral Sea Marina, North Village, outside the front of Sorrento Restaurant. Parking is available next to the meeting point.
