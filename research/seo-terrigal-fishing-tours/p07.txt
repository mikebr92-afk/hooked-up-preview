Lunch Cruises / Corporate Charters
Group & Corporate Charters
The Central Coast is a perfect location for Group and Corporate Fishing Charters being only 60 minutes north of Sydney. We can provide you with a range of fishing experiences and sightseeing cruises. Catch up with friends or workmates on our beautiful waterways. Enjoy some quality family time or do some team building. The day can be customised to suit your needs.
We can accommodate 14 guests and also have the option of using our sport rib to take the numbers to 24.
Its BYO drinks. We can provide a BBQ lunch, gourmet sandwich platters, seafood platters or you can bring your own snacks.
We operate 7 days a week & can build the trip to suit your needs. 
Lunch cruises are for Private Charters only. 
A Day on the Bay
Explore the stunning scenery of Broken Bay in safety and style on your private cruise. Enjoy the dramatic scenery, sandy beaches, and pristine National Parks.
A 5-hour tour which includes fishing, watching for dolphins & seals, swimming, stand-up paddle boarding and a lunch stop. Learn about the area’s rich history and marine life from our trained crew.
A 5-hour charter departing from Hardys Bay into Broken Bay.
Enjoy a scenic cruise then we can anchor up in an exclusive bay and enjoy fishing, paddleboarding, swimming and a BBQ Lunch.
We are fully licensed and equipped for charter fishing so if there are any keen anglers on board, we can stop for a fishing session either in the bay or offshore.
We can depart at a time that suits you between 9 am to 12 midday.
BBQ lunch featuring chicken skewers, Pork sausages, coleslaw, potato salad and condiments.
Fruit Platter, mineral water.
BYO Alcohol.
Platter of Prawns Upgrade (Market price on request)
A Day on the Bay is for Private Charters only.
Max 14 Pax. Larger groups please enquire.
