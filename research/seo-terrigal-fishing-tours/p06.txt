Hardys Bay Charters
We provide a range of charter tours departing from the delightful seaside village of Hardys Bay on the NSW Central Coast only a short drive from Sydney.

Book Private Fishing Charter 
Family Fishing Charters
What a perfect birthday or holiday activity for kids. A kid friendly fishing charter. Give your kids a real fishing charter experience in a safe and educational environment. Enjoy some fishing action with your friends. Learn about sustainable fishing and the marine environment. 
We depart from the delightful seaside village of Hardys Bay. This gives us access to a range of habitats including estuaries, bays and the offshore reefs and wrecks of the Bouddi Peninsula and their bountiful fish. Depending on the confidence of your crew, we can head offshore, or we can stay within the bay for some calm water fishing around the headlands and estuaries. With plenty of options for a great day of fishing.
Duration 5 Hours.
We supply quality rods, reels and bait.

Broken Bay Wildlife Cruise
Explore Broken Bay in safety and style, aboard your chartered adventure tour vessel. Cruise along the dramatic sandstone coastline of Broken Bay, a mix of steep cliffs, sandy beaches and pristine National Parks. V isit a Seal Colony and enjoy their interactions as they hunt fish, swim and rest on the rocky foreshore. Visit a colony of endangered Little Penguins who are occasionally sighted fishing in the bay. Dolphins are regular visitors to the bay and often engage with us when they are in a playful mood. 
Party Cruises
Explore the beautiful Broken Bay in safety and style on your private cruise. Enjoy the stunning scenery, pristine National Parks, beaches, wildlife and history. A 5-hour lunch charter departing from Hardys Bay into Broken Bay.
This cruise is perfect for families, friends, international visitors or a team building day.
We depart from the delightful seaside village of Hardys Bay and head out to enjoy the stunning scenery of Broken Bay and the Hawkesbury River. The bay is surrounded by pristine National Parks with rugged cliffs and remote sandy beaches. We normally see marine wildlife which can include dolphins, seals and eagles. There is also a colony of Fairy Penguins in the bay and occasionally we spot them out fishing. The bay also hides some intriguing war history of mini submarines and Z Force Commandos. Following a scenic cruise we can anchor up at an exclusive beach and enjoy fishing and a BBQ Lunch.
We are fully licensed and equipped for charter fishing so if there are any keen anglers on board, we can stop for a fishing session either in the bay or offshore.
A Day on the Bay is for Private Charters only.
We can depart at a time that suits you between 9 am to 12 midday.
