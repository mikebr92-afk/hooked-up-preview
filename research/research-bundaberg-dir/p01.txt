About
River For those who prefer a calmer fishing experience, our half day river fishing charter is the perfect option. Explore the beautiful Burnett River and its estuaries while targeting species such as Bream, Flathead, and Mangrove Jack. With fishing grounds just a short distance from the Burnett Heads Boat Ramp, you'll spend more time fishing and less time travelling.
The half day river fishing charter runs for 5 hours and includes all fishing equipment, bait, and tackle. Water and soft drinks are also provided, but feel free to bring your own beverages and snacks. The charter operates at two different time slots: 5:30 am to 10:30 am and 2:00 pm to 7:00 pm, allowing you to choose the time that suits you best.
Pricing for the half day river fishing charter is $220 per person, making it an affordable option for individuals, families, or groups. Immerse yourself in the tranquilly of the Burnett River while reeling in some fantastic catches.
Truansea Charters is owned and operated by Luke, an experienced and passionate fisherman. Luke's love for fishing and the ocean is infectious, and he is committed to providing a memorable and enjoyable experience for all his guests. With his wealth of knowledge and expertise, you can trust that you'll be in good hands during your fishing adventure.
In addition to fishing charters, Truansea Charters also offers surfing charters and bespoke maritime training packages. Whether you're a beginner looking to learn something new or a seasoned adventurer seeking a thrilling experience, Luke can tailor the perfect package for you.
To book your fishing charter, surfing charter, or maritime training package, simply contact Luke on 0423 015 490. He will be more than happy to answer any questions you may have or assist you with your booking. Truansea Charters is based out of Burnett Heads Boat Ramp in Queensland, Australia, providing easy access to some of the best fishing spots in the region.
Embark on an unforgettable fishing experience with Truansea Charters. Get ready to reel in your dream catch while enjoying the stunning beauty of the Southern Great Barrier Reef. Whether you're a novice angler or a seasoned pro, Truansea Charters has something to offer everyone. Book your adventure today and create memories that will last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I contact Truansea Charters to ask questions or book? 
You can call or text Luke on 0423 015 490 for any questions or to book your fishing charters or maritime training package.
What fishing charter options are available? 
We offer Full Day Reef Fishing, Half Day Reef Fishing, Half Day River Fishing, plus Surfing and Freediving options.
Where do I book from and where are you located? 
You can book fishing charters from Bundaberg or the Town of 1770, with boating from the Burnett Heads Boat Ramp in Queensland.
