NDIS SERVICES
Day Trippin Charters is a registered NDIS provider, offering inclusive, tailored fishing experiences for NDIS participants. Our goal is to create fun, accessible, and engaging outdoor activities that are designed to meet individual needs and personal goals'
Services We Offer: 
• 16-Week Fishing Program: Suitable for all skill levels, our program includes hands-on fishing experiences, skill development, and exploration of the stunning Newcastle and Lake Macquarie areas.
Check out our Program here! - Program Outline 
• Group and 1:1 Support Options: Whether participants enjoy socialising in groups or prefer personalised support, we offer flexible options to ensure everyone can enjoy a great time on the water.
• Skill Development & Social Connection: Our programs promote fishing skill development, confidence building, physical activity, and meaningful social connections in a supportive environment.
Funding Options: 
Day Trippin Charters services are available to participants with a variety of NDIS funding arrangements:
• Self-Managed: Participants manage their own NDIS funding and can pay directly for services.
• Plan-Managed: For participants who have a plan manager handling their NDIS funds, we can invoice the plan manager directly.
• NDIA/Agency-Managed: As a registered NDIS provider, we can also work with agency-managed participants, with services billed directly through the NDIS portal.
At Day Trippin Charters, we’re committed to making outdoor activities enjoyable and accessible for everyone. Contact us today to learn more and secure your spot in our next program!
