About
Mackay Fly & Sportfishing Charters, located in Mirani, offers a unique and thrilling experience for fishing enthusiasts. As the only guided sportfishing operation of its kind in the Mackay Region, MFSF specialises in fly and lure fishing. Led by the experienced angler, Paul Dolan, the charter aims to provide clients with the opportunity to connect with good fish and have an enjoyable day on the water.
With a strong focus on environmental awareness, Mackay Fly & Sportfishing is committed to the preservation and conservation of the creatures and fish that inhabit these waters. The charter strictly uses artificial hardbody lures, plastics, or flies and follows a catch and release policy on certain species, emulating famous sustainable sports fisheries around the world. Paul's intimate knowledge of the intricate ecosystem in which the charter operates enables him to consider all aspects, such as time, tides, winds, currents, and moons, to ensure successful fishing trips.
Mackay Fly & Sportfishing offers personalised full-day fishing adventures in both saltwater and freshwater, catering to both fly fishermen and conventional lure anglers. The charter provides opportunities to target a variety of great sportfish species, including Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, and many other inshore species. Clients can opt for tailored fishing packages that suit their preferences and skill levels.
The Seaforth area, where the charter operates, has recently been designated as a net-free zone by the Queensland Government. This designation is expected to boost the region's fishery, making it an ideal destination for anglers looking to land that fish of a lifetime. Mackay Fly & Sportfishing is at the forefront of this exciting development and guarantees unforgettable fishing experiences.
Whether you are an experienced angler or a novice seeking a thrilling adventure, Mackay Fly & Sportfishing Charters is ready to provide you with a memorable experience. Paul Dolan, with his years of experience and passion for fly-fishing, ensures that each client receives the best guidance and support throughout their fishing journey. If you have any questions or require further information, the team at Mackay Fly & Sportfishing is always available to assist. Contact them via phone or email to plan your fishing charter and make the most of your vacation time in the Mackay Region.
