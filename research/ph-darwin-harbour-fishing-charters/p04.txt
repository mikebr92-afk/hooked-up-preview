BARRAMUNDI FISHING CHARTER DARWIN
AT CORROBOREE BILLABONG or SHADY CAMP 
A Full Day Experience chasing the Top End Barra
If you want to experience a great day Barra fishing while visiting Darwin, Corroboree Billabong or Shady Camp is truly a wonderful place and consistently delivers great Barramundi fishing!
Mixed in with your days of chasing the Top End Barra you can see Lotus Lilies pink flowers in bloom in its full beauty. On this Pandanus lined billabong you can also expect to see a wide variety of Water birds, Sea Eagles, Crocodiles, Water Buffalo and Wild Pigs.
Our experienced Guides will ensure that your Top End fishing experience is a memorable one. And even if you are a rookie in fishing, we will be facilitating you with necessary tuition to become a pro.
What's Included
• Transfers in an air- conditioned four-wheel drive
• All fishing gear (Top Quality Rods Reels and Tackle) supplied on a replace if lost or damaged basis
• Snacks and Lunch. Please let us know if there are any special dietary requirements so we can cater for
• Tuition on Catching Fish if needed
What To Bring
• Wide brimmed hat
• Light cool clothing – long sleeves and pants are recommended
• Polarized sunglasses
• 30+ Sunscreen and Insect repellent
• Camera
• Bottle of Water that you can refill on board – You can bring your own drinks CANS ONLY – NO Glass
Half Day Barra Fishing Charter
$420 per person
• 4:30am – 12:00pm
• Dry Season 10:30am-8pm Max. 6 pax
Charter Itinerary
• You will be picked up from your accommodation at 4:30am or 10:30am
• One and a half hour drive to Corroboree.
• Snacks are provided.
• 20 minutes drive to the Corroboree Billabong which is part of the Mary River.
• Around sunrise begins your Great Fishing Adventure.
• Our boat offers a safe and comfortable platform for your day fishing.
• Fully qualified and experienced Guide that will provide expert tuition as required.
• Returning to your accommodation after 12:30pm or 8pm.
