Darwin Harbour Fishing Charters Experience Of all the great places in Australia to catch fish, few match the number and diversity of Darwin Harbour. Located on the north western tip of the continent, Darwin Harbour is home to over four hundred species of fish and the rare dugong, a marine mammal that is similar to a manatee. Many who visit the area will take advantage of the fishing charters Darwin has to offer in order to see more of this remarkable area and get in some great fishing.
