East Gippsland Sportfishing 
East Gippsland Sportfishing offers personalised guided fishing tours that operates in East Gippsland’s estuary systems of Mallacoota, Bemm River and Lake Tyers. They cater for all levels of experience by providing a range of tour options offering lure fishing using soft plastics, hard body lures complimented with quality fishing tackle. Venture Out is based on the beautiful Gippsland Lakes in Lakes Entrance and specialises in riding and paddling experiences. Take part in the tours or just hire the gear for a self-guided adventure. Sail Safari has been operating Sailing Charters on the Gippsland Lakes in Victoria for the past 20 years, offering a variety of experiences. From a full ‘learning to sail’ adventure to a more relaxed canal cruise around the Paynesville estate. Peels Cruises 
Peels Cruises have three cruise boats – the Stormbird, the Seabird and the Thunderbird. All three boats have undercover seating, and toilet facilities, the Seabird and Thunderbird also have outdoor seating and view decks. and a kiosk/bar on board. Lonsdale Cruises 
Lonsdale Cruises are located at Cunningham Quay in Lakes Entrance and offer a totally different cruising experience. Cruises run for three hours, travelling over 28 kilometres. Lakes Explorer : Wildlife Tours or Water-Taxi 
Lakes Explorer offers a water taxi service (up to four passengers) to visit remote lake-side jetties and beaches near Lakes Entrance. Fees are based on distance, number of passengers or time. Destinations include Lakes Entrance jetties, Barrier Landing, Flagstaff Jetty-Ocean Entrance, Rigby Island, Kalimna, Nyerimilang Jetty, Fraser Island Retreat (guests only).
