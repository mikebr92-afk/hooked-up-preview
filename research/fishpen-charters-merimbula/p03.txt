Kayak and SUP hire at Mitchies Jetty
Looking to hire a Stand up paddle board or kayak? 
you’ve come to the right place. 
At Mitchies Jetty we have Stand Up Paddle (SUP) boards and both single and double Sit on Top Kayaks for hire. 
Come down and explore the beautiful Merimbula Lake and all it has to offer. 
Check out the amazing aquatic life both in and on our lake. Abundant fish, massive stingrays, dolphins and seals as well as numerous varieties of sea birds all call Merimbula lake their home. 
If you have never paddled before don't worry, we will give you free instruction and have you up and enjoying yourself in no time.
Prices start at $30.00 per hour.
