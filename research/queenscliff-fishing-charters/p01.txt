IMPORTANT INFORMATION
We provide all gear including rods, reels, tackle and fresh bait. We also provide on boat assistance from our experienced and friendly Skipper and Deckhand. Safety equipment is on board and we provide a full safety briefing before departure.
For Fishing Trips please dress appropriately for fishing, wear loose clothing, covered footwear, hat, sunnies, sunscreen, lip balm for windier days. Bring along your esky to keep your food (no bananas please as they are considered bad luck on our boat) and drinks cool and to take home your catch. A Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence is required.
