Fishing Clinics & Camps 
Bay Fishing Clinic Information
We can accommodate your group for a 3 Hour Trip right up to a 5 Day Fishing Camp. Your trip can be tailored to your area of study. On board tuition by experienced mature Skipper and Deckhand including advice and demonstrations on how to bait hooks, hook a fish and wind in and fillet fish. The use of Rods, Reels, Tackle and Fresh Bait is included. All fish are cleaned and filleted onboard or released – Sustainable fishing is promoted.
Land Based Fishing Clinic Information
Our team is also available for land based Fishing tuition. This may include visits across the Bellarine to conduct Pier, Lake, Harbour or Estuary Fishing, Live Bait collection including sand pumping for Bass Yabbies and casting demonstrations and tuition.
Recommended Groups
Suitable for Schools/Groups/Clubs and non-profit organizations including those with additional needs from 12 Years of age, we accommodate a maximum number of 15 people, including Teachers/Carers.
Important Information
Over 18’s and Under 65’s require a Victorian Fishing License (pensioner’s exempt). We have full comprehensive public liability insurance and a safety and risk assessment plan is available on request. A safety briefing is conducted onboard before departure.
What to Bring
Dress for all weather conditions in appropriate Fishing clothing and footwear. Please ensure you bring sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses as well as your food and drinks.
Cost
Please contact us for a quote. We understand non profit organization and our prices are kept low to support these groups.
