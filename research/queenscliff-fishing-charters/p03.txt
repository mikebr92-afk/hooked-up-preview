About Us 
QUEENSCLIFF FISHING CHARTERS
At Queenscliff Fishing Charters and Scenic Tours we offer Bay Fishing Charters from Queenscliff. We also offer Fishing Clinics from Queenscliff, and a range of educational and Scenic Tours. Queenscliff Fishing Charters also operate Offshore Sportsfishing Charters from BOTH Queenscliff and Sorrento. 
We are a locally owned and operated family business. We pride ourselves in offering a personalized, “family friendly” and educational experience. In a safe and fun environment, at an affordable price.
Located 90 minutes from Melbourne and Ballarat, 45 minutes from Torquay and only 20 minutes from Ocean Grove and Geelong. Our Fishing charters leave from the newly developed Queenscliff Habour next to the Queenscliff Sorrento Ferry or Sorrento Pier for Sorrento departures. Our trips run all year, weather permitting.
Our friendly skipper and deckhand will welcome you aboard; they are extremely patient and experienced. Most importantly they have local knowledge of the waters of Queenscliff and surrounds and know all the best spots.
We are fully licensed and have Working With Children check and First Aid training.
