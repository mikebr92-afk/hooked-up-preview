Ashes Scattering 
Ashes scatterings are becoming increasingly common for those wishing to rest their loved ones out at sea. This is a private trip offering an intimate setting for up to 20 people inside Port Phillip Bay. We are able to offer a personalised trip catering for any cultural or ceremonial requirements. Eg. Some may have a specific site they wish to visit. Our crew provides plenty of privacy for you to have with your friends and loved ones.
Please contact us to discuss your requirements and a quote for your group size.
Prices start at $700 for 10 people up to 2 hours.
