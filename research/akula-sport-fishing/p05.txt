Explosive Reef Popper Action on the Great Barrier Reef
Giant trevally fishing is where legends are made, defined by raw power, violent strikes, and unforgettable surface explosions. Akula Sportfishing offers an elite reef popper fishing experience, targeting one of the ocean’s most aggressive predators: the giant trevally.
Departing from Port Douglas, we head to remote reef structures where GTs patrol the surface, crushing bait with unbelievable power. From explosive surface strikes to unstoppable runs, fishing GT is not for the faint-hearted, but it remains one of the most thrilling and rewarding fishing experiences in Australia.

Frequently Asked Questions
What Size Popper for GT? 
Use poppers between 130g and 230g for giant trevally, depending on their size and the conditions. 
What Is the Best Bait for Giant Trevally? 
Large soft plastics, stickbaits and poppers are most effective. 
What Size Reel for GT Popping? 
A heavy-duty spin reel minimum 10000 series with a strong drag system is ideal for GT popping. 
Is Giant Trevally Hard to Catch? 
Yes, GTs are strong and aggressive predators, making them challenging but rewarding to catch. 
What Lures Do Trevally Like? 
Trevally respond well to poppers, stickbaits, metal jigs, and large soft plastics that mimic fleeing prey.
