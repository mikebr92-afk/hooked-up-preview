ABOUT AKULA SPORTFISHING
Built on Experience. Driven by Adventure. Made for the Reef.
We believe every trip should be more than just fishing. It should be a true offshore adventure, shaped by local expertise, high-quality service, and a genuine passion for the ocean.
We are the most experienced on-water professionals, based in Port Douglas, with several decades in this business, which has made us the premier professional fishing charters in the region.
You may be holding a rod for the first time, or you are an experienced angler pursuing the reef powerhouses; we are here to ensure that your day is an experience worth remembering.
Ready to fish with the locals who know these waters best?

A LITTLE ABOUT US
Local Crew. Local Waters. Real Results.
Akula Sportfishing is proud to be based in Far North Queensland. Our 30+ years of experience in local waters have earned us a reputation for safe, exciting, and action-packed trips to suit each guest’s needs.
Our crew is aware that each charter is unique, just like every guest. This is the reason why we concentrate on giving you a seamless service from the time you enquire till the time you come onshore with the memories and photos, you will wish you can go through again and again.
• Great for families, mates trips & couples
• Beginner-friendly guidance
• Serious angler-ready fishing grounds

A Lifetime on the Water
I am the owner and operator of Akula Sportfishing, and I have devoted over 30 years of my life to establishing my business around boats, the ocean, and the thrill of the hunt. I started fishing out of Mooloolaba on the Sunshine Coast of Queensland, and then years of experience eventually led me to the north, where the Great Barrier Reef provides some of the most thrilling marine fishing ever.
That experience and passion drive every one of our trips.
Reef structure, tides, current motions, and seasonal variations, we are always considerate about every factor that should be the best for fishing conditions.
Looking to fish with a crew that truly lives and breathes the reef?

OUR CHARTER BOAT
Meet AKULA: Built for the Reef
Take a ride aboard AKULA, our high-performance charter boat designed to handle reef conditions with speed, stability, and comfort.
We operate a 28-foot alloy catamaran powered by twin 250hp outboard engines, offering a smooth offshore ride, generous deck space, and the comfort you expect on a serious fishing vessel.
• Full-length sun shade for comfort and protection
• High-quality safety gear and equipment
• Built for reef fishing, light tackle & topwater action
If you want a premium fishing day without feeling cramped, rushed, or overlooked, this is the boat for you.
Follow us for the latest fishing news and book your next trip today! 
As the owner of Akula Sportfishing, I have over 30 years of professional marine experience.
