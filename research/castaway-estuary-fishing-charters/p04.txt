The Vessel
Castaway
Castaway is a custom-built, plate aluminium Bar Crusher 535XS centre console Fishing Weapon! Powered by a Yamaha 90hp outboard, the boat is also fitted with a bow-mount Minn Kota Terrova electric motor for sneaking around over shallow sandflats, working along deep river banks, and holding position near likely-looking structure. 
Castaway is purpose-built for lure fishing, featuring an open layout with a forward casting deck and ample casting space. Castaway also features Bar Crusher's innovative water ballast system, QuickFlow , for excellent stability at rest. Castaway is fitted with a Garmin GPS Map 1653XSV 16-inch display mounted on the console and an additional Garmin GPS Map 1253XSV 12-inch display mounted on the casting deck. 
Castaway is a safe and comfortable estuary lure fishing platform with the latest electronics to get you on the fish. 
Stay in touch with Castaway!
