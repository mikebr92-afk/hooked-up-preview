Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters
FAQ
Q: WHAT SORT OF FISHING WILL WE BE DOING?
A: Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters specialises in light/medium tackle estuary lure fishing. We use a variety of lure types over the course of the day including soft plastics, vibes and hardbody lures. Prior lure fishing experience is highly beneficial, but not essential. 
Q: WHAT DO I NEED TO BRING ON CASTAWAY?
A: All fishing tackle is supplied on Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters and we also provide bottled water. Please remember to bring adequate sun protection ( hat, sunglasses and long sleeved clothing is advised) and a rain jacket . 
Q: HOW MANY PEOPLE CAN YOU TAKE?
A: Castaway is licensed to carry 3 people (two anglers + skipper/guide). 
Q: IS THERE AN AGE LIMIT?
A: The minimum age for kids on Castaway is 12 years old (must be accompanied by an adult). 
Q: CAN I KEEP MY CATCH?
A: Anglers are welcome to keep their catch. Please remember to bring a bucket or cooler to transport your catch. Castaway is required by law to comply with NSW DPI – Fisheries size and bag limits. Occupational Health & Safety policy also prohibits the guide/ Castaway to process (clean, fillet etc) fish caught on charter. 
Q: HOW DO I PAY?
A: Castaway accepts payment via bank transfer, EFTPOS or cash. A 50% deposit is required 7 days before the charter date. 
Q: WHAT ABOUT BAD WEATHER?
A: The guide will monitor weather and river conditions before your charter. If adverse weather is forecast the charter may need to be cancelled (full refund or charter rescheduling is offered). 
Q: WHAT TIME DO WE START AND WHERE DO WE MEET?
A: Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters depart from 6am (spring and summer) to 7am (autumn and winter) unless otherwise advised by the guide. Please meet at McInherney Park boat ramp , accessed via River Park Road.
