Castaway Guided Estuary Sportfishing | Mid North Coast, NSW
The objective: No average days on the water! 
Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters specialises in private guided lure sportfishing experiences across the renowned estuary systems of the NSW Mid North Coast. 
Every charter is tailored to prevailing conditions, seasonal fish behaviour and your individual goals, whether you’re chasing a personal best, learning modern lure techniques or simply experiencing some of Australia’s finest estuary fishing. 
Private Charters | 1–3 Anglers | Premium Tackle | All Experience Levels Welcome 
Enquire now to secure your preferred date. 
The Castaway Difference 
Every Castaway trip is built around one objective—helping you become a better angler while putting you in the best possible position to catch quality fish on lures. 
Your guide, Patrick Linehan , has spent more than two decades refining lure fishing techniques throughout the Hastings, Macleay and Manning river systems. Every decision—from the locations fished and tides targeted to lure selection and presentation—is based on experience gained through thousands of hours on the water. 
Whether you’re an experienced lure angler or picking up a rod for the first time, you’ll receive personalised coaching throughout the day, leaving with new skills and greater confidence as an angler. 
Trophy Estuary Fishing 
The NSW Mid North Coast is home to some of Australia’s finest estuary sportfishing, producing exceptional numbers of trophy fish each season. 
Castaway specialises in targeting: 
Trophy Dusky Flathead 
The region has earned a reputation for producing monster flathead , with fish exceeding one metre landed. Learn how to locate, present lures to and safely handle these incredible predators while exploring expansive sandflats, drop-offs and productive structure. 
Mulloway 
Few estuary fish command more respect than the 'silver ghost'. From subtle bites to blistering runs, targeting these powerful fish on artificial lures is one of Australian fishing’s greatest challenges—and one of its greatest rewards. 
Australian bass 
Fishing for Australian bass offers not just a thrilling battle but also a chance to connect with nature. Luckily for us, the Mid North Coast is home to some of the best, scenic wild bass fishing in Australia! 
Depending on the season, you’ll also encounter yellowfin bream, whiting, tailor, trevally and more. 
Fish Proven Estuary Systems 
Every charter is planned around where the fishing is at its best. 
You’ll fish one of three exceptional waterways: 
Hastings River – Port Macquarie 
Macleay River – South West Rocks 
Manning River – Harrington 
No two trips are the same. Conditions, tides and seasonal movements determine where you’ll have the greatest opportunity for success. 
Purpose-Built for Serious Fishing 
Your day is spent aboard Castaway, a Bar Crusher 535XS , widely regarded as one of Australia's finest plate aluminium fishing boats. 
Equipped with premium Garmin electronics, Minn Kota electric trolling motor and top-tier Shimano tackle, it’s designed specifically for lure fishing, allowing quiet, precise boat positioning and maximum fishing time.
