Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters | Guided Lure Fishing Tours
Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters is a locally owned fishing charter business based in Port Macquarie, owned and operated by experienced angler Patrick Linehan. Founded in 2006, Castaway offers guided calm water estuary lure fishing tours on the Hastings River, Manning River and Macleay River. 
Castaway specialises in private charters, offering half-day, full-day and and multi-day fishing trips for 1-3 anglers. Castaway is dedicated to delivering memorable and productive fishing adventures and each charter is tailored to daily conditions — targeting dusky flathead, mulloway, bream, whiting, and Australian bass on lures. 
All charters include premium-quality rods, reels, lures, and personalised lure fishing tuition, catering to both beginners and experienced anglers.
