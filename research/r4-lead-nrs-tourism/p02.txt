Simon FITZPATRICK - Northern Rivers Sportfishing
Fishing Guide / Captain / Charter in Tweed Heads
Join me on a bass fishing charter in the beautiful Tweed Valley in Northern NSW. Your courtesy pick up from your accommodation will take you to the most pristine waterways where the bass are fat and healthy. “I will accompany you throughout the day, sharing all the secrets on catching Australian Bass.” - Simon Fitzpatrick Bass Fishing Guide.
