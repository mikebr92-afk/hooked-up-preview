Our Story
Northern Rivers Sportfishing
Explore the beautiful wetlands of the Northern Rivers in search of one of Australia's favorite sportfish, the Australian Bass. Your guide will provide you with all the secrets and local knowledge to catch your fish. Everything you need for the days adventure is supplied; boat/kayak, tackle & snacks.
