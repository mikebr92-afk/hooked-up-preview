We are a locally owned and operated charter business at Dundee Beach with the best reputation for delivering an exciting and informative fishing experience. Our passion for adventure and fishing is infectious. You’ll be fishing with people who are just as excited about your trip as you are! We believe a fishing trip should be about catching lots of what you want to catch and having a great time with lots of laughs while you’re at it. Our professional guides, know where and how to find the fish. They fish most days of the week even when not on charter. 
There are so many options out of Dundee that we tailor the trip to suit the best tides and sea conditions, hot bites or your personal preferences that can range from chasing barra to pelagics and sail fish, reef fishing for goldies, trout, and emperor or bottom bouncing for our legendary huge black jewfish. We cater for hard core fisho’s and new comers looking for a once in a lifetime experience and we are more than happy to share our knowledge and skills to help you become a better angler.
On the bluewater, offshore conditions are generally the calmest of the year between October and December, so it's often possible to travel long distances, targeting the Top End's best sport and reef fish species. Spanish mackerel are still active at this time of year and reef fish such as golden snapper and jewfish congregate in large numbers on the inshore reefs around Dundee Beach and Fog Bay. The build-up is often the best time to enjoy long day’s reef fishing out of Dundee.
Our one day charters include:
· Lunch, snacks and non-alcoholic drinks
· Expert guiding from our experienced and professional fishing guides
· A maximum of six clients plus guide fishing from 7m sport fishing boats 
· Quality rods, reels and tackle are all supplied
Our charters depart Dundee boat ramp at 7am and return by 4pm. The price for fishing charters is $440 per person 
Open and Closed dates: November - January ( Weather dependant )

General Terms and Conditions for Dundee Beach Fishing Charters
Deposits
A deposit is required within 7 days of making the initial booking. The deposit is required in order to secure the trip date. If a deposit isn't taken within 7 days then the trip dates go back on the market. The minimum deposit required is 20% deposit of all fares. Final Payment is due 30 days prior to commencement of charter. Full payment is required at time of booking for all bookings made inside 30 days of charter.
Cancellations
Cancellations must be made in writing and can be via e-mail (info@dundeebeachfishingcharters.com.au) or by letter. No cancellation will be accepted unless received in writing.
All charter bookings cancelled outside 60 days incur 2% charge of booking cost.
Bookings cancelled between 60- 30 days incur 50% charge of booking cost.
Bookings cancelled inside 30 days incur 100% charge of booking cost.
Bookings rescheduled to a later date do not incur charges.
Not included
Prices DO NOT include airfares (unless specified) airport taxes any costs incurred en route to and from the destination meals (unless specified) alcoholic beverages telephone calls items of a personal nature and personal services not specified as being included in costs with information on this website.
Travel Insurance
We strongly suggest that you take out travel insurance. It is important that this insurance covers you for involuntary cancellation of your holiday including airfare(s).
Events Beyond Our Control
Dundee Beach Fishing Charters does not accept any liability whatsoever for any injury damage loss delay additional expenses or inconvenience caused directly or indirectly by any events which are beyond our control including but not limited to war civil disturbance fire floods acts of God acts of government or of any other authorities accident to or failure of machinery or equipment or industrial action.
Weather
All charters are subject to weather conditions and we reserve the right to cancel any trip due to the bad weather (even at short notice). You may nominate a full refund or choose an alternative day if your trip has been cancelled due to the weather.
