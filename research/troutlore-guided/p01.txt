Guided Fly Fishing
It is with great pleasure that we are now offering guided fly fishing for trout and carp, in and around the Goulburn Valley. Guided fly fishing days are now available to book with Rob Gee from the Troutlore fly tying store.
Let me work with you to improve your fly fishing skills, whilst we enjoy a great day on the water. Day trips are available for one or two anglers, although a second highly experienced guide can be arranged for larger groups.
Simply click any of the above images to go through to the Guided Fly Fishing packages page and select the service you'd like to book!
Whether you're just new to fly fishing, or a seasoned veteran, I am confident we can help you improve on your fly fishing journey, and have a lot of fun in the process.
For those of you who haven't considered fly fishing for carp before, cast aside any previously considered aspersions, and get ready for some of the most engaging, and ultimately enjoyable fly fishing you'll like ever have encountered. This is sight fishing at it's best, with a target species that is not easily fooled, and one that puts up a fight that outperforms most freshwater species.
A current Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence is required for participation in a day's guided fly fishing (or a valid exemption). You can purchase your fishing licence online from Services Victoria in advance. 
If you have any question about our guide service, especially if you're wanting to check available dates, please email me at rob@troutlore.com.au or call on 0418 304 414 as I am always happy to help.
Go on, treat yourself to a day on the water!
