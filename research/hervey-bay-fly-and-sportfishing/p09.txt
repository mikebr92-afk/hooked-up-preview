THE FISHING
Hervey Bay is the gate way to Fraser Island and the Great Sandy Straits Marine Park , and is situated 300km north of Brisbane. Hervey Bay and the Fraser Coast boast’s some of the best sport and fly fishing to be had on the east coast of Queensland.
FISH SPECIES
Hervey Bay & Great Sandy Straits
This unique fishery is one of very few places that has an overlap of southern and northern species.
Just a few of the species we could encounter include…
Longtail Tuna
Mackerel
Queenfish
Cobia
Golden Trevally
Black Marlin
Snapper
Threadfin Salmon
Barramundi
Flathead
along with many more trevally, estuary and reef species!
Spending some time on our waters you will quickly see how diverse and special this fishery is right on South East Queensland’s doorstep.
Hervey Bay Fly and Sportfishing caters for all levels of experience. From first timers, right through to the most seasoned angler. Our experienced guides will offer any tuition required to help your day on the water be more successful and one you won’t forget.
Fraser Island to the East protects the Bay from the south easterly trade winds and with the maze of creeks in the Sandy Straits the Hervey Bay area has plenty of options for all weather conditions. Come out an enjoy a day with Hervey Bay Fly and Sportfishing Charters and let us show you this unique fishery.
