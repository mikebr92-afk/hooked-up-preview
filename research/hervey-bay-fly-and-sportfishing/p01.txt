Welcome to Hervey Bay Fly and Sportfishing
Our Fly and Sportfishing Charters operate in one of the most unique fisheries on the East coast of Australia. The sheltered waters of Hervey Bay and Fraser Island are one of the very few places in Australia that have an overlap of southern and northern fish species.
You can follow all our awesome fishing adventures in Hervey Bay and beyond on our YouTube channel and our popular Instagram account .
OUR FISHING GUIDES 
So, what can we catch?
See below for just a few of the fish that can be caught when fishing the sheltered waters off Fraser Island with the Fraser Coast’s most experienced guides!
Longtail tuna
Mackerel
Queenfish
Cobia
Golden Trevally
Black Marlin
Snapper
Threadfin Salmon
Flathead
Barramundi
along with many more Trevally, Estuary and Reef species!
Charter Packages
We have a wide range of fishing charter options, single day and half day charters to week long sportfishing packages for all levels of experience. We also offer private mothershipping onboard our 58ft Catamaran which gives anglers a unique experience.
Come and experience this world class fishery with Hervey Bay’s most experienced and passionate local guides.
