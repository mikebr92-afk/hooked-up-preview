Australian Wild Tours are unlike any tour you will ever experience.
Australian Wild Tours are an exclusive operation which have been hand crafted by survivalist and wildlife Expert Andrew Ucles. 
The tours are designed to challenge and encourage individuals to connect with their wild side.
An adventure that blends both the outback spirit and the ancient landscape with a hint of adrenaline and excitement, you’ll undertake a journey unlike any other.

Tours available
Outback Camp Day Tour 
Join Australia’s wildest experience, hand crafted by globally renown wildlife ambassador Andrew Ucles, this tour is exclusive, personalised and unlike anything else in the Territory.
Become part of an adventure and embrace an authentic Australian outback journey. Immerse yourself into a wildlife wonderland situated on the floodplain labyrinth of the Finniss River, an epic piece of the Top End.
This tour is hands on and shoelaces tight, your survival expert Andrew Ucles teaches you how to survive in the Australian Outback, from close encounters with crocodiles to catching wild boars on the floodplains, this is a tour unlike any other. 
Show more 
Outback Camp Overnight Tour 
You haven’t experienced the Territory until you’ve slept under the stars amongst a symphony of nature’s calls in our remote and secluded piece of paradise set upon an escarpment ridgeline. A once in a lifetime adventure with Australia’s most entertaining survival and wildlife expert Andrew Ucles.
This tour has everything to offer from crocodile interactions, wildlife encounters and guided walks on the open wetlands – after a day of adventure, relax and unwind at the Outback Camp and embrace the ambience of a campfire with a chef prepared meal whilst sharing stories under the expansive night sky. 
Show more 
3 Day Outback Tour 
3 Day Exclusive Tours uniquely tailored by wildlife and survival expert, Andrew Ucles. An authentic and immersive journey for a maximum of four guests only. Discover, explore and ignite your fire of adventure at the Finniss River Station, one of the most incredible and diverse natural environments in the top end of Australia.
For those who have followed Andrew’s escapades know that his character is unlike any other and you will be surely entertained by the stories of his adventures through the Amazon, the Serengeti and Arnhem Land.
Andrew’s insatiable and spirited passion for wildlife, nature and survival delivered in a colourful and comedic context is what makes this tour experience unlike any other. 
Show more 
Fishing Charters 
Australian Wild Tours offers a daily fishing charter experience targeting some of most iconic species the Northern Territory has to offer. From Barramundi, Saratoga and Jewfish to Threadfin Salmon and Tarpon this amazing opportunity will evoke excitement with every cast.
