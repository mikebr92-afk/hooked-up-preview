Cape Adieu Cruises and Charters
2 tours available

Show all photos 
Cape Adieu Cruises and Charters is a family run business in Darwin.
From Stokes Hill Wharf they run fishing charters year round and sunset dinner cruises through the dry season.
Their fishing charters head out on a custom designed and built fishing vessel, for small group trips chasing the Top End's iconic species: barra, golden snapper, black jewfish, coral trout, red emperor, nannygai, Spanish mackerel, tuna, queenfish, giant trevally and more. Half day and full day charters are available, quality gear is provided, and the crew help everyone from first timers to experienced fishos get the most out of the day.
The signature four course Sunset Dinner Cruise aboard the Cape Adieu showcases the Territory's finest produce, with fresh seafood, native ingredients and tropical flavours prepared on board by their chefs as the sun sets over the harbour. The vessel is also available for private celebrations and corporate functions.
For the ultimate Top End day, Catch, Cook and Cruise combines both. Start with a fishing charter, then return to the Cape Adieu where your catch is cooked and served on deck. This is the Territory's most iconic experience!
For something more remote, Cape Adieu also runs multi day liveaboard fishing safaris along the remote NT & WA coastline.

Signature Four Course Dinner 
A luxurious Darwin Harbour Cruise, where breathtaking sunsets, fine wine, and exquisite cuisine come together for an unforgettable evening.
Indulge in our signature four-course Dinner Cruise, featuring a set menu that highlights the finest Northern Territory produce.
Carefully curated by our chefs, each dish showcases regional ingredients, offering a true taste of the Top End.
Relax and savour the flavours of the north with our small tasting hors d'oeuvres, followed by entrées, mains and dessert as you glide across the serene waters of Darwin Harbour.
​​Pair your meal with a selection from our carefully curated drinks menu, featuring refreshing cocktails, fine wines, and local brews to complement every bite.
Discover why we've been awarded Darwin's Best Seafood and received the TripAdvisor Certificate of Excellence for five years running.

Full Day Offshore Reef & Sport Fishing Charter 
For those chasing a hands-on adventure, our Full Day Fishing Charter is the ultimate Top End day out. Guided by our experienced crew who'll take you to the best local spots, you will drop a line for iconic species like barramundi, golden snapper, black jewfish, and coral trout.
Get ready to cast off on an unparalleled fishing adventure. Our fishing charters take you to the best of the Top End's fishing grounds, including reefs, drop-offs, and wrecks. You'll target a wide range of species from the highly prized golden snapper and black jewfish to the powerful Spanish Mackerel. For those chasing pelagic thrills, schools of tuna and speedy queenfish await, offering an exciting challenge.
We take you beyond Darwin Harbour into the rich waters of the Timor Sea and Beagle Gulf. Here, the reefs, wrecks, and bluewater drop-offs come alive with action. Spanish mackerel, tuna, queenfish, coral trout, and giant trevally are just some of the species you'll encounter. 
Whether you're jigging for reef fish, casting for GT's, or trolling for tuna, our expert crew will guide you every step of the way. We provide all the gear you need for a fantastic day out on the water.
