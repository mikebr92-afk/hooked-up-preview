Exciting and affordable Darwin fishing adventures.
The brand new 7.5m Contender fiberglass fishing boat offers an exciting fishing experience around Darwin and surrounds including Dundee Beach and the Peron Islands. With 400HP you arrive at the premier destinations quicker to enable dropping a line and getting onto all the action. 
On board is a toilet, all the latest fishing equipment, lunch and soft drinks are provided, all you need to bring is your own alcohol and a strong arm for pulling in the catch. Lincoln is a very experienced fisherman and guide and has many satisfied return customers.

Tours available
Full Day Shared or Private Barra or Bluewater Fishing Charter 
Take the Darwin fishing trip you’ve always wanted with the help of Barra or Blue Fishing Charters. They have all the boats, rods, reels, equipment and the best guides you need to have a great day on the water.
At Barra or Blue Fishing Charters, they can cater for any of your inland or offshore fishing requirements including Barra fishing and Bluewater fishing. The owner, Lincoln is a Fishing Guide with over a decade of experience and has the local knowledge required to help you angle your next catch. You have the freedom to customize your fishing charter to any waterway within a 200km radius of the Darwin area, just ask them and they will cater to your preferences. 
Show more 
Barra or Blue Extended Fishing Charters 
All-inclusive packages available including accommodation, fishing charters (barra or blue or mix of both), meals and snacks, soft drinks and water, all fishing gear, transfers from and return to Darwin in aircon.
The accommodation at our Dundee Beach BnB has all your needs covered for your stay to be more than just welcoming and comfortable, with pool, mancave to relax after a big day out on the water. You don't even have to cook your own private chef will have your mouth watering with catch of the day, fresh mud crab plus much more!
At Dundee Beach fishing is first class with a great range of species and lots of big hard fighting fish right on your doorstep! All our guides are expert fishermen with local knowledge and will ensure you are in the right spot at the right time to maximize your chances.
Barra or Blue Team cater to your needs to ensure your stay with them, and your fishing experience is a memorable one. 
Show more 
Half Day Private Fishing Charters 
Only got time to wet the line for a few hours - Why not jump on board Barra or Blue Fishing Charters. They have all the boats, rods, reels, equipment and the best guides you need to have a great time on the water. Up to 4 PAX.
Half Day Private Fishing Charters available from May to August out of Darwin (& surrounds). Charter times run for approx. 4 hours am/pm will be confirmed by guide prior to scheduled Charter weather and tide dependent. 
All fishing rods, tackle and bait provided. 
Water and soft drinks provided - BYO alcohol, lunch / snacks.
