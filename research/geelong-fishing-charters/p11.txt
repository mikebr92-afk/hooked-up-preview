VIP Membership
VIP PACK 2023 
If you or someone you know is an avid fisher, then Geelong Fishing Charter’s new exclusive limited edition VIP PACK is the perfect way to start the new fishing season this September 2023! With only 100 membership packs available throughout the whole year, and at the introductory price of just $385.00 (but valued at over $800.00!!!), it’s a deal almost too good to be true! 
The VIP PACK 2023 is the perfect gift for Father’s Day, Christmas (pictured), milestone birthdays, graduation, long service employment, retirement, or even just because you want to treat yourself or someone close to you. 
Each VIP PACK 2023-24 includes: 
*your unique numbered Geelong Fishing Charters VIP keyring (value priceless!)
*5 x free fishing charter vouchers up to $90 value (value $450)
*4 x 10% off guest fishing charter vouchers (value up to $100 in total)
*4 x free drink vouchers (value up to $32 in total)
*1 x free stubby holder voucher (value $10)
*1 x 10% off private fishing charter voucher (value up to $250)
To purchase, contact us by phone on 0491 713 236, by FB messenger, by Insta message, or email info@geelongfishingcharter.com.au 
TERMS & CONDITIONS: 
• VIP Packages must be activated prior to use by registering the owner details with Geelong Fishing Charters
• VIP Fishing Charter Vouchers are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for cash
• VIP Fishing Charter Vouchers can only be redeemed upon presentation of the Geelong Fishing Charters VIP keyring by its owner
• VIP Fishing Charter Vouchers are valid for the season starting 1st September 2023 and ending on 10th June 2024, demonstrated by the year 2023 on the associated keyring. No compensation will be given for unused vouchers. 
• All bookings with Geelong Fishing Charters are subject to weather conditions and availability
• Minimum numbers apply in order for fishing charters to run with Geelong Fishing Charters
• Unless otherwise stated, the cancellation policy for fishing charters with Geelong Fishing Charters is 72 hours
