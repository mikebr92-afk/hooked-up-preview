Charter Vessel Windarra
Windarra is purpose built Charter vessel constructed by a local company, Sailfish Catamarans in 2C Survey to carry 11 people (2 crew and up to 9 passengers).
She is a 9m Cat, with twin 150 hp Honda four stroke engines, very quiet, stable and comfortable boat, for Fishing , Whale watching , Snorkelling , etc.
Windarra is set out with comfort in mind; a large wheel house for those cold or rainy days, shaded seats, shower and toilet, kitchen, TV, sound system for your convenience and comfort.
Access to Windarra is from our jetty at Brunswick Heads Boat harbor, Stay dry from the minute you leave to when you safely return.
