Byron Bay Charters
We are your professional charters service in the Byron Shire, if you are interested in Fishing ,  Whale Watching , Scuba Diving, Snorkelling or Sight Seeing we can cater a Charter for you.
We have a purpose built Charter vessel Windarra . She is a 9m Sailfish in 2C survey for 11 people a large stable vessel with comfort in mind.
Equipped with comfortable seating inside and out, fitted with a toilet, shower and state of the art electronics. We operate 7 days a week.
