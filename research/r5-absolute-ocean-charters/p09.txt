Broome Bus Town Tours
Broome Bus Town Tour 
Cruise Special Broome Bus Town Tour 
Exciting new tour where we’ll bring the ocean to you!
Despite a trip to Broome not being complete without an adventure on the water (yes we are a little biased!), there’s also plenty to check out from the shore. Join us for a unique and all-enthralling town tour of Broome’s famous sites and hear fascinating insights into the marine environment around Broome. Our expert guides will tell tales of days gone by and inform you on life in Broome today.

Have Questions? 
What’s involved?
While some of your family or travel group may be busy landing their bucket list catch on-board an AOC fishing charter or taking in the sights on one of our amazing eco wildlife cruises, we can take care of you aboard one of our super friendly and relaxed town tours. Our unique land-based tour provides all guests with a fun and easy way to get to know the bustling tourism and pearling town of Broome.
You’ll be collected from your accommodation in our comfortable, air-conditioned bus and whisked away for a sightseeing showcase of our backyard.
The voyage will highlight the beauty and intrigue of Chinatown including Streeter’s Jetty (the only remaining jetty used by the original pearling fleet), a scenic drive past The Mangrove Hotel and famed Matso’s Broome Brewery, enroute to the Town Beach precinct overlooking the tidal wetlands of the Roebuck Bay Marine Park. Time to stretch the legs in the parkland and hear about the Catalina Flying Boat wrecks from WWII that are visible on super low tides as well as the staircase to the moon , a natural phenomenon that occurs over the bay on a full moon.
We’ll then take a short drive to the newly renovated Broome Golf Club (one of only two decent hills in Broome) to soak in the views of the Broome Wharf and Simpsons Beach before heading westward towards Gantheaume Point.
Again, it’s time to hop-off and explore Gantheaume Point, with its vibrant colours and rich history, it is an extraordinary part of Australia’s coastline. The kids will be delighted with the chance to walk in the footsteps of dinosaurs, and the unique lighthouse offers plenty of photo opportunities. Continuing on our journey, we end up at world-renowned Cable Beach. Boasting some of the most magnificent sunsets in the world, Cable Beach is a must-see! You can choose to end your Broome tour here or we’ll drop you back to your accommodation.
Broome Town Tour Facts
Departs 9am, finishes 11am (pick-ups start at approx 8.30am with return to accomm by 11.30am).
Top sites include Streeters Jetty, Matso’s Broome Brewery, Roebuck Bay, Gantheaume Point, Cable Beach, plus more!
Discounted fares for guests also joining another AOC adventure.
Bring your own water bottle, or we supply complimentary cold water.
Fun and entertaining!
What are you waiting for – come and join us!
We love our little Broome town and can’t wait to share the rich history and local secrets with every one of our guests. The AOC town tour adventure is approximately 2hrs in duration and we promise the kids will be entertained with frequent stops and the chance to hop-off the bus and explore! 
For anyone that has booked an AOC fishing charter or eco wildlife cruise, you will receive a discount on your bus tour for yourself and/or family and travel group members. 
The main stops included on our Broome tour are subject to change based on the season, weather and availability. So guests can join us more than once knowing that the tour will be slightly different each time!
Town Tour FAQs
Where do we go on the tour? 
Our tour visits top sites including Chinatown, Streeters Jetty, Matso’s Broome Brewery, Roebuck Bay, Town Beach, Gantheaume Point, and Cable Beach, plus more!
What do I need to bring on my tour? 
Bring personal items such as a camera, hat, and sunglasses, water bottle and wear comfortable clothing. Any footwear is fine, including thongs.
Are children welcome on the tour? 
Yes, children of all ages are welcome. Parents are responsible for their children at all times, and they must be seated. Children 4 years and under are free. We do not have booster seats for children.
How many passengers are on the bus tour? 
The bus tour can accommodate up to 12 passengers or 21 passengers (depending on which bus we use).
How often do the bus tours run? 
Bus tours operate every Tuesday and Friday. The tour departs at 9 am and finishes at 11 am. Pick-ups start at approximately 8:30 am, with return to accommodation by 11:30 am. If you would like to join us on a different day, please call with your request.
Are there any weight or disability restrictions? 
There are no specific weight or disability restrictions, but passengers need to be physically able to embark and disembark the bus safely. We can fit a foldable wheelchair, but navigating into the bus is required. Please contact us for further details.
How do I make a payment? 
Payment can be made by credit card (Mastercard or VISA) over the phone, by direct debit to our bank account (details provided via email), or by cash/card at our office in Broome. Payment is required at the time of booking to secure your spot.
Do I need to bring money? 
We stop for a coffee break where you can purchase hot or cold beverages from a local Broome establishment. Additionally, bring money or your credit card if you wish to purchase AOC merchandise.
What if I have to cancel? 
Cancellation Policy: AOC reserves the right to cancel or postpone any tour due to unforeseen circumstances. If we cancel a tour due to safety issues or insufficient numbers, you will be offered a spot on the next available tour or receive a full refund.
If you cancel:
• 72+ hours notice: Full refund.
• 24-72 hours notice: 50% cancellation charge.
• Less than 24 hours notice: No refund.
There are no refunds for bookings cancelled within 24 hours of tour departure date, or tours purchased but not utilised.
Refunds will be made in the same manner as the original payment.
Is the tour fun and entertaining? 
Absolutely! Our tours are designed to be fun, informative, and entertaining, with complimentary cold water provided for all guests.
Feel free to contact us for any additional questions or to make a booking. We look forward to showing you the best of Broome!
