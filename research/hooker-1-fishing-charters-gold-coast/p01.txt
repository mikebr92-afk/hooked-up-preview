Whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned angler, Hooker 1 Gold Coast Fishing Charters has the experience and expertise to provide you with a great, fun filled fishing and boating experience. Clarifying Your Charter Experience
Frequently Asked Questions
What do I need to bring?
Bring your sunscreen, sun hat and your mobile phone ready to take pictures. You are welcome to bring some sandwiches, burgers for lunch. We provide bottled water and light snacks.
Can I bring alcohol? 
NO SPIRITS.NO GLASS BOTTLES.
If you are bringing alcohol please bring it in can.
If you arrive intoxicated for the Safety briefing you will not be allowed to board Hooker 1 for the Charter and no refund will be given.
Any abuse of staff due to intoxication will absolutely not be tolerated and will result in the Charter being ended and Hooker 1 will return to port.
We all like a good time at Hooker 1,so bring a few drinks and enjoy yourself….Just keep it respectful and in moderation.
Will I get sea sick?
Hooker 1 is a very stable fishing platform but as a precaution we recommend taking some sort of motion sickness pills to ensure you have a good day on the ocean.
What is your refund policy?
If for what ever reason you need to cancel your booking we require at least 48 hours notice.
If you arrive after the departure time and Hooker 1 has departed you will not recieve a refund,it is your responsibility to be on time.
When to arrive for departure?
Please arrive a Minimum of 15 mins before departure time as sign in and a safety briefing need to be completed.
It is recommended that you work out travel times and how to get to our location the night before your charter.
On all mixed group charters it is your responsibility to arrive on time.As we run on a tight schedule unfortunately we dont wait for late arrivals. 
If you arrive intoxicated you will not be allowed to board Hooker 1.
