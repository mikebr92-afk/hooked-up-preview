ABOUT Us
HOOK DREAMS 
DIVE INTO OUR PASSION
We Promise An Unforgettable Fishing Experience With Expert Guidance And Top-Notch Equipment Every Time.
At Hooker 1 Gold Coast Fishing Charters, we are passionate about providing unforgettable adventures on the beautiful waters of the Gold Coast. Whether you’re an experienced angler looking to reel in the big catch or a first-timer eager to explore the stunning coastline, we offer charter boat experiences to suit all interests and skill levels. From deep-sea fishing to scenic tours, our expert crew guides you every step of the way, making sure your experience is both enjoyable and safe. With a commitment to excellence, we strive to deliver an adventure that is more than just a trip – an experience that will stay with you long after you return to shore.

Why Choose Us

RELAXING BOATS

GOLD COAST MARINE Adventures
Our marine activities are designed for all adventure seekers. We provide a range of exciting options including reef fishing, game fishing, and deep-sea fishing, along with whale watching, Gold Coast cruises, and fishing charters. Enjoy an unforgettable experience with all the unique opportunities to explore the beauty of the coastline and its marine life.
Reef Fishing
Experience reef fishing on the Gold Coast with our skilled crew, guiding you to the best fishing spots.
Game Fishing
Feel the excitement of chasing big catches in the abundant, vibrant waters of the Gold Coast. 
Deep Sea Fishing
Enjoy thrilling deep-sea fishing, trolling for marlin, tuna, or reef fishing for snapper and pearl perch.
Whale Watching
Witness Humpback whales up close as they migrate through Gold Coast waters during the journey. 
Gold Coast Cruises
Enjoy stunning waterways, scenic views, and tranquil atmosphere with our Gold Coast relaxing cruises.
Fishing Charters
Embark with Gold Coast fishing charters for the best fishing experience in the rich waters, guided by experts.
