6 x Custom Built Fishing Dories/Tenders
The 6 x Custom built fishing dories/tenders are very comfortable and safe. They are a 4.7mt fibreglass boat with 18HP outboards. Equipped with
• VHF radios
• Dedicated Tracking System monitored by the mothership
• Echo sounder/fish finder
• Good Quality Anchor and Rope
• Gaff Comfy Seats
• Fish measure
• Large Fish Esky with pilchards bait and ice included
• Rod holders
• All Mandatory Safety Equipment
To operate our fishing dory/tenders you will require a recreational ship master license (speedboat ticket). All Australian State and Territory Licenses are excepted.
