General Info We carry four Quintrex 4.2m wide body dinghies are available for your use.
Owned and operated By Michael and Helen Ross. Together with our experienced crew and a wealth of local knowledge; you can be assured all your onboard needs will be met and your charter fishing trip will be one you won't soon forget.
Australiana Charters offers 7-10 days fishing charters for group bookings of up to 14 guests, departing from Gladstone Queensland to the Swains Reefs. We are also available for charter anywhere along the Qld coast.
Length: 18.98M / 63ft
Draft: 2.5M
Beam: 5.8M
6x2 berth Air conditioned cabins
1x4 berth Air conditioned cabin
Spacious bathroom amenities are located outside on the main deck area as well as 1 toilet inside
Australiana Charters is a purpose built steel monohull with a cruising speed of 8.5 knots. Air-conditioned throughout and up to 14 guests can be assured of comfort.
