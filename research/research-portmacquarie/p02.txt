• Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters
Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters specialises in calm water, guided estuary lure fishing tours based in Port Macquarie, NSW.
Castaway lure and fly fishing guide, Patrick Linehan, is a passionate and highly-regarded angler with over two decades of experience fishing along the NSW Mid North Coast. 
Castaway charters operates on a custom-built, plate aluminium Bar Crusher 535XS centre console Fishing Weapon! Powered by a Yamaha 90hp outboard, the boat is also fitted with a bow-mount electric motor and the latest electronics for a modern lure fishing experience. 
Castaway Estuary Fishing Charters is nationally renowned for its success targeting mulloway, 'croc-sized' dusky flathead, bream, whiting and Australian bass on lures in the Hastings River, Camden Haven River, Macleay River and Manning River.
