Fishing Charters
Sport fishing is all about the fight. Chase kingfish, tuna and mahi mahi on light tackle off Sydney, casting, jigging and live baiting for the ultimate rod-bending challenge. Premium gear supplied, first-timers to gun anglers welcome.
The ultimate challenge Sport fishing off Sydney
Sport fishing is perhaps the greatest challenge going for the modern angler: hooking big, fast, powerful fish on the lightest line you can get away with. Sydney’s harbour reefs, washes and offshore grounds are made for it, holding hard-running kingfish, tuna, mahi mahi and more that will test your skill and your drag to the limit.
We run whatever method is firing on the day, casting poppers and soft plastics, jigging the reefs, or live baiting with yellowtail, all on top-of-the-range Shimano Stella and Saragosa reels. The crew coaches you through every run, so whether you are chasing a personal best or your first proper sportfish, you are in the box seat for the fight.

What you'll catch Sydney's sport fish
Light tackle turns every one of these into a genuine battle. These are the hard-running, fast species we chase most on a sport fishing charter, with albacore, jewfish and marlin in the mix when they show.
Yellowtail Kingfish
Sydney's premier sportfish. Brutal, dirty fighters that will bust you off in the reef if you let them.
Yellowfin Tuna
Blistering runs and serious stamina. A yellowfin on light tackle is a fight you will not forget.
Mahi Mahi
Bright, acrobatic and fast, mahi mahi love a cast lure around floating structure offshore.
Bonito
Pocket rockets that hit hard and run fast, brilliant light-tackle sport and great fun for everyone.
Tailor
Fast, aggressive and always keen, tailor smash lures in the wash and are a blast on light gear.
Australian Salmon
Hard-pulling, dogged battlers that school up in numbers, non-stop action on soft plastics and lures.
How we fish Every technique in the book
Sport fishing is an active, hands-on style, and we switch it up to match the fish and the conditions on the day.
Casting & jigging
Fire poppers and soft plastics at the reefs and washes, or work a metal jig down deep and hang on. It is fast, physical fishing and seriously addictive, and we show you the technique, so no experience is needed to get amongst them.
Live baiting & trolling
When the big kings and pelagics are on, we live bait with yellowtail or troll lures across the grounds. It is the surest way to tempt a trophy, and the strike, when it comes, is heart-stopping.
Your skipper Coached by a champion
Sport fishing rewards good technique, and there are few better teachers than George. A champion angler with more than 30 years on Sydney’s water, George knows exactly where the fish hold and how to tempt them on light gear.
He and the crew talk you through the cast, the retrieve and the fight, so you get the most out of every hook-up. When he says he will put you on the fish, 40 five-star Google reviews back him up.
