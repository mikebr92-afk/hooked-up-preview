The ultimate offshore challenge Big game fishing on Sydney's doorstep
Dreaming of hooking a real contender? Sydney is one of the rare places on earth where world-class game fishing sits just a short run offshore. Clear the Heads and within the hour you can be in the deep blue water where marlin, tuna and sharks hunt. Wahoo Fishing Charters has chased these fish off Sydney for more than 30 years, so we know where the current lines, temperature breaks and bait schools set up on the day.
Every trip is led by award-winning angler George and rigged with top-of-the-range Shimano game tackle. Our purpose-built boats carry the latest sounders, GPS and outriggers, so whether it is your first blue-water session or you are hunting a personal best, you are fishing with the best local knowledge and the right gear for the fight.

What you'll catch The fish you're chasing
Sydney's blue water holds some of the hardest-fighting fish in the country. These are the trophy species we target on a game fishing charter, along with striped and blue marlin and mako sharks when they are running.
Black Marlin
The ultimate prize. Fast, powerful and acrobatic, black, blue and striped marlin run off Sydney through the warmer months.
Yellowfin Tuna
Brutal, dogged fighters that test your stamina to the limit, at their best through the cooler months.
Bluefin Tuna
Southern bluefin push up the coast in winter, a genuine heavyweight and a battle you will not forget.
Mahi Mahi
Bright, spirited and superb eating, mahi mahi love the warm current and floating structure offshore.
Black Marlin
Blue marlin roam the warm blue water wide of Sydney, and a hookup is the pinnacle of game fishing.
When to go Game fishing season by season
There is a game fish worth chasing off Sydney almost all year. The target just changes with the water temperature.
Summer to autumn: marlin
From November through to May, blue, black and striped marlin move into the warm current off Sydney. This is prime time to target a billfish, and the run of mahi mahi and yellowfin through the same months makes for some of the most exciting fishing of the year.
Autumn to winter: tuna & sharks
As the water cools, the big yellowfin and southern bluefin tuna arrive, along with mako and other sharks. These are hard, grinding battles on heavy tackle, and a cool-season charter is your best shot at a true heavyweight.
Your skipper Fish with a champion
Game fishing rewards experience, and few know Sydney’s blue water like George. Over more than 30 years he has landed some genuine giants, including a 207.5kg blue marlin and a 280kg mako shark, and he brings that instinct to every charter.
George and the crew coach you through the strike, the run and the fight, so you get the most out of every hook-up. When he says he will put you on the fish, 40 five-star Google reviews back him up.
