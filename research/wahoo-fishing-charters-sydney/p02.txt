Eight ways to fish Sydney Our Sydney Fishing Charters
From heart-pumping game fishing to a relaxed reef session or a big day out with the crew, there is a Wahoo charter for every angler. Pick your adventure below.
Choose your charter Eight kinds of fishing charter
Every trip runs from Sydney Harbour with a champion skipper, complimentary Shimano gear and no experience needed. Tap any charter to see the details, target species and pricing.
Bucks Party Charters
Give the groom a send-off he will actually remember with a Sydney bucks party fishing charter. Get the lads together, chase a few fish and enjoy a big day out on the harbour and offshore.
We handle the gear, the local know-how and the good spots, so you can focus on the celebration. Gourmet catering is available on request to keep the crew fed and the day rolling along.
Corporate Charters
Impress clients and reward the team with a corporate fishing charter out of Sydney. It is a relaxed, memorable way to build relationships well away from the boardroom.
From small groups to larger parties across our fleet, we tailor the day to suit. Add quality catering on request while the crew looks after the fishing from start to finish.
Deep Sea Charters
Head wide into the deep water off Sydney, where the serious fish live. Deep sea fishing charters target hard-pulling species over the offshore reefs and drop-offs for a genuine test of stamina.
Our purpose-built boats are rigged with the latest sounders and GPS to find the fish fast. Bring your crew and settle in for a full day of blue-water fishing with everything provided.
Game Fishing Charters
Chase Sydney’s biggest offshore predators on a dedicated game fishing charter. Marlin, yellowfin tuna and mako sharks all run in our waters, and we set you up with the latest big-game rods, reels and teasers.
Every trip is guided by a skipper who has spent decades reading the currents and temperature breaks off Sydney. Whether it is your first blue-water session or your fiftieth, we put you in the box seat for the fight of your life.
Jigging Charters
Jigging is fast, physical and seriously addictive. Work a metal jig through the water column off Sydney and hang on for explosive strikes from kingfish and other hard-fighting reef dwellers.
It is heart-pumping action and a genuine workout rolled into one. We supply the specialised jigging outfits and show you the technique, so no experience is needed to get amongst them.
Kingfish Charters
Sydney is one of the best kingfish fisheries in the country, and these bruisers do not give up easily. Our kingfish charters put you over the reefs, wrecks and washes where the big models hold.
From live baiting to jigging and casting, we run whatever method is firing on the day. Beginners are welcome, and the crew is always on hand to help you land the fish of the trip.
Reef Fishing Charters
Drop a line over Sydney’s hidden reefs and pull up a feed of snapper, flathead and other prized eating fish. Reef fishing is the perfect mix of steady action and quality table fare for anglers of every level.
We know the local reef systems inside out, from the inshore ledges to the deeper offshore country. All bait and top Shimano tackle is supplied, so you can simply turn up and start catching.
Sport Fishing Charters
Sport fishing is all about the fight. Target fast, aggressive species like kingfish, tuna and mahi mahi on lighter gear for the ultimate rod-bending challenge on Sydney’s water.
It suits keen anglers chasing a personal best and first-timers wanting a proper adrenaline hit. We coach you through every run and supply top-of-the-range Shimano gear for the day.
Not sure which one? Find the right charter for your crew
Every charter is welcoming for beginners and kids, so the best pick really comes down to what you want out of the day. Here is a quick guide.
New to it, or bringing the family
Start with a reef fishing charter or a harbour sport fishing charter . They deliver steady action, calmer water and a feed of quality eating fish, which makes them ideal for first-timers, kids and anyone who wants a relaxed, scenic day on Sydney Harbour.
Chasing a trophy or a challenge
If you want your arms tested, point the boat wide. A game fishing charter or deep sea charter targets marlin, tuna and sharks in the blue water, while kingfish and jigging charters serve up fast, physical, hard-pulling sport closer to home.
Got a group to entertain? Our bucks party and corporate charters wrap the fishing, the gear and the good spots into one private day out. Still weighing it up? Just get in touch and we will match you to the right trip.
The fleet Two boats, every kind of catch
All eight charters run aboard our two purpose-built vessels, catering for 1 to 12 anglers across the fleet. Prices are per charter, not per person, so grab your crew and split the day.
