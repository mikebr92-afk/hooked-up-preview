Team building, done right Corporate fishing charters on Sydney Harbour
Looking to reward the team or impress a client? A corporate fishing charter is the ultimate team-building activity in Sydney. Get everyone out of the office and onto a private boat for a day of sea fishing on Sydney Harbour, where shared effort, a bit of friendly competition and a great day on the water do what no meeting ever could.
It is an exclusive, relaxed and genuinely memorable private fishing trip, and we take care of everything, the boat, premium gear, the best fishing spots and full gourmet catering. Whether your crew are seasoned anglers or have never held a rod, everyone gets involved and has a ripper time.
Check availability 
What's included Your corporate charter package
We handle every detail so you can focus on the team and the day. Here is what comes with your corporate charter, tailored to suit your group.
• Private boat hire, just your team on board 
• All premium Shimano rods, reels, tackle and bait 
• Full gourmet catering and refreshments 
• BYO welcome with plenty of fridge space 
• Flexible timing to suit your schedule 
• Expert crew with introductory lessons for beginners 
• Catch-and-release options available 
• The best fishing spots around Sydney Harbour and offshore 
• A comfy boat with shade, seating and a sound system 
• Memorable team photos and a shared experience that lasts 
The day, your way Tailor the trip to your team
A corporate charter can be whatever you need it to be. We tailor the day to your group and goals.
Choose your style
Keep it relaxed with a reef session chasing snapper and trevally, ramp up the excitement with light-tackle sport fishing, or head wide for game species like tuna and marlin. Catch and release is available too, and the crew looks after beginners every step of the way.
Full catering and extras
Add full gourmet catering with fresh local produce, drinks and refreshments, so the day feels genuinely special. We can also arrange transport, photography and branded merchandise to make the event complete. It is your day, and we make it seamless.
Your skipper & crew Expert crew, safe hands
A corporate day out has to run smoothly, and with George and the crew it does. A champion angler with more than 30 years on Sydney’s water, George knows the premium spots and exactly how to get a mixed-ability team onto the fish.
The crew share advice, run introductory lessons for the first-timers and keep the whole day relaxed and safe, so your team has a genuinely memorable time. When they say they will put you on the fish, 40 five-star Google reviews back them up.
