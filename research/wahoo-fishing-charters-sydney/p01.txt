Fishing charters Sydney The best sea fishing adventure of your life starts here
Deep sea fishing in Sydney Harbour
Some of the world’s best and most exciting deep sea fishing is right here in Sydney Harbour, if you know where to go. Wahoo Fishing Charters has fished these waters for more than thirty years, so we know exactly where the exciting fish bite, and the ones powerful enough to test your skill and strength.
Whether you are brand new to fishing or a seasoned pro, our local knowledge, Sydney-based charter fleet, top-quality equipment and endless energy add up to the most unforgettable day on the water. There is nowhere else like Sydney Harbour, and no other fishing charter in Sydney quite like Wahoo.
Sydney's best travel fishing charter company
Led by champion angler George, our skilled crew is on hand with the local knowledge, advice and coaching to put you on the fish. Our charter boats come packed with the latest sounding and GPS technology and modern comforts, so whether you are planning a day out for one or twelve, you will travel in style.
Top-of-the-range Shimano rods and fresh live bait mean everything you need to get the big fish biting is already on board. Turn up, step aboard, and get ready for some memorable fishing action.
Sydney's best fishing charters - made for every crew Choose your fishing adventure
From heart-pumping game fishing to a relaxed reef session or a big day out with the lads. Choose the Sydney fishing charter that suits your crew.
Bucks Party Charters
Give the groom a send-off he will actually remember with a Sydney bucks party fishing charter. Get the lads together, chase a few fish and enjoy a big day out on the harbour and offshore.
We handle the gear, the local know-how and the good spots, so you can focus on the celebration. Gourmet catering is available on request to keep the crew fed and the day rolling along.
Corporate Charters
Impress clients and reward the team with a corporate fishing charter out of Sydney. It is a relaxed, memorable way to build relationships well away from the boardroom.
From small groups to larger parties across our fleet, we tailor the day to suit. Add quality catering on request while the crew looks after the fishing from start to finish.
Deep Sea Charters
Head wide into the deep water off Sydney, where the serious fish live. Deep sea fishing charters target hard-pulling species over the offshore reefs and drop-offs for a genuine test of stamina.
Our purpose-built boats are rigged with the latest sounders and GPS to find the fish fast. Bring your crew and settle in for a full day of blue-water fishing with everything provided.
Game Fishing Charters
Chase Sydney’s biggest offshore predators on a dedicated game fishing charter. Marlin, yellowfin tuna and mako sharks all run in our waters, and we set you up with the latest big-game rods, reels and teasers.
Every trip is guided by a skipper who has spent decades reading the currents and temperature breaks off Sydney. Whether it is your first blue-water session or your fiftieth, we put you in the box seat for the fight of your life.
Jigging Charters
Jigging is fast, physical and seriously addictive. Work a metal jig through the water column off Sydney and hang on for explosive strikes from kingfish and other hard-fighting reef dwellers.
It is heart-pumping action and a genuine workout rolled into one. We supply the specialised jigging outfits and show you the technique, so no experience is needed to get amongst them.
Kingfish Charters
Sydney is one of the best kingfish fisheries in the country, and these bruisers do not give up easily. Our kingfish charters put you over the reefs, wrecks and washes where the big models hold.
From live baiting to jigging and casting, we run whatever method is firing on the day. Beginners are welcome, and the crew is always on hand to help you land the fish of the trip.
Reef Fishing Charters
Drop a line over Sydney’s hidden reefs and pull up a feed of snapper, flathead and other prized eating fish. Reef fishing is the perfect mix of steady action and quality table fare for anglers of every level.
We know the local reef systems inside out, from the inshore ledges to the deeper offshore country. All bait and top Shimano tackle is supplied, so you can simply turn up and start catching.
Sport Fishing Charters
Sport fishing is all about the fight. Target fast, aggressive species like kingfish, tuna and mahi mahi on lighter gear for the ultimate rod-bending challenge on Sydney’s water.
It suits keen anglers chasing a personal best and first-timers wanting a proper adrenaline hit. We coach you through every run and supply top-of-the-range Shimano gear for the day.

Good to know Frequently asked questions
Everything you need before you cast off. Still not sure about something? Just ask when you enquire.
Do I need any fishing experience? 
None at all. George and the crew coach you through every catch, so complete beginners and kids are just as welcome as seasoned anglers.
What's included in the price? 
All rods, reels, bait and tackle are supplied, top-of-the-range Shimano gear. Gourmet catering is available on request. Fuel is included, and there are onboard restroom facilities.
What should I bring, and can I bring food and drinks? 
Bring sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses and a camera, and wear comfortable layered clothing with non-slip shoes. You’re welcome to bring your own food and drinks, and catering is available on request. Alcohol is fine, enjoyed responsibly.
Where do we depart from? 
Our charters run out of Sydney Harbour. Most trips depart from the Harbour, and we keep pick-ups flexible , we can arrange to collect your group and drop you back at almost any wharf or jetty around Sydney Harbour. Just tell us your preferred spot when you enquire and we will sort out the details.
How long are the trips? 
Half-day charters run 6:30am to 1:00pm or 1:00pm to 6:00pm. Full-day game fishing runs 7:00am to 5:00pm.
How many people can come along? 
Anywhere from 1 to 12 anglers across the two boats. On Point takes up to 4, and Allie Hunter up to 8 depending on the trip type.
What's your cancellation and weather policy? 
A 50% fee applies for cancellations within 21 days of the charter, and 100% within 14 days. If conditions are unsafe to head out, we’ll reschedule your trip or refund you.
How do I book? 
Booking is enquiry-based, with no online payment. Send us your preferred date, group size and trip type, and we’ll match you to the right boat and get straight back to you.
Can I keep my catch? 
Yes, you can keep what you catch, as long as it is bigger than the legal size limits.
