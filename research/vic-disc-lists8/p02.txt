Episode 11
This week ET is competing in the Nagambie GoFish Tournament in Victoria with mate Jack chasing that great Aussie icon: yellowbelly. In the Surtees 540 on Lake Nagambie, the pair use the Garmin sounder's live scope to view the underwater environment and locate yellowbelly lurking among some sunken tree branches. With the Garmin's screen as a guide, they position their live shrimp to entice them to attack. ET then travels to Lake Mulwala and heads out in his Hobie to do battle with the great Murray cod. An exciting final session with Murray cod expert Jack Hocking produces several more Murray cod including a Lake Mulwala monster. It's Live view action all the way.
