Multi Days Private Fishing Charters – May – Dec
Two days, two fishing grounds and a comfortable Darwin Bayview marina base. Extend to five days with a Dundee Beach trip., explore a variety of barra & reef species out wide — all in one incredible experience.
‹ › 
🔥NT Fishing Staycation 🔥 
Fish by day. Unwind by the water at night.. 
On this fishing holiday, where you stay matters as much as where you fish. Your base is Bayview Fishing Paradise, a beautifully kept home overlooking the marina in Darwin. Wake up to boats on the water, head out with your private guide, then come home to a cold drink by the pool as the sun goes down.
Start with our 2 day, 2 night staycation. Extend to five days and finish with two nights at Paradise Dundee Beach.
Available from May to December. 
Where you’ll stay 
Bayview Marina Base
A comfortable, marina-side home with views over the water and boats moored right outside. It’s clean, spacious and set up for anglers, so it suits families, groups of mates and couples alike.
• Marina views
• Three bedrooms with quality mattresses
• Pool and outdoor area
• BBQ facilities to cook up your catch
• Air conditioning throughout
• Laundry and dryer
• First floor access, so no lugging gear up endless stairs
• Plenty of parking out front
Paradise Dundee Beach
On the extended trip, you’ll spend two nights at Paradise Dundee Beach. A relaxed coastal stay, close to the fishing, so you spend less time travelling and more time on the water.
Your guide plans each day around your goals, whether that’s a trophy barra, a good feed of reef fish or simply a relaxed day on the water with family.
What’s included? 
🏷️ Private charter: the boat and your guide, Floran, are booked just for your group. No sharing with strangers.
🏷️ Professional guide: Floran is our owner and operator and a true barra enthusiast. He’s called the Northern Territory home for over 10 years and knows these waters well. He turned his love of fishing into a career so he could share it with other anglers.
🏷️ Fishing: quality barra, reef fishing for jewfish and golden snapper, or a crab pot session. Everything is on our 6.1 m custom boats.
🏷️ Fishing gear: high-quality rods, reels and lures, plus Livescope sonar to target fish more precisely.
🏷️ Accommodation: Bayview Marina Base is a clean, comfortable and fishing-friendly stay with the marina lifestyle on your doorstep. It suits families, groups of mates and couples.
🏷️ Transfers: Complimentary pick-up and drop-off from Bayview Marina Base to each day’s fishing ground.
🏷️ Catch care: free cleaning and filleting, so you can enjoy your catch without the fuss. If you catch crabs, we’ll cook them for you too. It’s hard to beat fresh NT mud crab.
Fishing destinations 
Escape to Dundee Beach, a renowned fishing destination in Australia’s Northern Territory! This small coastal town, conveniently located 59 kilometres southwest of Darwin, offers unparalleled access to both freshwater and saltwater fishing. The nearby Finnis River system and expansive Fog Bay provide a vast playground for anglers of all skill levels.
Experience the thrill of targeting barramundi in the river, explore vibrant reefs teeming with diverse fish species, or try your hand at crabbing. Dundee Beach offers a unique blend of fishing experiences, from the calm rivers to the open waters of Fog Bay.
