The Capn's 
Vision
To instill a passion and a love for fishing and the outdoors in every person I meet.
To assist in transitioning away from bait fishing and into the world of soft plastics and hard body lures.

The Boat 
Stessl Warrior 440 powered by 60HP yamaha 
The Gear 
Wilson Blade n Tails rod paired with ATC reel. Lures provided by Zerek, McCarthy baits, Missing at Sea, Gobblers Lures and Zman. 
What makes us Different?
Not only is the purpose of the Capn’s Charters to put you onto some quality fish, the primary focus of the charter is to assist in any way possible to improve your skills as a lure and soft plastic angler.
Most charters in the market will put you onto fish, however they don’t spend the time to educate you, teach you, answer any questions you may have in regards to this style of fishing, and have you leave feeling confident in your own ability to target fish on lures and plastics.
