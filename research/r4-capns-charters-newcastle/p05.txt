Full Day Charters
Capn’s Charters can tailor a session to your wants and needs. For any questions or booking enquiries please do not hesitate to contact us! Tight Lines!
Capn’s Charters promotes sustainable fishing – Catch & Release 
Join either Amy or Lowy for an unforgettable 8-hour fishing charter , targeting big flathead, jewfish and a whole lot more on the scenic waters of the Hunter River or Lake Macquarie . This exclusive charter is designed for serious anglers looking to hook into trophy-sized fish with expert guidance.
What’s Included: 
• 8-hour fishing experience 
• Targeting big flathead, jewfish and more in prime fishing spots
• All fishing gear provided – rods, reels, tackle, and lures
• BYO food & drinks for a relaxed day on the water
Cost: $675
Prices reflect total for charter duration including up to 2 participants.
You may add a 3rd participant for an additional $100
Sessions from: 6am – 2pm
