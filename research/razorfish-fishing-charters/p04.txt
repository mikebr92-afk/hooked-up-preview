EXPERIENCE THE BEST OF WHITSUNDAYS REEF FISHING
BIG FISH. GOOD COMPANY. ONE HELL OF A DAY ON THE WATER.
Looking for a fishing charter in Airlie Beach or the Whitsundays without booking the whole boat? Join Razorfish Fishing Charters for a full day exploring some of the region’s most productive fishing grounds, from the Whitsunday Islands to offshore reefs, shoals and the Great Barrier Reef.
Whether you’re a solo fisho keen to get amongst the action, a couple looking for something different, a few mates chasing their next PB, or a dad wanting to take the kids fishing, our shared charters offer a genuine Whitsundays fishing experience with a small crew of like-minded people.
With eight hours on the water and a maximum of just six guests, there’s plenty of opportunity to chase quality fish. Depending on the weather, tides and conditions, we target species including Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Large Mouth Nannygai, Sweetlip, Tuskfish and Spanish Mackerel, using a mix of bottom fishing, jigging and trolling.
Our experienced skipper and deckhand are there to help you make the most of your day, whether you’re an experienced angler or picking up a rod for the first time. We supply quality Shimano fishing gear, bait and tackle, so you can concentrate on the fishing while our crew takes care of the rest.
Our shared fishing charters generally depart from Cape Gloucester Resort, within the Whitsundays region near Airlie Beach. Departure locations may vary depending on weather and fishing conditions, with Airlie Beach departures arranged when suitable. At the end of your charter, we’ll clean, fillet and bag your catch ready to take home.

FULL DAY REEF & OFFSHORE FISHING ADVENTURES
Make the most of a full day fishing in the Whitsundays, with eight hours to explore productive reef and island fishing grounds. From bottom fishing over reef and shoal country to working jigs or trolling for pelagic species, every trip is shaped by the weather, tides and fishing conditions.
Our experienced skipper and deckhand know these waters and work together to help you get the most out of your day. With a maximum of six guests aboard, there’s room to fish comfortably, learn a few local techniques and enjoy the experience without the crowds of a larger charter boat.
Whether you’re chasing your first Coral Trout, hoping to land a Red Emperor or simply keen to spend a day fishing the Great Barrier Reef near Airlie Beach, our Full Day Shared Charter offers a genuine offshore fishing experience with everything you need supplied.
FISHING Locations
Whitsunday Islands, inshore and offshore reefs, shoals and the Outer Great Barrier Reef.
DEPARTURES
Cape Gloucester Resort, with Airlie Beach departures possible depending on weather and fishing conditions.
WEATHER CONDITIONS
Fishing grounds and departure times are confirmed based on weather and sea conditions.
