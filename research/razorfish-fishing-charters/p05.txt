THE PERFECT FISHING GIFT IN THE WHITSUNDAYS
Looking for a memorable gift for someone who loves fishing? Give them an unforgettable day on the water with a Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charter Gift Voucher.
Set amongst the spectacular Whitsunday Islands and Great Barrier Reef, our fishing charters explore some of the region’s most productive reef, island and offshore fishing grounds. From chasing prized Coral Trout and Red Emperor to enjoying a genuine fishing adventure in the beautiful Whitsunday region near Airlie Beach, it’s an experience they’ll remember long after the day is over.
Whether you’re celebrating a birthday, Christmas, Father’s Day or another special occasion, our fishing gift vouchers are ideal for experienced anglers, first time fishos, couples, families and groups of mates. With private and shared charter experiences available, you can give them something far more memorable than another present.
