ABOUT THE VESSEL
Purpose-Built for Comfort, Speed & Serious Fishing
Razorfish is a fast, comfortable, and purpose- built 7.5m Sailfish Commercial Catamaran , designed for private and shared fishing charters in the stunning waters of the Whitsundays. Powered by twin 200hp Mercury outboards , the vessel reaches speeds of up to 40 knots , getting you to the fish faster with a smooth, stable ride. 
We accommodate up to 6 guests , plus a professional skipper and deckhand , with spacious seating, open deck space for fishing, and all the features needed for a productive and enjoyable day on the water – whether you’re an experienced angler or giving fishing a go for the first time.

About the Skipper – Meet Kris
Passionate about fishing. Focused on your experience. Committed to the catch.
Kris , owner and skipper of Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters , has spent years fishing the east coast of Australia and brings a deep passion for sharing that experience with others. His goal is simple: to give every guest – whether a complete beginner or a seasoned angler – the best possible day on the water. 
With decades of boating and fishing experience, Kris has owned multiple vessels and skippered larger offshore boats. His extensive knowledge of local waters, tides, and fish behaviour means you’ll be in expert hands. Most importantly, safety and comfort always come first . 
Kris loves helping people land that dream fish – whether it’s your first catch or a personal best. He caters to all ages and skill levels, from families and seniors to corporate teams and groups of mates heading out for a day or overnight trip. 
The Whitsundays offer fantastic fishing year- round, with species such as Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Nannygai, Tuskfish, Mackerel , and Tuna . The islands are especially productive for trolling lures and targeting both reef and pelagic species. 
Kris and the Razorfish crew work hard to keep you on the fish, follow all Marine Park regulations, and ensure everyone leaves with a smile ( and hopefully a few fillets). If you’ve got questions, feedback, or requests – we’re all ears. 
At Razorfish, it’s all about a great day on the water, every time. We look forward to welcoming you aboard.

Fishing the Whitsundays
Unmatched variety, epic scenery, and fishing tailored to you.
Fishing in the Whitsundays offers incredible variety and action all year round. With warm waters, healthy reef systems, and close access to both island drop- offs and the outer Great Barrier Reef , this region is a dream for both casual fishos and serious anglers. 
At Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters , we target a range of prized species including Coral Trout , Red Emperor , Nannygai , Mackerel , Tuskfish , Tuna , and more. Whether you prefer trolling, bottom fishing, or simply chasing whatever’s biting, we tailor your charter to suit the conditions and your goals. 
Our charters explore some of the region’s best- known fishing grounds across the Whitsunday Islands , inner and outer reefs , and surrounding reef systems. Every trip is guided by local knowledge, current weather, and fish activity – giving you the best chance at landing something memorable. 
From shallow reef drop- offs to deep offshore marks, the Whitsundays is a region where year- round fishing meets postcard- perfect scenery. Whether it’s your first time with a rod or you’re chasing that next big catch, we’ve got the gear, the crew, and the spots to make it happen. 
OUR PROMISE
Tailored charters, trusted crew, and a promise to go the extra mile.
At Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters , we’re committed to making your time on the water safe , enjoyable , and unforgettable . Whether you’re here to chase big fish, unwind with your crew, or try something new, we’ll make sure it’s a day worth talking about. 
We pride ourselves on local knowledge, personalised service, and going the extra mile. If you have questions, special requests, or feedback , we’re always happy to hear from you – it helps us keep improving every charter we run. 
Book your trip with Razorfish Whitsundays Fishing Charters and experience world- class fishing in one of Australia’s most iconic marine playgrounds – the Whitsundays and Great Barrier Reef. 
Proud sponsor of the Stonka Fishing Challenge celebrating community, competition, and conservation in the Whitsundays.
Every line you reel in at the Stonka Fishing Challenge does double duty: it’s not just about the catch, it’s about caring for our community. All of the money raised through entry fees, raffles and special events goes straight to the Leukaemia Foundation’s Leukaemia House in Townsville.
By taking part you help keep a roof over the heads of families who’ve travelled long distances for blood-cancer treatment – covering their accommodation, meals and support services so they can focus on what matters most. Fish hard, have fun, and be proud that your weekend on the water will make a real difference for people when they need it most.
