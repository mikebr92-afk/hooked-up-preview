Lets Fish Lake Mac 2026 
For NDIS Participants only
After the great success of the previous festivals please join Cooper again this year to experience Day Trippin Charters , a unique NDIS-supported fishing experience ! 
On Saturday 21st and Sunday 22nd November, as part of the 2026 Lets Fish Lake Mac Festival Cooper will bring his boat to the disability accessible Bolton Point Jetty at 68 Middle Point Road, offering exclusive trips designed specifically for NDIS participants. These 60-minute fishing trips will start every 90 minutes from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm (with the final trip finishing at 1:30 pm).
Each trip accommodates up to 4 individuals or 2 groups of 2, with NDIS participants allowed one accompanying carer or parent. During the hour-long fishing experience, you'll enjoy quality time on the water, while the remaining 30 minutes will allow for a smooth transition between groups. Please note when booking with 2 people to put both people in the 1 booking (not 1 separate booking for each)
Spaces are limited, so come along and experience the thrill of a personalized fishing adventure!
Please note, unfortunately, wheelchair access is not available at this time.
