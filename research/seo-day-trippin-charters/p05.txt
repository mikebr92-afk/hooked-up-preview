Full Day Fishing Charters in Lake Macquarie and Newcastle: The Ultimate Adventure
Welcome to Day Trippin Charters, where a full-day fishing charter means more time on the water, more opportunities to find the fish and a relaxed day exploring Lake Macquarie and the Hunter River.
Our full-day charters give you 10 hours to fish, explore different locations and adapt to changing conditions. With Captain Cooper’s local knowledge, you can follow the fish, work different spots and make the most of the tides and bite throughout the day.
Epic Full Day Fishing Trips: More Time, More Fish
The Full-Day Advantage
Ten hours on the water gives you the freedom to fish without feeling rushed. Rather than being limited to a few locations, we can move between productive areas as conditions and fish activity change.
Captain Cooper follows the tides, bait activity and fish movement to determine where to fish next. If the bite slows down, we keep moving and look for another opportunity.
More time also means more chances to target different species and try different fishing techniques throughout the day.
Explore Lake Macquarie & the Hunter River
Our full-day fishing charters take place across the productive waters of Lake Macquarie and the Hunter River.
From channels and drop-offs to deeper sections and areas holding bait, there are plenty of environments to explore. Captain Cooper uses his local knowledge and experience to choose locations based on the conditions and what you're hoping to catch.
Enjoy a Relaxed Day on the Water
A full-day charter isn't just about fishing from start to finish. With 10 hours on the water, there's time to take a break, enjoy the scenery, grab some food and soak up the experience.
There are plenty of opportunities for toilet breaks throughout the day, and you can bring lunch and drinks with you. The goal is to give you a comfortable, enjoyable day on the water with plenty of time to wet a line.
Why We’re Lake Macquarie and Newcastle’s Best Full Day Choice
Experienced, Hands-On Skipper
Captain Cooper is involved throughout the entire trip, from finding productive fishing areas to helping with techniques and tackle. His local knowledge helps you make the most of changing tides, conditions and fish activity.
Quality Gear & Bait Provided
We provide quality rods, reels, tackle and bait, along with the fishing licence for your trip. Just bring suitable clothing, sun protection, a hat and food and drinks for the day.
Keep Your Catch
Legal-sized fish you choose to keep are spiked, bled and iced during the trip to help maintain freshness. Your catch can also be cleaned and filleted at the end of the charter, ready to take home.
Small Group Experience
Our charters are kept small, giving you more space and plenty of individual attention. Captain Cooper can provide hands-on assistance and fishing advice throughout the day.
Suitable for All Experience Levels
Whether you're an experienced angler or new to fishing, you'll receive guidance throughout the trip. Captain Cooper can help with casting, bait, lure techniques and other fishing skills.
Ready For The Big One? Secure Your Full Day Newcastle Charter!
Want more time to fish, explore and enjoy Lake Macquarie or the Hunter River? A full-day charter gives you 10 hours on the water and more opportunities to find the bite.
Join Captain Cooper for a personalised fishing experience with quality gear, local knowledge and plenty of time to wet a line.
