WA Fishing & Boat Charters 
Epic Fishing on the trip of a lifetime! 
Apache Charters is a WA family owned business established in 1999 by Matt Howard. We operate all year round and offer overnight ‘live aboard’ fishing charters in the Abrolhos Islands, Shark Bay and Montebello Islands. We also offer day fishing trips in Perth, Rottnest & Carnac Island boat charters and Swan River cruises throughout the summer months. 
“With Apache Charters you are always guaranteed a quality experience and professional, friendly service. We are dedicated to ensuring the best charter experience for our clients and our reputation in the industry is testament to this.”

Where we operate from 
Our live aboard fishing charter locations include Abrolhos Islands , Montebello Island and Shark Bay from March to November when the fishing is best. 
We also offer Perth based private boat charters during the summer months out to Rottnest Island & Carnac Island as well as Swan River boat cruises. Throughout summer we also offer Perth fishing & Rock Lobster day tours departing from Fremantle.

Rottnest Island Boat Charters
Cruise out to Rottnest Island on your own private boat charter onboard the 70ft Lady Grace. Take in scenery, sunbathe on the large deck area or get into the water on our sup boards and inflatables.
