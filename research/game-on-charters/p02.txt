Welcome to Game On Charters 
We are based in beautiful Portland, Victoria, where Bluefin tuna is right on our door step. 
Come aboard with Joe and enjoy a fishing trip experience you will never forget. 
If you're chasing the dream of catching a 100 kilo bluefin tuna or bagging out with quality fish. Joe at Game On Charters can make this dream become a reality. 
Game On offers fishing charters for: 
• Bluefin tuna, May through to August 
• 
• Bottom Bouncing, all year round 
• 
• Deep drop fish electric reels, all year round 
Our fishing charter is for any skill level, whether you’re a seasoned angler, enjoy the occasional fish with mates, or a complete beginner – we’ll get you enjoying every moment hook, line and sinker.
