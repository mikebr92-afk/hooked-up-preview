Fishing in Portland is impressive! People flock to the coast of Portland because they want to catch game fish, Bluefin tuna and also a variety of quality table fish. 
Visitors will never go away disappointed. 
Joe, your host will take you on a memorable trip, he is 100% local and has a wealth of knowledge about fishing and also the local area. 
Game Fishing | Gallery | Bottom Bouncing 
Game On ! 
We carry a variety of quality fishing gear, including full range of Shimano and Okuma gear. 
Daiwa electric rods and reels for deep water fishing, capable of dropping in on fish at 700m! Joe also doesn't mind if you bring your own gear. 
Game On caters for individuals, small and larger groups, (maximum of 8), corporate team building days, bucks weekends and family days out. 
No matter your level of experience Joe and his crew will guarantee fish and an adventure you will never forget. 
Currently we are taking bookings for offshore and deep sea fishing. 
At present there is an abundance of gummy shark, big flat head, snapper and nannygui fish. 
Joe will meet you at the boat ramp near the Portland Yacht Club , 7am in winter and 6am in summer. 
Before departure Joe will run through safety regulations and requirements. 
A full day runs for approximately 8 hours. Joe welcomes clients aboard his, safe, warm and comfortable 30 foot Kevlacat, with twin 300 HP, 4 stroke outboards. It's quick, quiet and no fumes. 
Catering is unavailable on the charter. Please feel free to bring your own Eski with your own food requirements. 
Saying that, sashimi is always best when fresh!
