About
MV Night Crossing is a fishing charter and boat rental service located in Gladstone Central, Australia. With a focus on providing an unforgettable fishing experience, MV Night Crossing offers customers the opportunity to embark on a six-day live-aboard offshore reef fishing charter.
Departing from Gladstone every Friday afternoon, guests have the chance to explore the remote and stunning Swain Reefs. Travelling 120 nautical miles offshore overnight, MV Night Crossing arrives at the outer Great Barrier Reef, where guests can fish from one of their six custom-built fishing dories. The charter provides the unique opportunity to catch a variety of fish, such as Coral Trout, Red Throat Emperor, and more. Guests can take their catch home, as it is processed, bagged, and snap frozen, ready to cook.
The luxurious MV Night Crossing can accommodate up to 18 guests, ensuring a personalised and comfortable experience. The ship features air-conditioned cabins, providing a relaxing environment after a day of fishing. The vessel is staffed with 4-5 experienced crew members who are dedicated to providing quality service, delicious meals, and expert fishing knowledge to all guests.
When booking a fishing trip with MV Night Crossing, guests can expect an experience of a lifetime. Whether it's the thrill of reeling in a big catch or the awe-inspiring beauty of the Great Barrier Reef, every moment on board is sure to be memorable. The crew is passionate about providing top-notch service and ensuring that guests have an unforgettable time on their fishing adventure.
For more information, interested individuals are encouraged to contact the friendly staff of MV Night Crossing via phone or email. The team is always ready to assist with any questions or inquiries. All customer details are treated with the utmost confidentiality, ensuring a safe and secure booking process.
Overall, MV Night Crossing sets the standard for fishing charters and boat rental services in Gladstone Central. With their luxurious ship, experienced crew, and focus on providing an exceptional fishing experience, MV Night Crossing is the go-to choice for anyone seeking an unforgettable fishing trip on the Great Barrier Reef.

Frequently Asked Questions
What fishing trips do you offer? 
We run a 6 day live aboard offshore reef fishing charter on MV Night Crossing, including time on the outer Great Barrier Reef from our custom built fishing dories.
Where and when does the trip depart from? 
The charter departs from Gladstone Qld every Friday afternoon, with travel offshore overnight to the Swain Reefs.
How do I join or make an enquiry? 
Send us a message using the contact form or reach our friendly staff by email or phone, and share your enquiries in the form.
