FISHING PACKAGES
SCROLL DOWN TO SEE OUR PRICING AND CLICK ‘BOOK NOW’ TO BOOK ONLINE
Come and enjoy a great day on the water being professionally guided around our beautiful waters of Hervey Bay. Being a born and bred local with a lifetime of experience on the waters of the Hervey Bay, let Andrew and his team show you the best fishing on offer in our great region.
Our fishing packages include drinks, morning tea, snacks and bottled water. You will need to bring your own lunch, or we can supply at an additional cost.
All the tackle, rods, reels, lines and lures you need are supplied, (excluding fly fishing flies) and we use quality Shimano spin outfits and Sage fly rods and reels. 
We target a large range of species including:
• Golden Trevally and many more trevally species
• Snapper
• Mackerels (school, spotted and spanish)
• Tuna (longtail and mac tuna)
• Queenfish
• Black Marlin
• Cobia
• Reef Species including coral trout, goldspot cod, sweetlip and many more
• Threadfin Salmon
These are just a few that we may encounter, using mostly lures and soft plastics.
We also cater for fly fisherman and have some of the most unique flats fishing in the world.

MOTHER SHIPPING PACKAGES
Join us for an extended liveaboard fishing experience for up to 3 days/2 nights! 
Mothershipping at the northern end of Fraser Island offers guests a unique experience. Waking up minutes from busting tuna, black marlin and getting that daylight snapper bite is certainly an advantage when mother shipping. All meals are prepared by our onboard chef on these trips which make the experience first class. 
All mothership trips are private bookings on an on-demand basis. Please contact us for more information. 
Aquila Sunrise
We also offer mother shipping staying aboard the power catamaran Aquila Sunrise. This Powercat has 3 separate cabins and is ideal for smaller groups.
Tasman Venture
For larger groups (up to 6 persons) our mothership Tasman Venture is a luxury 20m Catamaran. The Tasman Venture offers spacious, air-conditioned comfort, ample viewing decks and underwater viewing windows for overnight escapes.

THE BAY TO BARRA DRIVE
2 Destinations… 1 Adventure! 
What could be better? Fishing the diverse waters of Hervey Bay for our pelagic speedsters then heading north to picturesque Lake Awoonga to take on the mighty barramundi!
This self-drive getaway is the perfect adventure for those looking for a road trip and exploring the outdoors in beautiful Queensland.
Your itinerary includes the following:
N 
3 nights’ accommodation in Hervey Bay at the Boat Harbour Resort in a 2 bedroom villa or similar.

N 
Self-drive to Boyne Island in Gladstone 
N 
3 nights’ accommodation at Boyne Island Motel & Villas, in a 2 bedroom villa or similar 
For details please visit www.discoveryholidayparks.com.au/caravan-parks/queensland/gladstone-tannum-sands 
N 
Pick up/drop off daily from Boyne Island Motel 
N 
2 days fishing for barramundi on Lake Awoonga
