Discover Sydney’s premier fishing experience with Sydney Charter Fishing. Whether you’re a first-time angler or a seasoned fisherman, we offer unforgettable adventures across Sydney Harbour, the coastline, and offshore waters. 
Our main Charter Options:
4 hour, half day offshore charters: Ideal for families, groups, and beginners departing Manly Wharf 3 at 1pm
Our 7 hour extended offshore charters: Perfect for new anglers and serious enthusiasts chasing trophy fish and remote hotspots departing Manly Wharf 3 at 6am.
All sessions are offshore trips targeting snapper, kingfish, and more on Sydney’s deep-sea reefs.
Enjoy stunning harbour views, nearshore reef action, or the thrill of open-ocean fishing with our expert, licensed crew. Our vessel, with a marine toilet is fully equipped for bottom fishing, reef and sport fishing, game fishing, and seasonal whale watching.
