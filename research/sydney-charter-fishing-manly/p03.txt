At Sydney Charter Fishing, we’re absolutely hooked on what we do! Our handpicked crew lives and breathes fishing, with expertise in catching snapper, kingfish, marlin, and more. They’re always ready to share tips and techniques to help you up your fishing game.
We strictly follow NSW DPI Fisheries size and bag limits to protect Australia’s fish stocks—no illegal activities are permitted aboard our vessel.

Vessel: The A-TEAM
Vessel: The A-TEAM
CFB Licence #22055
The MV ‘A-TEAM’ is a purpose-built 38ft charter fishing vessel, powered by a powerful 225hp turbo diesel 6.8L engine. With a modern fibreglass GPR hull, this boat offers outstanding stability, even in rough seas, allowing you to spend more time fishing and less time travelling. It’s built for comfort and efficiency— because you deserve to get what you pay for! 
The A-TEAM features a spacious rear deck, providing ample room for up to 16 guests offshore or 27 guests for inshore trips , making it perfect for any fishing adventure.
Fully licensed and insured, the A-TEAM complies with all Australian Maritime Security Authority (AMSA) and NSW RMS Maritime regulations. With an On Premise License, Survey Permit No. 22055 certifies it as a professional charter vessel, ensuring your safety and satisfaction on every trip.
