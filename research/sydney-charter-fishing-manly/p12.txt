Reef Fishing Trips
A “Wharf to Wild” fishing day out, leaving directly from Manly Wharf 3. For a real adventure why not join our year-round deep sea reef fishing charters, perfect for all skill levels, offering expert guidance, top-notch gear, and unforgettable catches just offshore! We are the closest fishing charter boat company to the open ocean that increases your time spent fishing specialising in tuition for young children, beginners, ALL international guests and experienced anglers !
A “Wharf to Wild” fishing day out, leaving directly from Manly Wharf 3. For a real adventure why not join our year-round deep sea reef fishing charters, perfect for all skill levels, offering expert guidance, top-notch gear, and unforgettable catches just offshore! We are the closest fishing charter boat company to the open ocean that increases your time spent fishing.
This reef fishing charter is our most popular trip, taking you to some of the best fishing reefs off the coast of Sydney. We typically fish between 3 and 12 nautical miles from shore, in waters as deep as 120 meters . During the trip, we target a variety of reef species using top-quality Shimano Penn rods, reels, and fresh bait .
Your skipper, Adam , along with his skilled crew, will provide fishing tuition throughout the day. Whether you’re an experienced angler or a beginner, you’ll walk away having learned something new. All fish caught will be cleaned by the crew, ready for you to take home and enjoy.
We run two reef trips per day, with times of 6:00am – 1:00pm , and 1:00pm – 5:00pm ,
Our A-Team charter vessel can comfortably accommodate up to 14 guests , offshore and up to 28 guests inshore making them perfect for birthdays , corporate functions , or a social day out with friends. We offer deep sea reef fishing all year round.
