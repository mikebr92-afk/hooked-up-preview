Privacy Policy 
At Sydney Charter Fishing, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. We value your trust and want to ensure that your personal information is handled with the utmost care. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data. 
​ 
Information Collection and Usage 
We may collect information about you to fulfill your orders, provide you with updates, and enhance your shopping experience. This information includes, but is not limited to, your name, contact details, and order history. Rest assured, we never pass on, sell, or swap any of your personal details with third parties. 
​ 
Cookies 
Our website uses cookies to improve your shopping experience. Cookies are small files sent to your computer that help you log in, maintain a shopping cart, and make purchases. These cookies are temporary and only last while you’re browsing our website. We do not store persistent cookies on your computer. Cookies also enable us to provide a more personalized shopping experience by displaying products that match your interests. 
​ 
IP Addresses 
When you access our website or any other site, the web server needs to know your computer’s network address (IP address) to deliver the requested web pages to your browser. IP addresses are anonymous but may reveal the general geographic location of the computer. We do not record IP addresses unless you provide information about yourself, in which case we may record your IP address for security purposes, such as when you make a purchase. This allows us to track your order and ensure its security. 
​ 
Data Protection and Security 
We take appropriate measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Your information is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized personnel. 
​ 
Updates to Our Privacy Policy 
We may periodically update our privacy policy to reflect changes in how we handle your data and to comply with legal requirements. You will be informed of any significant changes. 
​ 
Contact Us 
If you have questions or concerns about our privacy policy or how your information is handled, please feel free to contact us. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to addressing any issues promptly.
