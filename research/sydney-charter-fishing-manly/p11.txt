Sports Fishing Trips
Adrenalin junkies jump on board and experience the thrill of Sydney’s sports fishing trips, targeting Mahi Mahi, Marlin, Tuna, and Kingfish, with high-octane action, expert guidance, and the chance to test your skills on tough Kingfish off Sydney’s famous Peak or at the 12-mile reef!
Adrenalin junkies jump on board and experience the thrill of Sydney’s sports fishing trips, targeting Mahi Mahi, Marlin, Tuna, and Kingfish, with high-octane action, expert guidance, and the chance to test your skills on tough Kingfish off Sydney’s famous Peak or at the 12-mile reef!
Our sports fishing trips are all about targeting the big, predatory pelagic fish, including Mahi Mahi , Marlin , Tuna , Kingfish , and Jewfish (just to name a few). Depending on the conditions, we’ll light troll to 9-mile , 12-mile , or long reef for Marlin and Tuna , then switch to live bait and jigging for Kingfish . We’ll even throw in a bit of reef fishing to ensure you get the most out of your day. Additionally, we fish around Sydney’s FADs (artificial fish aggregating devices) for an extra boost.
For the modern angler, our sports fishing trips are a true adrenaline rush, so make sure you bring your camera to capture the action with your mates. The 12-mile reef is home to some of the toughest sports fish around— Kingfish that’ll test your skills. Are you up for it?
