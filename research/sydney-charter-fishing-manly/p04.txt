Sydney Charter Fishing is one of Sydney’s leading providers of fishing experiences, with over 25 years in the game. We offer Deep Sea, Reef, Game, and Sports Fishing Trips, as well as Harbour Cruises and Bucks Parties.
Reef Fishing Trips
Reef Fishing Trips
A “Wharf to Wild” fishing day out, leaving directly from Manly Wharf 3. For a real adventure why not join our year-round deep sea reef fishing charters, perfect for all skill levels, offering expert guidance, top-notch gear, and unforgettable catches just offshore! We are the closest fishing charter boat company to the open ocean that increases your time spent fishing.

Sports Fishing Trips
Sports Fishing Trips
Adrenalin junkies jump on board and experience the thrill of Sydney’s sports fishing trips, targeting Mahi Mahi, Marlin, Tuna, and Kingfish, with high-octane action, expert guidance, and the chance to test your skills on tough Kingfish off Sydney’s famous Peak or at the 12-mile reef!

Game Fishing Trips
Game Fishing Trips
Our year-round game fishing trips offer a full day of action, targeting Sharks, Tuna, and Marlin, with thrilling fights against some of the ocean’s biggest and toughest fish—prepare for the catch of a lifetime!
