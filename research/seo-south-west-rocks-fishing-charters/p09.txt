Accommodation
Relaxing Beach House
Fish hard and relax in the comfort of our beach house each evening.
Come home to the “Beach House” after a big day of fishing. Easily accommodating large groups of up to 11 people (4 bedrooms nine single beds!), you have private use of the entire house.
The “Beach House” is situated only one minute to the beach and country club. We offer the comfort of a fully self contained home complete with full kitchen, lounge and dining area. There is a large gas BBQ and outdoor table set up undercover, in a large, quiet garden setting with plenty of off street parking for cars and boats.
“Our fishing groups receive breakfast, morning tea, accommodation and fantastic fishing…all part of our amazing package”
