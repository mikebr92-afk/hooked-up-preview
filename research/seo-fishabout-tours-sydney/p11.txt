Destinations | Western Australia
We are excited to be able to provide the choice of the best camp on the Kimberley Coast, motherships that deliver the ultimate overall Kimberley experience and the leading fishing focussed operator who chase big barra, giant trevally, queenfish, coral trout and an array of other species. A trip to the Kimberley can be totally fishing focussed or embrace everything else the area has to offer including Wandjina and Bradshaw rock art that are thousands of years old, thundering waterfalls with swimming holes at their base, unique flora and fauna and some of the most stunning scenery in the country. The rivers and creeks deliver some of the best barra fishing anywhere where catching a trophy metre plus specimen is an achievable goal, amongst a whole lot of other action. You will get your arms stretched by giant trevally, tangle with leaping queenfish and deliver golden snapper for the chef to prepare for dinner. What we offer here is the absolute best of what can be experienced on the Kimberley Coast.

Western Australia 
Ord River / Kununurra
The Ord River and adjacent impoundments are exceptional barramundi fisheries. The Ord is the largest river in the East Kimberley and has the reputation of producing big barramundi. 
Featured , Western Australia 
Cocos Keeling Islands
Even by Kimberley standards, this is a remote location with access being limited to either float plane or helicopter. It’s amazing to think that accommodation of this quality is available on a corner of the Admiralty Gulf. 
Western Australia 
Rowley Shoals
Rowley Shoals, situated 168 nautical miles west of Broome is made up of 3 main systems, Imperieuse Reef, Clerke Reef and Mermaid Reef. All provide awesome sport and game fishing. 
Western Australia 
Kimberley Fishing Safari
Whilst all Kimberley motherships offer opportunities to fish, few have it as their primary focus. Reel Teaser’s crew are all qualified experts in their field and have the experience and knowledge to deliver the fishing trip of a life time. 
Western Australia 
Kuri Bay
The variety of fishing options in the crystal clear waters around Kuri Bay will astound. Sand flats, gorges, estuaries and creeks through to pelagic action and many tasty reef dwellers including coral trout. 
Featured , Western Australia , Corporate 
Kimberley Coastal Camp
Kimberley Coastal Camp is one of the very few places in the world where you can enjoy fabulous scenery, gourmet food, unique beach front accommodation, and Spectacular Fishing. 
Western Australia 
Kimberley Cruises
The ultimate mothership options that cover the vast majority of the Kimberley Coast from Broome to Wyndam over 14 days, with options of the 8 day condensed version covering Broome to Mitchell Plateau or Mitchell Plateau to Wyndam.
