Destinations
Fishabout is dedicated to delivering you the ultimate adventure fishing holiday. We are the premier fisherman’s Australian and International fishing travel agent and guide service for anglers worldwide. The fishing destinations we’ve chosen include a range of superior lodges, motherships and camps across Australia and around the world. We can help you chase barramundi through the top end, trout in New Zealand, dog tooth tuna in the South Pacific, black bass in PNG, snapper in NSW, tigerfish on the Zambezi, chinook salmon in British Columbia and a whole lot more.
