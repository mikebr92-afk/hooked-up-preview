Destinations | NT
Across the Top End we offer a range of the best of lodges, camps and motherships. We cover most of Arnhem land, the Tiwi Islands, major rivers like the Daly, Mary and South Alligator, stunning billabongs plus the coast and rivers all the way to the WA border. Barramundi are our favourite sportfish and we can give you access to the best destinations with the best guides during the best seasons and tides.
Whether it be the Mini Mini System in Western Arnhem land which has a reputation as “a land of giants”, Eastern Arnhem land which is fed by the Arafura Swamp making it one of the most exciting fisheries in the country, the Tiwi Islands that mix awesome barra fishing with exciting bluewater options or billabongs full of barra and saratoga, we can get you there.

Featured , Northern Territory 
Endyalgout Island
Endyalgout Island has built its reputation as a big fish mecca, a reputation it may deserves today more than ever. 
Its not just big barra, the biggest threadfin, black jewfish and golden snapper we’ve ever seen come from here 
Northern Territory , Corporate 
Melville Island Lodge
Some of the worlds best estuary and bluewater reef fishing right on your doorstep, with over 50 quality sports and tablefish within close proximity, many less than 10 minutes from the lodge. 
Northern Territory , Corporate 
Dhipirri Lodge
Dhipirri Lodge is without doubt one of the best remote fishing lodges in the country. Nestled in the heart of the Northern Territory's pristine wilderness, Dhipirri Lodge offers an exclusive fishing escape like no other. 
Northern Territory , Corporate 
Arnhem Land Barra Lodge
Barra Lodge has deservedly earnt the reputation as one of the premier barramundi and sportfishing lodges in the country.

Cobourg Bluewater and Barra Mothership
Fish Croker Island, Cobourg Peninsula and some of the Top Ends most spectacular fishing spots onboard a purpose built fishing mothership. 
Northern Territory 
NT Run Off Fishing
The Northern Territory comes alive between March and June as the vast floodplains awaken with the last of the wet season's nourishing rains. During this time, the creeks and rivers become a bustling hub of activity as fish of all sizes, from juveniles to magnificent females weighing over 40 pounds 
Northern Territory , Corporate 
Seven Spirit Bay
Seven Spirit Bay is an awesome fishing lodge but offers so much more for partners who may be more interested in other activities. It delivers the best accommodation and on-site facilities of any lodge in the NT. 
Northern Territory 
Helifishing
Helifishing for barramundi is a real adrenalin experience. You’ll reach destinations that are out of reach by any other form of transport and therefore experience no fishing pressure. 
Northern Territory 
Corroboree Billabong
Corroboree is part of the stunning Mary River Wetlands that deliver breathtaking beauty and unique wildlife. It’s home to the largest saltwater crocodile congregation in the world, provides critical breeding grounds for magpie geese and is home to 280 species of birds including jabiru, brolga and flocks of black cockatoo making it a haven for photographers and bird watchers alike. 
Northern Territory 
Cobourg Coastal Camp
Fishing tours are based out of a unique safari-style coastal camp overlooking the bay of Port Essington, with numerous fishing options nearby 
Northern Territory 
Cannon Charters
Fish some of the most remote and unspoiled fishing spots around the spectacular coast of Australia’s Top End aboard the “MV Cannon”. Trips combine fantastic fishing opportunities with great scenery and the very best of luxury travel.
