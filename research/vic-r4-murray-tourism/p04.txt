Fishing
World-Class Angling
Lake Eildon is one of Victoria's premier fishing destinations, renowned for trophy trout, Murray cod, and golden perch. Whether you're a seasoned angler or trying your luck for the first time, the lake offers exceptional fishing opportunities year-round.
Highlights
• Trophy-sized rainbow and brown trout 
• Murray cod up to 100cm+ 
• Golden perch (yellowbelly) 
• Redfin and macquarie perch 
• Excellent shore and boat fishing 
• Eildon Pondage for trophy trout 
Local tips
· A Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence is required for all anglers 18 and over
· Best trout fishing is during cooler months (April-October)
· Murray cod season runs December 1 to August 31
· Early morning and late evening are prime fishing times
· Try trolling for trout along the dam wall and deeper channels
· Shore fishing at Eildon Pondage is excellent for families
Popular locations
Eildon Pondage
Stocked with trophy-sized rainbow trout. Excellent shore access and family-friendly.
Jerusalem Creek
Popular arm of the lake for trout and redfin fishing.
Big River Arm
Excellent for Murray cod and golden perch.
