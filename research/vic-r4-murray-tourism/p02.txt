Swan Hill
Welcome to the Swan Hill area, a vibrant agricultural hub centred around the charming city of Swan Hill, just 3.5 hours from Melbourne and 5.5 hours from Adelaide. This sunny destination, with an average temperature of 23°C, offers a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and regional charm. The friendly locals and strong community spirit make visitors feel right at home, whether you’re exploring historic townships, enjoying a drink at a country pub, or learning about the rich Indigenous heritage of the Latji Latji, Wemba Wemba, Barapa Barapa, Tati Tai, and Wadi Wadi Nations.
Snapshot of Things To Do In Swan Hill
• Spoons 
Enjoy delicious food and stunning Murray River views at this popular cafe and restaurant. Perfect for a relaxing breakfast and lunch.
• 
Pioneer Settlement 
Relive history at the Pioneer Settlement, a re-created riverside village showcasing life along the Murray in the 19th and early 20th centuries. 
• Laser Light Spectacular 
Be mesmerised by the award-winning Heartbeat of the Murray laser light show, a dazzling celebration of the region’s history, culture, and natural beauty.
• 
Paddle Steamer 
Take a relaxing cruise along the Murray River on PS Pyap, an historic paddle steamer and learn about the river’s significance to the region.
• Murray Downs Golf & Country Club 
Tee off at this championship golf course and enjoy a round surrounded by picturesque scenery. Challenge yourself and then enjoy the club's facilities.
Art & Culture 
Explore the Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery (currently under redevelopment - temporarily located with a much smaller display at the Swan Hill Library), which houses incredible collections of Indigenous and contemporary art. Step back in time at the Pioneer Settlement, a re-created riverside village showcasing life along the Murray in the 19th and early 20th centuries. Don’t miss the ‘Heartbeat of the Murray’ or the ‘Legends of the Mallee’ laser light show, a spectacular celebration of the region’s history and natural beauty.
Tracks & Trails 
Stroll along Riverside Park , where lush greenery meets the Murray River, or explore the picturesque Murray Downs Track, perfect for walking and cycling. For a unique adventure, take a drive along Pental Island Road, offering stunning river views and opportunities to spot native wildlife.
Outdoor Activities 
Play a round at the world-class Murray Downs Golf and Country Club , renowned for its challenging layout, pristine greens, and stunning Murray River views. Recently, Murray Downs gained national recognition as the host of the NSW Golf Open 2024, solidifying its reputation as one of Australia’s premier golfing destinations. For a more relaxed experience, take a leisurely paddle steamer cruise along the Murray River, soaking in the serene scenery and abundant wildlife.

Food & Drink 
Indulge in authentic Thai cuisine at Java Spice , enjoy Japanese-inspired dishes at Niko Niko, or sip on expertly crafted coffee at Arc Espresso. For a taste of local produce, visit the region’s markets and wineries, or dine along the banks of the Murray River at Spoons Riverside. The annual Food & Wine Festival is a highlight, showcasing the best of the region’s gourmet offerings. Embark on the Swan Hill Pub Grub Trail , a delightful road trip radiating from Swan Hill, exploring charming towns and their unique pubs offering classic fare and warm hospitality.

We acknowledge the Traditional Custodians of the land and waters that surround the Murray River. We acknowledge and respect their history, culture and continuous connection to country.
