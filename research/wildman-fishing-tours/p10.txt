Groups
Looking for a unique team-building experience?
Let us organise a fishing tournament for your next team-building event. Chase prized Barramundi in one of the Northern Territory’s best fishing destinations aboard a fleet of premium fishing boats.
We provide all the fishing equipment, guides and on-board meals & refreshments. We also set out the guidelines for your fishing tournament, judging as well as a presentation at the end of the competition.
What’s included ? 
• Fishing vessels
• Fishing gear
• Professional fishing guide
• Team shirts & caps
• Lunch & refreshments
• Rules and guidelines
• Scoring and presentation
• Transfers
• Accommodation
• Meals &beverages
Want to step it up a notch? 
At Wildman Fishing Tours we are all about creating awesome experiences and have access to all types of vehicles, including helicopters, small planes and buses. If you would prefer your guests to arrive in Rockstar style, why not book a scenic flight to take them directly to the fishing action or their accommodation.
Accommodation 
Choose from various accommodation styles to suit your budget. Options include everything from outback luxury to mid-range lodges.
Optional extra’s 
Depending on the tastes of your group event, we can also arrange some exciting additional tour extra’s, such as:
Master class on Barramundi preparation and cooking
• Airboat and river cruises
• Bushwalking & bush tucker demonstrations
• Gourmet picnic hampers and themed dinner events
Let us do the planning and deliver an awesome experience to be had by all.
