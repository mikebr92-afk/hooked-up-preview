2 DAY BARRAMUNDI FISHING + ACCOMMODATION 
The ultimate escape for 2 days on the water to get in some serious Barramundi Fishing, with accommodation and meals taken care of.
Chase wild Barramundi as well as massive Threadfin Salmon and Jewfish.
Our expert guide combined with a comfortable performance boat will ensure you have a great time on the water. You’ll be fishing from a 7.8m Raysea boat that was custom built to suit the conditions.
Whether you’re a lure fisherman, fly fisherman, or eco-adventurer, we’ve got you covered and all fishing gear is provided.

• 2 Days Fishing

• Meals

PACKAGE COST
• Accommodation Option 1 (Liveaboard) $1,770 per person
• Accommodation Option 2 (Landside) $1,620 per person
See below for details of accommodation options.
ACCOMMODATION OPTIONS
Option 1: Barra Boatel – Liveaboard Experience 
Immerse yourself in the ultimate fishing adventure with our exclusive liveaboard accommodation aboard the Barra Boatel. Situated at Shady Camp, just opposite Sampan, this 70ft mothership offers a unique and comfortable stay for avid anglers. Our liveaboard experience includes:
• Air-conditioned Twin and Quad Rooms 
• Full Kitchen and Dining Facilities 
• Onboard Showers and Amenities 
• Comfortable Lounge Area 
• WIFI Access 
After a hard day’s fishing, the Barra Boatel provides the perfect setting to rejuvenate, share fishing tales, and prepare for another exciting day on the water.
Option 2: Landside Accommodation – Cabin Style Rooms 
For those preferring solid ground, our landside accommodation option is just a short 40-minute drive from Shady Camp. Stay in comfortable cabin rooms with modern amenities, including:
• Air-conditioned Rooms 
• In-room Fridge 
• Ensuite Facilities 
• Access to Restaurant and Bar Facilities 
This option provides a convenient and comfortable base for your fishing expedition, combining the thrill of angling with the comforts of a cozy stay.

Location
Choose from the following destinations 
Shady Camp , located on the Mary River, approximately 2.5hrs drive from Darwin.
Your fishing tour will include 4WD transfers for up to 6 people to and from your fishing destination on the day of fishing from Darwin CBD and surrounds.
Your pick-up time will depend on the tide times, and you will be notified at the time of the booking.
SELF DRIVE
Self-driving to Shady Camp is easy and stress-free. After leaving the airport, you head along the Arnhem Highway, which is a long a smooth journey that will take you to the Point Stuart Road turnoff. After a short 40kms on Point Stuart Road, you will hit a graded dirt road, which you should follow for a further 10kms which then turns left and directly towards Shady Camp for another 15k. Shady Camp has free parking, camping and toilet facilities.
Google Map, Darwin to Shady Camp, link, here .
When self-driving from Darwin to Shady Camp, there are 2 petrol stations on the Arnhem Highway. The first is Corroboree Park Tavern and the second The Bark Hut Inn . If driving from Jabiru, we recommend refueling before leaving, as the closest petrol station is Corroboree Park Tavern. There are no petrol or store facilities at Shady Camp or close by.
AIR TRANSFERS
For a helicopter or light aircraft transfers, both options depart from the Darwin Airport .
A helicopter from Darwin Airport to Mary River will take approximately 1 hour and light aircraft, approximately ½ hour.
Minimum passenger numbers and weight limitations apply. Additional costs apply.
