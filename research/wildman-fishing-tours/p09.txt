Fishing and Feasting Package
The Northern Territory is renowned for its hard-hitting Barramundi and massive mud crabs. Not only are they seriously fun to fish for, but when cooked right, they are an absolute delight for food lovers.
In this package, you will spend a day fishing, chasing wild Barramundi, and dropping a few mud crab pots for good luck. You will also get to savour the rewards of your efforts at one of Darwin’s top restaurants, Pink Chopstix, where the local chefs will prepare a seafood spread fit for a king or queen.
Our expert guide combined with our comfortable performance boat will ensure you have a great time on the water fishing at either the Mary, Finniss rivers or Darwin Harbour . Drinks and snacks are all included, as well as your picnic lunch. 
After fishing, dinner is included. We’ve secured your table at the Pink Choptix, where you’ll savour a delicious spread, featuring your Barramundi or mud crab if you were lucky enough to catch one.
Fish all day, and feast all night like a true Territorian! 
DESTINATIONS
Choose from The Mary River, Shady Camp, the Finniss River, or Darwin Harbour.
Pink Choptstix at the Waterfront, Darwin
