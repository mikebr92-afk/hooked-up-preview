Fishing
Hook up one of our professional fishing guides and head out for some serious fishing in the Top End waterways.
Chase wild Barramundi, Jewfish, Threadfin Salmon, Mangrove Jack, Trevally, Coral Trout and Spanish Mackerel and more.
We’ll let you know which destinations are hitting and you can choose from the Mary River, Shady Camp, Finniss River or Darwin Harbour and surrounds.
Fishing options include:
• Barramundi Fishing 
• Fishing & Charter Flight Option 
• Top End Bluewater Fishing 
The Wildman Boat 
Our 7.8m custom-built boat is perfect for 2 -6 people. It’s got plenty of grunt with a Honda BF250 engine, a top of the range Lowrance fishfinder and an awesome selection of pro fishing equipment. Relax as you head out on padded seats and enjoy the shade when needed, retractable for when the serious fishing starts.
Why Fish in the Northern Territory? 
The Northern Territory has long been renowned for its pristine natural environment and exceptional fishing locations. Prize Barramundi, hard-hitting Jewfish and massive Threadfin Salmon are just a few of its delights. To top this, endless rivers, vast floodplains and spectacular escarpment make for the perfect backdrop to the ultimate fishing experience.
