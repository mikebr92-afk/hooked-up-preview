Are you ready to go hunt down the Million Dollar Fish at the coordinates where tagged Barramundi were dropped? 
Join Wildman Fishing Tours for the fishing trip of a lifetime. Fish at the published coordinates where the tagged Barramundi, one of which could potentially be the Million Dollar Fish, were dropped, in and around Darwin Harbour by the Million Dollar Fish crew. 
On this 5 hour fishing experience, you will be fishing from our 7.8m Raysea boat that was custom-built and powered by our Honda BF250 engine, to get you straight to the coordinates without a minute to spare. 
There’s enough room for 6 guests and to add something extra for comfort, there’s also a canopy and an esky to keep your drinks and food fresh and cold. 
All you need to do now is register for Season 9 of Million Dollar Fish. 
Your Vessel – 7.8m Raysea boat custom built – Seats up to 6 people – Onboard shade & portable toilet 
Location You will be fishing in the Darwin Harbour, at known coordinates as published by the Million Dollar Fish Crew. Your fishing tour will include 4WD transfers from Darwin CBD and surrounds. Your pick up time will depend on the tide times and you will be notified at the time of the booking. 
What to bring – Long-sleeved shirt and pants, preferably UV rated – Wide-brimmed hat – 50+ sunscreen – Sunglasses, preferably polarized – Neck scarf – Preferably closed-toe shoes or sandals – Insect repellent. Please note this tour is not exclusive. If you wish to have the boat to yourself, please enquire about our private tours. 
• Cost $485pp, min 2 
• Includes Drinks and refreshments 
And be sure to register for your chance to win before you join us for your day’s fishing!
The following information is from the Million Dollar Fish Webpage, (link for more): 
Any type of fisho can reel in the Million Dollar Fish, and there are plenty of ways to win. 
SEASON 9 
The Million Dollar Fish Season 9 runs from 1 October 2023 and concludes at 11:59pm on 31 March 2024.
In addition to the tagged barramundi released across the Territory, the competition also includes a Lucky Prize Pool. Just by registering, participants have the chance to win great prizes throughout the season…( link to register )
