Bluewater Fishing Adventure: Chasing Barramundi and Pelagics 
Discover the heart-pounding excitement of bluewater fishing with Wildman Fishing Tours. Our expert guides will take you along the stunning Darwin shorelines, providing an unforgettable experience chasing Barramundi and pelagic species. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or a first-timer, we cater to groups of all sizes, ensuring a fantastic day on the water filled with thrills and unforgettable catches.
Multiple Departure Points: Darwin Harbour or Dundee Beach 
Choose the backdrop for your adventure with departure options from Darwin Harbour or the serene Dundee Beach. Our skilled guides know the best spots to maximize your chances of reeling in the big ones, creating a tailor-made experience for your group.
Customizable Packages: Just Bluewater or Ultimate Darwin Fishing Tour 
At Wildman Fishing Tours, we understand that every angler has unique preferences. That’s why we offer flexible packages to suit your needs. Opt for a focused Bluewater Fishing trip or elevate your experience with our Ultimate Darwin Fishing Tour. This comprehensive package includes both Bluewater and Freshwater fishing, ensuring a diverse and thrilling angling experience.
All-Inclusive Options: Accommodation and Transfers 
Make your fishing adventure stress-free with our all-inclusive options. Our packages can be customised to include accommodation and transfers, allowing you to focus solely on the excitement of the catch.
Personalized Assistance: Contact Our Reservations Team 
Whatever your fishing needs, our dedicated reservations team is here to assist you. Reach out to us, and we’ll help you organize the perfect fishing getaway, tailored to your preferences and group size.
Prepare for an adventure of a lifetime with Wildman Fishing Tours. Book your Bluewater Fishing experience now and immerse yourself in the thrill of the Top End’s incredible fishing opportunities!
Along with tour booking conditions please ensure you understand our websites Terms & Conditions.

Location
We have a number of fishing destinations to choose from. They including Shady Camp (salt water side), Dundee, Darwin Harbour and surrounds.
Shady Camp is the premier destination for both fresh and saltwater Barramundi fishing. Shady Camp is located on the Mary River, approximately 2.5hrs drive from Darwin.
Getting to Shady Camp includes both drive and charter flight options.
Accommodation options at Shady Camp include camping or 3.5 – 4 star outback accommodation. Our reservations team will guide you on options at the time of booking.
Dundee Beach is located approximately 1.5hrs drive from Darwin on the Southwest side.
There is plenty to see and do here, including Barramundi, Bluewater Pelagic sport or Reef fishing.
Getting to Dundee Beach includes both drive and helicopter options.
Accommodation options at Dundee Beach include camping or 3.5 – 4 star outback accommodation. Our reservations team will guide you on options at the time of booking.
