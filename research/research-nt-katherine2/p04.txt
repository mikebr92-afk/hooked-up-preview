Pack the kids and your sense of adventure, Katherine is the Top End’s playground. Paddle or cruise beneath the soaring cliffs of Nitmiluk Gorge, splash about in the Katherine Hot Springs and shaded rock pools at Leliyn, wander glittering chambers in Cutta Cutta Caves, and cheer on working dogs and horses at a true-blue outback show.
Easy trails, big skies and bigger smiles mean no screens required, just wildlife encounters, stargazing, and stories you’ll be telling long after the car is unpacked. An easy drive from Darwin, it’s the spot where family weekends become legendary. Pack the snacks, load the playlist, and let Katherine turn “Are we there yet?” into “Can we stay longer?”
