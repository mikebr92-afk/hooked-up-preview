Home Portfolio Item Fishing Adventure 
fishing adventure 
3 nights | 4 days 
Experience the best of Tiwi fishing with three unforgettable days exploring the island’s diverse fishing grounds. From bluewater reef fishing offshore to estuary fishing for barramundi and beach fishing at sunset , this all-inclusive adventure is designed for anglers of all experience levels. 
Between fishing sessions, relax in beautifully appointed accommodation, enjoy fresh local cuisine and unwind with a cold drink as the sun sets over the Arafura Sea. 
3 NIGHTS | 4 DAYS | BLUEWATER CHARTER | ESTUARY CHARTER | BEACH FISHING | ALL-INCLUSIVE

Inclusions 
• Beautifully appointed accommodation 
• Return charter flight to Darwin 
• Guided bluewater fishing charter 
• Guided estuary fishing charter 
• Guided beach buggy fishing experience 
• Boat transfers between airstrip and Retreat 
• All meals, including sunset canapés and cocktails 
• Premium beer, wine and selected spirits

A TASTE OF TIWI 
Every Tiwi escape is a little different.
Here’s a glimpse of how your days might unfold, with experiences shaped by the weather, tides and conditions.
DAY 1 
Arrive & settle into island life 
Charter flight from Darwin Lunch at the Retreat Beach Buggy Fishing Sunset drinks & canapés Dinner
DAY 2 
Chase the big ones 
Breakfast Guided Bluewater Fishing Charter Lunch at the Retreat Time to relax & explore the island Sunset drinks & canapés Beach dinner & bonfire
DAY 3 
Fish, explore & unwind 
Breakfast Guided Estuary Fishing Charter Lunch at the Retreat Free time to relax & enjoy the island Sunset drinks & canapés
DAY 4 
One last taste of Tiwi 
Breakfast Final morning to relax & explore Charter flight back to Darwin
