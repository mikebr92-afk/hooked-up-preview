Tiwi Island Retreat
Add to my trip 
Show all photos 
Escape to a remote, natural paradise where you’re completely off the grid and anything is possible.
Set amongst a pristine marine sanctuary, Tiwi Island Retreat is one of Australia’s premier barefoot destinations. 
Hidden away on the western edge of Bathurst Island, the Retreat offers a spectacular view of the exclusive beachfront location, and provides the perfect base to enjoy the Retreat’s inclusive package options that includes; world-class fishing adventures, Indigenous culture tours, and unique wildlife encounters.
Offering four unique styles of accommodation with Village Rooms, Ocean Rooms, Family Ocean Room and Luxury Glamping Tents. 
The Village Rooms are best suited to groups or families wanting a more casual stay. The interiors are fresh and simple with a range of bedding configurations including; twin king single rooms, queen rooms and family rooms. The Village Room quarters feature a large deck with an outdoor sitting area, and shared shower / toilet facilities.
The Ocean Rooms feature queen beds with crisp white linen facing out onto sweeping views across Port Hurd with a private ensuite. Each Ocean Room welcomes guests with chic interiors and luxurious finishes, that transport you to the very best in island relaxation.
Tiwi Island Retreat is a 30 minute flight from Darwin with a minimum two night stay. It is also available for private group bookings and as a wedding venue.
