Escape to a remote, natural paradise where you’re completely off the grid and anything is possible. Set amongst a pristine marine sanctuary, Tiwi Island Retreat is one of Australia’s premier barefoot destinations. Hidden away on the western edge of Bathurst Island, the Retreat offers spectacular views of the exclusive beachfront location, and provides the perfect base to enjoy the Retreat’s inclusive package options that include; world-class fishing adventures, Indigenous culture tours, and unique wildlife encounters.
Discover more 
Relaxed barefoot living 
The Retreat’s architecture references a contemporary beach shack vibe and offers accommodation including; relaxed group rooms, ensuited ocean rooms suitable for couples, and luxuriously appointed beachfront glamping tents.

inclusive
package options
Our inclusive packages have been thoughtfully designed to offer you the very best of the Tiwi Islands. Design your stay by choosing from our various accommodation and adventure options. All packages include; transfers, meals, drinks – and a couple of surprises in-between.

Experience The territory’s Best 
From our world-class fishing adventures to being immersed in the Tiwi Islands rich Indigenous culture, we offer some of the Northern Territory’s most memorable experiences.
