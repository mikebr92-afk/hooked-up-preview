Whether you’re planning a full day on the reef or the fishing trip of a lifetime, FNQ Fishing Adventures has a charter to suit you. Choose from private one-day reef fishing charters , extended multi-day adventures, specialised topwater GT fishing trips , or light tackle sportfishing for black marlin, sailfish and other pelagics. Every trip is tailored to the conditions, your experience and the style of fishing you want to do, giving you the best possible chance to experience the incredible fishing Far North Queensland is famous for.
Our charters are also perfect for families, first-time anglers and small private groups , with a maximum of just 4 anglers on board. From relaxed Great Barrier Reef fishing for coral trout, red emperor and nannygai to fast-paced jigging, trolling and topwater fishing, there’s something for everyone — whether you’re introducing the kids to reef fishing or chasing trophy fish with experienced mates.
Looking for the right trip? Choose from our range of private adventures, including Cairns reef fishing charters , GT and topwater fishing , light tackle marlin and pelagic fishing , multi-day fishing trips , and Cairns barramundi fishing charters .
