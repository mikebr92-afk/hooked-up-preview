WHY FISH WITH US 
At FNQ Fishing Adventures, EFFORT and PASSION come first. Every charter is private, personal and built around giving you the best possible fishing experience Far North Queensland has to offer — whether that means casting poppers at trophy GTs, trolling the Great Barrier Reef for marlin and pelagics, dropping baits for quality reef fish or chasing barramundi through tropical waterways.
For our offshore adventures, we operate a purpose-built 6.5m centre cab , giving our small private groups a comfortable and capable platform for Cairns reef fishing, topwater GT fishing and light-tackle game fishing across the Great Barrier Reef. With a maximum of just four anglers, there’s more room to fish, more time on the rod and the freedom to tailor the day around your group.
For our inshore and barramundi fishing, our 4.5m Polycraft is set up specifically for fishing tight water, structure and productive tropical systems. Equipped with LiveScope technology , it gives us the ability to actively locate fish, watch how they respond to lures and make adjustments in real time — creating a more specialised and premium fishing experience.
From first-time anglers and families to serious sportfishers chasing a bucket-list GT, marlin or trophy reef fish, our goal is simple: work hard, fish hard and give every group a day on the water they’ll be talking about for years.

MEET THE TEAM
BRODIE QUAAS
Deciding to go all-in on his dream, Brodie turned his love for fishing into his livelihood. Moving from NSW to QLD with no real experience fishing the waters of Far North Queensland, he committed himself to learning the reef, the rivers and every body of water in the surrounding areas of Cairns. Having now caught plenty of trophy fish himself across the different species, Brodie’s passion now lies in giving you the fishing trip of a lifetime!
FOUNDER & HEAD GUIDE

That, with his years of experience fishing for barra’s, marlin, GT’s etc., is exactly why he’s the perfect fit for your charter. CANCELLATION POLICY
• Weather Cancellations: If a charter is cancelled by FNQ Fishing Adventures due to unsafe or adverse weather conditions, guests will receive a full refund or may choose to reschedule at no additional cost.
• Guest Cancellations: If a guest cancels a booking for any reason, a voucher equal to the amount paid (including deposits and full payments) will be issued. This voucher can be used to reschedule a future charter date .
• Late Cancellations: If a booking is cancelled within 21 days of the scheduled charter date and a new date cannot be arranged, all payments will be forfeited .
