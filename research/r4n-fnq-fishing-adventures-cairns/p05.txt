FNQ Fishing Adventures gives anglers access to some of the most exciting Great Barrier Reef fishing off Port Douglas , targeting everything from explosive topwater Giant Trevally and light-tackle black marlin to prized reef species like coral trout, red emperor and nannygai .
Our Port Douglas reef fishing charters are built around the incredible variety this part of Far North Queensland has to offer. Chase trophy GTs on poppers and stickbaits, troll reef edges and current lines for black marlin, sailfish, Spanish mackerel, wahoo and mahi mahi, or fish productive reef country with baits and jigs for some of the best reef fishing Port Douglas has to offer.
For serious sportfishers, our topwater GT fishing charters from Port Douglas can deliver unforgettable action, while light-tackle trolling gives anglers the chance to experience screaming runs, aerial billfish and hard-hitting pelagics. For families, first-time anglers and anyone who simply loves catching quality reef fish, our Great Barrier Reef fishing charters from Port Douglas are a perfect way to experience the reef.
Every trip is private with a maximum of 4 anglers , giving you more room, more fishing time and a personalised day on the water. Whether you’re chasing your first coral trout, a bucket-list black marlin or a trophy GT, FNQ Fishing Adventures showcases the very best of Port Douglas reef fishing and Great Barrier Reef sportfishing .
