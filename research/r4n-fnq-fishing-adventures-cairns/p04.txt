Cairns reef fishing charters don’t get more authentic than this. 
FNQ Fishing Adventures delivers a true Great Barrier Reef sportfishing experience , targeting everything from explosive topwater Giant Trevally and light-tackle black marlin to prized reef species like coral trout, red emperor and nannygai .
Our Cairns fishing charters are built around the incredible variety the reef has to offer. Chase trophy GT’s on poppers and stickbaits, troll reef edges and current lines for black marlin, sailfish, Spanish mackerel, wahoo and mahi mahi, or drop baits and jigs into productive reef country for some of the best reef fishing Cairns has to offer.
For the serious sportfisher, our topwater GT fishing charters deliver some unforgettable action, with the right conditions producing incredible numbers of Giant Trevally. For anglers chasing pelagics, light-tackle trolling can turn into chaos in seconds when a marlin, sailfish or big Spanish mackerel lights up the spread. And for families, first-time anglers or anyone who simply loves catching quality fish, our Great Barrier Reef fishing charters for coral trout, red emperor and nannygai are hard to beat.
Every trip is private with a maximum of 4 anglers , giving you more room, more fishing time and a personalised day on the water. From your first coral trout to a bucket-list black marlin or trophy GT, FNQ Fishing Adventures showcases the very best of Cairns reef fishing and Great Barrier Reef sportfishing .
