OFFSHORE
Big Cat Reality
Big Cat Reality is a 25-metre live-aboard charter vessel operated by Reality Fishing Charters out of Burnett Heads, Bundaberg. It’s purpose-built for serious long-range reef and sports-fishing adventures, comfortably accommodating up to 21 guests with air-conditioned bunk cabins, spacious dining/lounge areas, multiple showers, and heaps of deck space. The boat carries seven dedicated 4.3m fishing tenders, giving anglers the flexibility to fish smaller reefs, bommies and shoals each morning and afternoon before returning to the mother ship.
• 
Big Cat Charters Offered:
• 7-Day Swains Reef Line Fishing Adventures – their most popular trip targeting remote reef systems with very little pressure.
• 9-Day Coral Sea Sports Fishing Expeditions – heading to ultra-remote locations like Cato Reef, Wreck Reef, Kenn Reef and other Coral Sea atolls.
• 3-Day Bunker Group Trips – shorter, action-packed local reef adventures for anglers wanting a taste of the Big Cat experience.
• 
Gear Requirements – BYO: 
Big Cat Reality is strictly bring your own fishing gear. Anglers bring their own rods, reels, tackle, terminal gear, jigs, soft plastics and any specialised setups they prefer. This is a major part of why Big Cat trips appeal to serious anglers - everyone sets up exactly how they want, from heavy jigging outfits to bottom bashing combos. The crew provide tender fuel, bait storage and all fish-handling, but all gear is fully BYO.
Species You Can Expect to Catch: 
The fishing varies from classic reef bottom-bashing to high-energy sportfishing. Common catches include coral trout, red throat emperor, red emperor, spangled emperor, tusk fish, job fish, cobia, hussar and other classic reef staples. On Coral Sea trips, anglers often encounter giant trevally, wahoo, yellowfin tuna, dogtooth tuna, spanish mackerel and other pelagic species. The tender setup allows you to chase whatever suits the conditions - jigging, trolling, bottom fishing, casting or topwater.
On-Board Food & Amenities: 
One of the biggest highlights of a Big Cat Reality trip is the onboard catering. A qualified chef prepares cooked breakfasts, hearty lunches, fresh snacks and afternoon tea, and delicious nightly dinners—plus a themed Seafood Night during the trip. Meals are generous, varied and designed to keep you fuelled for big days on the water. The boat is fully licensed with a bar-tab system, and guests can relax in air-conditioned comfort between sessions, enjoy dessert after dinner, or unwind on the deck as the boat steams to the next location.
The Overall Experience: 
A trip on Big Cat Reality is a full immersion into remote reef fishing. You fish sunrise to sunset if you want, sleep in comfort, eat like a king, and explore reef systems that most people will never see. The crew take care of everything—cleaning, filleting and cryo-vac sealing your catch; running tenders; keeping everyone safe; and ensuring the trip runs smoothly. For many anglers it’s a bucket-list live-aboard experience combining adventure, good company, serious fishing and great food.
For a more detailed overview of the Big Cat Reality experience or to make any bookings or enquires, visit their website here

OFFSHORE
Truansea Charters
Truansea Charters operates out of Burnett Heads in Bundaberg and specialises in small-group offshore reef fishing. Their two main reef trips - a full-day and a half-day option - are designed to give anglers access to quality local reef systems with a personalised, hands-on experience. The boat typically carries a maximum of around six anglers, meaning plenty of space, one-on-one guidance, and a relaxed atmosphere compared to large multi-party charters.
• 
Full-Day Reef Charter: 
This is a roughly 10-hour offshore mission targeting prime Bundaberg reef species. Depending on weather and conditions, the crew may travel anywhere from the closer inshore and mid-shelf grounds right out to the wider reefs up to 50 nautical miles offshore. Anglers commonly chase coral trout, red emperor, sweetlip, tusk fish, hussar and other classic reef staples. All rods, reels, bait and tackle are provided, and the crew offer help with techniques, rigging and fish handling throughout the day. Bottled water and soft drinks are supplied, and guests can bring their own lunch and snacks. It’s a full day on the water built for anglers who want quality fishing and personalised service without a big-boat vibe.
Half-Day Reef Charter: 
The half-day option is a shorter 4–5 hour trip designed for those wanting a taste of reef fishing without committing to a long offshore run. These trips usually stay closer to shore, focusing on productive shallow reefs, rubble patches and nearby artificial wrecks. Anglers can still expect a mix of reef species like those mentioned in the full day charters. All gear, bait and tackle are supplied, making it a great choice for families, beginners, visitors passing through Bundy, or anyone wanting a lighter offshore session.
Overall Experience: 
Both reef trips offer a friendly, down-to-earth, small-group environment run by an experienced skipper who knows the Bundaberg reef systems intimately. With all tackle provided, no bar crossing to worry about, and a focus on customer experience, Truansea Charters delivers a relaxed but productive day chasing quality reef fish in the waters just off Bundaberg.
If you would like any more information about a Truansea charter, visit their website here. Alternatively, you can give Luke a call on 0423 015 490

RIVERS 
Bundaberg River Fishing Charters 
The Experience 
Bundaberg River Fishing Charters offers calm-water, family-friendly estuary fishing on the Burnett River, perfect for beginners, kids, families and anyone wanting an easy-going day on the water. Trips are relaxed, informative and hands-on, with all bait, tackle and equipment supplied so guests can simply step aboard and start fishing.
Charters Offered 
Bundaberg River Fishing Charters provides a range of estuary-focused trips tailored to different groups and timeframes:
• Half-Day River Charter – A relaxed 4-hour session exploring the river’s mangrove banks, rock walls and sand flats.
• Full-Day River Charter – A longer estuary adventure allowing more time to move between spots, change techniques and target a wider range of species.
• Custom/Private River Charters – Flexible sessions that can be tailored for families, beginners, children, or more experienced anglers wanting specific styles of fishing or certain species.
Target Species 
Depending on the season, tides and locations fished, anglers may encounter whiting, bream, flathead, mangrove jack, cod, grunter, queenfish and blue salmon. The skipper adjusts techniques and areas throughout the session to suit conditions and maximise productivity.
On-Board Experience 
The vessel is stable, comfortable and set up for small groups, making it ideal for kids and new anglers. All gear is included, and the skipper offers constant guidance on casting, baiting, lure technique and reading the river. It’s an easy, stress-free way to enjoy Bundy’s estuary system without needing any prior experience or equipment.
Why It Stands Out 
Bundaberg River Fishing Charters is perfect for families, visitors and first timers wanting a smooth, approachable fishing experience without heading offshore. With small groups, personalised attention and all gear supplied, it delivers an enjoyable and educational day on the Burnett River.
If you would like any more information about a Bundaberg River Fishing charter, visit their website here. Alternatively, you can give Troy a call on 0419 767 396

Lake Monduran 
Flick-It Fishing Charters with Terry Allwood 
Guide: Terry Allwood 
The Experience 
Flick It Fishing Charters also delivers one of the most advanced, technique focused barramundi experiences on Lake Monduran. Guided by tournament angler and barra expert Terry Allwood, these charters revolve around reading conditions, working structure efficiently, and using modern lure techniques and forward facing sonar to consistently target large impoundment barra. It’s a premium charter built for anglers who want genuine coaching, high level insights and the best possible shot at trophy fish.
Charters Offered 
Flick It Fishing Charters provides a range of lure only barra sessions tailored to maximise Monduran’s unique conditions:
• Half-Day Barra Charter – A focused session built around prime bite windows, ideal for anglers wanting high-quality guiding in limited time.
• Full-Day Barra Charter – A complete barra fishing experience covering multiple areas of the lake with technique adjustments throughout the session.
Target Species 
The entire experience centres on targeting Monduran’s iconic impoundment barramundi, with metre plus fish a genuine possibility every trip. Terry’s understanding of seasonal movements, water temperature fluctuations, pressure changes and under water structure gives anglers a strategic advantage when chasing these larger, more experienced fish.
On-Board Experience 
These trips are premium, small group sessions run with a technique first approach. Terry provides high end rods, reels and proven lures suited to the current bite. His boat is set up specifically for casting, equipped with advanced electronics - including forward facing sonar - to pinpoint fish, track movements and guide precise lure presentations. Throughout the day, Terry coaches casting accuracy, lure control, retrieve cadence and how to work different structure types effectively.
Marine Electronics Dial-In Sessions (Your Boat) 
A unique premium service Terry offers is on-your-boat marine electronics fine tuning. He will jump onboard your vessel to optimise your sounder setup, ensure the transducer placement is correct, adjust settings for clarity, teach you how to read your sonar properly, and help you get the most out of modern technologies such as side scan, down scan and forward facing sonar. This personalised setup service ensures your system is dialled in perfectly - saving you time, improving your fish finding ability, and giving you confidence in your electronics every time you hit the water.
Why It Stands Out 
Flick It Fishing Charters is one of the most effective ways to accelerate your progression as a barra angler. Terry’s combination of tournament experience, deep local knowledge and real teaching ability creates a premium, technique-driven experience that delivers far more than just a day on the water. Whether you’re chasing a trophy fish at Lake Monduran or wanting your own electronics dialled to perfection, this charter provides exceptional value for anglers who take their fishing seriously.
If you would like any more information about a Flick It Fishing charter, you can visit their Instagram here and Facebook here. Alternatively, you can give Terry a call on 0400 860 122

Impoundment Barra Sport Fishing 
Guide: Jake Fairweather 
The Experience 
Impoundment Barra Sportsfishing, guided by Jake Fairweather, delivers a premium and highly technical barramundi experience on Lake Monduran. Jake specialises in modern lure fishing and provides a skill based charter for anglers who want more than a casual day on the water. His sessions focus on understanding barra behaviour, reading conditions, analysing structure and using precise lure presentations with forward facing sonar to target trophy impoundment fish.
Charters Offered 
Impoundment Barra Sportsfishing offers a range of barra sessions designed around bite windows, seasonal movements and advanced lure work:
• Half Day Barra Charter - A focused session built around prime conditions, perfect for anglers wanting high quality coaching in a shorter timeframe
• Full Day Barra Charter - A complete sportsfishing experience that allows Jake to work multiple zones, shift techniques through the day and fish both morning and afternoon bite periods
• Technique Specific Barra Sessions - Fully custom trips aimed at developing advanced skills such as swimbaiting, jerkbaiting, fishing timber, suspending lure work or using forward facing sonar to target active fish
Target Species 
The focus is entirely on Lake Monduran’s powerful impoundment barramundi. These sessions are built to target quality fish using smart lure strategies and accurate casting. Jake’s approach is all about sportsfishing, with realistic chances of connecting to metre plus fish throughout the year.
On Board Experience 
Jake’s boat is set up for serious lure casting, equipped with premium electronics, a comfortable casting deck, quality rods and reels, and a handpicked range of proven barra lures. Throughout the session, Jake coaches cast angles, retrieve timing, sounder interpretation, structure mapping and strike triggering techniques. Every part of the experience is tailored to anglers wanting to fish smarter and target bigger fish with confidence.
Why It Stands Out 
Impoundment Barra Sportsfishing is a top choice for anglers wanting a high performance guided barra session on Lake Monduran. Jake Fairweather combines modern lure knowledge, advanced technology and deep local understanding to give guests the best possible shot at trophy barra. Whether you are chasing a personal best or looking to improve your impoundment fishing skills, this charter provides a polished and highly personalised day on the water.
If you would like any more information about an Impoundment Barra Sport Fishing charter, you can visit their Instagram here and Facebook here . Alternatively, you can give Jake a call on 0474 496 709

Mondy Man Fishing Charters 
Guide: Sam Rowley 
The Experience 
Mondy Man Fishing Charters, guided by respected local barra angler Sam Rowley, offers a dedicated and highly focused barramundi experience on Lake Monduran. Sam is known for his calm teaching style, strong understanding of impoundment barra behaviour and a deep knowledge of Monduran’s constantly changing conditions. His charters are designed to put anglers in the right places at the right times using modern day forward facing sonar paired with local knowledge all whilst teaching the techniques needed to tempt big, hard fighting fish from the lake’s points, bays and timbered country.
Charters Offered 
Mondy Man Fishing Charters provides a range of lure based barra sessions tailored to daily conditions and seasonal patterns:
• Half Day Barra Charter - A targeted session that concentrates on prime bite periods and productive structure, ideal for anglers who want a quick but high quality impoundment experience
• Full Day Barra Charter - A complete Monduran adventure with time to move through multiple areas, adjust lure styles and fish both the morning and afternoon bites
• Custom Barra Sessions - Personalised trips that can focus on specific techniques such as soft plastics, hardbody fishing, working tight timber, topwater presentations or advanced barra retrieval styles
Target Species 
The focus is entirely on Lake Monduran’s iconic impoundment barramundi, with genuine opportunities for metre class fish throughout the year. Sam reads conditions closely, adjusting locations and lure choices to match water temperature, wind direction and fish mood to give anglers the greatest chance of connecting to quality barra.
On Board Experience 
Sam’s boat is set up for comfort, casting efficiency and modern barra fishing. Guests fish with quality tackle, proven lure selections and the support of a guide who explains what to do and why at every step. Sam offers guidance on casting accuracy, lure control, strike timing and how to interpret changing fish behaviour. The experience is professional and ideal for anglers wanting to learn as well as catch fish.
Why It Stands Out 
Mondy Man Fishing Charters is a great choice for anglers wanting an enjoyable but knowledgeable barra session on Lake Monduran. Sam Rowley combines a strong local understanding with an easy going guiding style that helps anglers feel confident on the water. Whether you want to learn new impoundment techniques, chase your first Monduran barra or simply enjoy a guided day with a skilled local, this charter delivers a reliable and genuine barra fishing experience.
