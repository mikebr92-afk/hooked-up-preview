TWILIGHT SNAPPER 
Departing Hastings
SIGN UP FOR OUR 
TWILIGHT SNAPPER CHARTER. 
DEPENDANT ON TIDES & WEATHER
There’s something pretty special about chasing snapper as the sun starts to drop over Western Port.
After lasts years fantastic sessions we are running twilight snapper charters again. Departing from Hastings , giving anglers the chance to sneak in a quality fish after work or enjoy an evening on the water.
These sessions will be all about making the most of that late afternoon and evening bite heading out, getting the baits in the water and chasing one of Western Port’s most sought after fish.
Fill out the form below so we can contact you for our next departure for our Western Port Twilight Snapper Charter. or please call 0418 107 071.
