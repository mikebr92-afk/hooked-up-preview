NDIS
National Disability Insurance Scheme 
We are now running fishing trips for NDIS participants in the Northern Rivers of NSW. Our guides are qualified Support Workers and our fishing trips can be customized to suit the participants requirements and abilities. Transport can also be provided. 
“Hello, my name is Simon Fitzpatrick and I am a fishing guide in the Northern Rivers area. My son Jacob was blind from birth, but that didn’t stop his passion for fishing. As long as Dad baited the hook and untangled the line, Jake would happily fish all day. He was great at catching fish. Indeed, he had ‘blind luck’ as he used to say.” 
Fishing is a therapeutic activity and connects participants with the natural environment. Everyone should have the opportunity to go fishing. But not everyone knows how. 
Our guides are fully qualified and experienced at fishing with people with disabilities. 
• Working with children checks & police checks 
• First Aid & CPR trained 
• Fully insured 
• Trained Fishcare Volunteers . 
• Certificate of Operation (AMSA)
