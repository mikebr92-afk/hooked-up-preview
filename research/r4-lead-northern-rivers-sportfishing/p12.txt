Introduction to Fly Fishing
Fly fishing is great for the mind, body & spirit so no wonder more & more folks are giving it a go! 
• Learn about fly fishing tackle & techniques
• Learn how to cast a fly from shore
• Learn how to locate fish and present the fly
• Head out onto the water and start fly fishing!
• Less than 1 hr from the Gold Coast Airport
• Season: (all year)
• Species: Australian Bass
• Land based and Boat or Kayak Fishing 
• Locations: Tweed River or Clarrie Hall Dam 
• Suits: Beginners to Intermediate
