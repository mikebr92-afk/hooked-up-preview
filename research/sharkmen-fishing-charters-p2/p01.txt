OUR VESSELS 
Fish hard. Travel safe.
Our Sharkmen boats are designed to handle everything Victoria’s waters can throw at them, from choppy Western Port tides to offshore tuna grounds. Each vessel features modern electronics, commercial-grade safety equipment, and a layout built for comfort and stability. With trained, certified skippers at the helm, you can focus on the excitement while we take care of the safety.
FISHING GEAR
Gear that performs when it counts.
At our fishing charters, we spare no expense when it comes to the gear we use. We only provide high-end fishing rods, reels and tackle, ensuring every angler has access to the best equipment on the market. We invest thousands of dollars every season, without fail, to make sure our gear is always up to the highest standard. Our team spends countless hours meticulously inspecting and maintaining each piece of equipment to ensure it’s in perfect condition. For every snapper season, we refresh our entire collection with brand-new gear, so you can rely on the most advanced, dependable tools during your adventure.
SOME OF THE CREW 
Marco
Owner / Operator
Marco is the owner and operator of Sharkmen Fishing Charters, and the driving force behind every trip that leaves the ramp.
With years of experience fishing Western Port, Port Phillip and offshore Victorian waters, Marco has built Sharkmen on hard work, consistency and a genuine passion for putting customers onto fish. From kingfish and school tuna to snapper and offshore species, he knows when to move, when to change tactics and how to read conditions to give anglers the best possible opportunity.
Safety and professionalism are non-negotiable. Marco runs a tight, well-maintained vessel and prides himself on delivering a premium charter experience whether it’s your first time on the water or you’re a seasoned offshore regular.
For Marco, it’s simple:
Fish hard. Stay safe. Put people onto quality fish.
Goldy
Decky guru
Luke “Goldy” Goldstein is a core part of the crew at Sharkmen Fishing Charters , bringing energy, experience and a genuine passion for offshore fishing to every trip.
From chasing kingfish and tuna to helping first timers land their first snapper, Goldy keeps the deck running smoothly and the atmosphere upbeat. He is hands on with gear, quick to assist during a fight and committed to making sure every angler gets the most out of their day.
Professional, approachable and always ready for the next run, Goldy helps turn great fishing into a great experience
