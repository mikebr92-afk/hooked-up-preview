KINGFISH - TUNA
PROFESSIONAL KINGFISH & TUNA CHARTERS
PELAGIC POWER. KINGFISH & SOUTHERN BLUEFIN TUNA. 
OCTOBER - JAN - APRIL
This charter focuses on chasing hard fighting pelagics. Depending on conditions and recent reports, we will target either Kingfish or Southern Bluefin Tuna, whichever species is showing the best signs on the day. With two Sharkmen charter boats operating, you can book privately with your crew or secure individual spots across both vessels. More options mean more opportunities to get offshore when the bite is on. Kingfish offer powerful structure based battles, while Southern Bluefin Tuna provide exciting offshore action. Our focus is simple, put you on the most productive fishing available. Pricing from $300 pp week days and $350 weekends.
