SHARKS - PORTLAND
PORTLAND OFFSHORE SHARKS
PORTLAND OFFSHORE
SHARK ACTION. 
APRIL – AUGUST
This charter is all about targeting hard fighting sharks off Portland, one of Victoria’s most consistent offshore fisheries. Fishing wide over proven grounds, we focus on species like Gummy Shark, School Shark and the ever exciting Mako, using techniques developed through seasons of experience.
Expect solid runs offshore, heavy tackle, and powerful fights. Gummy and School Sharks provide steady action and great table fish, while Makos bring speed, aggression and unforgettable surface encounters. Every bite is different, and every fish demands respect.
We plan around weather and tides to give you the best shot at productive fishing, ensuring you are in the right areas when conditions are favourable.
This is about getting offshore, fishing the right areas, and making the most of what Portland has to offer.
If the date you require is unavailable, please call 0418 107 071 , as there may still be availability.
