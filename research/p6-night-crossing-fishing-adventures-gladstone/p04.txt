Fishing Trips
“MV Night Crossing” and her crew specialise in offshore reef fishing charters.
We fish from above the water only.
Sorry No Scuba Diving / Spear fishing or Snorkelling permitted.
Whilst most of our fishing charters are to the Swain reefs, Night Crossing can be chartered to various fishing and holiday destinations: Fully booked 2023 Book now 2024 / 2025
Swain Reefs
Approx 120nm off shore -Departs Gladstone Friday approx 3pm returns to port following Thursday morning approx. 5.00am
Saumarez Reef
in conjunction with the Swains – Friday to Thursday
Coral Sea Reef fishing
In the remote Coral Sea reefs eg.Marion, Frederick, Kenn and Wreck reefs approx. 200nm off shore. 10 day minimum required for these charters.
Plan your own itenerary, we do family trips to the Whitsundays or the beautiful Southern Great Barrier Reef like Musgrave Island or the Keppel Islands. “MV Night Crossing” can go anywhere you would like to holiday and fish on the Queensland coast.
