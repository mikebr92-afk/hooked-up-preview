Fish Photos
“MV Night Crossing” specializes in fishing trips. We fish the reef shallows from our 6 tenders, and at different times fish the depths of the shelf. Reef fish are not seasonal and can be fished all year round. We have experienced crew to take care of your catch, process it, snap freeze it, ready for you to take home. Guests bring their own fishing equipment. Pats Tackleworld 07 49723692 can help with your gear and lead with packages for the Swains charter guests.
