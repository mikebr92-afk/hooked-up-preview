Cairns Private Reef Charters
Great Barrier Reef private charters Cairns- Luxury Day trips, avoid the crowds
Big Fish Down Under on the Moana III Private Charter Boat offer an amazing personalized experience to enjoy the Great Barrier Reef. Options for luxury day cruising with your family and friends, work colleagues, business and corporate functions or experience the get away from it all, honeymoon cruise.
We will take you on your very own private charter. With our permits we have the flexibility and expertise to visit a wide variety of exotic locations and pristine coral reefs.
You can cruise along coastlines, explore islands. We can take you guided snorkeling to the best locations on Great Barrier Reef, or if you prefer, put your feet up and relax for the day while we take care of you with our luxury service.
If you're a keen angler we will also happily do some sportfishing for trophy fish as Marlin, Sailfish, Wahoo, Cobia, Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Jobfish, Mackerel, Tuna and many other varieties.
In certain months of the year, July to September, whilst doing luxury day cruises, we also see a large number of whales just out from Cairns.
Each year from June to October Humpback Whales migrate from the colder southern waters to the far northern waters off Cairns in large numbers. These warm waters offer an important winter calving ground for the graceful Minke and beautiful Humpback whales.
It is not uncommon in the local peak season to see several whales in a day.
So if you would like a private bird's eye whale watching opportunity, Moana III will happily take you on one of our famous luxury days and you could experience the brilliant performances of the Humpback Whale, July to September.
We also cater for small cocktail functions, or other functions of your choice and we also specialize in the 'get away from it all' honeymooners escape.
