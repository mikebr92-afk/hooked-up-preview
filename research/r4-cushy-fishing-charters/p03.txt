Our Boat – Fishing Charters
Welcome to Cushy Fishing Charters, your premier destination for an unforgettable fishing adventure! If you're looking for a Fishing Boat Charter or Charter Boat Fishing experience, you've come to the right place. Our expert team is dedicated to providing top-notch service and ensuring an exceptional fishing trip for all our guests.
We invite you aboard REEF HUNTER our 37ft Steber Fly bridge Pro-fisher. Reef Hunter is a fully insured, licensed, surveyed charter fishing boat CFB 12840.
Steber are the preferred vessels used by NSW offshore rescue operations. The NSW Fisheries and the NSW Water police are also using the Steber 37, renowned for their Strength, Stability and Comfortable ride.
REEF HUNTER is powered by a 375HP/3208/V8 Caterpillar Turbo Diesel and weighing in at under 10 tonnes, it is a very comfortable ride. Forward in the Wheelhouse you’ll find the master helm, galley, dinette and head (toilet). There is ample shade with two thirds of the vessel under cover.
REEF HUNTER carries the latest in SINRAD full colour fish finding equipment, and GPS technology.
REEF HUNTER carries a maximum number of 10 passengers on a fishing charter. It has a self draining, flush rear deck so there’s nothing to trip over and permitting easy access around the deck. 
Join us on one of our Charter Boat Fishing trips and embark on an adventure that will create lasting memories. Book your fishing charter today and discover the thrill of reeling in trophy fish while enjoying the beauty of the open waters.
