Welcome to Cushy Fishing Charters, your premier destination for an unforgettable fishing adventure! Our fishing boat charters offer a thrilling experience for anglers of all skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned fisherman or a novice angler, we have the perfect charter package tailored to your needs.
During the winter months we regularly encounter humpback whales on our fishing charters. We invite you to come and enjoy the amazing experience of being up close to these huge mammals as they follow their annual winter migratory path, north up the east coast of Australia, from May and returning south by September. A fantastic family day out Fishing and Whale Watching!!
Immerse yourself in the beauty of the open ocean as majestic whales breach and swim gracefully around our boat. These enchanting encounters create memories that will last a lifetime. Our knowledgeable crew will also share fascinating insights about these gentle giants, enhancing your appreciation for marine life.
Safety is our top priority, and our fishing boat charters are equipped with state-of-the-art equipment to ensure a secure and comfortable experience. Whether you're planning a family outing, a corporate event, or a solo fishing trip, we have the perfect vessel to accommodate your needs.
Book your fishing boat charter today and embark on an extraordinary adventure filled with thrilling catches and magical whale encounters. Get ready to create memories that will be the envy of all your mates!
