Welcome to Gone Fishing, Cooktown, Australia
Gone Fishing offers guided fishing charters and wildlife tours around Cooktown, Cape York and on the Great Barrier Reef, Australia.
Fish with lures or live-bait for barramundi, coral trout, tuna, mackerel, jacks, trevally and Queenies.
With over 15 years experience fishing locally, we pride ourselves on our personalised, professional and consistent fishing charters.
Our Fishing Charters
What makes us different? Our professionalism and consistent results. It doesn’t matter if you are doing a half day fishing charter or a 10 day fishing charter you will receive the same friendly, productive, informative, value for money charter that will be remembered for a long time.
