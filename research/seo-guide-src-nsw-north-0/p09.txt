About
Capns charters Newcastle provides family-focused fishing adventures on the Hunter River from Tomago. Led by Amy, trips blend patient instruction with practical fishing tips, teaching casting and lure techniques while supplying reliable gear. Guests praise a safe, engaging experience, plenty of fish, and memorable photo-worthy moments.
