Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters. 
A local vessel thats been catching fish for over 25 years. 
Offshore fishing adventures on board the Baitrunner, departing from Tin Can Bay.
We’re a small team of everyday fisherman and locals to Tin Can Bay, who are continuing the legacy of the Baitrunner! 
Learn More
Everything you need for a great day on the water.
🎣 All fishing gear provided 
Rods, reels, tackle and bait are all supplied. Just turn up ready to fish.
🌊 Full-day adventures 
We don't rush you back to shore. Our charters are designed to give you a full day on the water.
🐟 Experienced crew 
We know these waters and we're passionate about putting you onto fish.
💧 All the essentials 
Bottled water is supplied alongside refrigeration & an iced section for your food. Most importantly, toilet facilities are onboard for when nature calls.
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 First-timers welcome 
Never been fishing offshore? No worries. We'll show you the ropes.

What's included?
Your charter includes:
• Rods & reels
• Fishing tackle
• Bait
• Bottled water
• Ice / refrigerated storage
• Experienced skipper & crew
• A full day on the water
Just bring:
• Your own food & snacks to last you the day
• Hat
• Sunscreen
• Sunglasses
• Suitable clothing and sunnies 
• Any personal drinks
• BYO alcohol if you'd like - cans only no glass 
• A bloody good vibe, because who could be sad on the water!
