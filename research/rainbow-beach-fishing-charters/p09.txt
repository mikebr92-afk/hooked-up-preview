RAINBOW BEACH FISHING CHARTERS 
EXPERIENCE OFFSHORE FISHING 
Join us as we depart from Tin Can Bay, taking you on an unforgettable offshore fishing adventure East of Fraser Island, Rainbow Beach, and Double Island Point. 
We offer full-day fishing trips that come with everything you need for a fantastic day on the water tailored to individuals, families and groups of all skill levels.

frequently asked questions | 
frequently asked questions | 
• 
What do we bring? 
• Hat and sunglasses - we also recommend you wear a long sleeved fishing shirt
• A packed lunch and snacks to last you 8 - 10 hours - we have an iced section on board to keep everything cold
• Esky to take home your fish - while out on the water your catches will be iced on board
• BYO Alcohol is welcome - we love Jack Daniels and Cola or Brookvale Union Ginger Beers
• You can bring your own rod etc if you wish, just let us know beforehand
• 
Do I need fishing experience to go offshore? 
No prior fishing experience is required to join a charter with Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters. We welcome individuals of all skill levels, from absolute beginners to experienced anglers. Our knowledgeable crew offer full support during the trip, including help with baiting hooks, providing fishing tips and securing your catch. We supply all necessary equipment, including rods, bait, and tackle, to ensure a seamless experience.
• 
How long is the fishing charter? 
We offer standard charters which are 8-10 hour trips, or extended charters which are 12 hour trips. 
• 
What time is departure? 
Current departure time is 6am from the Tin Can Bay Public Pontoon.
