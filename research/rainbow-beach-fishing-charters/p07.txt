Terms & Conditions 
• $100 deposit (per person) is required to secure single and small group bookings 
• $1000 deposit is required for entire vessel/private charter bookings 
• If paying via bank transfer full payment is required 48 hours prior to the charter as there are no eftpos facilities on the vessel 
• Booking deposits are non-refundable but can be held on file as a credit and transferred to a new date. This proportionates to our time and expenses incurred in administration 
• Customers will receive a refund or credit (less the deposit amount) in case of operator cancellation due to weather or other unforeseen circumstances. 
• No refunds or credits will be given for cancellations closer than 24hrs to the commencement of your booking, or for no-shows. 
• Refunds will be processed within 30 days, but may take 3-5 business days to complete the transfer depending on your bank. 
• Guests must compensate for any fishing rods, reels, or equipment that are lost overboard or damaged due to their negligence before leaving the vessel.
