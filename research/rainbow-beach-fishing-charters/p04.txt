WHAT YOU NEED TO BRING
• Hat and sunglasses - we also recommend you wear a long sleeved fishing shirt
• A packed lunch and snacks to last you 8 - 10 hours - we have an iced section on board to keep everything cold
• Esky to take home your fish - while out on the water your catches will be iced on board 
• BYO Alcohol is welcome - we love Jack Daniels and Cola or Brookvale Union Ginger Beers 
• You can bring your own rod etc if you wish, just let us know beforehand
Our tackle consists of , Shimano Baitrunners, Shimano Saragosa, Fin-nor and Penn spin reels. All the best fresh baits are used, including live and cut yakkas.
