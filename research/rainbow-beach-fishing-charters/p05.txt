Introducing the Baitrunner 
Experience offshore fishing aboard the Baitrunner, a fast and comfortable 9.8M Cougar Cat powered by twin 250hp Suzuki 4-stroke outboards. 
For your comfort, the Baitrunner features a full bimini roof to shield you from the sun on hot days and includes toilet facilities on board. 
Equipped with the latest Garmin electrical gear, we’re well-prepared to help you find that big catch while maintaining safe communication with the shore. Before we set off, our crew will provide a safety briefing in accordance with maritime regulations.
Your safety is our top priority, and we take all necessary precautions when crossing coastal bars. Our goal is for you to have an enjoyable experience and return fully satisfied.
Please note that smoking is permitted in designated areas, and ashtrays are provided to protect marine wildlife. You are welcome to bring your own alcohol in cans, but we do not supply it.
The Baitrunner accommodates a maximum of 10 passengers, along with 2 crew members, ensuring a personal and attentive experience.

The Crew 
Kurt is the Owner and Operator of Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters. With over 20 years experience fishing from the waters of Brisbane to the picturesque shores of 1770, Kurt brings a wealth of knowledge and passion for fishing that is sure to enhance your charter experience. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or first time fisher, Kurt is committed to providing an unforgettable day out on the water and filling your esky with fish!
You’ll most likely talk to Georgia along the way too, she handles most of the bookings and admin side of things!
