Terms & Conditions
By making a booking with Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters, you acknowledge and agree to the following Terms & Conditions.
Bookings & Deposits
A $100 per person deposit is required to secure individual and small group bookings.
A $1,000 deposit is required to secure an entire vessel/private charter booking.
Your booking is not confirmed until the required deposit has been received.
Deposits secure your position on the vessel and the nominated charter date. Once a booking is confirmed, we may decline other customers seeking to book those positions or that charter date.
If paying via bank transfer, the remaining balance must be paid no later than 48 hours prior to departure, as EFTPOS facilities are not available onboard the vessel.
Weather & Marine Conditions
Offshore fishing is highly weather dependent and the safety of our passengers and crew will always come first.
The decision as to whether a charter can safely proceed is made by the Skipper, taking into consideration weather forecasts, sea and swell conditions, wind, bar conditions and any other relevant safety considerations.
Forecasts and marine conditions can change quickly. For this reason, a decision to postpone a charter may sometimes need to be made close to the scheduled departure time.
If Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters determines that it is unsafe for your charter to proceed due to weather or marine conditions, we will contact you as soon as reasonably possible to arrange a reschedule.
Weather Cancellations & Rescheduling
Where a charter cannot safely proceed due to weather, sea conditions, bar conditions or other circumstances outside our reasonable control, customers will be offered the opportunity to transfer their booking and payments to another available charter date.
We will work with you to arrange a suitable replacement date, subject to vessel availability, weather conditions and our operating schedule. 
Your deposit is held on file and transferred to your new booking date. We will continue to reschedule for 12 months and hold the deposit on file for 12 months. Once the 12 months lapses and if the service has not been provided we will refund you your deposit. 
Nothing in these Terms & Conditions excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Where the Australian Consumer Law entitles a customer to a refund or another remedy, that entitlement will apply.
Customer Cancellations
If you choose to cancel your booking, please provide us with as much notice as possible.
Cancellations made within 24 hours of the scheduled departure time and failure to attend your charter will result in the loss of amounts paid, subject always to any rights you may have under Australian Consumer Law.
For cancellations made more than 24 hours prior to departure, please contact us as soon as possible. Any applicable refund, credit, transfer or cancellation amount will be determined in accordance with these Terms & Conditions and your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
Change of Mind
Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters is not generally required to provide a refund where a customer simply changes their mind or is unable to attend for reasons unrelated to the service provided by us.
Where possible, we will work with customers to transfer their booking to another available charter date.
Travel & Accommodation
Customers are responsible for arranging their own travel, accommodation and other associated expenses.
As offshore fishing charters are weather dependent, we strongly recommend that customers consider flexible or refundable travel and accommodation arrangements where possible.
We will make reasonable efforts to provide as much notice as possible when a charter cannot proceed. However, weather and marine conditions can change with limited notice and passenger safety will always take priority.
Nothing in this section limits any rights or remedies available under Australian Consumer Law.
Operator Cancellations
If we are unable to provide a charter for reasons within our reasonable control, we will contact affected customers and provide an appropriate remedy in accordance with Australian Consumer Law.
Weather and marine safety cancellations are dealt with separately under the Weather & Marine Conditions and Weather Cancellations & Rescheduling sections above.
Safety & Passenger Conduct
All passengers must follow reasonable directions given by the Skipper and crew.
The Skipper has final responsibility for the safe operation of the vessel and may refuse boarding or discontinue a passenger's participation where their behaviour presents a safety risk to themselves, other passengers, crew or the vessel.
Passengers must complete the required safety briefing and passenger information prior to or upon boarding as directed by our crew.
Alcohol
BYO alcohol is permitted; however, alcohol must be brought in cans only. Glass bottles are not permitted onboard.
Passengers are expected to consume alcohol responsibly. The Skipper may restrict alcohol consumption or take appropriate action where a passenger's intoxication or behaviour creates a safety concern.
Fishing Equipment
Rods, reels, tackle and other fishing equipment supplied by Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters must be treated with reasonable care.
Guests may be required to compensate Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters for equipment that is lost overboard or damaged as a direct result of the guest's negligence or misuse.
Refund Processing
Where a refund is agreed or required, we will process the refund as soon as reasonably practicable, but no greater than 30 days.
Please allow additional processing time for the funds to appear in your account depending on your financial institution.
Australian Consumer Law
Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law.
Nothing contained in these Terms & Conditions is intended to exclude, restrict or modify any consumer guarantee, right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded, restricted or modified.
Where these Terms & Conditions are inconsistent with a customer's mandatory rights under Australian Consumer Law, those statutory rights will apply.
Acceptance of Terms
By paying a deposit or otherwise confirming a booking with Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters, you acknowledge that you have been provided with access to these Terms & Conditions and agree to the terms applicable to your booking.
We strongly recommend reviewing these Terms & Conditions before making payment.
