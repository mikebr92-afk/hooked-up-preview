Your day onboard the Baitrunner 
5.30AM - DEPARTURE 
We meet at the Tin Can Bay Public Pontoon and get underway early to make the most of the day.
ON THE WATER 
Once we're out there, we'll fish the areas and techniques that best suit the conditions on the day.

Bring your own food and snacks to keep you fuelled throughout the day. BYO alcohol is welcome, cans only please.
