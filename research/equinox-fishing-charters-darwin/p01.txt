Fishing Charters from Darwin, Northern Territory
PRICES & ONLINE BOOKING 
Experience a Darwin fishing trip you’ll never forget!
Choose a 5 hour, 8 hour or 10 hour (extended day) Blue water or Barra fishing charter and our experienced and knowledgeable crew will take you to some of the best fishing locations in the Top End. Destinations include Shoal Bay, Charles Point , Middle Reef , Fish Reef , Bass Reef, North and South Gutter, Tiwi Islands and maybe some Skippers’ secret spots! 
Every fishing adventure from Darwin includes all meals, tackle and bait, soft drinks and water. 
Equinox Fishing Charters from Darwin strictly adheres to all Northern Territory amateur fishing regulations and bag limits.
Equinox Fishing Charters is an Accredited Tourism Business.
So, for the fishing trip of a lifetime – contact us today !
