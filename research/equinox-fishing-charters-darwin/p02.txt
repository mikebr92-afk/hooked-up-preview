5 Hour Charter
Price $360
Our half day charters (5 hours) travel up to 20 kms from Darwin . All fishing equipment is provided – we even bait your hooks if required.
Whether you are booking as an individual or with a group of up to 8 people Equinox Fishing Charters will provide an experience you will never forget!
Our aim is to give you an unforgettable day’s fishing – whether you are a beginner or an experienced angler.
After boarding at the Cullen Bay Ferry Terminal, we head out to anchor on various reefs and wrecks.
Reef fishing including: Golden Snapper, Rock Cod, Tricky Snapper, Jewfish, Threadfin Salmon.
NOTE: Departure point and times subject to change due to tides.
Minimum of 4 passengers to run the charter.

8 Hour Charter
Price $500
Our eight hour charters travel up to 40kms from Darwin to some of the best fishing locations in the Top End of Australia. All fishing equipment is provided – we even bait your hooks if required.
Whether you are booking as an individual or with a group of up to 8 people Equinox Fishing Charters will provide an experience you will never forget!
Our aim is to give you an unforgettable day’s fishing – whether you are a beginner or an experienced angler.
After boarding from the Cullen Bay Ferry Terminal we’ll head out to some of our favourite fishing spots. We will anchor on various reefs and wrecks throughout the day.
Reef fishing including: Golden Snapper, Rock Cod, Tricky Snapper, Jewfish, Coral Trout and many more. We will also put baits out for Spanish Mackerel, Tuna, Cobia and Giant Trevally (tide dependant).
NOTE: Departure point and times subject to change due to tides.
Minimum of 4 passengers to run the charter.
