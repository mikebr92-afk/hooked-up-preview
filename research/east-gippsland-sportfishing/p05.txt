East Gippsland is a land of wonder where wild coastlines, ancient forests, pristine lakes, and dramatic caves create unforgettable scenery for every adventurer. Marvel at the sweeping views of the Gippsland Lakes and the iconic Ninety Mile Beach, wander lush trails in the Alpine High Country, or explore the spectacular Buchan Caves. Spot koalas on Raymond Island, paddle tranquil rivers, and unwind in picturesque towns filled with welcoming locals. From sunrise over wild ocean beaches to sunset at serene lookouts, this is a region where nature, wildlife, and culture unite—inviting discovery, awe, and joy.
