East Gippsland Sportfishing
• 
Coastal Wilderness , Mallacoota 
Tours & Hire 
East Gippsland Sportfishing offers personalised guided fishing tours that operates in East Gippsland’s estuary systems of Mallacoota, Bemm River and Lake Tyers.
They cater for all levels of experience by providing a range of tour options offering lure fishing using soft plastics, hard body lures complimented with quality fishing tackle. Target species include Dusky Flathead, Bream, Trevally, Estuary Perch, Tailor and Salmon.
They are Professional Fishing Instructors and Guides Association (PFIGA) accredited for your safety and piece of mind.
