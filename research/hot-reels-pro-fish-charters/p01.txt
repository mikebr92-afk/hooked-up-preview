A Great Hervey Bay and Fraser Island experience
LETS GO FISHING! 
Our aim is to give you the ultimate fishing adventure in the magical, pristine waters of Hervey Bay/Fraser (K’ Gari) Island and beyond on a uncrowded day out fishing. We cater for the amateur or experienced fisher person and everything is provided, so you can simply step on then step off with your catch.
We are Professional line fisherman that want to share our extensive knowledge and methods to help you secure the best catch possible on your charter. If the weather permits we can travel as far as possible/needed and do “the miles to get the smiles.”
We have based our operations around an exceptional fishing vessel, so you can enjoy your adventure with your own small private groups of family and friends if you wish, without the frustration of fishing on a overcrowded charter with strangers. This maximises the chances of you catching plenty more fish each and we can help customise your day by using a variety of fishing methods and techniques.
READ MORE 
We have the buissness name Hot Reels Pro Fish Charters, because we have been commercially line fishing and Sportfishing the Fraser Coast waters for decades and still do so when we are not on charter. The decision was made to transition into charters to share our knowledge and target trophy fish for the clients. These waters can hold massive numbers and varieties of trophy fish and many anglers have ticked of there P.B bucket list species.
So “LETS GO FISHING.”
Baiting/ Float Lining
If you’re chasing quality Reef Fish such as Coral Trout, Scarlet Sea Perch and the prized Red Emperor along with Snapper and Mackerel to name only a few.
READ MORE 
Jigging
Jigging for Reef Fish, Spanish Mackeral, Coral Trout, Dino sized Cobias plus a vast variety of Trevally species and Mackerel can be a extremely exciting and effective.
READ MORE 
Trolling
Towing around rigged whole dead baits and lures for pelagics fish such as juvenile Black Marlin, Large Spanish Mackerel, Snapper, Tuna, Reef Fish and more.
READ MORE 
Popping/ Stickbaiting/ Softplastics
This is our favourite way to fish the bait balls on the surface that are being harassed by Spanish Mackerel, Tuna, Spotty and school Mackerel, Golden Trevally, Cobia, Queenfish and more.
READ MORE 
Breaksea Spit (Not currently available)
On this extreme fishing charter we will be fishing the top eastern side of Fraser/K’gari Island, Breaksea Spit and beyond using a combination of the techniques listed above for big Red Emperor, Green Jobfish and much more.
READ MORE 
Shark Fishing – Tag & Release
If you wish to on your private charter, why not test your strength and skills on a wide variety of Sharks. We are tagging and releasing to help gather the much needed information in order to keep their numbers at a sustainable level.
