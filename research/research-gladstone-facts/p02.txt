MV Capricorn Star Fishing Charters operates from YEPPOON, QLD, 4703 
MV Capricorn Star Fishing Charters operates from Yeppoon in Queensland, Australia with direct access to The Great Barrier Reef where you can enjoy one of the best fishing, diving, research, reef walking or sightseeing holidays in the world. MV Capricorn Star Fishing Charters offers extended charters for group bookings of up to 12 to 16 passengers aboard our beamy 22m vessel, the MV Capricorn Star. Formerly known as A Myti Adventure.
