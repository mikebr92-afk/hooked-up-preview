Action-packed Reef Charter Trips from Gladstone
Updated on 28/07/2021 28/07/2021 Ros Leave a Comment on Action-packed Reef Charter Trips from Gladstone 
• 
• 
• 
• 
• 
MV Eastern Voyager is a family-owned-and-operated reef charter company dedicated to providing exciting fishing trips. We have over 40 years of experience in charter fishing up to 120 nautical miles offshore. Reaching a maximum of twenty guests, you’ll be able to enjoy a personalised experience with us.
Get a group of your friends or family together or join a trip if you’re flying solo. Eastern Voyager is a leader in reef fishing, employing an experienced crew so your charter trip out of Gladstone is the best it can be. Enjoy a relaxing fishing holiday on board our vessel at one of the locations on offer including the Capricorn Bunker Group, Shoalwater Bay, Swain Reefs and the Coral Sea.
When you embark on a reef fishing charter from Gladstone, you can experience the Swains from our custom-built fishing dories. You can fish then sit back and relax while we take care of the cooking and cleaning. All fun and no work. Take in the beautiful scenery and watch the variety of sea life like dolphins, turtles and whales as they pass by.
We are regulated by the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority, the Department of Primary Industries and Fisheries, and the Australian Maritime Safety Authority and you can trust our expertise. Whatever you catch you can take home to cook. To make it easier and more enjoyable for you, we will clean, process and freeze whatever you catch during your trip.
We look forward to seeing you soon
Chris and Ellen Pike
