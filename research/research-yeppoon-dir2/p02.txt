Double Threat Charters Yeppoon operates from YEPPOON, QLD, 4703 
Double Threat Charters Yeppoon offer a number of full day fishing charter trips. Since 2017, we have targeted Coral Trout, Reds and Spanish Mackerel in the fishing groups offshore from Yeppoon. Trips can be customised to suit your group of anglers needs.
Our boat DIVERSE 1 is a 2012 Leisurecat 8000 is a dry ride no matter the weather conditions, and features fridge, stereo, the latest electronics and a toilet.
We offer a variety of full day trips including an Inshore & Island trip, Wreck & Fern trip or deep water Reef & Shoals trip.
All trips include a light lunch, snacks box, soft drinks and water.
