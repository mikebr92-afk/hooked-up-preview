Fishing Charters operating in Yeppoon and surronds are listed below. There are 6 Fishing Charters listed in the Yeppoon area.
Carlo Fishing Charters - Swain Reefs 
YEPPOON, QLD, 4703
*** BOOKED OUT FOR 2024 ONLY TAKING ENQUIRES FROM 2025 *** The Carlo II hosts fishing charters to the middle and northern areas of the Swain Reefs. Liveaboard fishing charters to the Swains departing Rosslyn Bay on Saturday around 5 - 8pm generally and returning at approx. 5 am the following Saturday. Trips can be tailored to suit your preferences. The Carlo II takes up to 20 guests. The best times are April through to November. Target species include Coral Trout, Sweet Lip, Red Emperor, Jew, Spanish Mackeral and Yellow-fin Tuna. All bait, food and use of our six dories are included. Charges are $2950 per person for 7 nights for up to 20 guests which includes all charges. We take a minimum of 18 persons, taking 18 - 20 guests. If you have for example 10 - 12 persons, we can combine another group with you to make up the number of 18 - 20. For further information feel free to contact us again. Also check us out on facebook for photos and videos.
Roslyn Bay (Yeppoon)

Hooked Fishing Charters 
YEPPOON, QLD, 4703
Fish the Fitzroy River Delta and the Capricorn Coast departing from Yeppoon QLD with the local fishing experts at Hooked Fishing Charters. Charters can be customised to meet your groups needs. Fishing tours can be salt water or fresh water fishing. In the rivers and estuaries we target Barramundi, Thread Fin Salmon and one of our favourites the Mangrove Jack. Or on The Keppels inshore reefs we target giant Trevally, Mackerel, Coral Trout and more. Accommodating groups of 1 to 4 anglers.

YEPPOON, QLD, 4703
MV Capricorn Star Fishing Charters operates from Yeppoon in Queensland, Australia with direct access to The Great Barrier Reef where you can enjoy one of the best fishing, diving, research, reef walking or sightseeing holidays in the world. MV Capricorn Star Fishing Charters offers extended charters for group bookings of up to 12 to 16 passengers aboard our beamy 22m vessel, the MV Capricorn Star. Formerly known as A Myti Adventure.
