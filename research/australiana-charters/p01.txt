Welcome Aboard Australiana 
Charters 
Experience a memorable offshore fishing adventure with Australiana Charters, a trusted local business renowned for exceptional reef fishing expeditions to the Swain Reefs, departing conveniently from Rosslyn Bay Harbour, Yeppoon. Our charters prioritise comfort, safety, and access to some of Queensland's most sought-after fishing locations. 
Designed for passionate anglers, these experiences offer the opportunity to immerse yourself in genuine extended offshore adventure, supported by a knowledgeable crew, reliable equipment, and years of local expertise. 
Whether you're seeking that elusive bucket list catch or simply want to unwind on a well-equipped vessel, Australiana Charters delivers an experience that goes beyond the ordinary.
Purpose-Built Vessel for 
Extended Charter Fishing 
Our 60 ft purpose-built charter fishing vessel is designed for extended offshore trips, providing both comfort and practical space for multi-day fishing charters. With six twin-share cabins accommodating up to 12 guests, the vessel is perfectly suited for group bookings and mates who want to maximise quality fishing time on the water. 
We offer 7 to 10 day charter fishing trips, with 7-day charters the most popular option. These trips provide access to both coastal fishing grounds and outer reef systems, allowing groups to target a range of species depending on conditions. 
All meals, linen, and towels are included onboard, making preparation simple. Smaller groups or individuals can also join existing bookings when places are available. 
To maximise fishing opportunities, the charter also includes 4 purpose-built CNC Marine tinnies fitted with 20 HP Honda outboards, allowing small groups to access different reef zones and spread out across productive fishing areas. 
Guests bring their own tackle and fishing equipment, while bait and ice are available onboard as needed.
