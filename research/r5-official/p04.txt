​​​​New way to buy your licence online
We've upgraded our online licencing website to make buying your licence faster, easier and more secure.
To buy or renew your licence using the new website:
• Visit ​​ https://reclicence.fishing.tas.gov.au/ 
• Create an account (everyone will need to do this the first time)
• Follow the prompts to buy or renew your licence.
• 
​If you've held a licence before, you'll need to find and link your existing licence. 
• 
If you haven't held a licence before, you'll need to apply for a new licence. 
Once you've bought your licence, you can login with the account you've created at anytime to:
• ​check your licence details
• add a new licence type (vary your licence)
• access an image of your licence
• view your payment history
• update your contact details​
​​Want more detailed step-by-step instructions? Check out 
How to use the recreational licencing website ​​.​
​​​B​uy or renew sea fishing licence
The current licensing year is 
1 November 2026 - 31 October 2027 .
Licences are valid from the date of purchase until 31 October 2027. Short term licences are not available.​​
​ 
OR: Visit a 
Service Tasmania Centre in person to buy your licence​.
You don't need to have your licence with you when you fish, but you can carry a digital copy or your receipt ​if you wish. Plastic licence cards are not issued. 
​ 
What fishing requires a licence?
You 
don't need a licence for rod and line fishing in marine waters in Tasmania. You do need a licence for the following types of fishing:
• 
​ Rock Lobster Pot, ​​Ring or Dive* 
• 
Abalone * 
• 
Scallop* 
• 
Graball Net* 
• 
Mullet Net* 
• 
Beach Seine Net 
• 
Set Line* ​
*not available to children under 10 years. 
Season dates, catch limits, area restrictions, gear marking requirements and other rules may apply. For more information see 
Recreational Fishing Rules or download the Fishing Tas app ​. 
​​​Aboriginal fishers
If you're an aboriginal fisher, you do not need a recreational sea fishing licence. However, you may need a Unique Identifying Code (UIC). See 
Aboriginal Fishing for more information. 
​​​​​​Freshwater fishing
A licence is required for freshwater fishing. For freshwater licences, contact Inland Fisheries . ​​ 
​

NOTICE: The Fisheries Integrated Licensing System (FILMS) is an approved computer program under section 273B of the Living Marine Resources Management Act 1995, for the granting, variation, or renewal of recreational fishing licences.
Licencing ​​​support
We’re committed to providing support for recreational fishers, regardless of how you prefer to contact us. ​
If you encounter an issue with purchasing a licence, our staff will aim to assist you as soon as we can.
However, please be aware it may take up to 10 business days for us to respond to your enquiry, particularly during periods of high-request volume.
Email​​
If you need assistance, you can email 
fishing.enquiries@nre.tas.gov.au .
Phone​
​ If you'd prefer assistance over the phone, you can also call 1300 720 647. 
Please be aware we have recently transitioned this phone number to a call-back service. When you call, please leave a message with:
• ​Your name
• Contact number
• Licence number (if known)
• Description of issue
And we'll get back to you as soon as we can.​​​ However, pleas e be aware it may take up to 10 business days for us to respond to your enquiry, particularly during periods of high-request volume.​ ​
