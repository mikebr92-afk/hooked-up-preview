OUR Boat
All Equinox Fishing Charters vessel complies with Northern Territory Marine Survey requirements. 
We only use top quality, locally supplied tackle.
Equinox Fishing Charters operates one vessel
; 
LIFE OF WATER
Life of Water is an 8-meter centre cab fibreglass Westerner Leisure Cat. .
Comfortable and quite ride out to the fishing grounds.
BOAT FEATURES 
• 2C Survey for up to 8 passengers
• Powered by 2 x 175hp Suzuki motors
• Cruising speed of 28-30 knots
• New up to date electronics (the fish can’t hide)
• Quick to get out to the fishing grounds
• Fish all the way around deck
• Toilet on board
• Seating at front and on large centred esky
