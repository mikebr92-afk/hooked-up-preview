Groups & multi-day trips
Quoted for your group
The Goulburn River Journey 
Three- or four-day journeys, usually built around four anglers, two boats and two guides. Accommodation can be included in the proposal. Price depends on group size, fishing days and room arrangements.
Multi-boat groups
Friends, clubs and corporate groups can book several drift boats, subject to guide availability. Each boat still carries no more than two guests with its own guide. Contact Anthony for a group quote.
