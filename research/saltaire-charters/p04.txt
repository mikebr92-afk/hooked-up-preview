Destinations
Saltaire is a Custom Made vessel built specifically for our local Great Barrier Reef conditions...
Saltaire is a Custom Made vessel built specifically for our local Great Barrier Reef conditions and is beautifully designed for daily and extended trips.
Fishing Port Douglas and beyond, trips can include an array of fishing styles, adventure cruising opportunities to Sand Cays, tropical islands and private reef systems captivated with amazing aquatic life.
Snorkelling and diving can also be part of your day. Overnight charters can further include extending to the incredible Hope Isles, the Ribbon Reefs & Lizard Island depending on your time of travel.
Contact us for a tailored made trip.
