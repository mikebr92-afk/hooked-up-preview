Discover Victoria’s Outback. 
Explore isolated untouched landscapes rich with wildlife.
Experience a Wild and Beautiful Country. 
Only fully appreciated with guides who know its secrets.
Personalised 4WD Bus Tours - Mildura Victoria 
Outback Adventure Time! 
Take a tour of the myriad of magical creeks, lakes, and billabongs near the Murray River, to ancient red gum forests, open plains dotted
with salt lakes, flat horizons, vast blue skies, sand dunes and sandy Mallee scrub. 
Discover two of Victoria’s most remote national parks Murray-Sunset and Hattah-Kulkyne National Parks.
Marvel at the effortless power of kangaroos bounding over open plains. Be still and watch emus grazing close by.
Travel in the comfort and safety of our 4WD tour bus with an incredibly knowledgeable guide.
Choose one of our exciting tours! 
Priced from $125 - $190 per person for a 4 - 6 hour tour.
Enjoy a guided tour with morning tea and or lunch.
Tour options are flexible and personalised.
Create your own Special Memories.
