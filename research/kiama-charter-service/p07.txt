Finding Us
Kostalota is moored in Robertson Basin/Kiama Harbour which is off the south eastern end of Terralong Street, Kiama’s main street. 
Go straight through the roundabout at the Post Office on Manning and Terralong St corner, (turn right if travelling from the south) veer left & left again toward the boat ramp & rock pool.
Keep all the boats on your left and drive right around the harbour, which is shaped a bit like a horse shoe, past the slipway & fresh fish shop, until you get to the last boat.
Kostalota is the (12m) aluminium boat with a blue canopy over the back and the phone number 4236 1716 in 600mm lettering down the sides.
Please park at 90 degrees to the low stone retaining wall opposite the boat.
