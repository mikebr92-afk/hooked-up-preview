About Us
Exclusive private, calm water fishing adventures for 1-4 passengers. Quality Shimano, Samaki, and Atomic Arrowz fishing rods and reels. Fully guided fishing tuition for anglers of all experience levels, guaranteed to catch fish. Comfortable and safe boat, exploring and cruising the beautiful, protected waterways of the Gold Coast
