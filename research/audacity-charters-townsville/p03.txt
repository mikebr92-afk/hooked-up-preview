The Audacity Charter
Fleet
Vanguard
our new leader in reef fishing
Vanguard is a former Police vessel that operated out of NSW. As a former Police vessel, Vanguard is built for speed and rough seas, able to reach remote spots quickly without worries. This translates into a safe and effective fishing charter boat for our customers. 
Features
• 
55ft Austral 
• 
Twin 300HP Mercury Verado engines, cruises at 22 knots 
• 
Inboard with shaft driven Twin MAN 
• 
Suited for groups of up to 12 
• 
Fast charter boat that will visit multiple fishing spots on each charter to maximise your experience and manage our fish populations. 
• 
Perfect for reef fishing - coral trout, red throat emperor, red emperor nannygui, and more 
• 
Plenty of eskies and fridges on board, for your food, drink, and catches 
• 
Toilet facilities and multiple comfortable resting areas 
Experience the comfort
Vanguard has comfortable seats, and is fully fitted with everything you will need! The flybridge is a great place to relax when out on the reef. 
Top Quality Instruments
You can be assured of a great and safe experiencee, with our boats having the best quality navigational equipment.

Fishing Charters

Pilgrim
Our vessel for inshore fishing and sports fishing.

Experience amazing inshore and sports fishing with the crew at Audacity Charters, onboard Pilgrim .

AUDACITY
Audacity was where it all began.
Our original flagship that carried us through countless adventures. While she has now been succeeded by our new vessel, her story continues to shape who we are. 
Audacity, our 37ft Kevlacat, was a former volunteer marine rescue vessel that operated out of Airlie Beach. As a former rescue vessel, Audacity is built for speed and rough seas, able to reach remote spots quickly without worries. 
Features
• 
37ft Kevlacat 
• 
Twin 300HP Mercury Verado engines, cruises at 22 knots
