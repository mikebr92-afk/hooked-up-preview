The tours and pricing below are for charters conducted in Daly River, Northern Territory and offered year round depending on availability and pre season bookings. 
we also offer extended charters and different locations see our packages page. 
​ ​ 
FISHING CHARTERS 
Full day fishing charter with cold drinks, morning tea and lunch provided. 
$420 per person up to 8 hours. 
​ 
Half day fishing charter with cold water provided. 
$380 per person. Strictly 4 hours (Available June to September) 
​ 
Additional fees apply for exclusive bookings or full boat hire. ​ 
​ 
​ ACTIVITIES 
RIVER TOUR 
Take a relaxing trip on the river, see the wildlife, maximum 4 people 
(NO FISHING) byo drinks and snacks. 
1.5 hrs. $150 per person (Available June to September) 
​ 
4X4 TOUR 
Explore the local area by 4x4 with a 2.5 hr. guided tour of historic sites and amazing scenery maximum 6 people. 
Includes cold water & collection from your local tourist park of choice. 
$150 per person. 
​ 
4X 4 DAY TOUR 
Daly River region and Litchfield park day trips. 
We offer day trips from Daly River or can collect you from Darwin for an additional fee. 
During your day trip you be provided with snacks and drinks whilst visiting areas that our local guides have hand picked for your enjoyment. 
$350 per person Day Trip. 
​ 
GUIDED CAMPING 
want to spend the night in a swag under the stars in true bush style? 
let us take you to some of the amazing camping destinations the N.T has to offer, allow our guide to set up camp and cook a traditional camp oven bush meal. 
price dependent on duration and require ments. camping can be included within fishing packages (camping trips POA) ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ ​ 
​ 
ACCOMODATION 
We have a number of accommodation options for our guests all options have access to our p lunge pool, picnic & BBQ area. 
Our large air conditioned bunkhouse with kitchen and amenities has a capacity of 8 people. with linen and cooking utensils. $250 per night. 
Ever wanted to try caravanning but don't want to tow? 
we have a spacious modern onsite Jayco Base station caravan with everything you need to be self sufficient for your stay, capable of sleeping 6 people. $200 per night. 
​ 
Powered/unpowered campsites available with toilet block facilities shared between limited sites. 
$40/$20 per night 
Feel free to book into any of the amazing local tou rist parks, we can collect you for your tour direct from reception. 
​ 
TRANSPORT 
Transport to and from Darwin can be provided, collection direct from the airport or your motel of choice can be arranged. 
Enjoy being driven to your destination in our tourist vehicle accredited, air conditioned Toyota landcruiser . 
Transfers from Darwin to Daly River. $900 
​ 
ADDITIONAL INFORMATION 
All tours require a minimum of 2 clients to commence. 
Exclusive bookings will incur additional fee. 
Larger groups can be catered for up to 7ppl excess fees apply. 
​ 
Rods and tackle are supplied during fishing charters, damaged or lost equipment will incur full replacement cost. 
you are welcome to bring your own rods and tackle. 
All alcohol is BYO but can be pre collected from Darwin upon request. 
meals can be provided with any extended tour. 
​ 
Packages can be designed to suit your needs, feel free to contact us to discuss.
