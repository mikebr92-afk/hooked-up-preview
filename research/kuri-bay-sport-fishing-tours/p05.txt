Frequently Asked Questions
Home &raquo; Frequently Asked Questions 
When is the best time to come to Kuri Bay? Choose the optimum tide of the month you wish to visit ( refer to tour packages ). With 30 years of experience fishing the largest tides in the Southern Hemisphere, we can assure you will catch fish on any moon phase.
We operate from March through to January, with the best months weather-wise from May through to August — but don't let this influence your fishing desires, as the fishing in the hotter months is brilliant.
What species of fish are we likely to tussle with? Where do we begin! All of the tropical species abound and more.
Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Fingermark Bream, Blue Bastards, Queenfish species, Trevally species, Cobia, Mackerel species, Sailfish, Malabar Cod, Blue Nose Salmon, Black Jew, Blue Lined Emperor, Bar Cheeked Coral Trout, Black Spotted Tuskfish, Permit, and a whole lot more.
How many guests can you accommodate? We have a minimum of 4 guests up to 7 guests, with larger groups a possibility on request.
What is the accommodation, food and amenities like? The accommodation is the old pearlers' quarters. It is dormitory style accommodation where each individual has their own room — very comfortable and private.
We also offer a self-contained cabin for couples or small families.
We pride ourselves on preparing and presenting quality meals, from the catch of the day to freshly grown garden greens. With a full commercial kitchen and top cooks you will not be disappointed.
What kind of boats do we fish from? We operate two 5.7m custom built vessels, Koyoyo and Bidadjin. These boats are ideal for the inshore fishing and creeks and estuaries, with a maximum of 3 anglers to each boat.
Our 8m custom built Saba San is used for the offshore fishing, with a 5 angler capacity. We also have a 4.3m vessel, Gundah, ideal for two passengers.
All vessels are constructed from aluminium and are built for Kimberley coast conditions.
When do we need to be in Broome? Definitely the night before your scheduled trip.
Do you supply tackle? Yes we do: 6kg to 15kg outfits, both spin and baitcasting, a wide range of lures, terminal tackle and some fly outfits.
We do encourage you to bring your favourite outfit and lures. We have a replacement policy if outfits are lost or broken when you choose to use ours.
What should we bring on tour? A 6 to 8 kilo baggage allowance applies on float plane transfers. Please make sure you don't go over the limit, to avoid the disappointment of leaving behind some of your personal belongings. If you do need extra luggage left in Broome whilst at Kuri Bay, we can arrange for it to be kept secure.
Clothing can be kept to a minimum as we have washing machines and dryers for your use.
• Tropical style clothing, a spray jacket and light jumper
• Sturdy boat shoes and runners or similar
• Wide brimmed hat and polarised sunglasses
• Personal toiletries and medication
Cameras, binoculars, battery chargers etc. are part of your 6-8kg limit. Please note you do not need to bring towels or any bedding.
What if I don't want to go fishing every day? What other activities are there at Kuri Bay? If you are a keen bird watcher (twitcher), you will be in your element. The bush and associated walks around Kuri Bay will delight you.
There is a TV room and games room if this tickles your fancy, and of course a pool to cool off in. Jetty fishing is on offer and can produce some nice fish. Or just kick back, pull up a comfy chair on the balcony and read a book.
Can we see any of the pearling operation? Yes, this is possible on request.
What time do we need to catch our flight? Kuri Bay Sport Fishing has the options of flying you to our remote destination in either a Grumman Mallard or Cessna Caravan. Flexibilty for all bookings is essential and the options make for a well planned fishing holiday to suit tides and the month of year you chose to go. Pick up time is determined closer to the departure date but no later than 9am.
What time do we arrive back in Broome after our stay at Kuri Bay? Under normal circumstances you would arrive back in Broome at around 11.00am.
Can we buy alcohol at Kuri Bay? All alcohol requirements you will need to pre-order with us. You will be charged the total price of your order plus a handling fee.
Do you cater for corporate groups? Yes! We can cater for larger groups on request.
