Getting there 
Fly in over the Horizontal Falls
Kuri Bay Sport Fishing uses both the Mallard Flying Boat and Cessna Caravans to transport guests from Broome.
We can tailor your trip to maximise the best tides for the fishing action you desire. The flight from Broome to Kuri Bay takes approx 70 minutes and is without a doubt a big highlight of your fishing adventure.

Accommodation and facilities
Kuri Bay offers very comfortable accommodation and facilities for our guests.
Spacious dormitory style accommodation with your own room that has 24 hr power, overhead fan, completely screened louvre windows and plenty of shelf space.
A balcony extends the length of the building with all the comforts to relax, enjoy the surrounds and mingle with your friends after a great day out on the water.
A well-appointed ablution block with hot and cold showers, flushing toilets, large sinks and basins, and a washing machine and dryer all make your stay in a very remote area extremely comfortable.
Also available is the air conditioned, self-contained manager's cabin that offers complete privacy and stunning views — ideal for families or couples.
Other facilities include
• Swimming pool
• Gymnasium
• Bar and TV
• Jetty
• Games room
• Limited internet
• Phone

Dining at Kuri Bay
The dining at Kuri Bay is without a doubt one of the highlights.
Local delicacies — fish, oysters, fresh pearl meat when in season, and fresh greens from the local patch — all combine to make your meals, overlooking the bay from the dining room balcony, an unforgettable experience.
Yummy lunches provided whilst out fishing, eaten on an isolated beach, only add to the culinary experience. We cater for special food needs if required.
