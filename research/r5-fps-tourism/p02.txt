Acknowledgment
Destination Port Stephens acknowledges the Worimi people as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waterways on which we live, work and welcome visitors. We pay our respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. As modern custodians, we embrace the responsibility to protect, celebrate, and share the stories of Worimi Barray (Country). We invite visitors to walk softly, connect deeply, and experience the enduring spirit of this incredible place.
