Your adventure starts here.
Located in the heart of Nelson Bay, The Port Stephens Visitor Information Centre is your one-stop shop for local tips, unforgettable experiences and easy holiday planning.
From accommodation advice and tour bookings, to maps, souvenirs and hidden gems – their friendly team is here to help you make the most of your Port Stephens adventure.
Need inspiration? Whether you're chasing family fun, adventure-filled days, relaxing beach escapes or local favourites – they're here to help you discover the very best of Port Stephens.
Everything you need. All in one place.

Acknowledgment
Destination Port Stephens acknowledges the Worimi people as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waterways on which we live, work and welcome visitors. We pay our respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. As modern custodians, we embrace the responsibility to protect, celebrate, and share the stories of Worimi Barray (Country). We invite visitors to walk softly, connect deeply, and experience the enduring spirit of this incredible place.
