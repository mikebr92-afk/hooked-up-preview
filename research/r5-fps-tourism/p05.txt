Tours
The best way to discover Port Stephens, NSW is with the locals who know it best. From the water to the sand dunes, national parks and hidden corners of the region, there’s a Port Stephens tour for almost every kind of explorer.
Whether you want wildlife, adventure, culture, nature or local knowledge, joining a guided tour in Port Stephens takes you beyond the view – connecting you with the people, stories and extraordinary places that make this destination unique.
Cruise from Nelson Bay in search of resident dolphins, migrating whales, seals and marine life, learn to surf and snorkel, or head onto the spectacular Stockton Sand Dunes and Worimi Conservation Lands for a 4WD, sandboarding, quad bike or Aboriginal cultural experience. 
Explore the coastline on foot with a local guide, discover the landscapes and stories of Tomaree National Park, or experience Port Stephens from a completely different perspective on a scenic sightseeing tour.
For visitors wanting to sit back and enjoy the journey, local touring and transport operators including Port Stephens Coaches make it easy to explore beyond your immediate holiday base and discover more of Port Stephens and the surrounding Hunter region.

Acknowledgment
Destination Port Stephens acknowledges the Worimi people as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waterways on which we live, work and welcome visitors. We pay our respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. As modern custodians, we embrace the responsibility to protect, celebrate, and share the stories of Worimi Barray (Country). We invite visitors to walk softly, connect deeply, and experience the enduring spirit of this incredible place.
