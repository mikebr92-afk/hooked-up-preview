Fish Port Stephens Estuary Charters – Your Local Fishing Adventure
Experience the thrill of fishing in the beautiful waterways of Port Stephens with Fish Port Stephens Estuary Charters. Owned and operated by passionate local angler Paul Lennon, these personalised fishing charters offer the perfect opportunity to explore the region’s spectacular estuary while learning from someone who knows these waters inside and out.
Whether you’re an experienced angler chasing your next big catch, a beginner keen to learn or a family looking for a memorable day on the water, each trip is tailored to suit your experience and interests. Depending on the season, you could target flathead, bream, whiting, mulloway, kingfish and more, all while enjoying the stunning scenery and abundant marine life of Port Stephens.
With expert local knowledge, quality fishing equipment and a friendly, hands-on approach, Fish Port Stephens Estuary Charters delivers an authentic fishing experience for locals and visitors alike.
Ready to cast a line? Book your Port Stephens fishing adventure and make some unforgettable memories on the water.

Destination Port Stephens acknowledges the Worimi people as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waterways on which we live, work and welcome visitors. We pay our respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. As modern custodians, we embrace the responsibility to protect, celebrate, and share the stories of Worimi Barray (Country).
