One Mile & Boat Harbour
Beaches, Dunes & Coastal Adventures
Discover some of the most spectacular coastal scenery in Port Stephens, NSW, from the towering Stockton Sand Dunes at Birubi Beach to the sweeping sands of One Mile Beach and the secluded coastline of Boat Harbour. Experience sandboarding, 4WD adventures and camel rides at Birubi. Catch a wave or relax by the beach at One Mile, 
then explore Boat Harbour’s rocky coastline, lookouts and beautiful ocean views, a great spot for whale watching during the annual migration. Surrounded by nature and just a short drive from Nelson Bay, this stretch of Port Stephens is perfect for surfing, swimming, coastal walks, family holidays and outdoor adventures.
Where the bush meets the rugged coastline - the neighbourhoods of One Mile and Boat Harbour is famous for its natural beauty, secluded beaches, stunning coastline walks, rainforest and koala habitats.
If you want to get back to nature or you're backpacking around the globe - this is the perfect destination. Find inner peace at a wellness centre, stay in a treehouse, eco resort, holiday park or backpackers. 
During May to November, you will find the locals perched on the rocks at Boat Harbour spotting the whales as they pass through the humpback highway on their annual migratory journey.
One Mile Beach is one of the most popular patrolled surf beaches in Port Stephens. A long (one mile in fact!), white sand beach perfect for both families and surfers is patrolled throughout the summer months. For those learning to surf to the more experienced in search of a clean wave One Mile is the place to be. Samurai Beach just north of One Mile is another beautiful spot accessible by 4WD and is a clothes optional beach.
TOP THINGS TO SEE & DO 
• One Mile Beach: Swim, surf or relax on one of Port Stephens’ most popular ocean beaches.
• One Mile Beach Reserve: Make use of the kiosk, picnic and barbecue areas, public amenities and shady reserve behind the dunes.
• Boat Harbour Beach: Enjoy a smaller coastal beach backed by the village and rocky headlands.
• Boat Harbour North Headland: Take in ocean views and enjoy picnic facilities, barbecues, seating and a playground.
• Whale watching: Look for migrating humpback whales from Boat Harbour’s headlands during the annual migration season.
• Kingsley Beach: Discover a secluded beach within Tomaree National Park, ideal for a quiet coastal stop.
• Coastal photography: Capture rugged cliffs, surf, secluded beaches and expansive Tasman Sea views.
WALKING PATHS
One Mile to Boat Harbour Walking Track: 
The walking path connecting Boat Harbour and One Mile Beach is a scenic 2.5km (one way) coastal track forming part of the regional Tomaree Coastal Walk inside Tomaree National Park. The route features uneven natural terrain, boardwalks and key highlights like the Slot Canyon Lookout. Distance is 5km return, between Boat Harbour and One Mile Beach. Time: 1 to 1.5 hours return. 
One Mile Beach Walk : Enjoy a long beach walk along the golden sands of One mile Beach, backed by dunes and coastal bushland. Great for an early morning or sunset stroll.
Boat Harbour Headland Walk: 
Explore the rocky coastline and headland around Boat Harbour, with beautiful ocean views and great vantage points for spotting whales during migration season.
Kingsley Beach to Fishermans Bay: 
Start at Kingsley Beach in Boat Harbour and follow the Tomaree Coastal Walk south towards Fishermans Bay. The track takes you through coastal bushland and rugged headlands, with beautiful ocean views. Return the same way or continue along the Tomaree Coastal Walk towards Birubi Point and Anna Bay for a longer adventure.
Getting Around
Local Bus – The local bus travels to One Mile and Boat Harbour multiple times a day taking you to Shoal Bay, Fingal Bay and beyond. See map and timetable . 
Ride Share – Ride-share is available in Port Stephens – avoid looking for parking – check the app to see if there is a driver near you.

LOCALS TIP 
• Keen to catch a wave? One mile Beach is a local favourite for surfing, with waves suited to beginners through to experienced surfers. Never surfed before? It’s also a great spot to book a lesson and give it a go!
• Take a stroll around Boat Harbour Headland and soak up the rugged coastal views – don’t forget to keep an eye out for whales and dolphins!
