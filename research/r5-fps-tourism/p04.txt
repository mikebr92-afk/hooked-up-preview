Eco-Tourism
In Port Stephens, our backyard isn’t just our playground – it’s home to some of Australia’s most beautiful and iconic wildlife. From dolphins and migrating humpback whales to kangaroos, koalas and the incredible marine life found beneath the surface, there are countless ways to experience nature here.
Experience wildlife responsibly
We want visitors to enjoy the wonder of our wildlife, but just as importantly, we want to help protect it. By travelling thoughtfully, choosing responsible wildlife experiences and taking a few simple steps while exploring, you can help keep Port Stephens’ natural environment thriving for generations to come.
Some of the best wildlife encounters happen when we give animals the space to behave naturally. Choose accredited and environmentally conscious operators who are committed to protecting the places and wildlife we all come to experience.
Port Stephens is home to a growing number of operators recognised for their commitment to sustainable and responsible tourism, including Moonshadow Cruises, Imagine Cruises & Dolphin Swim, Oakvale Wildlife Park, Irukandji – Home of Sea Shelter and The Koala Sanctuary.

Look, don’t touch
Wildlife is best appreciated from a respectful distance. Resist the temptation to touch, feed or chase animals, even when they appear friendly. Give wildlife space to move, feed and rest naturally, and never interfere with their behaviour for a photo.
Whether you’re watching dolphins in the bay, spotting a koala in the trees or encountering kangaroos in the wild, look, don’t touch and let them be wild.
Take three for the sea
Help keep our beaches, waterways and ocean free from litter. If you’re heading to the beach, out on the water or exploring our coastline, consider taking three pieces of rubbish with you — yours or someone else’s.
It’s a small action that can make a big difference to the health of our marine environment and the wildlife that calls it home.
Leave no trace
From our national parks and beaches to our waterways and bushland, leave Port Stephens as you found it, or better.
Take your rubbish with you, stick to designated tracks, respect wildlife and avoid taking shells, plants or other natural treasures home. Every small choice helps protect the landscapes and habitats that make Port Stephens so special.
Explore with care
Responsible tourism doesn’t mean experiencing less, it means experiencing more thoughtfully. By choosing sustainable operators, respecting wildlife and leaving our natural places better protected, you can enjoy the very best of Port Stephens while helping safeguard our backyard for the future.
These businesses are helping lead the way in responsible tourism, with accreditations and initiatives including Ecotourism Australia certification, ZAA accreditation and Sustainable Tourism accreditation. Choosing a recognised operator is one way visitors can enjoy an unforgettable experience while supporting businesses that are committed to caring for our natural environment.

Acknowledgment
Destination Port Stephens acknowledges the Worimi people as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waterways on which we live, work and welcome visitors. We pay our respects to Elders past, present and emerging, and extend that respect to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. As modern custodians, we embrace the responsibility to protect, celebrate, and share the stories of Worimi Barray (Country).
