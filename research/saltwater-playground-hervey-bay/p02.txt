Discover Unforgettable Fishing Adventures with Saltwater Playground 
Saltwater Playground offers guided fishing charters in the pristine waters of Hervey Bay, catering to both beginners and experienced anglers. Our passionate crew delivers memorable days on the water with great service and quality fishing.
We offer a range of trips to suit your style:
Full-day Charters explore the middle of the bay and along K’gari (Fraser Island), fishing proven hotspots like Roonies Point.
Half-day Charters focus on productive inshore waters, including bay islands, sand flats, and reef systems.
Giant Trevally - Topwater Charters chase powerful GTs in untouched waters along K’gari and offshore systems. Explosive surface strikes and hard-fighting fish make this an adrenaline-filled, bucket-list experience.
Whether you’re chasing a trophy fish or a relaxed day on the water, we’ve got the perfect fishing experience for you.
our guide 
Meet Shaun Dionysius – Your Expert Fishing Guide in Hervey Bay 
With over 15 years of experience fishing the waters of Hervey Bay, Shaun brings a wealth of knowledge, skill, and passion to every charter. Known for his uncanny ability to find fish and read the local conditions, Shaun consistently puts his guests onto quality catches.
Shaun’s easygoing nature and professional approach make him the perfect guide for anglers of all levels. Whether you’re a seasoned fisherman or picking up a rod for the first time, Shaun creates a relaxed and enjoyable experience while sharing tips and insights that will improve your fishing game.
Join Shaun for an unforgettable day on the water, exploring one of Australia’s most unique and productive fisheries—right here in Hervey Bay.

the boat - Bonito 660 
Bonito 660 – Built for Serious Offshore Fishing 
The Bonito 660 is the latest evolution in a legendary line of high-performance offshore racing and fishing boats. Renowned for its incredibly soft ride, the 660 is built to handle serious offshore conditions with comfort and confidence.
With a sharp 53-degree deadrise at the bow and 25 degrees at the transom, it cuts through chop with ease—making it one of the smoothest rides in its class. A massive 350L fuel tank gives you the range to go further, while serious fishing features like through-hull transducers, underfloor kill tanks, casting deck, bait tanks, bait boards, deck wash, side pockets , and a T-top ensure you’re always ready to chase the next big one.
Whether you're heading wide or fishing the flats, the Bonito 660 delivers performance, practicality, and purpose-built design—offshore fishing at its best.
