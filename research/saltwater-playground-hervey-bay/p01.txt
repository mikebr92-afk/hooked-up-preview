Hervey Bay Fishing Charters 
GT, pelagic & REEF Adventures on PLAYTIME 
Step aboard PLAYTIME and experience Hervey Bay fishing charters like never before! Explore the stunning, UNESCO World Heritage-listed waters around Fraser Island with an experienced local skipper and chase giant trevally, pelagics, reef species, and trophy fish . 
Our guided charters take you to the most unique fisheries on Australia’s East Coast, where the sheltered waters of Hervey Bay offer an incredible mix of northern and southern fish species. Whether you’re after Tuna, Golden Trevally, Cobia, Mackerel, Snapper, Black Marlin, or reef species , every trip is customized to deliver action-packed fishing adventures and unforgettable memories . 
Book your Hervey Bay fishing charter today and target the fish you’ve been dreaming of!
