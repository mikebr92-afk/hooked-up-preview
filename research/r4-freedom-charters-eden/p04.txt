Reef Fishing
Reef Fishing
Welcome to Snapper country, reef fishing at its best. With good numbers of Snapper caught on almost every trip, along with Morwong, Leather Jackets, and the ever popular Flathead.
Eden fishes well all year round on the inshore reefs with wide reef trips also available during February, March and April, Morwong, Ocean Perch and Shark.
Bookings are available as either a single angler, for groups of up to 12 people or for exclusive full or half day boat hire. Combination reef and game trips have also proved to be very successful.
Accommodation packages available on request. Recreational fishing licences included in the price.
We can provide lunch on day trips. We can also cater for any dietary requirements if we are advised prior; nuts, gluten free, etc.
Contact us for departure times as this may vary due to daylight savings.
