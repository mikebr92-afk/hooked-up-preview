Fishing & Boating
With so many waterways it's no wonder the Sapphire Coast is a premier boating & fishing destination. 
​ 
Start here with some tips & the fishing report from Merimbula Big Game and Lakes Angling Club. 
​ 
There's plenty of good rock and beach fishing at one of our many beaches , or head to Merimbula Wharf and throw a line in.
