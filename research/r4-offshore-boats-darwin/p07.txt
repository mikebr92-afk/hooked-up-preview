Darwin Multi-Day Fishing Charters
If you’re planning a once-in-a-lifetime fishing trip to the Top End, you’ve come to the right place. We offer fully customised, multi-day fishing packages tailored to suit you and your group. Whether you prefer the convenience of staying in a Darwin apartment or crave a remote adventure aboard an air-conditioned mothership, we’ve got you covered.
For a true wilderness experience, consider our Mothership Fishing Charters in pristine locations like Shady Camp and Melville Island . To learn more, visit our dedicated Mothership Fishing Charter page . 
Darwin Based Multi-Day Fishing Charters
Our Darwin-based charters offer flexibility, comfort, and access to a wide variety of fishing styles and locations. These trips allow you to target species such as Reef fish, Barramundi, Billfish, Mudcrabs, and more in areas including:
• Offshore Darwin
• Vernon Islands
• Cape Hotham
• Bynoe Harbour
• Shoal Bay
• Adelaide River
By using a single, experienced operator across your entire trip, you’ll benefit from flexibility to adapt each day based on weather, tides, and the best fishing conditions.
Many guests choose to stay in modern, self-contained apartments in Darwin City, giving them the best of both worlds: epic days on the water and relaxing evenings enjoying the city’s restaurants, bars, and the popular Darwin Waterfront precinct.
Our local, professional fishing guides are passionate about delivering an unforgettable experience—and will tailor your trip to suit your group’s interests and fishing goals.
Target Species & Techniques
Each day is an opportunity to chase a wide range of species across diverse waterways. Based on your preferences, techniques may include:
• Sportfishing – jigging for Spanish Mackerel, casting poppers for Trevally, chasing Tuna on the surface, or stickbaiting for Queenfish.
• Reef fishing – bait, vibes and plastics for Jewfish, Golden Snapper, Emperor, Nannygai, Coral Trout and more.
• Billfish – trolling spreads for Sailfish and Black Marlin.
• Barramundi – casting vibes and soft plastics, trolling hardbodies or live baiting.
• Mud crabbing – setting and checking the pots for a feast of Top End Muddies!
What’s Included
Each day of your multi day charter includes
• Light breakfast, lunch, snacks, and cold drinks
• All fishing gear, tackle, lures & jigs (no extra charge for lost tackle)
• Sunscreen & insect repellent
• Daily fish cleaning, filleting, and bagging
• Freezing and packaging of your catch in airline-approved seafood boxes, ready to check in on your flight home.
What to bring each day
• Polarised sunglasses
• Cap
• Camera
• Comfortable clothes
• Alcohol is BYO
Vessels, Capacity & Rates
We operate four custom-built vessels: Offsure , Sea Star , Tropic Thunder , and Reel Work , with both private and shared charter options available.
Shared Charters (max 6 guests): 
• $575 per person per day
Private Charters (per day): 
• Offsure, Sea Star or Tropic Thunder – $3,360 (up to 6 guests)
• Reel Work – $4,150 (up to 8 guests)
We can accommodate larger groups of up to 26 across our fleet.
Accommodation
Private charter prices exclude accommodation and dinners. However, one of the key advantages of our high-speed vessels is the ability to return to Darwin each evening—so you can unwind in comfort.
