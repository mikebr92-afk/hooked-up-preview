About Fish Smarter Courses
Fish Smarter is a collection of online fishing courses showing you step-by-step how to find fish consistently and increase your catch rates.
We give a No BS approach to fishing education which has been proven over years of charter success; catching big fish for clients.
WHAT YOU’LL LEARN IN EACH OF OUR COURSES
These courses will teach you to be a MASTER ANGLER, you won’t just be following the heard but blazing your own trails and finding you own spots once you’ve learned from our:
• Concept Videos – this is the THEORY – the why behind the framework
• Demo Videos – this is the PRACTICE – demonstrating in the field how use the techniques
• An Infographic “Framework” – this ties together the THEORY and PRACTICE in an extremely simplified way. THIS is what you will use at the end of the day. It takes the complexity out of all of the variables and gives you a step by step format to learn and repeat in your local area.
This is what we call fishing with strategy. 
The Completed Framework enables you to SAVE TIME searching and trying to work it out yourself.
WHO FISH SMARTER COURSES ARE FOR
ANYONE who wants to improve their catch rates and fishing enjoyment.
Fish Smarter students range from beginners to advanced anglers, even charter fishing guides and tournament anglers take our courses and are reaping the benefits. If you’re curious if the course are a fit for your level of skill or will work in your area, check out our Success Stories pages, we have ENDLESS feedback from our students that you read.
Our courses are for you if you have ever caught yourself thinking or saying:
• I love my fishing and I want to be the best that I can be
• I don’t want to rely on known spots I want to find my own
• There’s no fish left, the pro’s have caught them all
• I struggle to know where to go, when
• I have all the gear but no idea
• I catch fish one day but not the next, where am I going wrong?
HOW THE COURSES WORK
• The program is delivered in video format on our secured membership site with easy to follow, actionable steps for you to take.
• Watch the videos at your own pace and review as many times as you’d like
• Lifetime access to course content
• Course upgrades (new videos) are included in your purchase
• Community of like-minded anglers to share trip reports, learn together and in some cases go fishing together.
• Access to Ryan – once you’re in our communities Ryan gives regular feedback and assistance, along with periodic webinars for all course participants.
DON’T JUST TAKE OUR WORD FOR IT
The power of the internet has opened up countless opportunities for people to attain knowledge.
And while many people scoffed “how can you teach fishing online?”, we have proved through our online fishing course that it’s not only possible, BUT IT REALLY WORKS.
Ryan has spent countless hours on the water, baking in the sun, identifying patterns over time that would otherwise be unknown.
He has identified strategies to learn fish behaviour that took me years to work out.
And with the help of Karen’s years at university for Marine Biology, we have put together a framework that anyone can learn.

Ryan's Story
I'm proud to have been helping people like you catch bucket list fish for over 30 years. During my fishing career, I held my hard earned secrets VERY close to my chest (typical fisherman ha-ha). 
But now my charter years are finished and I'm ready to share my secrets through my online courses. Call it my legacy. 
Ryan’s Story
One of the things I'm often asked is:
"HOW DID YOU GET STARTED IN ALL THIS?" 
Well... They say I was 3 when my Dad dragged me kicking and screaming into the boat for my first Maggie Island fishing trip. They also say he had to drag me out when the trip was complete.
Guess I was hooked from the start.
By 16 I was on the mackerel boats and at 18 was rubbing shoulders with the likes of INXS, the King and Queen of Sweden and boxing legend Barry Michaels chasing giant black marlin off the shelf.
In 1989 we took out the Sheraton international billfish tournament as well as the 4th Lizard Island Black Marlin Classic as well as second in the Australian International Billfish Tournament and second in the Dunk Island Billfish Classic. I had the pleasure to work alongside some of the best game fishing skippers in Australia including Greg Edwards, Calvin Tilley and Chris Jones.
I have been lucky enough to see the most amazing things during my career on the water in one of the most beautiful locations in the world, the Great Barrier Reef.
1989 was also the year I started my own fishing charter business, now Ryan Moody Fishing. So far I have had the privilege of assisting clients catch a milestone 2000 barra over one metre and fulfil their dreams of joining the metre barra club. That's over 2 kilometres of metre plus fish.
It's been a wild old ride, with green zones wiping out my reef business and two category 5 cyclones wreaking havoc, and at times I was so close to giving it all away... but I can't.
I love putting people onto fish. I think I get a little high, it gives me such a thrill.
And I am even more excited about the future. It gives me such pleasure to have witnessed the swing away from catch and kill to catch and release.
It seems hard to believe, but the euphoria of catching such a magnificent Australian sports fish is actually surpassed by the emotional rush of letting it go.
I've been fortunate enough to be featured in several successful fishing shows, radio broadcasts and popular fishing magazines. I hold the honour of landing the 2 biggest saltwater barra ever caught on Australian TV (Eddie Riddle's Fishin 4 Wheels). I wrote a regular column for Queensland Fishing Monthly (QFM) and was a Product Ambassador for Evinrude (BRP) outboards, Garmin Marine Electronics and Jarvis Walker Brands here in Australia.
Learn more about this on our Media page . 
So it is with much gratitude that I would like to share some of what I have learned along the way.
I'm here to show you how to dream big and take strategic action to become the best angler you can be.
I hope you enjoy the website we have created for you.
Thank you for taking the time to visit. I know your time is so valuable. I hope you'll be inspired to get out amongst it and live your dreams, as I have.
I met my lovely wife Karen when I was still doing charters, and she was an award-winning Marine Biologist.
Our first course was born in 2014 when we combined forces, my knowledge of fishing, and her ability to extract it into easy frameworks that anyone can learn.
