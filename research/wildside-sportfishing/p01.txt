SOUTH WEST ROCKS FISHING CHARTERS
Premium guided sport, reef & game fishing charters out of South West Rocks in Northern NSW, Australia.
Learn More 
Welcome to Wildside Sportfishing! One of NSW most popular fishing charters located in the spectacular sea side town of South West Rocks..
Our custom fishing tours have been created by passionate owner & lifelong angler, Jason Bettles, for fishing enthusiasts of all levels & ages – beginners through to expert.
No two trips are the same onboard Wildside! The only guarantee, is that your guide will put in 100% to try and provide the best fishing charter you’ve ever had.
Expertise and local knowledge are pitted against ever changing weather conditions, ocean currents, tides and fish behavior to provide you with the ultimate mid north coast fishing expedition. 
Offering small group sizes so you get personalized service, one on one tuition and space to fish effectively .
Fully licensed and insured, we have over 22 years experience operating fishing tours.
Watch our video 
‘Wildside’ 
Our vessel , a Contender 25 Tournament named Wildside , is surveyed for 30 nautical miles (55 kms) offshore. It is fast, comfortable and a true sport fishing weapon.
Wildside is fully set up for all forms of angling, including game fishing South West Rocks . Twin 200hp four stroke outboards, 112lb Minnkota, downrigger, outriggers, large live bait tank, flying gaffs, tail ropes, Black Magic Equalizer harness, high / low chirp sonar and plus side and down scan – just some of the equipment we have on board.
learn more 
What we catch
Target a range of species from Tuna, Snapper, Mackerel, Pearl Perch, Dollies, Kingfish and Marlin offshore to Mulloway, Jacks and Flathead in the Macleay River. No fish is safe!
Vertical jigging, soft plastic finesse, surface and sub surface stick baits, high speed spinning and trolling for Billfish are some of the techniques regularly used. We also have a massive live bait well on board which is filled with small fish such as slimey mackerel and yellowtail, at the start of a tour – covering all bases.

Fishing perfection-yet again
Our 3rd year in a row with Jason from Wildside Sportsfishing (having booked the 2 other local ones multiple times over the years) and it just gets better and better. Jason’s boat is immaculate, clean and organised with top notch gear. It’s also super fast so you get from great fishing spot to fishing spot without hours and hours of sitting around doing nothing.
Jason works tirelessly to make sure you catch excellent and varied fish. Literally trying multiple techniques and secret spots constantly until you come up with the goods. He really knows his stuff, is the speediest and most dry humoured deckie cum skipper ever and we really really enjoy a day out with him.
This year (yesterday) between 3 of us we caught 2 black marlin (safely released) a huge wahoo (dinner!) plus 2 massive bull Mahi Mahi’s and multiple good sized ones plus a great fun kingfish that was just undersized and released safely.
Last year we also went out with him and caught 3 huge mackeral-one striped one spotted one Spanish. Catches of a lifetime. I couldn’t recommend Wildside highly enough and wouldn’t consider using anyone in south west rocks again. Yes slightly more expensive than the others but you catch quality fish in a good environment without being overcrowded/having people smoking on top of you/waiting ages for lures to be to changed/tangles to be rectified etc etc
