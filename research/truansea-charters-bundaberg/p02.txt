Full Day Bundaberg Fishing Charters 
A full day of action
Our speciality- A 10 Hour day chasing all the beautiful tropical species that the Great Barrier Reef has to offer, including the highly sought after Coral Trout and Red Emperor! 
We are so blessed to have fishing grounds from a few miles out reaching 50 odd nautical miles to the shelf. As we want to get you on to that once-in-a-lifetime fish, we will usually fish quite wide so assume around 2+ hours travel each way (at high speed) to reach our fishing spots. 
Our maximum number of fisherpeople is 6 so you will never be crowded on your reef fishing charter. 
Don't forget a cooler box/ice in your car if you want to take home your catch as recreational bag limits apply. 
$400pp inc GST for the whole day, including all gear, fuel, bait, ice, laughs! 
Private Charters are available- cost is $2400 inc GST for up to 6 guests 
All fishing equipment/bait/tackle is supplied, but please bring your own reef fishing gear if you prefer. Cold water and soft drinks are also supplied. Feel free to bring your own beverages and don't forget your own food. 
*please note that you are welcome to pay with a credit/eftpos card on the day, but a 2.2% surcharge applies to card payments (that is exactly what we get charged by our Square EFT provider - sorry!) 
*Bundaberg Fishing Charters 
Full day reef fishing
A Full Day Reef Fishing Charter is 10 hours, usually 6am to 4pm, departing from the Burnett Heads Boat Ramp, Bundaberg. 
For an earlier/later departure, simply call us to discuss.
