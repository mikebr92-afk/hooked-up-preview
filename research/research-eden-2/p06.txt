Merimbula Activities & Attractions
Fishing/Fishing Charters
For the rod and line fisherman and kids, great opportunities to land a decent fish from the wharfs around Merimbula or try your luck from surf beaches. Merimbula has some of the best game fishing on the coast with charter boats available to take you out after the big ones. The game fishing season starts in November and runs through to the end of July.
