About
Freedom Charters in Eden offers an exceptional fishing experience for both seasoned anglers and beginners. Their expert guides, top-quality equipment, and genuine love for the sport create the perfect environment to reel in your ideal catch. The purpose-built fishing vessels allow you to explore the pristine waters of Eden and encounter a diverse range of fish species. Customers have praised their friendly and helpful staff, as well as the opportunity to enjoy whale watching during the off-season.
Why you should go
1
• Experience fantastic fishing trips with an expert skipper like Mark
2
• Catch a variety of fish species including red snapper and pikes
3
• Enjoy sightings of Whales, Dolphins, and Seals during your trip

Know before you go
• Book in advance to secure your spot on Freedom Charters especially during peak seasons or holidays
• Arrive early to make the most of your fishing experience; boats are prepared beforehand
• Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, a hat, comfortable clothing suitable for sea conditions
• Consider bringing motion sickness medication if you're prone to seasickness as waters can be choppy at times in Eden

Reviews
Freedom Charters in Eden has garnered exceptional reviews from visitors who have experienced fantastic fishing trips with Mark, the excellent skipper. Guests have praised the abundance of fish caught, including red snapper and pikes, as well as sightings of Whales, Dolphins, and Seals. The charter is highly recommended for its friendly and helpful staff like Christine for bookings and deck hand Lyndsey. Visitors appreciate the organized boat setup, great instructions provided during the trip, complimentary coffee and biscuits for morning tea, and even having freshly caught fish scaled and gutted by Mark. The charters are described as perfect for various occasions like bucks parties or family outings due to their knowledgeable crew led by Mark. Review score
