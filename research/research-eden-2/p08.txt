About
We provide on the charter, but the success of catching fish or seeing whales depends on various factors such as weather conditions and the behaviour of the marine life. However, our experienced crew will do their best to maximise your chances of a successful and enjoyable trip.
At Headland Fishing Charters, we offer a variety of trips to cater to different preferences and occasions. Our Reef Fishing trips target species such as Snapper, Morwong, Flathead, and Gummy Shark. These trips are perfect for those looking to reel in some delicious seafood and enjoy the beauty of the ocean. During these trips, it is not uncommon to spot Dolphin pods and perhaps even Whales if they are in season.
We provide all necessary fishing equipment and bait, and upon returning to shore, we clean the fish for you to take with you. Simply bring your own hat, sunscreen, food, and drinks, and we will take care of the rest. Our Reef Fishing trips are available as either shared charters or exclusive hires and cater to a maximum of 12 people. Prices start at $140 for adults and $120 for children under 16.
For the thrill-seekers and serious anglers, we offer Game Fishing trips targeting Black, Blue, and Striped Marlin, Yellow Fin Tuna, and Albacore. Available for exclusive hire only, these full-day trips are available in the months of January, February, March, and April, with prices starting at $1500. These trips are ideal for those looking for an adrenaline rush and the opportunity to catch some prized game fish.
During the months of September, October, and November, we also offer Whale Watching cruises. This is your chance to witness the awe-inspiring sight of Humpback Whales on their migration along the 'Humpback Highway. ' Our cruises last between 2 to 3 hours, depending on the whale's location and weather conditions. Whether you're a nature enthusiast or simply want to experience something truly unforgettable, our Whale Watching trips are a must-do activity.
Please bring your hat, sunscreen, water, snacks, and don't forget your camera!
Headland Fishing Charters has been operating in Merimbula and its surrounding areas for over 25 years, making us experts in the local fishing scene. Our vessel, Jadele II, is known as “the best fishing boat in town. ” With a length of 38ft and full Maritime survey certification for 12 passengers and 2 crew, Jadele II offers ample deck space for everyone, comfortable seating, and an onboard toilet.
Located in Merimbula, the tourist hub of the Sapphire Coast, our charter gives you the opportunity to explore the stunning beaches and pristine waters of this beautiful part of Australia. With countless fishing spots both offshore and onshore, the Sapphire Coast offers a fishing experience like no other.
To book your fishing adventure with Headland Fishing Charters, simply give us a call at 0448 451 134. If you have any questions, please refer to our FAQs section on our website. Please note that the success of catching fish or sighting whales cannot be guaranteed, as it depends on various factors beyond our control. However, we assure you that our experienced crew will go above and beyond to make your charter trip a memorable experience.

Frequently Asked Questions
What fishing or cruise experiences do you offer in Merimbula? 
We offer Reef Fishing, Game Fishing, and Whale Watching cruises. Reef fishing targets species such as Snapper, Morwong, Flathead, and Gummy Shark.
When do your whale watching cruises run? 
Whale watching cruises run in September, October, and November during the whales migration.
How do I book a shared trip or exclusive hire? 
Call 0448 451 134 to book. We offer shared trips and exclusive hire with package deals for occasions like birthdays and work functions.
