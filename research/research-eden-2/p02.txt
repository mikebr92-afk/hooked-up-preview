This three-hour charter is ideal for the beginner through to the keen angler.
The local reefs are plentiful year-round. Snapper, Morwong, Tiger and Sand Flathead, Trevally, Nannygai, Leatherjacket, not to mention the many varieties of Wrasses and Gropers are bottom bouncers' delight.
So if you are looking for that special variety of tablefish, be assured that the Sapphire Coast has them!
The boat Sensational II is 14 metres. Powered by three 425hp Yamaha outboards they get you out to the fishing grounds in comfort and speed.
She is in the AMSA survey for 50 people however they fish a maximum of 14 people to ensure you have plenty of space.
Onboard Toilet. Quality fishing rods and reels are provided. No fishing licence is required. Fish cleaned and bagged for you to take home and enjoy!
