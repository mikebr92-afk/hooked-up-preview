Description
Game and reef fishing aboard Rathlin II. Merimbula's most prestigious, professional and successful charter boat. Rathlin II Charters specialise in Marlin, Tuna, Sharks, Broadbill Swordfish and a wide variety of reef species. Having fished extensively all over Australia and many overseas destinations, contributor to leading fishing magazines and radio talk show regular, Captain Bruce Libbis offers you the best opportunity to catch that fish of a lifetime.
