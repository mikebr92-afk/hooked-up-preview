Sword Fish & Snapper
Fishing Charters Offshore Lakes Entrance have been booming with some great catches. 
Lakes Entrance has had a tough ride with Bush fires and then Covid, but the people coming to town over the past couple of months show that the community has our back and to see the Tourism industry, Hotels, Motels, Shops and the town buzzing again is great.
Now we are in May it`s pinkie Snapper time again. These fish turn up in Large numbers and we know where they hang out, so grab some mates and come to Lakes Entrance for a couple of days and we will send you home with a great feed of fish to enjoy with your family.
Sword fish season is also upon us and plenty have been weighed in. Waiting for the right weather conditions is a must when traveling 100km Offshore, and for the first couple of Sword charters I had booked, we gave it a miss because safety is first thing when planing a trip like that.
Looking forward to a great winter here in Lakes Entrance, it’s the only place you can catch Snapper in Victoria 12 months of the year.
Cheers Captain Tony Kemna
