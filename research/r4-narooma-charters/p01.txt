Barunguba Montague Island Tours with Narooma Charters 
Enjoy an island tour today!
Narooma Charters have provided Barunguba
Montague Island tours and
whale watching in Narooma for over 3 decades! Join us
on an excursion to the island and have the trip of a lifetime. You’ll enjoy with
your friends & family regardless of the season. 
From two of Narooma’s largest charter boats, our experienced crew will take you out to
Barunguba Montague Island in comfort. On your outing you can
catch fish , or go snorkelling or
diving with the friendly seals . You can explore Montague
Island with a guided tour. The view, room and comfort aboard our boats in unparalleled
– join us and relax!

WHALE WATCHING TOURS
Have a whale of a time with us
Pods of up to 10 whales are a common sight on
our whale watching tours , and they often swim to the
boat where they seem curious about the people watching them. In the last ten years we have
had a whale spotting success rate of 100%. 
Each pod of whales on their southern migration stops in the area for up to four weeks and
feeds on krill or jellyfish, and when they have had their fill they frolic around the
pristine waters of Montague Island.
More about whale watching See Pricing 
BARUNGUBA MONTAGUE ISLAND TOURS
Island expeditions with local knowledge
Visit Barunguba Montague Island , the eco-tourism hot
spot of the South Coast. 
Narooma is one thing, but the area comes alive when you leave the dock! And we know pretty
much all of Narooma’s secrets – because we’ve been showing people around since 1983.
Check out the seals and penguins of Montague Island and
stop for dolphins or whales along the way (season permitting). All tours involve a 25 minute
boat trip there and back. A Montague Island tour
is something that you have to experience – check out
our new gallery and see for yourself !

FISHING CHARTER TOURS
You’ll be the one who got away
At Narooma Charters we have
specialist fishing boats , the
latest equipment, and (most importantly) the best local knowledge. 
When it comes to fishing,
our 12.5 metre boats are some of
the largest charter vessels in the area and we are committed to giving you a fishing
experience that will bring you back for more.
On your trip, your fishing licences are covered and all tackle is included (on a ‘replace if
lost’ basis) and you get to keep all the fish you catch. So join us in Narooma for a fishing
trip you won’t forget – a holiday just isn’t complete without the thrill
of some big catches!

DIVE CHARTER TOURS
Dive in with Narooma Charters
Barunguba Montague Island is one of
diving ’s most diverse and best kept
secrets. 
Barunguba’s unique location offers dozens of dive locations and the advantage of dives in
almost any weather conditions. It is recognised as a world-class diving destination with
wonderful surprises throughout the various seasons. Not up for a Scuba dive? No worries
– let’s go snorkelling with the seals!

What to See & Do
Narooma , on the south coast of New South Wales, is one of the most beautiful places in
Australia. 
Since 1983 Narooma Charters has built a reputation as one of the most reliable and
consistent charter operations on the south coast.
Whether it’s a tour of
Barunguba Montague Island 
on your holiday, whale watching, snorkelling with seals, diving, or fishing
– Narooma Charters will show you the best the south coast has to offer.
