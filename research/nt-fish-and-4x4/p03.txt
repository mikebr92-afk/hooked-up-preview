​ Adventure experiences 
Ever dreamed of the ultimate top end experience? 
exploring untouched locations, gathering & tasting bush tukker, fishing in exclusive locations. or even camping under the stars. 
we have the capacity and contacts to make this happen and pride ourselves on providing a custom unique experience to suit your needs. 
Below are some suggested packages we offer. 
contact us to create your own unique experience today. 
​ 
Follow us on Facebook or Instagram for promotional offers and deals. ​ 
​ 
5 DAY BARRA & DRIVE. 
Available year round 
​ 
DAY 1 
Enjoy being collected from the airport or your motel in Darwin. 
Travel to the historic Adelaide river Inn and enjoy lunch, then cruise on to the majestic Daly River in air conditioned comfort. 
Arrive at our accommodation and allow us to unpack your luggage. 
Spend a relaxing afternoon in the pool and let your guide share some secrets whilst you prepare for 3 days of Barramundi Fishing. 
enjoy a BBQ dinner then Turn in for the evening and prepare for the next day. 
​ 
DAY 2 - 4 
Enjoy a continental breakfast and then hit the river for a full day of Barramundi fishing action. 
Finish the day with a cold beer while the guide cleans your catch and enjoy a meal at the venue of your choice (Banyan farm tourist park or the Daly River Inn) and then turn in for the evening in our air conditioned rural retreat. 
​ 
DAY 5 
Enjoy a continental breakfast whilst your luggage is being loaded for you. 
Then prepare to return to Darwin via Berry Springs tavern for the last beer and story before arriving back at the airport or your motel. 
​ 
$3250 per person all inclusive 
Additional fishing days can be added. Destinations can be altered for the Drive days of this package. 
Package includes lunch, snacks and dinner for the duration. 
Drinks are provided in the form of bottled water, juices and soft drink 
all alcohol is BYO and can be collected in transit. 
supply of rods and tackle ( see information page for conditions) 
All bedding and linen provided in accommodation. ​ ​ 
​ 
THE DRY SEASON ESCAPE 
Available from July to October 
Enjoy the best of the dry season and experience the ultimate 3 day adventure . 
​ 
Day 1 
Pick up from the airport or motel of choice, travel to Berry springs tavern for a late breakfast or early lunch. 
Travel through the breath taking Litchfield national park and experience the Reynolds 4x4 track with a swim at surprise falls. continue on to Daly River and settle into your accommodation for the evening enjoying a BBQ dinner . 
​ 
Day 2 
S tart the day with a continental breakfast then embark on a full day fishing trip chasing barramundi on the mighty Daly River, return to the accommodation to freshen up for dinner whilst the guide fillets your catch. 
​ 
Day 3 
S tart the day with a continental breakfast then embark on a local tour of Daly River before heading back to Darwin to be Dropped off at a location of your choice within Darwin. 
​ 
All inclusive $2150 per person . 
minimum 2 ppl to commence maximum 4ppl 
non alcoholic drinks, snacks and meals provided for the duration. 
linen provided in accommodation. 
​ 
THE BIG DAY OUT 
Available year round but tide a weather dependent. 
minimum 3 ppl to commence. 
​ 
Due to the nature of this charter we often recommend booking our accommodation the night before and after the charter. 
The big day out is exactly what it suggests and only recommended for people who can endure up to 12 hours aboard our vessel in tropical conditions. 
​ 
Depart from the Daly River public boat ramp at first light with your toasted sandwich for breakfast. 
Travel over 220ks down & back on the magnificent Daly River, enjoying the scenic flats and flood plains filled with bird life. 
Arrive at Anson bay and fish the many coastal creeks, chasing salt water barramundi, mangrove jacks and salmon. 
Saddle up with your fish on ice and take the 2.5 hour trip back up the river whilst enjoying a cool drink. 
Arrive back at the ramp on sunset. 
$650 per person . 
​ 
Please note NT Fish & 4x4 does not fillet or process reef fish only Barramundi. filleting table available at accommodation .
