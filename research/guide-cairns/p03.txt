Frequently Asked Questions
I’m not an experienced angler, is that ok?
Our anglers come from all walks of life and have all levels of fishing experience from beginners to serious anglers, we cater for everyone and enjoy nothing more than offering advice to those that are looking to improving their fishing skills.
Where do I check-in / meet for my charter?
✔ Check-in is at 6:00am ✔ Shop G10 – The Pier Shopping Centre ✔ 1 Pier Point Road, Cairns City
Google Map Link - The Pier Shopping Centre 
Click on the images below to enlarge the map for easier viewing.
What should I bring on a charter fishing trip?
Make sure you bring your hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, towel, cash and a camera. Otherwise, we’ll look after you!
How do you look after my safety on board?
Your safety is our number one priority and whilst we want everyone to relax and have a great day, we want it done safely. Our boats carry the latest in navigation and safety equipment and our crew are experienced, well-trained professionals. You can help ensure your safety by always following our instructions.
How many people will be on the boat?
We run with a minimum of 9 and up to 12 on Blue Whaler and 14 on Aquacat on share charters. Our boats are large and comfortable.
What should I wear charter fishing?
We have a fully covered deck area on our boats, however, wearing a long-sleeve shirt is always recommended to protect you from the sun and board shorts or comfortable pants. We sell CRF caps and t-shirts if you want to look the best out of your crew! Remember to apply sunscreen everywhere, feet, neck and legs too!
I have a dietary requirement, are there food options for me on board?
Yes! We cater for all types of dietary requirements. It is important you reach out to us and advise us of your dietary requirements or note it at the time of booking.
Do you only do fishing charters? 
We don’t just go fishing! You can organise a full day private snorkelling charter to Michaelmas Cay or check out our private charters page for our Fitzroy Island snorkel + BBQ half day or our sunset Cairns river cruise + BBQ. Perfect for those who don’t want to fish!
Are there bathrooms onboard?
Yes! All our vessels have full-size bathroom facilities on board.
Can I bring my children?
Children aged 10 years and over are welcome to join our Cairns Reef Fishing Shared Charters.
Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their children at all times and should be mindful of other paying guests on shared charters.
Please refer to our terms and conditions or contact us by phone prior to booking to discuss age suitability. Any exceptions to the minimum age must be approved in advance over the phone and may be considered for children with previous charter experience or for private charters only.
Can you fish off Fitzroy Island? 
Whilst fishing at Fitzroy Island is allowed, the area has been fairly heavily fished in the past and we choose to help regenerate the fish stocks by not fishing there on our 5 hour BBQ charters.
How much fish is caught in the Great Barrier Reef?
The Great Barrier Reef is an incredible resource for both commercial and recreational fishing and is one of the best managed, most sustainable fisheries in the world.
What are the best months for deep sea fishing?
Reef fishing off Cairns is generally consistent year-round, supported by warm sea temperatures throughout the year. Seasonal variation is relatively minor, although conditions during the warmer wet season months from January to March can be more variable due to weather patterns and water movement. During this period, bottom fishing can at times be less predictable, while pelagic species such as mackerel and tuna are often more active. Weather and sea conditions play a greater role than season alone, and different species respond differently to changes in temperature, currents, and bait availability.
Where are crocodiles in Cairns?
Crocodiles are common in both salt and fresh water in Cairns, particularly around estuaries (where the sea meets land). Don’t worry, there aren’t any crocs where we are fishing.
What fish can you catch in reef fishing?
Our target fish are coral trout, large & small mouth Nannygai, Red Emperor, Spanish Mackerel and Trevally. We may encounter Spangled Emperor, Cod, GT’s, Reef Jacks, Dolphin Fish, Sailfish, Wahoo, Marlin and Tuna.
What shoes should I wear on a fishing charter?
You can wear anything comfortable that can get wet, bare foot is ok and is preferable to thongs, please don’t wear heeled shoes.
