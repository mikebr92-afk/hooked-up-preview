DAY TRIP ENQUIRY 
Flats Fishing
Species: Golden trevally, queenfish, permit, tuskfish, GTs and more. Locations: Tropical flats fishing around islands, coastal flats and flats inside rivers. Sight Fishing: Crystal-clear water offering great sight fishing opportunities.
Our flats fishing options are weather dependent. If it’s too windy on the day we’ll fish inside the river or estuary instead.
River & Estuary
Species: Barramundi, mangrove jack, GT, tarpon, queenfish and more. Technique: Edge and snag fishing, surface and sinking lines. Great sight fishing options in the right conditions. Locations: Mangrove-lined rivers, flats inside rivers, lagoons, snags and runoff drains.
Tropical Sportfisher specializes in guided fly fishing and we will work to the conditions to offer the best fishing possible.
Freshwater
Species: Jungle perch, sooty grunter and barramundi. Technique: Dry flies, bass bugs, poppers and large hoppers. Fitness: Easy to moderate hiking with some cooling rainforest pools. Locations: Cairns, Port Douglas, Mission Beach. Pick-up & Drop-off: Cairns CBD or Port Douglas.
Full Day: 6:30am – 4:30pm Half Day: 6:30am – 12:00pm
Tinaroo Fly Fishing
Target: Giant impoundment barramundi, 1m+ fish.
Lake Tinaroo: The first ever stocked barramundi lake and famous for producing many world record barramundi.
Technique: Fishing brush flies on intermediate lines along grass edges, with sight fishing opportunities in shallow bays under the right conditions.
Sounder Tech: We use our Humminbird sounder to help locate fish.
Season: October – April (warmer months only).
Fly Fishing in the Tropics with Us
Specialists: Covering all aspects of tropical fly fishing. Exclusive: The only specialised guided fly fishing operator in the Cairns region. Local Expertise: Our guides live and breathe fly fishing.

Private Fishing Charters & Nature Tours 
Tropical Sportfisher is a locally owned fly and sportfishing charter based in Cairns, Far North Queensland. Led by experienced local guides, we specialise in guided barramundi fishing and light tackle lure and fly fishing across Cairns, Port Douglas, and Mission Beach .
We run private day trips for up to 2 anglers per boat, and offer multi-day remote adventures for small groups, including mothership and heli-access options.
Our trips include river, estuary, jungle perch, and inshore bluewater fishing. We also offer wildlife cruises , croc spotting, birdwatching, and private nature or photography trips to secluded beaches, waterfalls, or tropical islands.
Whether you’re chasing fish or exploring wild places, our charters are tailored to your interests and the conditions. Get in touch to experience the best of Tropical North Queensland with a local guide.
