Overview 
Location 
Better Than Working Charters operates from San Remo.
Description
Scenic Cruises with spectacular views, wildlife spotting and stargazing. 
Fishing in Western Port, Bass Strait and around Phillip Island. 
We take smaller groups so that you fish in comfort not in crowds. 
All bait and gear is supplied. 
We have complimentary tea, coffee, hot chocolate, water, biscuits and chips on board. 
Book a trip now and enjoy a great day out on our very comfortable boat. 
We look forward to welcoming you soon.
