• Gone Fishing Charters
Gone Fishing Charters is a leading provider of fishing charters in Queenscliff, Victoria, offering unforgettable experiences on the waters of Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait.
Conveniently located in Queenscliff, at the entrance to Port Phillip, this locally operated charter specialises in deep sea fishing, bay fishing trips, and private fishing charters for individuals, families, and corporate groups.
Known for targeting premium local species such as snapper, King George whiting, flathead, and gummy shark, Gone Fishing Charters provides all fishing equipment, bait, tackle, and safety gear. Whether you're an experienced angler or booking your first guided fishing tour in Victoria, the knowledgeable skippers deliver expert advice and access to productive fishing grounds.
Queenscliff's prime coastal location offers diverse fishing environments, from sheltered bay waters to offshore, making it one of the top destinations for fishing charters in Victoria. With a focus on safety, customer service, and consistent catch rates, Gone Fishing Charters is a trusted choice for anyone seeking a professional Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait fishing charter experience.

Gone Fishing Charters
Gone Fishing Charters offers professional fishing charters departing from Queenscliff Harbour, servicing the Bellarine Peninsula, Port Phillip Bay and offshore water. They target snapper, gummy shark, whiting and seasonal pelagic fishing trips, like their tuna and king fish trips, with Portsea pick-up available by arrangement.
Their 10.5 metre custom aluminum vessel Poseidon, provides a safe, stable and comfortable fishing experience for your shared or private fishing trips.

Queenscliff 5 hour Fishing Charters
Their five-hour Queenscliff fishing charter is all about enjoying your day out on the water. They focus on what's biting on the day, which could be ranging from squid, whiting, flatties, snapper and reef fish. Generally they fish Port Philip Bay however they can also move out to Bass Strait if the weather permits.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge Geelong and The Bellarine is Wadawurrung country. We welcome visitors to our region, a place we acknowledge has been cared for by the Wadawurrung people of the Kulin Nation for thousands of years. The environment is a key reason visitors choose this destination. Traditional Owners sharing the beauty and joy of these lands and waterways is a privilege, one we repay with respect for their country, Elders and people.
