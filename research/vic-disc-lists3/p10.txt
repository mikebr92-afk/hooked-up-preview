8 Hour Charter
Our 8 hour fishing charter is now available year round to enable all types of fishing and enough time for us to get the results we strive for, which may include heading off-shore for species such as Blue Fin Tuna, Shark (Gummy, School and Mako), Yellowtail KingFish and reef species including Snapper, Morwong, Nannygai etc.
Available: Year round
Per Person: $400 or
Private Boat Weekdays : $1,800 (up to 5 people)
Private Boat Weekends : $2,000 (up to 5 people)
Including public holidays & peak season 26 Dec - 31 Jan
(Not recommended for children U12 years)

General Queries
Things to Bring
• Victorian Fishing Licence
• Food & Drink
• Warm clothing
• Non-slip footwear
• Seasickness tablets if required
• Esky in the boot of your car to get your catch home*
* We clean the catch for our customers, so all you have to do is enjoy your freshly caught fish. 
Charters depart from either 
a) Port Fairy Boat Ramp, 26 Griffiths Street or
b) Portland New Boat Ramp, off Lee Breakwater Road
