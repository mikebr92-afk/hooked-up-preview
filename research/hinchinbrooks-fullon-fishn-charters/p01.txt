Guided Barramundi Fishing Trips in North Queensland
The ultimate Hinchinbrook Barramundi Fishing Charter & Estuary Adventure in Lucinda, North Queensland.
Nathan Kemp offers the ultimate Hinchinbrook Barramundi Fishing Charter, providing world-class Barra and estuary fishing in North Queensland’s stunning Hinchinbrook Channel.
Whether you’re after a day trip or a tailored multi-day experience with accommodation, meals, and personalised itineraries, Full On Fish’n Charters provides everything you need for the perfect fishing getaway.
Barra & Estuary Fishing
Come aboard on our expert-guided fishing charter. Anglers have a chance to reel in some of North QLD’s most sought-after species, including barramundi, mangrove jack, and fingermark, while enjoying breathtaking scenery and top-tier fishing technology.
Experienced Guide
Whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned angler, our local guide will ensure a hassle-free experience in one of North Queensland’s most spectacular fishing destinations, offering expert tips and tailored support for a successful day on the water.
Latest Technology
Full On Fish’n Charters takes the guesswork out of fishing the Channel with Garmin LiveScope. With real-time scanning, you can see fish movement, structure, and underwater features, allowing you to cast where the action is.
Fish with a Local!
With over 18 years of fishing charter experience, locally born and bred guide Nathan Kemp knows the Hinchinbrook Channel like no one else.
His deep understanding of the tides, bait movement, and fish behavior ensures you have the best chance at landing that prized barramundi.
If Nathan can’t hook you up with a catch, no one can!

Flexible Fishing Packages
We offer a range of customizable fishing trips, from single-day charters to multi-day packages with accommodation and meals included. 
Our signature 3-day barra fishing package with 4 nights of accommodation is the ultimate way to experience Hinchinbrook’s legendary fishing grounds. Get in touch with us today!
