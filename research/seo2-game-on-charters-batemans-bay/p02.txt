The ultimate Game Fishing Experience with Game on Charters Batemans Bay
Book Now 
Embark on a thrilling adventure to the continental shelf with Game on Charters Batemans Bay’s premium game & sport fishing charter. Our wealth of fishing knowledge and experience ensures we provide the ultimate big fish experience. We pride ourselves on offering a full day service aimed to satisfy all fishing enthusiasts from novice to experienced.
Exceptional Game Fishing
The south coast is home to some of the most spectacular game fishing in Australia. At Game on Charters Batemans Bay, we take full advantage of this blessing, guiding you to the continental shelf situated just 22 km from our marina and well known for its seasonal runs of big pelagic, our charters will not disappoint. Our timing and targeting vary throughout the year, catering to different species influenced by water temps, currents, altimetry and baitfish congregations. You can be assured we will put you in the prime position so you have the very best chance to catch that fish of a lifetime. 
Comfort Assured
To make your game fishing adventure all the more enjoyable, we offer the comfort and style on our 2 custom built vessels – 36 Riviera “Seabee” and our 42 Westcoaster “Knotorious” Kitted out with all the bells and whistles to ensure your trip with us is nothing less than comfortable along with plenty of space. Our highly trained staff are on-hand to make sure you have a fantastic time, regardless of your fishing experience level.
All-Inclusive Game Fishing Package
We provide all top shelf Shimano rods & reels and catch live bait once we arrive at the shelf, you just need to bring yourself your mates and some enthusiasm. Our game fishing package is designed to provide the ultimate fishing adventure. You just sit back and wait for that big reel to start screaming, if the game fish are a bit slow we also often target dolphin fish or do some deep dropping on the canyons with our electric reels.
Benefits of Choosing Game on Charters Batemans Bay
Our years of experience fishing the south coast set us apart from the rest. Being well-accustomed to the region’s diverse marine life, we offer expert guidance to ensure you make the most of your fishing trip. The full day game fishing experience with us offers an unprecedented opportunity to explore the Australian waters, learn new fishing techniques, and bask in the comfort of our purpose built Riviera game cruiser.
Choose Game on Charters Batemans Bay for a personalised, full day game fishing adventure. We guarantee an unforgettable experience.
We recommend keeping your groups to a maximum of 6 to ensure we have adequate room when dealing with the bigger fish.
