Book Now

The following are Cruise Charter inclusions and tour tips. 
WHAT YOU NEED TO BRING
• 
Hat 
• 
Sunscreen 
• 
Lunch 
• 
Seasickness medication if you suffer motion sickness 
• 
Esky or cooler bag to take fish home in 
Book Now 
CHARTERS INCLUDE
• 
Quality Shimano & Penn rods and reels 
• 
All bait and tackle 
• 
Fishing Licence 
• 
Tea/Coffee/Water & light snacks 
• 
Catered lunch can be arranged for an additional fee 
• 
Eftpos facilities available 
• 
Friendly Customer Service! 
• 
Coaching and assistance for beginners 
ACCOMMODATION
• 
We recommend batemansbayaccommodation.com.au for other packages. 
MEETING LOCATION FOR CHARTER
The designated meeting place for tour inductions is in the car park on Beach Rd, beside the Raine & Horne Office.
Located in the iconic Batemans Bay marina, you have the chance to charter our 36ft Riviera Platinum Game cruiser or our 42 ft Westcoaster centre cab. We offer comfortable vessels with all the bells and whistles to make your trip enjoyable.
