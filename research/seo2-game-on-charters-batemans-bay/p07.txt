Your Complete Guide
Welcome to Game on Charters , ( Batemans Bays’s premier charter company ) where we provide unmatched experiences on the water filled with fun, adventure and luxury. Located in the iconic Batemans Bay marina, we offer a range of services aboard our luxury Riviera cruiser, We are the number one choice among our competitors.
Why Choose Us?
As a locally owned and operated business we strive to give you the ultimate experience. We have a wealth of local knowledge, we are experienced and a thorough understanding of our waterways . Our sustainable practices ensure we will enjoy the waterways into the future. Safety is paramount to us, ensuring the safety of our guests is our number one priority.
Our Charter Services
Game on Charters Batemans Bay offers a large range of charter services.
Reef Fishing
Join us for half or full-day reef fishing charter. We know the spots where regular species like Snapper, King Fish, Trevally, Leather Jacket, Pigfish, and Morwong will be biting.
Game Fishing
Venture 18 nautical miles from our marina to embark on an exciting game fishing charter to the continental shelf chasing that fish of a lifetime. Target species such as Marlin, Yellowfin Tuna, Bluefin Tuna are an option depending on the time of year.
Deep drop fishing
Delve deeper into the ocean with our deep drop fishing charters, We have specialised rods, electric reels, heavy tackle, and gear tailored for deep drop fishing. Our experienced crew ensures a fishing experience like no other.
Whale Watching (Seasonal)
Witness the exhilaration of the Humpback and southern right whales as they navigate the migratory passage off the coast of Batemans Bay. Our whale watching tours offer an unforgettable experience watching these magnificent creatures in their natural environment.
Sunset Cruise Clyde River
Experience magic on the Clyde River with our Sunset Cruise. Stunning scenery, tranquil waters, and a sense of escape constitute the essence of this charter.
Snorkelling
Dive into an aquatic wonderland with our Snorkelling Adventure. Explore the vibrant marine life beneath the crystal-clear waters of Batemans Bay guided by our experienced staff.
Custom Cruise 
let us know what you want to do! mix and match our charters, maybe a relaxing cruise followed by a snorkel at the Tollgate islands? Maybe a fish and a relaxing swim at a secluded beach? the options are endless, give us a call to make your thoughts a reality.
Contact Us
Give us a call at 0499 499 600 or drop an email at info@gameoncharters.net.au to book your memorable trip with Game on Charters Batemans Bay.
