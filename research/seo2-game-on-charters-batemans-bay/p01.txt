Experience the Thrill of Reef fishing with Game on Charters Batemans Bay
Book Now 
Game on Charters Batemans Bay invites you to the exciting world of reef fishing. We are the premier charter company in Bateman’s Bay. We are known for our commitment to safety and ensuring an unforgettable reef fishing experience. Whether you’re sharing a half-day trip or taking a full-day charter our experienced team will provide an action packed day on the water. Whether you are an experienced fisherman or just starting out our knowledgable crew will ensure you have a day to remember.
Top shelf Fishing Experience
Our Reef Fishing charters promise a diverse catch, which includes species such as Snapper, King Fish, Trevally, Leather Jacket, Pigfish, and Morwong. We pride ourselves in knowing where and when the fish will be biting, we also troll a range of skirts and diving lures near headlands and bommies to mix it up, this can also produce great catches of King Fish, Salmon and Bonito.
Our Expertise
With years of experience fishing the Batemans Bay Area, we know where the fish will be biting and when. Our experienced staff have extensive knowledge about the fish species in these waters and are ready to help you catch the big one. With our local knowledge and passion for fishing, we guarantee a great day is in store.
Benefits of Reef fishing.
The thrill of reef fishing presents itself in many ways. Apart from the excitement of catching a big fish the experience is also highly educational and therapeutic. You will learn about different fish species, their habits and techniques used to catch them from our experienced staff. Furthermore, an experienced and friendly team guides your fishing trip, ensuring safety, fun, and a great day on the water.
Enjoy the Premium Fishing Experience with Game on Charters Batemans Bay
Your next reef fishing adventure awaits you at Game on Charters Batemans Bay. Contact us today for an unforgettable shared or private fishing expedition marked by the thrill of the catch, the beauty of the waters, and the excitement of the company. Ready yourself for the ultimate fishing trip.
