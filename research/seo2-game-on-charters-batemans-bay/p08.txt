Reel in the Adventure with Game On Charters Batemans Bay
Located at the d’Albora marina Batemans Bay, Game On Charters offers the chance to charter our luxury vessels for an action packed experience on the water.
Why Choose Game On Charters Batemans Bay
• Exclusive access to our Riviera Gamecruiser & Westcoaster centre cab
• 20 years of operational experience
• Family owned and operated
• Sustainable and eco-friendly practices
• Uncompromised attention to safety and customer service
Services Offered
Reef Fishing
Experience the thrill of fishing for Snapper, King Fish, Trevally, Leather Jacket, Pigfish, Morwong and more on the offshore reefs around the Batemans Bay Area.
Game Fishing
Experience the excitement of game fishing in some of the most productive waters on the coast. Our experienced staff and top shelf equipment will ensure you have a fantastic day on the water chasing that fish of a lifetime.
Deep Drop Fishing
Join us for a deep drop fishing adventure, fishing the canyons and sea mounts over the continental shelf for species such as Blue eye, gem fish, ling, cod and ocean perch with our specialised Shimano electric reels and equipment. We ensure your day will be memorable one.
Whale Watching – Seasonal
Experience the magnificent Humpback whales, dolphins & other marine life in their natural habitat with our exceptional eco whale watching tours.,
Sunset Cruise Clyde River
Relish a serene sunset cruise on the Clyde River. Take in stunning scenery and peaceful waters in the air-conditioned comfort of our luxury vessel.
Tollgate Island Dolphin Cruise
Join us for an enchanting dolphin watching experience around the Tollgate Islands. Witness the delightful antics of dolphins in their natural habitat.
Unforgettable Adventures Await
At Game On Charters Batemans Bay, we strive to ensure every journey on the water is memorable and enjoyable. Whether it’s a fishing charter, a sunset cruise, or a whale watching adventure, we are committed to giving you that experience like no other.
Contact us on 0499499600 or email us at info@gameoncharters.net.au to book your next adventure.
