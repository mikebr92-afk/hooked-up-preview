Looking for an adventure the whole family will remember? Our family fishing trips are designed to be fun, relaxed, and packed with action, whether you're introducing the kids to fishing for the first time or planning unforgettable father-son fishing trips in some of Australia's most spectacular locations. 
As part of our guided fishing charters in Queensland, we welcome anglers of all ages and abilities, and our experienced guides provide everything you need, making us the perfect choice for a beginner's fishing charter or anyone looking for guided fishing for beginners!

Fishing Adventures for Every Family
We offer a range of fishing experiences to suit every type of adventure. Our one-day fishing charter in Cairns is perfect for families wanting an exciting day on the water, while our multi-day fishing tour in Cairns allows you to explore multiple fishing locations and really immerse yourself in Far North Queensland's incredible landscapes. 
For families chasing a true bucket-list experience… our giant barra fishing charters in Cairns gives you the chance to target trophy-sized barramundi and create memories you'll be talking about for years! 
More Than Just Fishing
Our adventures take you through some of the most breathtaking locations in Australia. Explore remote rainforest rivers on our fishing trips from Port Douglas , chase barra in pristine waterways, or head offshore on our unforgettable Great Barrier Reef fishing charters . 
Whether you're booking your first fishing trip, planning a family holiday activity, or organising a special father and son adventure, our charters offer the perfect mix of excitement, scenery, wildlife, and world-class fishing. Best of all, no experience is required – just bring your sense of adventure!
