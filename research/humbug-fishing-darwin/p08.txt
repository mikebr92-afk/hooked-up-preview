Our Destinations
Seven destinations, one crew 
Humbug Fishing runs charters to seven named destinations across the Top End and the Kimberley, from Daly River to the Timor Box, 200 nautical miles offshore. Every charter runs liveaboard, aboard Starfire, or tag-along, using your own boat alongside the mothership.
Each destination fishes differently. River systems like Daly River and Moyle are built around the barra runoff, tides that turn on a three-month window each year. The Tiwi Islands, Cobourg Peninsula, and the Kimberley coast open up open-water species alongside barra. The Timor Box takes you into deep water, 200 nautical miles out, for a genuinely offshore trip. Wherever you want to fish, our team will match the destination and the tides to your dates.

Mary River System (Tommy Cut and Shady Camp)
The Mighty Mary River is renowned for its big Barramundi fishing.
Shady Camp in Mary River National Park, located about 200km from Darwin, is accessible by car in approximately 1 hour and 40 minutes (4WD only during the wet season). It offers two main rivers for fishing: the Sampan and Tommycut Creeks.
The area is especially popular between March and May during the “run-off” period when the Mary River is filled with big barramundi and plenty of crocodiles.

Daly River + Reynolds River Flood Plains
The Daly River is renowned for its exceptional Barramundi fishing. This river, located roughly 2.5 hours south west in the Northern Territory, offers year-round opportunities to catch this prized fish, with the peak season during the spectacular runoff period from March to May. The Daly River is considered the premier barramundi river in the Territory.
The river, extending over 40 km from the Daly River crossing to its confluence with Anson Bay, is characterized by various natural features such as snags, sandbars, S-bends, creeks, and rock bars. Anson Bay itself, along with its coastal floodplains, encompasses the Peron Islands, tidal flats, and the adjoining coastal floodplains of the Daly and Reynolds rivers and Docherty’s Creek.
The Daly-Reynolds floodplain system, a significant ecological area, is one of the largest in the Northern Territory, boasting the largest catchment area among the major freshwater floodplain systems in the region.

or Book today. 
Moyle + Little Moyle Rivers 
The Moyle Rivers, consisting of the Little Moyle River and the Moyle River, are located approximately 200 km southwest of Darwin.
The mouth of the Moyle River offers excellent remote fishing opportunities. During certain times of the year, the floodplain becomes accessible, providing a stunning view of abundant wildlife. Fishing in the Moyle River region is generally good throughout the year, with the floodplain offering particularly spectacular scenery long into the wet season and early dry.
Learn more about Moyle & Little Moyle Rivers 
or Book today. 
Tiwi Islands & the Apsley Strait
The Tiwi Islands and Apsley Strait is a remote fishing destination approximately 106km from Darwin.
Enriched with culture, stories, wildlife and just great fishing, this is an excellent all in one remote fishing destination.
If you’re after mud crabs, cod, golden snapper, black jewfish, and barramundi, this is a destination of choice. 
Learn more about Tiwi Islands 
or Book today.

The Kimberley
Experience the spectacular waterways and scenery of the Kimberley region and some of the ‘must do’ fishing adventures in world with Humbug Fishing. 
Whether we help you get to The Kimberley by air or by water, this beautiful remote region will take your breath away. It’s a fishing experience like no other. Nothing will ever beat the silence, the sun, the colours, especially knowing that great fishing is waiting for you after breakfast! 
Areas we can take you in the Kimberley Region includes the stunning King George, Montgomery Reef, The Mitchell, Cape Londonderry and more. 
Learn more about Kimberley Fishing Charters 
or Book today. 
Timor Box
The area of the Timor Box extends north-west of Darwin to the Western Australia/NT border and to the outer boundary of the Australian fishing zone 200 nautical miles offshore. The fishery has an area of approximately 8400 square nautical miles. Timor box is one of our favourite fishing spots for remote and deep sea fishing.
It offers a diverse range of fishing opportunities, from deep water fishing for Ruby Snapper and Gold Band Snapper Band Snapper to spearfishing for Coral Trout near the shoals. The variety of fish like Mackerel, Sail Fish and Dog Tooth Tuna also adds to its appeal.
Learn more about Timor Box 
or Book today. 
Cobourg / Arnhem
Cobourg Peninsula sits in Arnhem Land, and Humbug Fishing runs charters here from October to November, when weather and tides line up.
The New Year Island Group, anchored at the southern end of Oxley, McCluer, and Grant Islands, is a key destination within the region, fished either guided or tag-along.
Learn more about Cobourg Peninsula 
or Book today.
