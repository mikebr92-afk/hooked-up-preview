Commercial Marine Services
View our fleet of fishing boats 
All of our fishing boats are fit for purpose, maintained to the highest of industry and safety standards and are certified to current Australian Survey Requirements.
We have a range of boats which can reach smaller to large waterways. We service marine research projects, oil and gas industry, private enterprise, Government tenders and Australian Defence.

Brute Force
Brute Force is a sturdy aluminium service vessel with a wide range of capabilities, making it suitable for various projects in marine research, aquaculture industry, oil and gas industry, private enterprises, Government agencies and Australian Defence Force.
Features: 
• NSVC, 2B, 2C & 2D Surveys 
• Single CAT C18 875 HP Inboard motor 
• Licensed for 12 passengers and 5 crew 
• 24kva genset 
• Bow thruster 
• Hiab 140 deck crane (6000kg @ 2m 600h @ 12.5 winch 
• 2000L water with desalinator 
• 11 bed bunkhouse with air conditioning 
• 5000L + Fuel carrying capacity 
• 10 Tonne deck capacity 
• Kitchenette 
• Bathroom facilities

Starfire (FatCat)
‘FatCat’ is a sturdy aluminium service vessel with a range of capabilities making it suitable for various projects in marine research, aquaculture industry, oil and gas industry, private enterprises, Government agencies and Australian Defence Force.
Features: 
• 24m x 9m Aluminium Cat 
• Twin 250hp Cummins engine – 7 knots 
• 3B & 2B Survey 
• Licensed for 12 passengers and 5 crew 
• Fully equipped galley 
• 3 Bathrooms 
• 30,000L Fuel capacity 
• 2000L Water capacity 
• 100kva Generator 
• 3 Tonne capacity crane 
• 7 x 9m deck space and 8 x 6.5m upper deck space 
• 4 x air-conditioned twin share bedrooms + 1 x 4 bed bunk-room 
• Indoor 6 x 7m workspace with airconditioning
