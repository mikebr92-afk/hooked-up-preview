Fishing Charter Options
Why book a tailored fishing tour?
Humbug Fishing offers two ways to fish the Top End: liveaboard on Starfire, or tag-along with your own boat. Every charter is planned around your group, your dates, and the tides for what you want to catch.
You choose the boat, the tides, and the region, and our team builds the trip around it. No two charters run exactly the same way, because no two groups want exactly the same thing.
Get in touch to check available dates.
Book Today! 
Extended Live-aboard Fishing Charters 
Live aboard Starfire for the length of your trip. Charters run for groups of up to 8 people, or up to 12 with a third tender added, with a minimum of 5 days away.
Learn More 
Tag Along With The Mothership 
Fish from your own tender and use our mothership as your base each night. Meet us at your chosen location, or travel out together.
Learn More 
Fish The Kimberley 
Humbug Fishing holds a WA fishing charter licence, allowing charters into the Kimberley region, liveaboard or tag-along. Not every Top End operator holds this licence.
Learn More 
Every charter is planned around what you and your group want from the trip: the right tides, the right location, and a boat that suits your numbers. Provisions, logistics, and meals are handled before you arrive, so your time on the water is spent fishing, not organising.
Our fishing tours are built around you.
– Humbug Team
