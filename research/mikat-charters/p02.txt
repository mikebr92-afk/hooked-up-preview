About M.V. mikat 
Get Hooked On The Adventure​
Step aboard the M.V. Mikat . The Ultimate Fishing Trip Cruiser for extended voyages to The Great Barrier Reef, Swains Reef & Saumarez Reefs, Capricorn Bunker Group, Douglas Shoals and beyond. The opportunity exists for you and or your clients to join us for a fishing charter to some of the best sites available, try dory fishing on bombies around the shallow reefs, or off the shelf in the deepwater for sport fishing for the prolific pelagics.
The Mikat is a purpose built, well appointed 20 metre Aluminium Catamaran powered by V8 SCANIA DL 16 800Hp to give a very comfortable cruise speed of 15k, so less travelling time and more fishing. Being a catamaran with a beam of 7 metres it is a very stable platform for fishing and with USL 1 B Survey, we have the capability to do extended cruises to the very remote reefs, islands and cays in the Coral Sea.
Starting on the top deck is the bridge with high quality state of the art navigation equipment including computer plotter to precisely locate every fishing site. Behind the bridge is a large carpeted covered open deck area to relax with evening drinks and occasional al fresco dining
The main deck has a large saloon/dining area with television/video.. The aft end of this area is the galley where our on board cook converts fresh food into scrumptious meals, this is also the bar area. There are 3 bathrooms with full size showers, toilets and hand basins and below the main deck is the accommodation deck with 2 double and 2 x 6 share cabins. Comfort facilities aboard the Mikat include Air Conditioning, Blue Ray DVD, P.A. System & FOXTEL.
We have a highly experienced professional crew to ensure that you have a safe enjoyable fishing trip. Departure is usually from Gladstone, a major Central Qld Port serviced by regular flights, rail, coach travel and security parking can be organised.

How We Work 
What You Need To Know
Bookings & Cancellations
A 20% deposit is required to confirm the charter. Full payment is due if the booking is made less than 50 days in advance. If a cancellation is made more than 30 days before the trip and the trip is rebooked, a refund will be issued less than 10%; if a cancellation is made 30-15 days prior, a refund will be issued less the 20% deposit; and if a cancellation is made less than 15 days prior, the booking will be forfeited.
Charter Destinations
Our charter destinations include the Swains Reefs, Great Barrier Reef, Saumarez Reef, Capricorn Bunker Group, and Douglas Shoals (minimum 3-day charter)​
Weather
All trips are subject to change based on the Captain's safe net decision due to weather conditions. In the event of a cancellation, a full refund based on the number of days cancelled will be honored. Secure parking is available at Complete Angler, reachable at Ph 07 49727283.
Things to Bring
To ensure a great fishing experience, we recommend that you bring the following personal fishing equipment: 45/80lb line, 6,7,8,9 o hooks, 2,4,6,8s ball sinkers, 0 to 4 snapper leads, 10kg – 25kg rods, hand lines, lures, soft plastics, knot book, bait knife, pliers, de-hooker, gimble belt, gloves, trace wire, swimming/snorkelling gear, comfortable clothing, deck shoes, deck towel, sunglasses, hat, camera, and rain jacket/pants. Travel insurance is also recommended.
Our Value 
Live, Laugh, Relax While Fishing
Our Vision
We aim to provide our clients with an exceptional and exhilarating experience on the open sea. We strive to create a fun and relaxing environment that allows our clients to escape from their daily routine and enjoy the thrill of fishing in remote and pristine locations. 
Our Mission
Our mission is to deliver a first-class fishing adventure for our clients. We are dedicated to using our knowledge and experience to guide our clients to the best fishing spots, and provide them with top-quality equipment and facilities. Our goal is to create an unforgettable and satisfying fishing trip that leaves our clients with lasting memories and a desire to return.
