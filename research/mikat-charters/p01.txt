About M.V. Mikat 
Get Hooked On The Adventure
Step aboard the M.V. Mikat . The Ultimate Fishing Trip Cruiser for extended voyages to The Great Barrier Reef, Swains Reef & Saumarez Reefs, Capricorn Bunker Group, Douglas Shoals and beyond. The opportunity exists for you and or your clients to join us for a fishing charter to some of the best sites available, try dory fishing on bombies around the shallow reefs, or off the shelf in the deepwater for sport fishing for the prolific pelagics.
Read More 
Unparalleled Experience
Step aboard the MV Mikat and experience an unparalleled opportunity to catch big fish, explore remote locations, and unwind from the stress of everyday life
Trip Inclusives
We provide an all-inclusive trip, complete with food and beverages and comfort facilities to ensure your experience is as enjoyable as possible. 
Exceptional Crews
You'll have access to experienced and professional crews who will guide you through your journey, teach you on using your fishing equipment, and ensure your safety and comfort.
V8 SCANIA DL 16 800Hp Engine
M.V. Mikat is a purpose-built, well-appointed 20-meter aluminum catamaran with a powerful engine making it an extremely stable platform for fishing. We also can do extended cruises to the most remote reefs, islands, and cays in the Coral Sea.
