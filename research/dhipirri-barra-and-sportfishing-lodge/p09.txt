Available Boats at Dhipirri Barra Lodge for 2nd Half of 2026
Come chase Dhipirri Barra and attack the Bluewater before the end of 2026.
After a wonderful Run-off at Dhipirri with a record number of Metre Barra caught since we opened in 2013 (39 and counting) and a host of fat and healthy Dhipirri Barra in The Glyde (the river is still fishing great as we enter July, due to another quality wet season to feed it); we have some great opportunities to chase our Dhipirri Barra and abundance of Bluewater species during the 2nd Half of the year. 
Please refer to the calendar graphic below details on when we have our available boats, and refer to the Key – graphic was updated on 6 July 2026* .
Your best opportunities for a big group are at the back end of the year in the Build-up, but we have a few spots available during the rest of the dry season.
If you are interested in booking a trip before the end of 2026 – please call Kirstie Fairchild and Rahni Nickolls in our Darwin Office on 08 8983 3722 to discuss your options, and you can also email us at [email protected] 
*Our Booking Calendar is dynamic in nature, so please discuss with our Darwin office for the latest details in availability. 
For those interested in a 2027 trip? 
Our 2027 Run-off dates are already filling up – so chat to your friends, get them keen about a trip to Dhipirri, then please contact our Darwin Office to find a date that suits with our friendly staff, or as for 2026 dates, please fill out the enquiry form below.
