What guests have to say about Dhipirri
• Andrew Ettingshausen – Rugby League Legend and Fishing Show Host.
“Darren is an excellent guide and this is a superb fishing location. One of my favourite times fishing was on the first morning when the water was very still and incredibly clean – we could see through the water and spotted Barramundi cruising up in the shallows. Sight fishing is always the best sort of fishing you can do – what a fantastic thrill in such a remote location.” 
• The March Tourists – GOAT, Doc, G-Russ, Russ, Waz, The Judge, Mee Mee, Big Tone, Bakes, Karl, Roy, Andrew, Scrubber, Steve, Peter.
“Thanks again Darren & crew – we love this place! … Another brilliant trip! … Doc for Most Valuable Tourist! … Time of my life … Fantastic lodge … I’ll be back … Thank you for the good company and service … Most enjoyable few days of the year, great fun, great place, great people. 
• Mark Berg – Fishing Show Host.
Great food, fishing and awesome people. I’ll be back for sure. Cheers, Bergie. 
• Paganin Family (Mark, Ben, Luke & Nic), Perth, WA.
“A unique Australian place & people – an unbelievable experience. Thank you so much!” 
• Rhoul, David and Alex – Anaconda Management 
“This place is insane! … Life changing, see you again soon … Unreel fishing, best hospitality. Loved it.” 
• Koa Hodgins , Maui, Hawaii.
“Amazing place, good friends, awesome staff and a 116cm Barra to top it all off!” 
• Matt Armistead , Brighton, QLD.
“There’s so many awe inspiring locations so close to the lodge. And it’s such a great lodge with 1st class staff and guides and right on the waterfront. The highlight of the trip was definitely travelling up to the floodplains at the top of The Glyde River, such an amazing place.”
“It’s not all about the fish, the best part of every fishing trip for me is the mates you share it with and the incredible places you visit. Dhipirri had both. Can’t wait to get back there and try it after a big wet season.”
