The Fishing
The Fish
There are no shortage of fish to catch at Dhipirri….
Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, GTs, Golden Snapper, Spanish Mackerel, Queenfish, Blue Salmon, Mangrove Jack… the list goes on!
Read more → 
The Rivers & Islands
Dhipirri Lodge has easy access a range of fishing locations in the area. From chasing Barra up the Glyde River in the run-off, to hooking into lure crunching Pelagics on the Bluewater, there is always a fishing option at Dhipirri any time of year.
Read more → 
The Team
Dhipirri Barra & Sportfishing Lodge is proudly led by Darren and Sharon Nickolls, who are both highly regarded in the NT fishing industry. Since the Lodge opened in October 2013, the Dhipirri team has grown to over a dozen people, and each member aim to ensure your Dhipirri experience is one to remember.
Read more → 
The Boats & Tackle
There are 5 Bar Crusher boats currently operating at Dhipirri Lodge, with each able to accommodate 3 anglers. Top quality fishing gear is provided with Wilson and Reidy’s Lures supplying tackle to Dhipirri.
Read more →
