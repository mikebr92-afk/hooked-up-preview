About Dhipirri Barra Lodge
Dhipirri Barra and Sportfishing Lodge offers anglers a high quality and remote fishing experience in the beautiful expanse of East Arnhem Land, in Australia’s Northern Territory. 
We acknowledge the Traditional Owners of country throughout Australia and recognise their continuing connection to land, waters and culture. We pay our respects to their Elders past, present and emerging.
Owned and operated by Darren and Sharon Nickolls, they built Dhipirri from the ground up in 2013 and it provides anglers a wonderful opportunity to catch the mighty Barramundi, and a wide range of Pelagics, from March through December each year – with all fishing grounds found within a close proximity to the Lodge that sits on the Arnhem Land coastline. 
The Lodge’s accommodation and dining are first class and our coastal setting is stunning at any time of day. All our rooms are air conditioned with excellent amenities and a daily laundry service. 
Dhipirri’s private airstrip is the only way in to the Lodge and this allows clients to fly directly from Darwin or Nhulunbuy into the Lodge during daylight hours. 
We can’t wait to welcome you to our little corner of paradise in Australia’s Northern Territory.
