Trout Flies
Explore Our Range of Trout Flies
We’re proud to offer the same trout flies we use every day in our guided tours and personal fishing adventures — now available to both our clients and the wider fly fishing community.
Our collection includes a carefully curated mix of proven classics and custom fly patterns developed through years of hands-on experience across Tasmania’s diverse waters — from rivers and twig water to highland lakes and lagoons.
To make browsing easier, we’ve grouped our flies into four key categories: Mayflies / Terrestrials / Nymphs / Wet Flies 
Note: This page is currently under development. Individual photos and detailed descriptions for each fly will be added soon.
