Tasmania Fly Fishing Charters – Rates and Tour Details 
At Tasmania Fly Fishing, we offer premium guided fly fishing experiences for individuals, couples, families, and groups across Tasmania.
Every charter is tailored to your experience, interests and the prevailing conditions on the day. Whether you’re casting a fly for the very first time or chasing wild Tasmanian trout on remote waters, our aim is simple—to provide a relaxed, enjoyable and memorable day on the water.
All of our tours are private bookings. When you book with Tasmania Fly Fishing, your guide is dedicated exclusively to you and your group—we don’t combine unrelated bookings or ask you to share your guide with strangers.
From half day introductions through to full day adventures and personalised multi-day itineraries, there’s an option to suit every angler.

Half Day Fly Fishing Charters 
A relaxed introduction to fly fishing 
Our Half Day Fly Fishing Charters are ideal for visitors with limited time, families, complete beginners, or anyone wanting to experience Tasmania’s famous trout fishing without committing to a full day.
Operating from Launceston, these tours focus on productive nearby rivers, streams and small lakes, meaning less time travelling and more time fishing.
Whether you’ve never held a fly rod before or simply want a few enjoyable hours on the water, your guide will tailor the session to your experience and interests while sharing local knowledge, practical instruction and plenty of encouragement along the way.
Session Times 
Morning: 8:30 am – 12:30 pm
Afternoon: 1:30 pm – 5:30 pm
Early morning or late afternoon departures may be available by arrangement.

Three Quarter Day Fly Fishing Charters 
The sweet spot 
Our Three Quarter Day Charters are one of our most popular options, offering the perfect balance between time on the water and flexibility to explore a wider range of fisheries.
With six hours available, we can travel further from Launceston to reach beautiful rivers, highland lakes and more remote trout waters while still enjoying a relaxed pace throughout the day.
This option suits anglers wanting more fishing time than a half day without committing to a full day’s adventure.
Whether you’re developing your casting, exploring new water or simply enjoying Tasmania’s spectacular scenery, your guide will tailor the experience to suit the conditions and your goals.

Full Day Fly Fishing Charters 
The complete Tasmanian fly fishing experience 
For anglers wanting to fully immerse themselves in Tasmania’s world-class trout fishing, our Full Day Charter offers the ultimate experience.
With eight hours available, we have the flexibility to explore remote rivers, meadow streams, highland lakes and private fisheries while allowing plenty of time to observe fish, enjoy the surroundings and fish at a relaxed pace.
Whether your goal is learning the fundamentals, improving your skills or chasing memorable wild trout, your guide will design the day around you.

Multi-Day Tours, Corporate Groups & Custom Experiences 
Looking for something beyond our standard tours?
We’re always happy to create personalised itineraries for anglers wanting to spend several days exploring Tasmania’s outstanding trout fisheries.
Popular options include:
• The Launceston region’s rivers, streams and lakes
• Tasmania’s famous Central Highlands
• Exclusive private fisheries including Currawong Lakes
• Combination tours featuring multiple locations and fishing styles
• Corporate groups, clubs and special events
Every itinerary is individually planned around your accommodation, travel plans, experience level and the best fishing opportunities available during your visit.
We’ll happily provide a personalised quotation before confirming your booking.
What’s Included 
Every Tasmania Fly Fishing Charter includes:
• Premium fly fishing equipment including rods, reels, flies, waders and boots
• Friendly, knowledgeable local guide
• Tuition tailored to your experience level
• Transfers from Launceston or another pre-arranged meeting point
• Light refreshments on Half Day tours
• Full catering from our “Fishing Fuel” menu on Three Quarter and Full Day tours
*****
Additional Information 
*****
Private Guided Tours 
Every booking is exclusive to you and your group.
We don’t combine unrelated bookings or ask you to share your guide with strangers, allowing your guide to tailor the day to your experience, interests and goals.
On the rare occasion that combining bookings is the only practical option, we’ll always discuss this with everyone involved beforehand and only proceed with everyone’s agreement.
*****
Larger Groups 
While our published prices cover up to four participants, we regularly cater for larger family groups, corporate events, clubs and special occasions.
We allocate guides according to the size, experience and needs of your group to ensure everyone enjoys a safe, relaxed and personalised experience.
*****
Drift Boat Experience 
For anglers visiting Currawong Lakes, our beautiful handcrafted drift boat provides a unique way to experience the fishery.
Relax while your guide quietly rows you across productive trout water, positioning the boat for the best opportunities while providing instruction, quality equipment and catering throughout the day.
Optional Drift Boat: $250 per day (in addition to guiding fees).
*****
Gift Vouchers 
Tasmania Fly Fishing Gift Vouchers make a wonderful present for birthdays, anniversaries, retirements, Christmas or any special occasion.
Simply contact us with the tour you’d like to purchase, the recipient’s name and the occasion, and we’ll prepare a personalised voucher ready for gifting.
*****
Fishing Licenses 
Fishing licenses are required for Tasmania’s public inland waters.
No license is required when fishing private fisheries such as Currawong Lakes, although private access fees apply.
*****
What to Bring 
We recommend:
• Long sleeve shirt and long pants
• Waterproof jacket
• Hat
• Warm layers during cooler months
We provide polarised sunglasses, sunscreen and all fishing equipment.
*****
Payment Options 
We accept bank transfer payment methods and also offer PayPal for international guests (plus Pay in 4 for eligible clients, allowing you to spread the cost over four interest-free instalments).
*****
Safety & Accessibility 
Our tours involve light to moderate physical activity and operate in natural outdoor environments.
If you have any medical conditions, mobility considerations or special requirements, please let us know when booking. We’ll always do our best to tailor the day to suit your needs.
