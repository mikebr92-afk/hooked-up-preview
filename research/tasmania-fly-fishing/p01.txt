Guided Fly Fishing Tasmania 
Trout Tours & Charters in Wild Tasmanian Waters 
Welcome to Tasmania Fly Fishing — your home for guided fly fishing in Tasmania , with local experienced guides offering some of the most enjoyable fly fishing tours in Tasmania . We take anglers, both new and experienced, through wild trout waters that are as scenic as they are challenging.
Based in Launceston , our tours explore local rivers and lakes, the rugged waters of the Central Highlands of Tasmania , remote backcountry creeks, and the beautiful private fishery at Currawong Lakes . These waters mostly hold wild brown trout — and occasionally rainbows — all of them as cunning as they are beautiful. Chasing them offers something deeper than just numbers.
Whether you’re picking up a fly rod for the first time or refining your skills in new water, we offer guided fly fishing tours tailored to your level, goals, and curiosity. If you’re willing to give it a proper go, we’ll do everything we can to help you connect with the fish, the landscape — and the joy of the experience itself.
👉 See our Rates & Tours page for current details, or review Tasmania’s trout fishing regulations for licensing information.
What’s Included in Your Guided Fly Fishing Tour 
• 
Half, ¾, full, and multi-day guided fly fishing options 
• 
All fly gear supplied — rods, reels, waders, flies (but you can bring your own)
• 
Friendly tuition for beginners; technical support and spotting for seasoned anglers
• 
Catered lunches and refreshments (or snacks, depending on the trip)
• 
Pick-up and return from the Launceston area
• 
Access to private water options (including Currawong Lakes )
We mostly practice and encourage catch and release , but keeping a fish is sometimes possible where regulations allow.
All our guided fly fishing Tasmania trips include gear, catering, transport, and access to a wide range of waters — from slow-flowing meadow streams to remote highland lakes.
Meet Your Tasmanian Fly Fishing Guide 
I’m Scott Murphy , a lifelong Tasmanian angler and full-time guide. I personally handle all bookings, and we’ll communicate clearly from the outset — by phone, email, WhatsApp or Zoom — so you’ll know exactly what to expect and how to prepare.
We don’t do hype. The trout here are wild, the weather can shift, and nothing’s guaranteed. But come with a spirit of curiosity and appreciation, and you’ll leave with more than just a fish story — you’ll leave with a real experience.
Why Choose Our Guided Fly Fishing Tasmania Tours? 
• 
Local knowledge, personalised service
• 
Uncrowded, scenic waters away from the mainstream
• 
Honest guidance — no sugar-coating, just good fishing when it’s on and clear expectations when it’s not
• 
A respectful, conservation-minded approach to Tasmania’s fishery
• 
A touch of dry humour, and no unnecessary fanfare
Book Your Guided Fly Fishing Experience in Tasmania 
Take a look at the Rates & Tours page before getting in touch — it’s simple, clear, and should help answer most of your early questions.
When you’re ready, the best way to reach out is by email , or feel free to contact me directly by phone. I’ll help you work out the right option based on your timing, experience, and the conditions.
If you’re after a fly fishing experience that’s authentic, grounded in local knowledge, and guided by people who genuinely care — we’d love to host you.
