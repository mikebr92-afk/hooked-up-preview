4WD Safari Tour 
Open vehicle safari - 4wd Floodplain Wildlife/Sunset 
This is a great tour to see the vastness of the magnificent floodplains of the Mary River region with exclusive access to private areas. Travel in our comfortable all terrain safari vehicle past permanent billabongs, rainforest fringes, grasslands & paper bark forests. 
On safari guests are given an opportunity to view and photograph a variety of bird species, water buffalo ‘which are iconic to the Territory’, brahman cattle, wild pigs, wallabies, dingoes and the occasional crocodile. The ‘Top End’ sunset is amazing and unforgettable. 
Grab a beer or wine from the Saloon Bar to take with you. Relax and enjoy your refreshment as the sun disappears over the horizon.
Tour details
• Departs Point Stuart Wilderness Lodge at 5:00pm
• Approx 2 hour duration
• Esky with ice is available for your refreshments
• Cold water provided
• Operates from mid May to early October 
Cost: $98.00 adult Child 6-13 years $45.00 
*Minimum age 6 years (no children under 6 years) 
*Bookings essential 
Note: 15% Surcharge applies on NT Public Holidays
