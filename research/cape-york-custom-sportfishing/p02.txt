About Dynamic Web Solutions
Home / About Dynamic Web Solutions 
Dynamic Web Solutions Pty Ltd has been developing Australian websites for over 26 years and has a large and diverse portfolio of completed websites across a range of industries. We have experience with a broad range of web development customers, which means it is likely that we have done a web site for a business like yours . We are…
• a one stop website development shop – website design, website hosting and domain name registration in one place
• very skilled and experienced in making websites
• build websites using the WordPress open source software
• diversified across many industries
• here today and here tomorrow
• across new website trends and have web solutions for fast evolving web technologies like mobiles, tablets and social networking 
• a small team of distributed website developers in Australia and New Zealand
• looking for customers who want a long term website supplier – we value ongoing business relationships
• produce reasonably priced Australian websites
• owners of Host Here and Dynamic Web Solutions in Bay of Plenty, New Zealand
Our key strengths are…
• we offer a complete web design , domain name registration , website hosting and support service – always on hand to help.
• we have been in the web design game from the start. That gives us the power of retrospection which few suppliers can offer.
• we answer our phones without on hold messages and queues and are prompt with website and email support issues.
• we support the environment by offsetting carbon created by our Australian web servers.
Support
Dynamic Web Solutions offers WordPress and email support by Skype , email, phone and remote desktop software. Please see our extensive Internet tutorials section before calling. There are email set up instructions in this section. Support is not unlimited however . All support calls you make are recorded in client contact notes and if they accumulate to some hours, Dynamic Web Solutions reserves the right to charge you for this time.
