Fish Moreton is a locally owned and operated fishing charter and tour company based at Sandstone Point, near Bribie Island. With over 35 years of commercial experience in Queensland waters, they specialise in small-group adventures exploring the rich marine life and scenic beauty of Moreton Bay and the Pumicestone Passage.
Their tours range from hands-on fishing trips targeting local species like snapper, flathead, and mackerel, to relaxed sunset and eco cruises where guests can enjoy the sun setting of the Glasshouse Mountains or spotting dolphins, turtles, dugongs, and sea birds in their natural habitat.
They pride themselves on personalised service, safety, and sustainability.
Fishing Charter
Come aboard the Fish Moreton and book either a half or full day fishing charter.
All bait and gear supplied including water and soft drink. 
Fish Moreton is fully shaded and equipped with an onboard toilet. Due to its large hull, Fish Moreton is super stable, providing you with a more relaxing experience in those bumpier waters. Seating is available if required. 
Book your next fishing charter today!
Price: $
Discover hidden gems of Moreton Island - Swim & Cruise
Escape the crowds and uncover the peaceful side of Moreton Island on this relaxing 5-hour tour. Departing from Sandstone Point, cruise across the beautiful Moreton Bay to explore secluded swimming spots, calm turquoise waters, and a slice of island paradise that most visitors never see.
We'll make a stop at the famous Tangalooma Wrecks for a swim among the marine life, then head off the beaten path to find quiet, protected bays where you can float, swim, and soak in the serenity.
Perfect for families, couples, and anyone looking to slow down and truly unwind on the water.
Price: $ 150.00
5 Hour Tour, water and soft drinks supplied.
