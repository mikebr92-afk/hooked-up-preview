One Day Guided Fly Fishing in Tasmania 
Guided Fly Fishing 
Guided Tasmanian fly fishing days are for groups of one to four anglers. Our guides can safely take you into North Eastern Tasmania's best fly fishing locations including Brumby’s Creek, the South Esk and North Esk rivers, St Patricks River, Meander and Mersey rivers.
We cater for all skill levels, from beginner to advanced. You can bring your own equipment, or we'll supply you with everything you need.
90% of the time our guided anglers return with up to 12 trout, from half pounders to four pounders.
Get yourself to Launceston and we pretty much do the rest.
Two anglers – $550 ea
Single angler – $875 / day
Three or more Anglers - $550 ea (inc. minimum ratio of one guide per 2 anglers)
Includes morning tea and lunches, and the use of all equipment as required (including men's and women's breathable waders).
