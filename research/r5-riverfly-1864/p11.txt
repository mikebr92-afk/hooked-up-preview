Three Rivers Package 
Three Rivers Package 
Three Days, Three Rivers, Three Experiences
This package offers anglers the opportunity to fish three different rivers, offering three unique experiences, over three days. The destinations are tailored to your goals, as is the style of fishing. So we can tackle small streams or large rivers, or fish from rafts.
We recommend a vumber of accommodation venues in and around Launceston. Suggestions include Peppers Hotel Silos, or The Florence B&B.
3 days of guided fly fishing, ex Launceston 
Two anglers - $1650 per person
One angler - $2625
Includes guiding, morning tea and lunches, plus the use of all equipment required including breathable waders (men's and women's) and a choice of Epic, Sage and Orvis fly rods.
