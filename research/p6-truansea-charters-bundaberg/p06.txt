Half Day Bundaberg Fishing Charter on the Burnett River 
Want to go fishing without the need to worry about sea conditions, then a half day on the river is just what you need! 
A great option for when the wind is up. 
perfect for families
There is a chance to catch a variety of river species whether it be bream, whiting and flathead, to the prized mangrove jack or blue salmon. The Burnett River really does fish well and there is no need to travel. 
Half day river fishing
Departing from Burnett Heads, our half day river fishing charters run from 0700 - 1200, and 1200 - 1700. 
Price includes all equipment, bait/tackle, drinking water and soft drink. 
Rate is $700 for up to 4 persons. 
Not only do you get to fish, but also cruise and enjoy the beautiful Burnett River on your Bundaberg Fishing Charter
