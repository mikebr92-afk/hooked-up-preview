Half Day Reef Fishing Charter Bundaberg 
5 Hour Trip fishing inshore reefs for all tropical species including Sweetlip, Snapper, Spanish Mackerel and a multitude of other species!! 
All bait, tackle, fishing gear and bad dad jokes are supplied. You are welcome to bring your own fishing equipment. 
Fishing grounds will vary depending on weather and tides, from only a few miles out to about 20nm offshore. 
$250pp includes all equipment, water and soft drinks. 
Please feel free to bring your own refreshments and food. 
*Reef Fishing Charter Bundaberg* 
Half day reef fishing
Perfect for those that may not want to commit to a full day, our half day reef fishing charters still give you a chance at catching a trophy fish. 
Trips times 0700-1200 and 1215-1715.
