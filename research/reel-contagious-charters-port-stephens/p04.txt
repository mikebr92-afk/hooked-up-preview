MARLIN TEASERS 
Made To Order - $155.00 au (+ GST)
Want a teaser that is proven in Australian waters? 
• This Teaser raised a Tournament Winning Blue Marlin at the 2023
• Marlin Shoot Out, Port Stephens, NSW, Australia, weighing 205.6kg.
• Raiz’em teasers skip, dive, smoke and kick inconsistently, mimicking fleeing stripey / skipjack, dorado or small yellowfin.
• Made from a tough nylon composite material resulting in a light weight, strong and effective attractor that fish don’t shy away after they initially wack it.
• Effective on all Marlin, but you need to keep an eye on it, as the Blues love to eat them as well!
