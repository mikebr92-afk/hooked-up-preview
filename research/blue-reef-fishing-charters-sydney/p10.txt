Reef Fishing Trips
This charter is the most popular fishing trip that we offer, taking you out over some of the most incredible fishing reefs off the coast of Sydney. Typically, we fish between 3 and 12 nautical miles from the coast, in productive fishing waters of up to 120 meters in depth. We will then target all reef species, using the finest quality Shimano Penn rods, reels, and fresh baits.
Adam, your skipper, and his experienced crew will give fishing tuition throughout the day. Whether you’re an experienced angler or a first timer, you’ll be certain to learn something new. All of the fish that are caught will be cleaned by the crew, ready for you to take home and prepare for consumption.
We run one or two reef trips per day from 6:00am - 1:00pm, 1:30pm - 7:00pm or an extended charter for 9 hours 6:00am - 3:00pm.
Our vessel charter StrikeBack is large enough to accommodate 14 fishermen comfortably offshore and 25 passengers inshore, making it ideal for birthdays, corporate functions, or simply a social day with your friends. We offer deep sea reef fishing charters all year round.
Prices: These list prices are based on private charter bookings only. We don't cater for shared or mixed group bookings.
