Best Reef and Deep Sea Fishing Trips Sydney
Blue Reef Fishing Trips is one of Sydney's most experienced Fishing Trips, with over 25 years experience. Deep Sea, Reef, Game and Sports Fishing Trips, Harbour Cruises and Bucks Parties 
Reef Fishing Trips
This charter is the most popular fishing trip that we offer, taking you out over some of the most incredible fishing reefs off the coast of Sydney. Typically, we fish between 3 and 12 nautical miles from the coast, in productive fishing waters of up to 120 meters in depth. We will then target all reef species, using the finest quality Shimano Penn rods, reels, and fresh baits.

Sports Fishing Trips
The main aim of our sports fishing trips is to land the larger predatory and pelagic fish, like the Mahi Mahi, Marlin, and Kingfish. Depending on the conditions, we light troll to 9-mile, 12 mile, or long reef for Marlin and Tuna, and then live bait and jig for Kingfish or Mahi Mahi.
