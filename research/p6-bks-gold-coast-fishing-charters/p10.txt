Please enjoy a snapshot of our fishing charters by viewing our photo gallery
Each photo category has up to 30 feature images of moments on our Gold Coast fishing charters, which depart from Runaway Bay, only an hour from Brisbane CBD.
We fish reefs in depths of 16-100m for all species pictured (+ more). Our most regularly fished depths are 30-60m, on our popular half day fishing charters.
Please feel free to visit our Facebook, Instagram & YouTube pages, @bksgoldcoastfishingcharters, for heaps of regularly updated pics & vids.
Kids Catches Snapper Mackerel Tuna Jewfish Cobia Tusk Fish Pearl Perch Teraglin & Tailor Flathead Reef Fish Mahi Mahi Cod Wahoo & Kingfish Billfish 
Did you know you can easily get to our fishing charters from Brisbane?
Our charters depart from Runaway Bay at 6am and 11:15am, 7 days per week (weather permitting). It’s only a 1 hour drive to your fishing charter from the Brisbane CBD.
How can I get there from Brisbane?
All you need to do is travel south on the Pacific Motorway until you reach Hope Island (exit 57). Follow that road until you take a left onto Matthew Flinders Drive. Turn right onto Bayview St. Take a left into Runaway Bay Marina. That’s it!
