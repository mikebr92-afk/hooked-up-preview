Inshore Estuary Fishing Charters
Private half day Gold Coast calm water charters with BK's Gold Coast Fishing Charters.
Join Captain Bec or Kaleb for a half day calm water fishing charter in the sheltered waters of the Gold Coast estuaries, rivers & Broadwater. Departing from Runaway Bay or Paradise Point, we offer lure or bait fishing from the Nerang River to Jumpinpin for groups of up to 3 people.
Suitable for all experience levels, from the age of 6yrs+, we cater to your needs, from teaching the basics for catching a feed, right up to targeting big fish on live baits and big lures. 
The target species on our half day calm water fishing charters depend on the season and time of day/tide. They include flathead, whiting, bream, tarwhine, tusk fish, jewfish, mangrove jack, tailor, trevally, grunter, squid, sand/mud crabs & more. 
Your boat for your half day Gold Coast calm water charter...
‘Black Betty’ (or ‘BB’) is a 5.5m Stacer Northern Fisher. She is a stable, comfortable centre console that was built with one purpose in mind; fishing! Powered by a quiet, smokeless 115hp Yamaha 4 stroke engine, she is equipped with a T-top shade roof, seating, an electric motor, casting deck, large live bait tank, heaps of rod storage, stereo & a cold ice box for your catch as well as dry storage for your belongings. 
All of our departure locations have toilets, plus plenty of spots nearby no matter where we fish, where your skipper will happily stop in for a toilet break.
Visit the “ Your Crew ” page to meet the crew who will welcome you on board BB and assist you throughout your trip.
Inclusions on all inshore estuary fishing charters are:
Quality left or right-handed rods and reels, bait, tackle, lures, sunscreen, soft drinks, water and tips and assistance from your skipper. 
It’s your choice to keep or release your legal catch. We do have a boat rule of catch & release only on any flathead that is 60cm+, as these big girls are definitely all breeders and their release is important for the future of our amazing fishery.
Your kept fish are bled, gutted, iced down and bagged for you to take home. Visit our YouTube channel for filleting and preparation videos.
