Our Boats
Your fishing charter boats for your adventure
Your fishing charter boat for your offshore/ocean trip is a safe and stable fishing platform, as used by many rescue authorities.
Visit the “ Your Crew ” page to meet the crew who will welcome you on board the charter boat and assist you throughout your trip.
‘Reef Cat II’ is a 10 meter/33ft Cougar catamaran with fishing areas on both the front and back decks and is licensed to carry 12 passengers. Built to government survey regulations, survey safety equipped, and fitted with modern fishing electronics, she is a fast, stable, twin hulled, wide bodied, twin engine boat with ample seating under a large roof area. She is powered by two quiet and smokeless 250hp Yamaha four-stroke engines.
Reef Cat II is a purpose built fishing charter boat and therefore has a large , insulated icebox to chill your catch. 
For your comfort , there is an enclosed and modern electric flushing, highly sanitized , private toilet in the forward cabin. It is complemented with an electric fresh running water tap with basin, liquid soap, mirror and disinfectant deodorizers.
Your fishing charter boat for your estuary/calm water charter is a safe and stable boat, designed with FISHING in mind…
‘Black Betty’ is a 5.5m Stacer Northern Fisher, licensed to carry 3 passengers on our estuary/calm water charters. She is a stable, comfortable centre console designed for fishing, built to government safety regulations and fitted with modern fishing electronics. She is powered by a quiet, smokeless 115hp Yamaha 4 stroke engine, and is equipped with a T-top shade roof, seating, an electric motor, casting deck, large live bait tank, heaps of rod storage, stereo & a cold ice box for your catch.
