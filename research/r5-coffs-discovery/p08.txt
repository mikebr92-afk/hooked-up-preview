About Us
Coffs Harbour International Marina has been part of the local coastline for decades, evolving into one of the most recognised boating destinations on the Mid North Coast. Positioned between Sydney and South East Queensland, the marina was purpose-built to provide a safe harbour and a welcoming stopover for travelling vessels.
Over time, it has become more than just a marina - it’s a hub for the boating community, combining recently upgraded facilities & amenities, and the friendly service that Coffs is known for. Designed with both locals and visiting boaties in mind, the marina is dedicated to making every journey easier, safer, and more enjoyable, ensuring your time on the water is always memorable.
