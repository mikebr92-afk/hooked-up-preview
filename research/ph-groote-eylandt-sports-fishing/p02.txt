Questions? We're here to help 
If you have a question that isn’t answered below, feel free to call our friendly staff on (08) 8996 4200 or enquire online . 
Is there free WIFI throughout the lodge? 
Yes! We provide free WIFI in all rooms and through the lodge. Just ask our friendly staff for details.
What is the mobile reception like on Groote Eylandt? 
The Telstra network has excellent coverage across the entire lodge and Groote Eylandt. If you are with another mobile carrier, we recommend using a Telstra mobile hotspot or using your network carrier's call-over-WIFI functionality if available.
Are the rooms non-smoking? 
Inside the rooms is non-smoking, however, smoking is permitted on the outdoor private balconies and in the designated smoking area at the front of the lodge.
What are your check-in and check-out times? 
Check in time is 2pm and check out is 10am.
How do I get from the airport to the lodge? 
We can provide airport transfers if required. Call our friendly staff on (08) 8996 4200 to book your airport transfers.
What is your cancellation policy? 
Any bookings cancelled within 48 hours of the scheduled arrival will incur a one-night cancellation fee. Please forward all cancellation requests via email to res@gelnt.com.au .
What are the payment options for room bookings? 
Payment for room bookings can be made using all major credit/debit cards including Visa, Mastercard, AMEX and Discover.
Do you provide extra beds or cots for children? 
Yes we do. Call our friendly staff on (08) 8996 4200 to talk about your specific requirements.
