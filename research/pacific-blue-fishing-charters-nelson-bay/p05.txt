FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION
*What do I Need To Bring? 
Bring a Hat, Coat and Food /Snacks.
*Can I bring alcohol? 
YES, in moderation and cans only! NO GLASS
*Where do we meet? 
We are on Dock “C” at D’Albora Marina, Nelson Bay
*Do I need a fishing Licence? 
No, that is provided.
*Can I bring my own fishing gear? 
Yes, you can.
*Can I keep my catch? 
Yes, your catch will be cleaned and bagged for you ready to take home (Bag and size limits apply).
*What happens in the event of bad weather? 
We will contact you the day before to confirm the charter is good to go, if it is not deposits will be refunded or you can rebook for a later date.
If you have a question that is not answered here, please do not hesitate to send us a quick email
