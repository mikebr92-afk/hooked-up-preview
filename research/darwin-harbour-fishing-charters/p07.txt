" EXPLORE LITCHFIELD NATIONAL PARK WITH OUR LOCAL GUIDE AND DECADES OF EXPERIENCE GUIDING IN THE TOP END OF AUSTRALIA"
Enjoy peace and tranquility while relaxing under the waterfalls and basking in nature’s oasis while swimming in clear, freshwater pools.
If you love travelling and exploring as much as we do then Litchfield National park is a must-see. What an adventure!
From the plunge pools, waterfalls and rockholes to the 4WD track, it is a spectacular true beauty to experience.
Litchfield Park Adventures, a locally owned business with twenty years of experience guiding in the Top End of Australia, offer dedicated tours to suit the requirements of all travellers designed to evade the times when there are big crowds at the major waterfalls and lunch spots in the park.
The tours aim to provide you with an excellent experience were you can immerse in the tranquillity and the wonders of nature Litchfield has to offer whilst relaxing under the waterfalls and basking in nature’s oasis while swimming in clear, freshwater pools.​
We cater for small groups or a private charter tour.
Travel with ease and comfort whilst you’re on board the Rosa air conditioned van with wheelchair accessible.
Highlights of the tour
• Jumping Crocodile Cruise- Crocodylus Park
• Handling of crocodile experience
• Viewing of crocodiles from the riverside platform
• Butterfly Farm and Animal Petting Zoo
• Wangi and Florence Falls
• Buley Rock Hole
• Magnetic Termite Mound and Cathedral Termite Mound
• Darwin to Katherine
