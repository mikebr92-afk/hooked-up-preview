Your Next Fishing Adventure Awaits.
There’s nothing quite like a day fishing off Rainbow Beach.
For over 25 years, Baitrunner has been part of the local fishing scene. In December 2024 we took the helm, bringing fresh energy while proudly continuing the legacy of this much-loved charter.
We’re passionate about fishing, our local waters and giving everyone a bloody good day out whether you’re a seasoned fisho or picking up a rod for the first time.
We’re proud to carry the Baitrunner name forward and can’t wait to see what’s next.
Good fishing. Good company. Even better memories. 
9.8 metres of fishing history.
• Baitrunner is a 9.8m Cougar Catamaran , purpose-built for spending a full day on the water.
• With plenty of space onboard, comfortable fishing positions and the stability of a catamaran hull, Baitrunner is ready for everything from chasing reef fish to getting amongst the action offshore. We have carried out extensive renovations on the old girl to catch her up to 2026 without compramising her classic look and comfort. 
• The Baitrunner has a full bimini roof to give you plenty of shade on those hot Queensland days, as well as toilet facilities onboard for when nature calls!
• She’s fitted out with the latest Garmin electronics to help us find the fish, navigate safely and stay in communication while we’re out on the water.
• Before we head off, we’ll run everyone through a quick safety briefing so you know where everything is and what to do while onboard. Safety always comes first, particularly when crossing the bar, and we’ll only head out when conditions are safe to do so.
• You’re more than welcome to BYO alcohol, we just ask that you bring cans only, no glass . Smoking is also permitted in designated areas onboard, with ashtrays provided so nothing ends up overboard.
• The Baitrunner carries a maximum of 10 passengers plus our 2 crew , which means there’s plenty of room to fish without feeling like you’re packed in like sardines.
• All you need to do is bring your food, drinks and yourself, we will take care of the fishing gear and the rest! 
Meet the team
Kurt is the owner and lead skipper of Rainbow Beach Fishing Charters and has been fishing the waters from Brisbane right up to 1770 for over 20 years. He knows his way around these waters and loves nothing more than getting people out for a great day of fishing. Whether you’ve been fishing your whole life or have never picked up a rod before, Kurt will make sure you have a good time and head home with a nice full esky!
Billy is our qualified and highly experienced skipper, bringing plenty of knowledge out on the water to make sure everyone has a safe and enjoyable day offshore. Working alongside him is Ryan, our deckhand, who’ll be there throughout the day helping with everything from baiting up and rigging gear to landing fish and keeping things running smoothly on deck.
Together, Billy and Ryan make a great crew and are always keen to help you get onto the fish and make the most of your day aboard the Baitrunner.
You’ll probably chat to Georgia along the way too! She’s behind the scenes looking after most of the bookings, messages and admin, and will usually be the one helping you get everything organised before your day out on the Baitrunner.
What's included?
Your charter includes:
• Rods & reels
• Fishing tackle
• Bait
• Bottled water
• Ice / refrigerated storage
• Experienced skipper & crew
• A full day on the water
Just bring:
• Your own food & snacks for the day
• Hat
• Sunscreen
• Sunglasses
• Suitable clothing
• Any personal drinks
• BYO alcohol if you'd like — cans only
