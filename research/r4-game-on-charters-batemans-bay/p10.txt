Privacy Policy: 
At Game On Charters , the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This privacy policy document outlines the types of personal information that may be received and collected by Game On Charters and how it is used. 
Game On Charters values privacy and has strict website integrity and security controls.
We encourage you to read the following points regarding our privacy policies that may affect you. If you have any concerns please don’t hesitate to contact us. 
This website and its operation may collect the following types of personal information from you, particularly if you engage in email or phone conversation:
How the website may collect your personal information . 
• We use your information to provide our service to you. 
•We also use it to improve our service and to notify you of opportunities that we think you might be interested in. 
•We will not sell or rent your information to any other organisation. 
•We will never provide your information to third parties, except if approached by an Australian Government Dept that we are liable by law to abide by their instructions. 
Security of your personal information. 
• All reasonable security steps are taken by us to protect your personal information. However, we cannot be held liable for any unauthorized access to this information.
• You can access and update your personal information by contacting us direct. At all times it is essential you have our written acknowledgement of such communication. 
Website 
When you visit the website we may collect certain information such as browser type, operating system, website visited immediately before coming to the site, etc. This information is used in an aggregated manner to analyse how people use our site, such that we can improve service. 
We utilise the WordPress Plugin: Slimstats Analytics – this Plugin unlike Google, Jetpack and many other services (that have full unrestricted access to the data they collect on websites) at Slimstats they DO NOT share nor store users data, their goal is to make sure that our website is the sole owner of data collected by the plugin. 
Log Files, Cookies & Web Beacons 
Like many other Web sites, ours makes use of log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, type of browser, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyse trends, administer the site, track user’s movement around the site, and gather demographic information. IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. 
As is very common for most websites, ours does use cookies. You do not need to have any fear of cookies; they are not malicious programs that access or damage your computer. Cookies are very small files which a website uses to identify you when you come back to the site and to store details about your use of the site. 
This website utilises cookies to improve the experience of people using the website. 
Third party sites – LINKS 
At times, my website may have links to other websites not owned or controlled by us. 
We are not responsible for the content nor operation of these websites or the consequences of you visiting from the link, to them. 
Complaints about privacy 
If you have any complaints about our privacy practices, please feel free to send in details of your complaints to us. We take complaints very seriously and will respond shortly after receiving written notice of your complaint.
