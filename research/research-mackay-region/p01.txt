Get Hooked on Mackay
We know the Mackay Region is Australia’s premier recreational fishing destination – and pretty soon you too will be Hooked on Mackay! 
From hard-hitting pelagics offshore, iconic barramundi and sooty grunter in the dams - to the exciting sports fish right at Mackay’s doorstep, what’s not to love? Complete with our very own net-free zone, there’s something for the first-time fisher, through to the professional angler. 
Mackay boasts 365 days a year fishing and will have your lines tight and rods bent in no time. 
New to Mackay or looking for somewhere different to wet a line, we’ve got you covered with a location to suit every fishing style and target species.
