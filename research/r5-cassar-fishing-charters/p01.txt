Whats included 
Everything you need for your Melbourne Fishing Charter. 
Our Melbourne fishing charters are fully equipped with premium fishing tackle, quality rods and reels, bait and all the equipment you need for a successful day on the water. Whether you're chasing Snapper, Tuna, Kingfish, Gummy Sharks, Whiting or Squid, we provide the gear to get the job done. 
Fishing Tackle 
Our vessels carry premium fishing tackle, quality rods and reels, hooks, bait and all the equipment needed to target our range of species. 
Fish storage & Refreshments 
Our vessels carry a large esky and multiple chiller bags to keep your catch chilled throughout the trip. Bottled water is provided, and fish bags are supplied for you to take your catch home. 
New or seasoned, this is the charter for you 
Our fishing charters cater for everyone, from complete beginners to experienced anglers. Our crew is there to help throughout the trip, so all experience levels are welcome. 
What to Bring on Your Fishing Charter 
To make your day comfortable and enjoyable, we recommend bringing:
&bull; Valid Victorian Recreational Fishing Licence (if required)
&bull; Food and snacks for your trip
&bull; Seasickness tablets if required
&bull; Suitable non-slip footwear
&bull; Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
&bull; An esky in your vehicle to take your catch home
Bottled water is supplied onboard. 
Alcohol is welcome in moderation.
For safety reasons, glass is not permitted onboard — cans only.
