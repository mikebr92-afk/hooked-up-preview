Offshore Fishing in Melbourne 
The most famous species of shark to target in our local waters is the super popular MAKO SHARK. 
Now this shark 
means business, just by looking at the photos and videos does not compare until you finally see one in the flesh, they are mean and angry with a face full of sharp teeth 
but we still choose to catch them! 
Fishing 
with a big burly trail out the back of the boat often without a bait in the water we will have these sharks 
come up and circle our boat, its an amazing site to see as you hand feed one of these beast 
right next to the boat. 
We will also have several baits out on balloons at different depths. 
Once the shark 
eats the bait that is when it gets exciting, These sharks 
are known for being the fastest shark 
to swim in the ocean and known to jump up to 20 feet out of the water! 
We will be using wire trace rigs with Shimano Game outfits to target these sharks. 
Blue Sharks, Bronze Whalers, Hammerheads & Thresher Sharks 
are also a very popular species as we target sharks offshore. 
While we wait, we will be fishing on the bottom for species such as School Sharks, Gummy Sharks, Snapper, Flathead and more!
