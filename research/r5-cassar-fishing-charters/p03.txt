Tuna Fishing Locally & South West Coast 
Cassar Fishing Charters 
specialize in catching Southern Bluefin Tuna 
from the south west coast of Portland, locally out the front of the heads 
and offshore from Westernport . 
We have such a healthy and sustainable fishery that every year we get an incredible run of tuna 
up and down our coast. 
Weather it be the sized tuna 
or the famous fish of a life time BARREL TUNA we have got you covered. 
All of our gear is top of the range and maintained to perfection to insure that you can land the fish of a life time each and every time. 
A single fish might take a couple minutes to land or 5 hours to land, either way as long as it ends up in the boat that is all that matters. 
We often get a big run of bluefin 
close to home so keep a look out and stay in touch with our social media for updates! 
Bring the soy sauce and wasabi because fresh sashimi and sushi is on the menu! 
We specialize it TOP WATER 
fishing casting at southern bluefin tuna , A stick bait and popper rod is always rigged and ready to cast at a school of busting TUNA! 
You are welcome to bring your own personal top water gear along.
