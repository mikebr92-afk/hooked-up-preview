RiverFly 1864 is Tasmania's leading guided fly fishing operator, providing guided fly fishing day trips ex Launceston and extended wilderness fly fishing trips.
RiverFly 1864 are recipients of the Qantas Australian Tourism Award (2016 Gold and 2017 Silver), Specialised Tourism Services), and Tasmanian Tourism Award (Gold 2016 & 2017).
Based in Launceston, Northern Tasmania, RiverFly 1864 can tailor fly fishing trips from 1-10 days. Our speciality is sight fishing and dry fly fishing, all within a 60 minute drive of accommodation.
Their rates include the use of all gear as required.

Accommodation 
Gorgeous on Galvin
Launceston
$270 - $390
This charming home in South Launceston blends early nineteen hundreds character with modern comfort, offering three queen bedrooms and a spacious tiered backyard just minutes from the hospital, city centre and Cataract Gorge.
Ducted heating and cooling keep the home comfortable throughout the year, while a light filled lounge with a kids play area, comfortable leather seating and a smart television make for relaxed evenings in.
The fully equipped kitchen features a five burner gas stove and breakfast bench seating, opening onto a dining area with a six seater table, perfect for shared meals together.
Two queen bedrooms sit toward the front of the home, while the third, with its own access to a sun soaked back deck, adjoins a generous bathroom and integrated laundry.
Outside, the tiered backyard offers established trees, fruit trees and play equipment, with driveway parking and easy access to Launceston's cafes, restaurants and attractions.
Guests enjoy access to the whole home, making this property ideally suited to families and groups seeking a comfortable, character filled base in Launceston.

Mantra Charles Hotel
Launceston
$149 - $738
At Mantra Charles Hotel you'll find yourself captivated by Launceston. From visiting the acclaimed wine region of the Tamar Valley to discovering the beauty of the Cataract George, you'll quickly learn why the city is such a sought after holiday destination. 
Choose from several accommodation types, including the Hotel King Room, Hotel King Studio, Two-Bedroom Connecting and One Bedroom Spa Apartment. Mantra Charles Hotel has a unique art deco flavour and is within close proximity of the region's main attractions. 
This Launceston hotel has a great range of facilities including the onsite The Charles Restaurant, which serves delicious Tasmanian cuisine. If you're travelling to Launceston for business, the hotel lounge offers the perfect place to meet with a colleague or client and if you're planning to hold a conference or meeting there are three dedicated spaces to choose from. Wireless internet (Wi-Fi) is available throughout the hotel and there is onsite parking available. 
From Mantra Charles Hotel the airport is just a 12 minute drive away, making this hotel highly accessible and is a great place to stay for your next holiday.
This hotel does not accept cash as a payment method. Credit, debit and prepaid cards are accepted to buy goods and services.

Henry’s on Balfour
Launceston
$250 - $600
Henry's on Balfour is understated opulence in a two-story terrace located in the heart of cafe culture Launceston.
A unique Historic building paying homage to Tasmania's Victorian era, our light and bright abode will have you wishing for more time spent lounging in luxury.
Scrumptious linens to wrap yourself in, sumptuous bathrooms for every bedroom, two relaxing lounge areas, a sunny study and a modern kitchen, they know you'll never want to leave.
Henry's was built in 1881 and has all the charm of a two-storey Victorian terrace but with the luxury of a modernised interior. Large spacious rooms filled with natural light, high ceilings and carefully selected luxurious furnishings to ensure it will be very hard to leave.

Acknowledgement of Country
We acknowledge the Tasmanian Aboriginal people and their enduring custodianship of Lutruwita / Tasmania.
We honour the uninterrupted care, protection and belonging to these islands, skies and waterways, before the invasion and colonisation of European settlement.
As part of a tourism industry that welcomes visitors to these lands, we acknowledge our responsibility to represent to our visitors Tasmania's deep and complex history, fully, respectfully and truthfully.
We acknowledge the Aboriginal people who continue to care for this country today.
We pay our respects to their Elders, past and present.
We honour their stories, songs, art, and culture, and their aspirations for the future of their people and these lands.
