The MV James Cook is a custom built charter vessel operating from 1770! We offer a variety of charters on the magnificant Southern Great Barrier Reef from fishing & surfing to scuba Diving & snorkelling!
Description
The MV James Cook has been operating from the 1770 Marina by the Mergard family for the past 21 years. The 45ft steel charter vessel cruises the waters of the 'Capricorn Bunker Group' on the Southern Great Barrier Reef - a cluster of 21 reefs and 15 islands.
The vessel can accommodate for up to 10 passengers, with all meals and on board bunk accommodation included.
You can put together a group of mates to charter the vessel for your own adventure or join one of our ready made packages
Hours: 
7 days, 8am - 5pm
