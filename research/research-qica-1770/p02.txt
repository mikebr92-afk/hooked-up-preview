1770's Best Offshore Fishing Since 1996
Description
* Fishing charter business taking half day, 3/4 day and full day bookings. 
*Reef fishing to all the Capricorn and Bunker Group. Over 30 species of fish to catch. Local hot-spots. One on one or group bookings. 
*8.2 mtr boat, cruises at 20 knots. Designed for the capricorn coast EVERYTHING PROVIDED (except lunch). 
*Friendly, knowledgable skipper who can assist with baiting hooks and handling fish! 
Species: red emperor, coral trout, pearl perch, snapper, tusk fish, cod, hussar, rosy, sweetlip, black snapper, spangled emperor, mackeral, tuna, cobia, sailfish, marlin and LOTS more!
Hours: 
7am until 7pm, 7 days a week but phone is manned after hours
Map
741 Captain Cook Dr, Seventeen Seventy QLD 4677 Australia
Cancellation Policy
No cancellation fee if more than 48hrs notice given.
20% cancellation fee if less than 48hrs notice given & no refund given if no-show on the day
