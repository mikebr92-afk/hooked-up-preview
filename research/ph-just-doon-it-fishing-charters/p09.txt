Why Fishing Trips Make the Best Mates’ Getaway
Jayson & Kate Muldoon 
Oct 5 
2 min read 
If you’re tossing up ideas for your next trip with the boys (or the crew), let us make it easy for you. A fishing charter ticks every box! 
No overplanning, no stress, no “what are we doing today?” chats. Just good mates, solid fishing, and a few laughs along the way. 
Here’s why a fishing trip might just be the best getaway you’ll ever book: 
1. It’s All About Quality Time (No Distractions) 
Let’s be honest, How often do you actually get proper time with your mates these days? 
No work. No rush. No phones glued to your face. 
Out on the water, it’s just: 
• Casting lines 
• Talking absolute rubbish 
• And catching up properly 
It’s the kind of trip where stories get told, laughs get louder, and memories just happen naturally. 
2. Everyone’s Got One Goal: Catch Barra 
There’s something about chasing barra that brings everyone together. 
Doesn’t matter if you’re a seasoned fisho or it’s your first crack, everyone’s in it. 
• The banter when someone loses a big one 
• The hype when someone lands a solid barra 
• The friendly competition all day long 
Trust us… it gets addictive real quick. 
3. You Get the Full Experience (Not Just Fishing) 
It’s not just about what you catch, it’s everything around it. 
Early starts. Glassy water. Wildlife. That first coffee or cold beer after a session. 
It’s the kind of reset you didn’t realise you needed. 
And yeah… it definitely beats sitting in traffic or staring at emails. 
4. The Stories Get Better Every Time 
Every trip turns into stories you’ll be talking about for years. 
“The one that got away…” “That massive hook-up…” “That time your mate absolutely stuffed it…” 
And somehow, the fish always get bigger every time the story’s told. 
5. Zero Stress. We Handle Everything 
One of the best parts? You don’t have to organize anything. 
At JUST DOON IT. we take care of: 
• The boat 
• The gear 
• The spots 
• The knowledge 
All you need to do is show up and enjoy it. 
Easy as!
