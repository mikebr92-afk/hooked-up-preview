Frequently Asked Questions 
About going on a barramundi fishing charter in the Northern Territory. 
09 
What's the maximum number of people allowed on a charter? 
Our charters take a maximum of 4 passengers to ensure a personalized and comfortable experience, and for survey purposes. This allows for more one-on-one time with the guide and a better chance of catching barramundi. 
08 
Can I keep the barramundi I catch? 
Yes, you can keep barramundi within the legal size limits, and our crew can help prepare your catch for you to take home. We are a catch and release charter company - but permit a feed for 4 to take home. 
Yes, safety is a top priority. Our boats are equipped with all necessary safety gear, and our guides are trained in first aid. We also provide instructions on how to stay safe in the unique Northern Territory environment, including how to avoid crocodiles. 
Is it safe to fish in the Northern Territory? 
07 
01 
What is the best time of year to catch barramundi in the Northern Territory? 
The best time to catch barramundi is during the Run Off season, which typically occurs from March to May. This period follows the wet season, when water levels recede and barramundi are more concentrated in the rivers and billabongs. 
05 
How long are the fishing charters, and what is included? 
Charters can range from half-day to multi-day trips (2-5 days). Packages typically include pick-up and drop-off, all fishing equipment, safety gear, meals, and drinks. Accommodation may be included on multi-day trips and we can arrange a package for you for this. 
02 
Can I fish outside of the Runoff? 
YES - at Just Doon It, we are able to get you onto Barramundi in the Dry season when people come up north for the warmth during May-October. It can be a little quieter on the water but we have a few tricks to still get you some fish! 
03 
Do I need any prior fishing experience to catch barramundi? 
No prior experience is necessary. We will provide all the instruction you need, whether you're a first-time angler or an experienced fisherman. 
04 
What should I bring on a barramundi fishing charter? 
It's recommended to bring sunscreen (we also stock this), a hat, sunglasses, and a camera. We provide all fishing gear, and tackle. Light, long-sleeve clothing is advised for sun protection. Don't forget to bring a sense of adventure! 
While we can't guarantee a catch (because it's called fishing, not catching!), our aim is to use our extensive knowledge and experience, which significantly increases your chances of landing a barra. If you listen to the instructions given to you carefully - this also increases your chances. Also - if you don't have your rod in the water - you won't catch a fish - so make sure you always have tight lines Dooners!

Do I need a fishing license? 
No, you do not need a fishing license when you book a fishing charter with us. All necessary permits and licenses are covered by the charter. 
11 
How do I book a barramundi fishing charter? 
You can book directly through our here, by phone, or via email. We recommend booking in advance, especially for the Run Off season, as spots fill up quickly.
