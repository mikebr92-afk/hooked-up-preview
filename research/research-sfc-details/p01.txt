Fishing Offshore 1770 operates from SEVENTEEN SEVENTY, QLD, 4677 
Offers Reef and Offshore fishing charters on the Southern Great Barrier Reef. Our Wide Bodied Cougar Cat has space for 4-10 anglers, is powered by 2x 250hp suzuki outboards and cruises at 25 knots. We use quality Shimano gear. Targeting reef fish including Red Emperor and Coral Trout, and pelagic species including Spanish Mackerel and Tuna. All you need to bring is a hat, food and drinks.
