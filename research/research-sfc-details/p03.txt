Reef Fishing Charters 1770 operates from SEVENTEEN SEVENTY, QLD, 4677 
Offshore reef fishing trips available on 1770’s charter vessel the "Southern Comfort". Experience full day, 1/2 day fishing charters and private charters departing 1770 to the southern Great Barrier Reef. 
Powered by 2 x 225hp V6 Yamaha outboards, and fitted with the latest navigational and fish finding equipment, our modern Noosa Cat the "Southern Comfort" offers comfortable and trips out to the Great Barrier Reef. 
The Southern Comfort has a toilet, galley, comfortable cabin with table and seating. The back deck will comfortably accommodate seven anglers.
Our southern Great Barrier trips target Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Sweet Lip, Spanish Mackerel and more
