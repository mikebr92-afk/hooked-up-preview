MV Booby Bird operates from GLADSTONE, QLD, 4680 
Boobybird is a locally owned and a family operated fishing charter operating from Gladstone with over 30 years of fishing charter experience. Destinations include Capricornia Cays, Capricorn Bunker Groups and the Great Barrier Reef ex Gladstone.
Boobybird is a 23 metre liveaboard mothership powered by a single 425hp Caterpillar engine with a maximum speed of 8 knots. She has five tenders used to access the best fishing areas.
