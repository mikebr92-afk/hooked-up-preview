The Ultimate Dundee Beach Fishing Experience
Join us on one of our most rewarding charters! This full-day trip departs from the Dundee Beach boat ramp and runs down the coast to the Peron Islands, roughly 1.5 hours away, taking in some of the best reef and sportfishing water on the NT coastline.
You’ll be chasing the prized table fish Golden Snapper (Fingermark) and the arm-pulling Black Jewfish, backed up by Red Emperor, Grassy Emperor, Coral Trout, Tusk Fish and Cod, so there’ll be plenty of variety to keep everyone on the boat busy.
You don’t need to know where the fish are – that’s our job. Gerard puts 30+ years of local knowledge to work reading the season, tides and current conditions to find the best ground for the day, with dolphins and turtles for company and, if the timing’s right, Mackerel or Tuna busting up bait on the surface.

Prime Dundee Beach Fishing Year Round
Dundee Beach delivers quality fishing in every season. Whether you’re chasing hard-pulling bottom species or the chance at seasonal sport fish, there’s always something biting.
Don’t stress about picking the spot. We’ll confirm the best fishing ground before your tour based on current conditions.
All Year Round
The run down to the Peron Islands takes you through some of Dundee’s most reliable reef country, and the fishing holds up no matter when you book. Golden Snapper (Fingermark) are the prize most anglers are chasing – a genuine table fish that pulls hard and tastes even better. Alongside them, Black Jewfish provide some of the toughest fights you’ll find in NT waters, testing your arms as much as your gear. Round out your day with Red Emperor, Grassy Emperor, Coral Trout, Tusk Fish and Cod, and there’s plenty of variety to keep everyone on the boat busy.
• → Golden Snapper (Fingermark)
• → Black Jewfish
• → Red Emperor
• → Grassy Emperor
• → Coral Trout
• → Tusk Fish
• → Cod
Dry Season Bonus
May – October
When the water cools off through the dry, a second layer of fishing opens up on top of the year-round staples. Sport fish push through in bigger numbers, and the surface action gets seriously good. You can expect Queenfish smashing bait, hard-charging Trevally, and the odd Spanish Mackerel or Tuna putting your reel to the test – with a genuine chance of hooking a big Cobia along the way.
• → Mackerel
• → Tuna
• → Trevally
• → Queenfish
• → Cobia (seasonal chance)

Frequently Asked Questions
I've never fished before. Can I still come? 
Absolutely! Gerard has 30+ years of experience teaching people how to fish. Whether you’re a complete beginner or an experienced angler, you’re welcome. We provide all equipment and personalised coaching throughout the day.
How far is the trip and what's it like getting there? 
We run down the coast from the Dundee Beach boat ramp to the Peron Islands, about 1.5 hours away. It’s a full day out, and we’ll be back at the ramp by approximately 4pm.
Where exactly do we meet? 
Meet at Dinah Beach Boat Ramp at 5:15am for shared charters and private charters up to 4 people, with transfers included. If you’re self-driving for a private charter of up to 6, meet Gerard at the Dundee Beach boat ramp at 6:45am for a 7am departure.
What's the difference between the transfer and self-drive options? 
The transfer option (included in shared charters and private charters up to 4 people) means you can leave the driving to us. You’ll simply meet us at Dinah Beach Boat Ramp and we’ll get you to the boat. Self-drive is only available for private charters of up to 6 people, and suits groups who’d rather bring their own vehicle out to the Dundee Beach boat ramp, giving you a bit more flexibility with gear and timing on the day.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
Safety is our priority. We constantly monitor weather, wind conditions, and sea state before and during every charter. We stop fishing in electrical storms, and our boat has a hard-top roof and drop-down clears for protection. In the rare event of a cyclone or severe weather system, we’ll contact you to reschedule.
Can children come on the tour? 
Yes! Children aged 8 and above are welcome. It’s a fantastic family experience, and we provide fishing gear suited to all experience levels.
