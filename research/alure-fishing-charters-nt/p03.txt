Quality Fishing, Close to the City
Darwin Harbour and its surrounding grounds fish well year-round, with a few extra species joining the mix in the dry season. Fishing this close to the city means you won’t waste your time on the run out to other destinations, as these grounds are all close enough to Cullen Bay to maximise your time with a line in the water.
And the best bit?
You won’t have to stress about picking the right location.
We’ll confirm the best fishing destination before your tour based on current conditions.
All Year Round
Darwin Harbour’s reefs, wrecks and channels hold quality fish in every season, no matter when you book. Drop a bait into the right patch of structure and feel that unmistakable knock-knock-thud as a big Jewfish or Golden Snapper takes hold and bulldozes straight back for cover.
These aren’t fish that come easy. Black Jewfish fight dirty around structure and will test your tackle and your patience, while a quality Golden Snapper will test your drag all the way to the surface. Get it right and you’ll be icing down a feed that’ll last the week.
• → Black Jewfish
• → Golden Snapper (Fingermark)
• → Blue Salmon
• → Spanish Flag
• → Indonesian Snapper
• → Grunter
• → Coral Trout (Lee Point & Fenton Patches wrecks)
• → Tusk Fish (Lee Point & Fenton Patches wrecks)
Dry Season Bonus
(May to October)
When the water cools off through the dry, the bite changes character entirely. Instead of patiently working the bottom for Jewfish and Snapper, you’re casting to bait schools getting smashed on the surface – Tuna and Mackerel push into the harbour grounds and hunt in packs, often giving away their position with birds working overhead or bait scattering across the surface.
It’s fast, visual fishing. Get a metal slug or stickbait into the right spot and it rarely lasts more than a few seconds before it’s hit hard and you’re hanging on for a proper dry-season tug-of-war.
• → Tuna
• → Mackerel

Frequently Asked Questions
I've never fished before—can I still come? 
Absolutely! Gerard has 30+ years of experience teaching people how to fish. Whether you’re a complete beginner or an experienced angler, you’re welcome. We provide all equipment and personalised coaching throughout the day.
How do you decide which location to fish? 
We choose the fishing location based on three key factors: the season, current tide conditions, and recent fishing reports. You don’t need to pick, as we’ll fish the spot that gives you the best chance of success. We’ll confirm your destination 24 hours before your departure.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
We constantly monitor weather, wind conditions, and sea state before and during every charter. If conditions aren’t safe for fishing, we’ll contact you to reschedule. Your safety always comes first.
Our boat is equipped with a hard top roof and drop-down clears to protect you from sun, wind, and weather. We carry spare waterproof jackets onboard, but recommend bringing your own wet weather gear during the build-up and wet season months.
We stop fishing immediately in electrical storms. In the rare event of a cyclone or severe weather system, we’ll contact you to reschedule your charter.
Why choose a 7-hour Darwin Harbour Charter? 
A Darwin Harbour Charter is the ideal choice if you don’t want to commit to a full day on the water, making it perfect for clients with a late-night flight out of Darwin, or young families who want a shorter, easier day. You’ll still fish quality grounds and have a genuine chance at some great catches, just closer to the city and with less time travelling.
Can children come on the tour? 
Yes! Children aged 8 and above are welcome. It’s a fantastic family experience, and we’ve had many young anglers catch their first fish with us. We provide fishing gear for all experience levels.
Do you offer multi-day fishing trips? 
Yes! We can arrange custom multi-day fishing expeditions for both Barra and Blue Water, including accommodation and meals. Contact us to discuss your preferred dates and we’ll plan the ultimate Territory fishing adventure.
Where exactly do we meet? 
Meet us at Dinah Beach Boat Ramp at 6:45 am for a 7 am departure. We’ll have you back at the jetty by approximately 2pm.
What happens if I damage or drop equipment overboard on a trip? 
All rods, reels & lures supplied will require a replacement cost if lost overboard or damaged.
How many people can go on a fishing charter at one time? 
Please note there is a minimum number of people required for a public charter to proceed. If this number has not been met, we may reach out to you to reschedule.
