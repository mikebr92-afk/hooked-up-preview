Fishing Charters. 
Everything You Need Is Included
Just bring yourself and your sense of adventure—we’ve got the rest covered.
Premium Fishing Gear
Top quality rods, Shimano reels, premium tackle & lures provided. All gear is professionally maintained and chosen specifically for Territory fishing, so you can focus on the thrill of the catch.
Expert Coaching
Gerard demonstrates the most likely Barra ambush spots so you know where to cast, lure retrieval techniques, lure choice & how to stay connected to your trophy fish right up to getting it into the net to maximise your success.
Transportation Included
Pickup from Darwin at designated meeting point 5:15am for 5:30am departure in our Dodge RAM 4WD. Return at approx. 5-30pm.
Lunch & Refreshments
Lunch, bottled water, soft drinks, and ice for your BYO included.
Sun Protection
Sunscreen and hand sanitiser provided throughout the day.
Wildlife Viewing
Spot massive crocs, sea eagles, jabirus, buffalo, and more while you fish.
Meet Your Guide - Gerard
I look forward to sharing my knowledge and experience with you as we explore the Top End’s stunning scenery, abundant wildlife, and world-class fishing opportunities. Whether you’re chasing that fish of a lifetime in Darwin Harbour or heading out to remote NT destinations, I’ll ensure you experience the very best the Territory has to offer.
With over 35 years of Top End fishing experience and 30 years as a Darwin local, I’ve fished all the major river systems and explored most of the NT coastline. My background as a professional photographer means I can also capture those trophy moments for you. After first visiting Darwin on a fishing tour in 1989, I was hooked—literally—and made the permanent move from Victoria in 1996.
I’ll help you plan the perfect trip by recommending the ideal time of year, selecting the best days based on tides and local conditions, and choosing locations that are fishing at their peak as your booking date approaches.
