Two Prime Fishing Seasons
The NT has two distinct peak seasons when barramundi are most active. We fish year-round, but these periods offer the best action:
Don’t stress about picking the right location—we’ll confirm the best fishing destination before your tour based on current conditions.
Wet Season Run-Off
Mid February to May
Explosive wet season fishing action, as the flood plains drain into the creeks & into the main river, washing out bait to hungry Barra boofing everywhere, sucking in their prey.
Wait until you experience the thrill of a powerful strike from a Barra as it sucks in your lure when it hits the water. Hook-Up. Hang-On & Hang-Tight for a dirty fight, as they explode from the water trying to shake that head like crazy to throw your lure back at you.
You will be trembling with adrenalin when you experience the thrill & excitement you get when you’re hooked to a crazy Barra, dancing around the boat, trying to wear your Barra down, win the fight & land it in the net!
An experience you will never forget!
• → Daly River (normally Feb-March)
• → Shady Camp’s Mary River system (April-May)
• → Adelaide River (March-May)
• → 4WD access to remote launch points
• → Prime territory for big Barra
Build-Up Season
August to November
Offers consistently active Barramundi action as the water & daily temperature rise, along with the humidity (sweat season) The big female fish (over 90cm+) turn up at the river mouths to spawn along with the male Barra under 80cm in preparation for breeding & the approaching wet season.
They’re angry, hungry & fight like crazy. Hook-up. Hang-on. Hang Tight.
• → Adelaide River (upstream & mouth areas)
• → Tommy Cut Creek via Saltwater Arm
• → Wiltshire Creeks
• → Coastal boat access to prime spots
• → Corroboree Billabong (opens April-October)

Frequently Asked Questions
I've never fished before—can I still come? 
Absolutely! Gerard has 30+ years of experience teaching people how to fish. Whether you’re a complete beginner or an experienced angler, you’re welcome. We provide all equipment and personalised coaching throughout the day.
How do you decide which location to fish? 
We choose the fishing location based on three key factors: the season, current tide conditions, and recent fishing reports. You don’t need to pick—we’ll fish the spot that gives you the best chance of success. We’ll confirm your destination 24 hours before departure.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
Safety is our priority.
We don’t drive through rapidly rising creek or river crossings. We constantly review NT Road Reports, Weather & Wind conditions.
We stop fishing in electrical storms, we have protection from the weather with a hard top roof over the seats onboard our boat as well as drop down clears that stop the weather coming in from the sides. Storms normally occur late in the afternoons during our wet season, when we’re normally off the water.
We have spare water proof coats onboard, we recommend that you bring your own rain coat when fishing the Wet Season Run-Off.
When Barra fishing, we’re not on the open water & subjected to rough seas. We’re fishing river & estuary systems, that are in protected waters.
The only thing that stops your Barramundi Fishing Charter proceeding is the rare development of a cyclone, in these circumstances we will contact you to reschedule. In most cases we can fish areas to avoid the river systems impacted by a cyclone/severe weather event as they’re normally isolated to particular areas of the NT coastline & not inland waters.
Can children come on the tour? 
Yes! Children aged 8 and above are welcome. It’s a fantastic family experience, and we’ve had many young anglers catch their first Barramundi with us. We provide fishing gear for all experience levels.
Do you offer multi-day fishing trips? 
Yes! We can arrange custom 2-5 day fishing expeditions to locations like Daly River or Shady Camp, including accommodation and meals. Contact us to discuss your preferred dates and we’ll recommend the best destination for that time of year.
What's the difference between the fishing seasons? 
Wet Season Run-Off (Mid February to May)
Provides exceptional wet season fishing as the flood plains drain into the creeks & then into the main river, washing out bait to hungry Barra jumping over each other, competing for every morsel. Wait until you experience the thrill of a powerful strike from a Barra as it sucks in your lure when it hits the water. Hook-Up. Hang-On & Hang-Tight for a dirty fight as they explode from the water & shake that head like crazy to throw your lure back at you. An experience you will never forget!
Build-Up Season (Aug-Nov)
Offers consistently active Barramundi as the water & daily temperature rise, along with the humidity (sweat season) The big female fish (over 90cm+) turn up at the river mouths to spawn along with the male Barra under 80cm in preparation for the wet season.
They’re angry, hungry & fight like crazy. Hook-up. Hang-on. Hang Tight
Where exactly do we meet? 
We meet at a designated meeting point in Darwin at 5:15am for a 5:30am departure. (We will provide pick up location details on your booking confirmation email) We provide transfers from Darwin in our Dodge RAM 4WD to the boat launch location. Returning to boat ramp around 3:30pm & returning to Darwin drop off point at approximately 5:30pm.
