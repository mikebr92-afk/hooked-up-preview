The Ultimate Tiwi Islands Fishing Experience
Join us on one of our most popular charters! This full-day trip takes you out of Leaders Creek, past the Vernon Islands and the mouth of the mighty Adelaide River, up to the Tiwi Islands, one of the most pristine and scenic stretches of the NT coastline.
Gerard’s 30+ years of NT fishing knowledge means you’ll fish the spots that are firing, targeting the arm-pulling Black Jewfish and our prized table fish, Golden Snapper (Fingermark), along with Cod, Grunter and Salmon.
You don’t need to know where the fish are – that’s our job. Based on the season, tides, and current conditions, we’ll fish the location that gives you the best chance of landing that trophy Snapper or Jewfish, hanging out with turtles and, at the right time of year, spotting a manta ray or two as they swim around the boat.

Prime Tiwi Islands Fishing Year Round
The Tiwi Islands deliver quality fishing in every season. Whether you’re chasing hard-pulling bottom species or the chance at seasonal sport fish, there’s always something biting.
Don’t stress about picking the spot. We’ll confirm the best fishing ground before your tour based on current conditions.
All Year Round
The run out to the Tiwi Islands passes through some of the most untouched water in the Top End, and the fishing holds up no matter when you book. Golden Snapper (Fingermark) are the prize most anglers are chasing – a genuine table fish that pulls hard and tastes even better. Alongside them, Black Jewfish provide some of the toughest fights you’ll find in NT waters, testing your arms as much as your gear.
Round out the day with Cod, Grunter and Salmon, and there’s a good chance of sharing the water with turtles cruising past the boat.
• → Golden Snapper (Fingermark)
• → Black Jewfish
• → Cod
• → Grunter
• → Salmon
Dry Season Bonus
May – October
When the water cools off through the dry, a second layer of fishing opens up on top of the year-round staples. Sport fish move through in bigger numbers, and the sight-fishing gets seriously good. You can experience Queenfish smashing bait on the surface, hard-charging Trevally, and the odd Spanish Mackerel putting your reel to the test. It’s also prime time to spot manta rays around the boat, with groups of up to four seen on some of our trips.
• → Queenfish
• → Trevally
• → Spanish Mackerel
• → Manta ray sightings (seasonal)

Frequently Asked Questions
I've never fished before. Can I still come? 
Absolutely! Gerard has 30+ years of experience teaching people how to fish. Whether you’re a complete beginner or an experienced angler, you’re welcome. We provide all equipment and personalised coaching throughout the day.
How far is the trip and what's it like getting there? 
We travel out of Leaders Creek, past the Vernon Islands and the mouth of the Adelaide River, up to the Tiwi Islands. It’s a full day out, as we’re on the water by 7am and back around 4pm. We’ll be taking in some of the most scenic coastline in the Top End along the way.
Where exactly do we meet? 
Meet at Dinah Beach Boat Ramp at 5:15am for shared charters and private charters up to 4 people. If you’re self-driving to Leaders Creek boat ramp for a private charter of up to 6, you’ll meet Gerard at a predetermined location and follow him from there.
What's the difference between the transfer and self-drive options? 
The transfer option (included in shared charters and private charters up to 4 people) means you can leave the driving to us. You’ll simply meet us at Dinah Beach Boat Ramp and we’ll get you to the boat. Self-drive is only available for private charters of up to 6 people, and suits groups who’d rather bring their own vehicle out to Leaders Creek boat ramp, giving you a bit more flexibility with gear and timing on the day.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
Safety is our priority. We constantly monitor NT road reports, weather and wind conditions before and during every charter. We stop fishing in electrical storms, and our boat has a hard-top roof and drop-down clears for protection. In the rare event of a cyclone or severe weather system, we’ll contact you to reschedule.
Can children come on the tour? 
Yes! Children aged 8 and above are welcome. It’s a fantastic family experience, and we provide fishing gear suited to all experience levels.
