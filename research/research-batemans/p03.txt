Northern Rivers Sportfishing
Federal, NSW - Join us on a Tweed River Fishing Charter, approx. 1 
hour drive from the Tweed/Gold Coast. Your courtesy pick up from your 
Northern Rivers accommodation will take you to the most pristine local 
waterways where the bass are fat and healthy. Your bass fishing guide 
will accompany you throughout the day, sharing all the local secrets on 
catching Australian bass.
Contact : Simon Fitzpatrick
Tel : 0409760380 
Email 
Bay and Basin Sportsfishing 
Erowal Bay, NSW - Bay and Basin Sportsfishing " A guided fishing 
and boating experience". From Jervis Bay to Batemans Bay and 
beyond. Experience the exhilaration of fishing with soft plastic 
and hard bodied lures whilst surrounded by the stunning natural 
beauty of our pristine south coast bays, rivers, and estuaries. Some 
of the most common species we encounter are Flathead, Bream, Tailor, 
Mulloway (jewfish), Australian Salmon, Kingfish, Snapper and more. Bay 
and basin Sportsfishing is a highly professional operation set up 
to ensure you get the most out of your fishing experience in a fun 
and safe way. 
Contact : Greg Reid 
Tel : 
0413 610 832 Fax : 02 4443 0311 
Game Striker Fishing Charters 
Coffs Harbour, NSW - Catering for first timers or experienced anglers, 
Game Striker delivers the best Coffs Harbour has to offer. With a highly 
experienced crew & quality tackle we take care of everything - all you 
have to do it catch fish. Game Striker is a 38ft Flybridge - a boat 
designed specifically for Game Fishing. Our vessel is packed with 
features ensuring a comfortable safe & enjoyable day or night of 
fishing.
