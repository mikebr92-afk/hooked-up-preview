Read more 
Western Australia's North West Region is one of the world's last great wilderness areas, which has fewer people per square kilometre than almost anywhere else on the planet. An ancient land with pure Australian bushland, wild rivers, mountains, deep gorges, and where rustic red and green colours flood the landscape. It is also home to thousands of fresh and saltwater crocodiles. These living dinosaurs inhabit the river systems which swell with torrential seasonal rains. Read less

Accommodation in Western Australia is of a very high standard and there is a fantastic variety of places to stay to suit every purpose of travel, from a business trip and corporate requirements, to family beach holidays and adventures in WA's Outback. Many accommodation operators adhere to the worldwide star rating guides which allow you to easily browse your options.
