“MV Night Crossing” operates out of the Central Queensland port of Gladstone.
Departure from – 6 Beckinsale st Gladstone Qld 4680
Gladstone has an airport that flies direct in from Brisbane approx 1hour flight.
Blue and White Taxis (4972 1800) service the Airport to the Boat– approximately 10mins
If driving it would take about 6 hours from Brisbane. Approx. 600kms
Lock up car parking available free of charge at our check in facility.
If staying in Gladstone you’ll find info on the area at gladstoneregion.info.
