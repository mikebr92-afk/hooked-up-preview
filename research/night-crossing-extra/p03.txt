FYI
“MV Night Crossing” and her 6 fishing tenders are fully compliant to the Australian Maritime Safety Authority (AMSA) with current validations of
Certificate of Operation – Compulsory
Certificate of Survey – Non Compulsory – Issued only after an AMSA accredited surveyor has inspected the vessel to ensure that the vessel is maintained and operated to the highest standards.
“MV Night Crossing” also complies fully with both the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority and Qld Department of Primary Industries and Fisheries.
“MV Night Crossing” can operate in all Queensland waters and Coral Sea Territories.
Gladstone Coral Charters “MV Night Crossing” is a Proudly Australian Family owned and operated business. John and Joy Gleeson, Raymond’s parents pioneered the Swain reefs fishing charters from the 1970’s. Raymond, 2nd generation seafarer with over 30 years experience skippering fishing charters to the outer Great Barrier Reef, is proudly continuing the tradition.
