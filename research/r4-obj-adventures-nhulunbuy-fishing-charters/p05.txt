Our Fishing Nhulunbuy, Gove Charter Vessel 
Nhulunbuy, Gove Peninsula Fishing Charters 
At OBJ Adventures, our boats are an integral part of the adventure experience. Each vessel in our fleet is expertly maintained, fitted with all necessary safety equipment, and designed for maximum comfort and performance in the unique conditions of East Arnhem Land. 
Port Diver 
• Type: 7.2m Custom Built Aluminium Multihull 
• Features: Superior stability in all sea conditions. Equipped with a large canopy for weather protection and stairs at both the front and rear for easy and safe boarding. 
• Uses: Perfect for navigating the calm waters of the Gove Peninsula and ideal for both half-day and full-day fishing charters. 
Sea Ranger 
• Type: 7 .5m Custom Built Ocean Master 
• Features: An Ocean Master built tough for the conditions of East Arnhem Land, offering great stability, comfort, and reliability on the water while providing a smooth and safe ride for our guests. 
• Uses: Perfect for navigating the calm waters of the Gove Peninsula and ideal for both half-day and full-day fishing charters. 
Safety and Regulations 
• Our vessels are fitted with all necessary safety equipment, such as life jackets for all passengers, first aid kits fully stocked for emergencies, and the latest communication devices to ensure constant connectivity. 
• Each boat is surveyed according to Northern Territory regulations and undergoes regular checks to maintain our high standards of safety and functionality. 
Why Choose Us? 
Choosing OBJ Adventures means opting for safety, comfort, and reliability. Whether you're battling a giant trevally or cruising the coast, our boats are your gateway to a memorable fishing adventure in East Arnhem Land. 
Ready to set sail on a fishing adventure like no other? Book your trip today and choose the an adventure that best suits your style.
