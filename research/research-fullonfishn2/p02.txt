Explore the World-Class Fishing of the Hinchinbrook Channel
The Hinchinbrook Channel is a fishing paradise located between Townsville and Cairns in North Queensland. Just 1.5 hours north of Townsville and 2.5 hours south of Cairns, this pristine waterway is home to some of the best barra and estuary fishing in Australia.
Our home base is Ingham, situated at the southern end of the channel, just a 15-minute drive to the Dungeness boat ramp in Lucinda, where most of our charters depart.
Getting Here
• For Day Trip Anglers: Simply make your way to the Dungeness boat ramp in Lucinda, and we’ll take care of the rest.
• For Travelers Flying In: Arrive at Townsville Airport, and we’ll handle everything—from airport pick-up to accommodation and fishing—so you can focus on the adventure.
Fishing Hotspots
Hinchinbrook Channel 
A lure and live bait fisherman’s heaven, the Hinchinbrook Channel offers year-round fishing thanks to its protected waters. The diverse ecosystem is home to Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Threadfin Salmon, Finger Mark, Trevally, Queenfish, and more.
Departure Points: Lucinda & Cardwell
Missionary Bay 
Located at the northern end of Hinchinbrook Island, Missionary Bay features a large protected waterway with nine creeks, deep holes, and rock bars—perfect for live bait fishing for Barra and Mangrove Jacks.
Departure Point: Cardwell
Surrounding Creeks & Estuaries 
The Hinchinbrook region is surrounded by fish-rich waterways fed directly by the ocean. These creeks and estuaries, just a short drive from Lucinda, serve as great backup options if conditions in the main channel aren’t ideal.
