Fishing Adventures Tailored to You 
At Full On Fish’n Charters, we offer fishing experiences designed to suit every angler, whether you’re after a day trip or a multi-day fishing adventure. 
With access to the Hinchinbrook Channel, from Lucinda in the south to Cardwell and Missionary Bay in the north, we take advantage of the best fishing conditions all year round. Our trips are based on tides, weather, and the best fishing times—not the clock.
Fishing Packages 
We provide flexible charter options, from single-day trips to fully inclusive multi-day packages. The ultimate Hinchinbrook fishing adventure is a 3-day barra fishing experience with 4 nights of accommodation, but we can customise a package to suit your group’s needs.
Fishing Options 
• Day Trip (1 to 6 people) – Price on Application 
• 2 People – 2 Days Fishing / 3 Nights Accommodation / All Meals
• 3 People – 2 Days Fishing / 3 Nights Accommodation / All Meals
• 4 People – 3 Days Fishing / 4 Nights Accommodation / All Meals
• Custom packages available—just ask! 
For all pricing, give Nathan a call on 0429 332 566 
All fishing gear, bait, ice, and tackle are supplied. Day trip packages do not include food, but multi-day packages include all meals. Alcohol is BYO, but please drink responsibly. 
Accommodation 
Lucinda offers a wide range of accommodation options for visitors, ranging from caravan parks through to self-contained cabins.

What’s Included?
• All fishing tackle, ice, and bait
• Personalised fishing experience – from estuary fishing to chasing barramundi
• Upon request, filleting and freezing of your catch
• Multi-day charters include all meals 
No Set Start or Finish Time 
We go fishing when the conditions are best, and we return when you’ve had enough. 
That’s the Full On Fish’n Charters way—no pressure, just great fishing!
