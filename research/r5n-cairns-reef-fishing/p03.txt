Full Day Reef Fishing Private Charter
Private charter
$4,200 BLUE WHALER 
$4,800 AQUACAT 
$5,300 SERRANIDAE 
All prices are per vessel and include fuel, GST and applicable reef fees. No additional fuel levy applies. 
OVERVIEW 
• SUPPLIED FISHING GEAR
• BUFFET LUNCH & AFTERNOON TEA
• OPTIONAL GUIDED SNORKELING (+ Plus $375)
• EXCLUSIVE PRIVATE USE OF VESSEL
Our private charters are perfect for corporate events, birthdays, celebrations or simply enjoying a day on the Great Barrier Reef with family and friends. Enjoy exclusive use of your own vessel, experienced crew and the flexibility to combine reef fishing with snorkelling. 
Booking a private charter gives your group exclusive use of the vessel and catering options available to suit your requirements. 
Our vessels can accommodate the following fishing group sizes: 
• 
Serranidae: Up to 15 guests fishing 
• 
Aqua Cat: Up to 14 guests fishing 
• 
Blue Whaler: Up to 12 guests fishing 
Additional guests may be accommodated in certain circumstances, depending on the vessel and group requirements. Please contact our team to discuss your options. 
Inclusions

All fishing equipment, tackle and bait

Itinerary
• 
• 
7:30 AM – Check-in: Meet our friendly crew at our Check-in Hub, Shop G10, The Pier Shopping Centre, 1 Pier Point Road, Cairns. Please do not go directly to the marina, as all guests must check in at our Check-in Hub first. 
• 
Boarding: Our crew will welcome you and escort you to your vessel at Cairns Marlin Marina, where you’ll receive a welcome aboard and safety briefing.
• 
Departure: Sit back, relax and enjoy the scenic journey to the Great Barrier Reef. Travel time is approximately 90 minutes each way, depending on conditions and fishing locations.
• 
Let’s Go Fishing! Once we reach our fishing grounds, our crew will provide a brief introduction to reef fishing techniques before helping everyone get their lines in the water.
• 
Lunch: Enjoy a freshly prepared buffet lunch onboard around midday.
• 
2:30 PM – Homeward Bound: We begin our journey back to Cairns, giving you time to relax, enjoy a cold beverage if you wish, and take in the beautiful reef scenery. Afternoon tea will also be served during the return journey.
• 
4:00 PM – Return: Arrive back at Cairns Marlin Marina with your catch and some fantastic memories!
Optional – Catch & Cook Experience 
Why not enjoy your freshly caught fish for dinner? 
Our recommended Catch & Cook restaurant, Tha Fish , is conveniently located at The Pier Shopping Centre, just a short walk from Cairns Marlin Marina. 
Tha Fish can professionally prepare and cook your fresh catch, allowing you to enjoy the fish you’ve caught that very day! 
We recommend contacting Tha Fish directly to arrange your Catch & Cook experience and make a reservation at least 48 hours in advance , particularly during peak and holiday periods. 
Tha Fish Restaurant 
Shop G7b, The Pier Shopping Centre, Cairns 
Phone: (07) 4041 5350 
Website: https://thafish.com.au 
Please note: This itinerary is a guide only. Fishing locations, travel times and the daily schedule may vary depending on weather, sea conditions and the skipper’s discretion. 
What to bring
• Hat
• Sunglasses
• Sunscreen
• Camera
• Towel (if snorkel option selected)
• Cash for beer, soft drinks or merchandise
• BYO Drinks Welcome – Guests are welcome to bring their own drinks onboard. If bringing alcohol, please ensure it is mid-strength, in cans only. No glass bottles are permitted. 
• Swimmers and towel for snorkeling option
• Happy Vibes!
Fish species
There’s an incredible variety of fish waiting to be caught on the Great Barrier Reef! Some of our most commonly targeted species include: 
• Coral Trout
• Large & Small Mouth Nannygai
• Red Emperor
• Spanish Mackerel
• Trevally
With such a diverse range of species inhabiting the reef, you never know what you might hook! 
Optional extras
• Fish & Snorkel (+ $375 per vessel)
You can add a snorkel option to your charter. It’s a great way to mix up your day and is really the ultimate Great Barrier Reef day out. We suggest you fish in the morning till around 11am then we move into the shallows next to one of the sand cays and you can swim/snorkel in the crystal clear waters. After lunch you have the option of having another swim/snorkel or return to the fishing grounds for another fish.
• Snorkel Only + $375 per vessel Snorkeling only charters are enjoyed at Michaelmas Cay, a picturesque sandy cay on the Great Barrier Reef, known for its clear waters, vibrant marine life and bird sanctuary. 
• Standard Seafood Upgrade $34 per person – Make your day on the Great Barrier Reef extra special with a delicious selection of chilled local tiger prawns with cocktail sauce & cooked Morton Bay Bugs. 
• Deluxe Seafood Upgrade $40 per person – Make your day on the Great Barrier Reef extra special with a delicious selection of Chilled local tiger prawns with cocktail sauce, smoked salmon, pacific oysters with citrus & cooked Morton Bay bugs. 
• Other menu upgrade options also available, contact us if you’d like more information
