St Helens Region
Page Content
​Where to fish
Popular fishing spots
St Helens Wharf, Beauty Bay, Kirwans Beach, Talbot Street, Cunninghams Jetty, Stieglitz Jetty, Akaroa, Burns Bay, Maurouard Beach, Dora Point and Binalong Bay.
What can I catch from shore?
• Sand flathead, bream, silver trevally, garfish, snapper and King George whiting. 
Local fishing facts
• A recreational-only scalefishing area, Georges Bay holds a wide diversity of species including silver trevally, Australian salmon, King George whiting, bream and garfish. 
• From a boat, you can also catch calamari and Australian salmon. Offshore species include tiger flathead, striped trumpeter, morwong, tuna, blue-eye trevalla and mako shark. 
• The offshore waters of St Helens offer both deepsea and gamefishing action. Fishing charters are available for both inside Georges Bay and offshore. 
• Georges Bay is a 
Shark Refuge Area . No taking of sharks, skates or rays other than elephantfish. 
Netting is also prohibited in the Bay.
