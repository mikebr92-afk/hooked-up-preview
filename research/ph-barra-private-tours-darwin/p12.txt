New Caledonia 7 Days / 6 Nights Private Fishing Charter
Five days chasing GT and dogtooth tuna through New Caledonia's untouched lagoon, on a boat that's yours alone.
New Caledonia Fishing Safari: 6 nights, 5 days of fishing, small-group charter with a maximum of 6 anglers. This New Caledonia fishing safari puts you on some of the Pacific's least-pressured fishing grounds, chasing GT on the popper, dogtooth tuna on the jig, and reef species most anglers only read about. Book the boat privately with your own group, or join a share charter — either way, numbers stay small. Between fishing days, you're based in Nouméa's best beachside pocket and a remote lodge in the wild Grand Sud.With Floran as your local guide — raised in New Caledonia, as much at home on these waters as he is in Darwin — there's no language barrier and no guesswork. He fishes alongside you every day and handles the rest: accommodation, meals, transfers, airport pickup and drop-off, all covered under one price and one point of contact. Fully hosted, fully insured, from the moment you land to the moment you fly home.

Fishing an untouched, UNESCO-listed lagoon
In fact, New Caledonia’s lagoon is one of the largest and healthiest reef systems on earth. Inside the barrier reef, the water is generally calm and protected. Outside it, however, the drop-offs fall away into deep blue water.
As a result, you can pop the shallows for GTs in the morning and be jigging for dogtooth or trolling blue water by the afternoon.
Target species
Giant trevally Dogtooth tuna Mahi mahi Wahoo Black marlin Coral trout Reef species 
Techniques
• Popping for GTs on the reef edges and shallows
• Jigging for dogtooth along the deep drop-offs
• Trolling blue water for pelagics
• Bottom fishing for coral trout and reef species
Above all, every day is planned around your group. Floran reads the tides and weather first, then picks the grounds and style that suit you best.

Beachfront in Nouméa, a lodge in the Grand Sud
Anse Vata, Nouméa · 4 nights
A beachfront hotel on Nouméa’s best bay. Cafés, restaurants and shops are a short walk away, and so is the marina, so there’s no early-morning drive to the boat.
The Grand Sud · 2 nights
A luxury beachfront lodge in New Caledonia’s remote south, surrounded by mangrove estuaries, red earth and quiet beaches. Because of that, it’s the ideal base for the most untouched fishing of the trip.
Rooms
Prices are twin share, two guests to a room. If you’re travelling solo or with an odd number, let us know when you enquire and we’ll work out the best rooming for you.

Room for everyone to fish. 
Anchored up in the lagoon. 
Quick between grounds, comfortable on long days
You’ll fish from an 11-metre aluminium centre console with 500 horsepower on the back. It covers ground quickly, so you spend more time fishing and less time travelling.
The open deck front and back gives all six anglers room to cast and jig together. There’s also a toilet on board for long days out.
Meet Floran
Because Floran was raised in New Caledonia, he knows these waters well. He speaks English and French, fishes alongside you each day, and handles the local details so you can relax.
