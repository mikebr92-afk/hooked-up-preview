Go with the Flo
Private fishing in the Top End, a marina base to come home to, and a Pacific adventure with a guide who grew up there.
Barra Private Tours is a private fishing charter based in Darwin. We run half-day, full-day and multi-day trips for families, groups and corporate guests, plus a comfortable marina stay at Bayview. For something bigger, Captain Flo takes small groups to New Caledonia, where he grew up.
Every trip is planned around you, the tides and the season.

Welcome to Barra Private Tours
Discover exceptional fishing experiences in the Northern Territory and beyond.
At Barra Private Tours, we specialise in private fishing charters in Darwin, complemented by a comfortable marina-side stay at our Darwin Bayview base. For those seeking a more adventurous escape, we also offer exclusive guided fishing holidays in New Caledonia, led by Captain Flo.
Discover the Top End
Your boat, your day, your fishing adventure.
Our Darwin fishing charters are tailored around the tides, seasons and your fishing goals. From chasing barramundi in the estuaries to exploring the coastal reefs, we adapt each trip to give you the best possible experience.
Whether you’re joining us for a single day or an extended fishing getaway, our Bayview marina accommodation provides a relaxing place to unwind between adventures.
Explore New Caledonia
Take your fishing adventure further with Captain Flo in the spectacular waters of New Caledonia.
Born and raised in New Caledonia, Flo brings an intimate understanding of its lagoons, reefs and offshore waters. Join a small group expedition targeting giant trevally (GTs), dogtooth tuna and other exciting pelagic species in some of the South Pacific’s most remarkable fishing grounds.
Your Experience, Our Passion
Whether you’re travelling with family, organising a corporate getaway or sharing an adventure with a few fishing mates, every trip is thoughtfully planned around your group.
With years of local fishing knowledge, quality equipment, personalised guiding and a freshly prepared lunch on board, we take care of the details so you can focus on what matters most.
Small groups. Private experiences. Unforgettable fishing adventures.
We look forward to welcoming you aboard.

Northern Territory Fishing Day Trip
Half day or full day · Darwin Harbour · From $1,200 per boat
Your boat, your guide, your day
Book the whole boat for up to four guests. We plan around the tides, chasing barramundi when they’re on and switching to reef fishing when they’re not.

Northern Territory Fishing Multiday Trip
2 to 5 days · May to December
Fish by day, unwind by the marina at night
Two days, two fishing grounds and a comfortable marina base in Darwin. Extend to five days with two nights at Dundee Beach for river barra, reef fishing and crabbing.
+ See more 
Darwin Bayview Marina Base
Marina views · Pool · Sleeps 6
A fishing-friendly home on the water
Wake up to boats on the marina, cool off in the pool and cook your catch on the BBQ. A clean, spacious base for families, groups of mates and couples.
+ See more 
New Caledonia Multiday Fishing Holiday
7 days · Max 6 anglers · From $5,900 per person
Fish an untouched Pacific lagoon
Five days chasing GTs, dogtooth and pelagics across one of the world’s largest lagoons. Beachfront stays, meals and transfers are all organised by Floran, who grew up there.
