About Us
Barra Private Tours, based in Darwin, Northern Territory, Australia, specializes in tailored private charters for individuals and corporate groups. Led by our experienced captain, a long-time fishing enthusiast, we are dedicated to providing the ultimate fishing experience on the water.
Our Mission :
We strive to help clients catch their own fish while sharing our passion for fishing, guiding with dedication, and creating unforgettable moments that make every catch special. Witnessing clients land their first barramundi and sharing in their joy and excitement is what drives us.
Our 6.1m Australian Master Marine vessel, equipped with a 250 HP outboard engine and state-of-the-art navigation tools, including LiveScope for detecting fish movement, ensures an exceptional fishing adventure along the region’s rivers and coastal creeks. Accommodating up to four passengers, our boat offers a comfortable and memorable experience.
Whether you’re a beginner or an advanced angler, our skilled skipper will share expert techniques, find prime fishing spots, and work alongside you to catch your dream fish. Barramundi, a prized species known for its size, fighting spirit, and flavor, can reach over 100 cm and live up to 20 years. Catching one is a truly thrilling experience.
Bring along your sunscreen, hat, and sunglasses—and feel free to pack your own beer! Your guide will clean and fillet your catch, so you can take home a delicious memento of your adventure.
Our experienced guides ensure seamless communication in multiple languages, including English, French, and Chinese, guaranteeing a truly enjoyable fishing experience for all.
Join us for a personalized, one-of-a-kind fishing trip with Barra Private Tours. Book your adventure today—you won’t be disappointed!
Boat
AMM Pro Guide – 6.1 m powered by a 250 HP outboard engine.
Feature swivel seats, collapsible shades, Lowrance fish finders/sounders, GPS equipment, rod holders, forward casting platforms, first-aid kits, EPIRB, eskies, and refreshments.
Vehicle
Air-conditioned late model 4WD vehicles: Sahara | Land Cruiser , which are registered as tourist vehicles and are inspected and maintained in accordance with all official standards. This will be your transport to and from the airport or your accommodation depending on the tour.
Whether you’re a lure fisherman, or eco-adventurer, we’ve got you covered, and all fishing gear is provided.
If you have any special requirements, go to our contact page to address your proposal or call us.
