The Vessel
The Vessel Moreton Island Fishing Charters 2026-01-20T09:46:12+10:00 
PRESTIGE , is a state-of-the-art, full walk around, 10m Ocean Prowler alloy plate boat.
Custom built locally by well known boat builder Riptide, Prestige is powered by twin 300 HP Yamaha four stroke outboard engines cruising at 25Kts with a top speed of 40Kts. This means you get a quiet, comfortable and high speed trip to the fishing grounds which means you have more fishing time.
This is the fastest boat in the fleet
Prestige is capable of taking 12 people with 2 crew offshore fishing, but our preferred number is up to 8.
​
​Prestige is equipped with a full size toilet on board, refrigerator and sink with fresh water.
Prestige is fully insured with and complies with all Marine Safety Rules for Commercial Vessels.
​
The owner and other skippers are certified Coxswains or Master 4 with over 50 years experience between them.
Contact Us
Whether you’re a first time fisherman or a seasoned angler Moreton Island Fishing Charters’ Captain and deckhand are there to help you have the best fishing experience.
