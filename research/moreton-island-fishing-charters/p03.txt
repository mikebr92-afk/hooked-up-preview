Offshore Fishing Charters:
Reef Fishing:
There are many highly productive reefs, shoals and wrecks around the Cape Moreton area. Point Lookout reefs, 29’s, 33’s, Deep Tempest, Hutchinsons Shoals, Flinders Reef, Brennans and Roberts Shoals, Shallow Tempest, Caloundra Wide are some of the highly productive reefs within our fishing areas along with other known wrecks and bombies plus a number of good old fashioned “secret spots” known only to the MIFC crew.
Target species: Snapper, Pearlies, Sweetlip are predominant with Parrot, Wrasse, Cod and numerous reef dwellers always on the menu. Yellow Kings, Jew and Cobia are often found in abundance in season. You catch it – you eat it! Queensland size and bag limit rules apply.

Frequently asked Questions
How much is the charter? 
The charter cost $350 per person. Private hire for up to 8 people for $2800. Private hire for up to 10 people $3500. Please call for other options.
Where does the boat leave from? 
The boat leaves from Manly south boat ramp, Manly Harbour
How long is the charter? 
The charter leaves at 5am and returns at 1pm. (4:30 – 12:30 summer months)
What is included? 
All fishing gear, Bait, Soft drinks, Water, Crew (You can bring alcoholic drinks but they must be cans due to safety reasons)
Can I bring my own fishing equipment? 
Yes, you can bring your own fishing equipment
What can we expect to catch? 
A wide array of fish such as Snapper, Pearl Perch, Moses Perch, Parrot fish, Cod, Wrasse, Kingfish, Samson, Amberjack, Mackerel & Mahi Mahi (in season). If you have your line in the water the options are endless.
Can we bring our catch home? 
Yes you can bring what you catch home. Please bring an esky or ice to take your fish home. We ask that you leave these in your car until the end of the day due to space on the boat.
Do the fish get filleted? 
No, due to health regulations we are not able to ‘Process Seafood’ on board the vessel.
What happens if the weather is bad? 
The weather is continually monitored. A decision will be made approx 12hrs prior to your charter, a confirmation text is sent at 1630 the afternoon before. If cancelled you can choose to be re-scheduled.
Cancellation Policy 
Cancellation-Fourteen days’ notice must be given prior to your booking of your charter.
Cancellation less than 10 days for ANY REASON your deposit will be forfeited, and you may be eligible to receive a refund of up to 50%.
Cancellation 2 days before or a no show on the day will forfeit the full amount.
