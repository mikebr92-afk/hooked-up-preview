Description
Gift vouchers at $350 per person for a full day offshore fishing charter or purchase a voucher for monetary value of $50 or $100 to gift towards a full day charter.
Everything is included: All fishing gear, bait, tackle, water and soft drinks. With highly experienced Captain and Deckhand to help you out.
​Take home all the fish you catch. Just turn up and have a great day fishing.
​
We cater to single passengers and small groups, where you can join other passengers on the charter or we also offer the option to book the whole boat privately.
Voucher Terms: Vouchers purchased after 1st December 2021 are valid for 1 year from date of purchase, same booking and cancellation policies apply ​and voucher will be forfeited, vouchers are not redeemable for cash at any time.
