About Us
Moreton Island Fishing Charters is a privately owned and operated deep sea fishing charter business based at Manly, Brisbane, Queensland.
We offer a full day offshore fishing trip with everything included. Either in a mixed group or private full boat hire. Just step onboard with your hat to enjoy waters and soft drink, all tackle/gear, bait, captain and deckhand for an awesome day on the water!
Book Now 
Moreton Bay Fishing Charters also offer
• Family excursions
• Tangalooma Wrecks
• Mixed group charters
• Private charters
• Team building
• Island transfer transport
• Commercial work
• Private hire for special occasions e.g. Proposals, spreading ashes etc
• News reporting
• Research operations
• Company and business team events
• Moreton Island Camping transfers
