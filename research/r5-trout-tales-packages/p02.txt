Your trip overview
Picking up from either Hobart or Miena, we are able to access the best fly fishing locations in Tasmania including one of the many Central Highland lakes, our exclusively accessed private waters, and countless Derwent and Meander Valley streams.
A day with one of our highly experienced guides is a great way to get a taste of Tasmania's world renowned trout fishery, improve your fly fishing skills, or give fly fishing a go for the first time.
Package CostS:
Single angler - $980 
Two or more – $650pp
What's included in your trip?
• Transport to and from the Hobart or Miena areas;
• The use of premium quality Stalker rods, reels, breathable waders, boots, locally tied flies and all other gear required for your trip;
• The use of 4wd vehicles and custom built fly fishing boats to access unique locations and give you the best possible chance at that elusive brown trout;
• Access to private fishing locations, providing a range of exclusive fly fishing opportunities on lakes and rivers, simply not accessible to the general public;
• Chef prepared lunches and refreshments featuring local Tasmanian produce;
• Experienced, fully accredited and insured TGALT fly fishing guides;
your fishing itinerary
• Pick up from your accommodation in Hobart or Miena and travel to one of our many stunning trout fishing locations.
• Arrive at our chosen lake or river and get straight into the mornings fishing action - all gear provided
• Stop to enjoy and waterside Chef prepared lunch and refuel for the afternoon's fishing adventures. 
• Return to Hobart or Miena late afternoon to unwind and share the day's stories - hopefully of the one that didn't get away!

Fishing locations
Penstock 
Needing no introduction, fly fishing at Penstock Lagoon is ever common among anglers in Tasmania - known for both its wet and dry fly options.
Learn more about Penstock 
Little Pine 
Little Pine Lagoon is a fly-fishing destination nestled in the scenic Central Highlands of Tasmania, approximately two hours from both Hobart and Launceston.
Learn more about Little Pine 
28 Gates 
A private Derwent Valley fishery around an hour from Hobart, open year-round with no licence required and reliable dry fly fishing for all experience levels.
Herne Lagoon 
A secluded, privately accessed Central Highlands lake holding wild brown trout, with excellent wet fly, margin and dry fly fishing through the season.
Tyenna River 
A productive lowland river near Hobart, offering classic stream fishing for wild brown trout in beautiful surrounds.

frequently asked questions 
What’s the best way to experience trout fishing in Tasmania?
Booking an experienced and accredited guide such as Trout Tales , can greatly increase the enjoyment and success of your fly fishing journey in Tasmania . 
With so many trout fishing destinations, it can often be difficult for anglers new to Tasmania to decipher where is best at different times of the year.
A trout fishing guide can assist you with the right location, gear selection and weather/water conditions to look for to successfully fly fish in Tasmania.
What do I need to bring on my trout fly fishing tour?
Here are some handy items and attitudes to bring along to your trip with Trout Tales Tasmania:
• A desire to experience the Tasmanian wilderness;
• A current Tasmanian angling license (can be arranged on the day);
• Personal fly-fishing equipment (optional);
• Warm clothes, hat and polarised sunglasses.
Learn more about what to bring and what's provided on our Tasmanian trout fly fishing packages page. 
Where are the best fishing spots in Tasmania’s Central Highlands?
The Central Highlands of Tasmania is home to Tasmania’s famous trout fishing lakes and boasts some of the best trout fly fishing found anywhere in the world.
Lakes and lagoons recognised by anglers worldwide such as Great Lake, Arthur’s Lake, Little Pine Lagoon, Penstock Lagoon, Woods Lake and Nineteen Lagoons all hold fantastic fly fishing options. As the host to the 2019 World Fly Fishing Championships, the Central Highlands of Tasmania is on the bucket list for many fly fisherman.
You can read about these trout fisheries in detail in our blog post Trout Fishing Locations near Central Highlands. 
‍
Where are the best fishing spots near Hobart?
Hobart, being the capital of Tasmania, is a popular spot for many travelling trout fishers. Areas such as the Derwent Valley region, the Derwent River system and the Coal Valley region are popular destinations for trout fly fishers. You can check our our dedicated blog post for more of the best fishing spots around Hobart .
