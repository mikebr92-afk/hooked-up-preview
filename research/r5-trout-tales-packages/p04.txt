Two Days, One Night Fly Fishing Tour of Tasmania
Our two day, one night trout fly fishing tours provide the perfect opportunity to escape the city for an overnight trip to the stunning Central Highlands of Tasmania.
book now learn more 
5-star average Google review | Trusted by 300+ Anglers
Your trip overview
With a diverse range of lakes, lagoons, rivers and streams at your fingertips, two days amongst the Tasmanian wilderness will have you experiencing the world class trout fly fishing that this area is famous for.
Stunning lodge style accommodation overlooking the vast expanses of Great Lake, including comfortable rooms, evening meals and beverages featuring local produce next to open log fires and some great hospitality to prepare you for more exciting fly fishing action.
Package CostS:
Single angler – $2,450 
Two or more – $1,600pp
What's included in your trip?
• Transport to and from the Hobart or Miena areas;
• The use of premium quality Stalker rods, reels, breathable waders, boots, locally tied flies and all other gear required for your trip;
• The use of 4wd vehicles and custom built fly fishing boats to access unique locations and give you the best possible chance at that elusive brown trout;
• Access to private fishing locations, providing a range of exclusive fly fishing opportunities on lakes and rivers, simply not accessible to the general public;
• Chef prepared lunches and refreshments featuring local Tasmanian produce;
• Experienced, fully accredited and insured TGALT fly fishing guides;
• Lodge or cabin style accommodation including breakfast, evening meals and drinks next to open log fires.
your fishing itinerary
Day 1 
• Pick up from your accommodation in Hobart or Miena and travel to one of our many trout fishing locations.
• Arrive at our chosen lake or river and get straight into the mornings fishing action - all gear provided. 
• Stop to enjoy a waterside Chef prepared lunch and refuel for the afternoon's fishing adventures. 
• Check In to your Miena accommodation at the end of the day’s fishing for a warm shower, hearty meal and evening drinks next to open log fires.
Day 2 
• Pick up from your Miena accommodation at 8.30am to fish another lake or river, exploring different areas and techniques along the way.
• Return back to Hobart or Miena with your guide at the end of the day’s fishing to reminisce on an unforgettable two days fly fishing in Tasmania's rugged wilderness.
Visit Great Lake Hotel or Central Highlands Lodge to know more about accomodation

frequently asked questions

What’s unique about Trout Tales Tasmania?
Trout Tales Tasmania provides single and multi-day fly fishing tours around Tasmania , with our guides sharing over 50 years of experience fly fishing in Tasmania. 
With Trout Tales owner and guide Matt being a qualified chef, gourmet Tasmanian cuisine is incorporated into all our packages. We also work with local accommodation providers to develop all-inclusive fly fishing packages . 
What fly fishing gear do I need to trout fish in Tasmania?
With the diversity of Tasmania’s trout fishery, there are a few essential pieces of equipment needed to maximise your chances of success when fly fishing in Tasmania.
Generally 5-6wt rods are suitable, along with floating and sinking fly lines, a range of dry and wet flies and other equipment such as a good pair of waders and polarized sunglasses for spotting trout. 
If you're interested in learning more about some of the ins and outs of trout fly fishing across Tasmania , check out the Trout Tales blog .
Where are the best fishing spots near Launceston? 
Launceston is situated amongst some major river systems which hold some fantastic trout fly fishing opportunities. 
Rivers and streams such as the Mersey River, Meander River, Brumby’s Creek, South Esk River and the Macquarie River all provide great fly fishing prospects throughout the trout fishing season.
You can read about these trout fisheries in detail in our blog post Trout Fishing Locations near Launceston .
