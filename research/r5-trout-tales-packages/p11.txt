# Trout Tales Tasmania
> Trout Tales Tasmania is an all-inclusive guided fly fishing tour operator based in Hobart, Tasmania, Australia. Founded and led by Matt Stone — a TGALT-accredited guide with 20+ years of fly fishing experience and a professional chef — the business offers world-class fly fishing experiences across Tasmania's lakes, rivers, streams, and private fisheries for anglers of all skill levels.
All tour packages include expert guides, premium Stalker fly fishing gear, 4WD transport, custom-built fishing boats, access to private properties, and chef-prepared gourmet meals featuring local Tasmanian produce. Multi-day packages include lodge or cabin accommodation with breakfast, evening meals, beverages, and open log fires. Trout Tales Tasmania also operates an online fly fishing shop, runs fly fishing workshops, and produces educational video content and a Patreon series.
