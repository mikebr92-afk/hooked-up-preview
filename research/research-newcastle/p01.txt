Private Fishing Charters in Newcastle, Swansea, and Lake Macquarie
Looking to set sail on a peaceful angling experience in NSW? At Day Trippin Charter, we’re proud to offer our small-group fishing charters in Newcastle, Swansea, and Lake Macquarie. Whether you're a seasoned angler seeking an adrenaline-fuelled adventure or a novice looking to reel in your first catch, we cater to all skill levels and aspirations.
With years of fishing expertise and a deep understanding of the local waters, our seasoned skipper Captain Cooper brings a wealth of knowledge to your fishing adventure. His extensive experience allows him to identify prime fishing spots in Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, and Swansea, ensuring you have the best chance of making a memorable catch.

Why Choose Our Private Fishing Charters?
At Day Trippin Charters, our team is passionate about providing you with an immersive escapade that goes beyond fishing. With our private boat hire in Lake Macquarie and nearby areas, we endeavour to help you discover the beauty of the local waters and share unforgettable moments with your friends and family.
• 
Privacy and Exclusivity
By limiting the number of participants to just four, we can ensure privacy and a more individualised experience for everyone on board. This exclusivity allows for a more relaxed and enjoyable fishing trip, with ample space for you and your group to move around freely and focus on landing your next big catch.
• 
Access to Prime Fishing Location
With our private fishing charters, you'll have access to prime fishing locations that may be off-limits or harder to reach on public trips. Whether you're interested in offshore deep-sea fishing in Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, or Swansea, we can get you there and maximise your chances of reeling in a trophy fish.
• 
High-Quality Equipment and Gear
We understand the importance of using top-quality fishing equipment and gear for a successful fishing trip. That’s why our boat, Trippin, is equipped with state-of-the-art rods, reels, tackle, and all types of bait to provide you with the best tools for a productive and enjoyable time on the water.
• 
Customisable Trip Duration and Itinerary
At Day Trippin Charters, we're all about giving you options and keeping things flexible. Whether you prefer a half-day or full-day fishing trip, we can accommodate your needs. Additionally, we can work with you to create a customised itinerary that aligns with your desired fishing targets, preferences, and time constraints.
