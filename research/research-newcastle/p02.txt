B and L Fishing and Cruises PTY LTD
Established 1996. Owner operated by Brett Corbett and Brad Minors, both Master 5 mariners. Our decks are run by fully licenced contract deckhands. Your safety (and ours) are paramount . Our local knowledge and experience deliver fish with safety. 
We work 7 days a week from Swansea and service Newcastle, Lake Macquarie and the Central Coast. Our deep sea fishing charters cater for large and small groups, single anglers and couples. We have Bucks Party specials and discounts.We offer whale watching cruises (in season). Social club competitions, contract boat hire,etc .
