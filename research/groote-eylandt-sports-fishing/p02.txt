Experience a place like no other 
Groote Eylandt Lodge offers a unique blend of luxury and natural beauty, where stunning ocean views meet immersive cultural experiences. Guests can enjoy world-class fishing tours or explore the Eylandt’s ancient heritage while staying in tranquil and thoughtfully designed accommodation. 
• 
Experience world-class fishing in pristine waters brimming with diverse marine life. 
• 
Discover the Eylandt's heritage through engaging tours exploring ancient cave paintings, local art and breathtaking landscapes. 
• 
Enjoy luxurious accommodation that blends comfort with natural beauty. 
• 
Savour fresh, locally inspired cuisine and enjoy tropical cocktails with stunning ocean views.

Explore Experiences 
Explore the Eylandt's way of life
Life on Groote Eylandt is shaped by a harmonious connection with nature and deep respect for Indigenous culture. Enjoy a relaxed atmosphere where tradition and the beauty of the land guide daily rhythms. From sunrise to sunset, there is always something magical about Groote.
Accommodation 
View our range of bungalows and suites with stunning ocean views. Each room is thoughtfully designed with modern amenities, private balconies, and lush surroundings to enhance your tropical getaway.
Private Tours & Fishing Charters

Seagrass Restaurant & Bar 
With its ocean-view dining, observation deck and pool area, the Seagrass Restaurant and Bar is the ideal place to relax and unwind.
