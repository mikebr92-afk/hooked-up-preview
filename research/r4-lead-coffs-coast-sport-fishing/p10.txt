VISITOR INFORMATION OUTLETS 
The Coffs Coast has seven Visitor Information Outlets (see list below map) across the region where you can connect face-to-face, pick up some brochures, and find local recommendations.
You will also find more than 70 visitor information brochure racks at all major attractions (including The Big Banana, Botanic Gardens and Butterfly House), major resorts, holiday parks, shopping centres, and many accommodation providers throughout the Coffs Coast.

VISITOR INFORMATION 
The Coffs Coast has seven Visitor Information Outlets across the region, giving visitors the chance to connect face-to-face, pick up brochures and discover local recommendations from people who know the area best. Whether you are planning your stay, looking for things to do or need help while you are on the road, friendly local advice is never far away.
Use the interactive map below to find Visitor Information Outlets, Digital Visitor Information Kiosks, long vehicle parking and EV charging stations located close to the highway across the Coffs Coast. It is designed to help you plan your stop, access useful travel information and make your journey through the region easier.
