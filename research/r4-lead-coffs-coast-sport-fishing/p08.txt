Coffs Coast Sport Fishing Adventures
• 
• 
• 
Enjoy a day out fishing the picturesque coffs coastline or estuary systems with coffs coast sportfishing.Specialising in bluewater game and sportfishing,guided estuary fishing and eco tours.
FULL DAY BLUEWATER – Black N Blue Sole charter only-$1800 inc gst (approx 10 hours) Max 6 anglers Fish the awesome waters of the Coffs coastline with a choice of: HEAVY TACKLE GAMEFISHING: Targeting Blue, Black and Striped marlin, Yellowfin LIGHT TACKLE GAME AND SPORT FISHING: Targeting Marlin, Tuna, Wahoo, Mackerel, Dolphin fish and Cobia DEEP WATER JIGGING AND REEF FISHING: Targeting Kingfish, Samsomfish, Amberjack, Snapper, Pearl Perch Charters depart coffs harbor international marina. All tackle provided All food and soft drinks provided Please bring hat and sunscreen No fishing licence required
To view visit http://www.coffscoastsportfishing.com.au
