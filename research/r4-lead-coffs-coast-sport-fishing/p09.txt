Plan your trip to the Coffs Coast | Nature, adventure and unforgettable escapes in NSW
Whether you’re chasing rainforest adventures, laidback coastal walks, thrill-seeking adventure activities, sea-to-service dining or an unforgettable family getaway, the Coffs Coast has it all – just a short flight or road trip from Sydney or Brisbane. Use this guide to plan your trip, discover the best things to do in Coffs Harbour and beyond, and start your journey in one of Australia’s most naturally diverse holiday destinations.
Nestled on beautiful Gumbaynggirr Country, the Coffs Coast is a rare gem on the Mid North Coast of New South Wales. With its vibrant beach towns, ancient rainforests, profound cultural experiences and eco-friendly adventures, this is the kind of place where every type of traveller can find their flow.
As New South Wales’ first certified ECO Destination, the region is rich with nature-based experiences. Think hiking through World Heritage-listed Gondwana rainforests, diving alongside schools of grey nurse sharks in the Solitary Islands Marine Park, discovering hidden hinterland swimming holes, or spotting migrating whales from a soaring headland.
And the food? From paddock-to-plate dining to waterfront cocktails and vibrant farmers markets, the Coffs Coast food scene is fresh, distinct and full of surprises. Pair that with an impressive variety of places to stay – from luxury eco-lodges and self-contained city apartments to beachside cabins and family-friendly holiday parks – and you’ve got everything you need for a trip to remember.
So whether you’re a nature lover, adventure seeker, a slow traveller or a family planning your next coastal holiday, the Coffs Coast is a destination that welcomes all.

Getting To Coffs Harbour and Getting Around 
Coffs Harbour is easily accessible whether you’re flying, driving or arriving by train.
The regional airport offers direct flights from Sydney, Brisbane and Melbourne, with car hire available on site. If you’re hitting the road, it’s around five and a half hours north of Sydney or four and a half hours south of Brisbane – perfect for a coastal road trip. Daily train services also run through Coffs Harbour on the Sydney–Brisbane line, making it easy to enjoy the scenery along the way.
For travellers with electric vehicles, charging stations are available throughout the region , including in the Coffs Harbour CBD, with more locations being added regularly.
Once you arrive, exploring is easy. You’ll find a walkable city centre and seaside villages, coastal cycleways, and scenic drives that take you from the beach to the hinterland in under an hour. You’ll also find taxis, local buses, tours and rideshares.
Get Local Tips and Convenient Advice 
Want help planning your perfect holiday while you’re here? Drop into one of our friendly Visitor Information Centres in Coffs Harbour, Sawtell, Woolgoolga, Ulong or Nanaglen. The team can help with local insights, brochures, maps and more. You’ll also find digital info kiosks in key locations, which are an easy and accessible way to find nearby things to do
Travelling With A Caravan Or RV? You’re In The Right Place 
The Coffs Coast is caravan and motorhome friendly, with plenty of well-equipped parks and camping options close to beaches, rivers and hinterland towns. Many local holiday parks offer everything from powered sites and dump points to barbecues, camp kitchens and beach access, while state forests and showgrounds offer low-cost, nature-based camping for more independent travellers.
Stay Your Way With Ample Accommodation Options 
No matter how you like to travel, you’ll find the perfect place to stay on the Coffs Coast. From eco-accredited retreats and beachfront resorts to pet-friendly cabins and holiday houses, you’re spoilt for choice.So whether you’re looking for luxury stays, family accommodation, budget-friendly options, or somewhere to truly switch off, there’s a stay that fits your style.
Take The Hassle Out Of Travel With Local-Led Coffs Coast Itineraries 
Discover the best local secrets and plan your stay with ease with our ready-made Coffs Coast itineraries. From romantic three-day escapes for active couples , to luxury escapes with helicopter charters and fine dining , and four-day family-friendly holidays , you’ll find an adventure to suit you – or a brilliant jumping off point for planning your custom experience
Be Inspired By The Best Of The Coffs Coast With Our Blog 
Looking for holiday inspiration or insider tips for your trip to Coffs Harbour, Sawtell, Woolgoolga and beyond? Our blog is packed with local knowledge to help you uncover the region’s best-kept secrets – from epic nature walks and whale watching spots to hidden swimming holes, fresh food experiences and family-friendly adventures. So whether you’re planning a weekend escape to Coffs Harbour or dreaming up your next summer school holiday trip, let our stories spark your next great adventure..
