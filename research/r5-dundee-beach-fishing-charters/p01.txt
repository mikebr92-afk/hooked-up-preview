INCREDIBLE FISHING 
BARRA FISHING 
Barramundi fishing charters are a speciality at Dundee Beach. Successful barra fishing is based on having the right tidal conditions - we can help plan your trip to coincide with the best tides throughout the year for targeting saltwater barramundi. 
​ 
With 25 years' experience guiding from Dundee Beach you can be assured to catch lots of fish! 
REEF FISHING 
Our reef fishing charters are so much better than the average Darwin fishing charters - we specialise in small groups to ensure everyone has the best chance of catching the big fish! 
​ 
There's a huge array of fish species to choose from such as Jewfish, Golden Snapper and Cod on the inshore reefs. Further off-shore, we target Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Bluebone, Nanagai and Tricky Snapper. 
sports FISHING 
Fishing inshore reefs as well as grounds wide of Dundee to the Perron Islands, where there's a large array of fish species to choose from such as Queenfish, GT's, Mackerel, Tuna and Sailfish. 
Our team of professional fishing guides have expert local knowledge of the area and will offer the best advice on the fishing options to ensure you have a successful day, using techniques from casting, live bait to jigging.
