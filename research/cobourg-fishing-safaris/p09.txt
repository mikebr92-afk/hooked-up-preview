Tourism Awards
Our Award-Winning Experiences & Guides
Venture North is recognised as a national leader in cultural tourism, with a long history of industry recognition at both Territory and national level. Having won eleven NT Brolga Tourism Awards in Heritage and Cultural Tourism, along with three Gold Australian Tourism Awards , our achievements reflect decades of commitment to delivering authentic, respectful and meaningful cultural experiences. 
In 2018, Venture North was awarded the prestigious Chairman’s Choice for Tourism Excellence at the Brolga Northern Territory Tourism Awards. Unlike the standard award categories, the Chairman’s Choice recognises a tourism business for its broader commitment to excellence and contribution to the Northern Territory tourism industry.
To add to this recognition, three Venture North guides have been named the Northern Territory’s Outstanding Interpretive Guide , reflecting the exceptional knowledge, passion and storytelling they bring to every journey. Our guides are at the heart of the Venture North experience, bringing the landscapes, wildlife, culture and stories of the Top End to life in a genuine and engaging way.
Beyond our industry awards, Venture North has also been recognised by Australian Traveller, Australia’s leading travel magazine, featuring in two of their editions:
• Australia’s Hottest Travel Destinations – highlighting the Top End and East Arnhem Land, with Venture North recommended as the operator to take you there.
• The Australian Bucket List – naming Venture North’s 5 Day Kakadu, Arnhem Land & Cobourg Peninsula Safari as the No. 1 safari in Australia .
