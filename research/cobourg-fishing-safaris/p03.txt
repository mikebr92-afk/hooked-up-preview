Your exclusive fishing charter to one of Australia's best fishing destinations
The Cobourg Peninsula is one of Australia's most untouched fishing spots. Perfect for a boys trip with you and the lads or an adventurous couples getaway, our Cobourg fishing safaris offer world class fishing and nature experiences without the crowds.
Book your fishing charter!
We offer all inclusive fishing packages for between 3 to 10nights. All inclusive fishing packages start at just $4,900 per person.
Book Now 
From mangrove lined creeks and shallow sand flats, to offshore bombies and coral reefs, the marine diversity provides something for every fisherman. Catering for amateurs to the experienced, target methods include; surface fishing, sight casting, trawling, jigging and bait fishing.
We regularly catch
• Barramundi & Mangrove Jack
• Spanish mackerel, GT's, Queenfish & Long-tail Tuna
• Golden Snapper, Coral Trout & Jew Fish
• Mudcrabs & Shellfish
We take care of everything
An exclusive experience
You get exclusive access to one of Arnhem Land's best fishing spots. Escape the crowds to this unique fishing destination that's full of natural wonders. We cater for a maximum of 12 anglers between 3 boats.
Accommodation
Relax in comfort after a great days fishing at the renowned Cobourg Coastal Camp . Twin-share canvas safari tents, open-air dining and starlit monsoonal showers all add up to a unique and comfortable place to hang your hat.
Meals & drinks
All meals and non-alcoholic drinks are provided on your fishing trip. Meals are expertly prepared by our chef using fresh ingredients and, of course, the days catch. Premium cuts of red meat also feature on the menu. 
Airfares & transfers
Your all inclusive fishing package includes return charter airfares between the Cobourg Peninsula and Darwin. We also provide transfers from your Darwin hotel (or main terminal) to the charter plane. 
Experienced fishing guide
Our guides know where all the best spots are and when the fish are biting. We're here to make your fishing safari an amazing experience.
Boat & gear
Our fishing boats are 6.8m Custom Works Eliminator Pro's , custom designed for NT fishing conditions. All vessels are decked out with top of the range accessories & feature an extensive range of quality fishing gear and tackle.
