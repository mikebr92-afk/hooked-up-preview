Bespoke Safari Specialists
Boutique, micro-group safaris through Australia's Top End. Escape the crowds and see Northern Australia in style, supported by over 20 years of award-winning expertise.
5 Day Kakadu, Arnhem Land & Cobourg Peninsula Tour
This is our Signature Safari that extends beyond Kakadu, into Arnhem Land and far north to the remote Cobourg Peninsula. Experience ancient rock art with an Aboriginal guide, spectacular waterfalls, wildlife cruises, Ramsar-listed wetlands, and stay at our exclusive Cobourg Coastal Camp - a private wilderness retreat nestled within the Cobourg Marine Park.
• Per adult: $4,890.00 (twin-share)
• 6 people per tour 
• Available May through September
• Departs: Monday & Thursday

5 Day Litchfield, Katherine, Kakadu & Arnhem Land Tour
We have designed this tour to give guests an insight into the Top End's most iconic National Parks. Experience the highlights of Kakadu, Litchfield and Nitmiluk National Parks, along with a taste of Western Arnhem Land. The itinerary features spectacular waterfalls and swimming holes, ancient rock art sites with an Aboriginal guide, wildlife cruises and a breathtaking scenic flight over Kakadu. 
• Per adult: $5,690.00 (twin-share)
• 6 people per tour 
• Available May & September 
• Departs: Select Dates

4 Day Arnhem Land and Cobourg Peninsula Tour
Designed for travellers who have already explored Kakadu, this unique journey departs from Jabiru and ventures beyond the national park into the rarely visited landscapes of Western Arnhem Land and the Cobourg Peninsula. Travel off the beaten track through spectacular sandstone escarpments, discover ancient Aboriginal rock art, explore the historic ruins of Victoria Settlement and experience the pristine coastline of the Cobourg Marine Park.
• Per adult: $4,490.00 (twin-share)
• 6 people per tour
• Available May through October
• Departs: Tuesday & Friday

Cobourg Fishing Charters
Heralded as one of the best fishing destinations in Australia, the remote Cobourg Peninsula is only an hour’s flight from Darwin. Cobourg Fishing Safaris (operated by Venture North) offer fly in/fly out fishing charters based from Cobourg Coastal Camp. Catering for a maximum of just 12 fishermen at a time they offer a very exclusive fishing experience. 
• As per below
• 2-6 people per boat
• Available May through October

Kimberley Tours
Located in the far north of Western Australia, the Kimberley is one of Australia’s last great wilderness regions. Spanning more than 400,000 square kilometres, this vast landscape is home to spectacular gorges, cascading waterfalls, ancient rock art and remarkable wildlife. Explore it with Venture North on our 7 Day Kimberley Safari, a shared group adventure, or enjoy a private Kimberley tour designed around you. Our tours operate during the dry season, from May through to October.
• POA
• 6 people per tour
• Available May through October
