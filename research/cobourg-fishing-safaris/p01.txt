How do I get there?
Included in your fishing package are Darwin private transfers and return charter flights from Darwin to the Cobourg Peninsula (Cobourg Coastal Camp).
Upon arriving in Darwin you will be collected as arranged from the Darwin Airport or your Darwin hotel where you will be transferred to the General Aviation (located adjacent to Darwin Airport, this is where the small charter planes arrive and depart from).
Upon landing on the Cobourg Peninsula, staff from Cobourg Coastal Camp will collect you and transfer you to the camp.
Best time to visit?
We only operate as a designated fishing camp for the premier fishing months of the year being April, May, September, October & November. In the months of June, July and August, Cobourg Coastal Camp operates as a nature-based lodge exclusive to Venture North Safari Tours.
What’s included?
Everything is included in the price (inc. hotel transfers, return charter flights from Darwin to Cobourg, meals, accommodation, private chef, private skipper, fishing tackle etc.), the only additional option is alcohol which can be pre-purchased at bottleshop prices.
Do I need a permit to stay on the Cobourg Peninsula?
Yes, a permit is required to enter the Cobourg Peninsula (Garig Gunak Barlu National Park). Visitor numbers are restricted and permits need to be applied for well in advance. Cobourg Fishing Safaris organise permits on your behalf and this is included in your package.
Can I keep the fish I catch?
Most certainly. You have the option of keeping fish that are caught on your trip as long as it is within the NT possession limits. Our guides are more than happy to fillet, vacuum seal and freeze any fish you wish to take home. You will just need to arrange a box or bag for transportation. Be sure to check with your airline for any restrictions regarding this.
What skill level needed?
We cater for all skill levels from novice fisho’s that have never fished to the most experienced. As we operate private fishing tours only, this allows us to take all ages and abilities out on the water to guarantee every individual a tailored experience.
What to bring?
We recommend bringing a maximum of 15 kilograms of luggage/fishing equipment with you. For luggage we recommend a soft overnight carry bag as space in the charter plane is often limited. Fishing rod tubes must be no more than 2.4m in length. 
Upon confirmation you will be sent a detailed travel guide including a packing list.
We supply all the fishing gear (on a replace if lost/broken basis) but feel free to bring your favourite rods and lures along.
How far is the Cobourg Peninsula from Darwin?
The Cobourg Peninsula is approximately 570km from Darwin. If traveling from Darwin to Cobourg by road it would take about 7-8 hours. Our Fishing Packages include charter airfares between Darwin and the Cobourg Peninsula. The flight duration is approximately one hour, and Cobourg Coastal Camp is approximately 30minutes from the airstrip.
Can I be contacted in case of emergency?
There is a satellite Wi-Fi connection at Cobourg Coastal Camp that is used for emergencies only. Additional to this, each boat and vehicle is equipped with a Satellite Phones and VHF Radios.
Can I swim at Cobourg?
The Cobourg coast is a wild stretch of water where you’ll regularly see sharks and saltwater crocs. Often, right off the cliff at our camp. While the white sandy beaches and blue water is inviting, this is one place that you certainly want to stay out of the water! Swimming is not permitted at Garig Gunak Barlu National Park.
