🌊 The Blue Breeze Experience: Your Stress-Free Guide
Planning an event on the water shouldn’t feel like hard work. Whether you are stepping aboard the spacious 80ft Livewire or the sleek, high-comfort 62ft The Pelican , we’ve designed the entire experience to be completely hassle-free so you can focus entirely on your day out on the blue.
🥂 How Does BYO Food & Drinks Work?
• • 
Complete Freedom: You have full flexibility to bring your own selection of alcohol, mixers, and party platters on both vessels. We don’t charge any sneaky corkage fees.
• • 
Skip the Heavy Lifting: You don’t need to haul heavy bags of melting ice down the jetty in the Perth heat. For a flat $120 fee , we will have our massive onboard commercial eskies completely packed and pre-chilled with fresh ice before your group even arrives.
• • 
The VIP Bottle Shop Delivery: Want it even easier? You can order your drinks directly through The Carlisle Hotel bottle shop. Just tell them you’re boarding with Pelican Charters, and their team will deliver your entire order directly to our vessel eskies—icy cold and waiting for you on arrival.
🌦️ What If It Rains on the Swan River?
• • 
Completely Weather-Protected Fleet: Don’t let a shifting Perth forecast stress you out. Unlike smaller, open-air party pontoons, both Livewire and The Pelican feature fully enclosed, climate-controlled main saloons and extensive covered overhead protection.
• • 
The Party Keeps Rolling: If a quick coastal squall rolls through the river or out at Rottnest, your party simply shifts inside. The music stays up, the bars keep serving, and your guests stay in complete, weather-proof comfort.
🎵 Sound Systems & Entertainment
• • 
Plug & Play Bluetooth: Our fleet features high-fidelity marine sound systems that link instantly to your phone via Bluetooth. You control the playlist and the volume right from the palm of your hand.
• • 
Ultra-Quiet Engineering: Both vessels utilize advanced jet propulsion setups engineered to minimize engine noise. You get all the power without any deafening diesel rumbles, ensuring your music cuts through cleanly from the dance floor to the bow.
Vessel Performance & Comfort
⚙️ What defines our vessel standards? Our fleet is maintained to rigorous commercial standards. We prioritize passenger comfort through high-spec soundproofing, ultra-stable hulls for smooth cruising, and advanced navigation systems. You are stepping onto a professional-grade vessel.
📺 What amenities are onboard? Experience comfort at sea with premium amenities: wrap-around seating for maximum group interaction, high-fidelity Bluetooth sound systems, and integrated satellite TV to ensure you don't miss any major events.
⚓ Understanding your vessel Get comfortable on deck like a seasoned sailor: Bow (the front), Stern (the rear), Port (left-hand side when facing forward), and Starboard (right-hand side when facing forward).
Capacity & Specs
👥 What are the vessel capacities? We operate with professional capacity limits to ensure space and safety: Pelican accommodates up to 48 passengers, while Livewire is rated for up to 55 passengers in sheltered waters.
