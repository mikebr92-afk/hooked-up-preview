Montebello Islands Fishing Trips & Packages
Arguably some of the best fishing Australia has on offer! Enjoy world-class saltwater fishing holiday packages in Western Australia. Spend your days experiencing extreme offshore fish feeding frenzies and your nights relaxing inside a remote tropical paradise.
Request Fishing Availability ➔
The Ultimate Liveaboard North West Fishing Adventure
Our premier liveaboard fishing trips combine a high-octane angling schedule with a great, relaxing atmosphere on the open ocean.
Available strictly through the prime weather window of July to October each year, Pelican Charters delivers an unmatched deep-sea experience. Step aboard to fish hard all day, then sleep overnight inside calm, protected waters to wake up to a fresh hot breakfast, a piping hot coffee, and another action-packed day of North West fishing.
Top water fishing with stick-baits and popper fishing: GT, Spanish mackerel, wahoo, Trevally, Tuna, spangled emperor and Coral Trout are just some of the fish you can expect to see charging after your top water lures in these untouched waters.

Bottom Bouncing, Jigging & Surface Trolling
Experience world-class sport fishing and heavy demersal action in a pristine island environment.
Conquer the Montebello Fishing Grounds: 
• Heavy Pelagic Trolling: Trawl past expansive coral reefs full of thriving marine life to target premier game fish. Battle iconic ocean speedsters including Black, Blue, and Striped Marlin, acrobatic Sailfish, Wahoo, massive Yellowfin Tuna, and Dolphin Fish (Mahi-Mahi).
• Offshore Bottom Bouncing: Drop deep fresh baits along vertical reef ledges out to the deep blue sea to hook legendary, hard-fighting table fish. Our skippers will put you directly over massive structures holding prize Red Emperor and Coral Trout.
• Shallow Lure Casting & Jigging: Head off in a new direction at sunrise to flick soft plastics and heavy jigs directly into shallow sandy holes, reef channels, and coral pockets to entice hard-hitting reef predators.
Extreme 5-Day Wilderness Charters Departing From Dampier
We manage complete, high-end private group charters and saltwater holiday packages along the Pilbara Coast.
Our Operational Logistics Blueprint: 
• The Staging Route: Anglers simply fly from Perth to Karratha Airport. From there, we depart by boat directly from the Dampier Port, taking you on a scenic tour through the 42 pristine islands of the Dampier Archipelago before heading out into the deep blue.
• Dual Inflatable Tenders: We carry two specialized 5.5-meter tenders onboard Livewire. This allows our daily activities to remain completely flexible, letting us sneak right up into shallow reef zones or split groups up to keep everyone in your crew happy.
• Historical Sightseeing Excursions: Mix up your time on the rod with unique land-based sightseeing tours through the marine park, exploring remote coastal highlights and leading tourism attractions.

The Montebello Frontier
📍 Why choose the Montebellos? The Montebello Islands represent a true offshore frontier. Located significantly further north, these islands offer unparalleled seclusion and access to aggressive, high-energy pelagic species and reef fish that see very little pressure.
⚖️ What are the fishing limits and regulations? We operate with a focus on conservation to protect these remote grounds. Our vessel-specific limit is 4 fish per day, with a maximum of 15kg of demersal fillets and 5kg of pelagic fillets per guest for the total trip duration. Compliance is strictly enforced to ensure the future of this world-class fishery.
🎣 What can I expect from the fishing? Prepare for a high-octane experience. From massive Giant Trevally and Spanish Mackerel to high-quality bottom dwellers, the "Monties" are a bucket-list destination for anglers seeking serious, remote-area sport fishing.

📝 How do I secure a spot? Due to the remoteness and the nature of these expeditions, voyages are strictly limited. Contact our office directly to discuss upcoming expedition windows and cabin availability for this remote adventure.
