Frequently Asked Questions
🎶 Can we play our own playlist on the sound system? It’s the groom’s final fling, so the soundtrack is entirely up to you. Our onboard sound systems are fully Bluetooth-ready so you can easily sync your phone and pump your custom playlists. If you are hiring entertainment or a DJ for your Swan River bucks cruise, just have them touch base with our office beforehand to confirm our connection specs and power setup.
🍻 Can we bring our own alcohol and food? Yes! Our Swan River cruises offer flexible BYO options. You are more than welcome to load up on your own eskies, drinks, and a massive spread of food or snacks for the lads. We provide the massive commercial-grade eskies and fridge space on board; you provide the party. (Note: If you are organizing third-party catering or a food drop, let our crew know so we can coordinate logistics).
🧊 Can you help with ice before we board? To save you the heavy lifting and wet car boots on the day, we can arrange for ice to be pre-delivered straight to the boat for $110, provided we receive your request at least 48 hours before departure.
💃 Can we book entertainment like a life-drawing class or party host? Absolutely! We host plenty of epic bucks parties on board. You are welcome to bring along professional entertainment—such as a life-drawing model, entertainment host, or comedy crew—to give the groom an unforgettable send-off. We just ask that your crew keeps things respectful for our skipper, deckhands, and the vessel.
🚭 What is the smoking and behavior policy? Smoking is permitted only on the stern or marlin board—zero exceptions. We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy for throwing cigarette butts overboard to protect the Swan River. Keep the vibe great, respect the crew and the boat, and we’ll make sure you have an epic day out.
🎉 What are the rules for decorating the boat? We love a decked-out boat for the groom, but we need to keep the vessel in pristine condition for the next guests. You are welcome to put up banners and themed gear, provided you use non-damaging adhesive tape (no masking tape that leaves a sticky residue, please!). Strictly Prohibited: No confetti, glitter, or silly string—they are notoriously difficult to clean up and can damage the vessel’s finish or the marine environment.
⏰ What is the latest our bucks cruise can run? To comply with local Swan River navigation regulations, local noise management guidelines, and crew safety hours, evening party charters generally wrap up and conclude at the jetty by standard local curfew times (typically 10:00 PM for evening slots, though our daytime cruise slots offer plenty of flexibility).
🧹 What happens after the party finishes? You are here to give the groom a proper send-off, not scrub the decks! Once your group disembarks at Barrack Street or Elizabeth Quay, our professional crew handles all rubbish collection and general tidying to get the boat shipshape.
