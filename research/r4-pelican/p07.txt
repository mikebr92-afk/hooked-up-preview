Frequently Asked Questions
🎂 Can we decorate the boat for a birthday party? 
• 
Absolutely! You are more than welcome to bring along decorations like banners, streamers, and balloons to personalize the vessel for the birthday star. We just ask that you use non-damaging tape or clips and avoid confetti or loose glitter so we can keep our oceans and the boat clean.
🎶 Is there a sound system on board for birthday playlists? 
• 
Yes, our vessels feature a quality onboard Bluetooth sound system. You can easily connect your phone or device to stream your ultimate birthday playlist, whether you want high-energy party tracks for dancing or relaxed background tunes as you cruise.
🍹 How does the BYO drinks and food setup work? 
• 
Our charters are fully BYO, making it super easy to cater to your group’s exact tastes. Bring your favorite drinks and party food, and our crew will fire up the onboard commercial BBQs to cook your feast. We also provide ample fridge space and massive 300L ice eskies to keep everything ice-cold all day long.
🧊 Can you help with ice before we board? 
• 
To save you the heavy lifting and wet car boots on the day, we can arrange for ice to be pre-delivered straight to the boat for $110, provided we receive your request at least 48 hours before departure.
👥 What is the best group size for a birthday party cruise? 
• 
Our private charters are designed to comfortably host groups of various sizes—whether you are planning an intimate milestone gathering or a larger blowout celebration. Get in touch with our team with your estimated guest count, and we will match you with the ideal vessel for your birthday crew.
⏰ Can we extend our birthday charter if the party is going too well? 
• 
Depending on our schedule and vessel availability on the day, hourly extensions are often possible! Just chat with your skipper during the cruise if you want to keep the celebrations rolling a little longer.
🎂 Can we bring a birthday cake on board? 
• 
Definitely! No birthday is complete without cake. Feel free to bring along a celebration cake, and our crew will gladly help store it safely in the fridge until it is time to sing and serve.
