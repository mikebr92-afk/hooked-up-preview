Carnac Island Boat Hire & Private Day Cruises
Looking for a premium private island cruise without the long travel time? Discover majestic Carnac Island, a pristine marine sanctuary located just a short cruise from Fremantle. It is Perth’s ultimate playground for exclusive bucks parties, hens days, and celebrations on the water.
Picture this: Dropping anchor in a deeply sheltered bay, swimming alongside the island’s famous wild Australian sea lions, and firing up the onboard BBQ. Enjoy absolute privacy, luxury comfort, and a custom itinerary completely tailored to your group.
🏝️ Explore an Untouched Nature Reserve
While nearby Rottnest Island attracts large tourist crowds, a Carnac Island boat trip remains a serene, undisturbed escape. As a protected A-class nature reserve, this unique sanctuary is free from the hustle and bustle of mainland destinations, preserving its pristine beauty for an uncrowded experience.
Our luxury charter vessels feature shallow drafts, allowing our experienced WA skippers to safely navigate close to the island’s sheltered coves. This allows your guests to dive into calm, postcard-blue waters or stand comfortably right off our custom swimming decks.
🦭 Snorkeling with West Australian Sea Lions
The marine sanctuary surrounding the island is famous for its abundant wildlife. Immerse yourself in pristine waters to swim alongside playful local seals and witness majestic West Australian sea lions resting in their natural habitat.
The underwater reefs are highly active, offering unparalleled snorkeling and diving adventures teeming with colorful fish and diverse coral structures.

Frequently Asked Questions
🏝️ What makes a Carnac Island charter so special? Carnac Island is a protected nature reserve located just a short cruise from Fremantle. It is legendary for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, pristine sandy beaches, and its permanent resident colony of wild Australian sea lions. It’s the ultimate destination for swimming, snorkeling, and relaxing away from the mainland.
🦭 Will we get to see the sea lions up close? Yes! Carnac Island is famous for its playful sea lion population. While they rest on the beach or swim around the shallow bays, we maintain a respectful and safe distance in accordance with wildlife conservation guidelines so you can watch them bask and play in their natural habitat.
⏰ How long is the cruise to Carnac Island? Carnac Island charters are typically run as flexible 4-hour functions. Because it is much closer to the mainland than Rottnest, it’s the ideal duration for a half-day escape packed with swimming, socializing, and a relaxed BBQ on board.
🌊 What happens if the weather is rough? Your safety and comfort are always our priority. Carnac Island is naturally quite sheltered compared to open ocean routes, but if maritime conditions change, your experienced skipper will pivot to alternative sheltered waters along the coast or on the Swan River to ensure your group still has an incredible day out.
🍔 Can we bring our own food and drinks for the island trip? Absolutely! Our Carnac Island cruises are fully BYO. Bring your favorite drinks, snacks, and meats, and our crew will fire up the massive onboard commercial stainless steel BBQs to cook your feast while you take in the views. We provide the 300L ice eskies and fridge space to keep everything ice-cold.
🧊 Can you help with ice before we board? To save you the heavy lifting and wet car boots on the day, we can arrange for ice to be pre-delivered straight to the boat for $110, provided we receive your request at least 48 hours before departure.
👟 What should we pack for a Carnac Island trip? Pack your swimmers, a beach towel, sunscreen, a hat, and a light jacket or windbreaker for the cruise back as the sea breeze picks up. Casual clothing and footwear (thongs, sandals, or sneakers) are perfect for getting on and off the vessel.
