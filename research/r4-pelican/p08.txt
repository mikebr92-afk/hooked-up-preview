Frequently Asked Questions
🎶 Can we play our own bridal playlist? It’s your final fling, so the soundtrack is entirely up to you! Our onboard sound systems are fully Bluetooth-ready so you can easily sync your phone and pump your favorite tracks. If you are hiring a professional DJ for your Swan River hens cruise, just have them touch base with our office beforehand to confirm our connection specs and power setup.
💃 Can we book entertainment like a nude life-drawing class or stripper? Absolutely! We host plenty of wild hens parties on board. You are welcome to bring along professional entertainment—such as a life-drawing model, entertainer, or party games coordinator—to give the bride-to-be an unforgettable send-off. We just ask that you keep things respectful for our crew and the vessel.
🎉 What are the rules for decorating the boat? We love a festive, fully themed boat for the bride-to-be, but we need to keep the vessel in pristine condition for our next guests. You are welcome to deck out the boat with sashes, balloons, and banners, provided you use non-damaging adhesive tape (no masking tape that leaves a sticky residue, please!). Strictly Prohibited: No confetti, glitter, or sparklers—they are notoriously difficult to clean up and can damage the vessel’s finish or the marine environment.
🧊 Can you help with ice and party supplies? We provide massive commercial-grade eskies on board for all your BYO drinks and food. To save you the heavy lifting on the day, we can arrange for ice to be pre-delivered straight to the boat for $110, provided we receive your request at least 48 hours before departure.
🍾 Can we bring our own drinks and food? Yes! Our Swan River cruises offer flexible BYO options. You are more than welcome to bring your own spread of food, snacks, and celebratory drinks for the bride. We provide the eskies and fridge space; you provide the party. (Note: If you plan to cater through a third party or have a grazing table delivered, let our crew know so we can coordinate drop-off logistics).
⏰ What is the latest our hens cruise can run? To comply with local Swan River navigation regulations, noise management guidelines, and crew safety hours, evening party charters generally wrap up and conclude at the jetty by standard local curfew times (typically 10:00 PM for evening slots, though our daytime cruise slots offer plenty of flexibility for soaking up the sun).
🧹 What happens after the celebrations wrap up? You are here to celebrate the bride, not clean up! Once your group disembarks at Barrack Street or Elizabeth Quay, our professional crew handles all deck scrubbing, rubbish collection, and general tidying to get the boat shipshape.
