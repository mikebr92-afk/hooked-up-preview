Rottnest Island Boat Hire & Private Cruises
Experience the ultimate Rotto party boat cruise with Pelican Charters.
Whether you are planning a milestone birthday, a legendary bucks or hens night, or a corporate function, our Rottnest Island boat charter packages offer the perfect full-day escape from Perth. Cruise across the Indian Ocean in style and drop anchor in the turquoise waters of Thompson Bay or Geordie Bay. Our custom-built charter vessels are fully equipped for swimming, snorkelling, and hosting the ultimate island party..
Your Ultimate Rottnest Island Cruise Package Includes: Spacious Deck & Party Facilities: Plenty of room to dance, lounge, and socialise with a premium sound system.
BYO Food & Drinks: Bring your own beverages and food. We provide large ice boxes (esky), ice, and a complimentary BBQ with a cook-on-board service.
Rottnest Island Party Boat Amenities: Fully enclosed toilets, hot showers, and easy ocean access ramps for swimming.
Catering Upgrades: Want a stress-free day? Ask about our fully catered Rottnest boat hire menus, featuring local WA seafood and gourmet BBQ options.
Protected Rottnest Island Bays & Marine Life
Explore Rottnest Island’s most pristine, sheltered bays. World-class destinations like Thomson Bay , Longreach Bay, and Geordie Bay offer complete tranquility and calm, postcard-blue waters protected from shifting wind directions.
These beautiful, secluded spots provide the perfect backdrop for a private island boat party. Our luxury vessels feature shallow drafts, allowing them to safely moor close to shore so swimmers can stand comfortably in waist-deep water.
Private Rottnest Day Trips & Celebrations
Soak up the sun, float on the water, or step ashore to explore historic local sites and meet the island’s famous, friendly quokkas. A Rottnest private charter is the ultimate summer venue for milestone birthdays, hens days, bucks parties, or corporate team-building excursions.
Our team can drop your group directly at the main jetty, allowing you to walk the island trails, play beach cricket, or enjoy a premium lunch and refreshments overlooking the water at Hotel Rottnest .
Swimming and Party Essentials
Feel free to bring along your favorite inflatable floats, paddleboards, and water toys. Our vessels are fully equipped with wide swimming platforms and heavy-duty ladder access to make entering and exiting the water completely effortless.
Note: All visitors entering the Rottnest Island Reserve are subject to standard individual island admission conservation fees, which contribute directly to the facility upkeep and environmental protection of the marine reserve.
Snorkeling, Diving, and Seasonal Whale Watching
The marine sanctuary surrounding the island is a snorkeler’s and diver’s dream. As we cruise across from the Fremantle coast, keep your eyes peeled for local marine life—including the magnificent humpback whales that migrate through our waters seasonally.

Frequently Asked Questions 
• How long does a Rottnest Island boat hire take from Perth? A standard Rottnest Island cruise package is an 8-hour day hire. The crossing from Fremantle or Perth to Rotto typically takes about 45 to 90 minutes depending on weather conditions and your departure point. 
• Can we bring our own alcohol and food on the boat charter? Yes! We are a fully BYO-friendly boat hire service. We provide the ice, eskies, and BBQ equipment. Our crew will even cook your food for you while you swim. 
• What happens if the weather is too rough for a Rottnest cruise? Your safety and comfort are our top priorities. If conditions are unsafe to cross to Rottnest Island, we will modify the itinerary to cruise the protected waters of the Swan River or reschedule your charter.
