Good to know FAQs
What types of fishing trips do you offer?
We offer private game fishing charters in Perth, targeting the deep waters of the Perth trench for species like Mahi Mahi, Wahoo, and Marlin. We also run a Jurien Bay season from late March to the end of May, Fridays to Sundays, departing from Jurien Bay Marina and targeting the same species.
What fishing gear is provided?
We provide everything you need! This includes 5 x 37kg Shimano Tiagra reels on custom rods, 5 x 15kg Shimano Tiagra outfits, 2 x 10kg setups, as well as Shimano Stella spinning gear and Saltiga spinning gear. Whether you're after heavy-tackle game fishing or light tackle, we've got you covered.
How many anglers can join a fishing trip?
Our Perth and Jurien Bay game fishing charters accommodate up to 6 anglers, ensuring plenty of space and the best chance of landing a catch. If you have any questions about your group, please contact us for more information.
Do you provide food and drinks on fishing charters?
Yes, we provide basic food for the day: usually a fruit platter on arrival, ham and cheese croissants or pies mid-morning, and chicken tender wraps for lunch. You're welcome to bring your own as well. All alcoholic drinks are strictly BYO, but we provide water and soft drinks onboard.
How do I book a fishing trip?
You can easily book your game fishing trip through our Booking Page . A $500 deposit is required to secure your spot, with the remaining balance due 7 days prior to departure.
