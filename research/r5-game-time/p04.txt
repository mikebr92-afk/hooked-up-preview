Boarding information Getting on board at Jurien Bay Marina 
Our Jurien Bay season runs from late March to the end of May, Fridays to Sundays. We depart from Jurien Bay Marina at 7 am and return at 4 pm.
As always, please plan to arrive 30 minutes before departure so we can get away on time.
Jurien Bay Marina We will send you your exact berth well before your trip. 
Explore Jurien Bay Jurien Bay game fishing 
Jurien Bay sits on Western Australia's Coral Coast, north of Perth, and our fishing charters run from its marina.
We run charters out of Jurien Bay Marina from late March to the end of May, Fridays to Sundays, targeting the same species as our Perth charters: Marlin, Mahi Mahi, Tuna, Mackerel and Wahoo.
