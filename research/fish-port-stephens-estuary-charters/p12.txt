Wont be long now…. 
The first blog of the Fish Port Stephens Estuary Charters Page! This last year has been a blur with a lot of hard work in getting the boat built and ready, doing courses, getting licenses, permits as well as the seemingly endless stream of forms and paper work that has come along with it. The last couple of months has been especially tough as the sheer workload has even cut into my fishing time! The good news is its all starting to come togeather now and that Fish Port Stephens Estuary Charters is about to become reality. Wont be long now!
