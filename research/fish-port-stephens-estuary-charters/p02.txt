THE BOAT 
Fish Port Stephens Estuary Charters is a Custom Built 5.7m Aluminum sport fishing vessel.
It was designed by Paul specifically for charter fishing inside the Port Stephens Estuary. 
The Rear Console design and massive 2.5m beam provides ample seating as-well as plenty of room on the extra large front casting deck to comfortably fish 4 anglers.
The vessel is also very soft riding which is of great benefit to our clients meaning when the wind does chop the bay up you wont be pounded all the way home!
