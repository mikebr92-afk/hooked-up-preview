Our Queenscliff fishing charter structures aim to target assorted species whilst fishing Port Phillip Bay and Bass Strait fishing grounds, and for various durations to suit all anglers.
Charters include:
• All fishing equipment
• Toilet facilities
• BYO Food & Drink
• We clean the fish!
Corporate Bookings
Larger groups or corporate bookings can be arranged using a multiple boat fleet.
Deposit
• Deposit of 30% required at time of booking.
Remaining balance on day of trip
• Cash
• Credit Card
Departure Point
• Queenscliff Harbour and Portsea Pier. For directions and maps of our departure/meeting points view the map at the bottom of selected trip page.
• Please confirm your preferred departure point upon booking.
Please note that all bookings are subject to our Terms and Conditions .
