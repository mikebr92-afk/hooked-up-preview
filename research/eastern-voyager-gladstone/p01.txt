Explore the Southern Great Barrier Reef, or choose your own destination
Coral Sea
Get on Board! Explore the remote outer reefs of the Coral Seas beautiful reef system which offers up exceptional clarity for some great fishing where you can experience catches of dog tooth tuna, yellow fin tuna, wahoo, giant trevally, green job fish, coral trout and more.
Swain Reefs
Enjoy a trip with your mates and partners to these remote reefs and coral cays located 120 nautical miles from Gladstone in the Southern Great Barrier Reef. You can explore both deep and reef edge fishing, and it is an ideal location for all fishers. This remote reef system offers up some great varieties of fish from coral trout, sweet lip, spanish mackerel, red emperor and many more great species.
Capricorn Bunker Group
Offers similar fishing to the Swains. You can drift fish from the Eastern Voyager or fish from one of the five tenders we carry onboard with us.

Our Trips
EASTERN VOYAGER CATERS FOR SELF GUIDED GROUP BOOKINGS (15-20 GUESTS)
Eastern Voyager is a long range liveaboard vessel that specialises in fishing charters out of Gladstone to the Southern Great Barrier Reef waters. The minimum group booking is 15. Groups of less than the minimum 15 guests are welcome to book the vessel based on the minimum day rate. 
DESTINATIONS:
Capricorn Bunker Group: (4-6 nights). 7-10 hrs travel. Located 50 nautical miles from Gladstone.
Swain Reefs / Shoalwater Bay: (7 nights and beyond). 15 hrs overnight travel (Saturday to Saturday). Located up 120 nautical miles from Gladstone.
Coral Sea: (10 nights and beyond). Up to 30 hours travel. Located up to 260 nautical miles from Gladstone.
Travel times dependant on destination and weather.
BOOKING TRIPS:
BOOK in the number of guests for your trip. Once confirmed, you can advise the name and contact details of the individuals in your group. If two trips are booked on the same day, the first booking received will be accepted and secured once the deposit is paid. 
If you can't see the dates you're after, simply fill out our contact form, or phone the office on 0436640197 to add your name(s) to our waiting list. More information regarding the boat, fishing gear and charters can be found on the Eastern Voyager and FAQ tabs.
