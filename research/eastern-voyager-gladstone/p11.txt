Group Bookings
Swain Reefs, Shoalwater Bay, Capricorn Bunker: Weeks available in 2025 and 2026 
EASTERN VOYAGER CATERS FOR SELF GUIDED GROUP BOOKINGS (15-20 GUESTS)
The minimum group booking is 15. Groups of less than the minimum 15 guests are welcome to book the vessel based on the minimum day rate. Eastern Voyager is a long range (liveaboard vessel) not available for 1 day or week-end trips
Available for:
Minimum 4 nights and beyond to the Capricorn Bunker Group ( 7-10 hr travel (dependent on area of destination and weather –located 50 nautical miles from Gladstone)
Minimum 7 nights and beyond to the Swain Reefs / Shoalwater Bay areas ( over- night travel, travel to both areas, time similar dependent on area of destination and weather (located up 120 nautical miles from Gladstone).
Minimum 10 nights and beyond to the Coral Sea (located up to 260 nautical miles – up to 30 hours travel dependent on area of destination and weather.
1 Seat = 1 Group. Group bookings require 1 designated person (group organiser for your group booking). Once you have either booked online or contacted the office for your booking, an email information package with your booked charter dates will be sent to you including deposit and balance of your charter payment information to confirm and hold your booked dates.
If you are not able to organise a group booking click on our Join a Group tab. Check for availability and book online or phone the office of 0436 640 197 to:
– add your name(s) to our waiting list to join a group already booked looking for fill ins for their trip,
– or join one of our own organised group trips.
More information regarding the boat, fishing gear and charters can be found on the Eastern Voyager and FAQ tabs.
