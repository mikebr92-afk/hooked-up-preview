Disover Darwin’s best charter boats 
The Best Fishing Charter Boats in the Northern Territory
98% of small group charter boats in Darwin are in 2D Survey and are limited to fish just 1nm off the coastline which significantly limits your fishing options.
The Best Boats for Your Fishing Tours of Dundee Beach 
Our boats are built by fishermen, for fishermen. See the benefits that set our fishing boats apart. 
More Fishing Options
All Anglers Choice vessels are licensed and built to exceed Australian Maritime Safety 2C Survey which means we can legally and safely fish 15nm offshore giving you the angler so many more fishing options. Simply put, this means more fish for you from the safest boats possible. 
Safety Standards
We are serious about safety. Vessels are fitted with twin Yamaha engines and Minn Kota I-Pilot electric motors. We carry full safety gear including flares, EPIRBS, first aid kits, hard wired satellite phones and Zolios for added safety. Another survey requirement is foam filled hulls which create positive floatation. This makes them supremely stable, safe and unsinkable. 
Safety briefings are conducted each morning before departure and our guides are fully qualified and NT endorsed with fisheries so you can be sure of a professional experience. 
Built to Fish
Our 2 x 7.2m Ocean masters and 2 x 7.4m Custom works boats were built in the NT by leading boat builders. At home on the bluewater and in the rivers, our boats are spacious, uncluttered and comfortable. 
Our 9m boat Waverider was custom built in Broome and is the ultimate Dundee Beach Bluewater boat with internal seating, large shade deck and fully walkaround cabin allowing plenty of space to fight big fish. 
Having a fleet of vessels fishing from the one location also provides us with prompt assistance if ever required.
