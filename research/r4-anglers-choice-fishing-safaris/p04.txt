We are passionate about fantastic fishing! 
Customise Your Darwin Fishing Charters, Tours and Trips
At Anglers Choice, we are as passionate about fishing as we are about Darwin and the Northern Territory. Our Darwin fishing charters have been running out of Dundee Beach for 20 years and it's also the place we are proud to call home. We believe it offers some of the best fishing in Australia! 
Fishing Tours in Dundee Beach 
Darwin visitors can look forward to experiencing fully-accommodated fishing tours, including barramundi fishing tours, reef fishing charters and sport fishing charters. We provide a selection of top quality fishing tackle including overhead and spinning reels. Lures are provided on a replace-if-lost basis. 
Barra Fishing Charters
Barra fishing is a must do activity for anyone coming to Darwin! On one of our charters, you can expect to catch barramundi averaging between 65cm-80cm during the dry season and maybe land that metery during the run off and build up. Barramundi fishing trips are so much more than just catching barramundi. On one of our one-day charters, you can expect to see crocodiles, birds of prey, jabiru birds and the picturesque sights of the NT. 
Reef Fishing Charters
The reefs we fish are out of range for most Darwin fishing charter companies that depart from Darwin harbour. That's why we are proud to provide a quality fishing charter and specialise in taking small groups of 4-6 people on our one-day deep sea fishing trips. 
Sport Fishing Charters
If you are looking for an adrenaline-packed fishing adventure, whilst wrestling the reel to pull in a wide range of fish, then this is the charter for you! Utilising our custom-built sport fishing boats, we are able to cover wider areas over short periods of time and keep up with and chase after the faster moving fish. 
Find the Best Fishing in Dundee Beach
Having fished the local waters daily for over a decade, our local knowledge of the local area is second to none. Our friendly and experienced fishing guides will share their extensive knowledge with you throughout to help you to catch those big fish. 
Dundee Beach is located 165kms southwest of Darwin and it is the finest location to experience fishing charters as it provides easy access to some of the more remote fishing grounds. On our various fishing trips, Darwin holiday-makers can expect to encounter an incredibly diverse selection of target species including the Dundee beach trifecta of barramundi, jewfish and sailfish.

Book a Darwin Fishing Charter Today
We will help you plan every aspect of your fishing charter to Darwin. Before booking flights to Darwin, be sure to call and check fishing tides to ensure the best tides for your charter. 
We are local Territorians and passionate about fishing and passionate about Darwin and the Northern Territory. We offer you the complete Northern Territory fishing experience and can arrange everything from fishing charters, boat hire, accommodation and transfers from Darwin to Dundee Beach. 
We’ll send you an information pack outlining your fishing & accommodation options, what you need to bring and what you should expect on an Anglers Choice fishing charter at Dundee Beach.
