Relax, Go Fishing! (Relax, Go Fishing!)
We have a firm commitment to provide affordable and enjoyable fishing charters. Therefore we provide a selection of different trips designed to cater for all needs. We like to encourage families to get together and enjoy some quality time - to this end, we offer discounts for children (minimum age 5 years), senior, pensioners and students. 
Standard trips include:
Morning Trip - 7am - 12 noon and Afternoon Trip - 1pm - 5pm 
General/Mixed Bag Fishing - Port Phillip Bay is predominantly Flathead, but in season, there are always other fish around i.e. Snapper, various Sharks, Garfish, Rockcod, Whiting, Leatherjackets. Includes - rods, reels, bait & tackle, refreshments - coffee, tea, hot chocolate and soft drink as well as a sausage sizzle.
Twilight Trip - 6pm - 10pm (November to March ONLY) 
This trip specifically targets the elusive Snapper. Sit back and enjoy the stunning sunset that Port Phillip Bay provides as you try for those elusive Snapper. Trip includes - rods, reels, bait & tackle, refreshments - coffee, tea and soft drink as well as a complimentary steak & salad dinner. 
Every trip departs from Mornington Pier in Mornington.
