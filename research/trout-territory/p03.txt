FLY FISHING WORKSHOPS
Fly Fishing Tuition & Workshops for Groups
Introductory fly fishing workshops are booked by groups, and are very suited to beginners, and also masters, as well as groups with mixed skill levels. We start with group instruction on the very basics of casting, an overview of fly fishing, flies, fly lines, rods, knots, insects, trout, etc. then we spread out and each person in the group receives some individual coaching throughout the workshop.
We provide all fishing gear.
Groups make their own way to the venue. We let you know the location and meet you there.
Basic Workshop
The workshops run from 10.30am (4 hours). The focus is on building your casting and line control skills, your understanding of fly fishing, trout behaviour and habitat, etc. and does not provide the opportunity to catch fish.
The price is $200.00 per person (minimum price is $1,000.00 if there are less than 5 participants).
Extended Workshop
Participants may extend their Basic Workshop for a further 2 hours, giving them the opportunity to apply their newly acquired knowledge and skills, with an opportunity to catch a fish.
The price to extend the Basic Workshop is $100.00 per person, plus a fishing licence.
Fishing licences start at $29.00 for a 48 hour licence, free for kids aged 14-17 years, and under 14 does not require a fishing licence.
Workshop Location
Workshop locations are selected according to where your group is based, along with other conditions, i.e. weather, water levels, logistical arrangements.
How to book a fly fishing workshop
If you have a group and would like to book a fly fishing workshop, please contact Trout Territory:
Email: gary@troutterritory.com.au 
Gary: 0418 875 001
Fiona: 0417 975 006
Any questions, please ask, we’re happy to help.
TERMS & CONDITIONS OF THE WORKSHOPS
On Extended Workshops the fishing is generally from the bank.
Participants should bring a hat, sunglasses, sturdy boots/shoes, warm/rain jackets.
Payments: 20% deposit is required to secure your preferred date. Balance is due prior to the day. Payment options: Visa, Mastercard, direct transfer, cash.
Workshop Cancellations:
More than 28 days prior to the commencement of the workshop, 90% of deposit will be refunded.
28-15 days prior to the commencement of the workshop, 75% of deposit will be refunded.
14-8 days prior to the commencement of the workshop, 50% of deposit will be refunded.
7 days or less prior to the commencement of the workshop, no deposit will be refunded.
Cancellations due to Covid-19 border closures & Government quarantine requirements, full deposit will be refunded.
Deposit may be transferred to secure another date where available.
