About Us
Coffs Harbour Fishing Charters
Coffs Harbour Fishing is owned, skippered and crewed by locals who are all experienced fisherman. We know the local seasons, waters and fishing grounds and are all experienced with a variety of fishing.
We love to show people how good the fishing is on our beautiful Coffs Coast and try our hardest to make sure your day out with us is relaxing, fun and catch plenty of fish.
We operate 3 new boats out of Coffs Harbour. Our boats have been custom designed by us to suit the fishing we do at Coffs Harbour. All our boats are powered to cruise at 22 knots, so less travelling time, less sea-sickness and more fishing time.
Our fishing gear is high quality. We only use gear that we would personally use ourselves. We source local quality bait to ensure we have the best chance of catching plenty of fish.
