About
Port Douglas based MV Norseman is a fast and comfortable dedicated reef fishing vessel. Individuals, groups or corporate days can be catered for – if you want a great reef fishing adventure then Norseman is your best choice from Port Douglas .
• Specialises in reef fishing adventures for all of the top quality table eating fish found in the Coral Sea.
• Offers a daily reef fishing adventure where you can try your hand at landing some of the best fightings and eating fish the Coral Sea has to offer.
• Species such as Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Sea Perch (Nannygai), Mackerel, Cobia, Trevally, Cod, Spangled Emperor and Reef Red Bream (Mangrove Jack) will be targeted whilst bottom fishing, using either handlines or rods.
• Whenever possible, you can float live baits for GT’s (Giant Trevally), Spanish Mackerel, Tuna , and Wahoo on a Norseman reef fishing adventure.
Includes:
• Bait and tackle
• Handlines
• Delicious Tropical lunch, morning and afternoon tea
• Your catch cleaned and chilled
• Rod and reel
• We have a fully licensed bar on board – you can purchase soft drinks, snack and alcoholic beverages from our bar (no BYO)
• Air-conditioned saloon and bridge
• Toilet and shower facilities / Expansive upper and lower deck areas / Relaxing sun lounges
• Sportfishing platform
What to bring:
• Suncream
• Hat
• Sunglasses
• Camera
• Cash or credit card for onboard purchases
• Bring your own rod and reel if your prefer
• Face mask (if you prefer)
