Experience Reef Fishing on a Port Douglas Fishing Charter ! 
Port Douglas-based MV Norseman is a fast and comfortable, dedicated reef fishing vessel. Individuals, groups or corporate days can be catered for — if you want a great reef fishing adventure, then Norseman is your best choice for Port Douglas fishing. 
• Specializes in reef fishing adventures for all of the top-quality table eating fish found in the Coral Sea.
• Offers a daily reef fishing adventure where you can try your hand at landing some of the best fishing in Port Douglas and eating the best fish the Coral Sea has to offer. 
• Species such as Coral Trout, Red Emperor, Sea Perch (Nannygai), Mackerel, Cobia, Trevally, Cod, Spangled Emperor and Reef Red Bream (Mangrove Jack) will be targeted whilst bottom fishing, using either handlines or rods.
• Whenever possible, you can float live baits for GT’s (Giant Trevally), Spanish Mackerel, Tuna, and Wahoo on a Norseman reef fishing adventure.
Please give us a call for private fishing charter options! Book your Port Douglas fishing charters online today.
