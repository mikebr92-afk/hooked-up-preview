Central Coast Reef & Game Fishing Charters
Fishing charters from the Haven, Terrigal on the Central Coast since 1996.
DEEP SEA REEF FISHING | GAME FISHING | LEISURE CHARTERS | WHALE WATCHING
• phone 0427 66 55 44 
• 
We do fishing charters
for deep sea reef fishing, game fishing and whale watching off the beautiful Central Coast. No licence required.
• 
Join us for an exciting
reef fishing charter to catch sportfish & saltwater species including Marlin, Yellowfin Tuna, Albacore, Snapper, Jewfish and more.
• 
We have a fine reputation
for providing a quality vessel, first class customer service, excellent safety standards, fully qualified and experienced crew.
Reef & Deep Sea Fishing with an experienced qualified crew
Let our crew look after you, while you have a great day 
out on the water. Ideal day out for the fishing enthusiast, family or friends get-together or work bonding sessions.
We assist with fishing techniques and even clean and ice your catch ready for you to take home. 
All safety standards are met and our modern, fast and well 
maintained boat is fully equipped for deep sea fishing.
For your convenience, a shower, toilet, galley, canopy 
and seating on deck is available. We have rod holders, a live bait tank, fish cleaning 
tables and insulated fish and bait boxes, everything you 
need for a great day fishing.
Visit our Info page for pricing, terms & directions.
