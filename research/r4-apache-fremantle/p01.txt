Fremantle Deep Sea Fishing and Rock Lobster Tour 
Apache Charters now offers this exciting addition to our fishing charters from Fremantle! 
Come on board the 70ft Lady Grace for a full day of deep sea and Rock Lobster Fishing. Experience catching our iconic Western Rock Lobster and share in the spoils of this Western Australian delicacy. Line fish for species such as Dhufish, Pink Snapper, Baldchin Groper & Breaksea Cod. Along the way we will pull our many Rock Lobster pots (Apache Charters can now fish and pull 12 lobster cray pots on our fishing tours) and the crays that we catch will be shared out among our passengers (Lobster fishing only available in December). On our tours, we guarantee each person at least one Lobster freshly caught and snap frozen for you to take home. 
Please note: Due to current metro Demersal Fishing Closures these tours are not currently operating. 
Still want to fish? Head to our Abrolhos, Shark Bay and Montebellos page 
Tour Details 
• Whole boat private charter - $4500 (12 pax) 
• Individual bookings joining a mixed group - $390pp 
(minimum passengers numbers apply) 
• Tours run December to February 
• Departs from Sardine Jetty, Mews Road Fremantle 
• Departs 6am and returns at 2.30pm
• 70ft Vessel Lady Grace
Inclusions 
• All deep sea and Rock Lobster Fishing gear, bait, ice tackle, rods, reels, lobster pots 
• All fishing licenses and Demersal Tags provided. 
• Morning Tea, Cold Lunch of Sandwiches/wraps, tea/coffee, hot water, soft drinks and bottled water 
• Any fish and Lobster you catch which complies with all fishing rules and regulations you get to take home. Fish will be cleaned and filleted and Lobster will be professionally handled and packed for you to take home

The Vessel - Lady Grace 
The Lady Grace is a 70ft fully equipped vessel licenced for up to 70 persons fitted out to ensure your utmost comfort whilst onboard. 
Lady Grace is also in full commercial Australian survey which complies to the Australian Maritime Safety Authority Regulations and is inspected annually by AMSA so you can be rest assured you are on a safe and capable vessel when you book with Apache Charters.
