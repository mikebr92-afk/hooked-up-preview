Fly Fishing For Beginners
Fly Fishing for Beginners in Tasmania 
Simple, personalised learning with our local fly fishing guides 
New to fly fishing? You’re in the right place. At Tasmania Fly Fishing, we specialise in helping beginners learn the basics in a way that’s clear, fun, and rewarding. Our patient, local guides use practical coaching and hands-on experience to help you build confidence—and many of our first-time clients catch their very first trout on fly.
We believe fly fishing should be accessible to everyone. Whether you’ve never held a fly rod or just want to refine your cast, we tailor each experience to suit your pace, goals, and level of adventure.

Which beginner tour is right for you? 
Full-day tours 
For the best learning experience, a full-day tour is our top recommendation. You’ll spend the morning learning key skills with your guide—like casting, setting the hook, and reading the water—followed by a relaxed lunch break. In the afternoon, you’ll put everything into practice, with plenty of support to help you succeed.
Half-day and 3/4-day tours 
Pressed for time? These shorter tours are perfect for a quick introduction or refresher. They’re especially popular with beginners who want to test the waters before committing to a full day. You’ll still get quality time with a guide and a great chance to hook your first trout.
One-on-one guiding 
Prefer to learn privately? Book a personalised guided tour for a focused, flexible experience. Your guide will meet you where you’re at and tailor the day to your needs—whether it’s your first cast or your first fish on dry fly.
