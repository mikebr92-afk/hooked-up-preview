Places To Stay
Accommodation Recommendations 
We’re often asked by new clients for suggestions on where to stay while fly fishing in Tasmania. Below is a short list of trusted accommodation options that our regular clients consistently speak highly of. We’ve included a mix of locations and budgets to suit different preferences.
Accommodation on or Near Fisheries
The following places offer direct access to trout water or are located right on active fisheries. For keen anglers, staying close to the water often adds another layer of enjoyment to the trip — whether it’s a quiet evening rise after your guided day or a morning cast before breakfast.
We have a long-standing working relationship with Currawong Lakes and a growing connection with Tin Dragon Cottages — both offering unique stays that align beautifully with the spirit of fly fishing in Tasmania.
