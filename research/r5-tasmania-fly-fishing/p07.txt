At Tasmania Fly Fishing, we are proud to announce our partnership with Composite Developments , a renowned New Zealand-based supplier, manufacturer, and importer of premium fly fishing gear. For decades, Composite Developments has been celebrated for its innovation, precision craftsmanship, and unwavering commitment to quality, earning a reputation as a trusted name among anglers worldwide.
Locally Owned, Globally Respected 
Composite Developments combines a deep understanding of local needs with the expertise to create products that meet international standards. As a locally owned and managed company, they embody the values of quality, reliability, and a passion for fishing that resonates with anglers throughout the Southern Hemisphere.

Taimer Fly Reel: Precision and Power in Every Turn – $330.00 
The Taimer Fly Reel combines advanced engineering with elegant design to deliver unmatched performance on the water. Designed to perfectly complement the Composite Developments XLS 11 and ICT 11 Fly Rods, this large-arbor reel offers smooth, reliable operation and the durability needed for demanding fly fishing adventures.
Exceptional Features for Optimal Performance 
• Large-Arbor Design : Ensures rapid line retrieval, reducing memory and improving casting efficiency.
• Smooth Cork Disk Drag System : Features a large-diameter cork drag with minimal start-up inertia for a seamless, responsive feel.
• One-Way Stainless Steel Spindle Bearing : Enhanced by an internal cap and O-ring seal for smooth and reliable performance.
• Sealed Drag System : Protected by a high-density ABS cap to keep out moisture and debris, ensuring long-lasting functionality.
• Easy Spool Removal : Sealed O-ring screw cap locks tightly to the spindle while allowing for effortless spool changes.
• Silent Retrieve with Subtle Clicker : Provides a refined experience, whether retrieving line or hearing the soft click of a trophy fish taking off.
• Right- to Left-Hand Conversion : Easily adjustable to suit every angler’s preference.
Engineered for the Serious Angler 
Designed with precision and built to perform, the Taimer Fly Reel delivers an unbeatable combination of power, smoothness, and reliability. Whether you’re targeting wild trout in pristine streams or handling tough runs on larger waters, this reel has the versatility and performance to meet your needs. We use these in our guiding service and are Scott’s reel of choice. Available in sizes 5/6 and 7/9.
Ready to Cast?

When it comes to tackling cold waters and rugged conditions, the CDX Neoprene Chest Waders are the ultimate choice for fly fishers who demand comfort, durability, and functionality. Designed with thoughtful features to keep you warm and dry, these waders are built to enhance your time on the water.
Features Built for Tough Conditions 
• 4.5mm Thick Neoprene Construction : Provides superior insulation and comfort, keeping you warm even in icy waters.
• Stitch and Taping Seam Construction : Stitches are fully sealed on the inside to ensure leak-proof performance.
• Front Hand Warmer Pocket : A cozy haven for your hands on frosty days.
• Functional Storage : Includes three front pouches for easy access to essential gear.
• Padded Knee Reinforcement : Extra protection and durability where you need it most.
• Fully Lined 4.5mm PVC Boots : Integrated with cleated soles for superior grip and comfort.
Adjustable and Practical 
With adjustable suspenders and a comfortable fit, the CDX Neoprene Chest Waders provide flexibility and ease of movement. Available in US sizes 6/7, 8/9, 10/11, and 12/13, we recommend choosing a size up to accommodate thick socks for maximum warmth and comfort.
Built for Anglers, Trusted by Guides 
Whether you’re wading into frigid streams or navigating rocky riverbeds, these waders offer unbeatable warmth and durability. Trusted by professional guides, the CDX Neoprene Chest Waders are a must-have for anglers who refuse to let the elements hold them back.
