World-class fishing. Wild locations. Endless ways to cast a line.
From tidal creeks and inland lakes to remote coral reefs and offshore islands, Australia’s North West offers some of the most diverse and rewarding fishing experiences in the country. Whether you’re flicking a lure from your own tinny, heading out on a day charter, fishing from a luxury lodge, or dropping a line during a Kimberley cruise — this is a destination built for anglers of every kind.
Whether you’re in the Pilbara, Broome or the Kimberley, the region is renowned for healthy fish stocks, iconic species and vast, uncrowded waters — all set against some of the most jaw-dropping landscapes you’ll ever fish.

WHAT YOU CAN CATCH
Creek & Estuary Fishing
Slow-moving creeks, mangrove systems and tidal flats deliver some of the North West's most iconic sport fishing — led by the legendary barramundi. These warm, tidal waters are also home to threadfin salmon and mangrove jack, making creek fishing one of the region's most rewarding and accessible experiences.
Best enjoyed across Broome, Derby, Kununurra, Lake Kununurra and Lake Argyle, creek fishing can be done DIY or with experienced local guides.
Demersal Fishing
While parts of Western Australia's coastline have restrictions on demersal fishing, recreational and charter demersal fishing remains available in Australia's North West, making it a major drawcard for travelling anglers.
Target prized reef species such as red emperor, coral trout, bluebone, fingermark and rankin cod, with demersal fishing featuring heavily on offshore charters and expedition cruises around remote reefs, islands and coastal waters.
Pelagic Fishing
Fast, powerful and adrenaline-fuelled, pelagic fishing is a highlight offshore throughout the region.
Anglers can target species such as sailfish, marlin, Spanish mackerel and tuna, particularly from the coast and offshore islands, as well as along remote stretches of the Kimberley coastline.
Mud Crabbing
For many visitors, mud crabbing is a quintessential North West experience. The region's extensive mangrove-lined creeks, tidal estuaries and sheltered coastal waters provide ideal habitat for the prized mud crab, renowned for its sweet, succulent meat.
Popular throughout the Kimberley and Pilbara, mud crabbing can be enjoyed as a DIY adventure or with local guides who know the best waterways and seasonal conditions. Setting and checking crab pots is a rewarding way to experience the region's unique coastal environment, with many anglers combining mud crabbing with a day of creek or estuary fishing.
Always check current regulations, licensing requirements and seasonal closures before crabbing. 
DAY CHARTERS 
Perfect for visitors short on time, day charters offer local knowledge, quality gear and access to prime fishing grounds across Broome, the Pilbara and the Kimberley.
Target iconic species like barramundi, threadfin salmon, mangrove jack, along with reef and pelagic favourites offshore.

Where to Fish in Australia’s North West 
Spanning the Pilbara, Broome and the Kimberley, Australia’s North West offers extraordinary fishing diversity — from inland lakes and tidal creeks to coral reefs and remote offshore islands.
Across the region you’ll find barra-filled creeks and estuaries, vast river systems such as Lake Argyle and Lake Kununurra, offshore island groups including the Mackerel Islands, Montebello Islands and Rowley Shoals, and productive coastal waters around Broome, Derby, Karratha, Dampier Archipelago, Port Hedland and Onslow.
Whether you’re shore fishing, launching your own boat, joining a day charter, fishing from a luxury lodge or casting a line during a Kimberley cruise, the North West delivers iconic species, uncrowded waters and wild fishing locations set against some of Australia’s most dramatic coastal and outback landscapes. 
Fishing Comps & Events
Hook into the action with community comps, serious sportfishing and unforgettable days on the water.
Planning Your Fishing Trip 
Licences & Tours 
A Recreational Fishing from Boat Licence may be required when fishing from a powered vessel. Charter tours include all required licences and equipment.
Fishing Safely 
• Be croc-wise near rivers, creeks and estuaries
• Check weather and marine conditions
• Carry safety gear and let someone know your plans
Fish for the Future 
Australia’s North West supports responsible fishing practices to protect fish stocks for generations to come. Observe bag limits, size limits and seasonal closures, and release fish carefully where required.
Tides & Conditions 
Tides play a critical role in fishing success across the region. Checking tide charts and local conditions will help plan the best time to fish — particularly in tidal creeks and coastal waters.
Whether you're chasing barra in a Kimberley creek, dropping lines over reef systems offshore, or fishing remote waters during a Kimberley cruise — there's a fishing experience for everyone in Australia's North West.
