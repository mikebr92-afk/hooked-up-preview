Experience Mackay fishing just like a local
Book a charter today!
If you’re looking for a guided fishing trip to really sink your teeth into recreational fishing in Mackay, then a local charter might be for you. We have a range of experienced charter operators in our region waiting to get you hooked up to a range of trophy species. From long-range offshore trips to the Great Barrier Reef, to customised inshore trips focusing on your bucket list, through to freshwater flyfishing - there is a trip for every taste and style of fishing. 
Not sure where to start? Check out the below charter operators for more details on their fishing and trip styles. We recommend you get in touch in advance to secure a booking. 
For advice and assistance with booking your next Mackay Region getaway or fishing charter, please contact us and our friends from Mackay Tourism will be in touch. 
Contact Us
Inshore Fishing Mackay 
Explore Mackay’s estuary and inshore fishing with tailored fishing adventures by local angler and qualified skipper, Jason Kidd. With over 25 years’ experience of fishing the Mackay region, you’ll enjoy targeting our iconic barramundi and all estuary species such as mangrove jack, fingermark, threadfin salmon, flathead and grunter to name a few.
0457 254 740 
Elizabeth E II 
Go further than ever before with the Elizabeth E II. Enjoy all the creature comforts of this versatile live-aboard charter vessel, suitable for any type of custom charter, from extended fishing excursions to cruises and dive trips. Fish, cruise, visit islands or swim on the reef – Elizabeth E II can tailor a charter to suit your needs.
07 4955 2563 
Mackay Fly and Sportfishing 
A fishing charter like no other, Paul at Mackay Fly & Sportfishing (MFSF) specialises in fly and lure fishing. MFSF offer personalised full day fishing adventures in both salt and freshwater for a variety of great sportfish species, for both fly fisherman and conventional lure anglers.
0407 674 350 
Cast and Catch Fishing Charters 
Based in Alligator Creek, just 26km south of Mackay and launching from Hay Point, Cast and Catch Fishing Charters offers an unforgettable fishing experience. Aboard the "Obsession", a 24ft custom-built boat, anglers of all levels can enjoy the best fishing the region has to offer. Whether you're targeting spanish mackerel, coral trout, or other prized catches, Cast and Catch Fishing Charters has your next adventure covered. Contact Alvan today to book your next fishing trip!
0429 833 045
