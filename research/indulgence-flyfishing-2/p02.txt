Customer Reviews 
SOME THOUGHTS FROM HAPPY OUR CLIENTS 
“ Scott McPherson knows his stream craft as well as anyone I’ve come across. But as well as being a fly-fishing guide, Scott’s forte is to value add to the fishing through his cooking. A former chef, he calls his business Indulgence Flyfishing… and you find out why over breakfast, lunch and dinner. ”
Steve Cooper , Sunday Herald Sun 
“Just came off my ‘bogey’ river today, the Toorongo. Up until now I have struggled on this river and I hadn’t fished it for a while. For those who dont know it, it has small fish which can be skitterish and had to catch without experience and some reasonable level of skill. I took a dozen today, all released, and missed a few as well,a great session. Before my lesson with Scottie I was a struggling Fly fisher but after a days guided session with Scottie I now regularly catch good numbers and good size fish to boot. Before my day with Scottie I had no idea how bad I was with both casting and streamcraft but thanks to his patience, tolerance and perseverance…and a great sense of humour I am now well out of the novice class.
I can’t recommend Scottie highly enough, he is brilliant with a novice such as I and if your thinking of booking with him, don’t hesitate, he will do wonders to your skills and your enjoyment of this fabulous pastime.”
Anthony Wakeling 
“Knowing my passion for fly fishing and as a surprise for my 50th birthday my wife Barb contacted Scott McPherson from Indulgence Flyfishing to book a few days on the beautiful Mitta Mitta River. The booking included two of my close friends Marc and Ros Poulton and we stayed in a quaint rural cottage nearby the enchanting Witches Gardens just 2 minutes from the river. 
Scott provided the essential ingredients for a memorable few days by pampering the girls through his culinary delights and providing a sensational flyfishing experience for both Marc and me through his expert guidance. Catching a couple of feisty brown trout was a bonus and exploring the picturesque Mitta Mitta River and surrounds was a flyfishing experience I won’t forget. I can recommend Scott’s Indulgence Flyfishing experience to everyone, not just the avid flyfishing enthusiasts, but the wives and partners just looking for a relaxing few days in the magnificent Victorian countryside. Keep up the good work Scott…” 
Leigh Wallace 
“Scotty’s a master in one of the best trout regions in Australia. Great teacher, host and mate. “ 
Doug Brooke 
“A fly-hooked trout breaking the sun-sparkling surface of a high country river. The delicate aromas of a gourmet breakfast being prepared camp-fire style floating through early-morning mist. Soft talk of big fish, wild streams, friendship forged through shared experience and an appreciation of the natural world that only the high country can evoke. Indulgence Flyfishing offers all this, and more! It really is a unique experience.”
Kim MacDonald , Documentary Maker 
“As three novices we couldn’t have asked for a better introduction to flyfishing. Our day on the Nariel was memorable not only for providing me with my first trout on fly but also for establishing a starting point for a future, hopefully full, of fly fishing adventures.” 
Guy Wilkinson 
“Although I had done quite a bit of lure casting for trout, I had never used a fly rod on a stream so decided to spend three days with Indulgence Flyfishing in the hope that Scott McPherson could teach me how to fly fish on mountain streams.
He did it well, with a mixture of skill and patience. Scott taught me what to look for (behind and above me as well as on the water). He taught me roll casts and side casts to use in tight spots (and it is amazing how good holes or runs are often in tight spots). Into the third day I was hunting fish with a purpose and was able to utilize skills that Scott had imparted to me.
I had not expected to catch many fish because it was calm, clear, warm weather at the end of a busy season but ended up catching an even dozen. I thoroughly enjoyed the entire experience and am confident that I have a good foundation on which to build many years of happy fishing. 
By the way, I’m 64 years of age, therefore I suppose it could be said that Scott has the rare ability to ‘teach old dogs new tricks’!” Thanks to Scott.” 
Alan Stevens

Indulgence Flyfishing specialize in guiding Northeast Victoria. Whether you are a beginner, or an experienced flyfisher, we can tailor a package that will ensure you get the most from you time with us. Guide Scott McPherson have been flyfishing Northeast Victoria for longer than he can remember. His intimate knowledge of the local rivers and streams, along with the ability to help flyfishers of all skill levels improve their casting and streamcraft is what makes time spent with Indulgence Flyfishing so valuable. Whther you’re coming up from Melbourne, dropping down from Sydney, or flying in from any part of the world, the experience of flyfishing Northeast Victoria is one that you will never forget. Get in touch with Scott today to discuss guiding in Northeast Victoria.
