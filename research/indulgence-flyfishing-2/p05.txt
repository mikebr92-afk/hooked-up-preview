What you will need
Your fishing clothing should be either neutral colours or a camo style. Long sleeved shirt and trousers are essential, as is sunscreen, a hat and sunglasses.
Thigh boots will cover most wading situations, although chest waders can come in handy on some of the larger rivers and lakes. Mind you, wading wet during the hotter months can be a lot of fun too!.
In general, a #4 to #6 weight setup will cover all situations that arise, although a #2/3 weight outfit can give you the thrill of a lifetime on some of the smaller mountain streams. If you don’t have the necessary gear, we will supply waders and/or fly rods upon request.

Illness policy
Indulgence Flyfishing understands that people have changed boundaries after the spread of Covid in recent years. I respect my clients as to not infect them with any illness, and I expect the same respect back towards myself and my business.
If anyone is booked in with me and shows any sign of illness, including but not limited to Covid, I will require immediate notification so that we can postpone the trip to another date.
If anyone turns up on the day and shows systems of contagious illness, or claims to be recovering, the day will be forfeit and NO REFUND or alternative date will be sorted.
I cannot afford to throw my time and money away on anyone who will not respect myself or others.
I am a pretty fair and understanding guy and I know things can go haywire at times. If you are fair to me; I will be more than fair to you.
I hope that you all understand from where I am coming, and know that in good health it will be my pleasure to guide you in this and future seasons.
