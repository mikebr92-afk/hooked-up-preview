Career opportunities at Reflections Holidays
We care for 40 accommodation locations and 45 community Crown reserves throughout NSW, so there is a wealth of tourism and hospitality career opportunities. 
We are looking for people who love the outdoors and care deeply about creating great experiences for our guests while caring for communities, culture, and nature.
We have permanent, part-time, casual, and seasonal opportunities just waiting for you. To apply for a role in Current Opportunities, click on the Apply Now button under your desired role.
For general HR inquiries contact hr@reflectionsholiday.com.au .
Explore current opportunities 
Our local hiring policy
Reflections Holidays is committed to supporting the social and economic well-being of the communities in which we operate. 
As a profit-for-purpose organisation, we recognise that our success is intrinsically linked to the prosperity of local communities across New South Wales. 
This Local Hiring Policy formalises our commitment to prioritising local employment opportunities across all park locations, contributing to stronger communities, enhanced guest experiences, and sustainable regional development.
View our local hiring policy 
Why work with us?
Employees at Reflections Holidays can take advantage of great benefits such as: 
• Flexible working arrangements, including a hybrid work environment
• Generous accommodation discounts at 39 holiday parks throughout NSW
• Additional leave benefits such as paid parental leave, birthday leave, volunteering leave, wedding day leave, moving day leave and more
• Salary Packaging options, including novated leasing through Maxxia
• Professional development and study support through our education assistance program, with up to 6 days’ study leave per year
• Annual flu vaccinations
• 24/7 support for staff and their families through our EAP program
Who we are
Here at Reflections Holidays, we share nature’s playground with our guests, while caring for communities, culture and the environment.
Our parks are located on the shores of some of Australia’s most epic beaches and inland lakes across NSW, providing experiences that stay with our guests forever.
As a profit-for-purpose organisation, we care equally about profit, people, planet and indigenous culture. Officially this is our quadruple bottom line, though we tend to think of it simply as good business.
We believe that values shouldn’t just be words on the lunch room wall: they are our promise to our guests and each other. We Care Deeply, Say It, Do It, Grow Together and Make a Difference. This is who we are.
We are a dynamic and purpose-led team and our organisation is in an exciting growth phase. You can read more about our plans here. or follow us on LinkedIn. 
Our values say a lot about us
Grow together 
When we work as a team, everyone benefits. We grow when our people, our places and our communities are at their best - teamwork helps us all grow and learn.
Say it, do it 
We deliver on our promises - we are all as good as our word. We are solutions focussed.
Make a difference 
In partnership with others, we achieve better outcomes. We believe everyone can make a difference in the lives of people, places and our planet.
Care deeply 
We are genuine and demonstrate care for our customers, our communities, our people, our places and our world. We deliver to a high standard.
We are privileged to be on a journey of growth as a business and supported by the best team!
We believe our people and the communities in which we live and work are at the heart of what we do. And quite simply, we couldn't do it without you.

What's in store for the future of Reflections Holidays?
With people at the heart of what we do, Reflections has a very exciting future ahead. 
While our strategic plan lays out a path for where Reflections hopes to be by 2030, our annual conference offers a whole team approach to our business going forward.
We have also released our first RAP, which we are incredibly proud to have been able to do, and are already planning our next one to make a greater commitment to reconciliation. 
Explore more about these great initiatives below:
Reconciliation Action Plan
In 2023, we launched our first Reflections Reconciliation Action Plan (RAP) and our second in late 2025. 
They explores how we will use our sphere of influence to advance respect, relationships and opportunities, contributing to more opportunities for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, now and for future generations.
Read our RAP 
We have big plans
Our 2030 Strategic Plan has an exciting vision to significantly boost regional tourism and economies with improved facilities, innovative accommodation, expanded nature-based attractions, local Aboriginal experiences, and more food and beverage offerings for guests.
