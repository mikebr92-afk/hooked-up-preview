Murray Cod, Australia's most iconic freshwater predator. Known for their impressive size and formidable strength, Murray Cod are the crown jewels of freshwater fishing, offering an unforgettable angling experience. 
Murray cod occupy a number of our lower high country streams. These clear waters, surrounded by the serene beauty of the High Country landscape are teeming with life, and provide the perfect habitat for Murray Cod and fly anglers. The challenge of targeting these elusive giants in such clear waters adds a thrilling dimension to your fishing adventure. Their cunning nature and the need for precise, stealthy approaches make every catch a rewarding accomplishment. 
We are also home to Lake Eildon, a renowned mecca for Murray Cod, boasting vast expanses of water where these mighty fish thrive. The lake's varied terrain, from deep channels to submerged timber, offers diverse fishing environments. Here, you can target trophy-sized Murray Cod using a range of fly fishing techniques. The anticipation of a aggressive strike from a hidden giant lurking in the depths keeps the adrenaline pumping. 
Murray Cod are not just a catch; they are a challenge that tests your skill, patience, and determination. Their explosive takes, powerful runs, and the sheer size make them a coveted prize for any fly angler. Whether you're a seasoned angler or new to fly fishing, the pursuit of Murray Cod promises an adventure filled with excitement and awe. 
Join us on a guided fishing trip and experience the thrill of fly fishing for Murray Cod in the clear waters of high country streams or the famous Lake Eildon. Let us guide you to these majestic fish and help you create unforgettable memories on the water. 
Drift Boating 
Full day Murray Cod Drift $ 900
Experience the ultimate angling adventure with our full day guided drift boating trip, targeting the mighty Murray Cod. This journey down some of Australia's most scenic and productive waterways offers a unique opportunity to catch the country's most iconic freshwater fish from the comfort and stealth of a drift boat. 
The Drift boating Experience 
Glide silently through the serene waters, accessing each and every part of the waterways, our drift boat provides an ideal platform for fly fishing, allowing you to cover vast stretches of river efficiently while offering a stable and comfortable base from which to cast. The boat's low profile and quiet presence ensure you remain unnoticed by the wary Murray Cod, enhancing your chances of a successful catch. 
Targeting Murray Cod 
Murray Cod are renowned for their size, strength, and aggressive strikes. Our experienced guides will share their extensive knowledge and techniques, helping you to effectively target these formidable fish. You'll learn to read the water, identify prime holding spots, and present your fly in a manner that entices even the most elusive cod. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a newcomer to fly fishing, our guides will tailor the experience to suit your skill level. 
What’s Included 
• 8 Hours drifting and fly fishing: for up to 2 anglers, cover large amounts of water in a single day and be put into positions within the river which are otherwise extremely difficult to access and cast in to 
• Expert Guidance: Benefit from the expertise of our professional guides who know the waters intimately and can provide valuable insights and tips.
• Top-Quality Gear: We provide high-quality fly fishing equipment, including rods, reels, flies, and all necessary accessories.
• Comfort and Convenience: Enjoy a day on the water in our well-equipped drift boat, designed for both comfort and optimal fishing conditions.
• Gourmet Lunch: Relish a delicious lunch prepared with fresh, local ingredients, keeping you energised for a full day of fishing.
• Stunning Scenery: Take in the breathtaking landscapes of the river and its surroundings, enhancing the overall experience of your fishing adventure.
Join us for an unforgettable full day of drift boating for Murray Cod, where adventure, tranquility, and the thrill of the catch come together to create a truly remarkable experience. Whether you seek the excitement of landing a trophy fish or the peacefulness of drifting through beautiful landscapes, this trip promises a day to remember.

Where? 
Where to fish for Murray Cod
North East Victoria is home to an abundance of productive Murray Cod waters, and we’re fortunate to guide on some of the region’s most exciting and scenic rivers. Whether you’re vacationing in popular destinations like Bright, Whitfield, Beechworth, Wodonga, Khancoban, or anywhere in between, we can tailor a Murray Cod adventure to fit your plans. 
With a range of accessible waters, each offering unique opportunities to chase this iconic species, we’re able to design trips that suit any itinerary or skill level. 
Get in touch with us to discuss the best options based on your location and schedule. No matter where you’re staying, we can create an unforgettable fishing experience, bringing you up close and personal with one of Australia’s most prized native fish. 
Tailored Trips
Interested in exploring several rivers over a few days? We offer custom multi-day trips that allow you to maximise your time on the water targeting Murray Cod. With each new day comes the opportunity to fish a different river, giving you a variety of environments and challenges. Plus, enjoy savings with exclusive discounts and benefits for booking multiple days. 
Get in touch to tailor your perfect multi-day fishing adventure and make the most of driftboating for Murray Cod in North East Victoria! 
Lake Eildon Fly fishing 
Lake Eildon Fly Fishing Package
Join the ultimate fly fishing adventure with our Lake Eildon Fly Fishing Package, as standard a 3-day, 2-night experience (customisable on request) that combines the thrill of targeting trophy-sized Murray Cod with the luxury and comfort of staying aboard our well-appointed houseboat. This unique package is perfect for anglers seeking both excitement and relaxation in one of Victoria's premier fishing destinations. 
Aboard the Houseboat 
Your accommodation for this adventure is our Lake Eildon houseboat, offering a perfect blend of comfort and convenience. With spacious living areas, modern amenities, and stunning views of the lake, you'll enjoy a cozy retreat after a day of fishing. The houseboat provides a unique vantage point, allowing you to wake up right on the water, ready to begin your fishing day. 
Fishing for Trophy Murray Cod 
Lake Eildon is renowned for its large Murray Cod population, and during the colder winter months in particular offering anglers the chance to catch trophy-sized fish. Over the course of three days, you'll venture out with our expert guides to some of the lake's most productive fishing spots. Whether its deep in the river inlets or along the banks of the main arm, our guides will share their extensive knowledge and techniques to help you successfully target these impressive fish. 
What's Included 
• Luxury Houseboat Accommodation: Enjoy two nights aboard our houseboat, featuring comfortable sleeping quarters, a fully equipped kitchen, and inviting living spaces.
• Fully Catered Stay: Guests with have all meals and non alcoholic beverages catered for during the course of their stay, savor delicious meals prepared with fresh, local ingredients, including hearty breakfasts, packed lunches for your fishing excursions, and gourmet dinners prepared by our chef aboard the houseboat. 
• High-Quality Equipment: We provide top-of-the-line fly fishing gear, including rods, reels, flies, and all necessary accessories to ensure a successful and enjoyable fishing experience.
• Expert Guidance: Benefit from the expertise of our professional guides who know the waters intimately and can provide valuable insights and tips. Guests will branch out from the houseboat aboard our specifically set up fishing boats for the days fishing
• Scenic Serenity: Experience the tranquil beauty of Lake Eildon, with its pristine waters and picturesque surroundings, offering a perfect backdrop for your fishing adventure.
Join us for a Lake Eildon fly fishing adventure that promises excitement, relaxation, and the opportunity to catch trophy Murray Cod. Whether you're an experienced angler or new to fly fishing, this package provides everything you need for an exceptional fishing experience. Don't miss the chance to combine luxury and adventure on the beautiful waters of Lake Eildon.

Itinerary 
Day 1
• Meet your guides in the early am, on the lake where we will immediately commence the days fishing. As you venture out your bags will be transported by one of our staff members to the houseboat ready for your arrival.
• Enjoy the day fishing where you will be able to enjoy warm coffee and a cooked breakfast/lunch on the boat
• After 8 hours fishing, the guides will transport guests to the houseboat where you will be able to organise your gear, have a shower and relax for the afternoon before being served a chef cooked dinner and enjoying on board the boat
Day 2
• The fishing day starts early at approx 5am to target prime bite times, guests will be prepared a cooked breakfast and have the option of having breakfast on board the house boat or taking it to eat "on the go" aboard the fishing boat
• Spend the morning again hunting large Murray Cod at the hands of our experienced guides
• Return to the house boat for lunch and some down time before spending the afternoon with guide and Back of Beyond owner Trent talking about and teaching some insights to tying Murray Cod flies, a great opportunity to try your hand at tying flies as well as all other things cod fishing
Day 3
• Day 3 is scheduled the same as day 2.
• Guests will be packed up for "Check out"/Departure as they leave to go fishing 
• After the days fishing guests will be transported back to their vehicles where your luggage will be waiting for you and your ultimate Murray Cod trip will conclude
Lake Eildon Guiding Season 
May - August
Lake Eildon is one of the largest artificial lakes in Victoria, Australia. It covers an area of approximately 13,800 hectares (34,100 acres) and has a capacity of 3,334,158 megaliters (883,000 acre-feet) when full.
