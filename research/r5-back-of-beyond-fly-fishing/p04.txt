There is one aspect of Fly Fishing in the Victorian high country that can not be matched. The remote and wild rivers that occupy the High Country offer so much more to your typical day fly fishing. 
The sights, the sounds, the water and the solitude experienced by spending a day on a High Country river can often eclipse the fishing itself! However we want to combine the two and show you some of the special places and special fishing the High Country has to offer. 
Based out of Mansfield, the gateway to the Victorian High Country, We tailor guided Fly Fishing trips to the many enchanting High Country rivers for anglers at all levels of experience. We also cater to those who maybe be camping or 4WDing through the area, a great way to make the most of your time in the area and on the river! 
Led by guide Trent, who has unrivalled knowledge and experience of the High Country and its Fly Fishing opportunities all around the Mansfield area, you'll be in the best hands possible. Get in touch to discuss your fishing adventure and plan a trip to some of the most scenic trout streams the High Country has to offer, We will be sure to make a trip that delivers much more than just enjoyable fishing!

Full Day
Join us for a full day of guided fishing in the captivating Victorian High Country. Our comprehensive package ensures a seamless and unforgettable experience from start to finish. 
Convenient Pick-up: We'll begin the day by picking you up from your preferred location in Mansfield, Whitfield, or a pre-determined surrounding area. Our goal is to make your journey as comfortable and convenient as possible, allowing you to relax and enjoy the anticipation of the day ahead. 
Tailored Fishing Locations: Our experienced guide will assess various factors, including your desired fishing experience, time of year, prevailing weather conditions, physical fitness, and your fly fishing experience. Based on these considerations, we will determine the optimal fishing location for the day. With up to 60 minutes of scenic driving, we'll transport you to the perfect spot to maximise your chances of success. 
Extensive On-Water Time: You'll have ample time to immerse yourself in the day of fly fishing, with approximately 8 hours of on-water time. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced angler, our guide will provide personalised attention, offering tips and guidance to enhance your technique and increase your chances of landing that prized trout. 
Premium Equipment and Gear: We believe in providing the best for our guests. That's why we offer top-of-the-line equipment from leading industry brands. You'll be equipped with high-quality waders and wading boots to ensure comfort and safety in the water. Our carefully selected rods, flies, and tackle are designed to optimise your fishing experience and increase your chances of success. 
Nourishment and Refreshments: To keep you energised throughout the day, we provide a delicious lunch, snacks, and ample fresh water. Our menu is thoughtfully prepared to satisfy your appetite and fuel your angling adventures. 
Fly Casting Tuition: Whether you're a beginner looking to learn the fundamentals or an experienced angler aiming to refine your skills, our guide will provide comprehensive fly casting tuition. Benefit from personalised instruction tailored to your skill level, helping you improve your casting accuracy and presentation. 
Photography: We understand the importance of preserving your unforgettable moments on the water. Our guide, Trent, will ensure that your experience is thoroughly documented through a collection of high-quality photos and videos. Relive the excitement, tranquility, and triumphs of the day, allowing these cherished memories to last a lifetime.

Drift Boating
Drift Boating
Multi Day with High Country Camp
Victoria and southern New South Wales are home to a selection of breathtaking and highly productive tailrace rivers that offer some of Australia’s best trout fishing. These rivers, fed by upstream dams and reservoirs, typically feature larger, deeper waters than traditional trout streams, making them ideal for driftboat fishing. Unlike walk-and-wade fishing, driftboating allows us to cover substantial stretches of river over the course of a day, navigating prime fishing waters and accessing areas that would otherwise be difficult, if not impossible, to reach. Driftboating has quickly become one of our most popular and exhilarating fishing options, perfect for those looking to maximize their time on the water and immerse themselves in the river's stunning natural surroundings. 
A Unique and Immersive Experience 
Driftboating offers a truly unique way to enjoy trout fishing in Australia. Seated in our specially-designed fishing drift boat, you’ll have an unmatched vantage point, gliding quietly down the river as you soak in the sights, sounds, and serenity of the river ecosystem. Unlike bank fishing or wading, driftboating allows anglers to experience the river from a different perspective, creating a sense of immersion as you float downstream, For both experienced anglers and beginners, it’s an adventure that combines the thrill of fishing with the tranquility of being on the water. 
Incredibly Effective Fishing 
Driftboating isn’t just about the experience – it’s an incredibly effective way to fish. The ability to cover large sections of river means we can explore a variety of water types in a single trip, from deep runs and pools to shallower riffles and undercut banks where trout love to hide. With the boat providing effortless mobility, we’re able to position ourselves perfectly for each cast, adapting to the river’s flow and targeting areas where trout are most likely to be holding. This approach significantly increases your chances of finding active fish and gives you the opportunity to hone your skills on different types of water. 
Explore Some of Australia’s Most Picturesque Trout Waters 
Driftboating offers access to some of Australia’s most scenic and productive trout rivers, from the lush river valleys of the Goulburn Valley and North East Victoria to the wide-open waters of southern New South Wales. As we float downriver, you’ll be surrounded by incredible natural beauty – from towering river gums and lush native vegetation to occasional glimpses of local wildlife. Driftboating allows anglers to fully appreciate the landscapes and pristine environments of Australia’s trout fishing hotspots, adding a scenic element to an already rewarding fishing experience. 
Multi Day with High Country Camp
Multi Day with High Country Camp
Multi Day with High Country Camp
Our multi-day fishing adventure is designed for those who want to immerse themselves fully in the beauty of the Victorian High Country and maximise their time on the water. Combining multiple days of fishing with an overnight camp out, this package offers a truly unforgettable experience. 
Fully Catered Convenience: When you choose our multi-day adventure, you can leave the logistics to us. We provide everything you need for a comfortable and enjoyable camping experience, including camping gear, food, and beverages. You can relax and focus on the fishing, knowing that all your needs are taken care of. 
Customisable Options: We understand that every angler has unique preferences and requirements. That's why we offer customisable options for our multi-day adventure. Whether you prefer a fully catered experience or a partial BYO (bring your own) option, we are happy to discuss and accommodate your needs. Get in touch with us to tailor the package to your preferences. 
Continuation of Full Day Fishing: During the multi-day adventure, all aspects of our full day fishing experience remain the same. You'll enjoy a minimum of 8 hours of on-water time, often more guided by Trent. We provide top-of-the-line equipment, including waders, wading boots, rods, flies, and tackle. 
Late Evening Fishing and Dry Fly Hatches: Camping out overnight provides a unique opportunity to extend your fishing experience into the late evening. As the sun sets over the high country, you can continue fishing and potentially witness a late afternoon dry fly hatch. During the summer months, spinners and grasshoppers may emerge, creating an exciting feeding frenzy for the trout. This magical experience adds an extra layer of excitement and adventure to your trip. 
Get the Most Out of Your Time: Our multi-day adventure allows you to get the most out of your time with a guide. By combining fishing with camping, you can fully immerse yourself in the tranquility of the high country, enjoying the peacefulness of the surroundings and the camaraderie of fellow anglers. It's a chance to disconnect from the hustle and bustle of daily life and reconnect with nature in its purest form.

Multi Day Backpacking
Our multi-day backpacking adventure takes you on a journey into the remote and self-sufficient realms of the Victorian High Country. Leave behind the beaten path as you hike your way through closed 4WD track gates or along secluded river valleys, immersing yourself in a truly remote environment. This is the epitome of adventure, allowing you to go beyond the typical fishing locations and experience the untamed beauty of the backcountry. 
Immerse Yourself in the Remote: With your supplies packed snugly in your backpack, you'll embark on a thrilling exploration of the backcountry. Our carefully curated trips range from 2 to 4 days, offering a variety of productive locations to suit your preferences. Some routes follow defined hiking trails, while others take you completely off the grid, immersing you in untouched wilderness. 
Truly Self-Sufficient: Backpacking provides the ultimate self-sufficiency, allowing you to disconnect from the modern world and rely on your own skills and resources. As you venture into the depths of the high country, you'll carry everything you need to sustain yourself, including camping gear, food, and fly fishing equipment. This self-sufficiency adds an extra layer of fulfilment and adventure to your experience. 
Exploring Uncharted Waters: By venturing off the beaten path, you'll discover hidden gems and pristine fishing locations that are rarely frequented by anglers. These secluded spots offer the perfect opportunity to encounter wild and elusive trout, providing a sense of accomplishment that comes from fishing in untouched waters. Prepare to be captivated by the raw beauty of nature and the tranquility of these remote fishing spots. 
A True Wilderness Experience: The essence of our name, "Back of Beyond Fly Fishing," comes to life during our multi-day backpacking adventure. Pushing deep into the backcountry, you'll experience the thrill of adventure, solitude, and connection with the natural world. Far away from the hustle and bustle of civilization, you'll immerse yourself in an unspoiled environment that awakens your senses and rejuvenates your spirit. 
Unleash Your Adventurous Spirit: Backpacking is not just about fishing; it's a holistic adventure that challenges your physical endurance, navigational skills, and mental fortitude. As you hike through diverse landscapes and conquer rugged terrains, you'll gain a profound appreciation for the untamed wilderness.

Take the scenic route as we whisk you away in a helicopter to rivers that are typically inaccessible by vehicle. Unparalleled Access: By utilising a helicopter, we can transport you to remote and pristine fishing spots that are otherwise difficult to reach. These hidden gems, tucked away in the heart of the high country, offer waters teeming with trout. As we fly over breathtaking landscapes, you'll witness the vastness and grandeur of the region, gaining a newfound appreciation for its natural wonders. 
Optimised On-Water Time: With the convenience of helicopter access, you'll enjoy maximum on-water time. By eliminating long drives or hikes to the fishing location, you'll have more hours to cast your line and immerse yourself in the thrill of angling. This optimized on-water time allows for an enhanced fishing experience and increases your chances of encountering that elusive trout. 
Diverse Fly Fishing Experience: While the use of a helicopter to access remote spots doesn't guarantee more or bigger fish, it offers a diverse take on fishing in the Victorian High Country. The aerial perspective and the journey itself add an element of excitement and adventure to your day. You'll witness the beauty of untouched wilderness, soar above rugged terrains, and land in idyllic fishing locations that are off the beaten path. 
Unforgettable Scenic Flights: The helicopter ride itself is an experience to remember. Enjoy panoramic views of the high country as you glide through the sky, capturing bird's-eye views of majestic mountains, winding rivers, and sprawling valleys. Expert Guidance and Safety: Our experienced guide Trent will accompany you throughout the adventure, ensuring your safety and providing expert guidance. You can fish with confidence, knowing that you are in the hands of a skilled professional.
