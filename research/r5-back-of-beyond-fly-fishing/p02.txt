Frequently Asked Questions 
Please reach us at info@backofbeyondflyfishing.com if you cannot find an answer to your question. 
Do I need any prior experience? No, we offer guided trips for first timers through to experienced anglers. Tuition is fully included in the days guiding and we love teaching beginners how to fly fish! 
Do I need any specific equipment? No, everything you need for a comfortable and safe day on the water is supplied. We provide top quality waders, wading boots, rods and accessories as part of the guiding cost. We will give you some details on the most appropriate and recommended clothing to wear as part of our pre trip planning 
Where will we go? The location we fish will be determined by a number of factors. firstly we take into account your desired experience and what it is you want out of your days guiding. additionally we consider your fly casting experience and ability, physical fitness and ability, and of course what Trent determines is the best location for that time of year and the weather on the day 
How many people can come? Guide prices stated allow for up to 2 anglers, this is logistically the most practical as any more than this and your fishing time will be significantly diluted between each angler. Additional anglers can be included in specific scenarios in consultation with us. 
Can we return to fish in the location we were guided? Absolutely, all rivers we guide on are public and you may wish to return by yourself to put into practice the skills you learnt with your guide. It is however important to respect the location, practice catch & release, and rest the spot adequately, and not go back day after day to flog the location so that the trout population can be appropriately maintained 
Will there be phone reception? Most of the time there will not be any mobile phone reception. We will give indicative pick up and drop off times so that you can communicate this with anyone as needed. Whilst we do travel into locations with no cell service, Trent has a Garmin in-reach with satelite messaging and SOS functionality in case of emergency and always leaves a detailed plan of the day and expected return time with an emergency contact who monitors this. 
Can we keep the fish we catch? Keeping the fish for consumption or taxidermy is prohibited on our guided trips. We encourage catch and release angling to preserve and improve the trout population. we recommend trying one of our surrounding lakes or pondages that are regularly stocked if you wish to keep your catch 
Got more questions? Get in touch now, we would love to have a chat!
