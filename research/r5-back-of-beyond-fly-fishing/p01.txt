BACK OF BEYOND FLY FISHING 
At Back of Beyond Fly Fishing, we offer more than just a typical day on the water. Our expansive range of fly fishing experiences is designed to immerse you in some of the most breathtaking and diverse fishing landscapes in Victoria. 
From the remote and wild Trout rivers of the Victorian High Country to the clearwater lowland rivers where our drift boating expeditions offer an exciting way to target the elusive Murray Cod. Or maybe its fishing for trophy size Cod in the famous Lake Eildon, We have trips tailored for all skilled beveled fly anglers. 
Our expertise extends beyond local waters. Join us on hosted trips to iconic fly fishing locales such as New Zealand's South Island, Alaska's vast wilderness, and the remote salt water flats of Cape York. Each journey is meticulously planned to ensure an unforgettable experience, from travel arrangements to on-the-ground guidance. 
Led by experienced guide Trent, who possesses a wealth of knowledge of Australia's diverse fishing environments, you'll be in expert hands. Whether you're a seasoned angler or a beginner, we tailor our trips to match your skill level and aspirations, ensuring a memorable and rewarding fly fishing adventure. 
Get in touch with us to plan your next fishing adventure and experience the wonders of fly fishing with Back of Beyond Fly Fishing. Join us to explore the world's most stunning and productive waters, creating memories that go far beyond just a great day of fishing. 
Victoria is home to Australias best Murray Cod fishing & some of its most scenic trout fishing locations
