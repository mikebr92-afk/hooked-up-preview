Arnhem land Fishing Charters at the Endyalgout Barra Camp
Overview
We are super excited to have access again to one of our old stomping grounds, the Arnhem Land Barra Camp on the Endyalgout Island.
It’s been a few years since we have used the camp but we have been fishing area (Mini Mini System) on our Mothership Charters now our Arnhem Land Fishing Charters utlise our camp on Endyalgout Island.
With over 20 years in existence the Barra camp and the area is a well known Big Barra destination. We just love fishing this area and are sure you will also.
Arnhem land Fishing Charters at the Endyalgout Barra Camp
Situated on a shell grit beach are a combination of metal huts and bungalows, these are designed to catch the sea breezes and are fitted with comfortable bedding, fans & air-conditioners that ensure a good nights sleep after a hard days fishing and a meal of Barra and mud crabs. Each hut is setup for 2, 3 or 4 people, single bed configurations depending on who’s at the camp at the time.
Common areas include a substantial mess area with indoor and outdoor under cover dining and lounge areas. The kitchen is incorporated in this area.
Flushing toilets and showers with great water pressure are in a separate building.
It’s pretty unique to have a set up where you can land light aircraft a couple of minutes walk from camp and the boats are moored on the beach where the camp is situated. This certainly minimises any mucking around and maximises time spent fishing.

The Fishing
Barramundi is the prime target and we consider this a big Barra hotspot. There is a fantastic variety of fishing options. Cast lures to feeding schools of Trevally and Queenfish. Threadfin salmon patrol mangrove channels and drains. Estuary cod and Groper lurk in holes and rock ledges to pounce on passing lure with savage strikes and bulldogging fights. Golden Snapper here grow big and mean, the way they eat lures we are not sure if they love them or hate them. Black Jewfish here are not uncommon on lures while trolling for barra.
All our safari boats are 5.5 metre to 6 metres in length and run 4-stroke environmentally friendly outboards. The Boats are built to government regulations. Our professional guides are fully accredited with many years of experience fishing the tropical waterways of the Northern Territory and no one knows this area better. 
River and estuary species include: BARRAMUNDI, THREADFIN & BLUE SALMON, QUEENFISH, GOLDEN SNAPPER, ESTUARY COD, QUEENSLAND GROPER AND MORE. 
Saltwater and ocean species: QUEENFISH, GOLDEN & GIANT TREVALLY. 
When to go
Our remote Arnhem Land Barra camp operates between June and November each year with the most comfortable weather being between June and August. Like all tidal Top End destinations, the periods closer to the wet tend to be more reliable a greater chance of trophy fish. Tours are organised to coincide with the preferred moon phases & tides.
Getting There
The Mini Mini River / Endyalgout Island Remote Camp is a scenic 50 minute flight from Darwin along the coast and you will be greeted with the sight of a complex extensive mangrove system with islands and coral reefs scattered on the seaward side Your aircraft will land on one of these islands only 5 minutes from camp.
Meals
The camp has a large kitchen and dining area with fans, 240 volt power supply, ample fridges and freezers. Just because you are in the middle of no where doesn’t mean you have to miss out on a good feed. Mud Crabs that can cost $40 a serve in restaurants and Barra are always on offer. 
You will also be pleased with great BBQ meals and roast dinners. Make sure you get the chef to whip up some of his famous beer battered Barra. 
Finish the day sitting around the dining table swapping yarns with a cold drink or two before crashing in your comfortable bed to rest before doing it all again the next day. 
In the morning after a good breakfast which can comprise cereals, toast or a cooked meal you will fish until a lunch that usually comprises sandwiches made fresh aboard but can include treats like chilled mud crab (If you’ve left any from the last nights feast).

Luggage Limits 
Exact baggage limit will be advised when light aircraft is booked however due to light aircraft travel, it is likely to be around 10kg per person, and the maximum length of a rod tube must not exceed 100cm.
Fishing Gear 
All fishing tackle is supplied on a replace if lost or broken basis.
If you would like to bring your own gear you are more than welcome. Due to a rod tube limit of 1metre it will need to be a travel or 2 piece outfit.
Can I take fish home 
No. This is a sport fishing destination, we actively promote catch and release fishing. We do however keep enough fish & crabs for evening meals, with all surplus fish being released.
Alcohol 
We will ask for your order prior to the trip. We will purchase on your behalf and have ready for you at the camp. You just fix us up for what it cost us.

Run Off Barra Fishing Kakadu 
Darwins North Australian Barra Fishing offer extended Run Off Barra Fishing charters from 2 to 14 days, tailored to meet your personal requirements.
Wet season run off Barra fishing in the tidal rivers of Kakadu during March, April and May offer some of the best barra fishing in the Top End and a great fishing adventure. Fishing the magnificent rivers in Kakadu National Park the South and East Alligator Rivers you also have the option to fish the reef out the front of these rivers for Golden Snapper & Black Jew Fish on the smaller tides.
Only licensed guides can fish the world heritage listed National Park and we have met the stringent criteria imposed to be one of the few guides fishing the park. .
Be part of the landscape most Territory visitors never experience.
See the renowned wildlife - the ancient crocodiles and vast majority of the birdlife.
Visit the beautiful sights of Kakadu National Park with its unique scenery

Darwin Harbour Fishing Charters 
Darwin Harbour Fishing
We are blessed to have a harbour on our doorsteps that offers some great fishing charter options.
Within 15 minutes from your hotel pickup you can be on the water and fishing. A normal day will see you on the water for around 7-7.5 hours. Times will vary depending on the tides.
Depending on tides on our Darwin Harbour Fishing Charters there are a variety of options and species to target.
On the bigger tides we will fish predominatly for Barra but also may mix up the day chasing some queenfish. On the smaller tides chasing long tail tuna and Mackerel comes into it’s own.
We can advise what present the best fishing opportunities for you when you are looking at booking your trip.

Barra Fishing Day Tours, Corroboree Billabong 
Corroboree Billabong is one of our favorite fishing locations. A Corroboree Barra fishing trip calls for an early morning departure from Darwin having you arriving at the billabong around sunrise. Enjoy the spectacular sights of the Top End wildlife whilst fishing from our shaded custom-built fishing vessels. This is an opportunity to take on the challenge of fishing in the Top End.
Enjoy a lunch of cold meat and salad luncheons. Lunch is served on board. We also provide all light non-alcoholic refreshments and cold water during the day.
We cater for amateur fishermen as well as the experienced anglers. We encourage the use of catch-and-release techniques on our tours, but you are welcome to keep some fish in line with NT Fisheries bag-limits. Our Guide will be only too pleased to take photographs of your catch to remember this rewarding and unique fishing opportunity.
Our experienced tour guides will point out flora and fauna highlights during the trip and stop for photo opportunities. Our company welcomes inquiries on unique packages to suit corporate customers and VIP visitors.
On completion of your tour you will be returned to your accommodation in Darwin by air conditioned 4-wheel drive vehicle arriving after 3.30pm.
