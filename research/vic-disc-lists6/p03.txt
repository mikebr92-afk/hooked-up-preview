Portland - Shoreline Charters
Explore Portland’s coastline with Shoreline Charters on a wildlife cruise or fishing trip. Wildlife tours visit places such as Lawrence Rocks, the Cape Bridgewater Seal Caves and Deen Maar (Julia Percy Island). Depending on the trip, you may see Australian fur seals, gannets and other seabirds, or dolphins. Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed.
Cruises range from two-hour trips to Lawrence Rocks to four-hour trips to Cape Bridgewater or Deen Maar. Fishing charters include shorter bay trips and full-day offshore trips.
Opening Hours:
Trips operate by booking. Check availability and book ahead.
