Included 
Prices include all your gear
All prices include everything you may need for the day: equipment for trolling or fly fishing, waders and boots, lunch, snacks and refreshments. State-of-the-art rods and reels are supplied. All you need to do is turn up ready to fish.
What to bring 
A few things to pack
• Warm and wet weather clothing
• Broad-brimmed hat and sun protection
• Polarised sunglasses
• A current NSW or Tasmanian fishing licence (apply online)
Ways to fish Choose your kind of day
Not sure which suits you? Get in touch and I will help you pick.
Flyfishing Charter
A guided flyfishing charter is the fastest way to get onto trout in the Snowy Mountains lakes and, over summer, the shallow lagoons of Tasmania's Central Highlands. Whether you have never held a fly rod or you have fished for years, we fit the day to you.
Read more 
Trout Trolling Charter
Trolling is a relaxed, social and very effective way to fish the big Snowy Mountains lakes. It suits families, groups and mixed-ability anglers, because everyone can join in and everyone gets a fair chance at a trout.
Read more 
Guided Flyfishing and Personal Trout Guide
A one-on-one day is the best way to make real progress with your flyfishing. It is just you and me, the whole day built around what you want to learn and the pace that suits you.
Read more 
Brown Trout Guide
Wild brown trout are the fish that keep me coming back. They are cagey, they reward a careful angler, and stalking them in shallow water is some of the finest trout fishing anywhere in the Snowy Mountains and Tasmania.
Read more 
Rainbow Trout Guide
If you want a trout that fights hard and does not give up, the rainbows of the Snowy Mountains are your fish. Hooked in open water they run, they jump and they test your gear all the way to the net.
Read more

The Troutfit Guiding 
The Troutfit Guiding is based in Cooma, in the heart of the Snowy Mountains NSW. We host Flyfishing and Trolling trips on Lake Eucumbene and Lake Jindabyne, and during summer in the Central Highlands of Tasmania.
