About Us A local guide who knows this country
The Troutfit Guiding is a Cooma-based trout fishing guide working the Snowy Mountains lakes and rivers and, each summer, the Central Highlands of Tasmania.
Fly, troll or lure. First-timers to seasoned anglers. Fully surveyed boats and all the gear supplied. Whatever you are after, the day is built around you and the water is fished by someone who has spent a lifetime on it.

The Troutfit Guiding 
The Troutfit Guiding is based in Cooma, in the heart of the Snowy Mountains NSW. We host Flyfishing and Trolling trips on Lake Eucumbene and Lake Jindabyne, and during summer in the Central Highlands of Tasmania.
