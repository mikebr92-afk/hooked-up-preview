A day on the water with Troutfit Guiding, in our guests' own words.
★★★★★ 
5.0 
on Google 
★★★★★
Mick is a great guide and a knowledgeable local to hear stories from.
He arrived promptly at 7:30am on Saturday morning and launched the boat before our arrival. The boat is super clean, and it gave us such a welcoming feeling to get onboard.
The day we chose was hot, meaning a tough day for fishing. But Mick was very patient, cruising around multiple spots and telling us interesting stories of the local community.
We finally got 5 trout to finish a nice morning. It was a very enjoyable experience.
Chen &middot; 2024

The Troutfit Guiding 
The Troutfit Guiding is based in Cooma, in the heart of the Snowy Mountains NSW. We host Flyfishing and Trolling trips on Lake Eucumbene and Lake Jindabyne, and during summer in the Central Highlands of Tasmania.
