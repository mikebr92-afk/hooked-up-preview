Enjoy the boat to yourself on a Half Day Private Charter!
Want to know the tidal conditions for barra fishing or reef fishing in Darwin Harbour? We’ve got you covered.

Highlights 
On our private charter, you will be comfortable on board our sturdy boats with walk around deck, shade canopy and fully surveyed.
Your experienced guide will strategically navigate varied fishing spots, depending on tide and fishing conditions, to maximize your chances of a successful catch.
From baiting your hook to filleting your fish for take-home, our team is dedicated to ensuring an enjoyable experience. Please note we adhere strictly to Northern Territory fishing regulations and bag limits.
FAQ 
Q 1: How do I choose between reef fishing and barra fishing? 
Reef Fishing 
If you’re looking for fast-paced action and the thrill of catching a variety of fish in a single trip, reef fishing is your best bet. You’ll enjoy excitement as you reel in species like coral trout, red emperor, and snapper.
Barra Fishing 
If you want to test your casting skills and target trophy-sized barramundi, barra fishing is the way to go. This adventure challenges anglers to land one of Australia’s most iconic sportfish in its natural habitat.
Q 2:Why 6 Hours Beats 4 
Darwin’s tides shift dramatically — often 4–5m in just a few hours — so timing is everything. A 4-hour trip usually only catches one phase of the tide cycle, while a 6-hour trip lets you fish across both the run-in and run-out, hitting more active feeding windows. It also gives access to more fishing grounds as water levels change, more time on the water relative to fixed travel time, and room to adapt if conditions or the bite shift. Simply put: more tide, more ground, more fish, less rush.
Contact us to check the tidal condition, and we’ll help you pick the perfect experience!
