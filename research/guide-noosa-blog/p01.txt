Offshore Fishing in Noosa: A Guide to the Best Seasons and Species
From tasty reef fish to a huge range of game species, it’s no wonder that Noosa has cemented itself as a premier destination for deep-sea fishing. And with a number of different fishing locations to explore, fishing offshore Noosa offers a variety of exciting experiences for anglers of all skill levels. That said, different seasons and conditions lend themselves to several key species found off the Sunshine Coast.
So, if you’re looking to secure your spot on an offshore fishing charter leaving from Noosa, we’re here to break down the best time to go fishing based on what fish species you’re hoping to land.
Why go fishing offshore in Noosa?
The ocean off Noosa and the Sunshine Coast is home to a long stretch of scenic coastline and deep waters, offering a diverse range of fish species. With a number of abundant reefs scattered just off the coast, including North Reef, Sunshine Reef, Chardons Reef and the Barwon Banks, Noosa is the ideal location for both reef and deep-sea fishing adventures.
Boasting sunny weather and ideal fishing conditions year-round, Noosa really is one of the best places to experience the thrill of offshore fishing.
Best seasons for offshore fishing in Noosa
Each season brings something different to the waters off Noosa. Whether you’re hoping to target sailfish, wahoo or coral trout, here’s a breakdown of fishing by season in Noosa.
Summer (December – February)
The warm summer waters draw in fast pelagics, making it the ideal time for game fishing. That said, you might still be lucky enough to hook a deep reef species that prefers cooler waters. Here’s what’s biting over summer:
• Spanish mackerel and yellowfin tuna
• Mahi-mahi (dolphinfish)
• Marlin (black and blue)
• Sailfish
• Coral trout
• Sweetlip and grass emperor
Autumn (March – May)
While the waters are still warm from summer, autumn is a transitional time that brings with it a mix of summer and winter species. The pelagics are still firing, and the calmer seas make for a great day on the water. Here’s what you can hook over autumn:
• Longtail tuna
• Spotted and Spanish mackerel
• Wahoo
• Mahi-mahi (dolphinfish)
• Snapper, pearl perch, coral trout, sweetlip and Moses perch
• Cobia
Winter (June – August)
Winter brings with it cool, crisp mornings, glassy water and sunny days, making it one of the best times of year for offshore fishing. With reef species more active, it’s the perfect time to land:
• Snapper
• Red emperor
• Sweetlip
• Moses perch
• Tuskfish, cod and coral trout (in deeper reef areas)
Spring (September – November)
As the waters warm once again, early pelagics are on the move, chasing bait closer to the coast. That said, the weather can be unpredictable with rough seas and increased storm activity, so the windows for offshore fishing charters can be narrow. Here’s what biting:
• Tuna and mackerel
• Snapper, coral trout and emperors
• Cobia
• Spangled emperor and larger cod or trout (as reef fish push out)
• Wahoo and mahi-mahi
Tips for a successful offshore fishing charter from Noosa 
Want to get the most out of your offshore fishing adventure? Follow these simple tips to help you get the most out of your charter:
• Set off early: Early morning departures are usually best. Enjoy calmer seas, cooler air and more fish activity.
• Check forecasts carefully: Time your offshore fishing adventures with the weather, especially in summer when there’s a higher chance of tropical storms. It’s common for deep-sea charters to be cancelled if winds exceed 15–20 knots or swells build above 2m.
• Choose professional offshore fishing charters: Opt for an experienced, local charter operator, like Noosa Bluewater Charters . With three custom-built charter vessels, an experienced skipper and crew, and a huge range of charters to choose from, we offer unforgettable reef and game fishing adventures.
• Use the right gear: From bait and boats to rods and tackle, it’s essential to tailor your fishing gear to the species you’re targeting. One of the bonuses of booking with Noosa Bluewater Charters is that we provide all the gear. In fact, our crew will even bait your hooks and clean your legal catch for you so you can focus on the fun of deep-sea fishing.
• Be sun safe: The offshore sun can be unforgiving, especially when it’s reflected back off the water. Be sure to stay hydrated and use plenty of sun protection, including SPF 50+, long sleeves, a hat and sunglasses.
FAQs
Do I need a license for fishing offshore in Noosa?
No, you don’t need a license for recreational, deep-sea fishing off Noosa or the Sunshine Coast. That said, you still need to abide by recreational fishing rules , including size and possession limits. The team at Noosa Bluewater Charters are across Queensland requirements, so we’ll be able to help you navigate the rules if you jump on one of our offshore fishing charters .
What’s the best month for deep-sea fishing in Noosa?
The best time for offshore fishing from Noosa really depends on the type of fish you’re trying to catch. The period from May through to October is generally considered the best window for reef and deep-sea fishing. While reef species tend to be more active over the cooler months, you can still enjoy the thrill of game fishing from September onwards.
Can beginners go offshore fishing in Noosa?
Absolutely! At Noosa Bluewater Charters, we welcome guests of all skill levels, from novices through to seasoned anglers. Our experienced skippers know exactly where to go to land the best catch and our friendly deckies are on hand throughout the charter to help you land quality fish. Whether you’re an individual, family or group booking, you’re sure to have a great day on the water with Noosa Bluewater Charters.
What should I bring on a Noosa offshore fishing trip?
Noosa Bluewater Charters supplies all the gear you’ll need, including rods, reels, bait and tackle, plus the expertise to help you make the most of your trip. All that’s left to pack is:
• Hat, sunglasses and reef-safe sunscreen,
• Light, long-sleeve clothing,
• A waterproof jacket,
• A camera or phone,
• Esky (optional),
• Seasickness tablets, and
• Snacks or special drinks.
When it comes to offshore fishing charters from Noosa, you can expect incredible conditions and catches year-round. That said, it helps to understand what’s biting across the seasons, especially if you’re chasing a specific fish species.
