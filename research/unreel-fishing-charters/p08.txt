KING GEORGE WHITING CHARTERS
Victoria's Finest Table Fish
Renowned for their incredible eating quality and found in excellent numbers, King George Whiting provide fun, action-packed fishing for anglers of all ages and experience levels. 
Book Your Whiting Charter
Target Victoria's Favourite Table Fish
King George Whiting are widely regarded as one of the finest eating fish found anywhere in Australia and are a favourite target among anglers of all skill levels. Not only are they exceptional on the plate, but they are also great fun to catch and are often found in impressive numbers throughout Port Phillip Bay and Western Port Bay.
At Unreel Fishing Charters, we target King George Whiting year-round, giving our guests the opportunity to enjoy action-packed fishing and take home some of Victoria's best seafood.
Western Port Bay King George Whiting
From January through to April, we target King George Whiting in Melbourne's Western Port Bay, departing from Hastings and Stony Point.
These charters are often highly productive, with quality whiting regularly caught alongside a variety of other species that add to the excitement. Whether you're an experienced angler or trying fishing for the first time, Western Port whiting charters offer a fantastic day on the water.

Port Phillip Bay King George Whiting
From April through to October, our focus shifts to Port Phillip Bay, with departures commonly from Mornington, Rye and Sorrento.
While the cooler months can bring fresh conditions, the fishing is often outstanding. This time of year also provides the opportunity to combine King George Whiting fishing with Southern Calamari fishing, creating an action-packed charter and an incredible seafood feast to take home.
