SNAPPER CHARTERS 
Experience Melbourne's most exciting fishing adventure and battle hard-fighting trophy snapper in the rich waters of Port Phillip Bay and Westerport Bay. 
Book Your Snapper Charter
MELBOURNE’S MOST EXCITING FISHING ADVENTURE 
Snapper are without a doubt one of the most exciting fish found in Melbourne waters. Every year during October, November and December, anglers from across Victoria eagerly await the arrival of snapper season—and for good reason.
Known for their powerful runs, aggressive strikes and incredible eating quality, Port Phillip Bay snapper provide an unforgettable fishing experience for anglers of all skill levels. At Unreel Fishing Charters, we've spent decades targeting these iconic fish and helping our clients experience the thrill of catching a genuine "Big Red".
Even after catching thousands of snapper over the years, we still get a thrill every time a rod buckles over and a quality fish powers away from the boat. It's a feeling that never gets old.

WHEN IS SNAPPER SEASON? 
SEASON : October - December
Snapper season in Port Phillip Bay typically begins to fire up from mid to late October, when large schools of fish move into the bay and become increasingly active. As water temperatures rise, anglers eagerly await the first reports of quality fish, marking the start of one of Victoria's most anticipated fishing seasons.
November is traditionally the peak month, with excellent fishing and consistent catches often experienced throughout the bay. As one of the busiest times of the year for Melbourne fishing charters, popular dates can book out quickly, so early bookings are highly recommended
