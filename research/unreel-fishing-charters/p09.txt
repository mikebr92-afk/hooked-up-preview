Southern Calamari Charters 
Fun. Delicious. Perfect For The Whole Family.
Book Your Calamari Charter
Fun, Action-Packed Fishing for the Whole Family
Southern Calamari are one of Victoria's most popular and enjoyable species to target. Easy to learn, exciting to catch and absolutely delicious to eat, they are the perfect option for families, beginners and experienced anglers alike. 
One of the best things about calamari fishing is that almost anyone can do it. Whether you're introducing the kids to fishing for the first time or simply looking for a fun day on the water, Southern Calamari provide plenty of action and a great chance to take home some of the finest seafood Victoria has to offer. 
When Is the Best Time to Fish?
Like many species, calamari are often most active during low-light periods. For this reason, we like to tailor our trips around the first few hours of daylight and the last few hours before dark, maximising your chances of success. 
Our flexible charter options allow us to fish when the conditions are most favourable and the calamari are feeding. 
Why Fish for Southern Calamari?
Southern Calamari are not only fantastic on the plate, but they also make exceptional bait for larger species such as Snapper, Kingfish and Sharks. 
They are aggressive feeders, exciting to catch and often found in excellent numbers, making them one of the most reliable species available throughout the year. 
Fishing With Unreel Fishing Charters
We target Southern Calamari year-round, primarily throughout Melbourne's Port Phillip Bay. During spring, we also venture into Western Port Bay in search of larger breeding calamari, which can provide some outstanding fishing opportunities. 
Having spent many years targeting these unique and fascinating creatures, our skippers have developed proven techniques and local knowledge that consistently produce excellent results. More often than not, our customers enjoy outstanding catches and plenty of action throughout the day. 
Combine Your Charter
Depending on the season and conditions, we can combine your calamari charter with other top Victorian fish species.
For example, on a 5-hour charter you might spend the first part of the trip targeting calamari before switching to snapper or whiting for the remainder of the day—all for the same charter price.
