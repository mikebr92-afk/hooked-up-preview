BLUEFIN TUNA FISHING 
Victoria has firmly established itself as one of Australia's premier destinations for Southern Bluefin Tuna fishing, attracting anglers from across the country in pursuit of these incredible fish.
Widely regarded as one of the ultimate offshore fishing adventures available in Victoria, Southern Bluefin Tuna are renowned for their size, power and fighting ability. In recent seasons, fish exceeding 150kg have been landed in Victorian waters, highlighting the world-class fishery we have right on our doorstep.
These magnificent fish can be found along much of Western Victoria's coastline, from Apollo Bay through to the South Australian border. However, Portland has consistently proven itself as the standout destination, earning a reputation as Victoria's most reliable and productive Bluefin Tuna fishery.
WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW 
Trip Duration Up to 10 Hours Full Day Offshore Adventure 
Suitable For Experienced & Beginners All Levels are Welcome 
Season Schoolies: April - July Barrels: March - October 
Possible Locations Apollo Bay, Port Fairy, Portland & Sorrento 
CHECK OUT THE ACTION 
WHAT TO EXPECT 
Southern Bluefin Tuna are true ocean giants and are often found well offshore, sometimes over the continental shelf. While the journey out can be long, it provides a unique opportunity to experience some of the most spectacular sights the ocean has to offer, from abundant marine life to breathtaking offshore scenery.
Most of our tuna fishing involves trolling an assortment of carefully selected lures while searching for signs of feeding fish. We keep a close eye on diving birds, bait schools and surface activity, constantly looking for clues that lead us to the action.
There are few fishing experiences that compare to the excitement of Southern Bluefin Tuna fishing. Watching tuna explode on the surface before a reel suddenly screams into life as a powerful fish tears off at incredible speed is something every angler should experience at least once.
It's an adrenaline-filled experience you'll never forget.
