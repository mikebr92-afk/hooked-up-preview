your guides 
Trent Storer - Owner & Head Guide
Your Guide, Trent has grown up fishing the King River on their family farm in Whitfield and with a love for fishing and the Victorian High Country has gone on to explore all of the rivers the Whitfield and Mansfield area has to offer. With an intimate and extensive knowledge of access tracks by both vehicle and foot, surrounding bush and seasonal flows and conditions Trent is the most well equiped guide for the area and will deliver a trip that best suites you! 
After exploring the local rivers and bushland from top to bottom, time and time again, Trent started guiding as he thinks Fishing, Camping and Hiking and all outdoor pursuits are best enjoyed and shared with like minded people and he can't wait to show you some of his favourite spots! 
Trent has introduced and successfully tutored many first time and experienced anglers the art of fly fishing, incorporating location specific tips and techniques that equip each person with the skills and knowledge required to be successful in their fly fishing endeavours beyond the days guiding 
In true Aussie style Trent travels these areas in his fully equipped Toyota Landcruiser, and utilises a Garmin InReach with satellite messaging and tracking to ensure your safety is a priority from start to finish. There is no stone left unturned in Trent's efforts to make your day on the water as comfortable, safe and enjoyable as it can be! 
Nathan Polkinghorne - Guide, Mansfield & Surrounds
Nathan is the embodiment of everything that Back of Beyond Fly Fishing stands for. With an unyielding sense of adventure and a passion for the art of fly fishing, Nathan has spent countless hours exploring the rivers, creeks, and hidden waters around Mansfield and the surrounding High Country areas. His dedication to the craft has transformed him into a true expert on these local rivers, giving him an intimate understanding of their nuances, seasonal changes, and the unique experiences each waterway has to offer, ensuring that every angler has the best chance of success. 
His calm, friendly demeanour and his ability to stay positive no matter the conditions make him an ideal companion on any fishing adventure. Nathan’s approach to guiding is centered around enjoyment and education. He takes genuine pleasure in watching others succeed, sharing his wealth of knowledge, and helping anglers at all levels improve their skills. 
For Nathan, every trip is a chance to create lasting memories, and his enthusiasm for fly fishing is infectious. He believes that the journey is just as important as the catch and takes pride in creating a welcoming and stress-free environment where clients can truly relax and enjoy. From teaching you the finer points of casting to spotting that elusive fish or discussing the natural history of the riverside landscape, Nathan is dedicated to making sure you leave with more than just fish stories 
Since joining the Back of Beyond Fly Fishing team, Nathan has quickly become one of our most valued and trusted guides. Clients consistently praise his expertise, warmth, and genuine passion for helping others experience the magic of fly fishing in Victoria’s pristine rivers. His knowledge of the local ecosystem, combined with his easy-going attitude and commitment to making each outing memorable, make him a guide that clients are always happy to return to 
Unrivalled experience and knowledge of the Victorian High Country and it's Fly Fishing Opportunities
