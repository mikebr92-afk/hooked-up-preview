Kids Fishing Charter
3 Hours of Fun on the Water!
Capn’s Charters promotes sustainable fishing – Catch and Release
Let the little adventurers experience the thrill of fishing with Cap’n’s Charters Newcastle! Our 3-hour kids’ charter is designed just for young anglers, offering a fun and safe fishing trip on calm waters.
• All fishing gear provided – no experience needed
• Kid-friendly charter – patient and experienced crew to help
• Smooth waters – perfect for first-time fishers
• Small group experience – up to 3 kids or 2 kids + 1 adult (ages 6-16)
• BYO snacks & drinks – enjoy a relaxed outing on the water
Price: $300
