The Good Stuff Starts Here 
The best days in East Gippsland rarely go exactly to plan.
A short walk becomes an afternoon adventure. A quick stop turns into a long lunch. One attraction becomes three more recommendations from a local.
Paddle the Gippsland Lakes, explore ancient caves, ride scenic trails, spot wildlife, wander coastal villages or simply follow a road that looks interesting.
Around here, the hardest part isn’t finding something to do. It’s deciding what to do first.

All kinds of wonder
Home to the iconic Gippsland Lakes, Croajingolong National Park, and the Snowy River, the region offers endless outdoor adventures, from boating and fishing to hiking and wildlife spotting. Rich in Indigenous culture and history, East Gippsland is also known for its charming towns, fresh local produce, and laid-back lifestyle, making it a perfect destination for nature lovers and explorers alike.

See & Do
East Gippsland Rail Trail
Ride or hike the East Gippsland Rail Trail from Bairnsdale to Orbost and soak up nature at every turn—lush forests, rolling farmland, rivers, and classic timber bridges await. Enjoy village hospitality and taste Gippsland’s famed produce along your adventure-filled route.
Learn more 
See & Do
Great Alpine Road
Travel the Great Alpine Road from Bairnsdale to Omeo and immerse yourself in panoramic mountain views, lush valleys, alpine forests, and welcoming country towns. Discover historic pubs, scenic lookouts, local produce, and endless adventure on Victoria’s most stunning road journey.
