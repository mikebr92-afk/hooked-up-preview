Get on a fishing charter to some of the best fishing locations around Whitsundays in Airlie Beach, QLD!
Our 10.8 metre vessel, Stryker, which has a 6x6m fishing platform and includes quality Shimano SWA 8000 HG fishing reels, jewel jigging rods, and tackle fishing is perfect for going around the Islands & Shoals and the outer Reef for those chasing game fish.
What will we be doing and what will we be targeting? You will be reef fishing/bottom bashing for coral trout, sweet lip, red emperor, and more. You are guaranteed to always be able to secure a catch for the day and get plenty of exciting memories along the way.
