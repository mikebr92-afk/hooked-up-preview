Private cruises 
Looking to book an entire boat for an event? Our private cruises let you fully customise your charter – whether it’s a scenic cruise, ash scattering ceremony, scuba diving adventure, or filming project. Perfect for intimate gatherings, celebrations, or corporate events, you get complete flexibility to create a unique experience on the water.
pricing 
4-hour minimum charter within 30 Nm (up to 22 passengers):
$1,600 
Additional hour:
$400 
A 4-hour minimum charge is required to secure your booking, with any remaining balance due the day before your scheduled charter.
Public holidays will incur a 15% surcharge 
charter schedule for 2026 
🗓 Operating Days : Thursday to Tuesday 
Available on weekends from 1:30 PM only
🚫 Closed Days :
• Every Wednesday (except in December & January)
• 12 – 23 October 2026 (vessel maintenance)
• Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year’s Day, 2 January
Customise Your Private Charter 
Tailor the experience to suit your needs. Ideal for activities including (but not limited to) scenic cruises, scuba diving, filming, animal tagging, and ash scattering ceremonies.
Explore Blue Water to learn more about the vessel and start planning your private charter today.
What’s Included: 
• Morning tea with coffee, tea, and biscuits.
• Onboard BBQ available for guest use.
• Experienced crew, including a professional skipper and deckhand, assisting you throughout the trip.
• Unisex toilet onboard.
additional information 
• A deposit of 4-hr minimum charge is required at the time of booking. Any additional hours accrued on the day of the charter must be paid at the end of the service.
• There is no wheelchair access to the boat. Passengers must be able to descend and climb a vertical ladder of 3 to 5 steps from the berth to embark and disembark the boat.
• Our charters operate rain or shine. In the event that the weather is deemed too dangerous or unfavourable for a safe experience, such as thunderstorms, high winds and/ or strong swells, our skipper will make the decision to cancel the charter. See below for more info on our cancellation policy.
• BYO food and drinks are allowed, including alcohol in moderation.
• All prices shown are in Australian Dollars, and include GST, but exclude card surcharges and booking fees.
Booking Request 
Please note, a 4-hour minimum charge is required to secure your booking, with the remaining balance due the day before your scheduled charter.
Prefer to Call? Book by Phone!
cancellation & reschedule policy 
• All bookings are transferable but strictly non-refundable for changes of mind. Cancellations made by you will result in forfeiture of the deposit. 
• Illness, injury or other personal circumstances do not automatically entitle you to a refund or reschedule. Our standard Change & Cancellation Policy will apply.
• Weather conditions can be unpredictable and can change at any time, which is out of our control. As a result, cancellations usually occur the day before or the morning of your scheduled charter . Kindly take this into consideration when booking accommodation specifically for our charter. We highly recommend checking with your accommodation about their policy on last minute changes and cancellations.
• Cancellations made by us due to bad weather or mechanical issues, you will be entitled to one of these options:
• Reschedule to another day
• Get a refund (or receive a Return Voucher if a gift voucher was used for the original booking)
• Receive a Return Voucher that is valid for 3 years, equivalent to the value of your booking 
• Complimentary reschedules are offered up to 10 days before the charter. Any changes made after this time will result in forfeiture of the deposit.
