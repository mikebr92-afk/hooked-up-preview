This impressive 60ft Conquest is designed for the ultimate fishing experience. Powered by a robust 1,000 horsepower V8 MAN main engine and twin disc gearbox, Blue Water ensures a smooth and powerful ride.
Step aboard and enjoy an expansive deck and fishing platform, perfect for up to 18 passengers to fish comfortably at any one time, with a maximum capacity of 22 passengers and 3 crew members. The vessel is licensed for operations out to 100NM.
Blue Water is fully equipped with a walk-around deck, a Furuno navigation system featuring 3D bottom mapping, radar and AIS, an ice machine, TV, air conditioning, a BBQ, a large outdoor table, and a daybed. For your convenience, it also includes a toilet with a shower. Please be advised that there is no seating available on the deck while fishing. Guests are required to stand while fishing. However, ample seating is provided in the cabin and the sheltered area of the deck for your comfort.
Every Per Person and Whole Boat charter includes morning tea and a delicious sausage sizzle, making your fishing adventure even more enjoyable.
A little bit about us
We are an award-winning fishing charter company based in Wollongong, New South Wales. 
First established in 1992, Aquilla Fishing Charters is an Australian family-owned business with over 50 years of local fishing experience in the region passed down through generations. We pride ourselves on having a solid reputation for our quality experience, great customer service and a high level of safety.
Aquilla Fishing Charters, proudly family-owned by Bobby and Audrey, was honoured with the Excellence in Micro Business Award by the Illawarra Business Awards in 2023.
