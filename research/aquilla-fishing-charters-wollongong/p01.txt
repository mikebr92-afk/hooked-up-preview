deep sea fishing in wollongong 
Join us for an unforgettable fishing adventure in one of New South Wales' most stunning coastal areas! With three generations of experience in Wollongong, we know the best local fishing spots. No licence needed! Whether you're a pro or a beginner, we tailor each trip to your skill level, providing top-quality gear, bait, safety equipment, and cleaned catches to take home. Just bring your enthusiasm (and a drink or two), we’ll handle the rest!

Junior Anglers

Hook Your Child’s 
First Fishing Adventure!
Follow us on socials for upcoming school holiday dates 
Give your child the thrill of their very first fishing adventure! Our Junior Anglers School Holiday Sessions are designed for kids aged 8–16 to get hands-on with their own rod, try real fishing skills, and experience the excitement of catching fish safely. With a supervising adult included in the booking, it’s a fun, short, and safe 2-hour adventure that your young angler will remember forever.
No fishing licence required! 
Whether you’re a seasoned angler or just starting out, we offer the perfect trips that are catered to individual skills and interests. We supply high-end fishing gear, safety equipment, bait, and your catches of the day – gutted and cleaned, ready for you to take home. Just bring your enthusiasm for fun (and a bevvy or two), and let our experienced crew take care of the rest.

where do we fish
Depending on the weather and fishing conditions, we often drift along the South Coast of NSW between Stanwell Park and Shellharbour. We fish anywhere from off the beach to 10 miles offshore. Here are some of the spots we frequent (but not limited to):
• Stanwell Park
• Bellambi 
• Wollongong
• Five Islands (Port Kembla)
• Shellharbour
