Autumn/Winter 2025 Newsletter 
What a year we have had so far with weather challenges! We still have made the most of our opportunities. The tuna season was a great one with good numbers of longtail tuna from February to late May. They did drop off a little earlier than we would have liked but while they were here it was certainly a great season.

Spring 2024 Newsletter 
This Spring has seen some fantastic fishing with some big trophy fish landed by guests. Quality cobia, golden trevally, bonefish, longtail tuna and more were stand outs over a busy spring season. Late winter also provided the goods with snapper, goldens, spanish mackerel and queenfish giving guests some great days on the water. 
READ MORE 
Autumn/Winter Fishing Report and Newsletter 
What a mix of weather we have of late! We have had some beautiful days on the water with guests but we have also experienced a few bad periods of wind and rain. For now it looks like we have some more consistent winter weather ahead with south west winds and clear skies. On the fishing front, we enjoyed some cracking Autumn fishing with a wide range of species being caught into winter.

Jan, Feb & March 2024 Fishing Report and Newsletter 
It’s been a fantastic start to 2024 for HBFSF! As we get deeper into autumn, we are excited for the changes in season as we see different species move into the bay. Tuna is our mainstay this time of year with big trophy longtails the prize. We have been lucky enough to boat some great fish with plenty over the 20kg mark.

Happy New Year from Hervey Bay Fly and Sportfishing! 
A huge thank you to everyone that fished with us during 2023 along with everyone that has followed our guest’s journey through our socials! Thank you and all the best for 2024!
Liv and I would like to personally thank our professional team of guides John, Beno, Sunny and Pete for going above and beyond for our guests.

Winter / Spring 2023 Fishing Report and Newsletter 
Well spring is here and it is one of our busy periods that’s for sure. All our guides have been flat out putting guests onto some great fish. Through winter we saw some patchy fishing for snapper but had a great run of other species including golden trevally, queenfish, giant herring, Spanish mackerel, longtail tuna, mac tuna, coral trout and various reef species.
READ MORE 
Autumn 2023 Fishing Report and Newsletter 
Autumn is always a great season for us and this year was no different! The range of fish species is at it’s peak during the autumn months and sees some great size and epic captures as bigger specimens move through. In particular big longtails are on the move and a little easier during Autumn as they crash the surface and you make no mistake identifying these keg size fish.
READ MORE 
January - February 2023 Fishing Report and Newsletter 
What a start to 2023 with some fantastic fishing and time spent with guests. The weather has generally been very kind compared to the last few years which has been a nice change. The school holiday period saw the younger generation get into the action. Spotted and school mackerel always provide great fun for the kids and is a perfect introduction for them into sportfishing.

Merry Christmas from Hervey Bay Fly and Sportfishing 
From Pete, John Cross, Justin, Benno, Liv and myself, we wish all of our anglers a safe and Merry Christmas and Happy New year. 2022 was a roller-coaster ride of weather and fishing but we look forward to another epic year in 2023! December marlin season has continued to be amazing along with our pelagics firing up. Don’t forget to book for 2023 marlin season as we are filling fast.
