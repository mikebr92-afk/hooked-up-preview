Weipa Fishing Packages 
The best all inclusive sport fishing and fly-fishing packages in Weipa. 
Let us simplify your dream fishing holiday. We'll organize your transfers, accommodation and meals for you as part of your fishing package. Or if you prefer, we can tailor a custom package to suit you. Anything from a package with the lot to just the fishing, we can cater to your needs. 
Relax and let us take care of everything. 
Accommodation Options 
Weipa Fishing Lodge 
Located on the northern boundary of the caravan park overlooking the mouth of the Mission river with a wonderful ambiance with sensational views. The Fishing lodge is competitively priced, has 5 rooms, can accommodate up to 12 people and is serviced by a private chef looking after your exclusive menu. 
Albatross Bay Resort 
The Albatross is the oldest accommodation in Weipa it has hotel style facilities with bar and restaurant and bistro. The Alby is within walking distance of the main Weipa shopping area and 5 minute walk to the golf and bowls clubs. 
Beachfront Villas 
The beachfront villas can accommodate up to 4 people and have cooking facilities and water views and are a great budget option if you prefer to look after your own meals 
Heritage Resort 
Right next to the Weipa shopping centre, The Heritage Resort , has 30 rooms and a swimming pool. The Heritage is motel style accommodation, and also has its own restaurant and bar and courtesy coach. 
Weipa Caravan Park 
100m from the Weipa shopping centre and close to the post office and tackle shop in the middle of Weipa. 48 powered sites and self contained cabins and swimming pool. Great for the budget fisherman. 
These are just a few of the accommodation options available. If you have something specific in mind, please let us know.
MEALS 
What would a great holiday be without amazing food? We can organise a great variety of meal options for you to enjoy during your stay, and whilst out fishing. We can also cater for almost any dietary requirements. 
You're holidaying in the tropics, you're catching some of the worlds greatest fish, why not eat like a king while you're at it?
TRANSFERS 
No need to worry about hire cars or taxis, simply let us know your flight details and we'll happily arrange a transfer from Weipa airport to your accommodation, and return, so you don't have to worry. 
From individuals, to large groups, we can cater for all airport transfer requirements. 
Sponsors and Affiliations 
We are lucky enough to be supported by some of the best brands in the industry. These companies make our jobs a lot easier, and we only put our name to gear we can trust and rely on.
