We operate Fishing Charters from Melbourne chasing Snapper, Sharks (Gummy), Whiting, Squid, Kingfish and Bluefin Tuna.
Our Fishing Charters operate in Port Phillip Bay, Westernport Bay, Bass Strait, and the South Coast (off Portland) for Bluefin Tuna charters.
