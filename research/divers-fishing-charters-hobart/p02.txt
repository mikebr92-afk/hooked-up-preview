Fishing Charters & Boat Tours in Hobart
Browse our selection of fishing charters and boat tours available in Hobart, Tasmania. Diver's Fishing Charters offers a range of experiences designed for all ages and skill levels. Our Sunset Fishing Cruise is perfect for those who want to experience the magic of a Hobart sunset while fishing in productive local waters. For the more adventurous, our Half-Day Hook, Dive and Grill trip combines fishing, diving, and a fresh seafood BBQ on board. We also offer fully customisable Private Charters for groups wanting a personalised Tasmanian boating experience. All charters include professional guides, quality equipment, and safety gear. Select a tour below to learn more and book your adventure online.
3-Hour Sightseeing Fishing Cruise
Our 3-Hour Sightseeing Fishing Cruise is ideal for anyone short on time in Hobart but eager to explore its beauty from the water. Take in the stunning coastal views, spot wildlife, and unwind in the comfort of our enclosed cabin—relax on cozy sofas, enjoy fresh fruit, and listen to music as you soak up the scenery.
