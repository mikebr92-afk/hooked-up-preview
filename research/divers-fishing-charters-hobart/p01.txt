Common questions
I'm new to fishing. Which trip should I choose?
The 3-hour shared cruise suits first-timers, families and anyone short on time. The half-day Hook, Dive & Grill adds diving and an onboard wagyu BBQ. A private charter gives your own group full flexibility.
Do I need a fishing licence in Tasmania?
No licence is needed for sea fishing. Diving for lobster or abalone requires a recreational fishing licence (around $80) from Tasmania Fisheries.
Do I need to bring fishing gear?
No — all fishing gear, air tanks and weights are included. Bring your own dive equipment if you plan to dive.
What happens if the weather is bad?
If your trip is cancelled due to bad weather, you can choose a full refund or reschedule.
