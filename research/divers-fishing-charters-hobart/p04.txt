Itinerary
Whether you're a dive shop or scuba club from the mainland or overseas looking to explore lobster and abalone diving or discover cold-water coral and giant kelp forests, a fishing enthusiast aiming for offshore bluefin tuna, stripey trumpeter, or deep-sea bottom fishing, or simply hoping to charter a boat for a private and memorable time with family and friends – we've got you covered. We also cater to corporate event, birthday celebrations, scientific research, and water-based filming projects. Whatever your needs, we're here to help – feel free to get in touch to discuss further. 
Cancellation Policy
You are required to pay a 30% deposit at the time of booking, with the balance payable on the day of the trip. Cancellations made more than 7 days before the trip will receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made within 7 days but more than 48 hours before the trip will receive a 50% refund or the option to reschedule. No deposit refund is available for cancellations within 48 hours of the trip. Your safety is our priority; if we cancel your trip due to weather conditions, a full refund will be provided.

How much does a private charter cost?
