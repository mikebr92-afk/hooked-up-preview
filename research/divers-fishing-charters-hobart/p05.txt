Experience the best sunset fishing cruise in Hobart, Tasmania with Diver's Fishing Charters. Our popular Sunset Fishing Cruise takes you to some of the most productive fishing grounds around Hobart as the sun sets over the Tasmanian horizon. This shared adventure is perfect for solo travellers, couples, and small groups who want to enjoy a relaxing evening on the water while targeting species like squid, gurnard perch, cod, bluethroat, blue mackerel and Australian salmon. Our experienced captain will guide you to the best spots and provide all the fishing equipment and bait you need. Watch the sky transform into stunning shades of orange and pink as you reel in your catch against the backdrop of Hobart's beautiful coastline and Mount Wellington.

3-Hour Sightseeing Fishing tours in Hobart 
Itinerary
Our 3-Hour Sightseeing Fishing Cruise is ideal for anyone short on time in Hobart but eager to explore its beauty from the water. Take in the stunning coastal views, spot wildlife, and unwind in the comfort of our enclosed cabin—relax on cozy sofas, enjoy fresh fruit, and listen to music as you soak up the scenery. Want to try fishing? Step out onto the open deck to give it a try and share some laughs with friends. Even beginners can easily catch fish with the help of our crew. This fishing cruise offers a perfect blend of simplicity and comfort, letting you enjoy sea fishing without leaving Hobart. We can also recommend local restaurants where you can have your catch cooked for dinner. For guests who want to venture offshore to fish for bluefin tuna or stripy trumpeter, or dive into the remarkable giant kelp forests in South Tasmania, explore the cold-water coral reefs or sea caves in Bruny Island, feel free to contact us for private charter bookings. 
WHO IT'S FOR
Perfect for travelers with limited time, rookie anglers, and those looking to relax and enjoy a sunset experience. (This tour can also be scheduled in the morning upon request.） 
Cancellation Policy
Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the trip will receive a full refund. No refund will be provided for cancellations within 48 hours of the trip. Your safety is our priority; if we cancel your trip due to weather conditions, a full refund will be issued.

3-Hour Sightseeing Fishing Cruise in Hobart
Hobart Fishing Tour for Beginners and Families
Our 3-hour sightseeing fishing cruise is designed for guests who want to enjoy Hobart’s coastal beauty while trying an easy and enjoyable fishing experience. It is ideal for beginners, couples, families and visitors looking for a relaxed day on the water. 
What to Expect on the Cruise
Cruise through scenic southern Tasmanian waters, enjoy the comfort of our boat, and try fishing with guidance from our crew. Depending on conditions, you may also spot local marine wildlife and enjoy beautiful coastal views around Hobart. 
Scenic Coastal Views and Local Fishing Spots
This Hobart fishing tour combines sightseeing and reef fishing in one easy-going experience, making it a great option for travellers who want to enjoy Tasmania from the water without committing to a full-day trip. 
Book Your Hobart Fishing Tour
Join our 3-hour sightseeing fishing cruise and experience one of the most enjoyable Hobart fishing tours for beginners, families and visitors. 
Common questions
How long is the cruise and how much does it cost?
The cruise runs for 3 hours and costs from $198 per adult. Children under 6 join for free, and ages 6 to 11 are half price.
What's included in the price?
All fishing gear, a skipper and one deckhand, bottled water, hot tea, soft drinks and a fruit platter. You are welcome to keep the fish you catch.
Is the cruise suitable for beginners and children?
Yes. Our shared trips mostly take place in calm waters, making them ideal for beginners, seniors and children.
