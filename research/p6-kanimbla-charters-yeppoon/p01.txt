Our Boats
Kanimbla is a 25m/82ft Aluminium ship whose catamaran design provides maximum stability at sea. Our cruising speed is 10 knots. Her wide beam and 3 levels afford a spacious living area and plenty of deck space for fishing and diving activities.
All living areas are fully air-conditioned and up to 20 guests can be comfortably accommodated in 4 and 2 berth cabins.
Dine and relax in our Saloon which is equipped with Foxtel, USB Jack and Sound System
For fishermen and divers, a large hydraulic platform allows for safe and easy access to the water or our tender dinghies.
Safety is our priority – Kanimbla has a ‘state of the art’ navigation system and communication centre. Guests can be contacted and call out via our satellite telephone.
Kanimbla’s water tanks hold 10,000L of fresh water and our desalination plant produces 150L/hr. Our on board dive compressor is available for SCUBA tank refills.
Kanimbla has 6 kittens!
To further enhance your fishing/diving experience we carry a fleet of 6 Webster Twin Fisher dinghies which allow you to access different reef environments.
The Twin Fishers are also of a catamaran design offering space and stability. Each dinghy has a 30hp outboard, depth sounder + AIS, safety gear and a UHF radio. (You will need a boat license.)
