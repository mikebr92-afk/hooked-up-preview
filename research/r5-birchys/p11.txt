Car, Boat & Camping Hire
Create you own Kimberley Adventure! 
Ready to explore the East Kimberley and beyond? We have got everything you need to make it happen, car, 4WD, camper and boat hire all in one place! 
Dreaming of tackling the legendary Gibb River Road in a rugged 4WD? Or prefer a relaxed self-drive through Kununurra's scenic sights, including the stunning Ord River Irrigation Area? Whatever your style, there is the perfect vehicle for your journey. 
Want to take to the water? Hire a pontoon to cruise on Lake Kununurra or Lake Argyle for a leisurely BBQ, or grab a dinghy and venture further. Feeling adventurous? Paddle into paradise with a kayak or stand-up paddleboard on Lake Argyle, the ultimate way to soak up the Kimberley magic. 
Let us help get you on the road or the waterways today, call the friendly, local team at Kununurra Visitor Centre on (08) 9168 1177 or email [email protected] . Your adventure starts here !
