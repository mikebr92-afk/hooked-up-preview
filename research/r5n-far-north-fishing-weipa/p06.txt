CAPE YORK FISHING WEIPA STYLE 
Cape York fishing is some of the best sport and fly fishing in the world. Our remoteness and small population ensure our fisheries are healthy and not overfished. This is still widely regarded as frontier territory in Australia. A vast wilderness, dotted with remote townships, cattle stations and mines. Cape York is far from any major centre and cut off by road for months at a time over the wet season (December – February), but still accessible by plane.
Cape York fishing Weipa style gives you the best of both worlds. By day, you’re fishing some of the best waterways for sport fish, bending rods, landing Barras (and more!) and casting poppers on the flats. By night, it’s hot showers, comfortable beds, amenities and fresh food. That’s why Far North Fishing Weipa chooses to do charters in and out of Weipa. We’re about premium experience and awesome fishing, for everyone from the novice to experienced angler.

CAPE YORK FISHING WEIPA STYLE 
Cape York fishing Weipa style is like nothing you’ve ever done before. Our unique part of the world makes a stunning backdrop for the fishing adventure of a lifetime, and you would be hard pressed to find anywhere else like it. 
Big blue sky. Mangrove lined rivers and estuaries. Coastal flats and inshore reefs make for some perfect fishing experiences. Also, being located on the west coast of Cape York means we have calmer waters than the east coast. That means we can fish year-round except for the wet season, which is usually from December to March.
For more information on Cape York fishing, or to book one of our charters, contact Far North Fishing Weipa on 0417 098 732 or fishing@farnorthfishingweipa.com.au
