Moreton Bay Charters operates from REDLAND BAY, MORETON BAY, QLD, 4165 
Fishing charters and day tours in Moreton Bay. Minimum 2 anglers required. Come fishing on our full day charter for Snapper, Squire, Flathead, Bream, Whiting, Tailor, Trevally, Tusk Fish, Mackerel, Tuna. Experienced fishing guide Sean Conlon uses a 6 metre Bonito centre cab and a Hummingbird side imaging sounder for day trips with larger groups of 3 or more anglers - with toilet on board. For smaller groups of 1 or 2 anglers, lure and soft plastic trips, Sean uses a 4.3 metre CrossXCountry with centre console - no toilet on board. The boat with toilet is available on request for smaller groups. Gift vouchers available. ** Please note all trips are booked heavily over the next two months **
