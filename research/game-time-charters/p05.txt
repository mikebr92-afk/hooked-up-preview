Our Perth fishing charters are private, so the whole boat is yours for the day. We leave Hillarys Boat Harbour at 7 am for a full day of deep sea game fishing in the Perth trench and are back by 4 pm, November to March, for up to 6 anglers, from $4,000 per day. From late March to the end of May we fish out of Jurien Bay instead.
Boarding information Getting on board 
To start your journey, head to A Jetty, directly in front of Nautica Residences Hillarys, where you'll board Game Time.
Please plan to arrive 30 minutes before departure to ensure everything goes smoothly.
A Jetty, in front of Nautica Residences Hillarys Boat Harbour, WA 
30 minutes before Arrive at A Jetty, in front of Nautica Residences. 
7 am or 9 am Game fishing charters leave at 7 am, Rottnest Island day trips at 9 am. 
Back by 4 pm Both charters return to Hillarys at 4 pm. 
Parking and amenities Park at the jetty 
There's ample parking available right in front of the jetty, making access to the boat hassle-free.
Grab a coffee, enjoy a meal, or browse the local shops, with a range of cafés, restaurants, and clothing stores within walking distance. The harbour also has public bathrooms and a pharmacy nearby for any last-minute essentials.
Explore Hillarys Boat Harbour More than a marina 
Hillarys Boat Harbour is a vibrant destination in its own right. Whether you're after a quick bite, looking to relax by the water, or picking up something you might need for your trip, the harbour has something for everyone.
From its lively atmosphere to the beautiful views of the marina, it's the perfect place to spend some time before or after your charter.
