At Game Time Charters, we're passionate about sharing the incredible beauty of Rottnest Island with you, just as our family has enjoyed for over 20 years.
This is more than just a day trip. It's a chance to live the lifestyle we've cherished for decades, from the pristine waters to the hidden gems of Rottnest Island.
Price From $3,600 for up to 8 passengers. You book the whole boat. 
Deposit A $500 deposit secures your booking, with the remaining balance due 7 days prior to departure. 
Season October to late March. 
Weather All trips are weather dependent. If the conditions aren't right we'll offer you another date, and if you can't reschedule you receive a full refund. 
Your full-day trip What's included 
Your full-day trip to Rottnest Island comes with everything you need for the ultimate day out.
01 Swimming and snorkelling
Dive into Rottnest's crystal-clear waters and explore the stunning coral reefs and marine life. Snorkelling gear is on board.
02 Wildlife watching
Look out for dolphins, stingrays, and even humpback whales in season. And of course, don't miss the island's iconic quokkas.
03 Pristine beaches
Experience the best of WA's stunning beaches, just a swim or paddle away from the immaculate sands of Rottnest Island.
04 BBQ lunch
Add a full BBQ lunch for 8 for $450. Contact Gabriel before your charter about dietary requirements and what's included. Bringing your own? Keep it to sandwiches and snacks, as there are no cooking facilities for BYO food.
05 BYO drinks
It's strictly BYO alcohol, but we'll have water and soft drinks available for you throughout the day.
06 Casual fishing
Fancy a fish? Casual fishing can be part of your Rottnest day, just let us know when you book. Game fishing is its own charter: see our private game fishing charters .
Boat hire or skippered charter? Rottnest boat hire, with a skipper 
Looking for Rottnest boat hire, or a private boat to Rottnest for the day? Game Time is a private, skippered charter rather than a self-drive hire boat or a set boat tour.
You book the whole boat for your group of up to 8, we leave Hillarys Boat Harbour at 9 am and have you back at 4 pm, and we look after the boat so everyone can swim, snorkel and relax. Add a full BBQ lunch for 8 for $450, or bring your own sandwiches and snacks. Alcohol is BYO.

Good to know FAQs
What's included in a Rottnest Island day trip?
Snorkelling gear to explore the island's vibrant reefs. Wildlife watching opportunities, including dolphins, quokkas, and, in season, humpback whales. Beautiful beaches with fun beach activities. Add a full BBQ lunch for 8 for $450, or bring your own sandwiches and snacks. It's strictly BYO alcohol, and we'll provide water and soft drinks throughout the day.
How long is the trip?
Our Rottnest Island day trips run from 9 am to 4 pm, giving you a full day to relax, swim, snorkel, and enjoy everything the waters and surroundings have to offer.
How many people can join the trip?
Our Rottnest Island charters can accommodate up to 8 passengers, making them ideal for business events, friends or families.
Can we bring our own food and drinks?
We can provide a full BBQ lunch for 8 for an additional $450. Contact Gabriel before your charter about dietary requirements and what's included. You're welcome to bring your own food instead, as long as it's sandwiches and snacks, because there are no cooking facilities for BYO food. Alcohol is strictly BYO, and we provide water and soft drinks throughout the day.
How do I book a Rottnest Island day trip?
You can book your Rottnest Island day trip by visiting our Booking Page and securing your spot with a $500 deposit. If you have any special requests, don't hesitate to contact us!
