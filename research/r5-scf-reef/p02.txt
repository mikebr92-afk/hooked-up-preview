Game Fishing Trips
Our year-round game fishing trips offer a full day of action, targeting Sharks, Tuna, and Marlin, with thrilling fights against some of the ocean’s biggest and toughest fish—prepare for the catch of a lifetime!
Our year-round game fishing trips offer a full day of action, targeting Sharks, Tuna, and Marlin, with thrilling fights against some of the ocean’s biggest and toughest fish—prepare for the catch of a lifetime!
We offer game fishing trips year-round, giving you a full day of action that will stay with you forever. We depart at 7am and return at 5pm .
Depending on the season, we chase different pelagic game fish:
• Winter (June – October) : We target Sharks and Tuna , including Yellowfin , Albacore Tuna , and Mako Sharks .
• Spring and Summer (November – May) : It’s Marlin season! We’ll go after Black , Blue , and Striped Marlin using heavy game lures, as well as Mahi Mahi , Wahoo , Short-Billed Spearfish , and a variety of shark species.
If you’ve never tried game fishing before, get ready for an intense fight with some of the ocean’s biggest and toughest fish. Even the smaller Marlin can weigh between 70-80 kg , more than the average human! Now, imagine the thrill of catching a Marlin that tips the scales at over 200 kg !
