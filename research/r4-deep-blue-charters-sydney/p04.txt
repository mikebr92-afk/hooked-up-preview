Where Can I get Picked Up?
Rose Bay Wharf
Address: Lyne Park, Rose Bay (Timber wharf between Rose Bay ferry wharf and boat ramp). 
Primary pickup location for all fishing charters. Located on the eastern suburbs, Ample parking with a car park located in Lyne Park (park in the 12 hour bays to avoid fines). 
Mosman Bay Ferry Wharf 
Address: At the bottom of Avenue Road, Mosman. 
Great location for clients located on Manly and the north shore. Parking available within a walking distance of the wharf. 
King Street Wharf, Darling Harbour
Address: The Promenade, Sydney NSW 2000. 
In front of Cargo Bar. Wharf numbers 6, 7, 8 and 9 pickup locations. There’s a Wharf booking fee of $60 per charter. Parking located nearby in parking lots and along on street parking. 
Casino Wharf, Pyrmont 
Address: 80 Pyrmont St, Pyrmont NSW 2009 (In front of The Star). 
Park at The Star (Casino) and you’re ready to go. Across the road from The Star (Casino). Booking fee of $60 applies.
