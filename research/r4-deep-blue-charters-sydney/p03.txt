Reef, Sports &amp; Game Fishing Charters sydney
REEF FISHING CHARTERS
Reef fishing is our most popular and booked charter. It caters for individuals to small groups and is suited to all kinds of fisherman, whether you are a budding beginner, experienced angler, or an adventurous kid. Our experienced crew are here to assist you, making your reef fishing charter experience a memorable one. With everything for fishing provided all you have to do is come on board.
WHAT CAN I CATCH?
We target Sydney’s top reef fishing grounds between 1 – 5 nautical miles (2 – 10km) offshore with depths between 30-100 metres. These Reef fishing grounds are abound with Snapper, Flathead, Morwong, wrasse, Pearl Perch and a variety of other fish species.
Bonus: Spotting Dolphins is a common occurrence as well as Whale watching (around May and November).
Deep Blue Charters explores the seas and searches for your perfect catch!

SPORTS FISHING CHARTERS
Sports fishing charters are for those who want more chase, hunt and adrenalin. This fishing trip is suited for novice or experienced fisherman seeking adventure and is catered for small groups only. Our helpful and experienced staff will be there to help you setup rigs, jigs, bait and much more to catch your big fish.
WHAT CAN I CATCH?
We troll to Long Reef, FADS, Artificial Reefs and/or various other spots.
Target species: Kingfish, Mahi Mahi (dolphin fish) and other pelagic species. Reef fishing can be combined on these charters to pump up your experience and catch.
Book Now 
FULL DAY (SPORTS AND REEF) FISHING CHARTERS
Want more fishing time?? You got it!! For maximum experience for all the fishing fanatics out there, we recommend the full day charter. With adventure of reef and adrenalin of sports fishing combined, you have up to 9 hours of fishing time.
WHAT CAN I CATCH?
Sports Fishing Locations: 12 mile, Long Reef, Fish Aggregating Device (FAD), Artificial Reef and/or various other spots. Target species is Kingfish, Mahi Mahi (Dolphin Fish) and other pelagic species.
Reef Fishing Locations: We then venture out to reef fishing grounds targeting Snapper, Morwong, Flathead and many other reef species. With all day to explore Sydney’s best fishing spots you have doubled your chances of catching your ultimate fish.
