Reel Adventure Fishing offers small-group sport and deep sea fishing charters departing Port Botany, Sydney. With a maximum of four guests on board, every trip is personal, relaxed and focused on getting a line in the water where the fish are.
All fishing gear is provided, including quality rods, reels, tackle, bait and safety equipment, so guests simply turn up ready to fish. Skipper Captain Vinnie brings extensive local knowledge of Sydney's offshore reefs and coastline, with hands-on coaching for beginners and plenty of action for experienced anglers. 
Half-day reef fishing charters run as a five-hour morning or four-hour afternoon trip, targeting kingfish, snapper, John Dory and flathead on Sydney's productive reefs.
The full-day sport fishing charter is an eight-hour offshore adventure chasing kingfish, mahi mahi, snapper and other species further out to sea.
From December to February, the full-day Marlin Adventure heads offshore in search of black marlin, one of Australia's most prized game fish.
Private charters are available for up to four people. Trips are subject to weather and sea conditions, and bookings can be made online.
