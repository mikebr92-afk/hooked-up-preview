Fishing Charters 
Experience world-class fishing action for many of Australia's premier sportfish. 
LOCAL 
KNOWLEDGE 
Fish with the local experts with local experience for personalised fishing adventures! 
We specialise in small groups taking a maximum of four clients per boat on extended safaris. With the option of 3-6 days of fishing, we would recommend 4-5 days for a complete Darwin fishing charter adventure. 
ENQUIRE NOW 
personalised ADVENTURES 
Target species to choose from include Jewfish, Golden Snapper, and Cod on the inshore reefs. Further off-shore there are Red Emperor, Coral Trout, Bluebone, Nanagai and Tricky Snapper. The tidal rivers and rocky headlands hold quality salt water Barra, Thread-fin Salmon, and Mud Crabs. Techniques range from conventional tackle, casting, trolling, and jigging. ​ 
ENQUIRE NOW 
FULL SERVICE 
Barramundi & Bluewater Safaris are fully inclusive and include return transfers from Darwin, accommodation at our Dundee Beach Retreat, all meals, all premium fishing gear and guided fishing experiences.
Located just five minutes from the Dundee Tavern and boat ramp, it's the perfect base to unwind after a day on the water.
With more than 25 years of experience guiding from Dundee Beach, you can fish with confidence knowing you're in the hands of the local experts who know these waters better than most.
