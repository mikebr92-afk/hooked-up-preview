Jarbu Lodge 
-->
Jarbu Lodge is a small privately run fishing lodge offering access to exclusive locations, including fishing rights to a number of bays on the mainland. With enthusiastic and professional guides ready to ensure you target the species of your choice, including the unstoppable giant trevally, queenfish, Spanish mackerel, or mangrove jack to test the limits of your line. And the famous barramundi can be caught along the beaches and rocky outcrops, an experience not to be missed. Only a limited number of people are able to visit Jarbu Fishing Lodge, ensuring protection of the area and vast fish supplies.
The unique accommodation is eco-tourism bungalows which are built on a platform, to allow ample ventilation and cooling sea breezes through the lush tropical trees and plants that surround the lodge. After an energetic day’s fishing, relax in the dining area and soak up the stunning sea views. Murin Airways service Croker Island via Darwin five times a week, so you’re just two hours away from a truly memorable adventure.
Touring Options
Jarbu Lodge Tailored
Jarbu Lodge have many diverse fishing options available and can tailor a thrilling fishing experience to suit your needs. With custom built boats to suit the area, you’ll venture into remote and exclusive fishing locations, and you may even discover a new fishing hot spot. Enjoy an energetic day’s fishing targeting your favourite species – giant trevally, queenfish, Spanish mackerel, mangrove jack or the famous barramundi.
Then come back to the lodge in its unique setting, where you can either unwind and relax on the balcony as you soak up the stunning views, or enjoy a sumptuous meal in the dining area and plan your next day’s adventure.

Explore The Outback
Australia has a vast remote interior, much of it largely untouched. By night, the outback is deathly quiet, with the only light provided by the stars and the moon - a perfect oportunity for stargazing. Explore the isolated heart of the country, meet and connect with Aboriginal people and experience one of the oldest living cultures in the world. Go ‘walk-about’ and immerse yourself with Australia’s endless outback horizons.

Australia's Best Islands

all who visit. Naturally, the Great Barrier Reef dominates any list of great Australian islands, with
pristine wonders like the Whitsundays, Dunk Island and Lizard Island. Further off the east coast is
