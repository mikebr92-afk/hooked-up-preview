Australian Tours and Experiences
With so much to see and do in Australia, tours are the perfect way to see Australia with the help and experience of those that know it best. There is almost a limitless range of Australian experiences that can be enjoyed as part of a tour.
Go snorkeling or scuba diving in and around the Great Barrier Reef, off the coast of Queensland, or explore this less-visited Ningaloo Reef, situated right across the other side of the country in Western Australia. Take a walking tour of the beautiful Blue Mountains, just a stone’s throw from Sydney.
There are even skiing trips that can be enjoyed on the many pistes of Victoria, Australia’s premier winter sports destination. For those that want to experience the country at a more leisurely pace however, horse riding tours take in all corners, as do hot air balloon trips . There is no better way to see the deep red earth at the centre of the country around Alice Springs than floating along in a balloon hundreds of feet above the stunningly beautiful landscape.
One of the most relaxing experiences in Australia is a tour that takes in the thermal pools, encircled by tropical plants and swaying palms, just south of Darwin. It’s another one of those Australian settings from which dreams are made.
The best way to see the country’s fabulous coastline is to get on a surfboard. Whether you’re a seasoned pro or a beginner (you can even learn to surf !), there are opportunities to ride waves throughout the country.
