One on One Charters
Capn’s Charters can tailor a session to your wants and needs. For any questions or booking enquiries please do not hesitate to contact us! Tight Lines!
Lures* – Hard bodies are supplied on a pay for loss basis. First lure is on the house.
Capn’s Charters promotes sustainable fishing – Catch & Release 
Book Charter
Come and enjoy either a half day (5hr) or full day (8hr) one on one fishing session focusing on enhancing your lure and soft plastic fishing skills whilst catching trophy fish. Fishing will take place in protected waters so sea sickness is nothing to worry about. This charter will be personally tailored to the individual, aiming to either introduce lure and soft plastic basics, enhance and refine your skills, learn to target a specific species of fish or answer any questions you may have regarding this type of fishing.
Sessions Available: 
Jumpinpin Half Day: $450 (+ GST) 
Jumpinpin Full Day: $600 (+ GST) 
Moreton Bay Half Day: $500 (+ GST) 
Moreton Bay Full Day: $650 (+ GST)
