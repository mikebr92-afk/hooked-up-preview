The Charter 
Experience
Capn's Charters caters to all skill levels and people of all ages. Whether you are a novice fisherman wanting to learn the basics of soft plastics and lures, to an experienced angler who wants to spend a day on the water, Capn's Charters can cater to your needs.
​
​
For novice and intermediate level soft plastic and lure fishermen the day will be spent targeting flathead. The charters focus will be to not only catch a trophy fish, but to teach the basics such as techniques, fishing locations and much more. By the end of the charter the Capn will have you feeling comfortable in your own ability to target fish on lures and soft plastics.
​​
​
For those with substantial soft plastic and lure experience, regular fishing charters are available. In this instance the Capn will tailor a charter to your needs and will try his best to put you onto a trophy fish.​
Meet Our Team
Capn Ryan Vines is a 35 year old avid fisherman who has developed a love of fishing through his grandfather. He is Brisbane born and raised and has been fishing the Jumpinpin region for the past 25 years. Over 10 years ago the Capn ventured away from the traditional bait fishing method, and developed a new found love for the skill and challenge that is lure and soft plastic fishing. 
Ryan Vines 
Owner / Skipper
Craig lives in Redland Bay and has been fishing the Moreton Bay and Jumpinpin area for a long time. Craig loves chasing his bread and butter species and is a bream and flathead expert. Craig also thorougly enjoys his freshwater fishing in Queenslands Impoundments chasing bass, saratoga and barramundi. 
Craig Templar 
Skipper
Matt has been angling in the SEQ waterways for over 20 years after moving up from Port Macquarie. Matt lives and breathes fishing and you will be hard pressed to find someone more passionate about the sport. Matt thoroughly enjoys both inshore and offshore fishing and prides himself on chasing trophy fish.
