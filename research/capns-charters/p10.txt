Jumpinpin
Fishing will take place in the Jumpinpin and Broadwater region, venturing as far north as the Southern Moreton Bay Islands and as far south as the Gold Coast Broadwater. Target species will range from Flathead, Threadfin Salmon, Jew fish, Mangrove Jack and much more. Our expert guides have over 20 years of fishing this area so get in touch and we can create a personalised fishing charter to suit your needs!

Moreton Bay
Fishing will take place in the Brisbane River and Moreton Bay regions. Charters will consist of chasing pelagics, fishing wrecks and reef systems. Target species will range from Snapper, Sweetlip, Mackeral, Tuna, Jew fish, Flathead, and much more . Our expert guides have over 10 years of fishing this area so get in touch and we can create a personalised fishing charter to suit your needs!
