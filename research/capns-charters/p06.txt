Exclusive Multi-Day Trophy Fishing Adventures
Limited Dates. Massive Fish
These premium multi-day charters give you the chance to land bucket-list species without travelling to the Northern Territory or Far North Queensland.
You get fully guided, fully catered adventures designed for anglers who want results.

What you can expect?
• Once-in-a-lifetime trophy fishing
• Expert guide with local knowledge
• Food, accommodation and gear included
• Comfort cabins/motels and premium equipment
• Live scope technology
• Seamless pick up and drop off (if required)
• Small private groups (1–3 people)
• 15% deposit required to lock in accomodation
Why these trips are different
• Limited to one trip per month (unless in demand)
• Strictly three-week in advance bookings
• Each experience can be completely customised to suit your fishing dreams
• You get the chance to chase:
• Threadfin Salmon, Jewfish, Snapper and Flathead locally
• Saratoga at Lake Boondooma (Option 2 package) 
• Trophy Barramundi at Lake Monduran
• These trips are rare, exclusive and designed for anglers chasing something unforgettable
