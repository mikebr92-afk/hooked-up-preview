Half Day Charters
Capn’s Charters can tailor a session to your wants and needs. For any questions or booking enquiries please do not hesitate to contact us! Tight Lines!
Lures* – Hard bodies are supplied on a pay for loss basis. First lure is on the house.
Capn’s Charters promotes sustainable fishing – Catch & Release 
Come and enjoy a 5-hour fishing charter where we target trophy fish in the Jumpinpin or Moreton Bay area. Fishing will take place in protected waters so sea sickness is nothing to worry about and all gear will be provided. Capn’s charters takes pride in the educational aspect of fishing and believe that is what sets them apart from any other charter on the market. Soft plastics and hard body lures will be used with a focus on teaching you how, where and when to catch fish using these methods, aiming to teach the basics, improve your current ability or introduce you to the most exciting and rewarding form of fishing available today. 
Available Sessions: Morning (6am) & Afternoon (1pm) 
Cost: 
Jumpinpin $500 (+ GST) 
Moreton Bay $575 (+ GST) 
*Price is NOT per individual. Price is the total for the duration of the booking. A 4th Participant may be added for an additional $100 to certain bookings*
