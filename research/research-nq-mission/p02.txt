About
Hooked Up Fishing Adventures is a well-established boat tour agency and fishing charter located in Mission Beach, Australia. With over 40 years of experience in the fishing industry, they offer a variety of fishing charters, as well as whale watching tours, providing customers with an unforgettable and exhilarating experience on the water.
One of their main offerings is their Mission Beach Fishing Charters. Whether you are a seasoned angler or a novice fisherman, Hooked Up Fishing Adventures guarantees to “hook you up” with the fishing experience of a lifetime. Their spacious and custom-built 26ft Sportfisher, the Spirit of Dunk, is equipped with state-of-the-art electronics to ensure that you have the best chance of catching fish. With their latest dynamic positioning system, you can spend more time fishing and less time waiting for anchoring.
Their experienced crew will take you to the best fishing spots, targeting a wide range of species including reef fish, game fish, and even big game fish. The game fishing off Mission Beach is known to be on par with anywhere else in Australia, giving you great odds of a successful fishing trip.
In addition to their fishing charters, Hooked Up Fishing Adventures also offers budget boat hire in Mission Beach. You can rent one of their Yamaha powered Smart Wave boats, which can accommodate up to four adults. Whether you want to head to Dunk Island for a day of exploration or go fishing on your own, their budget boat hire provides a convenient and affordable option. The best part is, no boat licence is required, and their team will handle the launch and retrieval of the boat for you.
For those looking for a unique gift or special occasion present, Hooked Up Fishing Adventures offers gift vouchers that can be purchased online. These vouchers can be customised with a personalised message and can be emailed directly to the recipient or to yourself for a more personal delivery. They offer immediate delivery to your inbox, making it a perfect last-minute gift option.
As a reputable boat tour agency and fishing charter, Hooked Up Fishing Adventures has received positive testimonials from various individuals, including Ian Stenlake from Sea Patrol, who had a fantastic fishing experience with the company. Their commitment to providing a fun and enjoyable downtime for their clients is evident in their meticulous attention to detail and local knowledge.
The Spirit of Dunk, their custom-built offshore Sportfisher, is designed and engineered to withstand the demanding conditions of the Far North. With its maximum capacity of 12 passengers for island tours and up to 9 passengers for Outer Great Barrier Reef trips, this boat is ideal for larger groups and private charters. Powered by a 2016 model 225 Yamaha engine, the Spirit of Dunk can reach speeds of up to 25 knots, ensuring a fast and comfortable journey.
For an unforgettable fishing adventure or whale watching tour in Mission Beach, look no further than Hooked Up Fishing Adventures. With their decades of experience, top-of-the-line equipment, and knowledgeable crew, they are dedicated to providing a memorable and thrilling experience on the water.
