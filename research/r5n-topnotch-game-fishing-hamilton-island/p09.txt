General Terms, Conditions & Cancellation Policy – Topnotch Game Fishing
Terms & Conditions 
Bookings are confirmed upon receipt of either a deposit or full payment for the charter. All outstanding balances must be paid and cleared prior to the charter departure date.
All charters are subject to weather conditions on the day. The Captain reserves the right to cancel or amend a charter due to unsafe weather conditions, unforeseen mechanical breakdowns, or any other circumstance that may affect the safety of guests, crew or the vessel. In these circumstances, Topnotch Game Fishing can offer an alternative charter date subject to availability or a full refund.
No refunds or credits will be provided if a guest is unable to continue or complete the charter due to seasickness or any other personal medical condition.
All prices are quoted in Australian Dollars (AUD).
The minimum age for all charters is 6 years. Shared charters are subject to minimum passenger numbers. The Pirate requires a minimum of six (6) tickets sold to operate. Moonshine, when operating shared charters in place of The Pirate, requires a minimum of eight (8) tickets sold.
Fishing Equipment 
Guests must use all fishing equipment responsibly and follow the instructions provided by the Captain and crew at all times. Our crew will demonstrate the correct techniques for using rods, reels and tackle to ensure both guest safety and the longevity of our equipment. Any rods, reels, tackle or other equipment that are lost or damaged as a result of negligence, misuse, reckless operation, or failure to follow the instructions of the Captain or crew may be charged to the guest at the reasonable cost of repair or replacement.
Guest Conduct & Safety 
The Captain’s decision regarding the safe operation of the vessel and the conduct of guests is final. Guests must comply with all safety directions and instructions given by the Captain and crew throughout the charter. Topnotch Game Fishing reserves the right to terminate a charter for any guest whose behaviour is unsafe, reckless, abusive, or places themselves, other guests, the crew, or the vessel at risk. In these circumstances, no refund will be provided.
Cancellation Policy 
* Cancellations made more than 48 hours prior to the scheduled departure time are eligible for a full refund.
* Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled departure time will result in forfeiture of 100% of the charter price.
Guest Amendment Policy 
Guests who request to reschedule a booking more than 48 hours before the scheduled departure time are eligible for amendment, subject to availability.
Fuel Levy Policy 
In the event of significant increases in fuel costs or fuel supply shortages, Topnotch Game Fishing reserves the right to apply a fuel surcharge to confirmed bookings. Any applicable fuel levy will be advised prior to departure and must be paid before the charter commences.
