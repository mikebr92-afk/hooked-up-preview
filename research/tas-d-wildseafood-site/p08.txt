Hobart’s Ultimate Seafood Boat Cruise
Hobart's Ultimate Seafood Boat Cruise 
Join your boat cruise at Constitution Dock in Hobart to head south along the River Derwent towards Bruny Island.
Your gastronomic maritime adventure combines the beauty of Tasmania's landscapes with the culinary delight of the freshest of Tasmanian seafood. 
Set Sail from Constitution Dock
Your journey begins at historic Constitution Dock on Hobart's waterfront, a popular spot for local yachts and fishing boats. As your cruise departs, you leave behind the bustling atmosphere of the dock and enter the waters of the River Derwent, the deepest sheltered harbour in the Southern Hemisphere.
Scenic River Derwent Views
Cruising along the river, you're treated to breathtaking views of Hobart's waterfront, with its charming blend of historic architecture and modern developments. You'll also be impressed with the backdrop of kunanyi/Mount Wellington.
As the city fades into the distance, you'll be cruising south towards the entrance to North West Bay and D'Entrcasteaux Channel. Bruny Island will come up on the port (left) side of the vessel as you pass by cliffs, beaches, and lush green hills.
Tasmanian Native Wildlife Encounters
Keep an eye out for the diverse marine life that calls the River Derwent home.
Seabirds are likely to soar overhead, and there's a good chance you'll spot seals on rocky outcrops or in the water. The crew onboard can give insights into the local wildlife and ecology.
Cruise for a Seafood Feast
The cruise will anchor at carefully selected spots to sample the local seafood on offer.
The crew are local fishermen, experts in their craft, and they'll bring aboard the freshest catches straight from the surrounding waters. There's nothing quite like a sea breeze as our expert chefs prepare a delectable seafood spread right before your eyes.
You'll learn about the different types of seafood available.
Indulge in a culinary adventure featuring Tasmanian treasures such as succulent oysters, plump abalone, and perhaps freshly caught fish grilled to perfection. The tastes and flavours reflect the pure, untouched waters of Tasmania.
Seafood is prepared right in front of you as the boat transforms into a floating restaurant, offering al fresco dining on deck. Enjoy with a backdrop of the ocean and Tasmanian wines, chosen to complement the seafood
As the boat cruises back to the Hobart docks, you'll really appreciate Hobart's maritime history and the bounties of the Tasmanian sea.
