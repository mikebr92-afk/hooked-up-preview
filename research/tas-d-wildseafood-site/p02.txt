Tasmania's Premier Marine Tours
Join us on an immersive marine adventure that connects you directly with the ocean, the people who work it, and the food it provides.
Grounded in our “deep to dish” ethos, every tour is authentic, hands-on and shaped by Hobart’s waterways. This isn’t observation; it’s participation.
From the moment you step onboard, you’re invited into a living, breathing environment where each experience is shaped by nature, season and story—crafted to feel raw, personal and impossible to replicate anywhere else.
DEEP TO DISH
Our signature experience and the ultimate expression of “deep to dish,” this hands-on adventure takes you from Hobart to the wild waters of Bruny Island, where divers harvest fresh wild caught periwinkles and sea urchin right in front of you to be enjoyed as part of an onboard seafood feast, also including fresh abalone, crayfish, and oysters.
You’ll be invited to shuck, prepare and taste it onboard - turning the ocean into your kitchen and the journey into something unforgettable.

Diver Reality
More than a world-first dive experience, this is an incredible on-water sightseeing journey along Tasmania’s dramatic coastline, taking in towering sea cliffs, blowholes and abundant marine life.
Then, without getting wet, you’ll ‘dive’ beneath your feet via live VR - following real divers in real time as they move through kelp forests and along the seafloor, transforming a spectacular cruise into a fully immersive, above-and-below ocean adventure.

SEA TO SKY
As daylight fades, step into a completely different side of Tasmania’s waterways on this atmospheric night cruise.
With seafood, warming soup, local drinks and storytelling from your crew, you’ll experience the magic of the ocean after dark, where stars, wildlife and stillness create something truly special.
