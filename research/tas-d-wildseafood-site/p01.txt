We take food-lovers, adventure-seekers and experience-collectors
on authentic marine experiences. 
Hosted by real, local, fishermen - on the waters of Tasmania.
From our signature “Deep to Dish” adventure - where you’ll harvest and prepare fresh wild caught seafood like oysters, abalone and crayfish straight from the ocean - to the atmospheric Sea to Sky night cruise and our world-first Southern Reef Submersion ‘diver reality’ dive, every journey is immersive, hands-on and unlike anything else. 
Led by real fishermen and divers, you’ll explore Tasmania’s wild coastline above and below the surface, experiencing the sights, stories and flavours of the sea in real time.
It’s a rare chance to connect with place through food, adventure and innovation - designed to surprise, delight and stay with you long after you’ve left the water.
THE ULTIMATE CULINARY SEAFOOD EXPERIENCE IN TASMANIA 
Our Deep-to-Dish: Tasmanian Seafood Cruise is our signature culinary tour - a decadent degustation dining experience where you will enjoy the spoils of Tasmanian seafood - oysters , abalone , Atlantic salmon, sea urchin (uni) - only moments away from Hobart's CBD . 
Watch on as the freshest wild seafood is caught by our divers and prepared in front of you on board our luxury two storey power catamaran ‘Cuttlefish’.
All this whilst cruising from the Hobart docks on some of the most untouched and idyllic waterways in the world.
