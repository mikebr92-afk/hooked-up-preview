Reef Fishing
Innkeeper Reef Fishing Adventure
Keen on catching a feed of tasty reef fish? Join us for a fun day of small group bottom fishing on our luxury vessels.
We will be fishing various depths (15m to 120m) and locations to keep you on the fish, targeting red emperor, spangled emperor, coral trout, rankin cod, scarlet sea perch and gold band snapper to name just a few of the superb eating fish Exmouth’s pristine waters are known for.
At the end of your day our crew will take the fillets off the fish you have caught and bag them up with the skin on, ready for you to take home..
Full Day Exclusive Charter – 44ft Fishing Vessel “INNKEEPER” 
Exclusive booking, your group only. Maximum of 8 people. 
Price: Please contact us
Includes: Western Australian fishing licence, all tackle, water and soft drink, (BYO lunch and alcohol).
Times: Leaves at 8:00am and returns at 4:00pm
Full Day Exclusive Charter – 25ft Fishing Vessel “KEEPERINN”
Exclusive booking, your group only. Maximum of 6 people. 
Price: Please contact us
Includes: Western Australian fishing licence, all tackle, water and soft drink, (BYO lunch and alcohol).
Times: Leaves at 8:00am and returns at 4:00pm
Full Day Single Person Rate 
Can’t get a group together? Let us find the other anglers for you. We have a maximum of 5 anglers on our fast center console to get you to the grounds, wasting no time.
Price: Please contact us
Includes: Western Australian fishing licence, all tackle, bottled water (BYO lunch and alcohol). Times: Normally leaves at 8:00am and returns before 4:00pm
