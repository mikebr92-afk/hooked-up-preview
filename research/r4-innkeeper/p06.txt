Innkeeper Live Aboard Adventure 
If you’ve been dreaming of a multi‑day fishing adventure on the Ningaloo Reef, now is the time to lock it in — our Innkeeper Live Aboard trips book out fast , especially during peak marlin, GT and reef fishing seasons. This is the ultimate way to experience Exmouth: untouched reefs, remote islands, world‑class sportfishing, and the freedom to explore it all from your luxury vessel.
Each trip is tailored to your group, built around your favorite fishing styles or designed to help you achieve long‑held angling goals. With full range across the pristine waters of Ningaloo, your adventure can include:
• Mind‑blowing inshore sport fishing around uninhabited tropical islands
• Casting poppers and surface stickbaits at untouched coral reef edges for monster giant trevally 
• Targeting all six Australian billfish species — blue, black & striped marlin, sailfish, broadbill swordfish and spearfish — along the Continental Shelf
• Jigging or dropping baits on unfished deepwater reefs for non‑stop action on hard‑fighting reef species
• Optional mother ship + SPORTSMAN centre console for the ultimate topwater casting experience
Each night is spent at sheltered, scenic anchorages where you can relax, recharge, and enjoy the serenity.

Montebello Islands Live Aboard Adventure 
If the Montebello Islands are on your bucket list, now is the time to lock in your trip! Our multi‑day live aboard adventures book out months ahead , especially during peak sailfish and GT season. This is the ultimate Monties experience: remote islands, untouched reefs, world‑class sportfishing, and the freedom to explore it all from your luxury vessel.
Each adventure is tailored to your group, focusing on high‑energy, mostly catch‑and‑release sportfishing. Expect unforgettable days casting lures at the islands, teasing sailfish for multiple hook‑ups, and working deepwater reefs for non‑stop action. Fresh fish is enjoyed most nights, and you’ll bring a feed home too.
Why These Trips Sell Out

• Scenic, sheltered anchorages every night
