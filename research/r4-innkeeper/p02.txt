About 
EXPERIENCE WORLD CLASS FISHING
Exmouth is recognised as one of the finest game fishing destinations in the world. With a canyon-riddled Continental Shelf line only a few kilometres offshore and the largest fringing coral reef in Australia (Ningaloo Reef) running hard against the coast, Exmouth’s is Australia’s premier sport and game fishing destination.
Encounter a Huge Variety of Species
Exmouth’s pristine, World Heritage listed waters are home to an amazing array of prized game and sport fish.
All six Australian billfish species (blue, black and striped marlin, sailfish, broadbill swordfish and shortbill spearfish) are encountered here, along with yellowfin tuna, mahi mahi (dolphinfish), wahoo, cobia, spanish mackerel, giant trevally, golden trevally, queenfish and amberjack just to name a few.
Fish with the BEST
Innkeeper Sport & Game Fishing Charters are ready to put you amongst the incredible action of Australia’s hottest fishing destination.
Our 44ft luxury game boat and 25ft fast centre console is specially equipped with the latest electronics, Offshore Custom Sport rods, Bonze lures and the very best in tackle to take on any of Exmouth’s long list of prized fish species while accommodating you in comfort and style!
Our highly experienced skippers and crew have a wealth of local knowledge of where, when and how to catch any of the sport, game and reef fish which call these amazing waters home, and will tailor your trip to ensure you experience the very best fishing Exmouth has to offer.
Give Captain Brendon Hawker a call today on +61 4 3757 9448
