Exmouth Gulf Sailfish
Innkeeper Gulf Sailfish Adventure
OCTOBER/NOVEMBER ONLY
Spend a day in the sheltered waters of Exmouth Gulf sight casting to packs of surface feeding sailfish. This extremely visual style of light tackle game fishing is only available in October/November when large numbers of sailfish enter Exmouth Gulf to feed on large schools of baitfish. Expect exciting action with multiple hook-ups and some stunning photos as these aerobatic sailfish dance all over the water surface.
Full Day Charter, Exclusive hire of our exciting fast Sportsman centre console and crew. 
Price: $2700 per day 
Includes: Western Australian fishing licence, skipper, all tackle, bottled water (BYO lunch and alcohol).
Times : Leaves at 8:00am returns at 4:30pm.
TERMS AND CONDITIONS 
Give Captain Brendon Hawker a call today on +61 4 3757 9448
