Our Boats 
MEET OUR FLEET
Innkeeper Sport Fishing Charters run two, custom built sport & game fishing vessels – INNKEEPER a 44ft O’Brien gamefisher and KEEPERINN a 25ft Sportsman centre console trailer boat. Both are fitted out with the latest fish finding technology and fishing equipment. 
INNKEEPER - DESIGNED SPECIFICALLY FOR GAME FISHING & EXTENDED LIVE ABOARDS
The INNKEEPER is a custom built 44ft O’Brien powered by twin 540hp Cummins motors. Launched in 2005, she was specifically designed and built for game fishing, with manoeuvrability and stability in mind. 
The INNKEEPER is a fast, spacious and luxurious vessel boasting top of the line Furuno Electronics.
Australia Wide AMSA SURVEY for 8 passengers + 2 crew and sleeps 7 for overnight live aboard charters. 
The boat is air conditioned throughout, with a fully equipped galley and spacious saloon. 
Our accommodation consists of a starboard side master stateroom with a double bed and ensuite bathroom with a shower and toilet. The forward berth consists of 4 roomy bunks with a separate shower and toilet. 
The INNKEEPER has a cockpit ready to fish, with a 115lt bait tank, 4 tuna tubes, 400lt freezer, 100lt drinks fridge and a 130lb Reelax game chair to get the job done. 
Boat Specifications:
LOA: 14.2M (46ft.7in)
Measured Length: 13.4m (44ft)
Maximum Beam: 4.6m (15.1ft)
Draft: 1m
Engines: 2x Cummins QSC 540 (derated to 500hp)
Transmission: – Twin Disc
Max Speed: 30 Knots
Cruise speed: 20-24 Knots
Generator: Onan 13.5kva
SK Water Maker: 100LPH
Maximum Fresh Water: 1000lt
Maximum Fuel: 2400lt
KEEPERINN - FAST & SPACIOUS DAY TRIP SPORTFISHER
KEEPERINN is a custom built 25ft Sportsman powered by twin 200hp Yamaha outboard motors. Designed, built and fitted out specifically for sport fishing, it is capable of reaching the furthest fishing grounds quickly, to make sure your day on the water is as action packed as possible. 
KEEPERINN is a fast, centre cabin Sportsman boasting top of the line SIMRAD Electronics and capable of reaching speeds in excess of 40 knots.
The vessel has an Australia Wide AMSA SURVEY for 6 passengers + 1 crew for day trip charters.
KEEPERINN has a walkaround, open yet shaded cockpit perfect for casting lures, reef fishing or any other sport and game fishing applications. Features a 100lt bait tank, onboard toilet, 22ft carbon fibre outriggers, upper captains helm for greater visibility and cockpit misters for those warm Exmouth days at sea. 
Boat Specifications:
LOA: 7.67m (25.2ft)
Maximum Beam: 2.79m (9.2ft)
Draft: 400mm 
Engines: 2 x Yamaha 200hp outboards
Max Speed: 44 Knots Cruise Speed: 20-35 Knots Maximum Fuel: 560lt
