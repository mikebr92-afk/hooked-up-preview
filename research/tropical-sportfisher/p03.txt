DAY TRIP ENQUIRY 
Guides work to the daily conditions to offer the best fishing available.
• Target Trophy Barramundi
• Live Scope fitted to both our guide boats
• Different Rivers and estuary locations, guide will chose daily location based on tides and weather conditions. 
• Target species include- Barramundi, Mangrove Jack, Queenfish, Tarpon, GT, Fingermark,Grunter and more.
• Different techniques include: casting topwater, weedless soft plastics , jigging, sight fishing, swim baits,
• Our guides are happy to teach/tudor anglers new to lure/soft plastics fishing
• Barramundi fishing 
• Both Boats fitted with Live Sonar
• Local experienced barra guides.
• We regularly guide anglers on to their first ever barramundi.
• We specialise in targeting trophy sized barramundi and run dedicated trips for dedicated anglers.
• Meter-plus barramundi can be found year-round in the Far North’s river and estuary systems.
• Our guides specialize in year-round barramundi lure fishing.
• Other target species on offer if barramundi fishing is tough
Top water Mangrove Jack Fishing 
• The Cairns region offers incredible topwater fishing for mangrove jack.
• Learn to skip cast weedless plastics/frogs into the mangroves with quick lessons and plenty of fun.
• All our charters focus on lure or fly fishing.
• Our guides are eager to teach anglers the fundamentals of lure fishing, providing skills to use in their home waters.
River Gt’s and Queenfish 
• Large Queenfish and Gts frequent the river systems throughout the year
• Topwater/stickbaits inside the river!
• A great option for if its too windy for Bluewater fishing
• These Gts and Queenfish offer great sight fishing for dedicated anglers.
Your Day: 
• Half Day pick up 630-drop off 12noon (up to 35mins travel to boat launch)
• Full Day pick up 630- drop off 400pm (up to 1.5 hour travel to boat launch)
• Lunch, snacks, drinks, and water are provided.
• Quality tackle supplied, including medium/light spin and casting outfits.
What to Bring: 
• Long-sleeved shirt, hat, sunblock, polarized sunglasses.
• Camera 
• Feel free to bring along your own lures and rod/reel outfits.

Private Fishing Charters & Nature Tours 
Tropical Sportfisher is a locally owned fly and sportfishing charter based in Cairns, Far North Queensland. Led by experienced local guides, we specialise in guided barramundi fishing and light tackle lure and fly fishing across Cairns, Port Douglas, and Mission Beach .
We run private day trips for up to 2 anglers per boat, and offer multi-day remote adventures for small groups, including mothership and heli-access options.
Our trips include river, estuary, jungle perch, and inshore bluewater fishing. We also offer wildlife cruises , croc spotting, birdwatching, and private nature or photography trips to secluded beaches, waterfalls, or tropical islands.
Whether you’re chasing fish or exploring wild places, our charters are tailored to your interests and the conditions. Get in touch to experience the best of Tropical North Queensland with a local guide.
