ENQUIRE ABOUT THIS TRIP 
Explore quiet, untouched tropical rivers teeming with birdlife and crocodiles on a personalised nature cruise. These private trips are ideal for bird photographers, wildlife enthusiasts, or anyone wanting to experience the quieter side of the tropics.
We are the only operator offering dedicated wildlife and birding tours on the southern river systems, where boat traffic is minimal and the atmosphere is truly wild. With local knowledge, silent electric motor access, and the flexibility to go where others can’t, this is an off-the-beaten-path nature experience.
Tour Highlights
Fully Private Experience – Max 2 guests (up to 4 on request)
Locally Guided – Remote rivers south of Cairns
Quiet & Immersive – Electric motor allows silent drifting
Tropical Birdlife – Excellent opportunities for bird photography
Hard-to-Reach Habitats – Access secluded waterways larger boats cannot reach
Wildlife Sightings – Frequent estuarine crocodile encounters

Private Fishing Charters & Nature Tours 
Tropical Sportfisher is a locally owned fly and sportfishing charter based in Cairns, Far North Queensland. Led by experienced local guides, we specialise in guided barramundi fishing and light tackle lure and fly fishing across Cairns, Port Douglas, and Mission Beach .
We run private day trips for up to 2 anglers per boat, and offer multi-day remote adventures for small groups, including mothership and heli-access options.
Our trips include river, estuary, jungle perch, and inshore bluewater fishing. We also offer wildlife cruises , croc spotting, birdwatching, and private nature or photography trips to secluded beaches, waterfalls, or tropical islands.
Whether you’re chasing fish or exploring wild places, our charters are tailored to your interests and the conditions. Get in touch to experience the best of Tropical North Queensland with a local guide.
