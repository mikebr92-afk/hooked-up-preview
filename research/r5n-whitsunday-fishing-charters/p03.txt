Frequently Asked Questions 
• What are the best things to do in Airlie Beach and the Whitsundays? 
The Whitsundays is famous for its turquoise waters, island beaches, snorkelling, and marine life. One of the best ways to experience it is on a private boat charter, where you can explore hidden spots, go snorkelling, fish, and enjoy the scenery away from the crowds.
• Do I need boating or fishing experience? 
No experience is needed. Our crew takes care of everything and guides you throughout the day, whether you're snorkelling or fishing.
• Is this suitable for first-time visitors to the Whitsundays? 
Yes — our tours are designed for all experience levels and are a great introduction to the region's highlights.
• Is a boat charter a must-do in the Whitsundays? 
Yes — a boat trip is one of the most popular and highly recommended experiences in the region. It's the best way to see multiple islands, coral reefs, and secluded bays in a single day.
• How long should I spend in Airlie Beach to see the Whitsundays? 
Most visitors stay 3–5 days to fully enjoy the islands, reef, and tours. A private charter is one of the best ways to maximise your time and see multiple highlights in a single day.
• Do we need to book in advance during peak season? 
Yes — the Whitsundays is a major tourist destination, especially during school holidays and winter months, so booking ahead is recommended to secure your preferred date.
