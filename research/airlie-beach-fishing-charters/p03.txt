About Us
Why Choose Airlie Beach Fishing Charters
Airlie Beach Fishing Charters
Airlie Beach Fishing Charters has become the region’s #1 choice for tourists and locals looking for a great fishing adventure. Departing most days, we offer half day up to full day fishing charters around the magical Whitsunday Islands and the Great Barrier Reef. Our vessel “Stryker”, is a 9m monohull aluminium plate boat powered by 2x300HP Mercury Sea Pro V8 outboards to get you onto those fishing spots quicker. Stryker also has a fishing platform that extends over the outboard engines, creating an additional 6sq/m of fishing room.
Our crew are dedicated to customer service and pride themselves in being at the very top of their game in the angling community. With the combination of local knowledge and commitment to finding new fishing ground, they will have you on the fish in no time and always be there to get you a cold drink out of the Eski (or Chilly Bin for our NZ friends). Whether you are new to fishing or want a tailored fishing experience, we can make it happen for you. We are very lucky to be based at Airlie Beach, QLD with access to the best fishing spots throughout the Whitsundays.
