Catch some of the 1300 different fish species at the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park in Airlie Beach, QLD!
Fish with a family-friendly crew dedicated to welcoming people of all abilities and giving you a great fishing experience and enjoy going around the Whitsunday Islands & Shoals during your fishing charter.
What will we be doing and what will we be targeting? You will mainly be reef fishing/bottom bashing to catch fish such as coral trout, sweet lip, red emperor, nannygai, and much more (trips may vary due to weather and tide conditions).
•

• 
• Bottled Water & Ice
• Lunch – Yummy Wraps
• Fruits
• Environmental Park Fee (Reef Tax) Per Person
• All High-Quality Equipment: Rods, Reels, & Bait – proudly sponsored by Shimano.
• All Fish Cleaned & Packed – for you to take with you!
