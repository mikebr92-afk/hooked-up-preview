Private Fishing Charter around Whitsunday Islands
Make the most of a 4-hour private fishing charter in the Whitsundays. This charter is a great option for families, first-time anglers, or anyone short on time who still wants to experience the region’s exceptional fishing.
Enjoy trolling between locations targeting Spanish mackerel and tuna, or reef fishing for species such as coral trout, sweetlip, red emperor and much more! 
AM and PM options available, operating from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM or 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM. 
Pricing is $1,950 for a private charter of up to 10 passengers. 
• 
Included with Every Private Charter
• 
• Complimentary Transfers from your Holiday Accommodation
• All high quality equipment (rods, reels and bait)
• Bottled water & Ice ( BYO Alcohol in cans please)
• All fish cleaned and packed
• 
What to Bring
• 
Hat, sunglasses, camera, snacks, and any alcohol you would like to bring (cans only). During winter, we recommend bringing a jacket or jumper.
• 
Meeting Point
• 
Meet at Coral Sea Marina, North Village, outside the front of Sorrento Restaurant. Parking is available next to the meeting point.
