Private Fishing Charter around Whitsunday Islands
8-Hour Private Fishing Charter
Spend a full day fishing the Whitsunday Islands aboard Stryker , our 10.8-metre charter vessel with a spacious 6 x 6 m fishing platform. Choose between fishing the islands and shoals for maximum time on the water, or head to the Outer Reef—around 40 nautical miles away—for the chance to target game fish.
Expect reef fishing and bottom bashing for species including coral trout, sweetlip, red emperor, nannygai and more. Trips are tailored around weather and tide conditions to give you the best possible fishing experience. Fresh sandwiches and snacks are included onboard.
6-Hour Private Fishing Charter
Enjoy an extended private fishing adventure to some of the Whitsundays’ best fishing grounds aboard Stryker . With six hours on the water, this charter is ideal for guests wanting plenty of fishing time around the islands and shoals.
Your day will focus mainly on reef fishing and bottom bashing, targeting coral trout, sweetlip, red emperor, nannygai and other local species. Trolling and lure casting can be arranged for groups of up to four passengers. Locations and activities may vary with weather and tide conditions.
4-Hour Private Fishing Charter
Make the most of a half-day private fishing experience in the Whitsundays. This charter is a great option for families, first-time anglers, or anyone short on time who still wants to experience the region’s exceptional fishing.
Fish the Inner and Outer Whitsunday Islands, with trolling between locations for Spanish mackerel or tuna, alongside reef fishing and bottom bashing for coral trout, sweetlip, red emperor and more. Our crew will use local knowledge and conditions to find the best available fishing spots.
• 
Included with Every Private Charter
• 
• Complimentary transfers from your holiday accommodation
• Quality Shimano rods, reels, bait and tackle
• Bottled water, ice and sunscreen
• Esky onboard
• Fish cleaned and packed on ice for you to take home
• $7 per-person Environmental Management Charge (reef tax) included in the charter price
• BYO alcohol welcome—cans only, please
• 
What to Bring
• 
Hat, sunglasses, camera, snacks, and any alcohol you would like to bring (cans only). During winter, we recommend bringing a jacket or jumper.
• 
Meeting Point
• 
Meet at Coral Sea Marina, North Village, outside the front of Sorrento Restaurant. For full-day charters, please be ready at 6:45 am for the crew to meet you. Parking is available next to the meeting point.
