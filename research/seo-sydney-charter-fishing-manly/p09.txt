What We Provide
What We Provide
• Top-quality fishing gear : rods, reels, bait, and premium braided fishing lines so you feel every bite.
• Comfortable facilities : seating, sun and rain cover for your convenience.
• Fish preparation : all catches are cleaned, gutted, and gilled. Why not take your catch to Felons Manly – for $20 they cook how you like and will serve with fries and sauce 
• Expert tuition : guidance from an experienced crew to enhance your fishing skills.
• Advanced equipment : modern fish-finding electronics to maximise your daily catch.
• Licensing taken care of : no fishing licence is needed—we handle that for you.
• State-of-the-art vessel : fast, modern, and ready to explore over 1,000 offshore reefs.
• Fully licensed and insured services .
• All safety equipment (first aid, sun cream, water etc)
• 
• 
What to Bring
What to Bring
• Food and motion sickness tablets 
• Esky or cooler to take your fish home.
• Weather-appropriate clothing (warm clothes in winter).
• A camera to capture the action.
• Hat, sunglasses, and sunscreen.
• Containers to store your fish.
• Wet weather gear (just in case).
• Comfortable enclosed shoes (sneakers work best).
Important Note : Sunglasses are highly recommended to protect your eyes from the sun and glare off the water.
Departure Info
Departure Info
• Arrive at Manly Wharf 3, the pickup wharf at least 15 minutes before departure .
• Illegal items are strictly prohibited—please review our Terms and Conditions. 
If you’re new to being on the water, consider taking a travel sickness tablet before boarding. We will not return early for sea sickness, nor are refunds offered for those who feel unwell. Wave and swell conditions are part of the experience, so be prepared!
Safety Note : Children under 8 years old are not permitted due to safety and lifejacket limitations. Our charters can be physically demanding, so please assess whether this is suitable for your child.
