Experience one of the most exciting forms of sportfishing on the Great Barrier Reef with FNQ Fishing Adventures’ Topwater GT Charters .
Giant Trevally are one of the hardest-hitting and most powerful sportfish found in tropical waters, and chasing them on surface lures takes the experience to another level. Casting large poppers and stickbaits across reef edges, pressure points and isolated coral systems can result in explosive surface strikes, brutal runs and unforgettable battles.
Our GT fishing charters from Cairns and Port Douglas are designed for anglers wanting to experience genuine Great Barrier Reef topwater fishing in some of Far North Queensland’s most spectacular fishing country.
The outer reef systems off Cairns and Port Douglas provide incredible habitat for Giant Trevally. Strong tidal movement, deep reef edges, isolated bommies and bait-rich coral systems create ideal conditions for large predatory fish.
Every day on the reef is different, and conditions, tides and fish behaviour all play a part. Our charters are structured around finding productive water and giving anglers the best possible opportunity to experience some quality GT’s exploding on topwater lures .
PRIVATE TOPWATER FISHING CHARTERS 
FNQ Fishing Adventures specialises in private fishing charters from Cairns and Port Douglas , allowing us to tailor the day around your group rather than operating a crowded shared charter.
Our small-group format gives anglers more casting time, more room to fish and a much more personalised experience on the water.
Whether you are an experienced lure angler specifically travelling to Far North Queensland to chase trophy Giant Trevally or simply want to experience one of Australia's most exciting styles of fishing, we can tailor the charter to suit your experience and goals.
A GT fishing charter from Port Douglas or Cairns is about more than simply catching fish.
It is a chance to travel through crystal-clear tropical water, fish remote coral reef systems and experience sections of the Great Barrier Reef that very few people get to see.
From huge surface strikes and screaming drags to sight-casting along untouched reef edges, this fishing charter offer an unforgettable way to experience Giant Trevally fishing in Far North Queensland.
If chasing a trophy GT on a surface lure is on your fishing bucket list, this is the trip for you.
