Charter Info
View Charter Options & Pricing 
Audacity Charters is a family run business based in and run out of Townsville, North Queensland.
‘Vanguard’ is our flagship and has been customised to suit Reef and Inshore Charters. ‘Vanguard’ is a 55ft Austral outfitted with Inboard with shaft driven Twin MAN and is the fastest and most comfortable charter boat in the region. Our skippers are experienced fishermen and our crew will make you feel welcome. Your day will feel like a day out with mates rather than a rigid charter operation. 
A charter with ‘Vanguard’ allows up to 12 guests at a time and we will get you out to the first drop within 1.5-2 hours depending on the weather conditions. You can book the whole boat and set the day up to suit your group or you can jump on one of our Mixed Group Charters for individual spots. Full Boat bookings can be booked any date with 7 days’ notice, whilst Mixed Group Charters are opened, by the team at Audacity based on availability and interest. Mixed group charter days are advertised through our Facebook pages. 
Your charter Includes
Your charter with us will include all bait, tackle, ice, bottled water and a selection of light snacks and soft drinks. All you need to bring is main meals and alcohol (if desired). You are also welcome to bring your own gear but it isn’t necessary.
Minimum Age restrictions
We do not have any age restrictions placed on our full boat bookings however please notify us when placing your booking if you have children under 16yrs of age. Children under 16yrs may require alternative safety requirements for their charter.
Due to the nature of mixed group charters, we normally recommend children be above 15yrs, and they must have a supervising adult also on charter with them. Should you wish to book a spot for a child under 16yrs, please notify us when placing your booking.
Departure Info
Charters leave from the Breakwater Marina on Mariners Drive in Townsville. Parking is available along the Strand and it is a short walk along the marina esplanade to the fuel finger where you will meet ‘Vanguard’ and her crew. Our 13- hour Day charters leave at 5am and return around 6pm. 12 hour Night charters leave at 3pm and extended charters, 15-20 hours, leave at times discussed with the crew when the booking is made.
Weather
All charters are booked pending weather forecasts. Our skippers watch the weather leading up to the charter date, and make the call 3-7 days in advance when the forecasts settle and are more reliable. Last minute cancellations may occur if there is an unexpected weather change and all charters are at the discretion of our skippers. Ultimately we use our expertise to ensure that you have a safe day and the very best experience. 
Deposits
A deposit for your full boat charter is $250 and a deposit for your mixed group charter is 10% or your booking or $50pp. Full payment of the outstanding balance will be due prior to the charter leaving, once your charter is confirmed good to proceed.
Cancellations
Cancellation within 2 weeks of your full boat booking will forfeit your deposit. Should you require cancellation prior to the 2 weeks, if you are unable to rebook, a full refund will be organised.
Cancellations within 48 hours of your mixed group charter will forfeit your charter deposit. Should we be required to cancel the charter, we will endeavour to rebook you onto another charter or offer you a full refund of monies paid.
Vouchers
Do you have a gift voucher with us and don’t want to organise a full boat? Contact us to inquire how we can get you onto a mixed group charter.

Don’t have 12 people or don’t wish to book a full boat for a small group? 
Mixed group charters allow you to book individual spots on a 12-13hr Mixed Group Reds and Reef Charter. Limited numbers of mixed group charters are made available each month via our website and extra charters are opened at the discretion of our skippers. 
Our skippers may open a mixed group charter with short notice (2-3 days) after they have reviewed the weather. Short notice mixed group charters will be advertised via our Facebook pages.
Our mixed group charters are set to a maximum of 12 people and have all of our standard inclusions. Mixed group charters require all spots to be filled prior to charter confirmation to proceed. 
If you have a committed group of 6 and don’t want to book the whole boat, we can discuss opening a Mixed Group Charter. Opening a Small Group Mixed Charter will require the remaining spots being filled before the charter can proceed. These spots will be advertised as per normal through our Facebook pages, with short notice, once the skipper has confirmed the weather is good to head out. Contact Siew-Ling to discuss your options.
Check out our booking calendar for available mixed group charters already open, and keep an eye on our Facebook page—our skippers will post details of short-notice mixed group charters as soon as they become available!
Number of Guests & Costs Info 
Mixed group charters are subject to minimum guest numbers in order for the trip to proceed. If the required minimum number of guests is not reached prior to departure, the total minimum charter cost may be divided between the confirmed guests booked on the trip.
In this situation, all guests will be contacted individually and given the option to proceed at the adjusted shared charter rate. Participation at the revised rate is entirely optional, and guests who choose not to proceed will be able to transfer their booking to another available charter date.
If there are insufficient guests willing to cover the minimum charter cost required to operate the trip, we reserve the right to cancel the charter. In the event of cancellation, all affected guests will be offered the opportunity to rebook onto another available mixed group charter.
