Join us as we take you into the Territory's wild outback for a serious fishing adventure. Chase Barramundi well over a metre long, Jewfish, Threadfin Salmon, Snapper and more…
Jump aboard our custom-designed 7.8m sports fishing boat for some serious onboard action using our first-rate equipment. Enjoy our professional guided service, all-inclusive meals and make memories for a lifetime!
